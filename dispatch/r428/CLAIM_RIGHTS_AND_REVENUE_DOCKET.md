## Source-level merchant adjudication

**Zen12** (https://zen12.com/about and https://zen12.com/): manufacturer describes twelve levels, four audio styles per level, full 48-MP3 library and twelve-minute sessions. The asserted "twelve minutes equals an hour of meditation" is a SELLER CLAIM, not an independently verified finding. Ingendoh et al. 2023 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37205669/) found inconsistent EEG entrainment results in fourteen heterogeneous binaural-beat studies. A 2025 controlled attention study (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39910150/) showed some parameter-dependent changes but no improvement in vigilance decline. Neither study tested Zen12 as sold. Different seller sales funnels display different packages/prices; no referral SKU, checkout price or discount can be asserted in our email.

**Total Focus** (https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus): seller specifies a 33-minute 47 MB MP3 by J. A. Amos, with separate single-recording and bundle offers. The seller says it improves concentration, productivity and other outcomes; independent product-specific evidence was not found in reviewed sources. NCCIH (https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/hypnosis) summarizes some condition-specific evidence for hypnosis, with caveats, not evidence validating this recording's marketing claims. A 2015 study (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25557062/) distinguishes hypnotic depth and suggestibility; it is not a test of Total Focus. Seller FAQ (https://hypnosislive.com/faq) describes a 14-day refund request window qualified by no more than five refunded sessions per year. Seller pricing does NOT mean a Final Vestige referred checkout is confirmed.

**Inspire3 affiliate portal** (https://affiliates.inspire3.com/): public program claims special issued earning links and commissions, but the public marketing page does not authenticate Final Vestige membership, specific merchant product approval, permissible email promotion or an exact tracking URL. A targeted connected Gmail search for named email rights on 2026-11-17 yielded no affirmative matching approval; absence in reviewed results is not proof that approval does not exist. Source-of-truth account-specific evidence belongs with Mind owner and must remain private.

**Legal**: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business requires honest headers and subjects, appropriate commercial identification, initiating sender's valid postal address and easy effective opt-out. Inspire3's London postal address is not the Final Vestige postal address. https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/email-appearance/ documents non-sending Preview email, but it has NOT been used for either candidate. Offline HTML cannot prove received-mail rendering or compliance.

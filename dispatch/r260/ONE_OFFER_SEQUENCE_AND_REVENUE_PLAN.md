## Status boundary for each signal

- **NOT A LEAD:** visits, subscribe clicks, submit attempts, visible download, PDF downloads, Reader-only follow, unconfirmed requests, welcome *configuration*.
- **CONFIRMED LEAD (ONLY IF PROVEN):** authoritative “Email subscription: Subscribed” state plus appropriate consent and a deduplicated provider event. No email addresses in analytics/issue comments; one private opaque key at most, under Claude's existing consent/analytics governance.
- **NOT A SALE:** open, click, guide view, affiliate outbound, cart, checkout start or merchant testimonial.
- **PAID SALE (ONLY IF PROVEN):** settled payment/commission from the authoritative commerce provider, properly matched where consent/partner reporting permit, net of refunds, chargebacks, partner fees and true costs; never fabricate attribution to Grok or Dispatch.

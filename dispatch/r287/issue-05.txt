Subject: Before you press play on a hypnosis audio
Preheader: Three questions that matter more than an impressive promise.
Type: commercial-review-pending

A recording offers convenience: you can revisit it, practice privately and decide whether a guided format suits you. That says something about the format. It doesn't settle what the recording can change.

In our historical Puységur files, the hardest part is separating what observers reported from the conclusions they attached to those reports. Modern hypnosis marketing deserves the same scrutiny.

Before buying any self-hypnosis audio, I'd ask three questions.

What is actually included? A downloadable recording, a course, a subscription, or something else? Are the instructions and usage conditions clear?

What is the claim? General relaxation, a pleasant guided experience and a guaranteed change in behavior are different promises. They require different kinds of support.

What happens if it isn't for you? Check current price, format, access terms and refund conditions on the seller's actual page, not in an old promotion.

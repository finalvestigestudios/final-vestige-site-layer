Twelve minutes. Two very different questions.

Starting a task and choosing meditation audio aren't the same experiment.

A clock can show twelve minutes. It cannot show you what those minutes accomplished.

Our free Focus Fieldbook uses a twelve-minute work-starting exercise. A meditation audio can also take twelve minutes, but that does not make it the same activity—or establish the same result.

Choose your question first

To start work, identify one avoided task and attempt a small step for twelve minutes. To try meditation, sit comfortably, notice breathing or ordinary sounds, and gently return when attention wanders. Write down what actually happened. Neither observation alone proves a clinical benefit.

The Zen12 seller page describes the Premium meditation library as twelve levels and 48 MP3 files, but its claim that twelve minutes equal an hour's meditation benefits is not independently established for the product. Generic NCCIH research is not an endorsement of this particular audio.

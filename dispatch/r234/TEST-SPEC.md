# R234 independent, non-sending source-acceptance specification

The contract and alternative are **not** a delivery mechanism. Before merge/release, check the exact committed bytes against this specification and the current six-issue manifest. Source-only preflight should reject each proposed unauthorized variant:

1. Any change from `HOLD_UNSENT`, or any assertion that `site_can_send=true` equals permission/received delivery.
2. More or fewer than six campaigns; anything but exactly four editorial and two commercial-review-pending.
3. Any commercial #03/#05 actual guide `destination` or earning URL before merchant approval, EMAIL permission and live vetted guide are all independently established.
4. Any claim that Jetpack can send these six markdown drafts as a drip without native new-post publishing/approval.
5. Any `publish`, `send_test_email_authorized`, global auto-email toggle, subscriber import, or direct merchant link inserted by this task.
6. A work-starting Fieldbook block described as equivalent to meditation or to one hour of meditation.
7. Subscriber-count additive math, a download button/URL query credited as provider-confirmed signup, or an outbound click credited as a paid order.
8. Legal footer placeholders removed or declared verified without genuine inbox evidence and founder-approved consent-safe test.
9. Any wording that the seller's brainwave/clinical benefit claims are independently established by generic meditation research.
10. Any conversion-rate or ROI percentage from the tiny list without settled orders and qualified-session denominator.

This document defines acceptance, not a claim that WordPress/merchant/native inbox/live tests were executed. Tests of source-level gates should be labeled as such.

The existing R233 six-manuscript source gate covers different problems (exact seller links and consent proof) and is **not replaced**. R232 separately audits synthetic hidden HTML legal-footers; also not replaced. The founder-approved six originals are unchanged.

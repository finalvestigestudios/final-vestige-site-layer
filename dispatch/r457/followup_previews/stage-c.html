What twelve minutes can—and cannot—tell you

Before buying Zen12, check the format, the evidence and the seller's actual terms.

Twelve minutes makes a small practice easy to picture. That's an observation about the time required, not proof that the result equals an hour of meditation.

Zen12's seller describes a twelve-level audio program with different listening styles. The seller also promotes benefits and equivalence claims that we cannot independently establish for Zen12. That difference matters more than a persuasive demonstration or an impressive timer.

If you consider any paid audio, check three things first. What's in the exact package? File formats, number of sessions, duration and how you receive them. What is actually supported? Separate general research on meditation from evidence for this particular product. What happens after purchase? Look at the exact price, merchant's refund policy, whether a subscription is involved and whether promotional terms will still be valid at checkout.

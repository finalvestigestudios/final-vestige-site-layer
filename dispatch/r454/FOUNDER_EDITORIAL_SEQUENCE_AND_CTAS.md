## Campaign opening and progression hypothesis — no new segmentation assumed

- **Week 1 / evidence first:** invite reader to challenge a perception, one relevant source, and one first-party investigation; do **not** mention Zen12.
- **Week 2 / trust:** show why an archived claim can be documented without proving it true. Invite source reading, not an offer.
- **Week 3 / choice:** offer a free seven-day, twelve-minute observation protocol and contextual skepticism. If partner approval is still missing, use the founder-approved non-commercial alternative from #201 **only after explicit adoption**, or HOLD Issue 03. Do not create a false live CTA.
- **Week 4 / original history:** strengthen the archival identity and let the reader see source precision practiced.
- **Week 5 / second reserved commercial slot:** no commercial send until specific Hypnosis Live evidence; can remain unsent while editorial schedule advances only under separate approval. The same personal review checklist can inform free choices without buying.
- **Week 6 / useful conclusion:** free Fieldbook exercise, one plain link; no manufactured “last chance,” no vendor miracle, no purchase dependency.

> A small habit is tempting to explain with a big promise. But if you feel more settled after twelve minutes, what caused it: the recording, the silence, taking a break, or simply having a routine?

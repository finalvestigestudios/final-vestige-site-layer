## One explicit audience hypothesis (not an existing segment)

High-intent **self-selected** readers who voluntarily navigate from an evidence-based attention story to the free Focus Fieldbook may be interested in a *critical buyer guide*, but this is a testable hypothesis, **not proof of intent** and not a basis to send behavior-triggered blasts. Jetpack's `newsletter_categories_enabled=false` means native interest segmentation is **not active**; there is no installed segmenting autoresponder. Use one existing list and a manually founder-approved weekly sequence, if eventually authorized, respecting opt-outs and provider frequency preferences.

## Proposed consent-to-customer event contract, not live instrumentation

1. `qualified_human_session`: only proven, bot-filtered visit with coherent source/asset/time; no raw emails/IPs in GitHub.
2. `article_to_focus_click`: click to /focus/ as diagnostic only; never equal signup.
3. `confirmed_signup`: **one** new provider-supported confirmation transition, not form attempt/Reader follow; dedupe in trusted system with private ephemeral opaque key, never storing identity in GitHub.
4. `welcome_received`: independent received-mail witness and working Fieldbook link; configured welcome message alone insufficient.
5. `email_to_guide_click`: only after actual authorized email exists and CTA is approved; first-party page event, not conversion.
6. `affiliate_outbound`: when partner-approved eligible guide link exists; distinct from paid buyer.
7. `merchant_confirmed_paid_conversion`: independently reported **settled** eligible purchase/commission, with currency, verified attribution, refunds, fees and reversals.
8. `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_humans`: only audited complete 7-/30-day cohort denominator and independently reconciled net numerator. Unknown if either absent; zero only if real complete ledger shows exactly zero.

## Mathematical acceptance

For each mature **7-day or 30-day** cohort, report: verified qualifying Grok-attributed human sessions; relevant CTA clicks (diagnostic); *new* confirmed consenting leads; customers / independently settled orders; gross realized receipts/eligible commissions; actual refunds/chargebacks; fees; net (gross − refunds − fees); and `1000 × net / qualified sessions`. If any underlying dataset is incomplete, emit `NOT_COMPUTABLE` and explain why. Use a tiny-sample warning at the current WordPress audience size. Do not declare lift or A/B significance.

**Business acceptance:** shipping an email, increasing visits, or collecting three existing subscriber records is not commercial completion. Neither a checkout visit nor an affiliate outbound click is a paid commission. Owner approvals and **founder final campaign approval** precede any email or publication.

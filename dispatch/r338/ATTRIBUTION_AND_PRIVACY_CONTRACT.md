# Dispatch consent-to-net-revenue event and privacy contract

This is an interface proposal for Claude's EXISTING first-party tracking; it deploys no script, tag or pixel. The only current Jetpack subscriber list remains in place. Grok owns creator assets; Praxxis owns shared TASK-008 independent review (existing Drive handoff preserved). Commercial settlement and guide eligibility belong to Commerce/Mind.

### Single consistent identifiers
Only after release approval: source UTMs from verified Grok assets: utm_source = actual youtube/tiktok/instagram/facebook/pinterest/x/bluesky platform; utm_medium = organic_social; utm_campaign = approved mind_pilot_2026_11; utm_content = exact verified asset_id. For a later authorized email guide CTA: utm_source=dispatch, utm_medium=email, utm_campaign=dispatch_pilot_2026_11, utm_content=issue_03_guide. No fabricated asset, no direct merchant tracking link inserted now. Existing first-party analytics owned by Claude, not duplicated here.

Privacy: no email, phone, postal address, name, IP, user-agent fingerprint, buyer medical description, email-derived hash or individualized order/referrer URLs in public GitHub, analytics events or UTMs. Provider's stable identity may be used privately under permissions to dedup a real new confirmed transition; a static provider Subscribed snapshot is **not** evidence of new signup. Merchant gift signup and SMS permission belong solely to the merchant and cannot become FV consent.

### Revenue arithmetic
Net USD/1,000 *qualified human sessions* = 1000 × (independently settled business revenue or commission minus refunds/chargebacks/selling fees) / deduplicated verified human visits. Reconcile 7d and 30d cohorts, public aggregate only, include unknown unattributed orders honestly. Missing denominator or numerator means **NOT COMPUTABLE** (not zero). Lead-to-paid conversion UNKNOWN without an authorized consented private order join. With tiny list, no statistically significant split-test rhetoric or identifying tiny-cohort ratios.

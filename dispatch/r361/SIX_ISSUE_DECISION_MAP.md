## One buyer-intent sequence, without promising nonexistent automation

A legitimate visitor follows a specific Grok historical/attention asset to a relevant live article. On the same site, a voluntary Focus form attempts enrollment in the **existing Jetpack list**. Provider evidence, not button click or a double-opt-in *request*, establishes `confirmed_signup`. The configured welcome message contains the Fieldbook URL; genuine received delivery remains unverified. Each editorial email gives one documented next step. In commercial Issue 03 only *after* approvals, the reader may choose a deeply sourced, live Zen12 buyer guide with an adjacent disclosure; a **verified issued affiliate link** may then link to an authenticated checkout. Issue 05 retains its separate reserved slot without reusing or inventing the Zen12 URL.

A commercial outcome occurs **only** when the seller independently reports a real, settled paid conversion, then fees, refunds and reversals are netted. Neither an email click, pageview, form attempt nor affiliate outbound click is a sale. 7/30-day revenue cohorts require actual timestamps and qualified-human session denominators. Don't infer conversion effectiveness from this three-follower population.

## Output quality gate

Subjects must remain accurate to historical source; preheaders promise actual material, not a teaser the issue fails to deliver. Each issue keeps a substantive evidence or usable exercise, one dominant CTA, accessible plaintext alternative, and linked original sources. Commercial issues require same-issue *and near-CTA* disclosures; the approved guide repeats disclosure near every eligible partner link. No vendor performance claim may be restated as independent science. Offline mockups cannot establish Jetpack rendering or a CAN-SPAM-compliant footer.

**Unresolved/unknown** are proper statuses; no synthetic 'PASSED' check licenses a send.

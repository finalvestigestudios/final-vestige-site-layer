## Issue 03: Zen12 Premium — first offer hypothesis
Official seller https://zen12.com/ states 12 levels, four versions per level and 48 MP3s with 12-minute sessions. The seller also claims "an hour of meditation" equivalence, measurable mental and health benefits, and "brainwave entrainment" effects. A product format can be documented; effects, equivalence, medical outcomes and claimed science are **not independent findings for the paid product**. No unverified discount, timer, testimonial or product footage/audio may appear as proof. Seller free-audio enrollment is an independent marketing relationship, not Final Vestige consent.

Optional subject for founder decision: "What twelve minutes of audio can—and cannot—tell you". Optional preheader: "A short format is measurable. The promised effects still need evidence." Lead with a free attention-practice check, then offer one optional first-party independent guide CTA only AFTER the guide is live, reviewed, and email-rights approved.

Proposed adjacent future disclosure: "If you buy through a qualifying link in the guide, Final Vestige may earn a commission. Product benefit claims are the seller's and have not been independently established by Final Vestige."

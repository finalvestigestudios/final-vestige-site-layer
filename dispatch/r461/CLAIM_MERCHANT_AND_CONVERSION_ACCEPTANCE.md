## Scientific alignment: narrower evidence cannot validate the SKU
NCCIH's hypnosis overview (https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/hypnosis) reports mixed/topic-dependent studies, including evidence for some specific applications and inconclusive results in others. It does NOT evaluate this seller's MP3 catalog. NCCIH's safety overview (https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/relaxation-techniques-what-you-need-to-know) describes rare adverse or unpleasant reactions to some relaxation practices. Therefore the R461 draft avoids guaranteeing any relaxation, behavioral, psychological, medical or other outcome. It never claims the studio used or tested a product. “Reprogram your thinking” on the seller product page is a **seller marketing assertion**, not an established effect for that MP3.

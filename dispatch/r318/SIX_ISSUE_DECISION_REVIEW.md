## Actual subject and preheader QA (not an invented inbox rendering result)

- **01:** "The mind is not a camera." Specific metaphor, but opening CTA should deliver an attention/perception source rather than repeated mesmerism.
- **02:** "Before hypnosis had a name." Grounded only when the Puységur historical era/source is accurately attributed; avoid a suggestion that no preexisting term ever existed.
- **03:** "The twelve-minute meditation question." Not a benefit guarantee. Final subject/preheader should name the *comparison* without price/claim promises not established in merchant evidence.
- **04:** "Fourteen seconds. A much longer explanation." **Editorially unsafe until exact timing proof exists.**
- **05:** "Before you press play on a hypnosis audio." Honest, but generic; a named product evaluation may better qualify buying intent, once authorized.
- **06:** "What did seven days of attention reveal?" Useful only if not implying readers participated. Avoid automatic assumption they downloaded or completed the Fieldbook.

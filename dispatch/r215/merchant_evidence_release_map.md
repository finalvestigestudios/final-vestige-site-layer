### Source review and claim-control exceptions
1. **Price is volatile** and promotional display != independently witnessed checkout; do not publish currency or deadline without dated screenshot and actual legal terms.
2. **Seller refund claims are not proof of refunded transactions.** Check merchant-specific exclusions, time window, purchase method, bundles and affiliate eligibility, and resolve ambiguity before publishing.
3. **Vendor's scientific language** about brainwave entrainment, NLP, hypnosis and psychological/health outcomes cannot transfer automatically from general meditation research to named product efficacy. No FDA/medical implication, no 12min=60min conclusion or guarantee.
4. **Seller video/audio demo** does not license reproduction in Dispatch or provide credible independent tests; exact format/content still requires actual rights-cleared inspection. Even seller-origin product specs should be dated and phrased as vendor descriptions.

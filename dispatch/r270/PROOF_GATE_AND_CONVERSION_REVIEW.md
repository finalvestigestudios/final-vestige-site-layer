## Demonstrated metric-trust defect (HIGH impact)

Existing R268 `dispatch/r268/cohort_audit_v2.js` is intended for offline modelling. With a **caller-supplied** `external_aggregate` payload, when four booleans `complete_qualified_sessions, jetpack_confirmations_verified, merchant_settlements_reconciled, cohort_attribution_verified` are set true, it can output numerical `matched_reported_orders`, `net_cents` and `net_cents_per_1000_qualified_tagged_sessions`. Its warnings say route/day agreement is not identity or proof, but these unqualified numeric fields can be copied into a business dashboard as though they were authenticated. Nothing in this JSON alone authenticates payment, consent, checkout, refund, fee or person-level attribution. **A boolean is not a merchant settlement receipt or an independently retrieved Jetpack provider confirmation.**

R270's **`proof_gate_v3.js`** is a fail-closed *offline* interface in front of V2, not a deployed replacement. For `external_aggregate`, every financial and lead number is withheld even if the upstream boolean checklist asserts verification. It returns `external_metrics=NOT_COMPUTABLE`, `eligible_recipient_count=null`, `authenticated_merchant_sale=false`, `confirmed_signup_verified=false`, and `HOLD_UNSENT`. For `synthetic`, illustrated negative net math may be returned under an explicitly named `demonstration` object with `all_values_hypothetical=true`; these are never business metrics. It rejects malformed or unauthorized source event types without leaking raw event, order, subscriber, or recipient references. **No input can turn a final release gate on.**

This is necessary but not sufficient: a future trusted-data adapter must independently authenticate real merchant settlement/commission reports, refunds and fees; resolve the original acquisition source against consented first-party sessions where legal and genuinely supported; verify `Email subscription → Subscribed` via Jetpack provider; and store proof privately with audit identifiers (no recipient emails in PR). A human review of these records must precede any actual KPI publication. No such adapter, proof or permission is represented as built.

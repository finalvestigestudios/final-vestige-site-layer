## Evidence-gated journey (not a send plan)

**Acquisition:** Grok-published, rights-cleared asset, immutable `asset_id`, publicly permitted destination and `utm_source=(youtube|tiktok|...)`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=<topic>_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<asset_id>` per TASK-008. Attribution lost across unclickable platform captions must be honestly labeled `unknown`, not attributed by guessing.

**Lead:** authentic visit to /focus/, optional free PDF download, *separate* Jetpack subscription request. Record exactly one `confirmed_signup` **only** when the provider has an authenticated eligible subscription state (not JavaScript `is-subscriber`, not `subscribe=success`, not a PDF hit). Pseudonymous keyed deduplication within a consented backend, not raw email, IP or name; restrict retention. Reading and confirmation do not certify delivery. No additional marketing-consent flag may be inferred from a general site follow.

**Nurture:** actual consent, valid receiving inbox status, welcome reception, ethical editorial value, relevant first-party article click. Conceptual audience clusters are only **on-site aggregate source/interest** for editorial planning until subscriber-controlled newsletter categories are explicitly approved and enabled. With WordPress category segmentation OFF, a "mind-interested cohort email" is **not implementable** through the documented current broadcast configuration.

**Purchase:** after source-of-truth approval, an email may contain one first-party verified independent warm-up guide CTA with disclosure **inside the issue and at the earning link on the guide**. Only merchant-confirmed paid order/customer, not email click, free lead or checkout start, is a sale. Require merchant payout/refund/fee report, exact SKU and date; never add unrelated consumer sale to Final Vestige.

## Attribution contract and tiny-sample safeguards

**Proposal only:** First-party email CTA link convention (not yet deployed): `utm_source=dispatch&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=dispatch_pilot_2026_11&utm_content=issue_0N` on the approved internal destination. The visitor's original Grok asset provenance must be preserved through consented analytics, while cross-device/cookie gaps remain **unknown**. Never append identifying emails or other PII. Do not append this provisional convention directly to an Inspire3 URL without merchant approval.

- Verified orders/customers = unique independent settled purchase evidence for the appropriate SKU and campaign.
- Net realized = collected attributable revenue/commission **minus actual** refunds, fees, chargebacks; if source reports incomplete, use **NOT_COMPUTABLE**, never a fake zero.
- North star = `1000 * realized_net / audited_qualified_human_sessions` by **7-day and 30-day cohorts**. Where denominator/settlements unavailable, display `NOT_COMPUTABLE`. If independently certified complete and zero purchases, report verified zero with very small-sample warning.
- Supporting funnel: legitimate source-attributed qualified human sessions → relevant landing visits → first-party CTA clicks → independent provider-confirmed eligible leads → merchant-confirmed paid orders → net. Do not imply deterministic person-to-person attribution without validated join/consent.
- With current Reader 3 / direct email 0 and incompatible dashboard categories, **no A/B claim or open-rate conclusion** is justified. No fake lift.

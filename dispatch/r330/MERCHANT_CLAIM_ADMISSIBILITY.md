## Buyer-first copy hierarchy for Issue #03 (not replacing original)

1. **Question:** can a twelve-minute practice fit a real person's schedule? Present as a *reader choice*, not a medical or productivity guarantee.
2. **Free control:** try a quiet twelve-minute period without purchasing, track time attempted, distractions and whether repeated practice is workable. A one-week diary cannot establish clinical efficacy.
3. **Seller SKU and limitations:** describe precisely what Premium includes only once the exact final linked SKU and policies have been verified. Do not imply independent lab testing that never occurred.
4. **One owned guide CTA, conditional:** the first-party independently sourced guide must be LIVE and owned by Mind #171; a non-existent link remains a literal HOLD marker.
5. **Separate list warning and disclosure:** near any qualifying recommendation state 'If you purchase through a link on the guide, Final Vestige may earn a commission.' Clearly distinguish any later merchant free-audio list enrollment.
6. **Uncoerced exit:** the free Fieldbook and investigations remain available without purchase, opting into a merchant email list or even subscribing to Dispatch.

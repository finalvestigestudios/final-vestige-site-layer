# R411: ONE-offer consent-to-customer progression (design only, no triggered mail)
**Owner:** Dispatch/Focus consent and attribution. **Offer:** Zen12 as a conditional commercial candidate, NOT an approved affiliate partnership. Mind/Commerce owns actual guide and earning link; Grok owns social media; Claude owns production analytics. No automated sequence, ESP, extra signup list, post or send is proposed.

## Explicitly separate editorial cadence from behavior-based delivery
Existing Jetpack supports manually selected new-post newsletter emails; **a behavior-triggered drip, segment routing and reader-level automations are not verified on this site.** Do not promise triggered messages, three-day sends or retargeting. Proposed six weekly editorial slots are all unscheduled and founder-controlled.

### 1. Source-tagged acquisition → one existing opt-in
Grok's approved asset ID may populate `utm_source=(youtube|tiktok)`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<approved immutable asset_id>` on a source-appropriate first-party landing route, **only after Claude checks URL, consent, cross-platform limitations, mobile behavior and existing analytics**. This does not establish a sale or qualified session by itself. Never add new scripts.

### 2. Jetpack provider-confirmed lead
A client-side `?subscribe=success`, `?subscribe=paid`, a download panel, or mere PDF click **does not count**. Define `confirmed_signup` only once per eligible provider subscription after private provider-confirmed timestamp, verified email delivery eligibility, unsubscribe/suppression reconciliation and deduplication. Keep addresses and raw identities out of GitHub; expose aggregate counts only. Current totals **3 total / 3 confirmed / 2 unconfirmed**, follower categories **3 WP.com / 0 direct-email** (historical read-only at 2026-11-17 00:31 UTC) do NOT establish the number of eligible inboxes.

### 3. Welcome / actual usefulness
Welcome message points to the 52-page Focus Fieldbook, but configured URL is not a proof it was delivered or the PDF opened. Actual authorized received-welcome witness and independent file-open QA remain separate proof gates. Avoid Issue 06 sending subscribers back through signup; prefer a distinct free follow-through activity such as the previously drafted *The Page That Changed* guide, pending founder copy acceptance.

### 4. Editorial value before a relevant commercial mention
Retain pilot allocation 01,02,04,06 editorial; 03 Zen12 and 05 Total Focus commercial-review pending. Subscriber-specific interest **cannot be inferred** from age, mental health or a download; only use voluntarily expressed preferences when supported and consented. Do not send commercial offers to all confirmed accounts by inference. One high-intent path is a reader already exploring a free twelve-minute practice and voluntarily opening the independent, vetted Zen12 buyer guide—**only after it is live and authorized**.

### 5. Monetization requires independent proof, not click metadata
For an approved first-party guide, proposed per-issue first-party CTA tags are `utm_source=dispatch&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=dispatch_pilot_2026_11&utm_content=issue03_guide`, but do NOT activate until privacy QA verifies the route and exact email link rendering. Treat `email_cta_click` as engagement, not purchase. Later `affiliate_outbound_click` is still not purchase. A paid order/customer requires independent seller settlement and refund/fee accounting, safely aggregated with merchant-authorized attribution. **No customer-level join based solely on URL or unconsented email identity.**

Report 7/30-day qualified-human sessions, verified orders, gross paid commission, refunds/fees, net realized USD, and net USD per 1,000 qualified-human sessions only where both numerator and denominator are actually independently evidenced. If either is missing, publish **NOT_COMPUTABLE** rather than guessed zero or A/B 'lift'. Tiny sample is not a causal test.

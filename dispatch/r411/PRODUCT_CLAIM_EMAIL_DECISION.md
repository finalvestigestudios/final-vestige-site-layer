## Zen12, Issue 03 — choose ONE eventual offer, not a general catalog
Official [Zen12 main page](https://zen12.com/) markets a 12-level, four-style, 48-MP3 audio course. It asserts that twelve minutes yields the benefit of an hour of meditation; that is **seller marketing**, not a product-specific verified scientific finding. Its [secret promotional route](https://zen12.com/secret/) advertises a coupon-specific $37 price from a crossed-out $87 and a countdown. This page also describes support and bonus items. Neither that price, its countdown nor bonuses establish the exact checkout, terms, timing or affiliate rights for Final Vestige. **Never imply a scarcity deadline exists or use a public coupon without written permission.**

Subscriber-relevant, defensible message: *A twelve-minute audio can be convenient, but duration does not establish depth, benefit equivalence or a medical outcome.* The potential guide should give sample availability, file access, refund and pricing review at the approved destination, buyer eligibility, free alternatives and disclosure. Avoid medical claims, testimonials as evidence, 'meditates for you', 'guaranteed relief' and 'scientifically proven' transfer from generalized meditation science to this SKU.

**Blocker:** no authenticated named publisher acceptance, Zen12-specific permitted EMAIL promotion, current approved email creatives, genuine earning URL, referred-checkout terms or published approved guide. The Mind Studio's [R431 draft #2802](https://github.com/finalvestigestudios/final-vestige-site-layer/pull/2802) is NOT live and is not a substitute.

## Hypnosis Live Total Focus, Issue 05
Official [Total Focus SKU](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus) describes a **33-minute / 47-MB** MP3, current public single-item price **$24.44**, struck **$29.95**, and a per-item **$19.95** price when bought as a two-item bundle. Official [FAQ](https://hypnosislive.com/faq) says recordings typically cost $22.95 and qualifies its 14-day refund offer with a maximum of **five refunded sessions in a year**. These price displays are separate contexts, not interchangeable or verified referred checkout terms.

The Total Focus sales page claims 'limitless powers', 'reprogram your brain' and '100% razor-sharp concentration'; those are **unverified seller efficacy claims**, not objective outcomes to repeat. For a rigorous email, describe the recording as a product under review rather than proof of improved concentration. No purchase/refund price promise in Issue 05.

**Blocker:** exact Total Focus SKU acceptance and EMAIL rights, issued earning URL and approved guide are missing. Public [Inspire3 affiliate materials](https://affiliates.inspire3.com/) or creative for other brands do not grant SKU/email rights to FV.

## Source hierarchy and copy-safety decisions
1. **Account-specific authoritative proof** for partner and email use, privately authenticated, product and domain scoped.
2. **Exact seller checkout and terms**, capture date/path and look for split-path inconsistencies; no inferred price.
3. **Independent evidence** for psychological/health efficacy before any endorsement.
4. **Separate disclosure** beside the recommendation and beside earning links on the approved guide.
5. **Native sender rendering** for the actual WordPress email, physical FV postal address and operational opt-out (never reuse Automattic or seller mailing address).

FTC [CAN-SPAM](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) applies to commercial email, including email to subscribers; it requires truthful subject/headers, an address and clear working opt-out, with proper suppression handling. WordPress [email preview instructions](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/email-appearance/) distinguish **Preview email** (no send) and **Send test email** (a real send). The connector currently does not expose the native editor preview; offline HTML is **not** proof.

Status: both commercial editions **HOLD_MERCHANT** and entire campaign **HOLD_UNSENT**. No referrals, email sends or tests authorized.

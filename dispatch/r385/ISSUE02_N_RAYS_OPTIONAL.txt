Subject: The prism was gone. They still saw the rays.
Proposed preheader: A 1904 experiment asks what happens when expectation becomes evidence.

In a French laboratory in 1904, researchers reported a faint spectrum of N-rays even after the prism supposedly needed to produce it had been removed. Rene Blondlot had announced the rays in 1903. Robert W. Wood's September 29, 1904 Nature letter described his investigation, against a background of unsuccessful independent replications.

The point is not that all observers were dishonest. It is that expectation may affect judgments of a weak signal, and an investigator's report should be read as a report rather than a recording.

## Reader hypothesis
A person arrives from a specifically documented Grok `asset_id` tied to a history-of-suggestion or attention investigation. They read sourced historical evidence rather than a broad "manifest abundance" pitch. Their next helpful action is the **existing** 52-page Fieldbook, without claiming that downloading or seeing its confirmation UI makes them an email subscriber. They can read all articles and take a free exercise without joining. Only if the provider confirms email subscription and delivery eligibility may a `confirmed_signup` be counted. The six-issue series itself stays a human-reviewed broadcast draft, not an automated drip.

## Attribution: events that may and may not count
- `utm_source` = actual platform; `utm_medium=organic_social`; `utm_campaign=cluster_pilot_2026_11`; `utm_content=Grok_immutable_asset_id` (only from actual Grok inventory; do not invent IDs).
- Landing view, page CTA and PDF click = observations, **not** a confirmed lead. A frontend `subscribe` URL query or `is-subscriber` CSS state is not confirmation.
- Provider-authoritative `confirmed_signup` = an eligible subscription transition, deduped by opaque provider id, without disclosing IDs or personal data publicly.
- Affiliate outbound click and merchant checkout start ≠ paid order. A merchant report with settled transaction/refund/fee details is mandatory for commission and net-revenue calculations.
- 7-/30-day cohort formula: net settled revenue / qualified unique human sessions × 1000. When either evidence basis or complete refund/fee ledger is absent, print **NOT_COMPUTABLE**; never fabricate 0 or claim statistical lift from a three-subscriber list.

# Dispatch R233: Exact offer source gap and consent risk (9 November 2026)
This is original, source-based editorial review. No newsletter is authorized or sent.

## What the actual six manuscripts reveal
Issue 03 (Zen12) cites general Zen12 pages and NCCIH background but **does not cite the specific audited Zen12 sales page** https://zen12.com/hnp. Issue 05 (Total Focus) cites NCCIH and the existing Focus Fieldbook but **does not cite the product's official seller listing** https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus. Neither source gap alone establishes that the underlying copy is false, but it prevents a finished seller-claim alignment check. These were found against the current R232 parent manuscript files, not guessed from the original October 30 edition.

### Official seller page: Zen12
The official seller page describes 12 progressive levels, four listening styles per level and 48 MP3 recordings in the Premium package. It claims 12-minute sessions can supply an hour of advanced-meditation benefit and describes stress, anxiety and health improvements. These are SELLER REPRESENTATIONS, not independently demonstrated product-specific results. The seller advertises a one-year guarantee. Checkout eligibility, price, eligibility for a refund and actual fulfillment are not authenticated by reading the page. The optional seller newsletter opt-in is separate from the Final Vestige subscriber consent.

### Official seller page: Hypnosis Live Total Focus
The product-specific listing describes a 33-minute, 47-MB downloadable audio recording. Seller assertions that it reprograms the brain or achieves perfect concentration are not FV evidence. Its FAQ says 14 days to request a refund with a maximum of five sessions refunded per year. Do not repeat an unconditional risk-free claim without the qualifier. Displayed prices and cached snippets differ; a genuine cart total and date-specific purchase terms have not been authenticated. No fixed price belongs in the draft.

### Different evidence categories must stay separate
1. A merchant's public sales page supports what the merchant advertises, not whether the efficacy claim is true.
2. A seller's marketing email in a connected inbox (observed read-only on November 9) is *not* an approved affiliate swipe file or FV-specific permission to market via email. No inbox message text, addresses, unsubscribe tokens or tracking IDs are stored in this repository.
3. An affiliate-dashboard-issued URL plus named approval must be independently obtained. General affiliate program availability is not individual acceptance.
4. A merchant's own London postal address is not Final Vestige's required publisher postal address.
5. A Fieldbook download display or subscription query parameter is not a provider-verified confirmed subscription. A click is not a paid order.

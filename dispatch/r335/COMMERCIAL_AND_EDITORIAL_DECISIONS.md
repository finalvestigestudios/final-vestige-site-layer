## Official offer alignment and reason to block live promotion

**Zen12 Premium.** Seller describes 12 levels and 48 MP3s in multiple listening styles. Seller claims 12 minutes provides the benefit of one hour of meditation; that claim is not independently established for this offer. The free Zen12 audio landing form says the customer receives the *merchant's* newsletter, not Final Vestige Dispatch. Special coupon pages, countdowns, guaranteed results and changing checkout prices are not reliable email claims. Review exact seller description https://zen12.com/about and free gift https://zen12.com/welcome .

**Hypnosis Live — Total Focus.** Official product page https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus identifies a 33-minute / 47 MB MP3, includes strong claims about concentration and brain reprogramming, and states a 14-day satisfaction/refund window. Price displays have varied across retrievals; never quote a price or percent off without fresh checkout and approved account/offer evidence. Scientific support for hypnosis in general cannot establish this recording's marketing claims.

## Non-sending subscriber-to-customer hypothesis

One eligible, independently provider-confirmed Dispatch reader → independently witnessed 52-page Fieldbook welcome → one source-grounded editorial edition → an optional independently reviewed first-party guide → a plainly disclosed merchant offer *only after account and email permission* → optional buyer click → independently settled commission/order evidence minus refunds, fees and clawbacks. No email personalization based on open pixels, no additional sends, no forced signup before guide access, no merchant-newsletter confusion.

CTA attribution after Claude approves existing infrastructure: campaign=dispatch_pilot_2026_11, issue_id=01..06, cta_id=primary, no subscriber ID, email hash, IP or personal tracking token. Preserve real Grok asset_id / platform UTMs from TASK-008 only when verified. First-party CTA click is a diagnostic, NOT paid revenue.

7-day and 30-day cohorts require separately validated qualified-human sessions, actual paid orders and realized net revenue. If numerator, denominator or independent attribution is unavailable, state NOT COMPUTABLE with tiny-list caution, never zero/estimate.

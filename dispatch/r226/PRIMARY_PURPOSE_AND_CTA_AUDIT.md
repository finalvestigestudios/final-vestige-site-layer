## NEW LEGAL-RELEASE CORRECTION — 4:2 is editorial, NOT statutory
The founder's four-editorial/two-promotional campaign allocation is editorial planning, **not** a CAN-SPAM exemption classification. The FTC defines commercial content to include promotion of products and material on commercially operated websites. Under **16 CFR §316.3(a)(3)**, messages mixing editorial content with commercial promotion turn on a *reasonable recipient's interpretation* of the subject and the message body, including the placement, prominence and relative share of advertising. **Merely signing up** does not make a weekly newsletter a narrowly defined transactional/relationship message. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/316.3 ; regulator: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business .

Do not automatically label the four editorial letters legally exempt.

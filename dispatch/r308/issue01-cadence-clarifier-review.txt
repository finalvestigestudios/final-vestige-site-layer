Subject: The Dispatch doesn't arrive all at once
Preheader: A source to examine now, and an honest note about what follows.

A witness can describe an experience with absolute confidence. The surviving
record may still leave the explanation open.

That distinction runs through our Puységur archive, where original accounts of
unusual behavior sit alongside questions the documents cannot settle. Read the
testimony, notice where observation ends and interpretation begins, and carry
that habit into any modern claim about attention or suggestion.

The Dispatch will bring you new investigations and useful evidence-led finds
when we publish them. This isn't an automated six-lesson course; readers who
join later may not receive earlier editions by email. The free Focus Fieldbook
remains a separate welcome resource, subject to your email confirmation.

One thing to try before our next publication: write down one observation and
one explanation for something surprising you saw. Are they the same sentence?

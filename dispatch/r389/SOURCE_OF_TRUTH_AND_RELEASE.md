## 1. Material correction: totals are not a mathematical contradiction
The current authenticated WordPress/Jetpack newsletter status shows **total=3, confirmed=3, unconfirmed=2**, suppressed=0, paid=0, imported-last-24-hours=0. Earlier audits treated total/confirmed/unconfirmed as inconsistent or anomalous. **WordPress's official guide says "Not confirmed" records are NOT counted in the total subscriber count.** Thus `3 total, 3 confirmed, 2 unconfirmed` can be semantically normal: THREE counted subscriptions plus TWO still-unconfirmed records. These figures do NOT establish five separate people, nor an actual email-recipient audience of three. The earlier baseline 2/2/2 can also be explainable for this reason.

Official source (last reviewed 2026-11-04): https://wordpress.com/support/subscribers/. Cross-account WordPress.com subscribers can be Reader-only, email-subscribed, paused, bounced, or not sending. The separately reported WordPress.com follower count=3 and direct-email follower count=0 are a different split from the API's confirmation status; do not equate them with actual inboxes. Provider eligibility requires the **Email subscription = Subscribed** filtered record/count. Do not publish/export addresses or create a duplicate signup list. Treat receipt as UNKNOWN until that provider filter is privately and authoritatively checked.

**Decision:** `COUNT_ARITHMETIC_CONFLICT = NOT PROVEN`; `ACTUAL_EMAIL_ELIGIBLE = UNKNOWN`. A change from 2 to 3 reported totals is not by itself proof of a new permissioned email-eligible subscriber or an attributable Grok conversion. The current connector returns diagnostic aggregates but no actual subscriber-status list. Do not assert per-person eligibility.

## 2. The double-opt-in and Fieldbook delivery nuance
Official WordPress guidance says addresses without WordPress.com accounts receive a confirmation request before a welcome, while signed-in WordPress.com users can skip the email-confirmation step and receive a welcome immediately: https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter-settings/. Therefore a universal `email_confirmation_clicked=true` requirement would incorrectly exclude some legitimately subscribed WordPress.com accounts. A canonical `confirmed_signup_v1` must mean provider-authoritative confirmed/eligible subscription, with provenance and deduplication, not merely the appearance of a confirmation link in Gmail.

### Why the new executable gate matters
R429 documented the attribution plan but did not independently execute a complete **cohort-to-paid-settlement falsification harness**. R430 adds a **pure offline** computation that only returns a revenue KPI when audited human sessions, campaign join, complete merchant reports, paid settlement/referral and refund/fee ledger are all explicitly attested. Conflicting duplicate sessions/orders, PII, client-only signups, opted-out leads, bot sessions, missing campaign joins and unverifiable/unmatched purchases are rejected or reported as uncomputable. It produces **synthetic** figures only in tests, never reports them as Final Vestige sales. Matching verified owned gross receipts and affiliate commissions are separate `revenue_type` values; settlement adjustments are not inferred from clicks.

**7/30-day rule:** `1000 × net realized attributed dollars / independently audited qualified-human sessions`. Counts and money unavailable → `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, not a displayed `$0`. A verified complete report with confirmed zero orders can yield measured zero, but an empty connector result is NOT a verified zero.

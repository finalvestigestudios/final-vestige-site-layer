## Journey and data contract

1. **Grok asset → qualified visit.** Grok/Praxxis supply actual unique asset_id and URL. Use utm_source=youtube or tiktok, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=perception_pilot_2026_11, utm_content=actual_asset_id. Claude validates sessions and crawler/QA exclusions; views are not human sessions. No extra analytics scripts.
2. **Visit → Focus.** Direct PDF click is a content interaction, not marketing consent. A form submit is signup_attempt, not a confirmed lead. Preserve one existing Jetpack list, no imports.
3. **Focus → confirmed_signup.** Only the provider's authoritative confirmed subscription state AND consent record can cause one deduplicated confirmed_signup. Canonical event key belongs privately in the provider/backend; analytics never stores email, name, phone or public subscriber IDs. Without a safe provider event feed, report UNKNOWN rather than guessing 3 from overlapping counts.
4. **Confirmation → welcome.** “Configured” does not prove “delivered.” Existing Oct 25 Gmail subscription confirmation is NOT the 52-page Fieldbook welcome. Verify receipt only via founder-authorized recipient-owned test; never send or subscribe an address in this sprint.
5. **Dispatch → one relevant destination.** Existing 01/02 repeat Puységur; 03/05 guides missing; 04 Apollo archive is coherent but subject vague; 06 directs a subscriber to acquisition-focused /focus/ again. Drafted alternatives elsewhere are not adopted. Single issue-specific CTA can use utm_source=dispatch, utm_medium=email, utm_campaign=dispatch_pilot_2026, utm_content=issue_XX_topic; no fabricated affiliate URL or premature UTM launch.
6. **Merchant confirmed sale → net revenue.** A CTA click, affiliate outbound, checkout start or confirmed lead is not paid revenue. Independently settled orders/affiliate commission, minus refunds/fees/chargebacks, is the numerator. Qualified human sessions is denominator; if either missing, net revenue per 1,000 is NOT COMPUTABLE. Maintain distinct 7-/30-day cohorts; no A/B in this tiny population.

Attribution note: an email click is a new channel touch. Do not falsely restore original Grok asset credit after a long email gap without lawful, privacy-appropriate first-touch retention. Email provider attribution must not overwrite Grok origin; session model remains Claude-owned.

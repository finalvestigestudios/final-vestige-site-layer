# Jetpack preview, postal footer, opt-out and welcome protocol — no sends authorized
**Current capability**: WordPress may send post notifications when explicitly enabled even if \`email_new_posts_by_default=false\`. The same WordPress documentation explains the **Preview email** button works when per-post email sending is OFF, while **Send test email** actually transmits mail. Therefore the presence of a preview feature is *not* authorization to invoke the test-email function or to publish a post. Sources: https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/email-appearance/ and https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/send-newsletter-emails/.

1. **Read-only now.** Re-check site settings, provider count buckets, founder's chosen email manuscript/subject and source-labeled launch owner. Collect only aggregate counts and no subscriber emails. A confirmed subscriber aggregate is not the same as a deliverable recipient census.
2. **Native on-screen preview, only if access permits with no writes or sends.** The owner may inspect a separately held existing WordPress draft whose per-post email toggle is confirmed OFF and click **Preview email** (NOT **Send test email**, Publish, Schedule or Send). Verify rendered mobile/tablet/desktop body, heading hierarchy, link destinations, subject derived from post title, disclosure beside a future approved offer, dark-mode contrast and plain text where supported. Record screenshot evidence and exact draft identity in private approved QA storage; not recipient details.
3. **No manufactured preheader.** The proposed preheader field in the source markdown is editorial metadata; no distinct native Jetpack preheader control has been independently verified. WordPress documents that notification subjects derive from post titles and that sender display name can change but email sender address is \`donotreply@wordpress.com\`. Do not promise exact preheader inbox display. Source: https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/email-appearance/.
4. **Postal address.** Verify Final Vestige's own valid physical mailing address with the founder. Do NOT substitute Automattic, a merchant's UK address, a founder home address inferred from unrelated records, or a placeholder in production. FTC allows a valid registered US PO box or qualifying registered private mailbox. No specific address has been verified or included here. Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business.
5. **Unsubscribe and actual receipt.** Require a founder-explicitly authorized controlled real message later to a designated test mailbox. Inspect the actual MIME footer and click a recipient-specific unsubscribe only if authorized; verify the opt-out outcome in Jetpack provider state. Do not send to current subscribers or make a fresh subscriber now. Confirmation request received in Gmail on Oct 25 is *not* final welcome/Fieldbook delivery; do not conflate it.
6. **Manual owner gate.** Only after verified consent, subscriber eligibility, approved copy and affiliate permission (if a commercial issue), native received-email QA, provider-compliant footers and explicit founder final audience/date approval may sending be separately considered. This PR always stays no-send; tools never perform that action.

Before you buy a focus recording, ask this

In 1784, a French commission that included Benjamin Franklin investigated animal magnetism. People reported genuine sensations; the commissioners asked whether an invisible magnetic fluid was necessary to explain them.

That historical record is a reminder that an experience and its explanation are different kinds of evidence. The commission did not test modern hypnosis.

Hypnosis Live sells Total Focus, described on its official page as a 33-minute, 47-MB MP3. Its promises about sharper focus and productivity are seller claims, not independently proven outcomes for this recording. A scientific study of hypnosis and attentional bias cannot be treated as product-specific validation.

Try something free first: For three comparable sessions, record the task you planned, the minutes actually spent on it, and interruptions. Compare your observations, without assuming your log establishes cause and effect.

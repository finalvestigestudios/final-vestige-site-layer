SUBJECT PROPOSAL: Before you buy a focus recording, ask this
PREHEADER PROPOSAL (JETPACK MAPPING UNVERIFIED): Franklin's 1784 investigation, a modern recording, and five questions.

In 1784, investigators including Benjamin Franklin studied reports of animal magnetism. They asked whether real sensations supported the proposed invisible magnetic fluid, and altered what subjects knew about a supposed treatment. Their inquiry challenged that explanation. It was not a test of modern hypnosis.

Today's question is smaller: should you buy a recording promising sharper concentration? Hypnosis Live lists Total Focus as a 33-minute, 47-MB MP3. Its strong focus and productivity promises are seller claims, not independently verified results for that particular file. A modern study of hypnosis and attentional bias does not validate a named commercial recording.

Start free: Choose a task, record intended and actual focus minutes, and count interruptions. Repeat on three ordinary days. A diary is not a randomized experiment, but at least it provides observations to compare.

FIVE QUESTIONS BEFORE BUYING
1. What file or access do I receive?
2. What independent evidence applies to this exact recording?
3. What are its specific checkout refund conditions? The seller FAQ describes a fourteen-day refund request window, maximum five refunded sessions per year; check your applicable terms.
4. Could I try a free attention routine first?
5. Will the publisher earn a commission if I buy?

## Material correction to R294

R294 `signupCandidate()` in `dispatch/r294/release_contract.cjs` requires `doubleOptIn === true` for **every** provider record. This is incorrect for people who already have verified WordPress.com accounts: WordPress's [newsletter settings guide](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter-settings/) says these subscribers **skip** the confirmation-email step and receive the welcome immediately. Non-WordPress account subscribers normally receive a confirmation email first. The new `subscription_evidence.cjs` supports **either authenticated confirmation pathway** while insisting on a provider-verified **email subscription = Subscribed** transition. It never infers a signup from a click, welcome configuration, success-page flag, PDF download, stats counter, Reader follow, or an unverified WordPress.com account.

WordPress's [subscriber status guide](https://wordpress.com/support/subscribers/) distinguishes **Not subscribed** (Reader only), **Not confirmed**, **Bounced**, **Emails paused** and **Not sending**. Its November 4, 2026 review explicitly says filter the Newsletter Subscribers tab by **Email Subscription** when counts disagree. WordPress's [troubleshooting guide](https://wordpress.com/support/troubleshooting-not-receiving-notification-emails/) adds recipient notification preference/frequency; a daily/weekly setting does not establish an instant welcome or send failure.

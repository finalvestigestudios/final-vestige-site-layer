## One existing-list, no-spam progression hypothesis

A verified Grok historical/perception asset with known `asset_id` and approved `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content=asset_id` → relevant historical investigation → existing Focus Fieldbook or Dispatch entrypoint → **provider-confirmed** signup (exactly once per subscription; existing WP.com verified subscriber may not need a fresh link click) → welcome / free workbook *if actually received* → useful editorial issue → ONE optional, clearly disclosed, independently vetted Zen12 guide *only after partner EMAIL approval* → merchant-confirmed **settled** sale. No presumed open-rate winner, uplift, click-as-sale or segmentation based on personal-sensitive traits.

For the reporting cohorts, use the formula:

`net_revenue_per_1000 = 1000 * (settled gross paid revenue - refunds - fees) / verified qualified human sessions`

Return **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0, if any denominator, independent settled order confirmation, refund/fee record, attribution window or qualified source cohort is missing. A negative realized net outcome is allowed when fees exceed net proceeds. Always flag tiny samples; do not publish private customer identifiers.

A twelve-minute promise, a much older question

William James on attention in 1890; a modern audio promise under examination.

In 1890, William James described attention as an act of selection. He also noticed something familiar: knowing what to do and still struggling to begin.

A short session sounds tempting. Zen12’s seller describes 12-minute audio, 12 levels and four formats per level. It also asserts one short session can deliver an hour of ordinary meditation. This is unverified marketing language, not a Final Vestige finding.

Before you spend, try twelve quiet minutes without notifications for three days. Record whether you began, noticed distraction, returned and would repeat tomorrow. This cannot prove health benefits or compare products; it can help you learn whether the routine is workable.

Before purchasing, inspect the exact package, access terms, claims, relevant refund policy and checkout. Coupons and old price snapshots do not establish an approved referral price.

The more useful question is whether what we tried, what we measured and what was promised are actually the same.

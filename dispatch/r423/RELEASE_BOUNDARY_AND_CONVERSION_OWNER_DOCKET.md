## What an approved experiment would actually mean
Target one high-intent audience already reading an attention/perception investigation. When a creator's existing *published* asset_id and literal hook can be independently matched, use the approved Grok UTM to the matching, already live investigation, with Focus as an optional free gift. No subscriber gate for a freely accessible buyer guide. A **commercial** email could be considered only for someone the provider establishes as individually email-eligible and not opted-out, within applicable consent and mailing law, after founder separately approves the content, recipients, dates and legally valid footer. A user who receives the Fieldbook is not necessarily a customer, and a click-through is not an order.

Event semantics: \`confirmed_signup\` is emitted once per provider-confirmed subscriber identity and consent scope at confirmation time, with a privacy-preserving internal key. Return visits, page-success parameters and downloads do not re-emit it. Track \`utm_source\`, \`utm_medium\`, \`utm_campaign\`, immutable \`asset_id\` and content/issue when grounded, and retain a non-PII attribution identifier that can be joined to an **independently** reported paid order or merchant commission. No click or view counts may substitute for paid settlements. A declared \`net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions\` requires audited denominator and net paid revenue (gross less refunds, commissions reversals and attributable fees); with either missing, report \`NOT_COMPUTABLE\` rather than zero.

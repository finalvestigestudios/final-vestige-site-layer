# R423 merchant and inbox source adjudication
**Decision:** commercial issues 03/05 remain gated. Public seller pages and promotional email received as a customer do **not** confer an affiliate license or permission to mail the Final Vestige list.

## Zen12 — source-specific claim boundaries
Official product route https://zen12.com/hnp (rechecked 2026-11-17) describes twelve-minute MP3 sessions, twelve levels, four listening styles (48 recordings for the complete package), and multiple product tiers. The same page presents headline claims equating one twelve-minute session to an hour of meditation, as well as health, stress, focus, creativity and brainwave claims. Those are **seller assertions**, not independently verified efficacy of Zen12. Do not imply that the regular twelve-minute Fieldbook routine is proven to confer the same effects. The public page shows a beginner/advanced $67.77 and premium $87.99 **at the page view**, while other seller-controlled funnels differ. Neither the final referral checkout nor coupon applicability is established; do not quote a price in subscriber email. A seller's one-year guarantee is not a reviewed, binding term of an unknown actual referred purchase.

Best ethically persuasive bridge: "A consistent twelve-minute pause can be observed and logged before choosing any recording. If you're considering a paid audio tool, first ask what the audio contains, what is established by independent studies, and which concrete terms apply to the checkout you actually see." Restrict commercial Issue 03 to **one future vetted on-site buyer-guide CTA**, only after Mind approves the published guide. Proposed near-offer disclosure: "If you buy through a link on the linked guide, Final Vestige may earn a commission." Do not embed a direct merchant URL pending rights.

## Hypnosis Live Total Focus — claim and returns distinctions
Official catalog https://hypnosislive.com/catalog identifies *Total Focus* among mental-skills sessions; the official FAQ https://hypnosislive.com/faq describes MP3 sessions and states most recordings last around forty minutes. Previous product-specific page inspections observed a 33-minute Total Focus recording, which must be rechecked at the exact seller SKU and referred checkout before quoting. Public FAQ states 14-day refunds **limited to five sessions per year**, with processing/statement timings; avoid describing the policy as unconditional or promising effects. The seller's general claims about NLP, hypnosis or concentration are **not product-specific independent trials**. Do not confuse this merchant with https://www.hypnosisdownloads.com/ (a different vendor). Issue 05 remains a reserved **separate** commercial slot, not an alternate route to the Zen12 checkout.

## Partner-channel permission
The public affiliate portal https://affiliates.inspire3.com/ proves only that a partner program is advertised. Searching the connected inbox for Inspire3/Zen12/Hypnosis Live partner terms yielded general merchant marketing and consumer promotional messages, not an independently authenticated permission letter naming *Final Vestige Studios*, EMAIL channel, specific SKUs, issued tracking/referral link, requirements or termination/creative restrictions in the reviewed results. This is not proof no such letter exists elsewhere. **All corresponding proofs remain UNVERIFIED.**

Minimum *private* Mind/Commerce attestation: program owner; partner account acceptance document; dated allowed traffic channels including EMAIL and restrictions; eligible product/SKU; link issued to Final Vestige; published first-party buyer guide; referred checkout and current price/returns independently captured; compatible copy/creative usage and geographic conditions. Summaries/opaque receipt IDs only in public issues. Avoid publicizing private affiliate tokens or credentials.

## Inbox legal boundary
WordPress currently has sender display "Final Vestige Studios," but that does not verify the RFC5322 From, visible subject, actual preview text, rendered postal address or working unsubscribe. Reply-to is **no-reply**, so neither a reply invitation nor Automattic's confirmation footer satisfies the publisher's opt-out duty. The FTC requires accurate sender/subject, conspicuous commercial disclosure, a valid sender physical postal address, and a usable opt-out in commercial messages. WordPress's 2026 support page documents non-sending **Preview email** as distinct from **Send test email**; neither has yet been witnessed for this campaign. The generic external HTML/TXT samples are NOT Jetpack MIME.

**Release:** no send; no test email; no commercial URL; do not use customer marketing copy as licensed affiliate creative. Written offer rights and actual mail receipt are separate gates.

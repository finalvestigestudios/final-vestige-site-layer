## Copy decision / honest offer objection map

| Buyer objection | Honest useful answer | Ineligible copy |
|---|---|---|
| Does a 12-minute routine need a paid product? | No: a free timed session is possible. Evaluate the *audio features* separately. | "12 minutes equals an hour" as a proven outcome |
| What am I buying? | Seller-listed 48 Premium recordings / 12 levels for Zen12; a single 33-minute MP3 for Total Focus. | Unlimited, clinically verified or personalized benefits |
| Is there evidence of my outcome? | Product-specific credible evidence not established; individual experiences can differ. | "Scientifically proven for this recording" |
| What does it cost / can I return it? | Show verified cart + terms on approved guide, timestamp review; no prices in draft email. | Timers, manufactured savings or absolute refunds |
| Will FV be compensated? | Disclose next to any *actual* qualifying link if approved; affiliate acceptance and EMAIL rights unknown. | Hidden general footer-only disclosure |

Safe framing: "The merchant says the session lasts twelve minutes; outcomes are not independently proven for this product." The NIH review of meditation cannot validate a proprietary audio outcome: https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/meditation-and-mindfulness-effectiveness-and-safety .
- **Zen12, deliverables:** Premium seller advertises 12 levels, 48 audio MP3s and four formats; exact tier selections and receipt at cart untested. Safe framing: "Seller-advertised contents, not a guarantee of package received or benefit."
- **Hypnosis Live Total Focus, seller:** 33-minute, 47-MB download; brain reprogramming, 100% concentration, productivity claims. Safe framing: "The listing describes a 33-minute downloadable recording; specific concentration gains remain unverified."
- **Hypnosis Live refund:** Seller FAQ advertises a 14-day refund, limited to five refunded sessions a year; checkout/refund execution untested. Avoid "risk-free" and "guaranteed."

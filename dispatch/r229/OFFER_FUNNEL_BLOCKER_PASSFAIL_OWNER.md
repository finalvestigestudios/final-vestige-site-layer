**Attribution contract:** acquisition `asset_id` / `utm_campaign` → qualified first-party session → relevant single CTA → provider-authenticated `confirmed_signup` (if needed) → verified outbound product click → independently reported settled paid affiliate order → refunds + fees → realized net. Unlinked stages stay UNKNOWN and are not retrofitted from coincidence. Deduplicate confirmed transitions by an opaque internal transition ID, never by storing a subscriber address in analytics or GitHub.

**No new platforms or scripts:** This is a measurement contract for existing infrastructure, not a second analytics tag or new subscription provider. All conditional offers remain disabled until verified and signed off.

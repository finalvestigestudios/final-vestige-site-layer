# R373 · One-offer subscriber-to-customer progression (NOT a mailing sequence)
Commercial candidate: Zen12 buyer guide, held pending named merchant approval. This is a hypothesis, not a running funnel or approval to send.

**High-intent reader:** A reader who has independently chosen to use the free Fieldbook and voluntarily wants to evaluate paid guided audio. Do not classify mental-health status, generate sensitive inferred segments, scrape inboxes, or track without proper consent.

**Stage A — Evidence:** Existing editorial Issue 01/02, historical-source CTA. At most the founder-approved weekly issue and only actual confirmed email-eligible recipients. No extra welcome email.
**Stage B — Utility:** Existing free Fieldbook/evidence log; do not prompt an existing subscriber to join the same list again. One substantive free editorial CTA.
**Stage C — Optional buyer research:** Existing original Issue 03 is one of only TWO reserved commercial weeks. If Mind proves live guide, SKU-specific EMAIL authorization and legal render, feature ONE independently reviewed guide CTA with adjacent commission disclosure. The merchant earning link belongs on the approved guide, not in email without rights.
**Stage D — Settlement:** Only independently reported paid orders and commissions (net of refunds/fees), audited against unique qualified human sessions and subject to attribution limitations. Reading, clicking, downloading and social views are not purchases.

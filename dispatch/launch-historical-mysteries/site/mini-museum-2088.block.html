THE REASON TO CARE

The past is more than an image.

We spend a lot of time looking at history through glass: ancient bones in a display case, maps reproduced on screens, the hardware of space exploration photographed from across a room.

A physical specimen changes the experience. You can return to it, ask where it came from and use its documentation to explore a bigger story. That doesn't make every object priceless or every origin story certain. It makes provenance part of the appeal.

Over the last seven letters, we've followed real historical records. Today, the evidence is fictional—but the decisions belong to you.

Cold Case Inc makes browser-based detective mysteries in which you examine a fictional police file, read statements, review interviews and footage, and decide who did what. The game supplies clues; it does not simply hand you a story to watch.

The best part is that you don't need to purchase a full case just to find out whether you enjoy the format.

The company offers a free introductory investigation called Flat 33. It takes roughly 10–12 minutes according to the publisher. You may need an email sign-in link to save progress, but the company says its marketing newsletter is optional. No payment is required for that short case.

If that is your kind of evening, its paid cases offer longer investigations. Compare the exact difficulty, expected play time, current price and terms for the case you choose. Some are more challenging than others, and the fictional scenarios can involve crime and sensitive themes.

We've put together a Final Vestige guide showing the free option, what paid cases offer and who should probably skip them. You can also return to our real Apollo 1 source archive if you prefer historical evidence to a fictional puzzle.

No grand promises. No fake mystery about an exclusive secret. Just an opportunity to see whether you can make the clues add up.

## Research claims and limits
- **Rensink/O'Regan/Clark 1997 — https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1997.tb00427.x.** Blank-separated alternating views made repeated scene changes harder to detect; location/object cueing improved detection. The paper does **not** establish that all memory is false or a general performance-enhancement product works.
- **Simons/Chabris 1999 — https://doi.org/10.1068/p281059.** Task-directed attention in dynamic events influenced detection of unexpected objects; **inattentional blindness is distinct from change blindness**. Never substitute a fabricated observation statistic or imply all people missed the same object.

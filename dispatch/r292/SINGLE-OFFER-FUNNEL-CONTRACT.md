# Dispatch R292 — one-offer non-sending subscriber-to-customer sequence

**Business objective:** realized, evidenced net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions, not newsletter volume or proxy clicks. This is an operator design, **not a live automation or mailing schedule**.

## Audience and offer selection

One high-intent audience hypothesis: adults arriving from Grok-controlled historical suggestion / perception videos, reading FV's sourced Puységur or attention content, and choosing the free Focus Fieldbook. Their actual purchase interest is **unknown until a voluntary action and source data support it**. The proposed commercial offer is a **specific Zen12 full-program buyer guide** *only after* Mind verifies publisher rights and the guide is live. Existing Final Vestige sellable digital products may take precedence if rights, price, delivery and independent settled-order reporting are validated; no owned SKU is assumed currently verified.

**Segmentation without a new list:** visitor/PDF viewer (not a lead), pending opt-in (not eligible), provider-confirmed recipient (potential eligible; suppression check required), voluntary topic-relevant guide click (preference hypothesis, not a buyer), merchant-confirmed paid purchase (commercial conversion). No subscriber-level email tracking IDs, no inferred health condition, no hidden cross-channel profile stitching. No new drip sender—Jetpack's present behavior is post notification; the weekly six issues stay draft.

## Only one confirmed signup event

`confirmed_signup` is an *attribution convention*, not existing deployed code. The provider must report an authenticated confirmed-and-current subscription with no suppression or unsubscribe; server-only opaque stable identifier ensures one event per confirmation lifecycle. Repeated form submissions, `/focus/?subscribe=unknown_state`, landing-page gift display, confirmation-request email, document access, and WordPress Reader followers are **not** evidence. Do not emit on these triggers. Separate current follower count 3 Reader / 0 email vs diagnostic 3 confirmed + 2 unconfirmed requests; no additive math, no public recipient list.

## Traffic and email UTM (proposed, not deployed)

Grok owns source assets and channel mix. Its agreed proposed convention: `utm_source=youtube|tiktok|...`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=<topic>_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<immutable_asset_id>`. Resolve `mind_pilot_2026_11` vs `mind_proposed_2026_11` discrepancy before deployment. Dispatch would use only privacy-safe aggregate `utm_source=dispatch&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=dispatch_pilot_2026_11&utm_content=issue03_zen12` on first-party vetted guide if approved. No subscriber email in URLs, no extra analytics scripts, no fabricated asset IDs, no assumed referrer recovery from platform bio links.

For each 7/30-day cohort: qualified human sessions → first-party landing → actual relevant CTA click → provider confirmed lead (if applicable) → merchant accepted attributed purchase → settled net commission/owned net order after refunds and fees. Attribution can be missing and must be stated. `net_per_1000=1000*(verified_realized_net_revenue)/(verified_qualified_human_sessions)` only when BOTH reliable inputs exist. Never confuse clicks, checkout starts, open rates or list followers with purchases. With a tiny list, no meaningful A/B efficacy claims.

Treat a presell CTA that promotes a paid offer as potentially commercial by *primary purpose*, even if no direct merchant earning URL is present in the email.

## Boundary decisions
1. No claim "Zen12 is as effective as an hour of meditation" in FV editorial voice; seller only, independently unproven.
2. No mental-health, wealth, memory or productivity improvement guarantee.
3. No affiliate price/refund promise derived from a different checkout or generic offer.
4. No fake scarcity, borrowed consumer testimonials, or unconsented targeting.
5. No commercial recommendation until **the specific account's email channel** and link rights are attested; a generic affiliate application or consumer mailing is not enough.
6. No unapproved guide activation; Mind & Affiliate Studio owns actual eligibility and warm-up pages.
7. No assumption that a no-reply sender, WordPress preview, an offline HTML board or a confirmation message establishes real received newsletter compliance.
8. Save only counts and source references in GitHub; no subscriber emails, inbox bodies, session identifiers or merchant credentials.

## Corrected priority
A defensible email conversion test requires one selected, merchant-approved offer, verified human reader segment, compliant received email and independently reconciled paid orders. Until then, more email assets are not commercial progress. All six remain HOLD_UNSENT.

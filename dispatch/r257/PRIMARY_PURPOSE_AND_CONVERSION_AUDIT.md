## New material finding: four editorial emails are not automatically noncommercial under CAN-SPAM
The canonical campaign calls Issues **01, 02, 04 and 06 editorial**, and Issues **03 and 05 commercial-review-pending**. That 4:2 split correctly allocates storytelling and affiliate coverage; **it is not a legal classification of each message's primary purpose**. The FTC explicitly notes that messages promoting content on websites operated for commercial purposes can contain commercial content. The controlling question is the particular message's primary purpose, interpreted from its subject and body, rather than whether our editorial spreadsheet says “editorial.” It would be an error to release four issues without confirming an appropriate postal address and unsubscribing path just because they contain no vendor link. The conservative preflight requires the actual **Final Vestige Studios** postal address, honest sender and subject, a visible unsubscribe route and its working behavior **for all six issues** until a competent message-specific review establishes otherwise. This is a compliance QA position, not a legal opinion that every issue necessarily qualifies as a commercial message.

Second finding: current Jetpack `reply_to=no-reply` is compatible with ordinary publishing but **cannot substitute for an actual functioning opt-out channel**. A footer shown in a local HTML specimen is not evidence that Jetpack generates the same footer when delivered; the 2026-10-25 subscription *confirmation* is not the promised post-confirmation **welcome** message. The vendor's and Automattic's mailing addresses are not FV's. Do not fabricate an address or manufacture an unsubscribe href.

FTC guidance reviewed 2026-11-10: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business (commercial primary purpose; sender; subject; postal address; opt-out; 10 business days). FTC disclosure guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking. A simple effective web unsubscribe or valid reply method is sufficient as applicable; **one-click is not independently required by the FTC guidance**. Release must verify the actual selected method.

## Material conversion QA finding
R318's useful proof model accepts a single upstream claim labeled `confirmed_signup` if it names an active Jetpack record. That upstream claim **must be generated from an actual NEW confirmed subscription event**, not from merely discovering an already-confirmed subscriber in today's database. Otherwise a fresh 7-day cohort could absorb legacy subscribers as new leads. R319 supplies a separate proof-transition classifier, with deterministic tests, demanding (1) provider-authenticated UNCONFIRMED observation, (2) subsequent ACTIVE observation for the same opaque subscriber reference, (3) unique confirmation event, (4) consent to Dispatch, (5) email delivery eligibility, (6) no duplicate counting and (7) cohort selection by the confirmation timestamp. First-seen ACTIVE rows stay uncounted and are reported as incomplete history. Raw signup attempts, PDF access, Reader follows and URL parameters do not count. The source strings are **not themselves proof of authentication**: Claude/Focus must implement this behind a real Jetpack provider read and preserve private evidence securely. No live subscriber addresses are processed or stored here.

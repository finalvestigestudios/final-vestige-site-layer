**Subject:** The twelve-minute question

**Excerpt / suggested email preview:** Can a short practice fit a real week? Try a modest experiment, record what happened, and skip the miracle claims.

**Kind:** fieldbook-retention

---

Twelve minutes is appealing because it sounds possible. It may fit between obligations, or before the day becomes noisy. But a routine being practical is not the same as a commercial audio product delivering everything its seller claims.

Start with the part you can actually measure. Pick a time, settle into a comfortable position, and choose one simple task or point of attention. When you notice your mind wandering, bring it back. You don't need to make a grand claim about your brain to learn from the experience.

Make a brief note afterward: Did you start? What interrupted you? How did it feel to begin? What would help tomorrow?

Try that for seven days. If the routine didn't suit you, record that too. A notebook full of ordinary honest observations is worth more than a perfect story invented after the fact.

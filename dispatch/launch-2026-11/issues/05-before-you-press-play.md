---

A recording can be convenient. It can also be pleasant, repeatable and easy to fit into a busy life. None of those qualities proves that the recording can reliably change behavior or produce a seller's promised result.

Before spending money on a meditation or hypnosis audio product, ask three questions.

First: what exactly is included? A single recording, a library, a course or an ongoing subscription? Second: what is actually being claimed? Relaxation, a guided experience and a guaranteed personal transformation are very different propositions. Third: what evidence and current terms support the claim, including price, access, cancellation and refunds?

These questions have an older ancestor. In 1784, a royal commission including Benjamin Franklin investigated claims surrounding Mesmer's animal magnetism. Its work is a case study in testing an explanation rather than taking its persuasive presentation as proof.

We have published a documented Mesmerism investigation that separates the commission's findings, later historical interpretations and what remains uncertain.

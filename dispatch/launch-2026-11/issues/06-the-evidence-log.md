**Subject:** What did seven days of attention reveal?

**Excerpt / suggested email preview:** Make a record of what worked, what didn't, and what you'd change. Then see where the Final Vestige archive goes next.

**Kind:** editorial-bridge

---

After a week of trying something new, memory tends to rush toward a verdict. It changed everything, or it changed nothing. The more useful answer is usually buried in the details.

Did you begin when you intended to? Which days worked? Where did you get interrupted? What helped you return? Those observations do not establish a medical result or prove one technique superior to another. They help you decide what deserves another week of attention.

Take your Fieldbook log and mark three things: one obstacle that repeated, one condition that made starting easier, and one change small enough to try again. That is a more grounded next step than chasing an impressive promise.

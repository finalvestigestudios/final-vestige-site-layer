Do not repeat as facts. No usage/testimonial of this specific product was performed by FV.

Causes (currency, cache, bundles, campaigns, geography) are unknown. Neither is an authorized FV-referred checkout receipt. **Do not quote an exact price or savings in the newsletter.**

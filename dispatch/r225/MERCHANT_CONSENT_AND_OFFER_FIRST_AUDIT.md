## Source 1 — Zen12's actual sales journey, not a catalog summary
Official exact page: https://zen12.com/hnp . Observed sections include “Everything you need” and Premium: twelve progressive levels and 48 downloadable MP3s. The same page **asserts** a twelve-minute session can give the benefits of one hour of meditation, plus broad cognitive, stress, brainwave and health benefits. Those assertions are not independently validated for this product. The page displays a one-year money-back promise and promotional package prices; **do not quote checkout total, guarantee or outcome in Dispatch copy** without verifying final offer terms, eligibility and substantiation.

**NEWLY MATERIAL CONSENT DETAIL:** the exact merchant page contains “Claim your FREE brainwave audio”, name and email fields, “You will receive our newsletter”, and “Skip this Gift”/“Or, just skip this free gift.” This is the **merchant's** optional newsletter offer, not a second Final Vestige mailing list. The webpage content does NOT prove modal visibility, whether skipping is equally easy on mobile, or whether actual checkout requires signup. Those browser/transaction states are NOT TESTED.

A buyer-guide passage for Mind approval, before a relevant merchant link: “On the seller's website you may see an optional offer to receive a free recording by email. That signup is operated by the seller, not The Dispatch. Check the seller's consent terms and use its skip option if you don't want another newsletter.” This is a proposed source-grounded disclosure, not deployed copy. Avoid marketing it as a free gift from FV or promising that it will work for every visitor.

**Conversion consequence:** seller-page visit ≠ FV qualified lead; entering the seller's own email form ≠ Jetpack `confirmed_signup`; clicking an eventual earning link ≠ merchant-reconciled paid purchase. No subscriber records or partner forms were touched.

A “no questions asked” quote without the cap could mislead. The efficacy, final currency/taxes, return processing, and buyer-specific refund rights remain UNVERIFIED. **Conversion consequence:** Mind owner should publish a consumer question checklist only after approval: product identity, format/language and preview, scientifically supported vs asserted outcomes, alternatives including FV's free Fieldbook, price inclusive of taxes at checkout, and exact refund conditions. No hard-sell scarcity, no fabricated testimonials and no implied clinical diagnosis.

## Commerciality and sender integrity
FTC CAN-SPAM: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business . A commercial message requires accurate sender/subject information, identifiable commercial content, a valid **publisher** physical postal address, effective unsubscribe, honoring opt-outs and ongoing sender responsibility. **Neither** the merchant's UK postal address nor Automattic's address in a WordPress confirmation meets Final Vestige's publisher-address proof. A general policy link does not prove the final received email contained a valid footer.

FTC material relationship and recommendation disclosure: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking . Future merchant earning links require clear disclosure next to the recommendation, not only in a sitewide footer. Editorial Standards: https://finalvestige.com/editorial-standards/ . A seller's “proof” section or historical meditation research does not prove an individual audio product works as marketed.

Actual Jetpack newsletter source: https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/send-newsletter-emails/ ; the post title becomes the subject. Offline Gutenberg-style specimens and our static buyer board do **not** verify the delivered subject, preheader, legal-footer text, alternative/plain part, or actual unsubscribe behavior. Native receipt and recipient eligibility remain blocked by required separate founder authorization.

## Compliance and truth-in-advertising

- FTC [Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance): vendor claims require relevant substantiation, not analogy from unrelated studies.
- FTC [Endorsement Guides FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking): qualifying financial interests should be disclosed plainly near recommendations.
- FTC [CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business): verify valid FV publisher postal address, conspicuous opting out, truthful sender and subject before any commercial message.
- WordPress [Email appearance](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/email-appearance/): post title/sending/preview mechanics are controlled by WordPress; a proposed preheader in Markdown is **not** independently verified in actual mail.
- WordPress [Email design](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter-email-design/): feature rollout is staged; an offline HTML file cannot represent guaranteed native Jetpack rendering.

No customer claims, no prices in consumer copy, no product endorsement as independently tested, no false A/B data, and no merchant outbound tracking.

## Funnel / leakage and ethics

**Grok qualified visit → /focus/ → authenticated Dispatch consent → useful history evidence → independent Zen12 guide → authorized merchant checkout → settled purchase → net revenue.** Each arrow requires a distinct provider or first-party record. The current public seller checkout asks for name, email, phone, postal code and card/PayPal information. This likely adds privacy and form friction, but no abandonment rate has been observed. We cannot assert a conversion rate or calculate net revenue.

**Buyer objection map:** 'Does the whole package really contain 48 MP3s?' (seller says yes, actual purchase untested); 'Does the audio equal an hour?' (seller marketing, unsupported independently); 'What if I dislike it?' (seller one-year guarantee, terms may apply); 'Will I get further messages?' (seller free sample email and possible buyer SMS marketing); 'Is the displayed discount truly limited?' (countdown unverified); 'Who earns a commission?' (FV disclosure beside proposed guide, but no live link).

**Actionable copy policy:** Use optional buyer-context notes; avoid testimonials, proof-by-association, manufacturer promotional language or duplicating vendor refund certainty. Do not place direct merchant URLs in the primary CTA. When a first-party guide is authorized, its own nearby disclosure and link must be authenticated in Mind-owned production; Dispatch has no right to activate it.

**Cross-check with FTC:** The current FTC CAN-SPAM guide requires truthful header/subject, clear commercial-purpose labeling where applicable, a valid sender physical postal address and a functioning opt-out, including for existing newsletter subscribers. Only an authorized actual received Jetpack email can confirm rendering; the seller's London address is not Final Vestige's publisher postal address.

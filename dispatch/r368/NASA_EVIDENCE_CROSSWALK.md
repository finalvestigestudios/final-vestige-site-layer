# Apollo 1 primary-source claim check (R368)

Review conducted against NASA sources November 14, 2026 PT. **Original issue #04 remains unchanged.**

| Claim | Evidence & decision |
|---|---|
| Date/place/crew | January 27, 1967, Cape Kennedy Launch Complex 34, Grissom, White, Chaffee; NASA's mission/history records. **SUPPORTED.** |
| Test was a launch | It was a ground 'plugs-out' test, not liftoff. **DO NOT CONFLATE.** |
| Exact ignition point identified | NASA's February 1967 interim report says the board had **not** identified ignition source and regarded electrical malfunction as most likely. **DO NOT DECLARE PROVEN SPARK.** |
| 'Fourteen seconds' in original subject | NASA's 2022 account instead uses 18 seconds from first fire call to radio transmission cessation; other accounts use 17 seconds from differing markers. **NOT A VERIFIED UNIVERSAL INTERVAL; candidate removes it.** |
| Why could they not exit? | Inward-opening hatch, latches/cabin pressure, fire and multiple hatch barriers. **SUPPORTED.** |
| Time for rescuers to reach inside | NASA 2022 article says about five minutes; avoid invented second-by-second rescue certainty. **SUPPORTED with approximation.** |
| The changes | NASA describes Apollo 204 Board recommendations affecting design, quality assurance, test planning, materials and hatch. **SUPPORTED.** |

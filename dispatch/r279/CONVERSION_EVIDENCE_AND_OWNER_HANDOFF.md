## Minimal event contract (proposed, not deployed)
- `landing_qualified`: first-party qualified human session with **real, already-published** `asset_id`, `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, public landing path; exclude crawlers and suspicious synthetic sessions using existing instrumented rules. Do not equate traffic with orders.
- `focus_signup_attempt`: unique eligible form submission attempt, not an opt-in, not a Fieldbook click and not a paid lead.
- `confirmed_signup`: first **provider-authenticated** transition to subscribed/authorized email state, with consent provenance, provider stable pseudonymous key and deduplication. WordPress.com previously verified accounts may not produce a new email-confirmation click. Suppressed, unsubscribed, Reader-only, paused and bounced are not eligible to receive. Dedupe **confirmation events**, not the latest current eligibility.
- `dispatch_cta_click`: one permitted source-tagged publisher CTA click `utm_source=dispatch&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=dispatch_pilot_2026&utm_content=issue_0N`; retain distinct originating social attribution without adding identifying address fields to URLs. Only measure real events through Claude-approved existing tools.
- `paid_conversion_verified`: provider/merchant or payment-settlement-verified actual paid order or commission with timestamp, source order ID held privately, currency, refund and fee adjustments; affiliate click and checkout page visit remain diagnostic only. Deduplicate settled order references and adjust for chargebacks.
- **North-star** `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_sessions = 1000 * (settled owned revenue + cleared affiliate commissions − refunds − fees) / authenticated qualified human sessions`. **NOT COMPUTABLE** without both numerator and denominator; never report an invented zero.

For tiny cohorts, report raw 7/30-day verified counts and missing attribution; no paid-sales A/B claims. No personal email addresses, device IDs, address hashes or cross-site consumer identity links in public artifacts. Privacy/consent and retention to be reviewed by owner before implementation.

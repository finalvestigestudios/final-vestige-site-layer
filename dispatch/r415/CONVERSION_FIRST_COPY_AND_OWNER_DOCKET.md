## Canonical conversion/copy critique — priority graded

**P0 — Issue 03 Zen12.** A well-targeted proposed guide CTA is still an inert placeholder, not a revenue route. Zen12's official seller page describes 12 levels, four audio styles and 12-minute sessions, but asserts a twelve-minute-to-one-hour benefit equivalence and substantial psychological outcomes without independent product-specific proof in the sources reviewed. The R413 optional copy responsibly contrasts those claims with the 1890 William James account and a published systematic review; it does **not** establish Zen12 efficacy. Mind owns a genuinely published reviewed presell guide, named FV account approval, SKU-specific EMAIL marketing rights, issued earning link, authentic referred checkout/returns and settlement reporting. **No substitute catalog or checkout link.**

**P0 — Issue 05 Total Focus.** The vendor's specific page describes a 33-minute/47-MB recording; public prices change with context and bundles. The seller presents a fourteen-day satisfaction policy, but its FAQ also describes a five-refunded-sessions-per-year restriction. These qualifications must be evaluated together against the actual referred checkout. The optional R406 manuscript makes a stronger consumer decision guide, but the first-party commercial CTA stays null until Mind supplies the same named permission, real guide and earning-link evidence. Do not promise 100% concentration, new habits, guaranteed focus or unrestricted refunds.

**P1 — Issue 04 Apollo 1.** Original subject *Fourteen seconds. A much longer explanation.* has no definition of what the interval measures. The official chronology gives first audible crew fire report 23:31:04.7 GMT and cabin rupture 23:31:19.4 GMT (~14.7 seconds), not an established ignition instant. The R412 optional issue is better specific history; edit/adoption requires a founder decision and the site's dated correction standard. This is the only editor slot marked HOLD_COPY as well as global send hold.

**P1 — Issue 01/02 audience retention.** Both original issues link to the same Puységur file across consecutive weekly letters, reducing the fresh-value proposition. R414 door-substitution study and R411 Franklin 1784 commission present separate primary-research questions with separate existing site destinations. Neither is an accepted replacement. Sources and small-sample context must remain visible.

**P1 — Issue 06 after welcome.** The Jetpack welcome already contains the 52-page Fieldbook. Sending a confirmed reader straight back to /focus/ repeats an entry step, not a progression. R407's 1885 Ebbinghaus-based candidate offers one existing, published *The Page That Changed* evidence guide (WP page 2209) as a fresh reader action. It is an editorial engagement hypothesis, not evidence of a sale.

**P1 — Subjects vs preheaders.** WordPress documentation supports post-based email notifications and native Preview. The post's title is the subject in current documentation; no independently controllable preheader feature is verified for this site's Jetpack implementation. The candidate preheaders are **editorial proposals only**, not guaranteed inbox strings. Preview original title, first body text and all links in native non-sending editor, then retain a redacted witness. Static HTML alone is not this proof.

## Revenue measurement (contract, not invented results)

One authenticated, deduplicated, email-eligible provider confirmation may support one confirmed_signup event. A Fieldbook download, browser success parameter, CTR, subscriber aggregate or newsletter open does not. Trace a Grok asset_id and campaign UTM through audited qualified sessions, a matching page, one CTA and an independently paid customer/commission, subtract refunds/fees, then calculate seven-/thirty-day net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions. With paid orders and audited denominator not available, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not a fabricated zero or A/B uplift.

## Primary sources and exact claims

**Historical:** The French commission's investigation of animal magnetism, commissioned in 1784, is preserved in an English-language 1785 report cataloged by [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/enhgscmf), with [full text at Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/78413/78413-h/78413-h.htm). Blindfolded subjects' reports were used to challenge interpretations of a purported invisible agent. This is a methodological analogy about what testimony can prove, not scientific evidence for or against Zen12.

**Independent science:** [Howarth et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12671-019-01163-1), a systematic review of 85 brief mindfulness intervention studies. The review reports effects on various outcomes, with heterogeneous methods and limited follow-up in many studies. **It does not evaluate Zen12 or demonstrate that 12 minutes of that branded product equals an hour of other meditation.**

**Actual seller:** [Official Zen12 sales page](https://zen12.com/) currently describes three materially different package tiers, with **Premium = 12 levels × four styles = 48 MP3s**. The seller advertises brainwave entrainment, an hour's benefits in twelve minutes, a one-year refund offer and promotional pricing. The last three are **merchant representations, not independent outcomes, authenticated referral checkout or verified contract terms**. Prices, limitations, buyer refund eligibility and exact offer terms are all held; never quote a price or unconditional guarantee from a public page alone.

**Affiliate program:** [Inspire3 affiliate portal](https://affiliates.inspire3.com/) is a public signup and promotional page. It **does not prove Final Vestige's named acceptance, permission to promote via email, licensed creative, approved SKU earnings link or paid commission**. Targeted connected Gmail searches for explicit approval and EMAIL permissions returned no account-specific proof; consumer marketing messages from Inspire3 must not be mistaken for publisher approval. The negative search is not proof that approval cannot exist elsewhere.

## US commercial-mail compliance release

[FTC CAN-SPAM guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) requires applicable commercial email to identify its sender accurately, have honest subjects, provide a valid publisher physical postal address, and include a functioning opt-out mechanism meeting applicable rules. **Neither an Automattic address in a platform confirmation nor Inspire3's London address verifies Final Vestige's own qualifying postal address.** Publisher postal, rendering and functional opt-out are hard holds; no real test email is authorized. Commercial relationship is disclosed adjacent to the disabled CTA, but placement has not been verified in a received client.

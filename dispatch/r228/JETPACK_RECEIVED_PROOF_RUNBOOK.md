STATUS: no real Jetpack preview executed, no message received and no test email permitted.

1. The founder must separately authorize any step that requires changing the individual WordPress "send to subscribers" checkbox or actually sending a test. WordPress describes "Preview email" on pre-publish screen when the newsletter send setting is on; never toggle it for this review. Offline Gutenberg or HTML specimen is not a received Jetpack email.
2. A third-party Automattic footer is not automatically the publisher's physical address.

If actual commercial delivery is later authorized, use one consented controlled recipient, not the subscriber list, inspect correct post title/subject/preheader in real client and inspect a real unsubscribe without contacting unconsented addresses. State its outcome and client/browser; do not infer universal rendering.
4. Use the existing Jetpack list only. WordPress verification may be immediate for verified account holders but new outside emails may need confirmation. A success screen/PDF/open/download/merchant newsletter signup is not the provider-authenticated consented lead.
5. For any issues classified legally as commercial, apply FTC primary-purpose requirements and maintain truthful seller claims, final publisher mailing address and working opt out. Founder final creative/audience/schedule approval stays mandatory even after every technical test passes.

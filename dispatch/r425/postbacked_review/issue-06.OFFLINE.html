What did seven days of attention reveal?

Proposed preheader · editorial source metadata only: A small experiment, a useful notebook, and no miracle claims.

After a week of trying to concentrate differently, it's easy to be left with one of two impressions: it changed everything, or it changed nothing.

Neither impression is very useful without a record.

A more practical question is what actually happened. Which days did you follow through? How long did you practice? When did you get interrupted? What made it easier to start the next day?

Those observations aren't a medical result or proof that one technique is superior. They're simply better information for choosing what you'll keep doing.

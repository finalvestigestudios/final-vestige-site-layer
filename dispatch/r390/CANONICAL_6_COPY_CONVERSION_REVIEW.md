### Copy-conversion edits proposed, not applied

**Issue 01** — make an observed historical artifact and an inspectable source appear in the opening 80 words. Current prose is credible but conceptual; a specific perception experiment with documented limitations increases value. Avoid treating a metaphoric camera comparison as proof of faulty memory in every case.

**Issue 02** — preserve firsthand vs inferred distinctions. Consider one actionable evidence worksheet instead of a second consecutive Puységur CTA to the same page. Check the exact dated edition before keeping “In 1785” as a firm date.

**Issue 03** — headline is truthful, but “twelve minutes” may inherit the seller's equivalence hook. State a practical twelve-minute habit is possible without implying the vendor audio equals an hour. The CTA is an unresolved placeholder; do not encode it as clickable or activate the coupon link. Price, refund and format require approved referred checkout, not a public landing snapshot.

**Issue 04** — **highest editorial accuracy priority**. NASA's [Review Board final report](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/apollo/apollo-1/report-of-the-apollo-204-review-board/) explicitly says no single ignition source was conclusively identified; most probable initiator was electrical arc. The [NASA chronology](https://www.nasa.gov/history/Apollo204/seamans.html) is preliminary and puts cabin rupture fourteen seconds after fire detection, not universal fire duration. The optional R390 specimen names the uncertainty and the consequences without a manufactured time-based curiosity hook.

**Issue 05** — the audience buys a specific recording, not a product family. The [Total Focus merchant page](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus) describes 33 minutes/47 MB and advertises dramatic focus promises. The [merchant FAQ](https://hypnosislive.com/faq) describes a 14-day refund and maximum five session refunds per year. Record prices and refunds only as dated seller-site observations, never as agreed earning-link checkout terms. No personal product testing is claimed.

**Issue 06** — keep the no-pressure, non-commercial close. Explicitly invite readers who have **not** completed the fieldbook, because delivery and adoption are not proven.

Before you buy a hypnosis recording

An old lesson about suggestion, and five buyer checks that work today.

In 1784, a French royal commission tested claims about animal magnetism by changing what participants knew and could see. Their investigation didn't test modern hypnosis recordings. It showed why an experience and the explanation offered for it need different evidence.

A guided audio may be convenient without proving promised changes in behavior, memory or health. Before paying, ask what arrives, whose claim it is, whether a representative sample exists, what the precise refund boundaries are and what you will measure.

Hypnosis Live's seller FAQ currently describes MP3 downloads and a 14-day refund request window, with a maximum of five refundable sessions in a year. Specific product and checkout conditions must be checked again.

Try this free baseline: spend five minutes in quiet on two days. Note whether you finish and what distracts you. An observation is more useful than an assumed outcome.

THE DISPATCH — ISSUE 05 (Hypnosis Live; NOT Total Focus)

In 1784, a royal commission used controlled observations to question
animal magnetism. Its work did not test any present-day hypnosis audio.
It reminds us that a compelling experience and a compelling explanation
are different things.

Before buying, examine:
1. What exactly arrives: an MP3, live meeting, series or subscription?
2. What does the seller promise, and what supports that product-specific claim?
3. Is there a representative voice/sample?
4. What are the actual refund conditions at the current checkout?
5. What would you measure before and after, without pretending a result?

The Hypnosis Live seller FAQ describes downloadable MP3s, a 14-day
refund request period and a limit of five refunded sessions per year.
Confirm the actual offer and terms independently.

Free exercise: spend five quiet minutes on two days, noting start
difficulty, completion and one distraction. This is self-observation,
not proof of a commercial audio's efficacy.

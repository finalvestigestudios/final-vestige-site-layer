**Reader intent:** an investigations reader who downloaded the FREE 52-page Focus Fieldbook, actually tested its seven-day exercise, and *independently* wants a convenient guided-audio meditation practice. Do **not** infer this from email opens or assign sensitive segments. Reader questions and voluntary clicks, not guessed personal traits, are the only proposed explicit-interest signals. No actual person assigned.

**ONE commercial offer under assessment:** Zen12 Premium, because its seller describes 12 progressive levels and four styles. The public seller main page advertises 12-minute listening and different product tiers; a separate promotional page shows a conditional coupon. Those are seller representations and **neither is an issued Final Vestige affiliate destination, referred price, medical substantiation, or proof of email rights**. Avoid performance claims (“an hour of meditation”, automatic relief, guaranteed focus, brain reprogramming). Mind owns an honest guide; Dispatch only points to it when published and verified.

**Without any product recommendation**, the free workbook is usable. Give an observation baseline: choose a task; note start/end, interruption count and how much was completed; choose whether silence, ordinary ambient audio or a guided session was useful. This is not a controlled clinical experiment. Explain expectation effects and limitations; do not present an evaluation log as scientific proof.

# R196 — exact merchant offer and email-channel evidence ledger

**Seller assertions are not independently proven product outcomes.** This is an UNSENT research record; no account acceptance, email channel terms or earning links were exposed.

## Offer A (pilot issue 03) — Zen12 Premium
Official page read 2026-11-07: https://zen12.com/hnp . The page offers **twelve levels** and **48 MP3 recordings** with four variants per level. It also advertises a **one-year** money-back guarantee and shows a Premium price on-page; none is evidence of final checkout, currency/taxes, refund performance, commission eligibility, account authorization or product-specific cognitive effectiveness. The claim 'an hour of meditation in twelve minutes' is **seller advertising**, not a finding we can independently endorse.

Offer decision: best aligned with the Fieldbook's twelve-minute **self-directed** practice, but do not claim they are equivalent interventions. The reader can do the free practice without a purchase. Proposed single commercial CTA: an *independent* source-led buyer guide owned by Mind Studio, only once **published and verified**. No destination is presently authorized. Example near-guide disclosure: 'The linked guide may contain affiliate links. If you buy through a qualifying link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.' Its presence in review copy does not activate a link.

## Permission gate (both offers separately)
Hard HOLD unless Mind Studio / actual partner dashboard supplies publisher-specific: (a) **accepted account identity** associated with Final Vestige, (b) current applicable program agreement and **email channel approval**, (c) allowed creative/claims, (d) correct issued trackable destination, (e) verified living independent guide (mobile and disclosure QA), (f) checkout, refund and region conditions, (g) real legal address/unsubscribe rendering in a received email, (h) separate founder final-campaign and recipient approval. Consent to Final Vestige Dispatch is not permission to enroll recipients in the merchant's newsletter. Public catalog pages and Gmail searches are insufficient evidence.

## Revenue and conversion definition
Affiliate click and an order-button click are **not** paid conversions. Only reconciled partner-confirmed settled commissions, with chargebacks/refunds/fees, qualify for net revenue. Without independent sales records plus a qualified-human-session denominator, **net revenue per 1,000 QHS = NOT COMPUTABLE**. With a tiny audience, no tests of subject winner, conversion uplift or profit probability are statistically credible.

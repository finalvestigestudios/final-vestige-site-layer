**Funnel order:** eligible verified human sessions (bot-filtered, unique asset) → matching published page load → CTA click → consented provider-confirmed subscriber where applicable → vetted approved guide → authorized issued affiliate earning click (distinct from sale) → merchant-attributed paid order/settled commission → chargebacks/refunds, fees, adjustments → net revenue. In 7- and 30-day actual cohorts, if denominator or paid settlements missing: `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, never '0.00' as if measured. Separate verified orders, paid conversion rate, leads and lead-to-order rate. Zero sales is a verified zero only after the payment/reporting source confirms it for the scope; 'no data' is NOT zero.

### Why this matters for conversion
The product-page headline describes a generic 14-day guarantee, but the seller FAQ supplies a meaningful **five-refund-sessions-per-year** qualification. A credible independent guide must tell readers about that limitation; concealing it would worsen trust and potentially induce refunds. The landing page's dynamically displayed discount is not evidence of genuine scarcity or final checkout terms.

### Partner approval still has zero affirmative evidence
The public Inspire3 network does not prove Final Vestige's account acceptance, email-promotion permission, assigned earning URLs, newsletter creative usage rights, payable commissions, current eligibility, geo restrictions or financial reporting. Private Gmail search during this run found no account-specific authorization matching the targeted permission query. No merchant contact was attempted. Seller public postal addresses do not replace a valid publisher mailing address.

### Commercial disclosure
Adjacent to future independently vetted guides: “If you buy through a qualifying link in this guide, Final Vestige may earn a commission.” Do not use an actual link until the Mind & Affiliate Studio authenticates it. Never use testimonials not tested, and do not substitute clinical hypnosis evidence for product-specific evidence.

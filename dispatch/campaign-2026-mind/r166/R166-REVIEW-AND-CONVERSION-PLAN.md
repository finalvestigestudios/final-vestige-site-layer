## Actual new bug: negative net income must remain a negative number
Inherited `r165/jetpack_transport_gate.cjs` `canReportRevenue` throws if `receipts < refunds + fees`, while `r162/provider_truth.cjs` `revenueKpi` reports NOT_COMPUTABLE in that case. This **censors possible campaign losses** from the most important KPI. A negative realized net is not a missing measurement. This can cause a revenue-first program to suppress bad results and overestimate economics. **R166 ships an additive offline comparator** (`revenue_truth.cjs`) that returns signed integer cents, including negative net per 1,000 verified human sessions, only if independent source, cohort, paid-order/settlement, refund and attribution proofs are explicitly attested. It preserves `NOT_COMPUTABLE` when proofs or denominator are absent. Its positive and negative test amounts are SYNTHETIC regression fixtures, not FV earnings. The add-on does NOT edit earlier branch files or alter live analytics. Analytics/Commerce owner must reconcile the old gates before any production metric interpretation.

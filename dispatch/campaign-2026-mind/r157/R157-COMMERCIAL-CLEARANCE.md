## FTC/legal difference that matters

The official [CAN-SPAM Act guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) covers commercial email including email driving traffic to commercial websites; a promotional issue must identify its business purpose and comply with header, subject, actual publisher postal address, opt-out and honoring rules. Neither Automattic's address in a subscription-confirmation request nor Inspire3's seller address substitutes for **Final Vestige's** mailing address. The FTC also warns that a company cannot outsource its compliance responsibility to the email provider. A link to a general affiliate-disclosure page alone is not a conspicuous notice adjacent to the qualifying recommendation. In both plaintext and HTML, use clear near-offer wording: "If you buy through a qualifying link in our guide, Final Vestige may earn a commission." This is conditional until a real earning link is installed.

[WordPress newsletter documentation](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/send-newsletter-emails/) distinguishes **Preview email**, a no-send visual preview, from **Send test email**, which delivers an email. Native preview is a suitable authorized inspection; no test recipient or any delivery has been approved. Zero mails were sent or scheduled this run.

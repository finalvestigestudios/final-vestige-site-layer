Thirty-three minutes. Forty-seven megabytes. One downloadable hypnosis recording.

Those are concrete details of Hypnosis Live's **Total Focus** listing. They tell you what format you're considering. They don't tell you how much your concentration will improve.

The merchant's page says the session uses hypnosis and NLP, and makes bolder promises about changing the brain and boosting attention. **Those are seller claims, not results independently verified for this specific MP3.** Research into hypnosis for other applications can't automatically establish this product's effects.

Before you buy, I'd ask three questions.

**What am I hearing?** The page includes an audio preview. It can help you judge whether the voice and structure suit you, not whether the recording will work.

**What is actually being promised?** An enjoyable guided session and a proven change in attention are different propositions. Ask what evidence supports each.

**What are the terms?** Check the latest price, download access and refund conditions at the actual checkout rather than trusting an older promotion.

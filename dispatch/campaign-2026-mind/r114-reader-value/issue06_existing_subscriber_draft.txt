Subject candidate: What did seven days of attention reveal?
Preheader copy candidate (native transport not verified): A small experiment, a useful notebook, and no miracle claims.

If you tried a short focus routine this week, what did you notice? Perhaps it was easier to begin when the task had an ending. Perhaps you were interrupted every time. Or perhaps seven days passed without a single attempt.

All three are observations worth recording. None proves that an audio program rewires your mind, and a workbook cannot diagnose a problem concentrating.

Try noting the task, the time you intended to start, whether you began, and what interrupted you. Repeat when it is useful. A small log can replace an impression with something you can revisit.

What did seven days of attention reveal?

If you tried a short focus routine this week, what did you notice? Perhaps it was easier to begin when the task had an ending. Perhaps you were interrupted every time. Or perhaps seven days passed without a single attempt.

All three are worth recording. None proves that an audio program rewires your mind, and a workbook cannot diagnose a problem concentrating.

Try noting the task, when you intended to start, whether you began, and what interrupted you. A small log can replace an impression with something you can revisit.

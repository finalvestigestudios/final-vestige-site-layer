## Reserved Issue 03 — Zen12, not yet approved

**Official specific product sales source:** [Zen12's actual package page](https://zen12.com/?no-popup=1) (retrieved from indexed merchant page; search's oldest crawl is over a year old, therefore **not a reliable current-price or current-terms attestation**). Product descriptions: Beginner = six levels/24 MP3s, Premium = twelve levels/48 MP3s, Advanced = last six levels/24 MP3s. Premium's 48 MP3s must **not** be advertised as included in every tier. The page advertises a one-year money-back promise and twelve-minute recordings. Its central *twelve minutes = the benefits of an hour* pitch is **a seller claim without verified product-specific controlled evidence**.

**Fit hypothesis:** a subscriber who opened the Focus Fieldbook and wants help establishing a modest audio routine may value a comparison of sound formats, duration, download format and self-directed alternatives. This is not proof that Zen12 changes cognitive performance.

**Allowed draft framing:** “The seller describes a series of short downloadable meditation audios in several course tiers. The useful question is whether the actual content, ongoing practice and purchase rules suit you.” **Prohibited:** equating 12 minutes to an hour as independently proven, guarantees of productivity/mental health change, fabricated personal use/testimonials, guaranteed savings, permanent sale price, live countdown or fabricated merchant rights.

**Buyer objections to answer before any referral:** what is in the selected tier, how the files are accessed, whether the customer can try free neutral practices first, exact refund process and constraints at time of purchase, purchaser-data handling, and independently supported effect claims. Never repeat a video/seller claim as an experimentally demonstrated FV outcome.

**Allowed draft framing:** “The seller lists a 33-minute, 47 MB self-hypnosis recording. Those are product specifications, not independent evidence that its promised improvements will occur.” **Prohibited:** “clinically proven to rewire the brain,” “guaranteed distraction-free focus,” universal no-limit refund, invented paid purchase, direct merchant link before permission, or extrapolation from general hypnosis research to this recording.

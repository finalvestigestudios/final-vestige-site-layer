SUBJECT: Zen12 Premium: what 48 recordings cannot promise
PREHEADER: Twelve levels, four listening styles—and a sensible way to compare the offer.

A sales page can tell you what comes in a box. It cannot tell you what will happen in your mind.

When we examine a historical document, we separate what the record says from what someone hopes it proves. A meditation-audio advertisement deserves the same courtesy.

Zen12's seller describes twelve levels of recordings, with music, guided meditation, nature sounds or white noise at each level. The Premium library is advertised as 48 MP3 files around twelve-minute sessions. These are product-format descriptions, not evidence of a specific result.

The seller also claims that a short session can yield the benefit of a much longer meditation. We have not verified product-specific evidence for that equivalence. We have not tested the one-year guarantee or final checkout terms.

The Seven Day Focus Fieldbook offered free to new subscribers lets you try a consistent routine, log interruptions and decide whether a structured audio format sounds useful—without buying anything. If you do compare Zen12 Premium, check current checkout price, included files and refund conditions before deciding.

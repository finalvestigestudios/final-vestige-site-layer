## The material new finding: preheader ≠ verified WordPress header
WordPress's [actual send instructions](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/send-newsletter-emails/) say the **post title becomes the newsletter email subject**. Its [settings instructions](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter-settings/) and [appearance guide](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/email-appearance/) document **full text vs excerpt** and how to set a **custom excerpt**; they do **not** establish a separately configurable native **email preheader** field in the currently accessible configuration. The master campaign has polished subjects and preheaders, but the latter are **copywriting candidates**, **not verified inbox preview text**. Never claim WordPress will deliver the requested preheader without observing its actual source/rendering.

[Official WordPress instructions](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter-settings/) explain that excerpt uses the post's custom excerpt, or (absent one) the start of the post, normally 55 words.

## The production constraint we must stop obscuring

A source-controlled newsletter manuscript is **not** a Jetpack email campaign. The live WordPress.com/Jetpack Newsletter is configured for **post notification**, not an independent six-message email automation. The proposed six files have polished subjects and manually drafted preheaders, but the native WordPress email subject comes from the **published post title** ([WordPress support](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/send-newsletter-emails/)). We have **no evidence of a separate native Jetpack preheader field** in the site's connected settings. Treat all six manuscript preheaders as editorial design metadata, *not* demonstrated inbox preheader text. Do not promise them to recipients or test artificial preview inbox screenshots as genuine native delivery.

A second compatibility gap concerns **excerpts**. The site setting is `email_content_format=excerpt`, with auto-new-post emails OFF. Jetpack's updated [4 November 2026 support guide](https://jetpack.com/support/newsletter/customize-the-newsletter-experience/) describes the first **350 characters** when an editor excerpt is absent; [WordPress.com support](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter-settings/) describes **55 words**. They may describe different products/contexts or revisions. These are competing documented models, **not verified subscriber inbox output**. WordPress may add a native **Read More** control to the new post; it does not necessarily link directly to the pre-existing Final Vestige investigation or buyer guide. Even with a carefully crafted manual excerpt, the new post itself needs to contain a coherent editorial reason and one working, appropriate final CTA. Genuine email preview is required.

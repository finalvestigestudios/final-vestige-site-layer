# R52 — Founder-only copy options; NEVER installed or sent

## New finding and decision
The live /focus/ form already tells readers that commission-earning recommendations may appear. The live /dispatch/ form does not; it promises investigations and archival finds. The configured welcome message likewise promises investigation and finds without explaining optional product evaluations. Both paths lead to the existing, single Jetpack newsletter.

This asymmetry is a meaningful editorial trust issue, not proof of an illegal mailing. US CAN-SPAM does not generally mandate advance opt-in. Our proposed campaign has four evidence-led issues and two potentially commercial issues. Subscribers may have seen different wording at signup; no personal histories were inspected.

### Option A — direct, evergreen /dispatch/ near-form statement
Join The Dispatch for original investigations, archival finds and occasional Mind & Perception research notes. Some editions may include an independent evaluation of an optional product. If a linked evaluation contains a qualifying purchase link, Final Vestige may earn a commission. We disclose that relationship clearly; buying is never required to read our work. Unsubscribe anytime.

### Option B — shorter /dispatch/ statement
Original investigations, archive finds and occasional research-led product evaluations. Some qualifying links may earn Final Vestige a commission. We label commercial relationships; no purchase required. Unsubscribe anytime.

Option A is clearer about the Mind & Perception bridge; Option B is more compact. Both are proposals, NOT live edits. The statement should sit beside the real Jetpack form. Do not create another signup list, provider or tracking surface.

### /focus/ recommendation
Leave the existing prominent near-form commission notice intact. It already says: Some emails may include recommendations that earn us a commission. Align the less explicit /dispatch/ surface, rather than weakening /focus/. Any change requires a separately approved WordPress edit and native mobile/keyboard QA.

### Optional welcome addendum, retaining the current 52-page book link
The Dispatch is an archive-first publication. Occasionally we'll examine attention and perception, including an optional product evaluation. If a linked guide could earn us a commission, we'll say so plainly in that issue. The investigations remain free to read, and no purchase is needed.

Do NOT install this prospectively until founder approval. It cannot retrospectively prove what current subscribers saw, nor that the current welcome email arrived.

### Optional transition for Issue 01, not a replacement of the canonical manuscript
You joined for investigations and archive finds. Those aren't going anywhere. We're also looking at the evidence behind what people think they saw and what modern focus products claim to do. Some later editions may contain clearly labeled, optional evaluations; any commission relationship will appear beside the recommendation.

### Reader-purpose and consent QA
- The full campaign remains weekly only as a draft proposal. Do not claim issues have been scheduled or mailed.
- Keep four editorial editions genuinely research-first. Commercial 03/05 need real first-party warm-up guides and account-specific permission for the actual named product and EMAIL channel.
- A sender's truthful subject is not a legal substitute for the correct promotional primary-purpose assessment or actual unsub/address footer.
- The 2 total / 2 confirmed / 2 unconfirmed dashboard numbers cannot identify unique opted-in recipients or which consent notice they saw.
- Automatic-post email being OFF is only a default: WordPress supports manually enabling delivery on an individual post.
- Excerpt format means a complete offline manuscript or proposed preheader is not proof of what will land in an inbox.
- Until legal/merchant/native-email/consent and founder approvals exist: all SIX HOLD_UNSENT, and 03/05 additionally HOLD_MERCHANT.

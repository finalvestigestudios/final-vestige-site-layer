## Finding: Markdown assets and Jetpack newsletter mail are different delivery products
Six `.md` letters are not automatically mail objects. WordPress/Jetpack sends a **post's** newsletter email when a post is published with its per-post **Send this post to subscribers** setting enabled. The global auto-new-post setting is currently **OFF**, but the individual editor toggle can still override that default. A public *page* is not an email newsletter post. There is **no approved delivery/sending workflow yet**. Official provider sources:
- https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/send-newsletter-emails/
- https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/email-appearance/
- https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter-settings/

Provider instructions say **Preview email** is a desktop/tablet/mobile preview, while **Send test email** sends a real message. The editor may require the per-post email toggle ON before it displays the non-sending preview; *enabling that toggle while editing a draft is NOT authority to publish, schedule or send*. The sender's visible name can be customized, but the sender email is `donotreply@wordpress.com`, while our live `reply_to=no-reply`. These constraints mean the manuscript's proposed **preheader** should not be claimed as verified inbox preview text until the actual Jetpack rendering is inspected. Do not paste the original `UNSENT DRAFT` banner or placeholder compliance blocks into a post that could publish.

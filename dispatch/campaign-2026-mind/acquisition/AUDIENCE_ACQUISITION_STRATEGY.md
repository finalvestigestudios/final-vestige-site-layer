## Creative/cadence system

- Keep the one-list promise **history + sources first**. Each weekly edition (draft only) has a related 20–45-second original social short with one clear curiosity hook, one verified point and an invitation to /dispatch/.
- First two social shorts seed **archive credibility**; issue 03 leads to a free attention practice, issue 04 restores a flagship historical case, issue 05 teaches a product-evaluation checklist, issue 06 promotes the already-live Focus Fieldbook.
- Reuse native captions, not letter-for-letter re-upload of someone else's content, on the project's already-established social accounts. Each narration is prepared here but **no video has been produced or posted**.
- The direct-response flow is **specific question → small evidence-led revelation → trust earned through sources → free Fieldbook invitation → optional newsletter → merchant guide only later with authorization**. Avoid giving subscribers the impression that they have entered a sales sequence.

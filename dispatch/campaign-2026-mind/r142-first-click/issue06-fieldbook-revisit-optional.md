After seven days of trying a new focus routine, it's tempting to decide the exercise either changed everything or changed nothing.

Neither verdict tells you much about what happened on Tuesday.

Start with something smaller. Open the log in your free Seven Day Focus Fieldbook. For your next five-minute check-in, record only three things: what you tried, what interrupted you, and what made it easier—or harder—to begin again. Repeat tomorrow. Don't give your performance a grand label. Just write what you observed.

A week of notes is not a clinical study. Your entries won't prove that a method works for everyone, nor will they verify claims made by a commercial meditation recording. They can tell you whether a routine fits the life you're actually living.

There's a reason we make this distinction at Final Vestige. Historians learn more from a surviving record than from a perfectly tidy account of what supposedly happened. A dated observation can be revisited and challenged. A vague impression can't.

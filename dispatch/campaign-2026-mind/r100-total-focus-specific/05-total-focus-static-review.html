The recording is 33 minutes. What does that prove?

Proposed preheader only: An exact product listing, a historical lesson, and five checks before spending. This is not a verified native Jetpack preheader.

A 33-minute recording is measurable. Its promise to change your concentration is another matter.

In Puységur's eighteenth-century files, what a witness reported matters. What was independently checked matters just as much. Modern sales pages deserve that same distinction.

Hypnosis Live lists Total Focus as a 33-minute, 47 MB MP3 recorded by J.A. Amos. These are product descriptions. The seller's stronger claims about productivity and concentration are not independently verified results for this exact recording.

Five checks before buying

  1. The exact SKU. This is one recording, not an entire course. Verify format, language, duration, purchase conditions and seller.
  2. Experience isn't outcome. A pleasant guided session and a demonstrated change in performance are different propositions. Demand product-specific evidence for outcome claims.
  3. The limits of the guarantee. The official FAQ and Terms of Sale describe 14 days and at most five session refunds per customer in twelve months. These are seller-published terms; don't assume a guarantee of fulfillment or permanent price.
  4. A free baseline. For three work periods, log the task, whether you started, time to first interruption and one setting adjustment. This is practical observation, not clinical evidence.
  5. Buyer privacy. Inspire3 says referred purchaser identity and item details may be shared with affiliates. We require written aggregate-only/no-identifying-buyer-data reporting before any earning link.

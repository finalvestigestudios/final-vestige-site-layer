## 4. The FTC's mixed-message reality

The FTC guide specifically says the CAN-SPAM Act extends beyond mass sends and can apply to email promoting commercial website content. An editorial-style article that routes to a product affiliate warm-up page can still be commercial depending on its primary purpose. **Do not assume that sending a link to a guide, rather than a checkout page, removes address, sender, opt-out, or disclosure requirements.** Correctly identify the sender, use a nondeceptive subject, identify promotional content, show Final Vestige's **actual qualifying physical postal address**, include a workable opt-out, and maintain suppression. Merely displaying "unsubscribe anytime" on a signup landing page does not establish the delivered email's functional unsubscribe. See https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business and https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking .

A US registered PO Box or properly registered commercial mail receiving agency mailbox **may qualify** under the FTC guide; the publisher must provide a real valid address. A postal address from a WordPress confirmation email belongs to Automattic, not necessarily Final Vestige. The seller's UK address printed on Total Focus also does not make it Final Vestige's mailing address. The newsletter `reply_to=no-reply` setting does not itself violate CAN-SPAM if a functional Internet-based opt-out is provided, but it further increases the need to verify that option in a real received message. Some email one-click headers may be desirable, but the FTC does not universally require a particular header. Authorization to test received email has **not** been given.

## 7. No fake analytics

For an audience that might be 0–2 actual inboxes, no split-test significance, click-through rates, opening rates, sales or forecasted return is supported. First-party pageviews can only establish pageviews; they do not identify campaign-attributed visitors unless privacy-safe approved campaign/source tags and a real message exist. Unique URLs, newsletter-specific query parameters, and affiliate attribution are currently NOT approved. Store all unobserved campaign metrics as `null` or `UNOBSERVED` rather than zero; avoid storing recipients, tokens, device fingerprints or purchaser data.

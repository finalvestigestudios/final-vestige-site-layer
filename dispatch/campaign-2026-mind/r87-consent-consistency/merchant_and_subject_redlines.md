## Seller source map: exact named products (NOT merchant-approved email copy)

### Zen12 — https://zen12.com/hnp

**Merchant describes:** 12-minute MP3 sessions, 12 levels across a complete program, four listening styles per level, 48 MP3s in the complete library, with a limited six-level/24-MP3 tier; current page also advertises package prices and a one-year guarantee. The headline asserts that 12 minutes brings an hour of advanced meditation, and promises changes to stress, health, focus and creativity. **Only format/package listing is seller-supplied product description.** The hour-equivalence and health/brain efficacy are *not* independently proven for Zen12 by that sales page. Program details, current prices, guarantee/refund terms and what checkout actually includes require merchant/checkout verification at promotion time. Never transplant price or a numerical efficacy promise into an email.

**Critical privacy nuance:** public Zen12 free-audio opt-in pages ask visitors to subscribe to the **merchant's newsletter**. Do not portray a third-party giveaway as the site's own no-strings Focus Fieldbook or silently enter Final Vestige subscribers in a second list. No newsletter link to vendor opt-in as a stand-in for the unpublished Mind-owned independent guide.

**Possible buyer-guide bridge — INACTIVE:** "A twelve-minute routine is easy to *try*. Whether a paid audio package helps is a different question. Our independently sourced Zen12 review will compare format, alternatives, disclosed seller claims and what evidence is still missing. [LINK PENDING FINAL GUIDES + EMAIL RIGHTS]. If you buy through a qualifying link in that guide, Final Vestige may earn a commission." No endorsement implied; offer reserved, not sent.

### Hypnosis Live Total Focus — https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus

**Merchant describes:** recording by J. A. Amos; 33-minute, 47-MB MP3; product page advertises standalone and bundle prices, a seller 14-day satisfaction policy. It also promises 100% focus and brain "reprogramming." These outcomes are *seller persuasion*, not independently demonstrated product-specific effects. Current listed pricing is not necessarily final checkout pricing or affiliate-approved representation. Distinguish the single recording from bundles, access/subscription claims and other hypnosis titles.

**Possible buyer-guide bridge — INACTIVE:** "A 33-minute recording can tell you its length, not what it will accomplish. Our Total Focus buyer's guide separates its advertised format from the evidence and asks what a purchaser should confirm before spending. [LINK PENDING FINAL GUIDES + EMAIL RIGHTS]. Final Vestige may earn a commission if you purchase through a qualifying link in the guide." No direct seller URL or performance guarantee.

### No seller authorization evidence

The [public Inspire3 affiliate portal](https://affiliates.inspire3.com/) is recruitment information; it does **not** verify Final Vestige's named-account acceptance, an authorized earning URL, product-specific approved creative, private commission/refund terms, permission to promote to an email list, or customer-data handling authorization. Gmail search returns consumer offer promotions and purchase receipts but **no matching Final Vestige-specific partner authorization or EMAIL-channel grant**; absence in searched messages is not proof none exists.

**In both issues 03 and 05, the original draft's disclosure follows the proposed guide CTA.** For transparency and FTC endorsement principles, recommend a visible disclosure in the opening excerpt AND adjacent/before the first-party guide destination, and repeat near any eventual qualifying outbound link on the guide. Commercial message primary purpose may trigger CAN-SPAM, even if linking only to an editorial review; a real physical address and working opt-out remain unverified.

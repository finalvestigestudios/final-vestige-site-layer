## Conversion and compliance implications
1. Original #03/#05 disclosures occur **below** inactive guide CTA, far past a typical default excerpt's first visible text. In a future separately approved custom excerpt, mention the possible commission *before* a paid-tool invitation and near its **one** actual guide CTA. Existing offline excerpt candidates are NOT sent-message proof. The WordPress post title is the email subject; a separate Jetpack-editable preheader field is unverified.
2. A consumer promotional email, seller's public affiliate enrollment, personal purchase, claimed affiliate dashboard, or authorization for a different product/channel **does not demonstrate permission to send these two specific offers in an affiliate email**. Never paste personal partner credentials or subscriber details into a repo or GitHub issue.
3. FTC [CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business): truthful From/subject, identify advertisement where applicable, **publisher's valid physical postal address**, clear functional opt-out and honoring opt-outs. Seller or Automattic postal address is **not** automatically Final Vestige's mailing address. One-click header format is not a universal requirement of CAN-SPAM; functional unsubscribe still must be verified in received email.
4. WordPress [email appearance](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/email-appearance/) docs: real preview is within a **post** Newsletter panel; custom excerpt is in the post excerpt field, and full versus excerpt is a site-wide setting. Official [send guide](https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/send-newsletter-emails/) says only posts generate send emails, each post sends once, and 'Preview email' is separate from **Send test email**. No email/test send or WordPress write authorized here.
5. Inspire3 [privacy](https://inspire3.com/privacy/) explicitly allows potential sharing of purchaser name/email/product with a referring affiliate. Final Vestige should adopt a **no-purchaser-PII intake** policy; only aggregated permissible reporting after partner/data-use review. The partner's [public enrollment](https://affiliates.inspire3.com/) describes special links and commission in general; it is not our own approval.
6. The official Total Focus [sales page](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus) shows an advertised 14-day refund guarantee; exact exceptions, caps and checkout/payment terms require a dated copy of the actual binding terms and legal review. No pricing or refund promise belongs in campaign copy until current, contextual verification.

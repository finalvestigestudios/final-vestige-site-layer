**Subject:** Before you press play on a hypnosis audio

**Preheader:** Three questions that matter more than an impressive promise.

**Content type:** commercial-review-pending

---

A recording offers convenience: you can revisit it, practice privately and decide whether a guided format suits you. That says something about the format. It doesn't settle what the recording can change.

In our historical Puységur files, the hardest part is separating what observers reported from the conclusions they attached to those reports. Modern hypnosis marketing deserves the same scrutiny.

Before buying any self-hypnosis audio, I'd ask three questions.

**What is actually included?** A downloadable recording, a course, a subscription, or something else? Are the instructions and usage conditions clear?

**What is the claim?** General relaxation, a pleasant guided experience and a guaranteed change in behavior are different promises. They require different kinds of support.

**What happens if it isn't for you?** Check the current price, file format, access method and refund policy on the seller's checkout page. Those details can change. We won't reproduce a temporary price, discount or refund limit in an email someone may read months later.

**The recording we're evaluating: Total Focus.** The official [Hypnosis Live Total Focus page](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus) describes a downloadable hypnosis audio recording lasting **33 minutes** and sized **47 MB**, with an audio preview. Those are seller-listed product specifications, not evidence of a measurable improvement in concentration. The page also promises unusually powerful focus and brain 'reprogramming'; **we have not independently verified those outcomes for this recording**. The [NIH's overview of hypnosis research](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/hypnosis) discusses evidence for particular uses of hypnosis, but it does not establish that *Total Focus* improves attention. Our evaluation will distinguish the exact product from the wider Hypnosis Live catalog.

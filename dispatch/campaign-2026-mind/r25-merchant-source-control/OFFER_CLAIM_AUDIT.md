## Corrected significant seller-package ambiguity

Official Zen12 sales page https://zen12.com/ and About page https://zen12.com/about reviewed October 31, 2026. Seller describes **Beginner = first 6 levels/24 MP3s**, **Premium = all 12 levels/48 MP3s**, **Advanced = last 6 levels/24 MP3s**. Four audio styles are described for each level: guided meditation, nature sounds, white noise, relaxation music. Earlier newsletter claimed "the package contains twelve levels and 48 MP3 files" without qualifying the tier. That could mislead someone considering a smaller package. Revised Issue03 explicitly names all three tiers and preserves one ethical free twelve-minute exercise. These are *seller-advertised inventory*, not independently examined delivered files. No current price or refund promise is embedded.

**Claim → evidence → permitted prose:** "12 minutes gives 1 hour of meditation," automatic brainwave effects and certain focus improvement are vendor efficacy assertions NOT independently verified for Zen12. A general meditation research review at https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/meditation-and-mindfulness-effectiveness-and-safety does not certify Zen12's efficacy or equivalence. We disclose the evidence gap and ask readers to record their own practice observations without calling the routine a guaranteed treatment.

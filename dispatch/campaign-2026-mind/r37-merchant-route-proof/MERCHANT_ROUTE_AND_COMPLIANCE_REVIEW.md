## Copy/conversion assessment against the actual official product page

Zen12 advertises four audio styles and three inventories: Beginner first six levels / 24 MP3s, Premium twelve / 48, Advanced last six / 24. These are **vendor-published specifications**, not inspected files or proof of therapeutic or attentional benefit. The site also claims an hour of meditation in twelve minutes and automatic brainwave gains; Final Vestige must not present those claims as independently established. A good buyer guide should address format and tier fit, full checkout total, recurring billing if any, refunds as actually displayed, support, accessibility/audio options, opportunity cost and the free twelve-minute practice. Do not use seller prices, countdown timers, named testimonials or consumer reviews as independent proof.

For Issue 05, the official Total Focus listing displays a single 33-minute / 47-MB audio and variable-promotional pricing; efficacy language about sharper concentration is advertising, not a validated product-specific result.

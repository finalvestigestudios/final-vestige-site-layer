# R178 | Exact product sales-page claim review — NOT commercial activation

**As-of:** 2026-11-06 PT. **Owner:** Dispatch Issue #174 in coordination with Mind & Affiliate #171. **Release:** Issues 03 and 05 `HOLD_MERCHANT`, all six `HOLD_UNSENT`. Official sales pages provide *seller* claims; they do not prove Final Vestige affiliate acceptance, link eligibility, permitted email channel or any buyer outcome.

## Issue 03 — Zen12 Premium

**Official inspected seller presentation:** https://zen12.com/hnp (search-captured 2026-11-06; no authenticated checkout). Seller describes a 12-level, 12-month program, 48 MP3s across four audio choices: relaxation music, guided meditation, sounds of nature, white noise. The Premium package display is **$87.99**, with a one-year money-back guarantee advertised. The seller says 12 minutes is comparable to one hour of meditation and offers stress, focus and other outcomes.

**What editorial copy can responsibly say:** The seller advertises a structured audio library with twelve levels and multiple styles; individual sessions are described as twelve minutes. A reader can compare formats and consider whether they prefer shorter guided practice. Those are **seller-provided product descriptions**, not independent efficacy conclusions. No approved email CTA yet.

**Must not claim without higher-grade evidence:** one 12-minute session *delivers* an hour of meditation benefits; automatic clinical benefit, instant anxiety relief, medical or cognitive improvement; reliable 'brainwave entrainment'; fulfilled refunds. The listed price is not actual checkout proof and must not enter approved email copy without checkout verification and partner permission.

## Issue 05 — Hypnosis Live Total Focus

**Official exact-product seller page:** https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus . The page advertises a **33-minute / 47-MB** downloadable MP3, credited to J.A. Amos. A site presentation shows **$17.95** sale / $22.95 struck through, and a **14-day** claimed money-back policy. It promises sharper concentration and brain 'reprogramming' through hypnosis/NLP.

**What editorial copy can responsibly say:** It is advertised as a thirty-three-minute audio recording marketed for focus. Some people may prefer a self-directed, repeatable routine while others might prefer a recorded format. Seller descriptions of format and duration do not establish clinical or task-performance efficacy. No approved email CTA yet.

**Must not assert:** limitless powers of concentration, brain reprogramming, 100% on-demand sharpness, productivity benefit, customer testimonials as scientific proof, verified full refund, or the offer's current payable price. The email must distinguish the audio recording from credible controlled research.

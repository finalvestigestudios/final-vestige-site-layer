## Concrete findings that change the buyer-guide review

**Zen12 — complete package vs entry tiers.** The seller advertises a *premium* twelve-level, forty-eight-MP3 package; *beginner* and *advanced* options have six levels and twenty-four MP3s each. All four listening styles are seller-described, not evidence of efficacy. Its public checkout displayed a premium total of **$87.99** on November 2 and a mobile-phone field. That observation establishes neither a permanent price, actual card charge, nor whether entering the phone is compulsory. The seller describes a **one-year refund guarantee**; Inspire3's Terms of Sale add that a card refund after 90 days may be handled manually through PayPal. No money, coupon, countdown, guarantee or phone requirement is promised in the newsletter. Source: https://zen12.com/ ; https://zen12.com/getstarted ; https://inspire3.com/terms/?s=zen12 .

**Total Focus — named item vs FAQ and buried refund cap.** The exact Total Focus page describes a **33-minute, 47 MB** recording and displayed **$24.44**; the general Hypnosis Live FAQ states sessions *typically* cost **$22.95**. The figures concern different representations and should not be treated as a stable, contradictory checkout guarantee. The merchant advertises **14 days** for refunds, but its FAQ and **Inspire3 Terms of Sale explicitly cap refunds at five albums per customer in a twelve-month period**. This material limitation belongs in an independent purchase guide before an outbound recommendation. Source: https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus ; https://hypnosislive.com/faq ; https://inspire3.com/terms/?s=zen12 .

**New publisher privacy consideration.** Inspire3's Privacy Policy says that when someone subscribes or buys through an affiliate link, the seller **may share the individual's name, email address and products purchased with the affiliate**. Its terms also say provision of a cellphone may result in **3–4 promotional SMS messages per week**. This is *possible merchant collection/data sharing*, not proof Final Vestige receives information. Do **not** enable an affiliate link, request such records, or assume the existing Final Vestige privacy policy covers this partner-data flow. Product guide must explain this possibility accurately, and publisher policy/compliance needs review before activation. Source: https://inspire3.com/privacy/ ; https://inspire3.com/terms/?s=zen12 .

**Rights:** The public Inspire3 affiliate recruitment page says successful affiliates receive special links and advertises general commissions; it gives **no named Final Vestige acceptance, authorized earning URL, approval to market Zen12 or Total Focus over email, or permission to reuse swipe/video/audio creative**. Inspire3 Terms of Sale reserve product/site intellectual property and require express written permission for use of materials. Public marketing newsletters received in a consumer inbox do not grant affiliate rights. Source: https://affiliates.inspire3.com/ ; https://inspire3.com/terms/?s=zen12 .

**Claims boundary:** Zen12 seller's "12 minutes = 1 hour," automatic brainwave shift, guaranteed health/focus outcomes, and Total Focus seller's on-demand 100% concentration / brain "reprogramming" are **not independently established for these named products**. Avoid endorsing the seller's testimonials as verified customer outcomes, quoting a transient deal, or implying Final Vestige has tested the audio. Independent NIH overviews of meditation and hypnosis research do not prove these product claims. https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/meditation-and-mindfulness-effectiveness-and-safety ; https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/hypnosis .

## New optional conversion copy, deliberately NOT adopted

The two `issue-03-preview.{txt,html}` and `issue-05-preview.{txt,html}` packages are **original, optional buyer-protection editorial inserts**, not canonical campaign editions and not replacements approved by the founder. Each puts a prospective commission notice immediately at the beginning, offers free practical value, distinguishes seller facts from product-specific efficacy, includes one *disabled* buyer-guide invitation, and exposes relevant purchase/privacy risks. Direct seller research URLs are displayed as literal source references **not actionable shopping calls-to-action**. Source links may be opened manually by an internal reviewer; the static HTML contains no merchant anchors or active purchase links.

The 55-word disclosure envelope is intentional: live Jetpack is configured to send **excerpts**, so a commercial disclosure buried in a full Markdown newsletter may not reach the inbox. These HTML files are **offline**, not rendered by Jetpack, and the excerpts are **not configured in WordPress**. Actual native preview requires a controlled unpublished post and founder-authorized preview procedure; a genuine test send, unsubscribe functionality and physical publisher address remain unverified. See https://wordpress.com/support/newsletter/email-appearance/ and FTC https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business .

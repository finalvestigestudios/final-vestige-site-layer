Suppose you listen to a recording and emerge feeling more determined. Was that a repeatable improvement in attention, a pleasant half-hour break, or a shift in expectation? All three could feel meaningful. Only a record helps tell them apart.

Before spending anything, choose one ordinary task. Set a three-minute timer, note where your attention wanders, then repeat on another day under similar conditions. Do not score yourself on superhuman concentration. Record disruptions honestly. This is not a clinical measure or a test of a product; it is a way of separating your own experience from a sales promise.

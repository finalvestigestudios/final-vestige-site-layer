## Standard of proof

A public seller sales page establishes what the **seller says**—not product efficacy, deliverability of a promotion, checkout prices charged to a specific buyer, copyright permission to reuse audio/video/graphics, commission eligibility, a partner relationship, or permission to promote via *our* email channel. A historical joint-venture invitation or third-party affiliate directory is not current authorization. A consumer marketing email in a private inbox is likewise **not affiliate acceptance**. None of those substitutes for a specific approval record from the publisher's actual partner account or written contract.

### Prices are especially unsafe for email copy

The product pages have surfaced different public promotional price displays across observations. On the Total Focus listing, indexed results displayed **$17.95** alongside **$22.95**, while other captured versions/promotions showed very different campaigns. Zen12 lists promotional product tiers as well. A public banner cannot establish a particular subscriber's final price, currency, taxes, optional upsells, recurring obligations, regional availability or eligibility. **Do not include a specific price, percent-off offer, “free,” or refund guarantee in the commercial newsletter until the actual applicable merchant terms are confirmed at checkout and authorized for this campaign.** These public numbers are research notes, not an offer.

### The affiliate-resource trap — a particularly important distinction

The official [Inspire3 Limitless Labs partner page](https://inspire3.com/ll/) offers email swipe copy and a ClickBank link generator **for Limitless Labs**. The same page explicitly says it only permits *approved affiliates* to promote **that product** and asks partners to request approval. It is **not evidence** of approval to email Zen12 or Total Focus, even though they share a parent company. Do not paste or repurpose Limitless Labs swipe copy into our brand; its availability is not a reusable license.

Third-party affiliate directories and older JV notices may describe commissions, email templates, or historical agreements, but cannot verify *our own* account, current product rights, or legal channel restrictions. No commission percentage or seller conversion estimate belongs in any Dispatch launch claim, dashboard metric or marketing pitch without contemporaneous account-specific evidence.

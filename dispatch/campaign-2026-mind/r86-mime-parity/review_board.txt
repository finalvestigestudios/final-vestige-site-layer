## 01 — EDITORIAL · HOLD_UNSENT
**Subject (optional):** The person changed. The conversation went on.
**Proposed preheader (NOT a verified Jetpack field):** An experiment in seeing, missing, and the stories memory invents.

Imagine speaking to a stranger, then continuing with a different person without noticing. Simons and Levin's 1998 field experiment exposed a gap between looking and registering a change. It doesn't prove every witness is unreliable. It gives us a better question to ask when interpreting evidence.

## 02 — EDITORIAL · HOLD_UNSENT
**Subject (optional):** Before hypnosis had a name
**Proposed preheader:** The strange 1780s accounts and what the record cannot establish.

Puységur's reports invite two easy reactions: believe everything or dismiss everything. Historical work permits neither shortcut. Begin with who wrote an account, when they wrote it, and which observations survive. The document matters even when its author understood the cause differently from modern researchers.

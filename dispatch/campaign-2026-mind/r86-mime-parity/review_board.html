The person changed. The conversation went on.

An experiment in seeing, missing, and the stories memory invents.

Imagine speaking to a stranger, then continuing with a different person without noticing. Simons and Levin's 1998 field experiment exposed a gap between looking and registering a change. It doesn't prove every witness is unreliable. It gives us a better question to ask when interpreting evidence.

Before hypnosis had a name

The strange 1780s accounts and what the record cannot establish.

Puységur's reports invite two easy reactions: believe everything or dismiss everything. Historical work permits neither shortcut. 04 / EDITORIAL · HOLD_UNSENT

Apollo 1: one spark was never the whole story

The official investigation separates known hazards from an uncertain ignition.

A simple cause makes a gripping headline. Apollo 1's investigation resists it: oxygen, combustible cabin materials, escape constraints and test conditions all mattered. The precise initiating spark remained uncertain. We should not replace that careful finding with a dramatic but unsupported countdown.

The person changed. The conversation went on.

Planning preheader: In a 1998 field experiment, an unnoticed substitution exposed a gap in what we register. Jetpack preheader control unverified.

A stranger asks for directions. Workers carry a door through your conversation. When it passes, a different person stands there. Would you notice?

Simons and Levin staged a substitution in a 1998 field study. In their first experiment, about half the pedestrians noticed. That describes a particular experimental situation, not a general rate for every eyewitness.

A free exercise: before entering a familiar room, note five objects and where you think they are. Check afterward. This is informal self-observation, not a validated laboratory or clinical test.

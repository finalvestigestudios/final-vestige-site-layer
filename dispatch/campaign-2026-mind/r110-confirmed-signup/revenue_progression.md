## Measurement contract and stop rules

For each 7- and 30-day cohort, report source asset/topic, qualified-human sessions, first-party landing visits, first-party CTA clicks, exact confirmed native email opt-ins, email-reachable subscribers, actual sends/deliveries (if any), unsubscribes, merchant-confirmed paid customers/orders, gross receipts, verified fees, refunds, and realized net revenue. Unknown values remain **null**, never zero. `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions * 1000` is valid **only** with independently verified numerator, denominator and attribution; avoid performance comparisons on two subscribers or fewer than 20 qualified sessions. Opens and clicks are diagnostic, not purchases. A newsletter is not declared a commercial success because it has an aesthetic layout, pages created, subscribers or clickthroughs.

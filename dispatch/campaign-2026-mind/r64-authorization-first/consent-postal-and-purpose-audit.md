## CAN-SPAM physical postal address: must be FV's
For commercial mail, include a **valid physical postal address for the sender** (current street address, properly registered USPS PO Box or qualifying accurately registered commercial private mailbox). The business may use a qualifying mailbox to avoid publishing a home address, but **must verify the registration and right to use it**. Neither an Automattic/WordPress postal address seen in a platform confirmation, the Inspire3 affiliate footer at 20–22 Wenlock Street, London, nor an Inspire3 LLC US address found on a merchant page belongs automatically to Final Vestige as sender. No Final Vestige qualifying postal address is verified in this packet. Do not place the founder's residential address in public repo content.

The legal requirement is not to display the address in a GitHub test artifact; it is to ensure the **actual commercial email** delivered to recipients carries a qualifying address. A mere placeholder or page-only address does not satisfy that.

## Opt-out test
Include a visible and functioning way for recipients to stop future commercial email; keep it active for at least **30 days after each send**, and honor opt-outs within **10 business days**. Avoid a claim that CAN-SPAM requires one-click for every message; the FTC accepts an email reply or another easy internet method and forbids charging fees, asking for excessive information or extra steps beyond a reply or single page. Verify the actual Jetpack footer and permitted test-token functionality, do not infer operation from website's 'Unsubscribe anytime' sentence.

FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business .

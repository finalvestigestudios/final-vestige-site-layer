## A new and distinct missing capability

We have multiple *editorial HTML mockups* and WordPress diagnostics, but still **no authenticated received `message/rfc822` of an actual Final Vestige campaign email** against which to inspect MIME presentation and footers. That distinction matters especially in Jetpack's **excerpt** mode: the original article's disclosure, CTA or legal placeholders may not survive its generated excerpt. This new read-only Python inspector accepts a **private local .eml** file, examines the real plaintext/HTML MIME alternatives, and emits ONLY non-identifying booleans and controlled labels. It never contacts WordPress, a subscriber, an affiliate, a merchant, or a URL. It cannot authorize publication or sending.

A message containing an unsub link is **not** proof that its opt-out endpoint works. A printed address is **not** proof that the sender is entitled to use it. A delivered confirmation is **not** a welcome delivery, and a synthetic email is **not** a real subscriber receipt. All release fields remain permanently fail-closed pending *separate human review*. The inspector deliberately NEVER emits raw subject, sender/recipient, contact identifiers, body copy, hrefs, embedded tracking tokens, or postal addresses. Do not commit any actual .eml, forwarded mail, screenshots showing inbox addresses, purchaser exports, or postal phrase to this public repository.

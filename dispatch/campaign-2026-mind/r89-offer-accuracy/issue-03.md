## An appealing unit of time

Twelve minutes fits into a crowded morning. That is a genuine advantage of a short routine, but a timer measures duration, not depth of meditation. A seller's claim that twelve minutes of its audio equals an hour of practice would need product-specific comparative evidence. We have not found evidence establishing that result.

## A free, three-session baseline

On three nonconsecutive days, choose twelve uninterrupted minutes. Note the time and a task you want to return to afterward. Sit comfortably and attend to a neutral anchor such as breathing. If your attention wanders, notice it and return. After the session, record whether you finished, what disrupted you, whether you liked the format and what you would change. If you miss a day, record the missed session rather than rewriting the log. This is a personal preference experiment, not a medical or scientific trial.

## What the merchant actually sells

Zen12's public offer describes twelve-minute MP3 audio in guided meditation, relaxation music, nature sounds and white-noise styles. It distinguishes an introductory six-level/24-MP3 option from a twelve-level/48-MP3 package. These are the merchant's descriptions, not independent validation of its advertised effects. A purchase decision would also require checking the current package, available demo, price, access conditions and refund terms at checkout.

## The claim we will not borrow

The vendor advertises brainwave entrainment, automatic deep meditation and broad health or focus benefits. Research on meditation in general cannot establish these outcomes for a particular commercial audio collection. We will not promise treatment of a medical condition, a faster route to mastery or a guaranteed change in attention.

## A better buying question

Would a guided format make the routine more inviting than a free timer, a quiet room or another no-cost audio? Someone who prefers structure might reasonably compare formats; someone happy with a timer may not need a paid recording. Neither answer requires purchasing something today. The evidence guide is being prepared, but it is not published or ready for an earning link.

## 01 — The mind is not a camera (editorial)

**Reader problem:** How can a sincere witness miss a conspicuous change? Open with an ordinary exchange rather than “your brain is lying.” The 1998 Simons–Levin door-swap field study observes *specific participants under specific conditions*. Its reported rate cannot establish that half of all historical witnesses lie or misremember.

**Source-led promise:** “We tend to protect details that fit our expectations; the record may contain the evidence our own account missed.” **Reader can do:** compare a familiar image's visible features against the caption, and label observation versus inference. **One web CTA (approval pending):** the Missing Caption investigation. Avoid inviting an unpublished /mind/ hub. **Editorial improvement:** give the observation/check exercise before any signup suggestion; a historical source is the climax, not a product.

**Subject:** keep original “The mind is not a camera” (conditional founder selection). **Inboxing caveat:** original `preheader` planning string is not independently controllable by Jetpack. **Source:** Simons and Levin, *Failure to detect changes to people during a real-world interaction* (Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 1998), doi:10.3758/BF03208840. Verify full text and its group design before final quotations.

## 02 — Before hypnosis had a name (editorial)

**Reader problem:** One person's documentary evidence may be another person's proof of a mechanism that never existed. Puységur's 1784 *reported* session is a claim from the historical record, not proof of animal magnetism. The source's May 4 described encounter and May 8 letter need separate labels; a disputed five vs seven-and-a-half minute retrospective duration should remain visible if used. The older first-mile text lacks this nuance; the more detailed R63 alternative remains unapproved.

**Source-led promise:** “An extraordinary account gets more interesting when we stop pretending the witness and the investigator are the same person.” **Reader can do:** make three columns—what was reported, what was inferred, what independent corroboration would require. **One web CTA:** published Puységur archive article; never link to draft Mind sales guide. **Subject:** retain original, pending source image review; no false physiological cure suggestion.

## 04 — Apollo 1: why one spark was never the whole story (editorial)

**Reader problem:** A dramatic radio timestamp is tempting to frame as a stopwatch for disaster. NASA's Apollo 204 board distinguishes a 17.1-second *radio-record interval* composed of 10.3 seconds of transmission and 6.8 seconds of silence. Neither “Fourteen seconds” (first-mile) nor “Seventeen seconds” (stacked) explains what the figure actually represents; the fire's full duration and crew experience cannot be inferred from it.

**Source-led promise:** “An investigation can establish a chain of conditions without identifying one initiating spark.” Show cabin atmosphere, materials, hatch design, and communication records in a causal chain; distinguish findings from hypothetical ignition. **One CTA:** the **existing** Hatch Opened Inward investigation. **Proposed nonnumeric subject requires founder decision**; it is not automatically substituted.

**Primary source:** https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/Apollo204/invest.html .

## 06 — What did seven days of attention reveal? (editorial)

**Reader problem:** An undated reconstruction of our week is too easy to mistake for a contemporaneous observation. Stone and colleagues' electronic-diary compliance research provides an instructive example **in a particular clinical/diary-study population**, not a universal deception statistic. It does not demonstrate a specific Focus Fieldbook efficacy outcome.

**Source-led promise:** “A notebook is evidence only of what the notebook actually records.” Ask the reader to write *date/time, task, interference, what changed*, and check it after seven days without retrofilling gaps. **One CTA:** published 52-page Focus Fieldbook at https://finalvestige.com/focus/, the existing lead magnet rather than a new list or different service.

**Source:** https://www.bmj.com/content/324/7347/1193 . Preserve population/context and distinguish claimed vs detected timing.

## Minimal measurement contract
- **Entry:** `utm_source=youtube|tiktok|other_verified_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=fv_<campaign_id>`, `asset_id=<non-personal asset reference>`; do not generate hundreds of links until Grok's existing TASK-008 convention is confirmed.
- **Landing:** count a qualified-human session only from the existing consent-aware instrumentation after bot/internal filtering. Distinguish site clicks and CTA engagement from business outcomes.
- **One `confirmed_signup` event:** emit only on first externally verified, consent-compatible **provider transition** to confirmed, not on form submit. Exclude duplicate attempts, downloads, Reader follows and previously confirmed addresses. Retain identity linkage privately at provider; publish aggregate counts only.
- **Email CTA:** campaign `fv_dispatch_pilot`, issue number `01..06`, link purpose `editorial|approved_guide`; preserve the original content path, no cloaking, no merchant redirect, no extra tracking pixel. Enrich existing instrumentation only after engineering approval.
- **Paid outcome:** a verified order/affiliate settlement ID from the real merchant report, actual gross, refunds, fees, currency and attributed source. A click or signup is not a sale. Review reversals before 7-/30-day cohort maturation.
- **KPI:** `1000 * (gross_paid - refunds - platform_or_affiliate_fees) / verified_qualified_human_sessions` only if complete, positive denominator and independently reconciled transactions. If missing, **NOT COMPUTABLE**. Keep tiny sample warnings; no fabricated uplift or significance.

## Why this change is worth considering

Original Issue 06 currently sends already-subscribed readers back to /focus/, which primarily asks for email signup. This is a **design hypothesis about CTA mismatch**, not observed conversion data. A retained subscriber should receive new evidence-led value after the welcome gift. This candidate uses an independently read-only verified published change-blindness investigation, including the author's original demonstration and 1998 primary study, as its ONE main content CTA.

Research boundaries: Simons and Levin's first 1998 door experiment involved 15 participants, of whom 7 noticed the swap. The small group is not a population estimate. We do NOT claim a seven-day Fieldbook practice improves cognition, change-blindness scores, or commercial-audio effectiveness. All seven-day log advice is self-observation, not clinical treatment or a controlled trial.

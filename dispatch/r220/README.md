# Dispatch R220 — source-of-signup ambiguity and commercial consent audit (UNSENT)

**Purpose:** Stop Final Vestige from crediting its Focus page or Grok social content for a lead solely because Jetpack's site-wide subscriber counter changes. All changes in this directory are source-controlled, non-sending research/QA. They are NOT a deployed analytics SDK, recipient database, WordPress draft, approved affiliate campaign, or permission to publish.

## Newly identified live multi-entrypoint confounder — November 8, 2026

Authenticated WordPress.com `get_settings` at 23:34 UTC showed **site-wide post-end Subscribe blocks ENABLED** and **site comment-form Subscribe checkbox ENABLED**. The distinct **notify me of new comments** toggle is also ON, but a comment notification is *not* a site newsletter subscription. Independent WordPress `/focus/` and `/dispatch/` both contain a Jetpack Subscribe block. Separate `wpcom_jetpack_stats_followers` showed **3 WordPress.com/Reader followers and 0 email-only followers**. Do not sum these with newsletter dashboard **3 total / 3 confirmed / 2 unconfirmed requests**. Actual deliverable-email audience is UNKNOWN. The default publish-email toggle remains OFF and FULL emails plus featured images are configured.

**Impact:** A change in the sitewide confirmed count can originate in at least four front-door experiences (Focus, Dispatch, end-of-post, comment form), or account-level Reader actions. A public `?subscribe=...` query, gift reveal, PDF click, comment subscription checkbox, WordPress.com Reader follower, or post-end form submit is not proof that Grok → Focus generated a new provider-confirmed email lead. This is a measurement **attribution risk**, not evidence of fabricated metrics or noncompliant subscriber behavior.

## The new, material discovery: apparently contradictory Total Focus pricing
**Merchant:** [Total Focus product page](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus), a **33-minute, 47 MB** single MP3 according to the seller. The product page as retrieved on November 13, 2026 displayed **$24.44 promotional / $29.95 list**, while an earlier web index surfaced **$17.95 / $22.95**, and the R348 audit recorded a **$2 promotion**. These observations are not the same thing as a first-party authenticated Final Vestige-referred checkout, and may reflect promotions, geographies, page variants, temporal variation or caching. **Do not pick any price and do not say the price changed in the live checkout.** The new offline gate detects conflicting public observations and freezes all exact-price language until Mind/Commerce provides a timestamped accepted-link and checkout verification.

**Refund:** Seller [FAQ](https://hypnosislive.com/faq) states requests within 14 days and a limit of **five refunded sessions per year**. This is a seller-described policy, not independent evidence of a customer's actual refund. A general 14-day promise without the qualification risks materially misleading the reader.

**Zen12:** [Official product page](https://zen12.com/) describes 12 levels, four styles per level (48 MP3s), 12-minute sessions, and a one-year money-back promise. It also claims an hour of advanced meditation benefits in 12 minutes and uses brainwave terminology. These are vendor assertions, not independently validated results for Zen12. A [coupon sales page](https://zen12.com/secret/) displays a different offer path; **do not transpose its coupon price to Premium** or repeat the coupon in Dispatch. No actual checkout, account-issued earning URL, named affiliate acceptance or permission to market by email was verified.

Preserve tags through Focus to downstream pages where technically and privacy-appropriately possible; avoid bespoke tracking scripts. A `confirmed_signup` requires an **authoritative Jetpack/WordPress confirmation**, not a page event or PDF download; only one confirmation per pseudonymous identifier. Actual recipient list still unknown. Independent reported settled commissions or paid FV digital orders plus merchant fees/refunds produce the realized numerator; privacy-preserving qualified-human sessions produce the denominator. Report 7-day and 30-day cohorts with small-N uncertainty; mark both NOT COMPUTABLE now.

**Net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** = 1000 × (settled net revenue after refunds and fees ÷ independently verified qualified human sessions). A verified zero requires a genuinely complete merchant zero-order report and a verified denominator. No proof ≠ zero sales.

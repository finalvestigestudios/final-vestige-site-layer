## Editorial copy rule (all six)
- **Hook:** named human question tied to a record or test. No shocking guarantees or fabricated first-person testimonials.
- **Value before sale:** primary sources, limits of evidence, how to verify for oneself. No medical or treatment claims.
- **One primary CTA:** link to a fully published **first-party** resource that meets intent. For issue 03/05, no active URL until Mind/Commerce verifies offer and guide.
- **Subjects/preheaders:** render as proposed editorial copy; Jetpack does not provide a verified distinct preheader field in this site configuration. Do not label an offline snippet as an inbox-tested preview.
- **Accuracy and accessibility:** plain text and HTML need the same essential claims, source links, optionality and near-offer disclosure. Native rendered email, footer postal identity and functional unsubscribe are still unsatisfied.
- **Conversion:** The subscriber-to-customer objective is more important than opens/clicks. To avoid measurable reputation damage with tiny unverified audience, keep all six unactivated until the legal/provider/merchant gates are closed.

**Recommendation for founder's future creative review:** adopt R348's Apollo-specific subject before any mail is approved; consider resolving the duplicated Issue 01/02 CTAs and Issue 06 resubscription CTA. These are suggestions only, not adopted editions. Prioritize valid partner EMAIL rights and publisher address over another copy variant.

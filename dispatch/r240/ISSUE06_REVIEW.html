Seven days aren't a verdict

A recovered notebook can be more useful than its author's grandest conclusion. It tells you what was written down at the time—not merely how the story felt afterward.

Perhaps you tried the twelve-minute starting routine in the Seven Day Focus Fieldbook. Perhaps you downloaded it and life intervened. Neither outcome deserves a victory speech or a guilty one.

Try three columns on one page: attempted, observed, unknown. Note what you meant to do, what actually happened, and what you cannot fairly attribute to the routine. “I started twice in four days” is a record. “This changed my brain” is not.

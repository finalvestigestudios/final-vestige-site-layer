## First purchase hypothesis: Zen12 Premium (ONE candidate only)
**Audience hypothesis:** A reader who completed the free Seven Day Focus Fieldbook and voluntarily explored shorter meditation routines may be interested in a structured, self-directed audio library. This is not inferred from a PDF click alone; subscriber opt-in and separately observed topic intent must remain distinct. The alternative is to continue the free routine; no subscription/paywall or compulsory purchase.

**Seller source:** https://zen12.com/ (checked 2026-11-13). The seller presents Beginner as first six levels / 24 MP3s, Premium all twelve levels / 48 MP3s, Advanced last six levels / 24 MP3s. It advertises a one-year money-back guarantee, but this is **seller-reported** until exact final checkout and relevant terms are inspected. The seller's hour-equivalence and "more focus" rhetoric are marketing claims, not product-specific independently proven outcomes. The offer must never claim clinical benefit, guaranteed focus, equal efficacy to one-hour meditation, or personal testing that never occurred.

**Separate free-sample relationship:** https://zen12.com/welcome and https://zen12.com/gift visibly invite a free audio signup and disclose the merchant's **own newsletter**. That consent is distinct from Final Vestige's Jetpack Dispatch. Do not quietly send existing Dispatch subscribers to a second form as though they are merely downloading our Fieldbook, and never attribute a merchant signup to a Final Vestige confirmed lead.

**Authorized warmup requirement:** Mail #03 may link to a first-party product-specific comparison guide only once #171 supplies an actually published, independently vetted page, exact approved earning link, affiliate terms and channel authorization. The guide should transparently identify (1) file format/levels, (2) actual purchase terms on day of launch, (3) claims not independently established, (4) a free twelve-minute control comparison, (5) affiliate disclosure immediately near the outbound link, (6) the merchant's second mailing-list consent when mentioning free audio, and (7) voluntary no-purchase alternative. Until then issue #03 retains its literal nonclickable CTA token.

## Audit secondary reserve candidate: Hypnosis Live *Total Focus*
Exact seller product page: https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus . Seller-reported audio is **33 minutes / 47 MB**. A fresh web retrieval on 2026-11-13 surfaced a **$17.95 sale display**, whereas a previous authenticated review in Dispatch R328 observed **$24.44**. These are different observations across time/representation, not evidence that anyone could complete checkout at either price. A third cached price may exist. **Do not put a price, savings, coupon, urgency or refund promise in the subject, body, linked guide or earnings forecast** until fresh matched-currency checkout and terms proof is obtained for the exact destination. Product claims like reprogramming the brain or 100% concentration are seller marketing, not independent findings.

## Copy-conversion decisions

Questions in #03 should focus on expected included files, ease of trying the free routine and realistic limitations. **One** relevant CTA, to a verified warm-up guide, not direct forced checkout. Commercial disclosure beside the guide CTA: "If you purchase through a qualifying link in that guide, Final Vestige may earn a commission."

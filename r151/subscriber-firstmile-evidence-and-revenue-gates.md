**Separate WordPress follower endpoint same check:** total=3, `wpcom=3`, `email=0`, comment=0, publicize empty, partial=false.
**Key nuance from WordPress's current official documentation** (last reviewed November 4, 2026): when a reader confirms a subscription, WordPress.com creates/uses a WordPress.com account; the former "email-only" classification is no longer a complete census of email recipients. Thus `email=0` is NOT evidence that no one is email-eligible. Conversely, `wpcom=3` / dashboard confirmed=3 is NOT evidence three people currently receive email, nor evidence of three new `confirmed_signup` events. The two unconfirmed rows are not additive. Only the native Subscribers tab's **Email subscription** status, with authoritative provider timestamps/consent metadata and recipient-specific delivery flags, can reconcile eligibility. **No subscriber addresses should leave the provider; no list export/import is authorized here.**
Primary official docs: https://jetpack.com/support/newsletter/ and https://wordpress.com/support/subscribers/ . The latter distinguishes `Subscribed`, `Not subscribed` (Reader-only), `Not confirmed`, `Bounced`, `Emails paused`, `Not sending` and explains why totals may include social/Reader followers. A "confirmed" dashboard field must NOT be reinterpreted as "currently receiving email" without those per-person flags.

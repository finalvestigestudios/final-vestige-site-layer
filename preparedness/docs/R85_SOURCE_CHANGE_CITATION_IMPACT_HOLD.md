## Engineering purpose

A citation can retain the same literal source ID and occurrence rank after its underlying source record, URL, or surrounding claim changes. Matching citation IDs do not demonstrate claim support or modern emergency safety. R85 produces a deterministic human-review docket of these dependencies. It is neither a citation verification verdict nor a publisher.

The tool reconstructs the complete original frozen/integrated/current/RC source snapshots through the sealed R82–R84 chain, retains unabridged source readings in each stage, and preserves original source identity scoped by contributor and literal ID. It rejects changed record digests, URL substitution, duplicated same-stage origins, omitted source edges, altered original citation anchor digests, and forged human approvals. Same-URL records from different research origins remain separate.

Each R83 current-to-RC occurrence-rank comparison joins its full original claim context and section placement to the exact current/RC R84 source variants. It flags changed original reading hashes, changed literal URLs, changed surrounding claims with unchanged citations, ambiguous origin variants and outside-scope references. Flag means **human review candidate, not confirmed error or unsafe instruction**. The unabridged R80 offline reading-room custody still covers **35 articles, six draft tools, 244 complete source readings, 2,173 citations, 2,359 origin-scoped source-variant edges and 4,070 complete DOM blocks**; R85's seven-contributor (29-author-article) historical scope is an additional narrower reconciliation. The 540 historical citations outside that narrow scope are not automatically unsupported.

The ledger is cryptographically sealed to detect mutation for **custody only**. Optional formula-safe CSV is produced only in the temporary runner directory and deleted; it must never become a GitHub public artifact, published article, WordPress/CMS page or email disclosure.

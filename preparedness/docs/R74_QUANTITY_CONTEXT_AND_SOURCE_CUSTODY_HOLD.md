## Distinct engineering lift

R73 identified 975 passages with topical action-language cues. Quantity-bearing guidance can also occur outside that lexical queue. A numeric claim is consequential only after a reviewer checks its exact subject, units, qualifications, reading scope, jurisdiction, formulation, current authority and claim support. An adjacent footnote is not evidence of support.

R74 examines **all 4,070 complete candidate DOM text blocks**, not just the 975 R73 packets. It finds likely numbers with explicit units and records exact Unicode offsets and entire text. It never converts physical units, infers recommended amounts, corrects dosages, or asserts that different numbers are contradictory. Simple singular/plural spelling is used for HUMAN REVIEW ROUTING ONLY.

Cross-document groups are flagged only when a common lexical topic/unit appears in at least two separate document identities with at least two different literal numeric tokens. Even then, groups do NOT establish comparable conditions, substances, ratios, units, risk or an actual defect. Regex cues have false positives and false negatives.

Each packet retains its exact block text, source citation ordinals, origin-scoped reading variants, complete reading SHA and exact URL (no aliasing), adjacent-citation hints, keyword topic and pending human/QD review states. R74 also preserves the entire parent evidence: 244 candidate source readings, 2,173 citations, 4,070 DOM blocks, 35 articles, six tools, all 975 action triage packets, original document/block order, shared URL/ID collision inventories, uncited readings and unattached citations. The historical 35+1 document baseline has 1,959 citations and is NOT silently compared as if it were the same document version.

Source identities retain contributor origin, exact URL and complete reading SHA; citation ordinals and source variants are checked without treating shared URLs or reused IDs as aliases.

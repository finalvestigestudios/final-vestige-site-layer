## Decision-changing source finding: two distinct safety questions, plus an unresolved storage-scope mismatch

1. **Microorganisms surviving drying:** [Utah State University Extension (Jan 23, 2026)](https://extension.usu.edu/foodsafety/research/can-i-freeze-dry-that-a-practical-guide-to-safe-and-effective-freeze-drying) was directly read Nov 18. Its food-safety fact sheet confirms that freeze-drying can preserve viable foodborne bacteria. Lowered moisture is not a validated kill step. This is **not** evidence that freezing wild game kills freeze-resistant parasites.
2. **Wild-game parasite boundary:** [NCHFP Jerky](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/meat-and-poultry/jerky/) was directly read Nov 18; its treatment language for pork/**wild game** must not be combined with [CDC trichinellosis prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/trichinellosis/prevention/index.html) (Mar 12, 2024; directly read) or [CDC 2024 MMWR outbreak report](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/73/wr/mm7320a2.htm) (May 23, 2024; directly read) into an all-species household process. CDC documents viable freeze-resistant Trichinella in frozen wild-game meat. **Keep times/temperatures and original processing instructions out of all public drafts pending qualified adjudication.**
3. **Separate and NEW storage-duration scope discrepancy:** NCHFP Jerky (based on *So Easy to Preserve*, 6th ed., 2014) states that properly dried jerky can be kept **two weeks** at room temperature in a sealed container. USDA FSIS's [official consumer answer](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/Is-jerky-safe-when-stored-at-room-temperature), indexed as updated **May 22, 2024**, instead discusses **two months for homemade jerky** and **twelve months for federally inspected commercial jerky**. **Direct FSIS page fetching timed out repeatedly Nov 18**; this is OFFICIAL INDEXED TEXT, **not direct verification**. Different assumptions on process, moisture and packaging may account for the difference. Neither time may be assigned to an unknown batch, and this is an **open qualified-review question**, not proof either source is wrong. **No numeric storage life appears in the proposed reader copy.**
4. **Package appearance does not validate preservation:** [FSIS vacuum-package Q&A](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/Can-I-keep-meat-in-vacuum-packages-at-room-temperature) likewise remained indexed-only after direct timeouts; it distinguishes perishable vacuum-packed meat from appropriately dry nonperishable food. [FoodSafety.gov FoodKeeper](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/foodkeeper-app) was directly read Nov 18 (last reviewed Apr 26, 2019); explicitly a freshness and storage quality resource, **not** safety clearance for unlabeled home meat or a substitute for an official warning. [FoodSafety.gov disaster food planning](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/food-safety-in-disaster-or-emergency), direct Nov 18, says a 3-day general emergency food stock, no specialty dehydrated-food purchase required.

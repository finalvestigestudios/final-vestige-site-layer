## New, directly checked authoritative evidence
- **USDA Agricultural Research Service (2024-08-02), full official original directly read 2026-11-17:** [PQA+ commercial-pork surveillance](https://www.ars.usda.gov/news-events/news/research-news/2024/usda-research-documents-absence-of-trichinella-infection-in-commercial-pigs/). The report documents testing **3,208,643 pigs raised under the U.S. Pork Quality Assurance Plus management program** and detection of **zero Trichinella** in this tested population. This is strong, *bounded surveillance* evidence about a managed-production context; **NOT** a claim of zero parasite risk in all pork, wild game, all foodborne hazards, or any single household batch, and **NOT** a validated freezer/dehydration recipe.
- **NCHFP Jerky (2014 book-derived)**, [full original](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/recipes/jerky/) read 2026-11-17, groups pork and wild game in a household-freezing paragraph, without resolving parasite species. **CDC public prevention** ([2024-03-12 original](https://www.cdc.gov/trichinellosis/prevention/index.html), directly read 2026-11-17) warns freezing wild game may not eliminate freeze-resistant parasites.
- **USDA FSIS Q&A [Does freezing destroy parasites?](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/Does-freezing-destroy-parasites)**, indexed as last updated **2025-02-03**, explicitly warns against relying on home freezing for trichina, but the full official source returned **HTTP 502** on 2026-11-17. **INDEXED-ONLY: DO NOT characterize full primary context as verified or use as a self-standing procedure.** Qualified reviewer must resolve this additional NCHFP/USDA applicability difference.
- Two **distinct** CDC outbreak studies remain verified: [MMWR 2024](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/73/wr/mm7320a2.htm) on 2022 bear meal (*T. nativa*, freezer temperature unknown) versus [EID June/July 2026](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/32/7/26-0062_article) on **2024 NC jerky** (*T. spiralis*, consumed jerky not frozen prior to preparation, weak larval motility not proof of infectivity).

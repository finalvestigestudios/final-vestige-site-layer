### Exact bounded subsection for comparison (NOT a household method)

> **One drying step does not establish a safe heat treatment**
>
> A dehydrator can remove water without answering another question: what actually happened to the disease-causing organisms that may have been present when the meat was raw? The answer cannot be read from a brown surface, a leathery bend, or the length of time a machine was running. A drying endpoint and a validated pathogen-control step are different kinds of evidence.
>
> Even current university references describe their heat-treatment sequences differently. The [University of Minnesota Extension home-drying page, reviewed in 2026](https://extension.umn.edu/food/preparing/cooking-at-home/food-preservation/drying), describes an oven treatment *after* drying jerky. The [National Center for Home Food Preservation's 2014-derived jerky chapter](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/recipes/jerky/) discusses alternatives involving heat *before* drying and heat *after* drying, with different process conditions. An [official USDA FSIS Q&A indexed as last updated February 4, 2025](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/How-do-I-dry-jerky) emphasizes heat *before* dehydration; its full original could not be retrieved in this review, so we do not treat the search excerpt as a fully verified procedure. [Penn State Extension](https://extension.psu.edu/lets-preserve-meat-and-poultry) independently describes that USDA-preheating emphasis in an indexed page, but its original was also blocked from direct retrieval.
>
> This is a difference in *described sequence*, not evidence that the publications are interchangeable, that one of the tested methods has been disproved, or that a reader can assemble a safer method by taking a step from each. The exact food, cut, equipment, sequence, internal measurement and storage conditions matter. A qualified food-safety reviewer must determine which complete validated process and current agency interpretation, if any, may be presented as household guidance. This article supplies no treatment schedule or permission to eat a homemade batch.

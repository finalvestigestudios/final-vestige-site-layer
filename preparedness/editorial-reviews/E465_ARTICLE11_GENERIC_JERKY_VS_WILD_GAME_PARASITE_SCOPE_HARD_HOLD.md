## Independent official-source comparison

1. [USDA FSIS Ask USDA, *How can I make meat or poultry jerky at home?*](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/How-do-I-dry-jerky), updated **2025-02-04**: generic USDA consumer meat/poultry jerky guidance. **Official search-indexed full text visible; direct article request timed out** this run — NOT directly full-page verified.
2. [CDC *How to Prevent Trichinellosis*](https://www.cdc.gov/trichinellosis/prevention/index.html), updated **2024-03-12**, directly read: pork example distinct from the explicit warning that some wild-game parasites resist freezing. Drying/smoking alone not consistently effective for Trichinella.
3. [CDC *MMWR*, 2022 cross-state bear-meat outbreak](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/73/wr/mm7320a2.htm), **published 2024-05-23**, directly read: freeze-resistant *T. nativa* in previously frozen meat, illness also among vegetable-only diners, and an outbreak-related parasite temperature figure **higher than generic USDA jerky meat guidance**. Source-specific guidance is **not** a validated homemade jerky protocol.
4. [CDC *Emerging Infectious Diseases*, 2024 North Carolina bear jerky](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/32/7/26-0062_article), **released online 2026-06-24, July 2026 issue**, directly read: implicated eaten jerky had **not** been frozen; report also cites a higher parasite threshold than the generic jerky FAQ. Do not misstate motile larvae in **separate frozen cuts** as proof of infectivity.
5. [NCHFP *Jerky*](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/meat-and-poultry/jerky/), based on 2014 edition, directly read: groups pork/wild game in a freezer process, but CDC species caveat and E464's 2011-vs-2014 version divergence prevent universal use.
6. [USDA FSIS *Does freezing destroy parasites?*](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/Does-freezing-destroy-parasites), updated **2025-02-03**, official search-indexed text says **home freezing should not be relied on against trichina**; the direct article request timed out. The Integration R472 agent has already identified this source; this memo does **not** claim that evidence as a new integration discovery.

**Interpretation:** These claims may concern **different endpoints (bacterial vs parasite), products and animal species**, rather than a literal contradictory agency rule. A general USDA jerky recipe must not be inferred to kill every wild-game parasite. Editorial is NOT choosing a different heat number or a new household method.

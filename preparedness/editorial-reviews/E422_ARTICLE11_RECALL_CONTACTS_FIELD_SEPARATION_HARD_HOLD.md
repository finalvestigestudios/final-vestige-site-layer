> **When the package identity or recall match is uncertain:** Do not turn a missing or unreadable code into a negative finding. For USDA FSIS-regulated meat, poultry or processed egg products, the [federal reporting directory](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-poisoning/report-problem-with-food) lists the USDA Meat and Poultry Hotline, **1-888-674-6854**. For FDA-regulated foods, the [FDA Human Foods Program](https://www.fda.gov/food/resources-you-food/get-assistance-fda-human-foods-program-hfp) lists **1-888-723-3366** and an official web inquiry. The FDA page gives current contact hours; check them before calling. Ask the relevant agency about the exact product and notice rather than assuming a match, nonmatch or safe-to-eat verdict. Restaurant and retailer problems may belong with state or local authorities. Record only the agency contact route and date in this offline worksheet, not names, addresses or medical details. If illness is suspected, seek clinical guidance and contact the local health department as FoodSafety.gov directs. This is a referral, not a substitute for the notice's instructions.

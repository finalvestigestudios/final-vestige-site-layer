Both current original legal sources were retrieved directly on November 17:

- **9 CFR 317.2 — meat labeling / ingredient declarations:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/chapter-III/subchapter-A/part-317/subpart-A/section-317.2
- **9 CFR 381.118 — poultry ingredient statements:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/chapter-III/subchapter-A/part-381/subpart-N/section-381.118

Both eCFR pages report Title 9 current **as of November 16, 2026**. The official eCFR is authoritative but unofficial, and qualified food/allergen applicability review remains necessary.

## Significant source-scope disagreement requiring qualified food-safety review

- **National Center for Home Food Preservation (NCHFP), University of Georgia** — [Jerky](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/recipes/jerky/), explicitly extracted from *So Easy to Preserve*, sixth edition (2014). Original page directly read November 17, 2026. Its parasite-control discussion contains a freezing statement covering both **pork and wild game**; it also includes conditional treatment, drying and shelf-life instructions. That sentence is **NOT** a reliable all-species, all-game home-freezer prescription. No recipe parameters or timings are reproduced here.
- **CDC** — [How to Prevent Trichinellosis](https://www.cdc.gov/trichinellosis/prevention/index.html), March 12, 2024, directly read November 17, 2026. CDC explicitly warns that freezing wild-game meat may not kill all worms because some *Trichinella* species are freeze-resistant. CDC also warns that curing, drying and smoking alone do not consistently kill the parasites.
- **CDC MMWR** — [Outbreak of Human Trichinellosis—Arizona, Minnesota, and South Dakota, 2022](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/73/wr/mm7320a2.htm), published May 23, 2024, directly read November 17, 2026. Six cases after inadequately cooked bear meat harvested in northern Saskatchewan, Canada; remaining bear meat contained motile *T. nativa* after 110 days frozen at **unknown household freezer temperature**. This demonstrates the danger of treating freezer time as a universal parasite-control certificate; it does not measure efficacy of every freezer or implicate freezing as the only failure.
- **CDC Emerging Infectious Diseases** — [Trichinellosis Outbreak Linked to Undercooked Bear Jerky, North Carolina, USA, 2024](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/32/7/26-0062_article), online release June 24, 2026 / July 2026 issue, directly read November 17, 2026. Three cases among six who ate implicated bear jerky; the jerky was marinated/dried **without previous freezing**. This is **NOT a failed-freezing trial**. Its slight postfreezing larval motility finding alone is not proof of infectivity.
- **USDA FSIS** — [Does freezing destroy parasites?](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/Does-freezing-destroy-parasites), indexed official result last updated Feb. 3, 2025. Full page timed out November 17, 2026. **INDEXED ONLY; not used as directly checked consumer guidance or safety clearance.**

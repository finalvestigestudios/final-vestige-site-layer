## Important correction after E445
E445 fixed the false statement that every FDA-regulated package must show a separate **Contains** allergen box. But E445's FDA-focused explanation was insufficient **for an article explicitly about dried meat**. FDA's *Food Allergies* page explicitly excludes meat, poultry and certain egg products under USDA FSIS from the relevant FDA major-allergen framework. FSIS official Q&As say ingredient lists on multi-ingredient meat/poultry products must declare ingredients by common/usual names, while separate FALCPA-style allergen declarations are **voluntary** on products under FSIS jurisdiction. These requirements are not interchangeable. **Neither a missing Contains statement nor a missing May contain advisory establishes allergy safety.** Do not extrapolate a product's regulator from appearance alone, including mixed foods, local retail and shell eggs.

## High-value source correction (directly checked 2026-11-17)

A **no-result check of FDA's public recall/safety-alert page cannot be interpreted as a complete or current product-safety check**. Three related but distinct official records were verified:

1. [FDA public recall and alert listing](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts): selectively aggregates some public press-release notices; FDA says **not all recalls receive a press release or appear on this page**. The page displays **three years** of notices before archiving older entries. The absence of a result has a specific search-window limitation.
2. [FDA Additional Information about Recalls](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/additional-information-about-recalls): explicitly separates press releases/public notices from recall classification. Public notices are not the complete FDA Enforcement Report.
3. [FDA Enforcement Reports](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/enforcement-reports) and its [interactive search](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/ires/): all FDA-monitored recalls appear once classified and some appear as **not yet classified** earlier; a firm can issue an alert before formal classification. FDA histories may revise code information, product description, reason and class. These are not guaranteed chronological steps, nor a certification for a particular food.

[FoodSafety.gov recalls and outbreaks](https://www.foodsafety.gov/recalls-and-outbreaks), last reviewed July 17, 2025, is a cross-agency entry point. Its specific product-match language is *not* independent scientific clearance of other foods. The direct USDA FSIS recall landing-page fetch returned HTTP 403 in this run; **no direct FSIS list verification claimed**.

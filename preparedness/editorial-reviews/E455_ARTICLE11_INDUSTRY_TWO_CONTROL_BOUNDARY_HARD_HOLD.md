## Directly inspected consequential primary source

USDA FSIS, [*Compliance Guideline for Meat and Poultry Jerky Produced by Small and Very Small Establishments*](https://fsistraining.fsis.usda.gov/pluginfile.php/17046/mod_resource/content/2/Jerky%20Compliance%20Guidelines%20.pdf), **August 2014**, read November 18 with **PDF page 5 visually inspected**. Establishments must substantiate **two distinct controls**: reducing pathogenic microorganisms during processing (lethality) and preventing harmful microbial growth in the finished food (drying/shelf stability). This is INDUSTRY-specific validation guidance, not a home method, not an independently tested unknown household batch, and not a universal safe-storage clock. No treatment temperatures, water activity targets, times, packaging recipes or cooking procedures have been carried into reader copy.

### Separate critical conflict

[NCHFP jerky](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/meat-and-poultry/jerky/) has broadly worded pork/wild-game freezing guidance; [CDC trichinellosis prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/trichinellosis/prevention/index.html) (March 12, 2024, directly read) warns some Trichinella in wild game are freeze-resistant. No general wild-game treatment can be inferred. [Utah State Extension](https://extension.usu.edu/foodsafety/research/can-i-freeze-dry-that-a-practical-guide-to-safe-and-effective-freeze-drying) (January 23, 2026, directly read) separately addresses viable bacteria after freeze-drying; these are distinct hazards and processes.

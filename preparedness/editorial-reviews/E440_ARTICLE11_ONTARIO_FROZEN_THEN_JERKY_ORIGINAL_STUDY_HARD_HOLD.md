## Substantive new research / correction of possible inference

E439 separated two distinct contemporary cases: a 2022 frozen-bear-meat outbreak later reported in CDC MMWR (2024), and a 2024 North Carolina bear-jerky outbreak reported in CDC EID (June 24, 2026) whose implicated jerky **was not previously frozen**. E440 adds the missing directly relevant original case where **both events occurred to the same meat**.

- **Original primary case series:** Dalcin et al., [*Trichinella nativa Outbreak With Rare Thrombotic Complications Associated With Meat From a Black Bear Hunted in Northern Ontario*](https://academic.oup.com/cid/article/64/10/1367/3045093), *Clinical Infectious Diseases* 64(10), first published February 22, 2017. Full original publisher HTML read 2026-11-17. A bear harvested in northern Ontario, Canada, in 2015 was frozen for about seven months; its meat was then used for jerky shared in March 2016. **All ten reported exposed people developed compatible illness**, with *T. nativa* identified in remaining bear meat. This is **one Canadian outbreak case series**, not a controlled household freezer/parasite efficacy study, prevalence estimate, or universal species/temperature extrapolation. We reproduce no freezer/jerky settings.
- **Authoritative provenance:** [USDA Agricultural Research Service original publication record #337068](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=337068) directly checked 2026-11-17; confirms frozen-then-jerky history, personnel and pathogen. **Same study, NOT independent outbreak replication.**
- **Correction audit:** [Publisher's July 6, 2017 erratum](https://academic.oup.com/cid/article/65/4/706/3930221) fixes a Figure 5 lettering error, not the exposure, ten cases or identified pathogen. Publisher indexed text checked 2026-11-17.
- **Background boundary:** [USDA ARS May 6, 2020 research report](https://www.ars.usda.gov/news-events/news/research-news/2020/new-freeze-resistant-trichinella-species-discovered/) documents further species-specific freeze resistance in wild carnivores but confers **no** household food-processing instructions.
- **Original source dispute remains open:** [NCHFP 2014-derived jerky chapter](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/recipes/jerky/) uses broad freezing language referring to pork/wild game. [CDC prevention page dated March 12, 2024](https://www.cdc.gov/trichinellosis/prevention/index.html) expressly warns freeze-resistant parasites occur in wild game. FSIS [*Does freezing destroy parasites?*](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/Does-freezing-destroy-parasites), index updated Feb 3, 2025, remains **INDEXED ONLY** after direct page timeout; do not represent as fully read or use as household procedure.

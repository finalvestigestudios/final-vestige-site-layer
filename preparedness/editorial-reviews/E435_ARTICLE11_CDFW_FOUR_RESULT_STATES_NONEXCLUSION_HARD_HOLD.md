## New, directly verified official evidence (2026-11-17)

[California Department of Fish and Wildlife's CWD surveillance portal](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Laboratories/Wildlife-Health/Monitoring/CWD) has **four statuses**: Pending (not ready), Not Detected (testing did not detect CWD), Positive (detected), and **Not Tested (sample poor/unusable; no inference is possible)**. The same current CDFW FAQ states more explicitly that a **Not Detected** result **cannot prove the animal did not have CWD** because of test limitations. The short results key and longer FAQ must be read together, not flattened into a clearance. The page's live 21-day wait display is volatile: neither reviewer candidate nor printable promises that duration. These labels apply to **California's program only**, not necessarily another state or lab.

[CDC's January 21, 2026 CWD overview](https://www.cdc.gov/chronic-wasting/about/index.html) reports no known human infections, persistent uncertainty about cross-species transmission, and instructs people **not to eat meat from CWD-positive animals**. No CWD test status, packaging history, product recall search or jerky treatment alone certifies food safety.

The official [FDA public recall page](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts) and [FoodSafety.gov recall guide](https://www.foodsafety.gov/recalls-and-outbreaks) were also directly checked, but recall status and wild-harvest CWD surveillance remain separate evidence streams.

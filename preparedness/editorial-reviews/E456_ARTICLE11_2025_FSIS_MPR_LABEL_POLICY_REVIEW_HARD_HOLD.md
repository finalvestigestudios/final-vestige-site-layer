- **Directly inspected:** USDA FSIS August 2014 [Compliance Guideline for Meat and Poultry Jerky Produced by Small and Very Small Establishments](https://fsistraining.fsis.usda.gov/pluginfile.php/17046/mod_resource/content/2/Jerky%20Compliance%20Guidelines%20.pdf), including screenshot of its printed p. 5, November 18. It describes separately validating pathogen reduction and control of growth in the finished product. **This is industrial guidance, not an available household preservation instruction.**
- **Later agency-attributed text, retrieval qualification:** a September 12, 2025 FSIS *Constituent Update* states the agency removed the outdated **moisture-to-protein ratio (MPR)** reference from the **Jerky** entry of the *Food Standards and Labeling Policy Book* in response to Petition 22-02. - The MPR change concerns an obsolete **labeling policy reference**, not withdrawal of a requirement to make meat safe.

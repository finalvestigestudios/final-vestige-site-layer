## Distinct official-source finding: FERPA and a household card are different domains

Directly read **Nov. 18, 2026**:
1. [CDC, Keeping Children Safe Away from Home](https://www.cdc.gov/children-and-school-preparedness/keeping-safe/index.html), **Dec. 16, 2025**: child-specific backpack and guardian wallet card roles, relevant medical/allergy information and contacts, school/childcare notification.
2. [CDC, Preparing for a School Emergency](https://www.cdc.gov/children-and-school-preparedness/php/preparation.html), **Oct. 28, 2024**: institution-held school records, languages, disability, guardian contact and emergency planning, **not a nationwide release policy**.
3. [U.S. Department of Education, FERPA health/safety emergency exception](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/faq/when-it-permissible-utilize-ferpas-health-or-safety-emergency-exception-disclosures), undated agency FAQ, accessed Nov. 18: limited release of **school-held education-record** PII in actual/impending/imminent emergency; **not** blanket sharing and **not** a law governing self-prepared FV cards.
4. [U.S. Department of Education, emergency-exercise question](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/faq/may-school-make-disclosures-under-ferpas-health-or-safety-emergency-provision-emergency), undated, accessed Nov. 18: the emergency exception does not justify disclosing school records for ordinary exercises.
5. [California Department of Education, Comprehensive School Safety Plans](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ls/ss/vp/cssp.asp), undated, accessed Nov. 18: parent reunification communication in understood languages, but sensitive tactical plan details not public; **California schools only**, not a national rule.
6. [CDC, 2020 original child/guardian cards](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/139829/cdc_139829_DS1.pdf), **E467 prior visual inspection, not newly re-rendered**. Both samples are child-specific; not compulsory uniform disclosures.

Do **not** extrapolate FERPA into a custody ruling; legal, care and local school policies require qualified review.

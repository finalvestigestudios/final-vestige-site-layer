## Exact bounded editorial addition
Added a 3-paragraph section **“The first notice may not be the last”** to the E437 reader alternative. The essential difference is between a **specific recall identification**, a **later expanded group of lots**, and an **incomplete/periodically updated database**. FDA documents that a firm's initial recall may later encompass additional lots, while openFDA's weekly FDA enforcement feed explicitly says it is **NOT** for live consumer alerting or tracking recall lifecycle. Manufacturer notices may provide codes, distribution areas and directions for downstream customers. The drafted consumer action is *record when the product was compared; consult the latest original agency notice; ask issuing authority when unclear*, not a new safety treatment. The original FoodSafety.gov wording “safe to use” is kept **within the narrow particular-recall nonmatch question**, contrasted with FDA's warning that its public-warning page does not list every monitored recall.

The free printable now separately records **first check** and **subsequent recheck/notice change** instead of collapsing update and check dates.

## Consequential new evidence boundary
The original R397 Article #11, 124 lines, Git blob `6fa55c397b161437e016d5d4a6c27da7ce3fa6bd`, discusses bacterial and *Trichinella* hazards, but its venison/wild-game context raises an **additional, different** issue: **chronic wasting disease (CWD)** in susceptible cervids. CWD is a **prion disease**, not a bacterial or parasite step. An animal can look healthy during a long presymptomatic period. Neither a complete verified bacterial-treatment process nor a wild-game parasite freezing claim determines whether a deer/elk was CWD-positive.

**Direct primary sources reviewed November 17, 2026:**
- [CDC, About CWD](https://www.cdc.gov/chronic-wasting/about/index.html) (Jan 21, 2026): **no reported human CWD infections; zoonotic risk remains uncertain**. Hunters should check current state wildlife/health testing rules, strongly consider testing in CWD areas before eating, and **not eat meat from a CWD-positive animal**.
- [CDC, CWD in Animals](https://www.cdc.gov/chronic-wasting/animals/index.html) (Jan 21, 2026): apparently normal animals may be infected; this earlier page references 36 states on an August 2025 basis.
- [CDC, Where CWD Occurs](https://www.cdc.gov/chronic-wasting/data-research/index.html) (May 18, 2026): now describes **37 states**. Different page dates, not a scientific contradiction; localized current wildlife advisories remain paramount.
- [USDA APHIS, CWD](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/cervid/chronic-wasting) (modified Oct 15, 2026): prion biology; long preclinical period.
- [Benavente et al., *Emerging Infectious Diseases*, Feb 2025](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/31/2/24-0906_article/): prion signal in raw and processed/cooked samples from **one CWD-positive elk**. This laboratory finding **does not establish human transmissibility, infectious risk after any particular recipe, or universal processing failure**.
- [Texas Parks and Wildlife Department, Precautions for Hunters](https://tpwd.texas.gov/regulations/outdoor-annual/hunting/cwd/precautions-for-hunters): **Texas-only** example; do not extrapolate to other jurisdictions.
- Existing [NCHFP 2014-derived jerky chapter](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/recipes/jerky/) and [CDC trichinellosis prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/trichinellosis/prevention/index.html) directly rechecked for separate hazard scope.

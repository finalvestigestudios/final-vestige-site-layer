## Material source-version finding (direct primary verification)
- NCHFP hosts the older [University of Georgia *Preparing Safer Jerky* 2011 PDF](https://nchfp.uga.edu/papers/UGA_Publications/prep_safe_jerky.pdf) **and** its [live *Jerky* chapter extracted from *So Easy to Preserve* 6th ed. 2014](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/meat-and-poultry/jerky/). Both describe broadly categorized pork or wild game, but they use *different parasite-freezing conditions*. **Reviewer-only exact comparison:** older 2011 PDF `5°F or lower / at least 20 days / <=6-inch portions`; 2014-derived web chapter `0°F or lower / at least 30 days / <=6-inch portions`. The PDF's first page was directly read AND visually checked; live NCHFP HTML directly read on Nov 17. These are *different editions*, not evidence that either schedule is universally effective for freeze-resistant species, that the older is superseded, or that parts may be combined into a validated recipe. **No numerical schedules in proposed reader manuscript.**
- The directly read [University of Alaska Fairbanks Cooperative Extension *Making Jerky* page](https://www.uaf.edu/ces/publications/database/food/jerky.php) contains general freezer language **and an explicit separate warning not to make bear jerky**. Do not pick steps from its different treatment method as a substitute. [CDC prevention guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/trichinellosis/prevention/index.html) (dated 2024-03-12) specifically warns that some wild-game *Trichinella* resist freezing.
- E440's 2016 Ontario frozen-then-jerky outbreak was independently rechecked in [Dalcin et al. 2017 original report](https://academic.oup.com/cid/article/64/10/1367/3045093). Ten exposed people were ill; `T. nativa` was identified in remaining meat. The [USDA ARS publication record](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=337068) corroborates *the same study*, not an independent outbreak. This is an Ontario, Canada case history, not proof of treatment temperatures for other species.
- USDA FSIS [*Does freezing destroy parasites?*](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/Does-freezing-destroy-parasites) has a search-index excerpt marked updated 2025-02-03, but direct primary fetching timed out. **INDEXED-ONLY, NOT directly verified.** Not used for new treatment advice.

## Newly detected source-scope tension, not a fifth-independent-adjudication approval

The [University of Minnesota Extension food-drying article](https://extension.umn.edu/food/preparing/cooking-at-home/food-preservation/drying) (reviewed in 2026, directly read November 15) distinguishes meat jerky from other dried foods with respect to refrigerated storage. A [different University of Minnesota Extension emergency-food article](https://extension.umn.edu/natural-resources/weather-and-climate/prepare-for-risks/cooking-without-a-kitchen) includes **jerky** in a list of room-temperature foods. Different editorial audiences and commercial-versus-homemade assumptions may explain the language, but that explanation is NOT demonstrated. A single institutional publisher is not proof of identical product or process scope. Do not extrapolate a general ambient-storage rule for homemade jerky.

The independently opened [NCHFP / UGA jerky page](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/recipes/jerky/) derives from *So Easy to Preserve* (sixth edition, 2014) and contains conditional homemade guidance; do not silently harmonize with either UMN article. The [USDA FSIS archived *Shelf-Stable Food Safety* GPO bibliography](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-A110-PURL-gpo38739) identifies the agency and 2011 issue date but expressly notes archived materials may not reflect current policy. The [newer USDA FSIS Ask USDA jerky FAQ](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/How-do-I-dry-jerky) has **not** had its full current body independently authenticated in this integration lane; an indexed snippet is NOT a complete live reading.

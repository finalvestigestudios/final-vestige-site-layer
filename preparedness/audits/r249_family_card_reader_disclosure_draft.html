Family emergency contact card: what prints

This is a proposed clarification of the existing free card, not a new product. Editorial review and real browser and print checks are still required.

Choose what to carry

The current shorter card can include six filled-in fields: household name, household phone number, out-of-area contact, out-of-area phone number, first meeting place, and review date. These details may be visible if a printed card is lost or shared.

The full card can include thirteen filled-in fields. The shorter card excludes seven fields involving additional meeting, school, caregiver and communication details. An empty field may not appear on the printout.

A blank card can be printed and filled in by hand with JavaScript turned off. The interactive form is hidden when JavaScript is off. Do not rely on the interactive form in that mode.

Check that any wording about local data handling, download behavior, and the statement about a written plan at home matches the actual tested tool. Do not add private identifying information to an unfamiliar or shared device without understanding its handling.

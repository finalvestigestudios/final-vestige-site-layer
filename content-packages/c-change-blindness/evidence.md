## Claim → source
Every number in the article is from S1 or S2 as listed. The statement "flickers more slowly than the lab version … under three flashes per second" is a design statement about our own demo (300 ms image + 100 ms blank = 2.5 image onsets per second), checked arithmetically, not a research claim.
The sentence "typically when an eye movement, a film cut, a blank screen or an interruption separates before and after" draws on S1 (saccade experiments, flicker blanks) and S2 (motion-picture studies listed in its discussion).

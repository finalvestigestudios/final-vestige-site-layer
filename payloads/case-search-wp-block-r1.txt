No search query is sent anywhere.</p></div><button class="fv-case-search__close" type="button" aria-label="Close Case Search">×</button></div><div class="fv-case-search__query-wrap"><label class="fv-case-search__status" for="fv-case-search-input">Search this investigation</label><input id="fv-case-search-input" class="fv-case-search__query" type="search" autocomplete="off" spellcheck="false" placeholder="Person, place, date, detail…"></div><div class="fv-case-search__scopes" aria-label="Search scope"><button class="fv-case-search__scope" type="button" data-scope="all" aria-pressed="true">All</button><button class="fv-case-search__scope" type="button" data-scope="story" aria-pressed="false">Story</button><button class="fv-case-search__scope" type="button" data-scope="sources" aria-pressed="false">Sources</button><button class="fv-case-search__scope" type="button" data-scope="figures" aria-pressed="false">Figures</button></div><div class="fv-case-search__results" role="region" aria-label="Search results"><p class="fv-case-search__empty">Search the published page.

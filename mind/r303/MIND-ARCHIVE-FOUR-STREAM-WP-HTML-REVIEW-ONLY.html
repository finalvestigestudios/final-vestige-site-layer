Evidence-reviewed audio buying guides separate advertised contents from independent science. Zen12, Hypnosis Live and Brainwave Shots are different products: format, price, policy and effect claims require individual checks.

The boundary

A witnessed event is not a verified explanation.

Puységur's notarial documents confirm a paper trail, not a magnetic force. A dramatic face illusion confirms a perceptual effect, not the efficacy of meditation recordings. Evidence and commercial claims remain separate.

## One high-intent audience and proposition

Audience: a curious reader who is already comparing *structured meditation audio versus free self-directed focus practice*. This is distinct from a viewer who arrived to learn why the Thatcher illusion works. Such a viewer should first encounter research and the original experiment—not an unsolicited psychological-improvement upsell.

Proposition: **Buy guided structure if that format genuinely helps you maintain a practice; do not buy a claim of guaranteed improved focus.**

Hook: “A meditation recording can give you structure. It cannot borrow proof from an unrelated experiment.” It speaks to the buyer's actual uncertainty. Source-supported details: Zen12 checkout advertises twelve levels and 48 MP3 recordings, user guide and support. Unsupported benefits are excluded; price is withheld because seller campaign routes vary.

Useful demonstration: try four minutes with one concrete task, record interruptions, and compare a similar unaided session. Not a scientific trial; not a promise that a four-minute test predicts Zen12 outcomes.

Buyer objections answered: feature contents, difference from Total Focus/Focus Shot, seller refund representations, benefits with and without audio, reasons not to buy, and the existing free Fieldbook alternative.

## Original optional media hypothesis (no implication of actual video)

**Variant A:** same exact original evidence-reviewed buyer copy with clear disabled purchase control, no film section.

**Variant B:** identical buyer copy plus an optional original 42-second **static film concept**, the timed transcript and proposed captions. No autoplay, script or audio on either prototype. Variant B is NOT a playable video and cannot be called a completed video A/B experiment. The source-controlled R303 specimen shows the proposed B content for review; R284 remains canonical.

Once independently produced/rights-checked, an eventual film would be a real video element with controls, metadata preload only (none until play if feasible), a compact poster, WebVTT captions, transcript outside the player, no external player SDK and a no-JS path. The 42-second script has four timestamped beats: feeling versus measurement, what research actually tests, seller-described 12-level package, free alternative and buyer choice. Grok owns social content. Mind creates the source/rights brief, not a Grok assignment.

**Hypothesis, not a finding:** a relevant optional film could reduce comprehension friction for some high-intent viewers, but it may lower conversion due to distraction, download delay or skepticism. Keep evidence, offer, CTA and placement equal. Random assignment and attribution are Claude/QA engineering responsibilities after founder approval. Do not claim win from clicks alone.

Stages: consent-respecting qualified human landing → relevant article or buyer-guide visit → visible actionable CTA → confirmed signup *only from Jetpack when available* → affiliate_outbound click **separate event** → merchant-confirmed attributable paid purchase → cleared payable/paid affiliate commission → adjustment/refunds/fees. A checkout page load, button tap, email-field submission or merchant gross order value is **not a paid FV conversion**.

**Metric definitions**: Paid conversion rate = merchant-confirmed attributable paid purchasers / qualified human sessions (only where cross-system matching is credible). Lead-to-paid = verified attributed purchasers from the confirmed lead cohort / confirmed leads in cohort (with delayed-conversion rules). FV affiliate realized net = actually cleared FV commission less incremental fees or refunds **not already reflected in that cleared figure**; do not deduct the same charge twice. Mixed revenue requires separately proven owned product gross, refunds, fees and net before combining. Key ratio = 1,000 × realized FV net / qualified human sessions. Denominator and numerator currently UNKNOWN; report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero.

Report seven- and thirty-day cohorts by first attributed qualified landing date, with explicit late conversions, unknowns, refunds, chargebacks, settlement windows and tiny-sample limitations. Source confidence flags: VERIFIED_PROVIDER, SELF_REPORTED, INFERRED, UNKNOWN. Never join identities across platforms without appropriate consent and defensible source data.

## Acceptance tests to execute before the release review

1. **Static source security**: Reject active seller href, untrusted external domains, inline handlers, scripts, iframes, forms, refresh redirects, active merchant buttons, hidden disclosure, invented approval or revenue.
2. **Truthfulness**: Review every product feature against the *exact* seller offer, mark seller outcome/marketing claims, link source ledger, exclude unsupported health/cognitive effects; review copyright and original-video rights.
3. **Independent browser QA**: Test at widths 360, 390, 768, 1440, 1920. Real keyboard/screen-reader order, skip link, color contrast, touch targets, reduced-motion, disabled vs active control semantics, no-JS and 200% text zoom. Run actual browser/Lighthouse and WordPress sanitizer before calling these PASSED.
4. **Media**: Real original 30–45-second encoded file, captions synchronized, poster rights and alt/text equivalent, no forced playback, performance budget verified on throttled mobile, no cross-site tracking.
5. **Checkout**: With founder approval and provider-specific validation, check the exact issued earning URL, permitted tracking params, referral preservation, landing price and terms, refunds. Do not make a test purchase or submit a signup just to claim validation without approval.
6. **Commercial correctness**: Verify affiliate publisher acceptance, merchant settlement reports, adjustments, net commission and human session denominator. No conversion claim without provider confirmation.
7. **Release controls**: Independently review #171, #820, QA #126, Dispatch #174 and art #150; specific founder authorization before CMS, navigation, affiliate activation, public video, paid acquisition, email send, merge or deployment.

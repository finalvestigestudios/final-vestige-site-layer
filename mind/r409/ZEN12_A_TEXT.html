An experiment about instructions, not an audio purchase

The word BLUE appears printed in red. If you must name the ink color, reading can interfere with answering. In 2006 researchers found that a specific instruction to treat words as meaningless symbols reduced this interference in highly suggestible participants, whether or not hypnotic induction preceded the instruction. This was a tightly defined experiment, not evidence that a recording changes everyday productivity. Original study.

Five questions worth more than a testimonial

  4. Cost: What does the actual checkout charge, including applicable tax, today? Public coupon presentations are not permanent prices.

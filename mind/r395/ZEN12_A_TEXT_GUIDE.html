Know What You Chose Before You Buy What You're Shown.

A researcher switches two jams. The shopper explains the one they didn't choose. What does that have to do with buying meditation audio? Not proof of a brain hack. A better reason to pause and compare your actual goal with an offer.

In a 2010 peer-reviewed experiment, supermarket visitors selected a preferred jam or tea, then were sometimes handed a switched sample and asked why they liked it. No more than a third of manipulated trials were detected. This is choice blindness, a finding about particular decisions and explanations. The study did not test meditation products. Read the original study.

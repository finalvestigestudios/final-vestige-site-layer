## 4. Attribution event contract (review-only, no deployed code)

Claude owns implementation against the existing first-party instrumentation; this document does not add a tracking script, pixel or SaaS.

Permitted source attributes: \`asset_id\` (opaque creator-supplied label, not user ID), \`utm_source\`, \`utm_medium\`, \`utm_campaign\`, \`landing_path\`, \`topic_key\`, \`variant\`, and timestamp rounded appropriately for aggregation. Validate against allowlists; strip arbitrary query strings, names, emails, IPs, device fingerprint data, raw URLs and referer parameters that may leak PII. For non-consenting users, essential navigation stays functional and analytics are suppressed or strictly aggregated according to existing policy. Do not install parallel measurement.

1. \`qualified_human_session\`: first-party event only after existing documented consent policy and traffic-quality filters; unique, deduped session counted once per campaign+asset_id, excluding identified bots and obvious reloads. **Cannot claim Grok attribution unless source tag or documented consent-safe attribution exists.**
2. \`landing_view\`: permitted page event from a specific known article, not total site views. Missing asset_id is UNKNOWN, not misattributed to Grok.
3. \`cta_focus_open\`: user's explicit click of existing free Focus route, not a signup.
4. \`lead_confirmed\`: Jetpack/Dispatch provider-confirmed consent/confirmation and email delivery evidence; **do not infer from PDF link visibility, downloads, or local CTA clicks**. No duplicate list or welcome series.
5. \`affiliate_click\`: only after all authorization and activation checks, from an actual user-initiated outbound earning link; click is not checkout, sale or commission. It is absent in current draft because the button is natively disabled.
6. \`partner_reported_paid_conversion\`: merchant/partner-authenticated paid order or commission matched to allowed campaign/cohort with provider-provided attribution, **never synthesized from clicks or estimated values**. Keep unrelated or unmatched revenue unassigned.
7. \`refund_adjustment\` and \`fee_adjustment\`: verified settlement adjustments tied to actual orders/commissions; 7-day and 30-day cohorts may mature at different speeds. Report unknown refunds or fees as unknown, not zero.

**KPI:** realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1,000 × verified settled net attributable revenue / deduped, consent-eligible qualified-human sessions. If the numerator cannot be attributed or denominator is unavailable, output **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0. A null numerator is not a measured zero. Supporting metrics: verified paid conversion rate, confirmed orders/customers, gross settled revenue, refunds, fees, leads and lead-to-customer rate where identity can be matched lawfully. Report UTC-coherent 7/30-day cohorts, known exclusions, missing fields, incomplete cohort age and denominators. For tiny samples show raw counts, not false statistical certainty.

## 5. Honest text vs. optional film experiment

**Hypothesis:** on the same high-intent arriving cohort, a short optional original explanatory film may improve *trust and comprehension* and possibly paid purchase relative to a full text-only buyer guide. This is a hypothesis, not a promise and not an A/B test that has happened.

Only after approvals and real film: equal eligibility, stable assignment via existing first-party server path or deterministic privacy-preserving experiment token (no fingerprint); no randomization-by-JavaScript dependency, and no forced consent or forced video. Both versions contain the exact same seller facts, independent buyer copy, headline, free Focus route, disclosed approved CTA position and refund/price caveats. B adds only the optional 42-second original MP4/WebM, poster, visible transcript and synchronized captions. \`controls\`, \`preload="none"\`, no \`autoplay\`, no \`loop\`, accessibility and reduced-motion compliance required. Film must not block meaningful content rendering or hurt mobile CWV.

**Primary endpoint:** partner-confirmed, net-of-refunds paid purchase per qualified-human session—not outbound click-through, video play, CTA focus or email signup. Secondary: confirmed Jetpack leads and source comprehension. Assignment integrity, approved partner tracking, traffic quality and source/product intent matching are acceptance requirements. No statistical “winner” from a handful of sales; disclose sample, uncertainty and non-significance. Experiment only after explicit founder production release.

**Fail conditions:** B copies differ materially from A, merchant link appears while partner approval absent, video silently loads without user input, disabled no-JS CTA becomes active, caption/transcript mismatch, missing mobile disclosure, user gets a duplicate email form, a generic meditation study is represented as Zen12 proof, unconsented PII collected, purchase inferred from a click, or any unpublished destination is presented as live.

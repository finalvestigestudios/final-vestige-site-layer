## One high-intent purchase hypothesis — buyer needs, not placebo storytelling
**Qualified audience**: a person considering a structured meditation-audio download, especially someone asking “Zen12 Premium review”, “what does Zen12 include”, “guided audio versus free practice” or “Zen12 refund terms.” This audience is different from a viral illusion viewer. The conversion path must be voluntary, contextual and evidence-led: **Grok factual clip → relevant investigation → independent buying guide → verified off-site offer** (when approved); optional Focus/Dispatch may appear between investigation and guide but is NEVER required to read research or purchase.

**Built R217 copy candidate:** [Zen12 Proof-First Presell — INACTIVE](zen12-PROOF-FIRST-PRESELL-INACTIVE.html) includes a bounded hook, specific seller-described product value, source trail, no-purchase exercise, real objections, disabled CTA, immediate prospective commission disclosure, and 42-second optional original storyboard with captions concept. It contains **no active off-site merchant hyperlink**. It is a copy-review alternative, **not another arm** of the deliberately frozen R210 A/B. Original R210 controlled A (text) vs B (same text + optional 42-second film) remains the first research question. Do not combine multiple unrelated copy differences into that test.

### Controlled comparison proposal (NO LIVE TEST)
- **A**: Existing R210 text-only seller-claims/evidence buying guide.
- **B**: The **same exact body text, source notes, CTA position and disclosure**, but add a user-triggered, genuinely produced 30–45 second original film, poster, WebVTT captions and a visible transcript. No autoplay, no forced embed, no JS-required navigation.
- **Hypothesis**: Optional film may help a high-intent user understand an offer or may distract; no directional improvement is a fact.
- **Primary endpoint**: merchant-confirmed paid order (owned) or independently reported cleared affiliate conversion, ultimately realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions.
- **Secondary endpoints**: consent-compatible verified lead, relevant CTA click, outbound affiliate_click; these are **NOT purchases**. Track slow pages and broken / blocked mobile CTA as negative guardrails.
- **Assignment**: after release approval only; stable pseudorandom first-party cohort assignment if governance/legal basis permits, avoiding individual profile or cross-site tracking. If consent or platform rules do not permit it, use an aggregate sequential comparison clearly labeled confounded, not a randomized causal effect.
- **Analysis**: run 7- and 30-day cohorts separately; include denominator and missing-match rates. Distinguish impressions from qualified humans; do not present tiny differences as significant. Report zero observed paid orders only if actual merchant ledger was inspected for that period; otherwise use **UNVERIFIED / NOT COMPUTABLE**.
- **Cost/performance**: no external player SDK, consentless analytics, new paid SaaS, autoplay or video-request-at-initial-page. First-party poster budget and media rights must be checked with Lighthouse/CWV; Graceful no-JS fallback is the complete guide.

## First-party measurement contract — proposal to Claude, not duplicate production instrumentation
Ingest only allowlisted **utm_source, utm_campaign and creator asset_id** (e.g., platform campaign identifier, not device ID), plus nonpersonal page_id and typed action. Never store an email, IP string, device fingerprint or identity in that campaign table. Reuse an existing production-approved analytics mechanism rather than loading a second script, and implement only with founder consent and Claude's engineering review. Do not claim there is already a real event pipeline.

1. **qualified_human_session**: estimate from quality-screened sessions, labelled with method and limitations; keep bot-filter heuristic and invalid traffic counts separate.
2. **landing_view**: observed article or buying-guide page from a known creator asset_id and campaign, after excluding obvious QA/automated hits.
3. **relevant_cta_click**: optional Focus or offer-guide intent, a non-sale.
4. **focus_confirmed_optin**: authoritative Jetpack confirmation record only; “download shown”, pageview and submit clicks are not valid confirmations.
5. **affiliate_click**: only after named publisher authorization and actually active link; a privacy-safe allowed redirect-out event. Does not equal any paid sale.
6. **owned_order_paid**: authoritative successful seller transaction tied by approved metadata to cohort and date; reconcile refunds and fees.
7. **affiliate_commission_cleared**: partner's finalized payable/paid commission record, with authorized attribution if possible. An unsettled referral, “pending” commission or click is NOT cleared revenue.
8. **refund**, **fee**, **chargeback**: actual merchant ledger, currency and reconciliation state, not guessed percentages.

**Formula for the headline KPI**:
`realized_net_revenue = settled_owned_receipts + cleared_affiliate_commissions - refunded_amounts - transaction_fees - chargebacks - attributable_variable_costs`
`net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * realized_net_revenue / verified_qualified_human_sessions`.

Do not divide when verified_qualified_human_sessions is missing or zero. Do not substitute all pageviews for qualified_human_sessions. If paid commissions are missing, report total KPI **NOT COMPUTABLE** instead of showing misleading zero. Keep site-level and campaign-specific revenues separate; unknown attribution reduces coverage and limits conclusions. Use 7d and 30d cohorts and a tiny-sample caveat, include known refunds/payment fees and missing matching rate.

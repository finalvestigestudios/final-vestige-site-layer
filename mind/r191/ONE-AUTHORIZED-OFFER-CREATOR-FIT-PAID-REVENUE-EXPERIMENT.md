# R191 | Choice blindness → independent purchase decision → verified revenue
**November 7, 2026 · Founder review only · Unpublished and unmerged · Mind lane #171 + business contract #820**

## One commercial problem, not ten more speculative pages

A visitor who learns that a switched face can lead someone to defend a choice they never made has reason to wonder whether their own purchasing rationale is stable. The defensible offer is not “buy a recording to prevent choice blindness.” No relevant experiment establishes that. The real commercial audience is someone **already considering a short, structured audio meditation program** and searching whether its specific promise is worth paying for. They need a written decision receipt, product details, alternatives, actual checkout terms and credible independent evidence—not pressure, faux scientific authority or an unrelated product placement.

**Conditional product:** Zen12 Premium, as described at https://zen12.com/hnp on November 7, 2026. The seller advertises twelve levels, forty-eight MP3s, a guide and supplementary materials, and a one-year guarantee. Those claims describe a catalog offer; nobody in this lane has authenticated the download, tested psychological efficacy, performed a purchase, confirmed refund processing or verified the final charge. The seller also advertises equivalence to an hour of advanced meditation; we do not endorse this as established. Its exact approved earning URL and Final Vestige's partner permissions are unknown.

**Authorized substitute if eligibility fails:** one already-live, original, rights-cleared **FV-owned Mind SKU**, but only after signed-in Payhip inventory, usable files, rights, buyer-facing price, merchant-of-record, taxes, checkout delivery, refund/return terms, support obligations and independently observed purchase report are documented. Issue #818 mentions Products 001–015 and Payhip but does not establish a specific live, rights-cleared Mind offer. The existing free Seven Day Focus Fieldbook is **not** an existing paid SKU. Until verification, keep all paid buttons disabled; do not fabricate a shopping experience.

## Exact attribution contract, privacy and legitimate paid outcomes

Use only existing permitted site analytics; no duplicate script, fingerprinting, client UUID, cross-site tracker or paid SaaS. Standard public campaign links when the creator platform allows: `utm_source=tiktok|youtube|instagram`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_audio_decision_2026_11`, `utm_content=REAL_GROK_ASSET_ID`. This string is a **proposal**, not authorization to launch a new convention. Use actual Grok-approved campaign and ID values from TASK-008. Do not collect names, emails, IPs, precise device identifiers, health traits or guessed per-person sessions in URL parameters. If consent configuration makes event measurement unavailable, state **UNKNOWN**, don't smuggle an alternative pixel.

Definitions in order:
1. `campaign_landing`: aggregate consent-compliant landing event tied to genuine posted media ID, not a verified human.
2. `qualified_human_session`: deduplicated meaningful visit after bots and test traffic are excluded according to Site QA's documented method. If impossible, denominator unknown.
3. `article_to_resource_click`: first-party interest. Not a subscriber.
4. `confirmed_signup`: Jetpack provider-side accepted and confirmed state, only when verifiable and permitted. A click, pageview or email entered doesn't count.
5. `affiliate_outbound`: permitted navigation to an approved advertiser earning URL, only after release; still not a sale.
6. `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`: provider- or payment-reconciled completed transaction with trustworthy attribution, deduplicated by merchant transaction key; no PII in reports.
7. `settled_affiliate_commission`: partner-reported accepted and paid commission, net of reversals; distinguish earned/pending/settled.
8. `refunds + processing/merchant fees`: matched to transactions and time windows, not silently subtracted twice.

**Primary KPI:** realized `net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1,000 × (settled FV receipts + paid affiliate commissions − associated refunds − fees) / qualified sessions`. Treat affiliate commissions and gross owned-product sales as separate revenue streams with non-overlapping reconciliation to avoid double counting. If denominator or paid numerator is unknown, show **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero. Report 7-day and 30-day click/landing cohorts, sample size, permission constraints and attribution confidence. Do not present clicks as sales, signups as purchases or social views as qualified site visitors.

### A/B design, read-only until eligible

Only begin with **founder-approved release + active lawful offer + consent-compatible measurement + stable actual merchant reconciliation**. Both A/B variants must present the same eligibility, copy, prices-if-checked, disclosure, offer timing and checkout route. Randomize by stable first-party server-side assignment only if consent and low-data requirements allow; do not introduce a new cookie or third-party SDK without privacy review. A deterministic anonymous session-level split or an approved paired-week switchback may be preferable, but temporal changes and platform distributions require explicit bias notes. Avoid treating campaign channels as randomized when they aren't.

**Primary endpoint:** attributed **confirmed net revenue per 1,000 qualified human landing sessions** by arm, with 30-day post-visit settlement lag disclosed. Secondary: paid conversion rate, confirmed orders, actual refunded orders, verified lead-to-customer conversion, mobile CTA reach, median payload and 75th percentile LCP. The optional film is an experimental *treatment*, not a verified converter. Use 7/30 day descriptive reporting; abstain from significance or causal impact claims when N is small, delivery differs, or order matching isn't independently established. If revenue is unobservable due to partner reporting, **do not run a “conversion” A/B and call outbound click a substitute**.

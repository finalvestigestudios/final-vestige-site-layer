## Measurement contract for the existing site analytics owner

Keep native/first-party measurement only, after appropriate privacy and founder review. No added tracker, popup, newsletter form, SaaS, affiliate redirect or personal identifiers from this sprint.

- `qualified_human_session`: count once per documented attributable visit, with source/campaign/content from a real creator ID, bot filtering and provenance. If classification is not possible, report “unverified visits.”
- `mind_article_view` and `presell_view`: diagnostic only; preserve normal aggregate routing.
- `fieldbook_cta_click`: site click, **not** a confirmed signup.
- `confirmed_dispatch_lead`: count only from existing Jetpack provider accepted/confirmed records when linkable to campaign without new identifying tracking; if not linkable, report unknown attribution.
- `affiliate_click`: future authorized outbound request only, separate from merchant `commission`. Do not create the event in production until an approved destination exists.
- `merchant_paid_order`: merchant-reported completed paid checkout or independently accepted affiliate report; never inferred from a click.
- `realized_net_revenue`: cleared paid receipts or commissions **minus** refunds, chargebacks, processing/affiliate fees, and relevant realized costs under an agreed recognition policy. Do not claim receivables as cleared cash.

`net_per_1000 = (realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions) * 1000` **only when both the numerator and denominator are substantiated and denominator > 0**. Otherwise output `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, *not zero*. Report campaign cohorts for 7 and 30 days, source IDs and evidence timestamps; never store/email visitor IPs, addresses, raw emails, cross-site IDs or fingerprint identifiers as part of this project.

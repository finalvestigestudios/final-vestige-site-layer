Variant B contains precisely the same substantive buyer text, CTA, prospective affiliate disclosure and limitations, plus an original optional 42-second storyboard/poster with six WebVTT cues and a full transcript. The film is not encoded or playable. Static poster, transcript and caption concept are **BUILT**, but audiovisual delivery and any A/B performance are **NOT TESTED**.

Hypothesis: among qualified humans with explicit meditation-audio buying intent, a voluntarily started, rights-cleared 42-second original explanatory video might produce more merchant-verified, refund-adjusted paid orders than the identical text guide alone, without reducing understanding, access or page performance. Null: no improvement or unacceptable costs. Register a pre-experiment sample/observation plan, avoid peeking, and show uncertainty on tiny samples. Same source, same SKU, same price, same disclosure, same CTA and same follow-up path in both arms. A consent/privacy-approved first-party or server assignment should prevent variant contamination; do not install duplicate analytics.

On the eventual video asset use no forced playback, no automatic network-heavy preload, captions, poster, descriptive transcript, keyboard/touch controls and reduced motion. Avoid remote players that insert third-party tracking. Browser QA target widths: 360, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920. ## Measurement contract for Claude's existing #820 implementation

Capture only authorized, privacy-safe, coarse first-party campaign information, not IPs, names, emails, fingerprint identifiers or pixel egress:

1. **qualified_landing**: genuine human landing session; allowed real Grok asset_id and utm_campaign/utm_content; content route; first-seen date; approved A/B arm; consent status; bot classification methodology.
2. **relevant_cta**: article to Fieldbook or approved guide clicks, separate from page load, lead and sale.
3. **confirmed_lead**: Jetpack provider-confirmed signup only where authorized and actually observable; avoid claiming confirmation based on click or form submission.
4. **affiliate_click**: a permitted, properly disclosed click to a named issued offer URL, not a sale or independently validated merchant attribution.
5. **merchant_paid_sale**: partner/merchant reported order attributed by acceptable authoritative identifiers, not a frontend click or page event.
6. **refund_fee_settlement**: merchant settled commission or owned-sale proceeds minus refunds, reversed transactions, platform/payment fees and related costs.

Where tracking terms do not permit deterministic matching, label sales **unattributable**. The **primary KPI** is 1,000 × verified realized attributable net revenue ÷ qualified-human landing sessions for the same allowed 7- or 30-day cohort, only with a known nonzero denominator. Show paid conversion rate, confirmed paid orders/customers, gross and net, refunds, lead-to-paid rate, attribution gaps and small-sample uncertainty. No invented conversions.

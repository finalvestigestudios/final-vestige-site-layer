A two-record experiment, with no deception

Choose one ordinary task. Write the first action and why you chose it. Spend twelve minutes on it. Later, without changing the initial entry, write what you remember intending. Compare the original line to the later explanation. Differences are normal: they do not prove manipulation, hypnosis or a mental deficit.

Why keep two lines? Studies of choice blindness showed how an altered outcome could be explained as though it had been chosen. Your own record is not that experiment; it simply makes source and interpretation easier to keep apart.

A product may fit a routine. It does not prove the research.

The Zen12 seller describes twelve-minute sessions and a complete twelve-level, 48-MP3 offering.

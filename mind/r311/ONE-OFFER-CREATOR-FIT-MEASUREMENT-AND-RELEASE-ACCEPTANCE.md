## Specific offer and what the reader is buying

Zen12's complete-course checkout lists a **12-level library / 48 MP3s**. It may appeal to a reader who wants multiple guided or audio-structured sessions rather than a single track. Do not assume the complete offering is identical to partial packages or every checkout variant. Contrast accurately with [Hypnosis Live Total Focus](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus) (seller's 33-minute, 47-MB hypnosis recording) and [Brainwave Shots Focus Shot](https://brainwaveshots.com/focus-shot) (seller's 15/30-minute versions). They are distinct products, funnels, representations and return policies. Do not infer any one seller's guarantee or support eligibility applies to another.

The proposed **real buyer choice** is between (A) the free 52-page Focus Fieldbook with its existing Dispatch opt-in and (B) independently selecting structured recordings if the reader wants an audio format after reading current merchant terms. Never gate the product guide behind email signup; retain direct source navigation and source links. Preserve one Jetpack list, its current welcome email and Dispatch ownership.

**Hypothesis:** After authorization and release acceptance, an original, reader-initiated short film may improve *qualified paid net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions* versus text alone for people who have already demonstrated meditation-audio purchase intent. The film could also hurt performance or have no detectable effect. A difference in pageviews, video starts, outbound clicks or unsubscribed email opens is not proof of greater sales.

**Experiment hygiene:** Same entry topic, verified creator asset, landing-copy scope, offer and approved earning destination, adjacent disclosure, pricing/returns framing, device eligibility and observation window. Randomization or route split should be handled by existing owner-approved first-party infrastructure only after consent/privacy review. No duplicate analytics scripts, third-party pixels, user fingerprinting, cookie walls, JS-required access, forced play, withheld transcript or silent audio. Media preferably poster + preload='none', explicit user play, original VTT captions and visible transcript, under the site's image/video performance budget. Cache timing and mobile UX need measured tests, not speculative Lighthouse scores.

**A/B acceptance:** If permitted and adequately powered, report number of qualified humans per cell, paid orders with independent order IDs, gross merchant receipts, verified attributable settled commissions (where applicable), refunds/fees, realized net FV revenue, conversion rate, net / 1,000 sessions, and uncertainty intervals. The low sample size case is INCONCLUSIVE: no winner based on single purchases or larger click counts. Test for 7- and 30-day cohorts and report missing join keys as UNKNOWN, not zeros.

## Revenue provenance contract (proposed, not deployed)

Each genuine published Grok asset should have a unique `asset_id` and `utm_campaign` issued by Grok/Praxxis. For permitted attribution store campaign name, content topic, landing path, variant and **aggregate qualified-human counts**, with no name, personal email, raw IP, cross-site identity, fingerprint or query text. Claude must decide what the existing measurement layer can legally/technically support without adding overlapping scripts.

```
qualified_human_session(asset_id, utm_campaign, topic, variant, period)
    -> landing_view
    -> relevant_cta_click
    -> provider_confirmed_subscribed_lead   [only when consent/provider confirms]
    -> optional affiliate_click              [only after approval and activation]
    -> merchant_confirmed_paid_order         [independently validated]
    -> merchant_settled_affiliate_commission  [attributed/cleared]
    -> refund / chargeback / payment fees
    -> realized_net_revenue
```

Every transition needs its own evidence. A click does not mean a lead. A lead is not a checkout start. A checkout start is not a paid order. A paid merchant order is not a settled commission. Public affiliate recruitment or a hypothetical payout percentage is not account-specific revenue.

Primary KPI = **1,000 × net settled FV revenue / qualified-human sessions**. Also report unique confirmed paying customers, paid conversion rate, lead-to-customer conversion, refunds, charges and sample size. If denominator, attribution, orders, fees or settlements are unavailable, display **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0. Do not estimate net revenue from EPC advertising.

## Disclosure block and ethics acceptance

Prospective exact wording beside disabled button:

> **Potential affiliate relationship:** If Final Vestige is approved and you later purchase through a clearly labeled earning link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. There is no activated affiliate link on this page.

When a real link is eventually authorized, place a plainly understandable disclosure **immediately before or alongside that exact link**. Review whether “no additional cost” is supported by checkout/terms, and avoid describing the link as impartial. [FTC endorsement FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) say mere “affiliate link” wording may not adequately inform visitors. [FTC health-product guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) requires appropriate support for conveyed objective health claims. Do not adopt Inspire3's meditation-hour equivalence, “reprogramming” promise, instant concentration, clinically effective brainwave outcomes, extraordinary wealth or guarantees as FV conclusions.

Founder must review specific price/checkout presentation, current refund/return terms, approved affiliate creative rights, available format, affiliate contract, email and social promotion permission. Site QA must test headings, labels, focus, mobile widths 360/390/768/1440/1920, 200%/400% zoom, no-JS, reduced motion, native-button disabled behavior, screen reader announcement, color contrast, external-link safety and WordPress sanitization. Claude controls site performance/attribution acceptance. All untested gates remain open.

## Measurement contract: purchases, not activity theatre

**Existing analytics only**, as allowed by Claude #820; this is schema specification, **not a new pixel**.

- `qualified_human_session`: unique consent-respecting first-party session estimate with known acquisition asset; exclude proven internal/test traffic and obvious bots; note bot-classification uncertainty.
- `campaign_landing`: campaign-tagged visit, source, content ID, landing route, date; no IP stored in the analysis ledger.
- `article_to_resource_click`: optional Focus CTA and content route. Lead only becomes `confirmed_signup` when existing Jetpack/Dispatch can authoritatively report it.
- `buyer_guide_cta_click`: an offer-interest action, **not** checkout or paid order. Disabled until permissions and launch.
- `affiliate_outbound`: outbound click after documented approval, separately from **`merchant_confirmed_affiliate_sale`**. Merchant sale must be supported by partner report.
- `merchant_confirmed_owned_sale`: valid paid owned-product order from commerce with refund/chargeback and fee reconciliation; no personal order records into editorial reports.
- `realized_net_revenue`: independently settled paid revenue less refunds, merchant/platform fees, and directly attributable variable costs, in one currency, using only authorized aggregate data.
- `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_sessions` = 1000 × realized net revenue ÷ verified qualified sessions for the same 7-day or 30-day cohort. If denominator, attribution, or settlements cannot be established, result = **NULL / NOT COMPUTABLE**. Not zero.

Separate dashboards: 7-day and 30-day cohort windows. Suppress small groups (<20 qualified human sessions) from published drilldowns and flag tiny paid-order samples rather than claiming significance. Avoid durable cross-site identifiers; use server-side or native privacy-safe aggregated reporting only if existing consent and review allow it.

**Interpretation examples:** 10 known outbound clicks and zero merchant report entries means *zero confirmed sales in available reporting*, not proof the merchant made no sales. A synthetic test with 2 mock purchases is not real revenue. A verified free subscriber is a lead only. The primary objective is net paid revenue, not impressions.

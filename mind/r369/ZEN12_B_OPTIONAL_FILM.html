What are you buying when you buy twelve minutes of meditation audio?

A 2007 study found that intensive meditation training was followed by a smaller attentional blink, a laboratory difficulty noticing the second of two quick targets. The study did not test Zen12. If your goal is a routine that you will actually follow, begin by separating a seller's usable audio package from its scientific promises.

Start with an experiment you can actually do

Take twelve minutes to start a delayed task, without audio. Write a one-line goal, put distractions away, work until a timer ends and record what you finished. Repeat on another day. This is personal note-taking, not a neurological test or health intervention. For a structured version, use Final Vestige's existing free Seven Day Focus Fieldbook. No new signup form is necessary.

What the scientific research actually tests

The original 1992 attentional-blink study examined rapid character reporting. The 2007 meditation study examined intensive mental training, not vendor MP3s. A 2023 EEG review found inconsistent outcomes across fourteen heterogeneous binaural-beat entrainment studies. None establishes that Zen12 treats a condition or reliably enhances cognition.

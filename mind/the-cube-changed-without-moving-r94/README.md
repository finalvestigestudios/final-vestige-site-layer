## Conversion hypothesis and measurement

**A** = uncommercialized evidence-first text buyer guide; **B** = identical guide with an optional short film concept. UX hypothesis: film may make the distinction between a vivid experience and supporting evidence clearer; that does **not** imply higher conversions. Eligibility to run experiment requires prior affiliate approval, compliant links, external landing URL checks, video accessibility QA, experiment/consent review and explicit release authorization. Proposed metric is aggregate qualified outbound intent by categorical source/variant, not revenue; partner-validated sales are a completely separate system. Without a privacy-respecting unique identifier, these synthetic event totals cannot be called unique visitors or conversion rates.

Do not route visitors from published investigations to these unpublished pages. Do not create a second signup: /focus/ and existing Jetpack remain the only subscription path.

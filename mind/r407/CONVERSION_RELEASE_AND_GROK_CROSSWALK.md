## Decision made this run

The **primary candidate is Zen12's twelve-level audio course**, for an interested reader who is considering buying a structured, downloadable routine and wants independent guidance. It is **not yet an eligible revenue offer for Final Vestige** because named Inspire3 affiliate acceptance, exact SKU/channel permissions and an issued earning URL have not been authenticated. The alternative would be a rights-cleared FV-owned digital Mind product, but no payable existing SKU with final rights, checkout, delivery and refund evidence has been found. The published 52-page Focus Fieldbook is free and is **not** relabeled as an owned paid product.

Consequently the immediate deliverable is a genuinely usable independent guide and a *nonpaying* controlled design comparison, not a secretly activated sales funnel. The guide is valuable without any affiliate link. We do **not** promote or link to a public non-earning checkout as though it will pay Final Vestige.

## Controlled A/B hypothesis

**Question:** Among similarly qualified people seeking a structured meditation routine, does a 42-second original *optional* visual explanation above the same source-first buyer guide generate a higher **independently confirmed net paid conversion per 1,000 qualified sessions** than the text guide alone *without* reducing mobile accessibility, source engagement or existing Focus consent?

- **A:** `ZEN12_A_TEXT.html`. **B:** `ZEN12_B_OPTIONAL_FILM.html`. Buyer explanation, offer wording, disclosure, links and native-disabled merchant CTA are identical. B only adds an optional <details> storyboard, original SVG, visible transcript and VTT cue concept, no script, external resource or playback. This is not a completed playable video A/B test.
- **Randomization after release approval:** stable, server-side consent-compatible assignment by anonymized experiment bucket. Do not plant a third-party cookie, collect user agent/IP in project code, fingerprint or replace production measurement. Claude owns implementation.
- **Primary success metric:** realized paid net revenue / 1,000 qualified-human sessions on 7- and 30-day cohorts. Realized paid net = independently verified order/commission after actual refunds, chargebacks, attributable transaction fees and known costs. If session denominator or merchant export missing, print **NOT COMPUTABLE**, never $0 by assumption.
- **Secondary guardrails:** verified paid order/customer counts, paid conversion rate, eligible lead-to-paid customer rate, refunds, complaints, actual mobile user navigation, accessibility and performance. Affiliate outbound click is a behavioral event, not a financial outcome.
- **Sample:** do not interpret a handful of sessions or one payment as A/B superiority. Pre-register test duration, sample threshold and stopping/decision criteria with independent QA before launch. No significance claims from tiny samples.
- **Performance:** poster SVG <2KB target, lazy/no-network optional figure, no autoplay, no preload of unavailable video, no external fonts or tracking, same content without JS. QA measure 360/390/768/1440/1920 CSS and browser WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced-motion, keyboard focus, 200%/400% zoom, voiceover, captions/play controls once video exists.
- **Security:** no merchant URLs in `href` or `form`; no `iframe`, `script`, untrusted data in JS URLs, cloaking, auto-redirect or additional subscription form. Mark source pages `noindex,nofollow` before CMS review. When a link is finally authorized, manually inspect exact URL destination and `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` as appropriate; show adjacent intelligible earning disclosure.
- **FTC:** any future monetary relationship must be conspicuously stated beside the product recommendation. Product efficacy remains unsupported unless independent product-specific competent evidence. The disabled prototype discloses a future hypothetical commission and clearly states no active link.

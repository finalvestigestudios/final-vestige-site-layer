Evidence note / A borrowing of touch

Two brushes. One uncertain hand.

In the famous rubber-hand experiment, a visible artificial hand and a hidden real hand are brushed together. Some participants report a sense of touch or ownership associated with the fake hand. Later research showed that estimated hand location and reported ownership are not interchangeable measures.

The original 1998 account and 2011 methodological follow-up are linked below; the original new Final Vestige essay is still unpublished and deliberately has no live route.

1998 primary experiment · 2011 measurement study · 2022 mechanism dispute

Interpretation boundary. The demonstration does not establish that hypnosis, meditation audio or “brainwave” products can make any promised change to your mind.

Buyer intelligence / Research before commerce

Zen12: what you would be buying, and what remains unproven.

On its official product page, the Zen12 seller describes a Premium library of twelve levels and forty-eight MP3 recordings, including guided, music, nature and white-noise listening styles. It advertises a one-year refund guarantee. We have not independently verified delivery, the final checkout amount, the specific refund conditions or the product's claims about brainwave effects and psychological benefits.

Questions worth asking before purchasing: Is the style of recording useful to you? Have you tried free guided practice? Does the evidence relate to this particular commercial product? What is the total at checkout, and how exactly do refunds work? A structured audio library may be convenient without the advertised efficacy being scientifically established.

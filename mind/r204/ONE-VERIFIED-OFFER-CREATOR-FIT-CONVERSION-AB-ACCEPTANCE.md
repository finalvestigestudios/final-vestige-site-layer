## Executive decision

The high-intent prospect is **someone already considering a paid structured meditation-audio library and comparing it with free practice**, not every reader of a visual-perception article. The buyer's question is practical: what comes with the audio, what is the claim, what is demonstrated, and how much risk does checkout add? Answering this clearly should precede an affiliate CTA. Do not convert a reader's fascination with the rubber-hand illusion into implied enthusiasm for a meditation merchant. Preserve the research as a separate public service.

## 1. Offer evaluation and buyer fit

The seller markets a twelve-minute session as equivalent to an hour of meditation and describes automatic changes to brainwaves and health. The latter claims are **excluded** from independent Final Vestige benefit copy: no adequately relevant trials for the precise branded product were established. Publicly cached/dated observations of promotional price differ; this is not sufficient to quote a firm checkout price. A verified buyer-facing price/returns statement requires an actually accessible order page, currency, tax, conditional guarantee text and evidence of the applicable terms.

Public Inspire3 affiliate recruitment at https://affiliates.inspire3.com/ proves only that applications are advertised, not that Final Vestige's account is accepted. The connected Gmail search on this run, despite including partner/acceptance/approval terms over ninety days, returned **zero matches**; it cannot inspect partner dashboards, other inboxes or historical off-mail approvals. Approval, channel permissions, issued earning URL, allowed creatives and attribution/commission contract all remain unresolved.

Fallback: an **already existing**, rights-cleared, truly purchasable Final Vestige Mind product listed through Payhip could become a nearer-term test. ## 2. Proposed presell structure

**True problem-to-insight hook:** “You can be interested in meditation without believing every claim made about a meditation track. What, exactly, are you buying?”

**Micro-story:** In 1998 Botvinick and Cohen showed how visible touch and felt touch can be brought into an unusual relationship; 2011 research then showed that reported hand ownership and a shifting estimate of hand location need not match. The important practical lesson is not “your brain is easily reprogrammed.” It is to distinguish an experience from the mechanism claimed to have caused it. That methodological frame makes a useful starting point for assessing advertised “brainwave” effects, but it is **not evidence for Zen12**.

**One useful demonstration:** An observation ledger with three columns: “seller's precise promise”; “what the product concretely includes”; “independent evidence for that exact promised outcome.” The rubric is a consumer decision aid, not a validated treatment, attention training or psychological experiment. A fully free option is to test a quiet self-directed twelve-minute routine using the existing Fieldbook, without pretending a single session validates any mental-health or performance outcome.

**What the product offers, concretely:** Based on the exact merchant sales page, its Premium offer describes 12 levels/48 tracks and selectable audio styles. These are inventory and convenience claims, not independently confirmed delivery or efficacy.

**Objections, respectfully:** Why pay for audio if free guided sessions are widely available? Is a structured sequence worth the fee to this listener? Do the seller's bold time-equivalence and health claims have branded trials? Is the refund actually unconditional in the buyer's location? Do technological formats work on the buyer's devices? No invented user testimonial or “guaranteed confidence” claim. No false scarcity. No countdown.

**CTA:** Inactive copy, until verified: “Review Zen12's current offer and terms.” With adjacent plain-language disclosure: “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” CTA is disabled as native `<button type="button" disabled>` until individual affiliate acceptance, permitted media/channels and issued URL are verified. Once authorized, it would become a normal transparent `<a rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer">` pointing to the exact permitted account-issued URL, without undisclosed cloaking. No URL is guessed.

## 4. A/B hypothesis and release prerequisites

**Decision hypothesis, not a result:** For visitors who intentionally seek independent guidance about a meditation-audio purchase, a short **optional** original 42-second explanatory film beside a high-quality unchanged guide *might* improve completed purchases relative to the text alone. It could equally decrease purchases or introduce distraction. The evaluation unit should be a **qualified human landing session**, not a video view or raw pageview.

A = source-based text with free alternative, transparent limitations and disclosed (currently disabled) merchant CTA.
B = identical guide/offer/disclosure/CTA plus one short optional video element with poster, native controls, captions, transcript, no forced autoplay, no third-party embed, no JavaScript requirement to read the core copy. The original storyboard and VTT are drafts, NOT a working video. A fair experiment should randomize eligible sessions consistently using an approved first-party mechanism; neither variant may silently change merchant terms, layout, prices or disclosures.

Before any traffic experiment, demand: editor-reviewed rendered MP4/WebM no more than 45 seconds; locally hosted poster; verified synchronized captions; transcript; visible play/pause; keyboard controls; `prefers-reduced-motion` and data-saver consideration; first-frame poster/no autoplay; mobile QA at 360/390/768/1440/1920; no horizontal overflow; readable disclosure in each variant; performance budget; no third-party player, cookie or duplicate analytics. **Real browser and Lighthouse evidence currently absent**. Do not present static assertions as WCAG certification.

## 5. Measurement contract and privacy limits

Reuse the **existing** first-party/Jetpack/approved analytics configuration; do not install a new script, new database, subscriber list or paid SaaS. Proposed dimensions: `utm_source` (actual social network), `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_cluster_pilot_2026_11` (only after approved), `utm_content=<immutable actual Grok asset_id>`. Verify that the link is actually available in the published video's native/bio destination; do not assume caption hyperlinks work on every platform.

Event meanings:
- `campaign_landing`: visited tagged route; still subject to bot/QA filtering.
- `article_to_focus_click`: user activated that CTA; **not** a subscriber.
- `confirmed_signup`: only from authoritative Jetpack list confirmation under existing consent; **not** a purchase.
- `affiliate_click`: click on approved earning link, only if existing authorized measurement can observe; **not** a sale.
- `merchant_paid_conversion`: uniquely reconciled paid order / affiliate partner's confirmed conversion report; **not** a checkout start.
- `net_revenue`: actual gross receipts or earned confirmed commission minus attributable refunds, fees and adjustments under the accounting definition. Avoid counting unsettled commission as realized revenue. Label timing/settlement clearly.

Primary KPI for a 7/30-day cohort: **realized net revenue × 1,000 / qualified-human landing sessions**. If denominator or merchant confirmation missing, label `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, never 0. Secondary: confirmed orders, conversion rate per qualified session, confirmed subscriber-to-buyer rate, median order value when available, refunded or reversed sales, time to paid settlement. Attribution from a third-party merchant may be incomplete; record *unattributed* orders separately instead of backfilling guesses. Very small samples require exact counts and uncertainty flags; avoid claiming uplift at a handful of orders.

Collect no names/emails/precise IPs in this draft schema and use no fingerprinting. Respect existing consent/cookie policies. Prefer aggregate per-asset counts with privacy-safe retention and access limitation. Any genuine checkout conversion must be corroborated with merchant records, not inferred from client-side button events.

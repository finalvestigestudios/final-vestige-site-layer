Researchers placed an unexpected gorilla in a task designed for attentive experts. The result is stranger—and more useful—than “our brains are easily fooled.” The real question is what the observer was looking for.

A case worth examining

Research evidence

The surprising image

In 2013, researchers inserted a gorilla image in a CT-scan search experiment. Twenty of 24 participating radiologists did not report it; the authors cautioned against assuming this experimental result measures everyday clinical error.

Notice what the task excludes

Examine a safe desk scene twice: first for writing instruments, then for light and shadow. Record what was observed versus what was inferred. This is a reflection exercise, not a diagnostic experiment.

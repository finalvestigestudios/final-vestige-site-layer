The Mind & Perception Archive

The mind is a witness that does not see everything. Here, an experiment is not a promise of transformation—it is the beginning of an investigation.

Explore attention's blind spots, the historical record of suggestion, low-pressure practices and independent examinations of tools that claim to alter awareness. Each route separates what was observed from what was inferred.

01 / Experiments

Perception & attention

Count the passes. Miss the visitor. Know the trick, and discover that a different detail can still escape. Our proposed The Gorilla Was in the Room investigation is unpublished; begin with the live Change Blindness article.

Research question: What does a sincere observation omit?

02 / The archive

History of suggestion

Read period claims alongside surviving documents and later experiments—not as a single seamless tale of psychic powers. Start with Puységur's Animal Magnetism Files and the published Franklin commission / Mesmerism investigation.

Research question: When did an interpretation become an alleged fact?

03 / Your notebook

Practice & reflection

Simple personal observations need no expensive app, exceptional claims or public self-disclosure. Our existing Seven Day Focus Fieldbook offers a starting place and continues to use the one established Jetpack funnel. Nothing on this archive creates another mailing list.

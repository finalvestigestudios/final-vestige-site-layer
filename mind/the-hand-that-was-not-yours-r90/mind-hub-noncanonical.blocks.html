Feature investigation / unpublished source manuscript

The hand that was not yours

In 1998, researchers showed how seeing a hand stroked in time with touches on a hidden hand could alter reported ownership. Twenty-two years later, expectancy researchers asked whether the participant already knew which experience the experiment expected. A puzzle about bodies became a puzzle about evidence.

Unpublished: this feature is a source-controlled editorial manuscript awaiting review. It is not linked to a dead or imagined public URL.

The archive standard

An observation is not a mechanism. A historical account is not independent corroboration. A seller's promise is not a trial.

Every investigation names its sources and limits. Commerce lives in clearly labeled guides away from investigative conclusions. Every proposed earning link must disclose commissions at the point of recommendation and remain disabled until separate approval is documented.

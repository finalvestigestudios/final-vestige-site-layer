## Measurement / user privacy / product truth
Use currently approved first-party analytics, not a second tracker/script or paid SaaS. Allowed campaign keys: utm_source=platform, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=mind_topic_yyyy_mm, utm_content=immutable_verified_asset_id. No invented actual IDs. Require consent under site's policy; report only aggregate qualified-human sessions. Never store a person’s IP, contact data, device fingerprint or full query-string in GitHub Issues.

Separate campaign_landing, article_to_focus_click, confirmed_signup (provider-confirmed only), affiliate_click (outbound intent only), merchant_confirmed_paid_sale (external settlement or authenticated owned order), refund, processor/platform fee and paid-commission settlement. A click is never a sale.

KPI when measurable: 1000 × (independently verified gross attributable paid amount − refunds − fees − attributable costs) ÷ qualified human sessions. Null/NOT COMPUTABLE when denominator or settlement evidence missing. Report 7/30-day cohorts, paid conversion rate, paid orders/customers, gross, net, lead→customer rate only if compliant linkability exists, and warn at fewer than 30 paid conversions. Never substitute views or followers for realized revenue.

Require real media, captions, transcript, controls, static fallback, WCAG/mobile and Lighthouse measurements plus merchant-approved tracking before running. No experiment results claimed.

## Independent A/B hypothesis, not evidence of success
- **Population:** only human-qualified consent-eligible sessions arriving at the exact approved first-party guide; bot/unknown quality separate.
- **Arms:** A contains a useful complete text guide. B has identical price/refund/support/evidence/CTA copy plus a non-autoplay short original video, captions, static poster and full transcript. Users who disable JS must be able to read and navigate both.
- **Randomization:** only if QA/consent/release approve, sticky within first-party pseudonymous consented cohort or server assignment without cross-site tracking. No browser fingerprinting, third-party tags or second analytics script. If assignment cannot be privacy-safe, use a descriptive sequential pilot rather than falsely calling it randomized.
- **Primary endpoint:** **independently confirmed paid customer/order within 30 days, net of refunds/fees**; 7-day early directional observation. Do not use affiliate_outbound as purchase.
- **Secondary:** qualified sessions, guide CTA clicks, confirmed leads, completed offers, refunds/chargebacks, source-to-page intent match, mobile accessibility/support questions. Do not optimize clicks as a substitute for sales.
- **Rate:** realized net revenue / qualified human sessions × 1,000, only if denominator and settled revenue are independently confirmed. Mark unknown vs truly zero. Also paid conversion rate = verified paid orders / qualified sessions, lead-to-customer = confirmed paid customers / verified confirmed leads where linkage and consent exist.
- **Decision:** no winner declaration under tiny samples; show sample sizes, uncertainty and conversion-event coverage. Do not manufacture statistical significance. Hold whichever design damages accessibility/trust or exceeds performance budget even if clickthrough appears higher.
- **Performance budget hypothesis:** B incremental media **≤1 MB** after encoding where feasible, preload=none, same first-content render budget, no external font/player/analytics dependency, poster ≤100KB, video on user intent only. These are targets not tested outcomes.

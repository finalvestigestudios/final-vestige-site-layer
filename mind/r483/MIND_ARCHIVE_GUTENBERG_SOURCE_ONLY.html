How to use this archive

Choose a phenomenon; inspect the original study; compare an alternative explanation; try a safe, optional demonstration; decide whether a free resource helps. The seller's product appears only as an independently assessed option, not as a conclusion of the investigation.

Unpublished commercial policy: If we later include a purchase link, any commission will be explained beside that particular recommendation. No subscription is required to read the investigation. The existing Jetpack/Dispatch opt-in and welcome remain unchanged.

Accessibility proposal: HTML headings and links work without JavaScript; card layout collapses to one column on narrow screens; no motion, overlay, autoplay or form. WordPress sanitizer, actual typography, 360/390/768/1440/1920 viewport, keyboard, contrast and screen-reader validation are not yet run. No canonical route or nav change authorized.

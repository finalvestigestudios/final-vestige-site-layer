Try this free evidence check first

Choose a harmless twelve-minute task and define one observable outcome before starting. Write down your predicted result. Run one quiet session and record interruptions, task completion and conditions. Repeat with a familiar, non-commercial sound on another day and log the same variables. Practice, sleep, timing and expectancy can confound a tiny comparison; it tells you about your preferences, not a product's established causal effect.

What the science actually says

A 2024 preregistered systematic review covered 19 digital mindfulness studies involving 1,654 participants; positive findings coexisted with at least as many nonsignificant results. A 2023 review of 14 binaural-beat EEG studies found five supportive, eight contradictory and one mixed result. Neither tested Zen12 itself. Digital mindfulness review · Binaural-beat EEG review.

Decide with clear limits

Good fit to consider: someone specifically seeking a structured downloadable meditation course who understands the evidence limitations. Not established: a clinical treatment, reliable productivity accelerator, guaranteed improvement, or superior performance against free practice and competing recordings. Check the exact checkout and refund conditions, content access, devices, and whether the price is acceptable without relying on untested promises.

## Actual high-intent presell
**Audience:** a person searching “Zen12 Premium review,” “Zen12 MP3 tracks,” “Zen12 refund,” or “is Zen12 worth it” who can verify features and compare free alternatives.

**Problem-to-insight hook:** “A recording may make starting easier; it cannot borrow an unrelated attention study as proof of an hour's benefit.” The short story of an overlooked tone is relevant as a warning against false inference, not an advertisement for a brain claim.

**One useful demonstration:** twelve-minute free sit/record/review to determine if a routine is worth pursuing. This is self-observation, not a medical or efficacy test. **Explicit offer value:** seller-described 12 levels × 4 styles = 48 recordings, differentiated from a free timer. **Limits and objections:** no personal product testing; product outcomes unknown; full price, format accessibility, refunds/fulfillment and buyer rights to be verified at purchase. **FTC disclosure:** immediately next to native disabled control. Public seller offer URL belongs in source matrix only; no account-issued earning URL until verification.

**Controlled hypothesis:** Among genuine high-intent visitors who reach a legitimately approved purchasing page, text-only A vs identical core copy + entirely optional 42-second captioned/video B might alter *independently verified paid-conversion probability* or improve decision quality. It might also harm load speed or distract. Hypothesis is not a forecast.

**Experimental preconditions:** verify named affiliate acceptance + approved channels/account link, founder commercial and editorial release, track actual posted creator assets, ensure real merchant order/commission outcome is observable without violating privacy, complete real browser a11y/AT, mobile 360/390/768/1440/1920, WCAG 2.2 AA and performance review; video itself must be original, rendered, caption-aligned, accessible without JS, with transcript/poster and no autoplay. Keep the preexisting R210 pair as canonical control until founder explicitly selects replacement; R219 alternatives are copy candidates. No live randomization yet.

**Randomization/attribution only after acceptance:** assign A/B server-side or by a lightweight privacy-safe method using existing approved instrumentation; avoid personal IDs or invasive fingerprinting. Keep all site navigation, free exercise, and CTA phrasing identical except the video. Collect no new PII and no new tracker. Use existing consent handling, platform policy and server/merchant-verified aggregation.

**Primary metric:** confirmed net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions. Secondary paid conversion rate, provider-reported cleared commissions, paid orders, refund/chargeback rate, and confirmed opt-in-to-order rate; tertiary CTA click and engagement. Observed uncertainty must be shown, with small sample warning. No claim of significant lift from a handful of purchases.

**Event contract and allowed data:** `asset_id` ONLY if actual supplied by Grok, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, page key, source category and a small aggregate timestamp bucket; no names, emails, IP hashes, persistent visitor IDs, cross-domain fingerprinting, copied order identifiers, or new paid SaaS. `qualified_human_session_estimate` → `mind_article_visit` → `focus_cta_click` (optional) → `focus_confirmed_optin` (provider-confirmed where permitted) → `presell_cta_view` / `affiliate_click` (outbound only) → `merchant_paid_order_verified` / `affiliate_commission_cleared` → `refund_or_chargeback_verified`.

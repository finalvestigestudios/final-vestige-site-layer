What was actually tested?

The visual task

Raymond, Shapiro & Arnell (1992) demonstrated poorer report of a second rapid target at particular delays after processing a first.

That is a result about an experimental visual task, not your permanent attention span.

The training study

Slagter and colleagues (2007) studied three months of intensive meditation training and an attentional-blink task. Zen12 was not the intervention.

Three months of training cannot be replaced in the headline by a twelve-minute branded recording.

## The footnote is part of the story, not a footnote to our checking

A celebrated 1973 study is often introduced with two numbers: eight apparently healthy pseudopatients and twelve psychiatric hospitals. Those were the figures David Rosenhan reported in the analyzed account in *Science*. But the lower left of printed page 258 contains a detail that matters: note 6 says that data from a **ninth pseudopatient were excluded** because the person had reportedly changed details of his personal history. The paper describes marital status and parental relationships as examples.

We inspected the university-hosted scan of Rosenhan's original article on 11 November 2026, particularly printed pages 251 and 258. Page 251 provides the analyzed eight and twelve hospitals; page 258 supplies the exclusion. This resolves a numerical ambiguity in the R299 manuscript. It does **not** independently establish whether the omitted participant existed, what happened at the hospital, or whether the published exclusion was justified. A historical writer who turns this footnote into a named biography without corroboration is making the same mistake the archive is meant to prevent.

The subsequent dispute has its own chronology. Psychiatrist Robert Spitzer questioned the inference made from a deceptive symptom-presentation design in the 1970s. Harry Lando's 1976 supplemental report describes both undetected simulation and aspects of positive hospital care, a combination that complicates one-sided retellings. A later author may associate Lando with the ninth participant, but the primary 1973 footnote does **not** provide his name. Positive treatment must not be advertised as the demonstrated reason for exclusion.

In 2019, journalist Susannah Cahalan revisited the participants and archives. Alison Abbott's *Nature* review continuation, printed page 623, reports Cahalan's search and her contact with Bill Underwood. It also describes discrepancies attributed to that work. We read that publisher-hosted partial review page, not all of Abbott's review, not Cahalan's entire book, and not private patient charts. References to six untraced people belong to later accounts and must remain explicitly attributed. An unlocated record is not proof that its subject was fictitious. In 2023 historian Andrew Scull made a more forceful fraud argument; state it as his scholarly characterization, not an independent final adjudication by Final Vestige.

## Product fit before conversion copy

[Inspire3's catalog](https://inspire3.com/) promotes a variety of self-development products with broad claims about mind and performance. Those are merchant representations. The exact [Zen12 checkout](https://zen12.com/getstarted) on 11 November 2026 described **12 levels and 48 MP3 recordings**, guides, support, bonus items, a money-back presentation, a seller-managed price total, and a time display. A checkout describes a package; it does not supply independent evidence of efficacy or even verified success processing a particular customer's refund. Avoid publishing the dynamic public price, discount, bonus countdown or guarantee as an FV entitlement. Do not invent a price or promise.

The seller's [affiliate recruitment page](https://affiliates.inspire3.com/) publicly advertises a network and compensation schedule. That **does not verify account-specific FV publisher acceptance**, permission to promote on a site or YouTube/TikTok/email, an issued earning link, the current product-specific tracking terms, cleared payout or refunds. The active CTA remains disabled. Hypnosis Live Total Focus and Brainwave Shots Focus Shot are different products with distinct audio formats and policies; general mindfulness/hypnosis/binaural-beat research cannot be mapped as evidence of benefits from those proprietary products. Any health, cognitive enhancement or financial promises require real relevant support; otherwise omit them.

The legitimate high-intent visitor is someone considering paying for a *structured recording library*, not someone seeking a supernatural or clinical cure. An original presell hook should ask a practical question: **“Would twelve organized levels help you maintain a practice, or are you paying for a result the evidence cannot promise?”** The honest package value is structured access; the unanswered question is product-specific outcomes. A free, quiet, twelve-minute observation routine and the existing [Seven Day Focus Fieldbook](https://finalvestige.com/focus/) make the comparison useful even if no purchase follows. The public Focus site advertises email welcome delivery, but provider-confirmed signup, consent, link receipt and attribution remain untested—not a failed delivery, merely **unverified**.

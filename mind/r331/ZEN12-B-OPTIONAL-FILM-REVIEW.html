Test the practical need for a recording

  1. Pick one small, safe reading or writing task.
  2. Set a twelve-minute timer and work without trying to eliminate thoughts.
  3. Write down what was actually finished and one interruption.
  4. Repeat on a comparable day. This is personal reflection, not a clinical experiment or a Zen12 trial.

Evidence: laboratory attention research is not an MP3 trial

Thompson's 1980 face illusion and later experiments show why a vivid experience does not establish a single mechanism. Wong et al. (2010) found comparable Thatcher-like inversion effects in non-face objects; Psalta and Andrews (2014) differentiated expression recognition from noticing grotesque feature orientation. Read the 2010 study abstract and the 2014 study abstract. Neither paper tested Zen12.

Try a Free Twelve-Minute Observation First

Pick one unfinished task. Write its smallest next step, start a twelve-minute timer, then record whether you began. Repeat on several days. This does not diagnose distraction or demonstrate medical benefit. It helps you discover whether a small routine fits your life.

The Experiment Is Not an Advertisement

Peter Thompson published the original Thatcher illusion in 1980, and later researchers found orientation-dependent effects with non-face objects. The work shows how perception can depend on orientation and task; it does not establish a therapeutic or cognitive effect of buying an MP3. Product-specific objective health claims need suitable independent scientific substantiation. FTC guidance also requires transparent disclosure of material commercial relationships.

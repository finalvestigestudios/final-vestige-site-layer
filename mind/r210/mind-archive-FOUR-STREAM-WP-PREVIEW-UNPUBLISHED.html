One useful test · No purchase required

Did you read the paragraph—or did your eyes?

Read a short unfamiliar passage. Mark the moments you notice your thoughts leaving it. Cover the page and write three things you remember. A mark records when you noticed, not when the lapse began. One attempt can show you a question worth asking; it cannot diagnose a condition or validate a product.

Primary research: Reichle, Reineberg & Schooler, *Eye Movements During Mindless Reading* (2010).

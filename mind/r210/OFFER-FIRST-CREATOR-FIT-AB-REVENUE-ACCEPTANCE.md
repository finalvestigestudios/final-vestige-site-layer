## Attribution — specification for Claude, not a deployed tracker

Events and evidence: `qualified_human_session` tagged by verified Grok `asset_id`, `utm_source`, `utm_campaign` → actual article visit → relevant internal `cta_click` → authoritative Jetpack `lead_confirmed` (optional) → gated `merchant_outbound_click` → independently verified `merchant_paid_order_confirmed` or `affiliate_commission_cleared` → `refund_and_fees_reconciled`.

Do **not** send email, IP, device fingerprint, customer names, full query strings or merchant customer IDs into page analytics. Aggregate consent-respecting source/campaign daily counts, filter nonhuman hits, suppress high-cardinality small cells. Dedupe paid orders only in restricted merchant reconciliation, expose aggregated cohorts. An outbound click is **not** a paid order, an email form interaction is not provider-confirmed opt-in, and gross sale value is not cleared affiliate revenue.

KPI (only when inputs authenticated): **(settled FV-owned cash receipts + cleared affiliate commissions − refunds − chargebacks − transaction fees) × 1,000 ÷ qualified-human sessions.** The actual value is **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero, without the denominator and settlement records. Respect independent owned-order vs affiliate-commission currency and cohort separation.

## Experiment (pre-registered design, no traffic experiment yet)
A: text-only independent Zen12 buyer guide. B: the identical main copy, adjacent disclosure and disabled CTA, plus optional original 42-second factual video segment with an original poster, complete transcript and .vtt. Source proof is parity after removal of the optional film block. Production film must be encoded, captions verified with the actual final audio, no autoplay, poster fallback, controls keyboard accessible, no heavy video load before user action, transcript visible to no-JS visitors, and no video if performance constraints fail.

**Hypothesis only:** B may improve buyers' comprehension of evidentiary limits and subsequent verified order rate. It could also distract and reduce purchase intent; do not claim it will improve conversion. Pre-specify traffic allocation on the approved first-party server or existing measurement stack; persist assignment only to the extent consent/legal review permits. Equal CTA, order eligibility, pricing and disclosure for both variants. Do not randomize users based on health profiling or create a new identifying cookie without consent review. No paid ad test or new SaaS.

**Primary commercial outcome:** independently merchant-confirmed, non-refunded net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions by cohort and variant. Secondary: paid conversion rate, unique paying customers, gross paid orders, refund rate, confirmed opt-in to customer conversion. Tertiary diagnostics: article-to-guide click, affiliate_outbound, sessions, view-through and engaged time. A click, a pending order, a signup and a "thank you" page view are NOT a paid conversion.

**Privacy-safe event contract / Claude engineering review only (NOT deployment):**
- campaign_landing: UTC date, first-party path, sanitized source, medium=organic_social, campaign=choice_blindness_pilot_2026_11, content=immutable published Grok asset_id (only after verified), variant A/B where applicable, consent status and qualified_human yes/no/unknown; no IP, user agent, unhashed email, precise geographic location or fingerprint.
- article_to_resource_click: aggregate link destination /focus/, source content topic, prior campaign attribution where trustworthy.
- confirmed_signup: Jetpack/Dispatch existing opt-in confirmation status **only if independently observable**; do not mirror addresses into analytics or create duplicate lead IDs.
- affiliate_outbound: event at disclosure-adjacent *authorized* merchant link; no event until partner terms permit; an outbound does not prove purchase.
- merchant_confirmed_order: ingested from authenticated merchant/payhip/refersion reconciliation only, order occurrence date, amount, currency, product SKU, authenticated settled status, refund and net fees at aggregate level; no unsalted raw customer identifiers.
- refund/chargeback: independent reconciliation and 7/30-day cohort updates. Data missing = UNKNOWN, not zero.

**Numerator:** verified settled gross receipts minus actual refunds, payment/affiliate/network fees and allowable adjustments (net as explicitly defined; distinguish revenue from profit). **Denominator:** qualified-human first-party landing sessions from campaign-linked published asset IDs in defined 7/30-day cohorts. Use only an approved identity-minimizing reconciliation method if merchant systems offer lawful attribution. If exact source-to-order mapping unavailable, report unattributed merchant sales separately without assigning them to Grok.

**KPI:** 1000 × net realized revenue / qualified-human sessions. If either side is unavailable or denominator zero, return NOT COMPUTABLE. Provide tiny-sample warnings and non-causal descriptive comparisons; no significance claims without pre-specified power and enough eligible orders. Record 7-day acquisition and 30-day refund-adjusted maturity, and do not mix cohort boundaries.

## Decision: do not confuse productivity with commerce

This lane has a large backlog of scientifically sourced, unpublished pieces and two versions of a meditation buyer guide. It has no independently confirmed approved Inspire3 earning link, no documented partner permission to use that link in website/social/email distribution, no rights-authenticated paid Mind SKU on Payhip, and no reconciled paid commission. A buyer-facing CTA that appears active before those approvals would be commerce theater, not conversion.

The current **smallest potentially high-impact defect is one step earlier**. Read-only authenticated WP inspection recorded by R141 found that the published Change Blindness post and Puységur archive page have **no direct contextual `/focus/` href in their stored content bodies**. A navigation link elsewhere is not an equivalent in-article transition. The first-party Focus landing exists; what is missing is evidence that a reader reaching the relevant paragraph encounters a compelling, voluntary next action. This is a proposed remediation, not a claim of measured abandonment.

We authored a single, standalone source-controlled `article-to-focus-cta-review-only.html` block that can be considered for *two* specific published documents, not inserted into every article mechanically. It uses one ordinary, visible `https://finalvestige.com/focus/` link, no extra form, no subscription promise, no scripts, no affiliate earning links, and no tracking. A copy review by Site QA, homepage lead, and founder must precede an engineering-owned CMS write in a separately approved release. If Site QA finds a genuine native Focus signup/confirmation failure, fix that before encouraging more traffic.

## Precise merchant and privacy review

**Zen12 Premium:** The [merchant presentation](https://zen12.com/hnp) describes twelve levels and four listening formats, totaling 48 MP3 recordings. It claims twelve-minute sessions provide benefits comparable to longer meditation; this has not been substantiated by independent research on this exact product. Do not promise reduced anxiety, medical improvement, wealth outcomes, accelerated cognition, or a specific final price. Compare only documented format and tradeoffs with free options. The official [affiliate portal](https://affiliates.inspire3.com/) advertises a public scheme but offers no proof of *our* account acceptance. Its sales percentages and earnings anecdotes must not become Final Vestige forecasted commission or revenue.

**Total Focus:** The [specific merchant page](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus) lists J.A. Amos, 33 minutes and 47 MB. It must not be confused with a 44-minute product, Hypnotize Yourself. The directly opened page shows multiple price options; none constitutes a verified affiliate checkout total. The merchant's concentration and NLP language is advertising, not independent proof. Its privacy policy permits some purchaser details to be shared with affiliates: [Inspire3 privacy](https://inspire3.com/privacy/). Do not accept unnecessary raw buyer names/emails as a condition of reporting if aggregate partner summaries can serve the purpose. Review negotiated terms before introducing any earning link.

**FTC requirement:** A future active earning recommendation will display the plain disclosure immediately beside that specific control: “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” General footer policies and ambiguous “affiliate” labels cannot substitute. The exact benefit claims must follow the [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) and [FTC Endorsement Guides](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking). An educational gorilla experiment is not product-specific substantiation.

## Controlled comparison, measurement and economics

**First experiment:** after acceptance, stage the in-article Focus card on two *known* content pages while retaining the current single Jetpack/Focus opt-in. Verify for source/caption integrity, 360/390/768/1440 mobile and keyboard/no-JS, reader comprehension and signup flow. Use a holdback or phased copy-only comparison only if the founder approves an experiment and the instrumentation can distinguish actual human sessions. Without that evidence, compare qualitative clarity, not claimed conversion uplift.

**Second experiment, conditional on valid affiliate authorization:** preserve Zen12 A as independent, useful text-first buyer information. B contains *the same* buyer information, source limits and near-CTA disclosure with an optional **original 40-second film** explaining how task instructions affect awareness. The existing film is currently a storyboard with six caption segments, poster and transcript; it is not a playable video. If produced, use a lightweight same-origin poster and accessible captions/transcript, native controls, preload none and no autoplay or third-party player. Check media rights and a no-JS equivalent. Allocate variants without altering the sales facts and without reducing the clarity of the disclosure. Conversion differences on tiny cohorts are hypotheses, not claims.

For an eligible paid funnel, use these stages: `utm_source` + `utm_medium=organic_social` + `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11` + `utm_content=<real_grok_asset_id>` → *qualified human* article visit → contextual resource click → observable native *confirmed* subscriber → independently observed guide view → approved affiliate outbound (distinct event) → actual partner-reported paid order/commission → refunds/chargebacks/payment fees → realized net revenue. A user can see the buyer guide without subscribing. A merchant purchase need not be attributed to a particular email address; use privacy-safe campaign aggregates and separate unattributed order buckets.

**KPI:** `net_revenue_per_1000 = 1000 × settled_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`, but only for a cohort with a nonzero *qualified-human* denominator and sufficient reconciled merchant returns/fees. Otherwise return `null / NOT COMPUTABLE`, not zero and not a confidently estimated income figure. Preserve seven- and thirty-day cohorts, age of cohort, merchant reporting lag, anonymized attribution coverage and tiny-sample caveat. Add no duplicate analytics scripts or paid services. No collection of personal contact information by landing-page JS.

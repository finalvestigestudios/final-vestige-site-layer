2. Name exact Total Focus recording and correct format/length/author as **seller-described**.
3. Separate independent research from intervention-specific proof: Raz Stroop experiment ≠ Total Focus outcome trial; Loftus misinformation effect ≠ clinical hypnosis or focus cure.
4. Acknowledge price instability and unavailable complete terms. The public page's fourteen-day satisfaction statement is a seller promise, not independent fulfillment verification.
5. Provide a useful no-cost, nonclinical three-day focus baseline and existing Focus Fieldbook route.
6. Address mental-health/medical overreach and safe listening: no driving/operating machinery while attention-altering audio plays.
7. Put an understandable prospective earning disclosure directly before the disabled offer control.
8. Do not imply personal use, shopper reviews, payout, price, product creative rights, merchant acceptance, therapeutic or financial guarantees.

## Conversion hypothesis, honest reporting and sample adequacy
Before any test, record eligible page views and a variant assignment methodology agreed with QA, test that variants differ **only** by optional film, control campaign/source mix, and ensure no personal identifiers are collected. When an approved affiliate link later exists, measure an anonymized outbound intent count by variant and topic, aggregated at >=20 cells; do not claim measured conversions from this alone. A sale needs authorized merchant reporting and explicit data governance—never individual purchaser PII. Users may reject analytics and still access the full guide and the optional existing Focus resource. If lawful aggregate measurement is not technically possible, run a qualitative editorial review without conversion claims instead.

Stop immediately if missing disclosure, fake efficacy implication, performance regression, inaccessible controls, caption problems, personal-data leakage, or forbidden merchant destination is detected.

**Event contract / source of truth**:
`qualified_human_session`: documented bot/QA exclusion and deduplication, with approved consent.
`landing_view`: unique page visit actually observable using existing first-party tools.
`article_to_focus_click`: navigational action, not a lead.
`confirmed_jetpack_lead`: authoritative backend double opt-in, not client-side PDF revelation, `?subscribe=`, `is-subscriber` CSS, email entered, or download click.
`independent_guide_click`: visit to purchase-evidence guide, not a checkout.
`affiliate_click`: outbound click to an *approved* earning link; event does not exist in the inactive version.
`merchant_confirmed_paid_order`: independently verified sale, with payment status and merchant order receipt where access is permitted.
`received_affiliate_commission`: merchant statement/settled amount, separate from a reported sale, adjusted for reversals.
`refunded_order`: posted refund or chargeback, not just refund requested.
`realized_net_revenue`: genuinely received revenue minus refunds, fees, chargebacks and applicable recorded direct costs; disclose incomplete costs.

**KPI:** `1000 × realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions` for a well-defined seven-day and thirty-day cohort; denominator > 0 and reliable settlement required. Otherwise report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not "$0", even if a tracking URL got clicks. Keep a separate verified-paid-conversion rate and independently confirmed lead-to-buyer rate when records support it. Never equate views, outbound traffic, checkout starts or signup UI with confirmed orders.

Use already-approved WP/Jetpack/Ahrefs native or existing server metrics as appropriate; no new scripts, pixels or paid vendors. Attribution often becomes partial at merchant boundaries. Label unknown/organic-unattributed honestly rather than inventing cross-domain identity joins. Only publish aggregate results with a minimum cohort threshold selected by the privacy owner. Obtain approval before any test account signup or payment.

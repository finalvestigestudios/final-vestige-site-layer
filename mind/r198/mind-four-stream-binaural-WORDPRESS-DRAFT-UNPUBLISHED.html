03 / The field method

Practice & Reflection

Try an observation worth keeping—without pretending a notebook is a clinical trial.

The three-column evidence drill

Spend ten quiet minutes on one small task. When finished, separate the conditions from the feeling and from the explanation. This is personal observation, not a treatment or proof of an EEG effect.

What I didWrite the duration, setting and task.
What I noticedRecord interruptions and sensations without diagnosing them.
What I can inferAvoid explaining your brain from one experience.

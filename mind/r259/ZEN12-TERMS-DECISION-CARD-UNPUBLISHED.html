Run the free comparison first

  1. Choose one small, measurable task and decide what completion means.
  2. For seven sessions, try twelve minutes of quiet, a free timer, or audio you already have permission to use.
  3. Record completion, not just your feelings: minutes practiced, interruptions, actual task outcome, and any discomfort.
  4. Separate observation from interpretation. Do not treat a personal routine comparison as a clinical experiment.
  5. Ask which additional feature would make a paid library useful: guided structure, convenience, variety or accessibility.

Control A is an independent text-only buyer guide. Treatment B has identical buyer-guide main content plus an **optional original 42-second film concept**, full transcript, source-created poster and six timed caption cues. No autoplay, tracking scripts, duplicate forms, JS dependency, merchant link or paid motion assets. This is a proposed *hypothesis* that a brief optional clarification may improve qualified purchase decisions; it is **not an observed lift**. Film is not encoded/playable or in a real A/B test yet.

**Primary success after release approval:** independently verified refunded/fee-adjusted **net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** in prespecified 7- and 30-day cohorts. Secondary metrics: paid conversion rate, confirmed orders/customers, gross/net, lead-to-paid transition if legitimately observable. Diagnostic only: article depth, compliant CTA clicks, first-party qualified sessions, confirmed Jetpack leads, film engagement. No revenue denominator → **NOT COMPUTABLE**, never fabricated zero.

**Randomization precondition:** Claude confirms existing first-party analytics architecture and consent; do not install an additional script. Preserve asset_id and utm_campaign at landing, assign variant using approved first-party mechanism and persist in privacy-safe fashion (no email/IP/device fingerprint). If this cannot be done without cost/privacy risk, test two equally sourced campaign links as a nonrandomized directional comparison and label it as such; do not claim causal A/B.

**Proposed minimal event schema, not deployed:** `{event_name, first_party_anonymous_session_id, campaign_asset_id, utm_campaign, landing_id, variant, timestamp_bucket, consent_state}`. Allow `landing_view`, `qualified_session`, `focus_cta_click`, `confirmed_focus_lead`, `affiliate_click` ONLY AFTER earning-link approval; merchant `paid_order_verified`, `refund`, `fee` require authoritative merchant exports. Never pass email, personal name, IP, full URL query text or raw message to attribution tables. Avoid public browser event payload containing payment/order details. Vendor reports reconciled securely by Commerce with opaque purchase IDs; merchant reports, not outbound clicks, determine paid sales.

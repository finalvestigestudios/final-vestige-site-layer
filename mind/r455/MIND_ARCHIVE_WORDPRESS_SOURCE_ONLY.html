How the archive makes its decisions

We begin with the original experiment or historical document, record what it actually measured or described, and distinguish interpretation from proof. We don't convert an experience of suggestion into a medical, cognitive or financial guarantee. We don't present customer testimonials we haven't obtained or retail products we haven't personally evaluated as firsthand reviews.

The published Affiliate Disclosure sets general principles; any future paid recommendation will also carry a visible near-link disclosure. See Editorial Standards.

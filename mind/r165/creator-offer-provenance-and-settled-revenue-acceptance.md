## Original product guide — control and treatment

**A (control):** independent evidence-first text guide, source references, a three-column free exercise, existing Focus CTA, same offer evaluation and near-control conditional affiliate disclosure. No added form, SDK, analytics vendor, auto-navigation, merchant URL, or JavaScript.

**B (hypothesis):** identical page with an original **42-second optional** captioned video component behind a manual play control, loaded only after user initiation. Poster, self-hosted lightweight MP4, WebVTT captions and readable transcript are required *before* real testing; today's draft contains a timed 42-second script and static visual concept **only**, not a playable video. A user who never presses play sees every critical source, product limit, disclosure and CTA. No coercive play, no artificial progress count.

For an authorized experiment, randomize only the allowed landing flow; declare both variants and fix the primary KPI before sampling. Do not let caching, redirects or bot traffic force assignment skew. Check 360/390/768/1440/1920; keyboard, screen reader, color contrast, tap targets, motion preference, no-JS, link security and Lighthouse. If browser testing does not occur, report **UNTESTED**, not WCAG passed.

**Primary KPI:** `(settled gross receipts - refunds - processing fees - attributable costs) / qualified_human_sessions * 1,000`, 7-/30-day acquisition cohorts. For affiliate orders, use **independently reported settled payable commission**, not the merchant's gross order value. Secondary: merchant-confirmed purchases / qualified-human sessions; confirmed-leads-to-purchasers where an exact joined consented cohort exists. If sessions are 0/unknown or merchant reporting absent, `NOT COMPUTABLE`. Label results descriptive if samples are tiny; do not declare a video winner from clicks alone. Visitor-level data should not be exported to the creator team.

**Privacy and attribution contract:** per Grok item, `utm_source=<actual_platform>&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=mind_<topic>_2026_11&utm_content=<immutable_asset_id>`. Do not mint fake asset IDs. Campaign values must be allowlisted (letters/digits/underscore/hyphen) and contain no emails, names, phone numbers or credentials. Existing first-party scripts, if any, own `campaign_landing`, `article_to_focus_click`, `offer_guide_view`, `affiliate_outbound_click`; separate `confirmed_signup`, `merchant_confirmed_purchase`, `merchant_confirmed_affiliate_sale`, `refund`, `fee`, `settled_revenue` must derive from authorized authoritative sources. Reject deduplication-unsafe event insertion and double counting. If no merchant join keys are legally/technically available, preserve separate aggregate reports and mark attribution **UNOBSERVABLE** rather than inferring causation.

### Review and research design
Phase 0 (now): run non-production source audit, independent editorial review and lab browser comparison with e.g. five common viewport widths, no-JS, keyboard, reduced motion, captions/transcript and forced media failure. Detect layout shift and network requests. No conversion claims.
Phase 1 (only after separate explicit approval): if first-party privacy review permits opt-in data collection and the merchant approves the channel, run a low-volume descriptive comparison of **eligible page presentations** and **approved outbound-link selections** for matched content. Predeclare assignment and exclusion rules. Counting presentations is not counting distinct people; bot traffic, repeat visits, route differences and small samples threaten validity. Do not claim causal conversion uplift without a defensible assignment and adequate sample.
Phase 2 (only if permitted by merchant): merchant's partner reporting may separately describe transactions; never merge click count into inferred sales.

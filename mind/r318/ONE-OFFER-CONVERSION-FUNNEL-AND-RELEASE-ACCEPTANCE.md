## 4. Controlled text-versus-video hypothesis (not an observed result)

**Research question:** Among *human, high-intent visitors who have already reached a source-based Zen12 offer comparison*, does the presence of an optional 42-second original explainer improve verified purchasing without reducing trust, accessibility, load speed or opt-in clarity?

- **A:** Complete text-only guide. **B:** Identical complete buyer text, plus optional original static poster/42-second transcript and captions plan. B currently has **NO encoded or playable video**. Each prototype is noindex and contains zero merchant links. Thus **no A/B test has run and no performance or conversion outcome can be inferred**.
- **Future release only:** Create an original compressed video with fully owned narration/art, static poster, WCAG captions/transcript; explicit user activation (no autoplay), `preload=none`, no third-party iframe/pixels. Provide a useful non-JS fallback and independent keyboard and screen-reader evaluation.
- **Randomization only after authorization and consent:** Stable source-neutral server-side A/B assignment integrated into existing first-party analytics (Claude owns implementation). Avoid session fingerprinting or repeat double counting. Disclose affiliate relationship near any earning link, and do not gate the free resource behind purchase.
- **Primary outcome:** Verified **realized net dollars / 1,000 qualified-human sessions** by 7/30-day acquisition cohort, not outbound clicks. Secondary: confirmed orders and customers, provider-confirmed leads and lead-to-customer rate, checkout error rate, refunds/fees. Diagnostic only: human sessions, scroll, watch-start and affiliate click. Flag tiny samples, intervals and inconclusive tests; do not pick a winner based on a handful of paid events.
- **Safety stop rules:** wrong seller offer, undisclosed earning link, medical claim, unusable mobile/keyboard route, misplaced CTA, broken payment or refund policy, cross-origin attribution without consent, site performance regression, or a conflicting homepage/Jetpack change.

Ordered, **separate** evidence:

2. `landing_visit`: an actual article or offer guide load; no automatic "qualified" inference.
3. `contextual_cta_click`: specific Fieldbook or properly activated merchant destination; record link type.
4. `provider_confirmed_lead`: Jetpack-confirmed subscription plus usable welcome delivery evidence where available, never a typed email or unconfirmed form submit.
5. `affiliate_click`: independently logged first-party outbound navigation after link authorization, not a sale.
6. `verified_paid_order` or `partner_reported_affiliate_conversion`: receipt or merchant report, with status.
7. `settled_net_revenue`: amounts actually accrued/cleared after refunds, discounts attributable to merchant terms, commissions, platform fees and other directly attributable costs. Never disclose customer PII into campaign dashboards.


## 6. Editorial and legal checks

FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking says readers must understand material relationships, preferably near the endorsement/link; sitewide affiliate disclosure alone is insufficient. An approved live link will display the actual plain-language wording: **"If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you."**

The button in both review prototypes is **disabled and contains no merchant URL**. Its adjacent disclosure explicitly states it is prospective and the publisher relationship is unverified. No suggestion of product experience, "clinically proven" Zen12 effect, cure, cognitive guarantee, or customer testimony.

FTC https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance requires competent reliable evidence for health-related product claims, including implied claims. Zen12 sales copy's strong promises—"meditates for you", hour-equivalence and anxiety disappearance—are classified **seller efficacy or health claims, excluded**. The primary 1976 McGurk evidence is a publisher abstract; later full 2018/2022 articles delimit generalization. That research does not establish an Inspire3 outcome.

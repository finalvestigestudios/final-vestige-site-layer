Why an audio guide can be useful without endorsing its science

An official seller may describe how a recording is organized, how long a session lasts, and what files are included. Those features can be evaluated separately from whether it creates a claimed cognitive or medical effect. The Zen12 seller describes twelve levels with four audio styles, while Hypnosis Live Total Focus and Brainwave Shots Focus Shot describe different single-session products. Nothing in perceptual psychology establishes those products' advertised outcomes.

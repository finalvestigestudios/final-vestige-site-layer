## One controlled A/B experiment, not a forecast

**A** — identical source-anchored independent buyer text with disabled CTA and prospective disclosure.

**B** — same complete buyer text, with an original optional **42-second visual concept** (new SVG poster, six caption cues and full transcript). Actual playable media **does not exist**; B cannot run live as a video treatment until original encoder output, caption timing, poster optimization, performance and accessibility are validated. The film is source-first, not testimonials or a fake consumer review.

**Hypothesis (unproven):** for qualified buyer-intent sessions, optional B could increase **settled net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions** compared to A by making the evidence distinction memorable. It could also reduce purchases by reminding visitors of uncertainty. Both are valid outcomes. Success is not an uplift in clicks alone.

**Assignment once authorized:** stable first-party random assignment, one variant per consenting eligible visitor or session per configured privacy policy, no fingerprinting, no external trackers, and no treatment that compromises the existing site. Keep exact audience/offer/campaign match between variants. Validate assignments across 360/390/768/1440/1920 widths and no-JS fallback. Do not assign variants in this draft.

**Primary outcome:** settled net contribution = independently reconciled merchant-confirmed purchases and affiliate commission payouts minus chargebacks/refunds, processing/affiliate fees and relevant direct costs; divide by qualified-human landing sessions for that variant, multiply by 1,000. If product income and affiliate commissions are mixed, report separate merchant/customer and program ledgers before summing, with appropriate attribution boundaries and no double counting.

**Secondary outcomes:** verified paid conversion rate = independently confirmed purchases / eligible human sessions; confirmed customer count (non-PII aggregate); lead-to-purchase rate only for provider-backed confirmed leads linked with permissible consent; cumulative gross, fees and refunds. Clicks, CTA impressions, article sessions, unconfirmed subscribers and watch retention are **diagnostics only**. Report 7-day and 30-day cohorts with event latency and refund-window maturity caveats. Show tiny denominators explicitly, no unearned statistical claims.

**Stopping / safety rule:** Stop a live experiment and disable the merchant action if authorization expires, link points to unexpected product, a claim/disclosure disappears, evidence shows checkout or refund friction, PII leaks, variant breaks keyboard/no-JS access, or paid reporting cannot be reconciled. Initial releases require founder approval independently of engineering readiness.

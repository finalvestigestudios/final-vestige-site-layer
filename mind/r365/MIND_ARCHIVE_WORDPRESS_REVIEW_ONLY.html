A field note on uncertainty

What would prove a claimed benefit?

First, identify the claim. Is the seller describing a format or promising improved concentration? Next, find the actual comparison: was the named product tested, on the claimed outcome, in a credible design with suitable controls? A legitimate study about suggestion does not answer every commercial question.

Read the FTC's scientific substantiation guidance. The editorial standard here is simple: show the document; show what it cannot tell us.

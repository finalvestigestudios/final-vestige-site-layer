Convenience is a feature. Efficacy is a claim.

Zen12 Premium, Hypnosis Live and Brainwave Shots are marketed for various mind-related experiences. Product descriptions are not proof that a named recording improves memory, relieves a disorder or compresses a longer practice into a few minutes.

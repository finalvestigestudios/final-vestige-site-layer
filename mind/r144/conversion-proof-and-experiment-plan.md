## Executive decision

**Prioritize documentary proof of one purchasable product over a larger volume of speculative pages.** The primary offer for high-intent readers remains Zen12 **Premium**, not its introductory program, Hypnosis Live's Total Focus, Hypnotize Yourself, or Brainwave Shots. The high-intent audience is a person already researching whether a structured set of twelve-minute meditation audio sessions merits paying for. A curious viewer of a visual illusion is **not automatically an eligible buyer**. Neither the active publication of a research post nor a visit to /focus/ constitutes a purchase.

The official [Inspire3 catalog](https://inspire3.com/) and [exact Premium sales page](https://zen12.com/hnp) were reviewed on Nov 5, 2026. The seller describes twelve levels, four style choices per level, and 48 MP3s, plus guidance and support. The advertised “one hour in twelve minutes” mechanism/benefit is seller marketing; no independent trial of this exact Premium product showing that equivalence was verified. The generic research base is **mixed**: [2019 pooled effects](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-018-1066-8), [2023 inconsistent EEG review](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0286023), [2023 null attention experiment](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12144-023-04681-3), [2024 mixed stress review](https://doi.org/10.1080/18387357.2024.2374759). A protocol-level effect must not be marketed as a product-specific clinical result.

**Eligibility first:** [Public affiliate enrollment](https://affiliates.inspire3.com/) offers signup and advertises commission possibilities, but is not a named Final Vestige acceptance record. A bounded Gmail search this morning found seller-promotional emails but no acceptance, approved exact product URL, permission to promote via YouTube/TikTok/SEO/newsletters, permission to use specific creatives, or privacy-safe sale reporting. This does **not** prove acceptance cannot exist elsewhere. Do not use any public percentage, EPC, sample link, or screenshot as FV's terms.

**Owned product fallback:** authenticated read-only WordPress site inventory currently shows **66 published posts, 81 pages**; /focus/ page ID 1579 is a free edition and /mind/ not published. Public WP search returned no Payhip content indexed under the term “payhip.” Neither that search nor earlier references to Products 001–015 is an authenticated inventory of Payhip merchant SKUs. The user-stated existence of Payhip products does not alone establish that one is a rights-cleared *paid Mind* product. **Do not name a guessed owned SKU or launch a checkout.** Commerce/Claude must provide an actual SKU, public merchant product page, ownership/rights proof, exact price/terms, fulfillment and refunds, merchant order export and paid test evidence.

## One high-intent person → one legitimate action

The approved *hypothetical* audience is an adult with explicit buying intent: “Zen12 Premium review,” “12-minute meditation audio worth paying for?” or “binaural beats science.” The buyer's pain is the decision itself: avoid paying for a promise not backed by evidence. The R144 presell answers five objections: exact contents; distinction between scientific mechanism and a demonstrated outcome; alternative zero-cost practice; price/refund uncertainty; and the seller's separate free-audio email consent. It concludes with a **disabled** merchant control and near-control FTC disclosure. Only the existing Focus Fieldbook link is active; it is a voluntary first-party resource, not a covert purchase prerequisite.

**Copy hook:** “Twelve minutes is a duration. Not proof of what those minutes can do.” This is not a promised mental transformation. **Source-backed micro-story:** two slightly different pitches can generate a perceived rhythmic fluctuation, followed by the methodological question of whether a particular audio session produces meaningful outcomes. **Useful demonstration:** free two-minute listening-preference reflection; explicitly no diagnostic or efficacy inference. **Offer value:** the seller-described choice between four audio formats across twelve levels, not any guaranteed therapeutic benefit. **Objections:** scientific uncertainty, comparators, convenience vs performance, actual checkout total and refund exercise, separate merchant newsletter.

### Controlled A/B hypothesis (NOT active)

Existing [R139 variant A](../r139/zen12-variant-a.html) and [R139 variant B](../r139/zen12-variant-b.html) retain identical purchasing information. B adds a **forty-second original video STORYBOARD**, poster/captions/transcript concept, not playable media. R144 adds a single independent presell guide and does not replace or secretly multiply the existing test arms. Hypothesis: a useful optional demonstration might improve trust and purchase completion relative to text alone; it might also distract or slow mobile users. **Neither direction is assumed.**

Before experiment activation: written partner approval and exact earning link/channel terms; seller checkout/return audit; original playable video file with captions/poster/transcript, media rights and self-hosting optimization; keyboard, 360/390/768/1440/1920, reduced-motion, no-JS and screen-reader checks; no unapproved extra tracking scripts; founder-specific approval. Preregister a single primary *settled* revenue endpoint, eligibility/traffic qualification, allocation and analysis windows. Avoid significance claims from tiny samples or early looks. If both variants don't have independent paid-conversion evidence, report experiment **NOT TESTED**, not a winner.

## First-party measurement and net revenue acceptance

**No new production analytics tags.** The event taxonomy is an engineering *contract* for Claude to implement with the existing system, subject to Site QA and founder approval. Do not claim instrumentation is live.

- `campaign_landing`: authenticated first-party page load tagged with allowlisted platform source, `utm_medium=organic_social`, campaign `mind_<topic>_2026_11`, and immutable actual Grok ID as `utm_content`. Missing asset ID => `UNATTRIBUTED`, not guessed.
- `qualified_human_session`: a conservatively defined deduplicated campaign session with strong signs of human activity under privacy/consent rules. Exclude identified bots and internal QA sessions; do **not** treat every Jetpack view as a human or store a cross-site fingerprint. Human classification can remain **UNKNOWN**.
- `article_to_resource_click`: voluntary link to the existing /focus/ page. Clicks are not signups.
- `confirmed_signup`: only the native Jetpack confirmed record, with consent-appropriate linkage if obtainable; unknown attribution stays unknown. Preserve existing confirmation and welcome content.
- `affiliate_outbound`: permitted offsite click after approved destination and consent/disclosure; never a paid sale. Do not build redirect/cloaking violating terms.
- `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`: actual partner/Payhip order with correct SKU, currency, status, transaction ID or safe dedupe key; no customer PII exported into the analytics ledger.
- `commission_reported`, `commission_settled`, `reversal`, `network_fee` and `cash_received`: distinct states; a merchant order is not cash income.

**Affiliate calculation:** Start from actual *settled* cash received for the selected cohort, including the network's netted reversals/adjustments. Deduct only additional unmatched post-settlement commission clawbacks and true affiliate/network fees not already netted. **Do not subtract a merchant's full buyer refund from an affiliate commission**, and never subtract the same commission reversal twice. Owned-product accounting is separate: verified paid product revenue less owner-funded refunds, merchant/payment fees and refunds/chargebacks not already netted (with the actual settlement ledger governing). If merchant only provides aggregate data, do not invent per-creator revenue allocation.

For each 7-day and 30-day cohort calculate `1000 * realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions` **ONLY** when the numerator, denominator and cohort linkage are defensible. Otherwise output `NOT_COMPUTABLE`. Also report orders/customers, refunds, paid conversion rate, lead-to-paid conversion if genuinely joinable, fee adjustment, source coverage, privacy/data quality and tiny-sample warnings. Never interpret unconfirmed leads, likes, views, taps or checkout starts as revenue.

**Data discipline:** allowlist known source/campaign/asset patterns; discard unrecognized query data; do not carry email addresses, billing information, raw IP, exact purchase identities or sensitive traits into a tracking URL. No custom pixels or duplicate Ahrefs/GA event scripts. Cross-source aggregation must not imply precise user identity. Retention/consent to be approved by site engineering and privacy review before live use.

# R82 — Zen12 text-only vs. educational-film pilot (REVIEW ONLY)

## Precise hypothesis, not a conversion claim
Version **A** presents the complete independently reviewed Zen12 source guide. Version **B** contains exactly the same guide content and adds an original optional forty-second educational animation, poster and transcript. The video illustrates a perception phenomenon, **not** the effectiveness of Zen12. No claim is made that B outperforms A. An animated patch is explicitly labeled an artistic change to a film, not a viewer's measured perception.

**Success is not a guaranteed purchase, a medical outcome, or an “hour of meditation in twelve minutes.”** If the founder later authorizes production, the first goal is readability, comprehension, source use and responsible decision-making. The commercial test is conditional on actual product-specific affiliate acceptance and a legal working destination. No web traffic or conversion data have been collected by this branch.

## Controlled comparison
- Keep the headline, word-for-word guide, disclosure, CTA text, source references, price policy, timing, and route to the existing Focus Fieldbook identical between A and B. Only the optional video presentation differs.
- Before any traffic allocation, establish that A and B load with no JavaScript, no extra forms, no autoplay, working keyboard/focus indicators, WCAG 2.2 AA review, clear controls and readable transcripts. Check reduced-motion preferences. Ensure video does not block guide reading.
- Film B should be first-party hosted, **preload="none"**, **controls** and **playsinline** with a sourced local poster; a matching English WebVTT track and visible transcript must remain accessible. Do not embed Vimeo/YouTube or seller media. The current source-controlled HTML intentionally has a storyboard/preview but **no video src or active player**.
- If a legitimate test becomes possible after approval, register a minimal 50/50 split method that avoids persistent IDs, cookies and fingerprinting. Server-side random allocation per independent page request can avoid collecting per-person identifiers, but it can also permit the same visitor to switch variants; document the estimand and limitations.
- **Primary editorial metrics (if privacy and traffic justify):** consenting aggregate guide reads with internal sampling? In current plan, page-view counts by ordinary first-party site aggregates only. No invasive analytics.
- **Secondary metrics:** Focus route clicks, meaningful source-link visits, eligible disclosed merchant outbound intents where partner terms allow; distinguish an outbound click from a purchase. No buyer PII intake.
- Attribute organic vs TikTok only through a short fixed source category **if** lawful, disclosed and approved. Do not record full referrer URLs, search query strings, device IDs, IPs, email addresses, raw user agents, precise timestamps or visitor histories.
- Report only synthetic prototype aggregate cells with n≥20; withholding cells does not alone prove full anonymity. Real implementation requires a privacy review covering other cross-tabulations and noisy totals. No claims of uplift from test harness outputs.
- Compute a preregistered confidence interval and practical effect threshold before calling B superior. Stop for broken accessibility, unsupported claims or data handling, not because one variant happens to lead briefly.

## Ethical copy test
Acceptable: “Zen12 is described by its seller as a series of twelve-minute MP3s in four styles; its benefits are not proven by the cited review.” Reject: “Scientifically proven to reprogram your mind, eliminate anxiety or produce an hour's meditation in twelve minutes.”

Prospective disclosure beside the disabled CTA: “If you purchase through a future approved link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. No earning link is currently active.” When a real link is approved the tense and precise actual relation must be checked; disclosing a prospective connection must never imply acceptance has occurred.

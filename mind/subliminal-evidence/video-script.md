# 40-second short-form film script: The Wrong Label

**Production status:** script, NOT an actual video file. Original graphics only. No copyrighted participant material, source-paper screenshots, product advertisements or affiliate links.

**00–06 |** Two unbranded, illustrated tape boxes on an obsidian surface. **Voice:** “What if a self-help tape made you feel better—without changing what it promised?”

**06–13 |** Minimal white label cards: MEMORY and SELF-ESTEEM. They exchange positions. **Voice:** “Scientists separated the words on the label from what was actually on the tape.”

**13–21 |** Original two-column document marked MEASURED / REPORTED. **Voice:** “In three double-blind tests, the promised benefits weren't found in the measures. Yet many listeners reported what the label led them to expect.”

**21–29 |** Slow closing of the illustrated case. **Voice:** “That doesn't make their feelings dishonest. It means a feeling and a finding aren't interchangeable.”

**29–36 |** Evidence ledger on parchment. **Voice:** “The experiment wasn't a verdict on every unconscious influence. It tested particular commercial recordings.”

**36–40 |** Original cairn silhouette, simple title “The Message Beneath the Music.” **Voice:** “Follow the evidence. Final Vestige.”

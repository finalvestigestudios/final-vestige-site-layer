## Copy architecture

**Original hook:** “Before you buy a ten-minute productivity recording, consider the study where changing the label changed what people said improved.” This is a source-supported mechanism hook, not a guarantee of success or a tease that a hidden message has proven power.

**Reader desire:** More dependable planning without an exhausting system overhaul. Treat this as a plausible audience question, not a fabricated customer quote. **Seller message match:** six musical styles + a spoken variant + seven entrainment versions, ten-minute listening, stated organization affirmations. **Proof boundary:** those are listing features; none establishes efficacy.

**Proof layers:** (a) 1991 controlled tape trials N=237, (b) 1994 78-person five-week study, (c) positive but *narrow* masked cue research, (d) specific seller wording and its refund caveats, (e) free action plan. **Objection handling:** “Does the subconscious really hear reversed messages?” — merchant claims yes, FV lacks independent validation of this album and does not adopt the claim. “Why choose it?” — only a preference for listening format can be discussed, not a scientifically demonstrated productivity result.

**Conversion and CTA:** one free Focus CTA, and a second product CTA that is intentionally disabled while merchant approval/rights/efficacy review is incomplete. If verified later, attach a plainly worded **near-link** commission disclosure. No fake scarcity, fabricated testimonial, cure/wealth claim or unverified deep link. The most ethical sales outcome may be no recommendation for this product; do not force it into affiliate inventory because Inspire3 is our primary intended commercial partner.

## Decide the audience before the button

The new Thatcher illusion investigation is an evidence-first article. People arriving to understand facial perception are **not automatically shopping for meditation audio**. Its legitimate path is the sourced article, optional already-live Focus Fieldbook, and independently chosen future reading. Never insert a buy button into the scientific conclusion.

The conditional commercial candidate remains **Zen12 Premium** for a different, narrower audience: an adult already asking whether a short structured, downloadable meditation-audio course suits their schedule, preferred listening style and budget. The product's alleged mental/health benefit claims do not convert that audience into evidence of efficacy.

The exact source is the seller's primary presentation https://zen12.com/hnp. The seller describes twelve 12-minute levels, four audio modes (music, guided, nature and white noise), and 48 Premium MP3s. In its inspected November 4 presentation it showed an $87.99 promotional Premium price. Another official marketing page https://zen12.com/secret/ displayed a conditional $37 coupon offer. **These are distinct marketing routes, not universally verified final checkout prices**. Do not display price as final without verifying actual buyer-eligible link and checkout. The seller offers a one-year satisfaction/refund claim; do not call it a verified refund experience.

The public program https://affiliates.inspire3.com/ advertises signup and commission possibilities. It is not evidence of Final Vestige-specific acceptance, accepted earning link, permissible TikTok/YouTube/website/email channels or commission terms. Manufacturer claims at https://inspire3.com/ and neurological claims at /hnp are seller claims only. The 2023 binaural EEG systematic review https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0286023 found inconsistent outcomes for entrainment. That is not proof of either failure or efficacy of this product; there is no independent product-specific benefit demonstration here.

**Critical privacy gate:** https://inspire3.com/privacy/ expressly allows buyer name/email/product information to be shared with the referring affiliate. FV needs written aggregate-only reporting and safe handling terms before link activation. A prospective disclosure alone cannot fix a privacy eligibility problem.

## Original buyer-guide argument — problem → insight → useful demonstration → conditional offer

**Working headline:** Twelve minutes, twelve levels: Should you pay for Zen12 Premium?

**Hook:** “You can find twelve minutes. The harder question is whether paying for a set of recordings will make those minutes easier to use. A seller may promise an hour's meditation in twelve minutes; that promise deserves its own evidence.”

**Illustrative micro-story (not a testimonial):** You reread a paragraph three times while nearby notification sounds keep interrupting. A well-organized audio library might appeal because choosing a session feels easier than inventing one; none of that establishes neurological transformation. Mark any scene as illustrative; do not suggest we tested the recordings.

**Free practical demonstration:** For twelve minutes, try comfortable quiet or a freely available non-proprietary audio. Note which style you tolerate, whether captions or a transcript are important, and whether you would repeat the habit. This is an unvalidated preference observation, not a diagnostic, mental-health treatment or proof of benefit. Keep it valuable for readers who never purchase.

**Supported offer feature:** The seller describes a sequence of levels, four styles and downloadable Premium MP3 library. That is a format/convenience proposition, not independently established efficacy. We have not used the purchased recordings.

**Limits and objections:** Seller-specific proof insufficient for health/cognition claims; material differences among promotions; final price unknown; refund results not verified; privacy policy may share buyer data; free practice and free FV Fieldbook exist; audio preferences and accessibility matter.

**Commercial CTA, not activated:** One clear, contextual off-site purchase button should eventually appear **only** on a buyer-intent guide and **only** after named Final Vestige approval, exact authorized URL, privacy protections, final offer policy validation, founder release, independent browser/site QA and revenue reconciliation. Until then use a natively disabled button that cannot navigate.

**Adjacent FTC text (prospective):** “If you purchase through a future approved link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” Before authorized activation change the wording to an accurate present-tense disclosure next to the actual active link; never rely on a buried policy footer.

This sprint adds an independent reusable decision insert, NOT another competing canonical landing page. The pre-existing unpublished A/B experience in draft PR #970 remains the preferred test bed.

## Controlled A/B — buyer comprehension may improve, but do not assume

A = existing complete independent text-only Zen12 guide (draft PR #970). B = same substantive guide plus original optional 40-second film with poster, captions and visible transcript. **No playable video yet.** Keep offer and proof, CTA placement, disclosure, and accessibility equivalent. No autoplay or third-party video embeds. Video should load zero bytes before the visitor asks. Test no JS, keyboard, 360/390/768/1440/1920 viewports, contrast, reduced motion, transcript/caption sync with eventual real file, CSS/layout shift, and Lighthouse on an actual staging browser.

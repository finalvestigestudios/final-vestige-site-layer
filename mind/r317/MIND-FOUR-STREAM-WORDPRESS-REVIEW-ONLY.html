How to read an experiment

Ask four questions: Who participated? What was compared? What was actually measured? Which parts of the explanation were not tested? A result from highly suggestible volunteers on a color-word task does not prove a branded recording can eliminate distraction.

Source and methods claims stay with the investigation. Commercial opinions, if approved, remain in separate labeled buyer guides. Corrections stay visible.

## One precise audience → offer decision
**Buyer intent:** an adult who has watched a credible attention or suggestion clip, understands the difference between an experimental finding and a benefit promise, and wants a structured short-audio meditation routine.
**Seller candidate:** Zen12 complete audio course, not Hypnosis Live Total Focus, not Brainwave Shots Focus Shot.
**Why this format fits:** official Zen12 product and checkout describe short sessions, a 12-level progression and 48 MP3 recordings. That is a coherent *format* comparison, not independent evidence of clinical effectiveness, and it requires no assertion that audio training “rewires” anything.
**Why competing formats aren't blended:** Total Focus is a separately sold 33-minute hypnosis recording. Focus Shot describes 15- and 30-minute versions. Different products require different exact merchant pages, buyer objections, allowable claims and potentially different permission scopes.
**Purchase-readiness:** NOT AUTHORIZED. The buyer guides intentionally have disabled controls and no merchant earning destination.
**Risk:** high if seller's cognitive/brainwave/hypnosis claims are repeated as verified, if a promotional price/timer becomes false scarcity, or if a buyer thinks the independent source investigation is an endorsement.

## Controlled A/B hypothesis
**A** is the text-only evidence guide. **B** is A with an original optional 42-second **static storyboard**: descriptive poster, full text transcript and separate WebVTT cue concept. The substantive buyer guide is deliberately the same. No playable film, autoplay, remote asset, tracker, new mailing list or JavaScript is installed.

**Hypothesis, not fact:** among qualified human visitors with the same creator topic and purchase intent, optional original video may improve comprehension and *settled paid conversion* enough to outweigh extra cognitive/time/performance costs. It may also make conversion worse. Predefine random assignment and cohort windows only after a platform-permitted, consent-compatible, independently reviewed experiment exists; don't repeatedly inspect click data and cherry-pick a winner.

**Primary:** realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions = (cleared attributable receipts/commissions − refunds − applicable fees and reversals) / qualified human sessions × 1,000. If paid outcomes aren't available, label NOT COMPUTABLE, not zero.
**Secondary:** verified paid order/customer count, paid conversion rate, qualified session-to-customer CVR, refund-adjusted net revenue, confirmed lead-to-customer conversion. Label exclusions/limits on double-counting across 7-day and 30-day attribution windows.
**Diagnostic only:** mobile loaded sessions, guide reads, engaged depth, CTA clicks, outbound affiliate_click, confirmed *Jetpack* lead events. Neither clicks nor email form success UI can stand in for settlement.
**Guardrail:** equal buyer copy and disclosure, no performance regression, equivalent accessibility, unchanged price/merchant pathway, no extrapolation of tiny samples. Predefine sample threshold; report uncertainty and traffic quality.

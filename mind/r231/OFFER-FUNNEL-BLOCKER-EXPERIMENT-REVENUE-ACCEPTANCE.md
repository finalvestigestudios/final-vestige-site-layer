### Editorial origin and intent

The new standalone 2,887-word investigation *The Detour You Never Noticed* is a distinct, source-grounded mind-wandering story. It distinguishes the Killingsworth/Gilbert observational association, Seli et al.'s intentional/unintentional distinction, the Baird creativity experiment and replication concern, and Mrazek's specific two-week randomized training course. It explicitly refuses to use historical Puységur evidence or group-level training science as product efficacy proof. **No commerce appears in the investigation**; a reader can choose the free Focus path or leave.

The new A/B presell drafts are framed by the question "Is a downloadable collection of short guided sessions worth paying for *as a structure*?" The text identifies Zen12 Premium's real contents, the unsupported equivalence of twelve minutes of Zen12 to a longer session, price/refund unknowns, user objection handling, and a free twelve-minute field exercise. A does not contain a film; B contains only a 42-second storyboard/narration/poster/caption concept. No video has been rendered. Both have the exact same source/price/terms/disclosure/purchase-state and free Focus pathway.

**Commercial disclosure acceptance:** Immediately before the disabled merchant button, readable text states: "If Final Vestige later publishes an approved purchase link, we may earn a commission if you buy through that link, at no additional cost to you. No affiliate link is active here." The future activation must change conditional wording to an affirmative, conspicuous disclosure adjacent to **each** paid recommendation; proper `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` where applicable; no affiliate URL cloaking. The film does not recommend a product and contains no commission CTA; should a future video endorse the offer, it needs its own clear spoken/on-screen disclosure too. ### Privacy-safe first-party attribution contract

Standard campaign fields **only when actual platform posting and links are verified**:
`utm_source=<tiktok|youtube|instagram|...>&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=mind_evidence_2026_11&utm_content=<immutable_grok_asset_id>`.
Use platform-specific permitted bio link when direct post URL is impossible. Do not invent media IDs. Do not use fingerprinting, IP/user-agent-based identity graphs, email-address query strings, or new vendor scripts. Only pseudonymous aggregate counts using already approved analytics and consent regime; document blockers to legitimate "human-qualified" classification instead of guessing visitors are human.

Events/ledger:
1. `qualified_human_session`: only when bot/internal exclusion method has been independently reviewed and same session definition is stable; group by actual `asset_id` plus landing URL.
2. `landing_view` and `relevant_cta_click`: observed first-party route and CTA event; outbound clicks use `affiliate_click` explicitly **not** sale.
3. `confirmed_focus_lead`: authoritative provider-confirmed record with consent and proper duplicate handling, **not** a `?success` URL or front-end message. No user email stored in campaign ledger.
4. `verified_checkout_start`: only if actual merchant event is independently available; otherwise NULL/UNOBSERVABLE.
5. `merchant_confirmed_paid_order` or `partner_confirmed_commission`: settle by merchant/partner reporting, with a documented agreed attribution method and refunds, chargebacks, fees, payout reconciliation and currency. Do not reconstruct orders from outbound events.
6. `realized_net_revenue`: separately total settled direct digital receipts and independently cleared affiliate commissions, less applicable platform/processing fees, refunds and chargebacks; flag any taxes, costs or unknown settlement treatment.

**Primary KPI:** 1,000 × reconciled realized net revenue / qualified human sessions, for both 7- and 30-day cohorts. If either numerator, fees/refunds, denominators, or attribution join is absent, return **NOT COMPUTABLE**. If verified denominator is zero, return N/A, not zero or infinity. Secondary metrics: verified paid orders/customer count, buyer conversion rate, provider-confirmed leads, lead-to-customer conversion and source/offer-specific gross/net. Small samples require raw counts and uncertainty labels; do not extrapolate conversion from 1–5 outcomes.

### Controlled A/B hypothesis and preregistration (not activated)

**Hypothesis only:** optional, original 42-second proof-led video on an otherwise identical guide may increase trusted buyer comprehension and merchant-confirmed purchases per qualified human session compared with text alone. It may also add friction or hurt load performance. No prediction of uplift.

**Experimental units:** qualified consenting visitor, stable once per first-party pseudonymous session if possible without fingerprinting. Must prevent repeat switching using permissible existing experiment mechanisms; if not possible, use time-balanced matched cohorts with explicit confounding and no causal claim. Primary outcome **merchant-confirmed paid conversions**, not outbound CTR. Secondary: scroll-to-terms, relevant CTA clicks, objections read, no-JS completion, free Focus verified opt-ins.

Editorial method

Start with a record. Stay with the uncertainty.

Striking experiences make good stories. The evidence makes them worth keeping. Mind & Perception connects modern cognitive science with documented histories of suggestion, without claiming that a perception experiment proves a commercial therapy or spiritual mechanism.

Every file aims to show an original source, one useful observation or exercise, and the boundary between what a paper found and what a reader might wish it found.

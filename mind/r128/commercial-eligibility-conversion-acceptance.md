## Funnel experiment contract (not deployed)

**Primary success event: independently confirmed paid merchant transaction and realized net commission.** Sessions, leads, clicks and checkout starts are diagnostic stages.

Proposed 7/30-day attribution path:
`asset_id` + `utm_source` + `utm_medium=organic_social` + `utm_campaign=mind_zen12_buyer_pilot_2026_11` + `utm_content=<published_asset_id>` → *qualified human landing* → guide reached → relevant CTA event → optional already-existing Jetpack verified-confirmed Focus lead → authorized outbound affiliate_click → merchant independently confirmed settled paid conversion → commissions minus fees/refunds/chargebacks.

No claim of attribution beyond what is actually joined by consented, non-identifying permitted campaign IDs. Use only existing WP/Jetpack/first-party analytics facilities; no extra scripts, tracking pixels, visitor fingerprinting, session replay, raw IP, raw email, customer ID, precise location, cross-site person identifiers or automatic social-browser PII capture. If merchant will not report aggregate settled paid conversions by subid, affiliate attribution becomes **unobservable** even if clicks appear.

**Event dictionary:**
- `campaign_landing`: first-party server or existing analytics report; is neither qualified-human nor sale by itself.
- `qualified_human_session`: count only after documented exclusion criteria (known automation, owner/staff QA, suspicious bot bursts, repeat test hits); explicit confidence flag.
- `guide_view` and `focus_cta_click`: inferred only if actually observable in existing instrumentation, do not install another script.
- `confirmed_signup`: verified only in Jetpack source-of-truth; page reveal and PDF link appearance are **NOT** confirmed opt-in.
- `affiliate_click`: conditional future event, must never be named `sale`.
- `merchant_paid_conversion`: merchant-issued order/commission confirmation, aggregated and anonymized for FV reporting.
- `merchant_refund_or_reversal`: subtract paid fees and reversals from net.
- `realized_net_revenue`: reconciled cash/settled commission less attributable fees and refunds; flag unknown unsettled commissions.
- `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_sessions` = realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions * 1000; null (not 0) if qualified denominator unavailable or realized net unverified.

**Tiny sample:** Below 30 verified paid orders or 500 qualified sessions per arm, report counts, exact denominators and wide uncertainty; do not announce a winner. Refunds may arrive late: label cohort provisional until the merchant reconciliation window closes. Use 7- and 30-day windows with source dates, don't mix click-time and settlement-time without explanation.

## A/B hypothesis and guardrails

**A:** full text-first independent review; **B:** substantively identical guide, plus original optional 40-second video *concept* with poster, shot list, timed captions and transcript. In these R128 artifacts the film is **a concept, not a playable MP4**. It must not be marketed as a ready video. All links, wording, disclosure and free resources remain matched; no auto-play, JS, third-party embed, new form, or CSS motion are involved. A creator-generated professional film is a later independently rights-reviewed asset.

Pre-register a buyer-intent experiment only when an **authorized payable offer** is verified. Random assignment without personal identifiers using a preexisting CMS experiment or first-party allowed mechanism, with consistent source/medium, no dynamic user fingerprinting. Primary endpoint: net settled commission per 1,000 qualified human sessions (when verifiable). Secondary: merchant paid conversion rate, refund-adjusted orders, confirmed lead-to-customer conversion; site performance/accessibility failures are hard stop. Assess A/B on same campaign traffic and comparable days rather than misleading historical before/after spikes.

Required actual QA: mobile widths 360/390/768/1440 px, 200% zoom, focus order, keyboard-only access, contrast, no-JS content, disclosure immediately next to disabled/active control, no fake countdown, inactive seller buy link, no duplicate Jetpack form, no PII in analytics event payload, original media licensing, transcript/caption parity, caption track and poster on real video, motion-reduction behavior, and lighthouse/performance footprint before launch.

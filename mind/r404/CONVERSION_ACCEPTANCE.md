First inspect existing WP/Jetpack stack and site QA/Issue #820 contracts; avoid sending personally identifying or health data in URLs or events. Sub-ID use must comply with the **actual** partner policy. Where partner IDs cannot be joined lawfully to first-party sessions, report sales as **unattributed** rather than manufacturing ROI.

**KPI:** realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions for a 7- or 30-day acquisition cohort = `1000 × (settled directly attributable order revenue or commissions − refunds − chargebacks − platform/payment fees) / qualified-human sessions`. Exclude unpaid, canceled, duplicate or unverified orders. Distinguish gross verified order count, confirmed unique customers, paid conversion rate, leads confirmed and lead-to-paid conversion. Avoid arithmetic on an unknown denominator. With tiny sample sizes, show raw n, large uncertainty, and no “winning” creative verdict.

## 4. Controlled test: identical guide A vs guide plus optional original film B

**Independent variable:** one original optional 42-second film, after an original rights-cleared video/audio file, caption track, poster and full transcript are actually implemented. No autoplay, no forced view, no paywall, no page-blocking media, `preload=none`, native controls, no client-only CTA.

**Control:** A and B have the exact same offer description, free baseline, sources, pricing caveats, objections, CTA state and adjacent prospective affiliate notice. Keep product, source asset IDs, user intent and CTA wording stable. Only the media differs. If eligibility later confirmed, activate the same authorized SKU link in both under separate founder release approval.

**Hypothesis:** multimedia may improve informed *paid conversion* for some readers, but it could equally cause delay, distract from the evidence or slow mobile. It is not guaranteed to convert.

**Primary endpoint:** verified realized revenue per 1,000 qualified sessions, using merchant accepted purchases net of refunds/fees. **Secondary guardrails:** evidence reading completion only where privacy-compliant, confirmed existing-list leads, accessibility, performance and bounce as diagnostics, never a surrogate for purchase.

**Assignment:** only after Claude and QA approve consent and experiment design. Use first-party privacy-safe ephemeral session assignment with documented deduplication (or server-side split) rather than invasive third-party trackers. Preserve opt-out and no-JS. With zero verified purchases and disabled offer, do NOT compute a “conversion lift.”

**Performance/UX preflight:** 360, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920 CSS widths; no horizontal overflow; tab/keyboard/visible focus; screen-reader labeling; semantic disclosure before button; contrast; no rapid flashes; `prefers-reduced-motion`; no-JS source readability; actual encoded film compression and bandwidth comparison; cache/offline resilience; external source links safely marked; WordPress sanitizer test before actual WP draft.

02 / THE EVIDENCE

What science can and cannot lend it

In September 2026, an open-access systematic review of clinical hypnosis for pain found modest advantages over nonactive controls but no clear superiority over active practices such as relaxation or pain education in the relevant analyses. Clinical hypnosis for pain is not Zen12. Its finding demonstrates why comparing against a useful alternative is more informative than quoting a before-and-after improvement.

THE SOURCE TRAIL / A SMALL HISTORICAL WARNING

When the paperwork was better than the explanation

In the 1780s, Puységur catalogued extraordinary reports of magnetic somnambulism with witnesses, predictions, accounts of successes, and even failures. The presence of careful-looking records made claims easier to inspect; it did not prove the proposed magnetic force. Final Vestige's Puységur evidence archive preserves uncomfortable episodes of pressure and questionable consent, too. What makes a story feel credible is not always what establishes its cause.

The same standard belongs in a modern buyer's decision: a product page can establish a format and the seller's promise. A properly controlled study is required for a confident claim about what the specific product causes.

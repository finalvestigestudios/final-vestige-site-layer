## 3. Copywriting and conversion ethics

The problem-to-insight hook is a product claim contrasted with a concrete file list: forty-eight files can be counted, an hour of meditation “equivalence” cannot be assumed. The sourced micro-story is the experimental difference between familiarity and independent verification in Hasher 1977 and Fazio 2015. The useful demonstration is a no-cost three-session task and attention log, not a pseudo-clinical test. The **value proposition** is the *seller-described convenience* of a structured audio library for someone already disposed to using such recordings, NOT guaranteed performance. The objections are evidence quality, price volatility, return/fulfillment uncertainty and no-cost alternatives. The commercial decision is conditional, never false urgency.

Near the actual future earning link, always include clear, visible text: **“If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.”** The R188 prototype includes a *prospective* disclosure immediately beside the **native disabled button**; no active affiliate link exists. The general affiliate disclosure page alone does not meet near-link visibility.

No invented first-person experience, seller reviews masquerading as independent testimony, clinical, cognitive or wealth outcome guarantees, fabricated scarcity or invented commission/payout. Advertising guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking and https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance .

How a claim enters this archive

Every investigation begins with a source—not a catchy result. We distinguish primary experiment from popular retelling, source repetition from independent corroboration, observation from inference, and commercial product description from independently validated benefit. When the evidence is ambiguous, the ambiguity stays visible.

Three questions to bring to any claim: What exactly was measured? What does the source not establish? What information could change the conclusion?

Start with curiosity. Finish with a better decision.

The intended creator pathway, subject to independently verified published media IDs, is: a truthful question in a Grok-owned short video → a matching published investigation → an optional free Focus Fieldbook → a clearly labeled independent tool review → an earning link only if authorized and disclosed.

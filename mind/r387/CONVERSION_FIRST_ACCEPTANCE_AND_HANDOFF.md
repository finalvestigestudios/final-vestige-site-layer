**Candidate high-intent audience:** readers who encountered a short, demonstrable change in perception or attention and are now evaluating a voluntary 12-minute structured meditation audio routine. **Fallback:** explore an actual FV-owned paid Mind SKU only if Commerce independently establishes its identity, IP/reuse rights, active payable checkout, price, delivery, payment settlement and refunds. The free Focus CTA is a legitimate reader-benefit step, **not** a purchase.

**Commercial outcome:** Confirmed paid purchase or independently reported paid affiliate commission **net of refunds/fees**, not raw outbound click or subscriber count. If no payable product is authorized, avoid producing new traffic requests that imply a purchasable outcome.

## 4. Acceptance test for A/B: what the founder's question actually requires

**Hypothesis, not a claim:** The short, self-selected film may help some high-intent readers understand why the evidence review is independent and voluntarily proceed to purchase more often than readers given the same guide as text alone. It could equally distract, slow loading or depress purchase rate.

**Design:** A uses only the long-form source-led text; B inserts a conceptually original optional 42-second film in the same position while preserving the **exact same substantive buyer-body HTML, same free Focus path, same prospective commerce control, same disclosure, same offer, same price caveats and same source citations**. B presently offers poster and transcript/caption plan only; it is **not** a runnable video-treatment experiment. When encoded, no autoplay, third-party iframe or forced playback. Defer any heavy video media until an explicit user action. Respect reduced motion and captions, use width/height to reserve layout. No-JS readers retain every claim and the same links.

**Denominator:** qualified human first-party landing sessions, deduplicated within consent-aware 7- and 30-day cohorts, excluding known bots, internal QA and invalid traffic. Preserve true Grok `asset_id` where available. Unknown attribution stays unknown; do not silently allocate it.

**Primary outcome:** independently reconciled, refund-and-fee-adjusted **realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions** in A versus B. Secondary: independently confirmed paid order rate, legitimate purchased/customer count, gross vs net receipts, opt-in-to-paid conversion where linkable with consent. Diagnostic only: landing views, source quality, disclosure visibility, CTA clicks, media play, abandonment. A/B power analysis before release; report confidence intervals and a conspicuous tiny-sample warning. Do not declare a winner on a handful of purchases or any outbound-click proxy.

**Safety stop criteria:** link goes to an unapproved merchant, display price differs from authorized checkout in a misleading way, seller terms drift, disclosures disappear on mobile, cognitive/health outcome claim is promoted without proof, opt-in duplicates Jetpack, unsolicited tracking captures personal data, or B media causes meaningful performance/a11y regression.

## 5. Privacy-safe minimal event contract (NO instrumentation deployed here)

Suggested data contract for Claude's **existing** first-party analytics stack, not permission to install a second SDK or duplicate GA4 tag:

| Event | Minimum consented fields | What it does **not** prove |
|---|---|---|
| `qualified_landing` | day cohort, anonymized session token with expiration, `asset_id` if verified, `utm_campaign` if verified, content page ID and A/B variant | Not a human purchase or subscriber |
| `focus_cta_click` | page ID, variant, user consent status, coarse campaign | Not a confirmed email lead |
| `confirmed_focus_lead` | provider-confirmed subscription ID held only in Jetpack, anonymous aggregate verification, consent record; no raw email in event payload | Not a purchase |
| `affiliate_click` | approved merchant ID/offer ID and originating page, only after partner authorization, no email or fingerprint | Not a sale or commission |
| `merchant_confirmed_paid` | separately reconciled partner order summary or first-party checkout status, period, offer ID, aggregate amount | Not settled net revenue if refunds pending |
| `refunded_or_reversed` / `settled_net` | payout reconciliation IDs held securely by Commerce, net amounts, cohort when consented and matchable | No invented attribution |

**Formula after verification:** `net_per_1000 = 1000 × (confirmed eligible settled receipts + independently posted affiliate commissions − refunds − platform/merchant fees − attributable costs) / qualified_human_sessions` for a fixed cohort/window/currency. Do **not** combine gross own-product sale proceeds with the affiliate commission on the same order. Keep unattributable revenue separate. Distinguish recognized versus settled revenue, never include simulated events. No universal cookie tracking across publisher and merchant without consent and legal review; zero personal or health data sent in UTM parameters.

**Debug fixtures / rejected examples:** missing asset_id→UNATTRIBUTED; multiple CTA clicks same session→not multiple purchases; consent withdrawn→no cross-session personal matching; chargeback→net adjustment; external partner does not share transaction-level IDs→aggregate partner report only; free Focus subscription→lead event, no sale; sampled cohort too small→no statistical winner. Maintain strict partner contract compliance, non-cloaked destination, FTC disclosure attached to link itself.

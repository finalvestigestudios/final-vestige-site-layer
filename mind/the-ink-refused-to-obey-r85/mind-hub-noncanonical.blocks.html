Editor's method / Source before certainty

The experiment is not the advertisement.

In Stroop's 1935 experiment, familiar word-reading responses interfered with naming the colors in which those words were printed. This historic effect cannot, on its own, verify a meditation recording. A complete archive preserves the original study, separates each claim from its evidence and offers a clear path to freely verify what matters.

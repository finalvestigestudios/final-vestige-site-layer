Featured investigation / Source-controlled draft only

The Syllable the Recording Never Played

Richard Warren removed speech from a recording, inserted a cough, and discovered that listeners could experience a word as intact. Later researchers showed why acoustic detail and linguistic expectation both matter. This new draft does not assert that perceived continuity proves brainwave audio works.

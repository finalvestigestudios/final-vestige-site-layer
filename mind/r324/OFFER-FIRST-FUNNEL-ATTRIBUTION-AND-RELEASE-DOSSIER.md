## 2. Why this investigation belongs in the archive

Unpublished original: **The Syllable the Recording Never Played**. The question is whether an audible replacement noise can lead listeners to experience an actually absent phoneme; the response is bounded by controlled original papers. An interruption masked by a cough and an interruption marked by silence are not equivalent, and perception is not a verbatim tape.

Original article links to the published [Puységur files](https://finalvestige.com/free-resources/puysegurs-animal-magnetism-files/), but as a method of separating source, testimony and causal interpretation. It does not diagnose eighteenth-century witnesses or call animal magnetism proven, and the study is not a bridge to endorsing a meditation MP3.

The independent Mind hub has four streams: Perception & Attention; History of Suggestion; Practice & Reflection; Tools Under Review. It links verified public [Change Blindness](https://finalvestige.com/2026/11/02/change-blindness/) and [Mesmerism](https://finalvestige.com/2026/11/02/mesmerism/), the existing Puységur Library, archival investigations and the single Focus route. Its featured phonemic article is explicitly **unpublished**; it does not invent a live permalink.

The current public [archive](https://finalvestige.com/archive/) has six historical category facets and overlapping counts. The October 30 Issue #171 61-post snapshot is historical inventory; do not add category totals together to infer a current post count. No extra category or navigation item is introduced automatically.

## 3. Original presell structure and buyer decision

Variant A is a complete, independently useful text-only guide; Variant B has **identical core copy** plus an optional original 42-second film **storyboard**, poster, timed transcript and WebVTT concept. There is no encoded video, no player, no autoplay, no network-loaded media and no fabricated results. Independent publishing requires filmed media and a tested accessible player; the storyboard is an editorial/production artifact only.

**Buyer path:** a curious person arrives with an intent-matched creator topic. The hero says a recorded routine can be convenient without guaranteeing outcomes. First, they may try an original twelve-minute exercise at no cost: choose an undisturbed time, record one modest intention, try silence or benign sound, then decide whether listening format fits. Next comes specific seller package detail, its limits and objections. A prospective disclosed Zen12 decision remains **disabled**. A fully functional existing free Fieldbook is optional and is not a required step to reach commercial information.

**Adjacent prospective disclosure used in both variants:** “Affiliate disclosure: If a future authorized purchasing link is provided and you buy through it, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” FTC's [endorsement guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) requires disclosing material connections clearly, not hiding behind the global footer. [Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) requires reliable substantiation for health-product claims; a disclaimer cannot rescue an unsupported affirmative promise. Copy is educational, non-diagnostic and avoids fake firsthand testing.

**Buyer objections answered:** “Does it guarantee focus?” no evidence here; “Do I need headphones?” merchant says not required, confirm instructions; “Is the price fixed?” no, dated checkout only; “Can I return it?” seller advertises a guarantee, verify contractual conditions; “Is free better?” may be, if audio structure adds little personal value.

## 5. First-party measurement specification — use the existing stack

Where existing site analytics and consent policies allow, define a privacy-minimized series of **distinct** aggregate events:

1. `qualified_landing_session` — uniquely counted eligible human-like session on the intended landing (bot screening, duplicate logic defined by Claude/site QA).
2. `article_to_fieldbook_click` — relevant first-party navigation, not confirmed subscriber.
3. `provider_confirmed_lead` — consent/confirmation backed by Jetpack provider status, not a query string or unlocked PDF display.
4. `buyer_guide_view` and `relevant_cta_click` — navigation/intent signal, not paid conversion.
5. `affiliate_outbound_click` — only for an authenticated allowed earning link after release. Not checkout, lead or sale.
6. `merchant_confirmed_paid_order` — independently documented purchase from merchant dashboard/Payhip payment record, with allowable attribution.
7. `cleared_commission` / `owned_net_receipt` — after refund, reversal, platform/processing fees and settlement reconciliation.

Attach `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, authorized `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` as actual asset ID **only after verified matching**, and content-version/offer IDs. No email address, phone, name, personal URL token, fingerprint or cross-domain tracking identity in public event payloads. No new JS tracker or paid SaaS. Existing analytics implementation is Claude's production engineering responsibility; Mind provides requirements and QA fixtures only.

Critical known bug class: the Focus page may reveal a free PDF from a query value without an authenticated provider opt-in. **Do not count a display as a subscriber**, and do not change the established welcome message or existing Jetpack list.

For 7- and 30-day cohorts:

`realized_net_revenue_per_1000 = 1000 × (settled affiliate commissions + verified owned-product receipts − refunds − processing/fulfillment fees − reversals) / qualified human sessions`.

If qualified sessions or settled paid outcome is missing, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero. Report confirmed orders, buyer count if legitimately available in aggregate, gross and net receipts, paid conversion rate and lead-to-paid conversion separately, with small-sample warnings, attribution uncertainty and refund maturation windows. Do not call unverified Payhip gross prices collected revenue.

## 6. Controlled text-versus-video test

**Hypothesis, not promise:** B's 42-second optional original film may improve high-intent reader understanding, which *could* change confirmed paid purchases. It could just as easily hurt comprehension or page speed. Avoid labeling it a winning variant before real transactions.

**Control A:** source-first text; **Treatment B:** same copy + original film, captions, visible transcript, muted or sound-opt-in, no autoplay, default `preload=none` once encoded. Media should be optimized to avoid degrading mobile LCP and keep poster lightweight. All meaningful offer information and CTAs must appear without JS and without watching. Reduced-motion/keyboard/touch and screen reader use require real-browser testing at 360, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920 px. Independent QA must check HTML sanitization in the intended WordPress environment, focus order, disclosure proximity, invalid markup, contrast and exact CTA href before any final approval.

The only test outcome worth declaring a commercial win is a **merchant-confirmed, refund-adjusted paid conversion**, with a properly measured human denominator and material sample. Secondary events, clicks, average time, page views and email signups help diagnose but cannot substitute.

No randomizer, tracking code or film has been deployed. Avoid A/B contamination caused by platform source differences or time-based cohorts; site engineering must review any future allocation and consent requirements. No synthetic orders may ever appear in financial reports.

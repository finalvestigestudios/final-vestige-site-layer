## Attribution measurement contract (specification only; no analytics write)

Use existing approved analytics and privacy/consent only, no duplicate pixels or paid SaaS. Canonical first-party campaign fields: `utm_source` (platform), `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_<topic>_2026_11`, `utm_content=<ACTUAL_GROK_ASSET_ID>`. Only if the asset ID and route exist and Grok validates them. Collect aggregate campaign counts and consent-safe signals; **no email addresses, precise IPs, session replay, new cross-site tracking IDs, fingerprinting, individual health information, or pass-through personal data**.

Event ordering and classification:
1. `qualified_landing`: independently qualified human session, with confidence/robot filtering documented by Claude and Site QA. Counting page views alone fails.
2. `article_to_focus_click`: existing first-party CTA interaction; **not** email signup.
3. `confirmed_lead`: only if Jetpack/provider reports opt-in at relevant cohort, and consent basis verified; **not** form impressions.
4. `affiliate_click`: optional *future* approved disclosed outbound merchant link interaction; **not** merchant checkout or sale.
5. `merchant_confirmed_paid_conversion`: independently reported completed paid order/affiliate conversion, matched to allowed cohort without PII. For own products capture order receipt and actual settlement; for affiliates commission must be accepted/cleared before realizing revenue.
6. `refund`, `chargeback`, `transaction_fee`, `cleared_commission`: from merchant/payments books. Never assume 0 where data are missing.

**Primary KPI:** `[(settled_owned_receipts + cleared_affiliate_commissions - refunded_amounts - transaction_fees - chargebacks - attributable_variable_costs) / qualified_human_sessions] × 1000` for separately identified seven-day and thirty-day cohorts. Show denominator, eligible sessions, exact source of authoritative settlement, refunded volume and match confidence. Secondary: paid purchase rate, true order count, gross/net, confirmed lead-to-customer conversion and attributable cost. Show small-N confidence: under 30 verified orders, avoid winner claims even if click rate differs. With present unverified merchant and human-session data, the KPI is **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero.

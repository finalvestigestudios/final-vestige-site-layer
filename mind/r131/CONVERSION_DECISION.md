## Controlled offer-guide test: A versus B

**High-intent audience:** a visitor already comparing paid 12-minute meditation recordings, not an arbitrary viral illusion viewer.

**Pilot A:** complete original evidence-first text buyer guide in pilot-A-text.html.
**Pilot B:** same complete buyer guidance plus optional original 40-second educational film *storyboard* and CSS poster, six caption time segments and transcript in pilot-B-optional-film.html. This is NOT playable video and no external player/JS/autoplay was introduced.

Both variants include the same free listening-preference demonstration, seller product description, scientific counterweight, alternative routine, merchant price/refund/privacy objections, original buyer copy and nearby prospective commission disclosure. Both have natively disabled merchant controls. They do not need JavaScript and contain no forms, scripts or tracked outbound earning URLs.

**Original funnel arc:** truthful problem -> evidence-linked distinction between perceiving a beat and validated product benefit -> useful 7-day preference exercise -> seller-described 48-MP3 library and progression -> explicit limits/objections -> independent resource -> disabled merchant purchase control. No fabricated first-person experience, clinical outcomes, wealth claims or urgency.

**A/B hypothesis:** An optional original short educational film could aid understanding or qualified buyer intent, but it could also distract or impair performance. No result is known. An A/B winner cannot be declared using only scroll depth, link clicks or pageviews. Before any experiment: approve merchant, prove paid product, choose allowed noninvasive randomization and consent, run user testing and define 7/30-day settlement reconciliation. Until then label only BUILT / structural tested.

**Primary outcome after release approval:** net realized revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1000 × (settled first-party gross receipts + verified affiliate commissions − applicable refunds − fees) / validated qualified-human sessions. Count affiliate commission, NOT the affiliate merchant's gross order value. Keep merchant settlement evidence and first-party product ledger distinct. If sessions or source-attributable transaction data are missing, report NOT_COMPUTABLE, not zero.

**Secondary outcomes:** confirmed paid orders/customers, paid conversion rate, verified gross and net, refunded orders, objectively confirmed Jetpack signups and lead-to-purchase where lawful correlation exists. An affiliate_click, checkout start, Focus page unlock, visitor count or promotional order-looking email is not proof of a paid conversion.

## The most actionable new finding

Zen12's exact official consumer page, inspected today, advertises Premium's 12 levels and 48 MP3s. The retrieved public HTML displays **$87**, whereas prior draft editorial research recorded **$87.99**. The public copy is not a verified final checkout total or proof of refund processing. These are copy-risk findings, not an allegation about merchant billing.

## One meaningful creative experiment

**A (text-only control):** `zen12-premium-A-text-UNPUBLISHED.html`. **B (hybrid hypothesis):** identical HTML plus one optional original static poster, full 42-second storyboard and readable transcript; no video player has been produced. Absolutely no autoplay, autoplay audio, JS dependency, external fonts, tracking pixels or additional subscription forms.

**One primary hypothesis** (when eligibility is verified and release allowed): among *qualified* visitors already seeking meditation audio, an optional sub-45-second source story might improve **verified net paid conversions per 1,000 qualified humans**, relative to a source-complete text guide. It could also perform worse by distracting or delaying buyers. We will not designate a “winner” from button clicks, scroll depth or sample noise.

**Fairness controls:** same verified offer, disclosure, CTA position and destination; same copy and copy length except the optional video block; same campaign landing promise, browser performance, device segments and 7/30-day cohorts. Assign variants with a privacy-safe consent-compatible first-party mechanism already approved under #820, not a new cookie or script. To avoid confounding creative and audience, do not alternate Grok assets as if they were randomized A/B visitor assignments. No separate email/Jetpack lists.

**Release safeguards for a future playable B:** actual original 42-sec MP4/poster, optional user-initiated playback, WebVTT captions, complete transcript, WCAG keyboard operation and focus, suitable contrast, reduced-motion check, page LCP/CLS on mobile, no autoplay, no excessive transfer; no-JS page remains readable and fully navigable. Founder approval and independent Site QA required.

## Measurement contract: events, confidence and conversion economics

**Before release:** record offer_id exact Premium, page_version, partner_approval_source, authorized_campaign_channels, approved_merchant_destination, permitted link, disclosed relationship, checkout proof, returns and support, responsible approver and QA screenshot evidence. All must be in non-secret controlled documents; private tokens/passwords never enter GitHub or Drive.

**Funnel when authorized:** Grok immutable public asset_id + platform URL + standard utm_source/platform, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=mind_premium_audio_pilot_2026_11, utm_content=<published_asset_id> → *qualified-human* landing session (bot/internal filtering specified by Claude under #820) → relevant guide impression → CTA click / `affiliate_outbound` only on a truly approved active link → independently reconciled partner-reported **paid sale** → commissions actually settled and received → refunds, chargebacks, fees and withheld adjustments → realized **net FV revenue**. The original visit and paid conversion may not be joinable without permitted merchant IDs; label unmatched conversions *unattributed* rather than guessing source.

**Direct audience pathway:** `focus_cta_click` → existing Jetpack double/verified-confirmed subscription only if observable with appropriate consent → welcome email actually delivered if observable → later independently confirmed order when merchant attribution permits. A form view or email enter action is not a confirmed subscriber. Don't insert hidden email pixels or pass visitor PII to a merchant.

**Calculation:** `net_FV_revenue = FV_owned_customer_funds_cleared + affiliate_commissions_received - refunds/chargebacks/fees/other adjustments not already netted`. Split by business line and account for discounts only if not already reflected in cleared receipts; never double subtract. For affiliate sales, *merchant gross retail basket is not FV revenue*. `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = net_FV_revenue / eligible_qualified_human_sessions * 1000` when denominator and settled revenue are trustworthy. Preserve negative results, unknown attribution, zero-denominator as **NOT COMPUTABLE**. Report 7/30-day cohorts with totals, refunds, variance and tiny-sample warning. Commission announced, affiliate click and checkout initiated are NOT paid orders.

**A/B analysis:** Primary outcome net FV revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions, secondary confirmed paid conversion rate and lead-to-paid conversion for opted-in users where independently joinable. Compare with randomization integrity, referrer and mobile mix, session quality, confidence intervals and enough independent buyer events. Do not manufacture statistical significance from tiny counts. If eligibility remains closed, the A/B hypothesis is NOT RUN, not a failed conversion test.

A story about explaining what you didn’t choose

In published experiments, researchers secretly replaced participants’ selections. Some people did not detect the mismatch and explained the substituted outcome. It is called choice blindness; it does not prove audio recordings change preferences. It does suggest an everyday precaution: record your requirements before reading a persuasive offer. Source: Johansson et al., 2005, DOI 10.1126/science.1111709.

What the actual Premium program describes

The official Zen12 Premium page describes twelve levels, 48 MP3 recordings, four listening choices and twelve-minute sessions. For someone who wants an organized sequence, that is a concrete format to assess. The seller says twelve minutes can deliver the benefits of an hour of advanced meditation. That equivalence is not independently established for this exact product here. A general meditation study, EEG review, or a testimonial would not by itself prove that particular equivalence.

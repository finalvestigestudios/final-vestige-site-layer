## Controlled A/B research hypothesis (NOT a live experiment)
**One audience:** a qualified reader explicitly asking "is a structured twelve-minute meditation-audio library worth paying for compared with a free timer?" No deceptive urgency or forced funnel. Variant A: complete static evidence guide. Variant B: exact same decision content plus an original 42-second poster and timed caption/transcript concept, optional and no-JS. Both routes lead to /focus/ as the existing optional FV list and display identical disabled Zen12 CTA, nearby prospective FTC disclosure and accurate seller-specific feature statements. The film concept is **not yet a hosted playable video** and the B variant is **not yet a valid test of actual watching**; before any authorized experiment, add rights-cleared, captioned, non-autoplay media with a stable poster without large LCP costs, and confirm it works without JavaScript.

**Primary success criterion (future, only if authorized):** *realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions* = 1000 × [confirmed paid affiliate commission or FV-owned paid-order net after refunds and directly attributable fees] / qualified human sessions. Never calculate when numerator or denominator is missing; label **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0. Compare randomized 7-day and 30-day cohorts only after merchant and platform acceptance, consent/privacy decisions and independent QA. Sample-size warning: tens of sessions and zero observed orders cannot establish an uplift. Do not infer causal impact if Grok audiences, landing copy, offer or checkout differ across variants.

**Secondary diagnostic stages:** `campaign_landing`, `relevant_cta_click`, `confirmed_lead_provider`, `affiliate_outbound_click`, `verified_checkout_start` (if merchant confirms), `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`, `cleared_commission`, `refund`, and `fee`. Outbound never means sale; clickthrough into external merchant is not observed checkout. `confirmed_lead` requires actual provider confirmation, not a click on /focus/. A purchase requires independently reported merchant paid transaction and settlement status, and refunds/fees must be subtracted.

**Privacy and attribution:** If Claude confirms native first-party logs can do this without adding scripts, use `utm_source=platform`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_audio_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=verified_asset_id`.

Start with the evidence ↓

Evidence before conclusion

A question can become part of the record.

When investigators changed a word in a question about a filmed crash, later reports changed too.

Loftus and Palmer's 1974 experiment shows a danger to the interviewer and the witness: information introduced after an event can influence subsequent accounts. It does not mean everyone's memories are false.

Examine the original experiment · An independent unpublished deep dive on memory and suggestion is in editorial review, not linked as a live article.

## 2. Exact audience, exact job to be done, exact honest promise

**Audience:** viewers who have watched a perception video, tried a few minutes of quiet or guided practice, and are evaluating the commitment to regular meditation audio. They want to understand the format, how sessions fit into a day, whether the claimed outcome has evidence, and what happens if it does not meet expectations.

**Reader job:** decide whether a collection of guided/music/nature/white-noise recordings would help them keep a personally preferred twelve-minute routine, rather than seeking a guaranteed clinical, cognitive or financial outcome.

**Ethical promise:** Final Vestige will tell you what the exact product page says is included, identify what research actually measured, name useful free options, and show the limitations before you choose. We do **not** promise that audio makes twelve minutes equal one hour of practice, automatically changes brain waves, cures anxiety, retrieves forgotten experiences, improves IQ, generates wealth or guarantees productivity.

**Distinctive micro-story:** in Loftus and Palmer's 1974 automobile-film experiment, changing a verb in a question influenced later eyewitness responses. The hook is not "all your memories are fake." It is "a story sounds stronger after someone gives it a detail." From this research, guide readers to examine sellers' words as evidence, too. The story establishes epistemic caution; it does not establish benefits or hazards for Zen12.

**Free demonstration:** write two columns: EXACT SELLER WORDS and WHAT THE CITED EXPERIMENT TESTED. For example, "twelve minutes equals an hour" needs a defined outcome and direct product comparison. A historical/hypnosis memory study cannot supply that demonstration. Free readers can then complete a twelve-minute timer exercise or use the 52-page Focus Fieldbook with its existing opt-in, without adding another list or being forced into a purchase.

Use privacy-safe first-party session bins and consent-aware campaign data where lawful; do not add trackers if Jetpack or existing instrumentation can supply reliable aggregated data. Do not run with two subscribers as if results will be statistically persuasive.

If outcomes are too sparse, report descriptive counts and uncertainties without significance claims.

**Performance budget:** B should lazy-load a compressed file only after user intent; a static SVG/CSS poster and transcript are sufficient without video. No new SaaS/fonts/scripts.

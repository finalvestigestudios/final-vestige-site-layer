The unreliable witness is your mind.

What you hear can depend on what you see. What you notice can differ from what you remember. What you expect can change what you report. Every claim deserves a source—and every source has a limit.

This archive places real experiments beside original historical records, free practical reflection and independent guides to commercial claims. Investigations remain available without subscribing or purchasing anything.

## Decision before design

**Pilot offer:** Zen12 Premium, **conditional only**. High-intent visitor: someone evaluating a paid, twelve-minute, structured meditation-audio library and searching “does Zen12 work / what does it include / what is the refund policy / is twelve minutes equivalent to an hour?” The visitor has concrete buying questions, not merely fascination with an optical illusion.

**Source-of-truth merchant offer:** https://zen12.com/hnp . On 7 Nov 2026 the public page displayed 12 levels, four styles at each level, 48 MP3s, quick start/manual, support and an advertised one-year guarantee. It displayed prices, but checkout total, taxes, currency, fulfillment, refund acceptance, upsell path and payment settlement were **NOT tested**. The claim that twelve minutes reproduces an hour of advanced meditation and the broader mental/medical benefits are vendor claims: no independently substantiated SKU-specific intervention finding. Official parent https://inspire3.com/ supports product existence; public https://affiliates.inspire3.com/ offers enrollment, **not** Final Vestige-specific acceptance, channels or links. Seller affiliate-advertised commissions are **not** contracted FV economics and must not appear as our expected earnings.

**Owned alternative decision:** The live 52-page Seven Day Focus Fieldbook is a **free** resource in the established Jetpack/Dispatch pathway. Public discovery did not authenticate a priced, rights-cleared, presently fulfillable FV-owned *Mind* Payhip SKU; therefore a paid-owned-product pilot cannot be declared saleable. Commerce owner must inspect the authenticated Payhip dashboard, actual SKU permissions, price/tax presentation, checkout, fulfillment and refund terms; a public search yielding no result is not an inventory audit. Do not manufacture a new paid Mind product or pretend the free Fieldbook generates direct orders.

**Run disposition:** Meaningful research and conversion engineering can proceed, but purchasing activation and A/B human testing are **HARD HOLD** until an independently verified paid destination exists. The useful output of this draft is the acceptance test and buyer-oriented evidence, not “conversion achieved.”

## Copy position: the useful pitch without the miracle

The strongest truthful offer framing is **structure rather than transformation**.

Proposed independently written introduction for the existing Zen12 guide: “Twelve minutes is a manageable routine. But a purchase should buy what the listing can actually deliver—a specified set of recordings, instructions and support—not an untested guarantee about your brain. Compare Zen12's twelve levels and four listening formats with the easiest free twelve-minute practice you can maintain. Decide whether the organization and convenience are worth the current *verified checkout* total. The research does not allow us to call the audio equivalent to an hour of meditation.”

Original research micro-story: In 1976 McGurk and MacDonald dubbed one spoken syllable onto a mismatched moving mouth; some participants heard a different syllable. The distinction between a **real experimental effect** and a **commercial outcome claim** is the central editorial bridge. That discovery does not imply meditation audio works. Do not sell a product on a false implication that being fooled by perception proves audio brainwashing.

Useful free demonstration: The new R197 investigation contains a three-column “audio source / lip source / report” paper exercise with explicit warning that it is **not** a working audiovisual test. The purchasing guide retains the independent checklist and a free-timer baseline. There is no mandatory video, subscription gate, personal data collection or claim to have listened to Zen12.

**Intended reader CTA, inactive in source-controlled draft:** “See the verified Zen12 Premium offer and decide for yourself.” It cannot become a clickable earning CTA until account-issued URL, allowed landing pages and marketing permissions are documented. Text-only variants can mention the official public information page labelled **non-affiliate seller source** without construing it as an affiliate conversion.

**Near-CTA disclosure when and only when approved:** “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” It belongs immediately adjacent to the actual earning anchor, in the same visual module, above the fold on common mobile screen sizes if the primary CTA is above the fold. In the current draft the message is **prospective** and the purchase button carries its real native disabled attribute. The FTC says “affiliate link” alone is not adequate for many consumers; disclosure on a standalone policy page is insufficient if the user does not notice it when deciding. Never disclose only at footer.

**Buyer objections answered without evasions:** What do I receive? Seller lists 48 MP3s. Do I need a full twelve-level course? Check which levels and formats are actually included in current checkout. Is meditation equivalence independently proven? Not for this exact product. What if I only need a timer? Try the free baseline first. Is the guarantee dependable? Merchant advertises a year, but refund performance and exact terms have not been verified by Final Vestige. Is the product suitable for treating insomnia, anxiety, ADHD or depression? No such branded therapeutic claim will appear absent adequate direct evidence. Does Final Vestige earn? Only after a properly disclosed authorized link, and an outbound click is not payment.

## Native attribution contract: qualified humans first

Where first-party capability already exists, the campaign link may use explicit parameters such as:
`?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=mind_perception_pilot_2026_11&utm_content=ASSET_ID_FROM_GROK`.

**Source-of-truth event boundaries** (no additional tracking script or third-party SDK approved):
1. **qualified_human_session:** deduplicated, bot/QA-excluded session with documented source, consent and time window; raw IP or personally identifying information must not be exported to the editorial ledger. Default “unattributed” when referrer or tagging is absent.
2. **landing_view:** first-party observed page arrival, campaign metadata only when legitimately present.
3. **relevant_cta_click:** distinguish article→Focus, article→independent guide, seller informational outbound and account-authorized `affiliate_click` if ever activated. Do not conflates them.
4. **confirmed_lead:** only when Jetpack/Dispatch authoritative double-opt-in/subscriber status confirms it and consent matches; an exposed PDF, arbitrary `?subscribe=` or `is-subscriber` CSS state is **NOT** evidence.
5. **verified_checkout_start:** only when merchant or authorized checkout integration reliably reports such a state, not from clicking a Payhip link.
6. **merchant_confirmed_paid_order:** authenticated, unique paid transaction, deduped by internal one-way key or merchant-side ID under appropriate access; only eligible orders, not coupon redemption or add-to-cart.
7. **received_affiliate_commission:** attributable paid commission from partner reporting, with pending vs approved vs paid distinguished. No self-estimated 60%-plus payout from public ad copy.
8. **refunds, chargebacks, VAT/taxes if relevant, processor fees, reversals and creator/paid costs:** reconcile from actual records before computing realized net revenue. Costs need consistent accounting scope; gross merchandise value is not business net revenue.

**Decision metric:** `1000 × realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`, reported separately at 7 and 30 days and only when both numerator and denominator are valid and appropriately attributable. For zero verified orders with a known complete denominator and reconciliation, report observed zero net revenue; when either is missing, show **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero. Add explicit small-sample warnings, uncertainty range where possible, and contribution of confirmed leads and paid conversions. Publishing a higher CTR without paid conversion is not commercial success.

**Privacy:** Avoid IP, email address, full query strings, session reidentification, fingerprinting and dark-pattern consent. Prefer aggregate site-owned counts and existing analytics/Jetpack, with explicit owner decision on whether consent is required for any extra instrumentation. Do not drop affiliate identifiers into a query until partner tracking terms are approved. Keep site QA/server access decisions with Claude and privacy owner.

## A/B hypothesis — experimental rather than aesthetic

**A:** Original text-only independent Zen12 offer guide. **B:** The exact same guide plus original optional 42-second film module, with poster, explicit play on click, captions, synchronized transcript and no auto-play; never embed remote media on initial load. Both existing drafts are in PR #1384 and source-controlled; this sprint does **not** create another speculative purchase page.

**H0:** Among comparable eligible, consented qualified visitors, optional film does not raise merchant-confirmed purchases per qualified visitor versus text-only. **H1:** Film raises merchant-confirmed, net-revenue-positive purchasing outcomes without worsening clarity, refund rate or performance.

**Experimental unit:** one qualified, consented visitor/session allocated once to A or B by approved first-party mechanism; predeclare allocation and reduce repeat-treatment crossover. Avoid algorithmically mixing Grok video audiences and plain search into opposite variants without stratification. Clicks to seller count only as intermediate diagnostics, not purchase conversions. A film-driven increase in curiosity clicks with no authenticated paid orders fails the revenue objective.

**Guardrails:** one and only one existing Focus/Jetpack form path; reader can navigate to independent research without email. No JS still displays all textual buyer guidance. Keyboard/focus order, 200% zoom, contrast, reduced-motion preference, poster alternative, transcript, captions, muted controls, lazy load and 360/390/768/1440 review. Measure p75 LCP/CLS and total bytes in real browsers; no third-party media iframe until activated by user. No video has been rendered, and **no genuine A/B traffic** can begin before publisher/checkout/analytics/founder authorization.

**Stop/review conditions:** unresolved buyer confusion, missing or obscured disclosure, remote media loading without consent, broken mobile purchase route, unverified session assignment, inaccurate medical claims, adverse performance trend, settlement/return rate deterioration, or insufficient sample for defensible conclusion. Prefer a pilot of stable exposure and wait for a real 30-day revenue cohort over declaring victory from one-day clicks.

A historical hinge / no sales inference

Between the word and the effect.

In early twentieth-century Nancy, Coué prescribed a simple morning-and-evening formula, counted across a string of knots. A 2006 laboratory study would later demonstrate a narrow effect of suggestion on Stroop performance in highly suggestible individuals. Neither fact proves the benefits of a commercial meditation MP3.

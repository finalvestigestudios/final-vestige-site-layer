AN ORIGINAL QUESTION

The face has not changed. Why has your judgment?

When a face is inverted, turning its eyes and mouth inside it can appear less strange than the same edit upright. That is the scientific problem behind Peter Thompson's 1980 Thatcher illusion. Later experiments found that orientation effects can arise even with an eye or mouth region shown alone.

The complete sourced Thatcher investigation is being reviewed as a draft; we do not present its unpublished destination as live.

Primary sources: Thompson (1980); Psalta et al. (2014). Do not claim the experiment tests meditation or hypnosis.

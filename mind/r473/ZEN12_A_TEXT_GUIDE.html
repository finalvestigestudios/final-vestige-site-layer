Try a free baseline before comparing products

For twelve minutes, sit comfortably and notice ordinary sounds. When your mind wanders, note it without judgment and return attention to a chosen sound. Write down whether the exercise seemed useful or uncomfortable, without interpreting any result as a diagnosis or a product-equivalence experiment. You do not need headphones, special audio, or any subscription for this exercise.

For a longer source-led practice, the existing Seven Day Focus Fieldbook remains free through the single existing Jetpack pathway; this guide has no new signup form.

Six buyer questions worth asking

  1. Which exact package is actually offered at checkout: six levels, twelve levels, or another bundle?
  2. Are the MP3s downloadable, and what usage/retention rights are provided?
  3. Do the advertised twelve minutes fit your schedule, and do you prefer music, guided voices, nature, or noise?
  4. What are the current verified checkout price, taxes, seller identity and payment terms, not an expired coupon or on-page countdown?
  5. What is the actual refund policy on the selected purchase, and are there exceptions or procedural requirements?
  6. Would ordinary free meditation/audio suffice? If seeking help for a health condition, which qualified clinician's advice is appropriate instead of an unproven product claim?

## A/B controlled hypothesis (designed; experiment NOT RUN)

- **A** = existing R210 Zen12 source-led text decision page, identical verified offer terms, disclosure and CTA.
- **B** = same core copy, source trail, disclosure and CTA plus **optional original 42-second film** with a lightweight poster, meaningful alt/description, human-reviewed synchronized captions, complete text transcript and **no autoplay**. Film is currently *concept only*; never describe it as built playable video.
- **Hypothesis** B might improve verified *paid* conversions and refund-adjusted realized net revenue, or might hurt both through distraction, processing load and accessibility/performance costs. Do not pick a winner by click-through rate.
- **Assignment** after authorization and release QA only: single stable first-party randomization using an already approved implementation; no fingerprinting, cookies without consent, third-party A/B vendor, new analytics script or high-cardinality identity store. Exclude obvious bots/automation, mark unknown traffic confidence, record opt-in/consent effects. No invented 50/50 allocation or observed sessions.
- **Accessibility** WCAG 2.2 AA review 360/390/768/1440/1920px, keyboard focus/tab order, touch target and contrast, no-JS navigation, caption/audio correspondence, poster descriptions, reduced motion and consent; static source checks aren't browser certification. **Performance** lazy-load film only after opt-in, poster local/static and compressed, measure LCP/CLS/INP in actual staging, never place remote-player on first paint. **Security** avoid unsanitized WP HTML/script, third-party tracking, raw UTM injection and new redirects. Founder and Site QA must explicitly accept before release.
- **Primary endpoint** cleared net dollars per 1k qualified-human sessions in 7- and 30-day attributed cohorts. **Secondary** authenticated paid orders per qualified-human session, confirmed customers, lead-to-customer rate when lead and attribution match, refund fraction, merchant completion friction, then relevant CTA clicks. **Never** use a click, form action, lead, checkout visit or unverified merchant sale as proof of paid conversion.
- **Small-sample rule:** fewer than ~30 confirmed paid orders in either arm or materially missing attribution → directional only / inconclusive. Any uncertainty intervals must be based on real merchant events; stop if adverse accessibility, disclosure or checkout impacts. Prespecify a minimum test duration and decision before starting; no fabricated significance.

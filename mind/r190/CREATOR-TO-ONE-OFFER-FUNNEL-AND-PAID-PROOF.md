## One high-intent audience before any funnel variants

Target: a reader who is **already considering a convenient short audio practice**, asks whether paying for pre-recorded meditation/hypnosis is worth it, and wants neutral evidence, clear features, alternatives and checkout terms. This is higher purchase intent than someone entertained by an optical illusion. The reader's real job is not “be persuaded to buy”; it is “decide whether this format is useful enough to pay for without confusing a wellness claim with proof.”

The pre-sell currently exists in R188, the inherited controlled text-only guide **A** and guide **B** with the same claims and an optional 42-second static poster/transcript concept. Keep both *unpublished and natively disabled* until the partner acceptance, product link, real QA and release gates pass. A future authentic MP4 would need its own original production and captions; the poster is not a playable film. No forced playback, remote iframe, extra form or JS are necessary. Independent topic investigations never insert a product endorsement into their conclusion.

**Copy spine for the decision page:** “A shorter session might be easier to keep. Does that make its advertised result true?” Lead with a sourced, sober micro-story: the 2025 published systematic review found only four of 168 included consumer hypnosis apps reporting a clinical efficacy trial. Explain explicitly that this is *not* a trial of Zen12. Let a visitor try a free twelve-minute timer. Clearly state what Zen12's seller says comes in Premium. Then state what independent evidence does and does not demonstrate. Give accessible free alternatives and transparent purchase questions. The single pending commercial CTA remains **Purchase option unavailable** with a native disabled attribute. If the agreement is eventually verified, an adjacent disclosure must read: “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” Never add unsupported urgency, testimonial, clinical, financial or promotional equivalence claims.

## Measurement contract: proven money, not click theater

Claude/Analytics owns implementation of any existing first-party analytics, not the Mind content lane. There will be no paid vendor, duplicate pixel or new tracking script from this PR. A campaign may carry non-PII `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` or `asset_id` when actual published creator IDs and platform placement are verified. Do not include email addresses, personal names, IPs, illness indicators, mental-health interests, precise geolocation or other viewer identifiers in UTMs. Sanitize unknown inbound parameters.

Required stages:
1. **Qualified human landing**: unique first-party session/visit attributed to one authentic Grok asset_id and approved UTM campaign, subject to site privacy policy/consent and bot filtering. Bot-heavy pageviews do not become qualified humans.
2. **Relevant CTA event**: source-to-Fieldbook navigation, independent buyer-guide navigation, or (once approved) `affiliate_outbound`; zero JS is a permitted functional route with server-side aggregate measurement only if existing infrastructure supports it.
3. **Confirmed lead**: *actual* verified signup/delivery from the existing Jetpack process. A button press, form impression or unconfirmed address is not a confirmed lead. Do not duplicate subscriptions or modify the welcome message.
4. **Confirmed paid order**: independent merchant record of completed Payhip sale or approved affiliate network sale, with permitted attribution and a non-PII verification reference. Checkout started, merchant click, product interest and coupon view are **not** sales.
5. **Realized net revenue**: recognized gross paid revenue or earned/received affiliate commission as supported by accounting, less refunds, chargebacks, transaction fees and other attributable costs; avoid mixing gross merchant sales with an affiliate publisher's actual commission.
6. **Revenue / 1,000 qualified humans**: 1,000 × realized net publisher revenue / qualified human sessions. Report 7-day and 30-day cohorts only when both numerator and denominator are reconciled and attribution is defensible. Otherwise label **NOT COMPUTABLE**. Include exact verified-order counts and small-sample caution. Do not substitute zero for unknown.

**Evidence store design (specification only):** creator asset IDs, privacy-safe campaign slug, approved final landing URL, count of qualified sessions, consent mode in aggregate, CTA event count, confirmed lead count, merchant order reference digest (not full PII), network conversion ID if policy permits, posting timestamp, settlement date, net amount, refund/fee component, provenance owner and reconciliation state. Never place private customer information into GitHub or shared creative documents. Respect merchant reporting limits and avoid persistence of customer-level raw click trails.

## A/B pilot: a narrow causal question with a real stopping rule

**Hypothesis:** adding a *truly optional, 30–45 second, original, captioned micro-explanation* to an otherwise identical evidence-based text buyer guide may improve **net publisher revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions**, not merely play clicks or scroll depth. This remains untested. Run A/B only after rights/partner approval, actual functioning commerce, a baseline verified event path, and founder release approval.

A = the established R188 text-only guide. B = the same guide plus video with poster, transcript, closed captions and no autoplay. Keep headline, merchant offer, disclosure, recommendation, page order, checkout link, language, UTM tagging and layout otherwise equivalent. Prefer session-stable assignment at the first approved visit, prevent variant crossover where feasible without invasive identifiers, and document bot filtering. No third-party A/B SaaS, fingerprinting, new mailing list or unconsented profiling.

**Primary acceptance:** paid purchase or independently reported paid affiliate conversion with realized net publisher earnings, attributed within a documented window. Track signed-out mobile and keyboard usability, successful CTA routing and mobile performance as hard quality gates. Secondary diagnostics: qualified-human visit-to-guide, guide-to-resource, voluntary video engagement, affiliate outbound, confirmed opt-in, and checkout-abandonment rates *where authoritative merchant visibility exists*. These are not paid conversion.

**Stopping/release rule:** no launch until merchant, owner and QA gates pass. If N is tiny or there are no verifiable orders, treat apparent lift as anecdote, not a winner. Monitor refund/complaint burden and accessibility; halt a failing variant before revenue optimization. In a genuinely powered experiment, predeclare exposure window, minimum meaningful net revenue improvement and error-control approach; do not search every hour for a winner and do not change copy between arms. If settled net revenue is too sparse to estimate, report **INSUFFICIENT DATA** rather than declaring a converter.

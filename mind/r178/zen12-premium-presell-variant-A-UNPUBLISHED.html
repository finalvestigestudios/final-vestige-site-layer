A strange, useful distinction

A sound you didn't hear on the track

In 1976, psychologists presented speech with mismatched mouth movements and sound. Some listeners reported a syllable that was never on the soundtrack. The experiment shows how seeing and hearing work together. It says nothing about the efficacy of a meditation MP3. Read the original study.

That's our standard: a fascinating scientific result is not permission to make untested product promises.

Useful before you decide

The free three-day routine-fit check

Try twelve minutes of quiet on one day, twelve minutes with an openly licensed guided session on another, and your preferred format on a third. Note whether you finished, what interrupted you, and which format was most comfortable. This is a personal preference exercise—not a treatment, scientific trial or measure of neuroplasticity.

Before any purchase

Does the full library fit your device? Have you heard an authorized sample? Do you prefer spoken guidance or simple ambient sound? Have you reviewed the actual price, refund conditions, accessibility features and any upsells? If a free timer already works, no purchase is necessary.

## Exact A/B implementation protocol — not activated

**Variant A:** `mind/r178/zen12-premium-presell-variant-A-UNPUBLISHED.html` contains the research-led guide, free exercise, one existing Focus link and a *disabled* prospective commerce control. **Variant B:** same copy plus an original poster and a 42-second caption/transcript *concept*; no playable file currently exists. A valid launch experiment would require a newly rendered, rights-cleared, lightweight, opt-in playable MP4 with matching captions/transcript, a still poster, user-initiated playback (no autoplay) and measured media size, LCP and mobile usability. Until then, **neither variant is a tested video treatment**.

Do not randomize live visitors, deploy an assignment cookie, add analytics JavaScript or establish vendor tools in this sprint. Claude may propose a first-party, server-side consistent assignment method only after founder and privacy review. A/B cohorts must compare equivalent eligible users and traffic sources; avoid comparing different weeks or campaigns and calling differences causal.

**Primary endpoint:** verified paid net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions per assigned variant over predeclared 7-day and 30-day cohorts. **Secondary:** merchant-confirmed paid conversion rate; source-confirmed orders/customers; verified lead-to-paid conversion where consent/attribution allows; net FV revenue; free resource engagements for diagnosis. **Guardrails:** unsubscribes (if applicable), disclosure visibility, page speed, keyboard experience, misleading claims, refunds/chargebacks, duplicate scripts. Tiny denominators require wide uncertainty statements, not statistical-significance theatrics.

**No-JS contract:** both HTML variants render all copy/links/buttons with JavaScript unavailable. Product controls stay native disabled; every disclosure is readable in source order. Video B currently contains a poster with text transcript; no video tag because no video exists. An actual B video must offer controls, captions (WebVTT), descriptive transcript, no remote autoplay and a responsive fallback. A and B must have equally visible free and commercial choices if activation becomes authorized.

## Privacy-first measurement and revenue accounting

**Data minimization:** request only `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` (real Grok asset_id) in existing approved first-party infrastructure. Avoid full IP, user-agent fingerprinting, email hashes, health information, cookie stitching or combining newsletter lists. Don't introduce a second Ahrefs/Jetpack counter or duplicate tracking snippet. Human qualification is subject to documented bot/owner/test-session exclusions and is NOT derivable from pageviews.

**Event contract:** `qualified_campaign_landing` (confirmed human session and single campaign), `article_to_focus_click`, `confirmed_signup` (only from authoritative opt-in source), `product_guide_view`, `affiliate_click` (observed offsite click, never purchase), `merchant_confirmed_purchase` (owned product exact order/status), `partner_reported_paid_conversion`, `affiliate_commission_paid`, `refund_or_chargeback` and `fees`. Assign event timestamps and source labels. Unsupported events = UNOBSERVABLE, not 0.

**Revenue:** owned product net = cleared FV customer receipts - unreflected refunds/chargebacks - fees/expenses not already deducted. Affiliate net = commissions actually received by FV - adjustments, reversals and costs **not already withheld**. Merchant basket total is **not** FV affiliate revenue. Never double-subtract discounts or refunds already reflected in receipts; do not clamp a negative value to zero. Keep 7/30-day realized figures distinct from forecast/approved but not paid commissions. If qualified sessions or settled revenue unknown, **net revenue per 1,000 qualified sessions = NOT COMPUTABLE**, not “$0.”

**Production gates:** (a) named acceptance; (b) exact terms and tracking rights; (c) validated exact offer and pricing/returns representation; (d) Site QA mobile 360/390/768/1440/1920 keyboard/no-JS/reduced-motion and proper disclosures; (e) no duplicated signup/analytics; (f) independent scientific review; (g) founder publish/activate approval. All commercial gates CLOSED until evidence.

The Gorilla the Experts Missed

Researchers inserted an unexpected figure into a medical-imaging search. Twenty of twenty-four participating radiologists did not report it. That is a remarkable result, but not a medical-error rate—and not evidence that a meditation recording prevents perceptual misses.

Start with the record, then examine the claim.

There is a striking difference between an observation and its explanation. An eighteenth-century testimony can document what a witness said without validating animal magnetism; a modern experiment can demonstrate a perceptual limit without validating a marketed mental-training product.

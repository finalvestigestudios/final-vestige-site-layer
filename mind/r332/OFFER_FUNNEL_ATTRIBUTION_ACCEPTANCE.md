## A/B comparison — measurable, not aesthetic
Control A is an original, full text-only independent Zen12 guide. Variant B contains **the same buyer copy byte-for-byte** and adds an original, static illustrated poster plus optional **42-second storyboard**, complete transcript and six-cue WebVTT file. No autoplay, no playable media or media preload, no JavaScript, no external player, no trackers. No-JS accessibility is inherent for both HTML review prototypes; real browser QA is still required. The film is not a valid randomized experimental treatment until an original rights-cleared playable encode, captions, reduced-motion review, transcript, mobile performance and founder approval exist.

**Hypothesis:** Optional film may help visitors understand the differentiation between product format and scientific proof without harming the final rate of real purchases. It is not guaranteed to improve conversions. Randomize or assign consent-respecting cohorts only after merchant approval and independent staged QA. Predeclare 7- and 30-day windows and stop if disclosure, user experience or consent is degraded. Interpret inconclusive small samples as inconclusive.

## Measurement contract and privacy boundary
Use **existing approved first-party instrumentation only**, not a new GA4/Ahrefs/Jetpack duplication. A genuine platform asset supplies **actual** utm_source, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11, utm_content=<authentic asset_id>. Capture campaign landing, qualified-human session (defined and excluded for bots/internal/staging), article or offer view, relevant CTA click, confirmed opt-in lead **only with provider-backed Jetpack status**, and an affiliate_click **only once an approved outbound earning URL exists**. The independently reported financial layer is *verified confirmed paid order or partner-confirmed paid affiliate commission*, then refunds, fees and realized net receipts. Do not join people across sites by personal email or device fingerprint. Respect privacy settings and platform link-placement restrictions.

Primary KPI: **net realized revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1,000 × (cleared gross receipts minus refunds, fees and costs included in the agreed net definition) / qualified-human sessions**. Zero verified eligible sessions or no credible paid settlement means **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero and not success. Auxiliary metrics: confirmed orders and customers, paid conversion rate, 7/30-day lead-to-customer rate, unknown attribution share, sample sizes and refund rate. Even large pageview totals cannot be used as qualified-human denominators without fraud filtering.

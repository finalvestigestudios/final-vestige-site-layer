## Experiment protocol, without a false success narrative

**Primary outcome:** realized net commercial revenue per 1,000 *qualified human sessions* by source asset and 7/30-day post-click/eligibility cohort. Define realized net as cleared paid orders and cleared affiliate commissions **received** minus transaction/platform costs, refunds, chargebacks and attributable variable operating costs; avoid subtracting already-net commission a second time. Do not count estimated earnings, pending sales, clicks or subscribers as net. If the paid settlement file and session denominator are absent, KPI is `NOT_COMPUTABLE`; do not report "$0" or "0%."

**Supporting funnel:** verified Grok platform `asset_id` (or clearly tagged channel-level link) → canonical UTM `utm_source=youtube|tiktok`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_zen12_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=actual_verified_asset_id` where link mechanics allow → qualified-human first-party landing → in-article CTA → optional Focus resource click → provider-confirmed subscriber (not form impression) → correctly disclosed affiliate outbound link click (`affiliate_click`) → partner-confirmed paid conversion/order → cleared commission → refunds/fees → realized net.

**Consent and privacy:** Reuse existing approved analytics; implement no new pixel, identifier, fingerprinting, cookies or paid SaaS. Aggregate per verified campaign/source and landing destination. Do not transmit email addresses, names or order-identifying data to analytics. Retain attribution labels with reasonable retention and consent controls. Ask Claude to determine whether native reports already provide needed metrics before proposing any additional instrumentation.

**A/B hypothesis:** Readers choosing a short structured audio experience *may* make better-informed decisions when offered an optional evidence video after the same source facts. The 42-second film is not guaranteed to increase purchase; it could distract, degrade performance or reduce trust. A and B must have same exact offer, evidence, disclosure, checkout, navigation and free alternative. Only B adds optional media with poster, captions, transcript and native user control. Prefer a deterministic, privacy-safe assignment mechanism if available without additional tracking; record version/exposure where approved. Compare independently settled orders and realized net per qualified human cohort, not mere CTA clicks. Report tiny denominators and uncertainty intervals; no statistical significance claims without a suitable sample and analysis plan. A test with a static poster is **not a video effect** test.

**Acceptance / rollback:** Gate rollout on authenticated merchant agreement, exact URL, no unsubstantiated benefits, near-CTA FTC disclosure, independent a11y/mobile/consent checks, integrity of no-JS experience, coherent original film captions, verified payment/refund reporting, and explicit founder production approval. Rollback proposal must keep core published investigations and Focus funnel functioning even if the optional commercial experiment is disabled.

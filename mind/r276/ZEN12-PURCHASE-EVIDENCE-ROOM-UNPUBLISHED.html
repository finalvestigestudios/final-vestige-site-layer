History meets evidence

A compelling story is not a controlled test.

Puységur's records of animal magnetism preserve reports of startling experiences and attempted cures. Their historical value lies in what people observed, wrote down and contested, not in a modern verdict that an invisible magnetic fluid had been proved.

Modern brainwave-audio marketing creates a similar evidence problem: a real auditory sensation, a claimed EEG response and a commercial promise are not equivalent findings. Read the original FV Puységur source archive before accepting an impressive mechanism as settled.

02 / Claim versus proof

What happens when we test the word entrainment?

A 2023 systematic review of 14 EEG experiments on binaural-beat stimulation found five broadly consistent with entrainment, eight contradictory, and one mixed. Study designs and measurement methods varied widely. A separate 2024 review of 31 studies of auditory stimulation and cognition found mixed or insufficient results.

Neither is a randomized product trial of Zen12 Premium. Therefore neither establishes that a specific twelve-minute session equals one hour of advanced meditation or produces any guaranteed treatment, concentration or creative benefit.

2023 review · 2024 review

Evidence hierarchy: Hearing an auditory beat ≠ measured brain entrainment ≠ improved behavior ≠ demonstrated effectiveness of a particular retail recording.

04 / Try before spending

A no-cost twelve-minute test of what you value.

Set a twelve-minute timer. Attend to a neutral point for ten minutes, noticing each time your attention returns. Use the last minute to note what was missing: structure, directions, pacing, the company of a voice, or simply a consistent time to practice. You can stop at any point. This is a preference exercise, not a clinical experiment, medical advice or measure of brainwaves.

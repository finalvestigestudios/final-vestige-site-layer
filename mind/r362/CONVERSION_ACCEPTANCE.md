## Conversion experiment: controlled A/B hypothesis, not a result

**A:** Independent Zen12 evidence-first buyer guide, including precise seller-described contents, critical claim boundaries, seller policy caveats, no-cost baseline and disabled commercial control.

**B:** Identical core buyer copy plus an **original optional 42-second** six-scene animated explainer *concept*: inline static SVG poster, no-JS readable transcript, complete six-cue WebVTT file. Film rendering and hosting NOT completed in this sprint, so B is **not a playable/video treatment** and cannot yet be exposed as a behavioral video A/B test. No autoplay; no third-party video player or script.

**Hypothesis:** On eligible authorized traffic, contextual 42-second original media may improve verified paid purchase rate or qualified lead-to-paid rate by clarifying the evidence promise. It may also distract visitors or reduce mobile usability. No direction or size of effect is assumed.

**Pre-registration for a future legal test:** single landing destination, stable allocation per anonymous eligible session via existing approved first-party infrastructure only; same copy, offer, CTA location, display price, disclosures, targeting and dates; media block is the only variant. Require comparable measured campaign source/asset IDs; count unique qualified-human sessions and report 7- and 30-day cohorts with very-small-N warnings. Primary outcome independently reported **settled paid orders and realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified sessions**; secondary signals confirmed leads, eligible CTA click and qualified lead-to-paid ratio. Randomization method must be approved by Claude and privacy QA without adding a second script or tracking vendors.

**Abort gates:** no authenticated affiliate permission/link; missing verified paid SKU; missing reliable merchant conversion export; mobile film > approved performance budget; absent accessible captions/transcript; disclosure separated from CTA; browser no-JS failure; new PII storage or cross-site identifier collection; misleading health or wealth implications. Do not ship A or B while any gate holds.

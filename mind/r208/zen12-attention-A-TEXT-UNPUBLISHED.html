The evidence

The second letter isn't your shopping recommendation

In an attentional-blink task, people can report the first target correctly yet have more difficulty reporting a second target presented a few hundred milliseconds later. Classic experiments study rapid visual targets, not any Inspire3 audio product. A 2007 study of three months of intensive Vipassana retreat training reported changes on this task and in an event-related brain signal. A retreat is not twelve minutes of prerecorded sound; the study cannot substantiate branded-product results.

Primary sources: Raymond et al., 1992; Chun & Potter, 1995; Slagter et al., 2007 full text. An investigation is in editorial review and is NOT publicly published.

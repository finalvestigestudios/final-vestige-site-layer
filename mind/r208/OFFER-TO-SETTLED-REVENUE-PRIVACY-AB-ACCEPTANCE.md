## A versus B: question and experiment

**Hypothesis, not a prediction:** An optional **original 42-second** film may help a motivated buyer distinguish visual experimental evidence from merchant claims and make an informed purchase decision. It may also waste attention or increase LCP, so text-only A is the baseline. Both A and B have an identical information core, objections, free option, price/returns uncertainty, near-link *prospective* affiliate disclosure and **native disabled** merchant purchase button. B adds only an original SVG poster, captioned transcript and a WebVTT draft. No rendered video, autoplay, remote embeds, forced playback or experiment allocation exists.

**Controlled variables:** Same headline, search intent, CTA position, disclosure, offer facts, page title except variant descriptor, forms (none), copy, and audience split rules. Do not experiment on a live audience until merchant permission, release approval, and accessibility/performance tests. Founder can choose randomized first-party campaign assignment (stable, no PII) or a sequential predeclared experiment with equivalent traffic, understanding selection bias. Every no-JS visitor receives full guide and a functional free Focus link. B's optional video must not block reading and should lazy-load local poster/player only on intentional action after approved render.

**Primary metric:** `1000 × (settled_owned_product_receipts + cleared_affiliate_commissions − refunds − chargebacks − payment_fees − attributed_variable_costs) / qualified_human_sessions` in a declared 7-day and 30-day cohort. This is a money-per-session outcome, not an order count divided into currency. **NOT COMPUTABLE** without authoritative verified money and a defensible human denominator. Secondary: merchant-confirmed paid orders/sessions, approved affiliate paid conversion, provider-confirmed lead-to-customer conversion, pages-to-CTA click, return-to-content, completion of optional video (only consented and privacy-safe). Diagnostic only: follower counts, pageviews and watch time.

**Small samples:** Publish counts with confidence and uncertainty; never announce an A/B winner from sparse traffic or zero merchant-linked receipts. 7/30-day cohort comparisons require the same allocation rule and refund maturity window; an immature settlement should be marked provisional, not final. Where users decline analytics consent, do not create an invasive cross-site identity graph. State unknown qualified-human denominator.

## Minimal privacy-preserving measurement contract (PROPOSED, NOT INSTALLED)

Existing Jetpack/Ahrefs should be inventoried **before** new code. No added analytics SaaS, duplicate measurement pixel, third-party redirect, querystring PII, fingerprinting, persistent unique user identifiers, nor hidden affiliate parameter insertion.

Allowed campaign tags after creator confirms the real asset ID: `utm_source=tiktok|youtube|instagram`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_perception_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<immutable_grok_asset_id>` (safely encoded, allowlisted, never invented). For a platform where clickable caption links are disallowed, Grok documents bio-link routing; no covert link cloaking. A qualified-human session is an **estimated, audited, de-duplicated, consent-respecting first-party aggregate**, not every raw request. Filter obvious bots/internal testing with documented methodology; do not pretend to know which anonymous visitor became a paying person unless merchant reconciliation legitimately permits it.

**Proposed events and allowed evidence:** `campaign_landing` (server or existing native analytics aggregate), `article_to_focus_click` (aggregate event only if existing tools permit), `confirmed_focus_signup` (only authoritative Jetpack confirmed state), `offer_guide_view` (aggregate), `affiliate_outbound_click` (only on actual authorized near-disclosed earning link), `merchant_confirmed_paid_conversion` (authoritative merchant order or affiliate report, without customer PII in GitHub), `settlement_adjustment` (fees/refunds). Distinguish states **DESIGNED**, **BUILT**, **SOURCE-TESTED**, **BROWSER-TESTED**, **LIVE**, **MERCHANT-VERIFIED**. Do not claim a paid conversion until the last two.

**Event field allowlist:** date_bucket_utc (daily), campaign_id, platform, real asset_id, route, event_name, variant and aggregated_count. Exclude IP, full URLs/query strings, email, customer names, cross-site tracking identifiers and free-form referrer strings that may contain PII. Merchant order reconciliation should stay in secured commerce reporting, not public GitHub issues or Docs.

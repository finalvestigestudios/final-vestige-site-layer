Editorial charter

Our method, plainly stated.

We distinguish primary record from later retelling; reported perception from verified mechanism; general meditation or hypnosis research from product-specific clinical evidence; seller-described contents from independently tested contents; and an outbound click from a confirmed paid order. Disagreements and failed cases are archived instead of removed. Commercial materials are always clearly separated from the conclusions of an investigation.

Potential purchases require verified partner approval, current checkout and refund terms, an adjacent FTC disclosure and independent source review. We do not claim to have personally used products we have not tested.

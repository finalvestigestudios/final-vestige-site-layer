## Privacy-preserving measurement contract (proposal, not deployment)

- For a **genuinely published** Grok asset only: `utm_source=(actual platform)`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=(real immutable asset_id)`. Platform caption/bio limitations must be represented as attribution loss, not made-up direct clicks.
- `qualified_human_session_v1`: only if Claude verifies consent/lawful basis, bot filtering, first-party page events and deduplication. Do not store raw IP, identity, email, fingerprint, or attach them to the event. Native site metrics only where sufficient.
- `article_to_focus_click_v1`: approved same-domain link click (diagnostic). `confirmed_signup_v1`: uniquely provider-verified consenting subscription. A `?subscribe=` flag **cannot** supply confirmation.
- `affiliate_click_v1`: approved attributed outbound link click only, only after legal partner approval and explicit required disclosures. `merchant_paid_conversion_v1`: merchant independently confirmed order or qualified affiliate sale; `settled_net_revenue_v1`: paid amounts less legitimate fees/refunds/reversals. Never map `affiliate_click` to paid.
- Seven- and thirty-day **qualified-human** acquisition cohorts: net revenue per 1,000 = `1000 × (verified settled gross receipts − fees − refunds − reversals)/verified qualified human sessions`. If numerator or denominator missing, **NOT COMPUTABLE**; do not substitute Jetpack views or followers. Any revenue per session, A/B result or retention assertion needs source-led transaction proof and tiny-sample warning.

## Controlled text vs film conversion hypothesis

**Variant A** contains substantive full buyer guide only. **Variant B** contains identical full guide with an optional 42-second original visual-film concept, poster, six WebVTT cues and full transcript. Currently B has **no encoded video or playable control**. It is a storyboard/accessible content specification, not a runnable treatment. No A/B experiment or sales inference can be claimed.

**Hypothesis:** for appropriately qualified users, optional visual explanation may improve paid purchase conversion *relative to an identical text-only guide*; it may equally reduce conversion because of distraction and page cost. The primary endpoint is verified, refund-adjusted paid revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions; do not optimize for superficial CTA clicks. Test only after authenticated approved product, rights-cleared encoded local film, owner-approved experiment, independent live browser WCAG/mobile performance and authorized checkout. Keep visible on-page claim/disclosure/CTA copy identical. No autoplay, third-party video embed or tracking pixel; use `preload="none"` or metadata with a local poster if an actual film is later created. No-JS reading must stay complete, poster under 100 KB and film under 1.5 MB are *budgets not measured assets*.

Assign variants using approved first-party approach without fingerprinting; predeclare 7- and 30-day cohorts, necessary consent, bot exclusion, uncertainty intervals and sample size limitation. Fail closed if B hurts accessibility, load performance or trust. No imaginary price, checkout event or revenue.

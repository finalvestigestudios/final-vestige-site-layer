H1: optional short film increases **realized net revenue per 1,000 independently qualified human landing sessions** compared with text-only, with no decline in accessibility, trust or page performance. H0: no meaningful improvement or lower realized net. This is a hypothesis, NOT a guaranteed converter. Assign 50/50 only after eligibility, founder approval, real encoded rights-cleared video, actual captions and browser QA. Keep copy, layout below experimental component, claims, disclosure, merchant checkout and price identical; differ only in optional film. Film must have static poster, transcript, accurate WebVTT, keyboard controls, preload none and no autoplay. No-JS users read the full guide. No new tracking scripts; Claude must integrate with approved existing first-party instrumentation.

Gate actual tests at 360, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920 CSS pixels, zoom 200%, keyboard tab sequence, focus/contrast, reduced motion, screen-reader announcements, light media weight, CSP, WordPress sanitizer and broken/unauthorized merchant link checks. Add an explicitly consent-compliant server-side allocation only if approved. Stop at evidence of misleading claims, broken mobile checkout, accessibility regression, unsuitable media/privacy handling or unexpected fee/refund restrictions. Review paid outcomes in seven- and thirty-day cohorts, note merchant attribution lag, compute uncertainty and refuse significance assertions on tiny samples.

### Privacy and revenue contract

- qualified_landing: human-filtered eligible landing session with genuine creator asset ID, campaign, source, page and assigned variant. A view is not automatically a unique human.
- guide_cta_click: genuinely enabled, authorized CTA after approval, never a sale.
- affiliate_click: approved merchant earning URL actually clicked, not a paid conversion.
- confirmed_lead: Jetpack/provider-confirmed consent-based signup, not an unverified submitted form; use existing list only.
- merchant_paid_order: merchant-reported completed paid transaction, not cart/checkout-start.
- merchant_settled_commission: authenticated partner-reported, eligible realized payout, not an estimate.
- refund_or_fee: merchant-reported adjustment; reconcile gross paid orders versus settled payouts and refunds.

No personal names, email addresses, raw IPs, user agents, cross-site fingerprints, attention scores or medical traits in the experimental schema. Do not implement new scripts, cookies or duplicate measurement providers. Preserve separately attributable and unattributable paid orders.

**KPI:** 1,000 × independently verified realized net revenue / independently qualified human sessions. If denominator, confirmed settlement, refunds or fees are unavailable, display **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero. Record source/asset, eligible sessions, visits, CTA, verified lead, verified paid order, refunds/fees and net separately with seven-/thirty-day cohorts. A few clicks or signup events are not revenue.

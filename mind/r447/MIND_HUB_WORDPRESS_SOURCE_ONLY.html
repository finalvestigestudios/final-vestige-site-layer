The method behind this archive

Observation is not a mechanism. A source can faithfully preserve what someone said or experienced while leaving its causal interpretation unresolved. Where science disagrees, we document the disagreement instead of selling certainty.

  1. Go to the original. Follow a primary experiment, archival scan or explicitly labeled abstract-only source.
  2. Ask what was measured. Report outcomes, controls and limitations. A laboratory attention result is not a treatment claim.
  3. Keep failure in the record. Negative results, small groups, disputed methods and consent concerns belong beside famous examples.
  4. Separate commerce. Product descriptions come from sellers; independent evidence must support independent claims. Disclose a material commission connection next to an approved recommendation.

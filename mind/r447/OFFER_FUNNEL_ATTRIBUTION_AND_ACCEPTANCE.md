**Event taxonomy, non-conflation:**

| Event | What it means | Not a substitute for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `campaign_landing` | Verified site visit on a specific destination, with consent/qualifying rules | Qualified unique human by itself |

| `settled_net_revenue` | Paid receipts attributable and settled less applicable refunds/fees | Estimated EPC based on clicks |

Declare the currency and payout/fee source with each future result; do not mix gross order value with earned commission or owner-book revenue. Report `paid orders / qualified-human sessions`, `confirmed leads / qualified-human sessions`, `paid orders / confirmed leads` only when each denominator is attributable and credible.

**Seven-day and thirty-day cohorts:** cohort by verified acquisition date, maintain separate source/asset and A/B assignment, exclude test/referrer spam/internal traffic with published eligibility logic, flag samples too small for a stable commercial inference, freeze no winner from an anecdote.

## High-intent fit: one principal audience and offer

**Principal buyer intent:** *Zen12 Premium worth it? What do I actually receive, and does the audio evidence justify the claim?* After a creator Short about how sight alters what we hear, a prospective buyer wants an independently sourced comparison of downloadable contents, limits, refund uncertainty and a free baseline. A visitor need not subscribe to read the buyer guide. Zen12 Premium is the conditional principal product because its seller describes a structured **twelve-level, 48-MP3** program; this is a package description rather than a verified psychological result.

**Presell angle:** "The mind can supply a sound that was not on the recording. That does not prove what any other recording can do for you." This is a literal and sourced problem-to-insight hook, not a manipulation claim. The article clarifies the original [1976 McGurk paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/264746a0), [2018 individual differences](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0207160) and [2023 heterogeneous binaural-beat review](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0286023).

**Offer relevance:** A buyer who values a short prearranged guided-audio library may consider its contents without assuming medical efficacy. A buyer needing mental-health treatment must not be directed to it as treatment. The merchant's public page lists six-level Beginner/Advanced and full twelve-level Premium tiers, guides and an advertised one-year guarantee. It does not prove checkout total, delivery, performance or refund handling.

**Conditional alternative:** Hypnosis Live Total Focus is a separate seller-described 33-minute/47 MB session, not a Zen12 tier. Public price observations conflict, so do not quote a live checkout price. Brainwave Shots Focus Shot is another distinct merchant catalog item; its exact final checkout and publisher rights must be reverified before any recommendation. ## A/B test — independent text vs optional original 42-second concept

**A =** full text evidence-first Zen12 Premium buyer guide, independent sources, free baseline, objections and a native disabled commerce control.

**B =** identical substantive copy plus original static SVG storyboard and six timed WebVTT caption cues with an accessible expandable transcript. **There is currently no playable encoded or hosted movie**. It is false to claim film completion or benchmark media weight based on a poster.

**Causal hypothesis, unproven:** for qualified humans arriving from a verified creator asset with clear buying intent, optional visual B might lead to higher **settled net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** than A by conveying the scientific boundary quickly. It might lower revenue by honestly persuading a visitor the offer is not suited to their goal. Either is an informative outcome. Do not declare victory based on video views or CTA clicks.

**Control:** isolate only the presence of the optional film. The offer, text, price caveats, disclosure, the free alternative, page hierarchy, CTA and destination must be identical. Native no-JS support, keyboard access, transcript and reduced-motion behavior in both. No treatment allocation or live tracking now. If authorized later, use permitted first-party assignment, session stability subject to consent policy, no fingerprinting or third-party data brokers, and avoid overlapping campaigns with changed prices or inconsistent checkout flows.

**Video release dependencies:** original recorded narration, rights record, captions verified against real spoken timing, human-reviewed transcript, poster optimization, optional controls, no autoplay, bandwidth cap, dimensions/CLS, mobile performance, stop/reduced-motion and no-JS fallback. Until then B is a non-playable design hypothesis.

**Success metric:** qualified-human sessions in each variant with `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign` and actual published `utm_content=asset_id`; separately count article clicks, Fieldbook CTA, provider-backed **confirmed** leads, merchant-verified paid orders/commissions, refunds, fees and legitimate direct costs. For settlement-mature cohorts:

`realized net revenue per 1000 eligible human sessions = (independently confirmed collected revenue or recognized affiliate commissions - refunds - chargebacks - processing/affiliate fees - direct allocated costs) / qualified-human landing sessions * 1000`

Do not double-count the same retailer order and affiliate payout as two customer purchases. If merchant reports only commission, report only commission recognized/settled, not full order gross. Payhip/FV and affiliate streams require different ledgers.

**Cohorts:** 7-day exploratory and 30-day reconciled cohorts, with refund maturity flags. With small samples report integer denominators and intervals or qualitative uncertainty—not arbitrary significance claims. No production traffic, sales, affiliate commission or settlement has been observed under R366; KPI **NOT COMPUTABLE**, **not $0**.

## Event and data minimization contract for Claude engineering #820

| Step | Event/observable proof | Not allowed to infer |
|---|---|---|
| Published Grok creative | Actual platform URL/immutable `asset_id`/date and approved destination | A view is a website visitor |
| Qualified-human landing | Existing first-party traffic counters + documented bot/internal/QA exclusion | Every raw pageview is a unique human |
| Useful next step | `article_to_focus_click` or `article_to_buyer_guide_click` only when actually fired | A click is a confirmed lead |
| Existing Focus opt-in | Provider-backed, consented **confirmed_signup** when observable | An entered email is a confirmed reader |
| Commercial outbound | `affiliate_outbound` only with an account-issued earning link | An affiliate click is a commission |
| Money received | `merchant_confirmed_purchase` / reconciled paid affiliate conversion / settlement | A hypothetical cart is a sale |
| Net result | Refunds, chargebacks, fees, actual payment status, cost and attribution confidence | A gross retailer order is net revenue |

Never put email addresses, full visitor IDs, transaction IDs, private locations or raw IPs into UTM strings, public GitHub or Drive handoffs. Do not add a duplicate analytics script when existing Jetpack/Ahrefs can supply the relevant aggregate. If provider-side lead-to-purchase stitching cannot be done with appropriate consent, report lead and sale cohorts **separately**, explicitly mark linkage unknown, and do not silently construct an identity graph.

**Attribution confidence:** HIGH only with publisher-backed live asset and independently reconciled merchant event; MEDIUM for confirmed first-party visits and CTAs without merchant proof; LOW for unattributed pageviews. Never attribute a platform view surge to purchase intent without actual click and buyer evidence.

## Controlled comparison: text versus film, not a guaranteed converter

**A:** Independent Zen12 buyer-guide text, study-led hook, actual bundle features, objections, free twelve-minute experiment, seller-claim limits and near-control prospective disclosure. **B:** Exact A buyer copy plus a new optional 42-second original storyboard/poster/transcript and 6-cue caption file. B's video is presently **not encoded or playable**. Both contain **zero active seller hrefs**, disabled controls and no tracking script.

**Hypothesis:** For the *same qualified, high-intent* readers from an authenticated permitted creator asset, an optional short explanatory video may improve comprehension or paid purchase completion versus text alone without hurting performance or accessibility. It may also distract, raise load cost or decrease conversion. No directional claim is justified yet.

**Experiment prerequisites:** named paid-offer rights, exact checkout, independently reviewed mobile design, proper captioned original video, real-browser keyboard and no-JS audit, existing first-party instrumentation, consistent source/campaign and 50/50 stable randomization at experiment-unit level with consent as required, pre-registered primary outcome, and real order/commission reconciliation. Avoid attribution loss when moving to the merchant; verify partner terms before query parameters or deep linking.

**Primary metric:** realized *net revenue per 1,000 qualified human landing sessions* in 7- and 30-day cohorts. Secondary paid orders per qualified session, refund/fee-adjusted net, provider-confirmed leads and lead-to-customer rate. Check landing relevance, misleading perceptions, accessibility complaints and performance as guardrails. Observe source/offer/variant mix rather than judging clicks alone.

**Stop criteria:** unauthorized link or channel, unsupported implication of efficacy, missing/ambiguous FTC material-relationship disclosure, duplicated list, inaccessible mobile CTA, heavy poster/video hurting Core Web Vitals, tracking of personal identifiers without consent, or merchant mismatch.

**Tiny-sample rule:** show numerator, denominator and 7/30-day maturation; no winner declarations from a handful of paid orders. If denominator or settlement unavailable, display NOT COMPUTABLE rather than zero or estimated revenue.

## Privacy-safe first-party attribution; no new pixels

First-party fields requested for existing production analytics only after Claude/privacy approval: `source_platform` (e.g. TikTok/YouTube), `grok_asset_id` only when real, `utm_campaign` (controlled allowlist), `landing_canonical_path`, `offer_id=zen12_full_unverified`, `variant=a|b`, coarse date and outcome event. Do not store raw email, IP, names, full referrer URLs with identifiers, contact IDs or fingerprint IDs in the editorial repo. No duplicate analytics tags or SaaS.

**Event definitions are not fungible:**

- `qualified_human_session`: deduplicated first-party non-bot visit matching a verified creator asset, with bot rules and definition version documented; measurement owner validates denominator.
- `landing_visit`: eligible observed landing path load; not a paying buyer.
- `editorial_cta_click`: user explicitly requests existing free route; not a lead.
- `focus_optin_attempt`: submits existing Jetpack form with consent; not confirmed.
- `focus_lead_confirmed_provider`: provider-level confirmed, deduplicated subscriber status; do not equate email delivery with confirmation.
- `affiliate_click_outbound`: a permitted link interaction only *after* authorization; not a checkout, order or commission.
- `merchant_order_reported`: signed/independently authenticated merchant result with dedupe/order ID, non-sensitive mapping and authorized scope; **not** necessarily settled.
- `merchant_commission_settled`: merchant-recognized payable affiliate commission with actual credited amount and settled status, independent of clicks.
- `refund_or_chargeback`: authenticated adjustment to credited order; assign to sale cohort and keep an audit trail.
- `realized_net_revenue`: collected and settled revenue or commission attributable to the cohort, less refunds, chargebacks, payment/platform fees and other direct transaction costs as actually recorded; never gross impressions.

`net_revenue_per_1000 = (verified_settled_net_revenue / eligible_qualified_human_sessions) * 1000`. Show NULL if numerator, denominator, timing or attribution missing. Reconcile 7- and 30-day cohorts; clearly identify partial/maturing cohorts. No synthetic test values enter production KPIs.

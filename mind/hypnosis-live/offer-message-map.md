# Hypnosis Live / Total Focus — exact-offer marketing alignment

**Reviewed:** October 30, 2026. **State:** Unpublished source-led copy strategy; no member-approved affiliate creative accessed.

## What the merchant actually sells

On https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus , the seller describes a *33-minute, 47 MB* MP3 attributed to J. A. Amos. The page frames it as a response to frequent distraction, task switching and difficulty concentrating, and makes striking brain-reprogramming and productivity promises. The first items are product-format details; the claimed effects are **seller assertions**, not independent findings.

On https://hypnosislive.com/faq , the seller says most tracks run roughly forty minutes, buyers download MP3s after checkout, and a buyer ordinarily pays once per recording. The FAQ states refund requests can be made within fourteen days but limits refunds to five sessions per year. Verify the current checkout and binding refund policy before any purchase recommendation.

On https://inspire3.com/ the umbrella company lists Hypnosis Live. The public https://affiliates.inspire3.com/ site is an application and login portal, not proof Final Vestige has been approved, given a tracking link, or permitted to email promotions. Private program swipe materials were **not** accessed, and no member-only creative may be described as verified.

The seller page showed inconsistent/dynamic promotional pricing. Therefore no price, discount, 93%-off claim, countdown or payout is quoted in the guide. The reader's actual checkout must govern the purchase.

## Buyer motivation and the limits of our promise

The relevant search visitor might arrive from a change-blindness video, a short about attentional blink, or the history of Puységur. Their practical question is often ordinary: “How do I stop abandoning a task the moment I start?” We can acknowledge that problem without diagnosing their attention or promising a treatment.

The strongest supportable offer positioning is a *format proposition*: “For readers who prefer a guided voice, the seller offers a downloadable recording about concentration. Compare the sample, duration, terms and evidence limits before deciding.” This is more precise and defensible than “This will reprogram your brain.”

The brand's mission is evidence-first, so the opening must **not** work by heightening insecurity or claiming readers are fundamentally incapable of focusing. The opening instead gives the visitor a useful baseline test they can perform without purchasing anything. The guide then shows exact seller features, explains what independent research does and does not test, and offers a free alternative.

## Objection map and honest replies

1. **“Will it work for me?”** General hypnosis research is not a controlled trial of Total Focus, and personal results cannot be promised.
2. **“What does the track actually include?”** A specific MP3 download. Seller lists 33 minutes and 47 MB. We have not purchased or listened; the seller indicates previews are available.
3. **“Is this a course?”** A single track differs from an interactive course or clinical appointment; do not overstate the product.
4. **“Will there be an ongoing charge?”** Seller FAQ says single-session purchases do not incur ongoing payments for the same recording; checkout terms must be checked.
5. **“Can I request a refund?”** FAQ specifies fourteen days **and** a maximum of five refunded sessions per year; never rewrite this as unconditional “risk free.”
6. **“Does hypnosis reprogram the brain?”** The seller uses this language, but we found no independent product-specific controlled support for that literal or implied result.
7. **“Is NLP scientifically proven here?”** Listing NLP in a method description does not substantiate the commercial product's results.
8. **“Is this better than Zen12?”** Different formats and purposes: Zen12 markets short meditation sessions/levels; Hypnosis Live sells topic-specific, often longer individual recordings. No head-to-head evidence established.
9. **“What if I don't want to buy anything?”** The full evaluation and the free Seven Day Focus Fieldbook remain available without a purchase, signup wall or payment.

## Presell creative architecture

**Lead:** “A quieter mind is not the same as a rewritten one.” This acknowledges the appeal and questions oversized promises without ridiculing the buyer.

**Immediate value:** A two-minute preparation and twenty-minute task log before any offer. This helps the reader distinguish a manageable habit from advertised transformation.

**Specific, concrete product details:** Name a single exact offer, Total Focus, with seller-attributed recording length, file type, author and preview availability. Don't link every perception article to a giant generic store.

**Independent evidence:** NIH/NCCIH and 2023/2026 scholarly reviews in specific domains; none validates Total Focus's claims. The history of suggestion is about evidentiary caution, not a precedent for modern product efficacy.

**Optional CTA (not yet active):** “Review the Total Focus recording and current terms.” Offer links must be normal transparent anchors only once partner-specific URLs/terms are approved.

**Near-link disclosure:** “If you buy through this link, Final Vestige may receive a commission.” Each placement should carry this immediately before/after the associated link; the general affiliate disclosure page is not enough.

**Conversion integrity:** Two restrained opportunities are enough: an offer-evaluation panel and an end-of-guide decision panel. No popup, countdown, fake scarcity, fabricated endorsements, forced account registration or “before and after” brain graphics.

## Conversion-quality hierarchy

**North-star:** realized net revenue per 1,000 *qualified human sessions*, for 7-day and 30-day cohorts. Qualified human eligibility must be determined by approved, privacy-respecting first-party analytics and invalid-traffic rules, not simply by counting WordPress views. Define the denominator in the report; if it cannot be established, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero.

**Event contract** (specification only; does not install a new event recorder):
1. `campaign_landing`: campaign and page visit using the existing first-party analytics solution, with `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_<topic>_yyyy_mm`, `utm_content=<actual_Grok_asset_id>`; no PII/fingerprints.
2. `article_to_resource_click`: relevant voluntary click to the *existing* /focus/ route; never imply signup.
3. `confirmed_signup`: existing Jetpack provider-confirmed consent/confirmation count if observable; do not equate form view or PDF download with confirmation.
4. `affiliate_click`: click to the issued, *authorized* merchant earning URL after disclosure. Must not be called an order.
5. `verified_checkout_start`: only if merchant provider supplies it with appropriate consent; otherwise unknown.
6. `merchant_confirmed_order` / `merchant_confirmed_commission`: provider receipts/affiliate statement, not client event assumptions.
7. `merchant_confirmed_refund`: reconcile refunds, chargebacks, platform fees, program deductions and settlement state. Reversals require adjustment of old cohorts.

Minimize and aggregate. No visitor IP, address, email, device fingerprint, raw click ID, tracking cookie or extra script in R340. Do not pass subscriber email or identity into UTM or sale reconciliation. Avoid double counting one purchased order appearing in multiple systems. Resolve identity with merchant-issued references only in restricted internal audit material, never the public issue. Cohorts need time window, source, qualified session denominator, complete data status, paid order count, order revenue, net commission, refunds, fees, currency and attribution confidence.

**Formula:** `net = settled_owned_gross_paid + settled_affiliate_commissions - refunds - fees`; `net_per_1000 = 1000 * net / verified_qualified_human_sessions`. If numerator and denominator are not provider-confirmed, leave KPI unavailable; report paid conversion and lead-to-customer rates only with comparable verified denominators. Show a tiny-sample warning for low traffic and never advertise lifts from one or two purchases.

We do not adopt the seller's twelve-minute/hour benefit equivalence. **Variant A:** source-first text guide, accessible HTML, one free twelve-minute task exercise, exact package table, independent study limits, four objections, current merchant consent note and *disabled* commercial control beside clearly worded conditional FTC disclosure.

**Variant B:** the **same buyer copy** with an optional original 42-second film *concept* (source-controlled SVG poster, six scene captions, transcript, performance requirements). Not a playable video. To run a true A/B experiment, encode and commit/host the approved MP4/WebM, ensure optional controls and caption sync, prove parity and measure financial outcomes via Claude's existing stack only. Do not assign live traffic to unrendered film or broken video src.

**Null hypothesis:** film adds no statistically/operationally meaningful improvement in **paid orders / realized net revenue per qualified human session**. Hypothesis: film helps some organic-video visitors understand the actual format and evidence limits before choosing a purchase. This is a question, not a marketing guarantee. Hold all experiment start conditions until eligible offer, approved use of link and release QA. At very small samples, treat all differences as descriptive.

**Disclose immediately before the eventual active link:** “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” Until verified there is no active earning link; the disabled control indicates no purchase action, and the free Fieldbook remains available independent of shopping.

**Separate seller newsletter:** https://zen12.com/gift says seller free MP3 also signs user up to *their* newsletter. Do not import or silently move visitors across mailing lists; the FV Jetpack Focus/Dispatch welcome message remains unchanged.

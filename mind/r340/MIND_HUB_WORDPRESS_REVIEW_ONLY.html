How we protect the evidence

We separate the fact that an experiment occurred, the authors’ interpretation, other researchers’ criticism, and a merchant’s promise. An affiliate relationship cannot establish an archival conclusion or demonstrate a psychological benefit. We do not imply that any audio has been personally tested when it has not.

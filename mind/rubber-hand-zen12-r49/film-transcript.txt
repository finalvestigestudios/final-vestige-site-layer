The hardest part of a short quiet practice may be starting. Twelve minutes is enough time to notice what format you actually prefer. Zen12 sells twelve levels of short audio in four advertised styles. Those are seller-described specifications, not evidence of a neurological transformation. A 2023 review of binaural-beat EEG studies found inconsistent results; it did not test Zen12. Before buying, try twelve free minutes with silence, sound and guidance. Choose what you would repeat. A clock can measure time. It cannot measure the benefits the seller promises.

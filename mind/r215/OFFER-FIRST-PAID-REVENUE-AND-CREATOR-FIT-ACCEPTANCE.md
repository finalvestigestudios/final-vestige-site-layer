## 4. Frozen experiment, not an invented winning variant

**Control A:** original frozen R210 text-only Zen12 guide.
**Treatment B:** the same R210 explanatory text and commercial disclosures plus a **user-optional**, rights-cleared, captioned 42-second original film (currently **concept only; not rendered**). R215 presell is a *candidate copy review*, not another A/B arm. R214 is also an alternate copy proposal, not valid B compared with R210 A because words/hook differ.

**Hypothesis:** optional film might improve comprehension and credible purchase decisions, but can also distract and harm mobile loading; the study has no a priori conversion winner. Must not run live until merchant authorization and owner release. At staging, the only randomized difference should be the optional video block. A and B otherwise use the same headline, offer, sources, button, disclosure, layout and metadata. B must preserve static poster and transcript without JavaScript, and use `preload="none"`, no autoplay, accessible captions and deliberate play once actual MP4 and synced VTT exist. No third-party media embed. If video pushes loading beyond agreed CWV budgets or harms accessibility, fail before release.

**Proposed assignment:** Claude engineers privacy-reviewed, first-party, server-side 50:50 assignment for eligible human sessions; do not add a vendor, identity fingerprint, third-party cookies or client-side network dependency. Document consent basis and choose a first-party short-lived allocation token only if approved by privacy review; without that, no reliable individual experiment grouping is claimed. Reject automatic random variant flicker on every request. Record allocation and observations without collecting names, email addresses, IP records or fingerprint attributes. Respect caching and automated-bot exclusion. Source QA must inspect 360/390/768/1440/1920 widths, real keyboard and assistive technology, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast/focus, low-bandwidth performance and native no-JS behaviors before actual staging release.

**Primary success metric is paid, not click:** settled purchase or cleared affiliate commission per qualified-human session, net of refunds and fees. Secondary: verified lead-to-customer conversion, purchasing-intent CTA click **only** as a diagnostic, buyer comprehension/objection survey if consented. Stop for misleading disclosure, false attribution, broken merchant link, new tracking/PII, checkout friction, increased opt-out complaints, inaccessible video, or uncertain refunds. On fewer than meaningful paid transactions, report descriptive counts and uncertainty—never significance or “lift.” Plan to inspect both 7-day and 30-day cohorts and distinguish refund maturity.

```text
merchant event    = verified_checkout_start if provider supplies it; merchant_confirmed_purchase or cleared_affiliate_conversion
refund/fee event  = merchant refund, chargeback, processor expense, affiliate reversal; from authoritative commerce statements
```

Do not install new scripts. Reuse approved existing telemetry if able to discriminate real events and exclude known bots; otherwise log the event as unmeasured. "affiliate_outbound" is **not** an order. Never pass PII, email, visitor ID, full query strings, or individual health/sensitive signals to a new attribution vendor. Verify consent and data-retention policy first.

**Scorecard 7d and 30d:** qualified_human_sessions; unique authentic campaign asset IDs; guide CTA outbound counts; Jetpack-confirmed subscribers; merchant-confirmed paid owned-product orders; independently settled affiliate commissions; gross receipts; refunds; chargebacks; actual processing/affiliate fees; realized net revenue. Cohorts should not silently equate booking/order date to cleared payment date. Record how refunds settle.

```text
realized_net_revenue = settled_owned_receipts + cleared_affiliate_commissions
                       - refunds - chargebacks - applicable_fees
net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions =
    1000 * realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions
```

If qualified_human_sessions is absent, or the merchant ledger has not been reconciled, show **NOT COMPUTABLE** (not "$0"). Paid conversion rate = merchant_confirmed_paid_orders / qualified_human_sessions only when both numerator and denominator are verified; include duplicate and cancellation controls. Do not imply that a click confirms purchase or that a Jetpack confirmation follows automatically from a Focus visit. Published pageviews/followers are diagnostic only.

The Final Vestige method

For every claim, separate the underlying document or observation, the speaker's interpretation, independent replication and the decision you are being asked to make. Repeated citations are not automatically independent witnesses. Failed predictions remain part of the case file. A scientific paper about a broad practice does not automatically authenticate a branded purchase promise.

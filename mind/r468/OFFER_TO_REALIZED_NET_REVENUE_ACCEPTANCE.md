## Measurement contract — first party / no personal identifiers

At the existing approved analytics integration, a session may carry **validated enumerated** `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_choice_or_attention_pilot_2026_11`, and `utm_content=<genuine_grok_asset_id>`. Validate that the asset ID was actually published; reject unapproved arbitrary identifiers. Record landing page slug, anonymized cohort day, consent state when applicable, and intended offer ID. Do **not** collect IP, names, raw email, user-agent strings, cross-site IDs or sensitive diagnoses in this workstream. No user fingerprinting and no unapproved cross-domain linking.

Strict event distinctions:
1. **qualified_human_session**: first-party rules exclude known bots and suspicious rapid bursts; maintain an auditable filter version and count uncertainty.
2. **landing_view / relevant_cta_click**: diagnostic; CTA clicks are not orders.
3. **fieldbook_signup_requested** vs **confirmed_lead**: use existing Jetpack provider confirmation/delivery state, deduplicated only by authorized provider reporting. Do not replay/replace welcome copy or claim a lead if only a button was clicked.
4. **affiliate_click**: valid off-site click to issued, authorized, disclosure-adjacent link; *never* a sale or commission.
5. **merchant_paid_order_verified / affiliate_paid_conversion_verified**: ledger/reconciliation evidence from provider, date, offer, amount and applicable reporting attribution. Where merchant returns aggregate only, record unmatched attribution as **UNKNOWN**, never allocate it to Grok based on hope.
6. **refund / chargeback / fee**: reconcile separately against confirmed payments. Do not count pending or unpaid commissions as realized revenue.

**KPI:** For each 7-day and 30-day acquisition cohort, `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * (settled_gross_receipts - confirmed_refunds - payment_processor_and_affiliate_fees - directly_attributable_costs) / verified_qualified_human_sessions`. Paid conversion rate and confirmed customers must use the same coherent cohort/offer population; distinguish *sold* from *settled*. For affiliate earnings, count only realized **publisher commission received**, not the customer's entire order revenue; refund reversals/fees come off that received commission. If session denominator or settlement data is absent, output **NOT COMPUTABLE**, never zero by assumption. Warn for small samples, bot contamination, delayed refunds, unresolved attribution and right-censored 30-day windows.

**A/B decision:** Primary outcome = settled net revenue per 1,000 qualified eligible sessions; secondary = independently paid purchases per eligible session, verified lead-to-customer conversion and refund rate; diagnostics = clickthrough/engagement. Pre-register allocation, minimum detectable effect and duration with Claude/Site QA; avoid winning on clicks when sales are absent. Report traffic mismatch if one Grok clip sends exclusively to one variant. Never force video playback or hide product limitations to improve conversion.

## One high-intent audience and one truthful offer

**Audience:** a reader seeking a short, guided listening habit who is deciding between a free routine and an audio package, and who is willing to read features and limitations before paying. Do not position as someone seeking treatment for anxiety, pain, trauma, depression, memory disorders or cognitive decline.

**Reader's job-to-be-done:** "I want to start a repeatable short attention practice; does a purchased recorded program give me a format I value enough to pay for?" That is answerable from format, usability, total checkout terms and alternatives. It does not require an unsupported claim that 12 minutes equals one hour of meditation.

**Trial value proposition:** "First try twelve minutes free. Then decide whether guided audio and multiple listening formats are worth the price and refund terms you see at the actual merchant." Source: Zen12 seller's own descriptions, not purported personal use. Offer CTA remains a disabled native button with a plain-language prospective affiliate disclosure directly above. The CTA does not transmit anything, log a click or leave the site.

## Privacy-respecting first-party measurement contract

**Existing analytics only:** Claude checks the installed Jetpack/first-party configuration. No fresh pixel, browser fingerprint, ad network, cookie, paid platform, redirect shortener or duplicate measurement script. A privacy-safe reporting implementation requires founder release approval.

Campaign URLs use ordinary, human-readable UTM fields, after verifying that Grok's actual platform allows link placement and that an approved campaign exists:

`utm_source` = youtube / tiktok / other verified actual platform;
`utm_medium` = organic_social;
`utm_campaign` = mind_source_audit_2026_11 (descriptive hypothesis, not proof of launched campaign);
`utm_content` = **actual immutable public Grok asset_id, NOT a guessed or hashed user ID**.

**Events:** campaign_landing (source, campaign, verified asset_id, route, day; qualified-human status from defensible aggregate filters) → article_to_resource_click (only existing Focus link, optional) → confirmed_signup (only if reliably observable in existing Jetpack, distinguish pending/confirmed and delivery) → approved_offer_guide_visit → affiliate_click (only a verified, authorized outbound URL and first-party event; it means *click*, not purchase) → merchant_reported_purchase (independently authenticated partner statement and attributable order / commission) → settled_commission net of refunds/fees. No raw visitor IP, email, health preference, cookie ID or individual cross-site fingerprint. Use aggregate day/week attribution where exact user joins are unavailable, report those as cohort associations not individual proof.

**Revenue acceptance:** report 7-day and 30-day cohorts separately. `net_revenue_per_1k_qh_sessions = 1000 * realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`. Realized affiliate revenue uses *settled publisher earnings after reversals*, **not gross merchant order value**. Owned sales use settled receipt minus refunds, chargebacks, merchant fees and directly attributable product costs where auditable. If session denominator is missing, paid order is unconfirmed or reconciliation impossible, show **N/A**, not zero, not estimated sales. Show full counts and tiny-sample warning for low-N cohorts. **Do not claim statistical significance** from low click counts. Deduplicate idempotent merchant receipt IDs within merchant-authorized reporting; never collect or publish individual buyer information.

## Controlled A/B experiment, not conversion theater

**Question:** among qualified consent-appropriate visitors to the exact authorized Zen12 guide, does including an optional original 42-second explainer change independently verified *settled* purchase conversion versus a source-rich text-only guide?

- **A:** complete text-only guide with a free twelve-minute baseline, seller-specific features, limitations, objections and a commercially disabled link while held.
- **B:** identical core guide plus an original, optionally played 42-second film with poster, valid WebVTT captions, complete transcript, no autoplay and a no-JS text fallback. Current R468 B contains poster, captions concept and transcript but **no encoded video**. No actual A/B run exists.
- **Assignment:** when permitted, implement a stable, privacy-conscious first-party assignment that does not require fingerprinting; no competing CTA copy, price display or merchant URL differences. Preserve no-JS access. Check consent and lawful analytics expectations.
- **Primary endpoint:** verified paid conversions and settled net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions, per 7/30-day cohort; not visitor clicks or time on page. **Secondary diagnostics:** guide comprehension, relevant CTA clicks, confirmed existing-list leads, video play rate when consensually measurable, Core Web Vitals and bounce/exit by device.
- **Counterfactual:** video may harm performance, distract from the offer, or introduce an extra demand to watch. A may outperform B. No assumed winner.
- **Interpretation:** document allocation, variant exposure, missing merchant match rate, denominator integrity, time windows, refund lag and sample size. If any missing, mark **not interpretable**, not pass. Do not silently compare bot-inflated pageviews.

## Disclosure and truthfulness contract

An authentic affiliate purchase link, if separately approved, must have **visible, proximate** plain-language text: "If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you." The general affiliate disclosure page alone is insufficient. Claim support must be **product-specific**, adequate and current. Never lift merchant countdowns, guarantees, mental-health outcomes, income promises, price snippets or testimonials into the page as independent proof. Seller feature lists remain attributed to the exact seller page. Cite original studies near explanation; peer-reviewed results do not prove the product's mechanisms or outcomes without direct testing.

## Accessibility/performance/security gates

Inspect 360, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920 CSS-pixel widths; keyboard links, focus contrast and order, screen-reader headings and disabled CTA status, 200% zoom, reduced motion, no-JS access, one H1, semantic landmarks and color contrast. B's future actual `<video controls preload="none" poster="...">` must NOT autoplay, must have accurate playable audio/video captions and nearby transcript; poster format/rights, encoding, total bytes and critical rendering path must be measured. Check mobile network and Lighthouse/CWV without claiming a score until run. Run the site QA sanitizer on a WordPress preview **only after** founder-approved draft staging; raw source-only HTML is not a CMS draft. Prevent untrusted tracking pixels, script injection, inline event handlers, leakage of affiliate tokens and new subscriber forms. Confirm link targets do not silently route through redirects that violate merchant terms.

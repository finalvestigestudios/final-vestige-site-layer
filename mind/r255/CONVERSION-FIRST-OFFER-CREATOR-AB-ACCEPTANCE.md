## Measurement contract / privacy
An event is a step, not a sale:
`asset_id` + `utm_source` + `utm_medium=organic_social` + `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11` + `utm_content=approved_asset_id` → **qualified_human_session** → **landing_view** → **offer_guide_cta** → optional **affiliate_click** → externally validated **partner_purchase** → **settled_commission** → nonduplicated refunds/fees/chargebacks → **realized_net_revenue**.

- An actual Grok post and matching signed-out destination must be proven before any session is called attributed; profile-link constraints can destroy post-level attribution. Never fabricate `utm_content`.
- Keep only consent-compliant first-party aggregates; do not store IP, fingerprint, full referrers containing PII, subscriber addresses, third-party private commission data or customer IDs in public Github/Drive. Existing analytics owner Claude reviews implementation, Site QA reviews mobile and bot filtering.
- **affiliate_click ≠ paid order;** provider-**confirmed** Jetpack subscriber ≠ purchase; Payhip checkout start ≠ completed/settled payment; partner-reported lead ≠ cleared commission.
- Reconcile actual paid orders and cleared commissions, refund/fee adjustments and real denominator, preserving exact currency, ordered timestamps and one-offer consistency. No double deduction where provider settlement is already net. Use a privacy-preserving contract/aggregation where provider permits it. Without cross-provider reliable evidence, mark conversion **NOT_COMPUTABLE** rather than zero.
- Main KPI `1000 × realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions` 7/30-day; guard denominator zero, unknown provenance, bot traffic, no attribution and uncertain settlement. Also show verified paid conversion rate, confirmed buyers, gross/net receipts, refund rate and provider-confirmed lead-to-customer conversion; never average incompatible cohorts.

## Buyer-specific copy architecture

**Problem:** short, repeatable listening preference and avoiding a confusing large music library. **Insight:** routine duration is not physiological proof. **Micro-story:** a perfectly functioning clock may feel frozen after a gaze jump—an independently researched example of the limits of subjective experience, with no false transfer of the mechanism to audio. **Demonstration:** free 12-minute no-purchase format diary. **Product fit:** seller-described twelve levels and four formats are useful decision criteria, not efficacy claims. **Proof:** clear split merchant specs vs independent meditation evidence. **Objections:** cost/need, attention, recording/device usability, disclosure, phone/SMS and refund text. **CTA:** disabled and immediately disclosed until written partner approval. Buyer not pressured by countdown or “one hour in twelve” claim.

## Founder A/B question and what constitutes evidence

A = complete independent text-only guide. B = byte-identical substantive guide plus an optional **original** 40s concept film with poster and no-JS transcript. B contains no autoplay, remote player or fake video button. Only after hosting and media QA may a native player be incorporated. The film is a separate explainer, NOT the same TikTok reused as bait. This is a hypothesis; no comparison data exist.

**Primary future outcome:** qualified outbound intent toward a properly disclosed authorized product URL; **secondary:** view of guide and noncommercial Focus click. **Confirmed revenue outcome:** only merchant account conversion reporting, if explicitly permitted, never inferred from clicks. Before approval all merchant click and revenue counters remain unavailable.

**Attribution minimization:** potential future first-party categorized counts (channel, topic, A/B group, action) aggregated only after separate approval. No cookies, fingerprint, referer URL, IP, UTM parameter retention, full URL, emails, purchase IDs, contact details, cross-site pixels or identity joining. Do not use a real visitor identifier to deduplicate; without that, describe pageviews/click actions, NOT unique people. Suppress groups <20. A future A/B split requires appropriate randomization and design review; campaign-link traffic allocation alone is confounded and not a causal experiment. The current `attribution.mjs` only validates synthetic fixture data and is disabled.

**Statistical discipline:** do not claim “+X% conversion” or even direction of lift from small samples. Pre-register attribution source buckets and definition of qualified outbound before activation. Record landing traffic in A and B with independent browser QA and ethical consent practices. A merchant-confirmed purchase cannot be retrospectively linked to a person on FV.

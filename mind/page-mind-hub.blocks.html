HOW WE TREAT COMMERCIAL CLAIMS

Curiosity isn't a blank check.

We're evaluating meditation, hypnosis and other mind-related tools. A seller's promise is not an independently demonstrated result. Our future product guides will explain what a product actually contains, who it may suit, the evidence and limitations, and any commercial relationship.

We won't recommend a tool merely because it pays a commission. No purchase is needed to read our research or use the free Fieldbook.

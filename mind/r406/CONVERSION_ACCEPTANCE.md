## The A/B experiment (hypothesis, not conversion result)

- A: useful, wholly readable Zen12 text-only source/buyer guide, native-disabled merchant control, free exercise and existing Focus path.
- B: **identical buyer copy** plus optional native-details 42-second original SVG/CSS timed illustration and visible matching transcript; separate VTT available. **This is NOT encoded or playable video**; a finalized video would require recording/encoding and full media QA, and must not be described otherwise. Opening the details triggers motion in CSS, and reduced-motion preferences show a still; no remote assets/scripts, no consent or prefetch surprises.
- Question: Is the optional original illustration helpful enough to justify effort without undermining the plain text? The hypothesis is not a guaranteed conversion lift. First test comprehension and qualified buyer intent; **primary business outcome**, only after approval, is independently confirmed paid orders or settled affiliate commissions, net of refunds/fees.
- Performance: no paid analytics vendors, new mailing list or remote video embed. Test with JS disabled and native keyboard, touch, screen reader, reduced-motion; Site QA owns real 360/390/768/1440/1920 px visual and WCAG 2.2 AA review. **None of these browser/device tests has run**.
- Commercial activation is gated by named publisher approval, actual permitted channel/SKU, exact earning URL and verified referred checkout/refund. If not obtained, the CTA remains disabled, and the A/B must not be counted as a sales test.

The evidence of being human

Your mind is an unreliable witness.

You can miss a conspicuous change, explain a choice you did not make, or mistake a convincing account for its cause. This archive follows the experiments, the historical papers, and the methods that separate experience from proof.

Our method

Start with the record. Keep the failures.

We connect modern perception science to actual historical documents, without treating testimony as an automatic mechanism or commercial copy as a study. Each investigation should let readers inspect the underlying source, see what was measured, and learn what is still unresolved.

The evidence rule

A demonstrated perceptual effect is not evidence that any consumer hypnosis, meditation or brainwave-audio product works. A careful editor keeps those claims apart.

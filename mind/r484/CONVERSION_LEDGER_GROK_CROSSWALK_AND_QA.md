## Measurement contract (implementation requested, NOT deployed)
**Join key:** independently authenticated `asset_id` supplied by Grok plus first-party `utm_campaign` using non-sensitive opaque values. Reject user names, emails, contact details, free-text query values, medical inferences, cross-site fingerprinting and hidden cookies.
**Suggested canonical event stages** — reuse Claude's existing instrumentation, do NOT install another tag manager:
- `qualified_human_landing`: human/filter criteria, allowed campaign identifiers, original source URL path, confirmed landing; not pageviews counted as sales
- `focus_primary_cta_click`: exact first-party Focus link and location; event not lead
- `focus_confirmed_lead`: only a provider-confirmed double opt-in record with lawful minimization/consent; do not record email address in event data
- `affiliate_click`: only after earned-link rights approved, with product/placement/variant; this is outbound intent, not sale
- `merchant_verified_order`: independent partner or FV checkout report, permissible opaque transaction join key and settled timestamp; do not infer it from referral
- `merchant_refund_or_fee`: documented reversal, chargeback and processing fees
**No new scripts until Claude validates existing event names, consent state and deduplication.** If affiliate reporting cannot attribute orders to one session, record aggregate paid orders separately; never fabricate join.

**Primary metric, 7/30-day paid cohorts:** realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions = `1000 × (merchant-confirmed settled payments – refunds – chargebacks – platform/processing fees) / independently verified qualified human sessions`. Use time zones and cutoffs consistently. Separate gross orders, confirmed customers, paid conversion rate, verified lead-to-customer ratio, and unsubscribes. With no authenticated numerator/denominator report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero and not “click revenue.” Report <100 qualified sessions per arm as tiny-sample descriptive only, and do not imply significance from small numbers.

## Controlled film A/B design (NOT LIVE)
**Hypothesis:** a user-initiated 42-second explanation of the evidence gap might improve the percentage of qualified readers who make an informed decision about the same offer. This is a hypothesis, **not a prediction of increased revenue**. Informed decisions may mean choosing free resources instead of purchasing; that is ethically acceptable. The cost of video must not overwhelm low-bandwidth mobile sessions.

A = inherited R284 text-only guide, frozen exact HTML; B = identical buyer content and action hierarchy, with the optional accessible player. The only treatment is the video and its necessary non-commercial captions/transcript. The same merchant offer, location of disclosure, link destination, confirmation flow and creator campaign must be used in both groups. Before deployment, select one approved /mind/ buying URL and mutually exclusive A/B assignment using existing first-party Claude analytics, with user consent as required; do not install a parallel third-party tracking SDK.

Preset review should check qualitative trust, mobile usability, broken outbound paths and verified commercial conversion, not just more clicks. Define assignment population and minimum observation/sample warning in advance. Do not infer statistical significance or declare a winner from low volume. No exposure/sale events have occurred in this pilot.

## Measurement contract: evidence first, arithmetic second
Approved creator asset_id must appear as a stable vetted token in `utm_content`, `utm_campaign` must identify the specific creator-topic pilot, and `utm_source` should reflect the true platform. A generic platform click or unverified referrer is not a qualified-human creator session. Attribution to a purchase requires seller/Payhip order-level proof or reliable aggregate merchant reconciliation—not a page visit or `affiliate_click` event.

Suggested first-party schema, after privacy/consent review with Claude: `landing_exposure`, `relevant_cta_click`, `confirmed_lead` (from provider, not form impression), `affiliate_click` (verified account-issued link only), `merchant_confirmed_order`, `commission_settled`, `refund_or_reversal`. No email address, IP, raw full referrer with personal query strings, browser fingerprint, or cross-site pixel is necessary. Dedupe with a short-lived first-party session surrogate under approved consent policy; never claim exact humans based only on bot-distorted pageviews.

**Net revenue per 1,000 qualified sessions = (independently verified settled net receipts, minus applicable refunds/reversals/fees) / eligible source-verified qualified sessions × 1,000.** If either numerator evidence or valid denominator is missing, return `NULL / NOT_COMPUTABLE`, **not zero**. Keep 7-day and 30-day cohorts separate and show sample sizes; show leads and clicks as diagnostic events, never financial outcomes. Even when both numbers exist, adjust for reversal lag and refund windows before calling revenue realized.

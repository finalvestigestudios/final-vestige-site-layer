The crucial gap in the claim

The merchant says listening can lead to focus lasting four hours or the rest of the day. No independently verified controlled test of this exact recording and duration was identified in the inspected material. Studies of general auditory beats are not interchangeable with proof of one consumer product.

Technical caution: The page says headphones are not required. Do not automatically borrow results from binaural-beat experiments that deliver different tones separately to each ear.

A 2023 review of 14 EEG studies found five supportive, eight contradictory and one mixed entrainment results. A separate meta-analysis reported an overall attention/memory effect across diverse binaural-beat experiments, alongside conflicting results. Neither measured this exact Focus Shot bundle.

Try something useful before buying

Choose one fifteen-minute, low-stakes task. Write down a single objective, use a free timer and note what actually gets done. Repeat another day with similar work and conditions. This is a preference check, not a controlled efficacy trial; sleep, task difficulty and expectation can change the result.

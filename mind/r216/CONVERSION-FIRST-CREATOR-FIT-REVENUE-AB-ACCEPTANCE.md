- **Hypothesis:** an optional visual summary may make the evidence caveat clearer and improve *eventual net paid transactions* from a genuinely high-intent audience. It may also distract, delay load or depress conversion; no positive outcome is assumed.
- **Staging rule:** no-JS is fully useful. When and only when a rights-cleared final film is rendered, Site QA validates optional deliberate playback, first-party host, poster, complete synchronized captions, transcript, no autoplay or preload of large video bytes (`preload="none"`), no media third-party request, no new instrumentation script. - **Assignment proposal:** Claude's already-governed first-party attribution lane (#820) should propose a stable 50/50 session assignment, cache-safe and consent-reviewed, without collecting names, email, IP address, fingerprint, health data or raw URLs. If a reliable assignment protocol cannot be implemented without new trackers or privacy problems, do not run an experiment or claim a treatment lift.
- **Success criterion:** after both offer approval and valid measurement, compare **realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** and independently confirmed paid conversion rate, looking at both 7- and 30-day cohorts. Print denominators, order counts, net amounts, refunds and how the cohort is attributed. Small sample → show uncertainty and descriptive counts only; no significance claims or winner without adequate data.

Field note 001 / the word and the ink

One card. Two competing truths.

In 1935 J. Ridley Stroop asked people to name the ink in which conflicting color words were printed. The word on the page could get in the way of naming the color. The original paper compared distinct tasks and controls; our illustration above does not reproduce those methods.

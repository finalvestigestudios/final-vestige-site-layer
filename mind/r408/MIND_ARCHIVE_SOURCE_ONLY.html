How to read these files

Start with the primary record or original scientific study. Distinguish a seller's account of a product from research into a general category. For health or psychological efficacy, look for product-specific adequate evidence and credible comparison conditions. A personal testimony can be historically valuable without proving a proposed physical explanation.

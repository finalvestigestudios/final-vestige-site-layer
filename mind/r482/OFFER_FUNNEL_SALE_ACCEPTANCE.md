## First-party event semantics (implementation specification ONLY)
Use the **existing approved** measurement facility after Claude reviews; no second analytics script, no new persistent cookies or paid SaaS. Do not collect names, emails, raw IP, unique cross-site fingerprints or sensitive perceptual/health states. Honor applicable consent settings.
- `campaign_landing`: timestamp-bucket UTC, consent state, anonymized single-session group where already available, canonical landing path, coarse device group, platform source, `utm_campaign`, exact **verified** `utm_content=asset_id`; dedupe within a session if supported.
- `guide_cta_view`: page/experiment_variant and exact offer-eligibility state; this is not a lead or a sale.
- `affiliate_click` (future, only authorized): outbound destination product code, approved URL-class, timestamp-bucket, variant and verified asset_id. This is an intent/click event, **not** proof of purchase.
- `confirmed_signup`: only on independently confirmed existing Jetpack record/consent or approved event, not button presses or an entered email.
- `merchant_confirmed_purchase`: only from merchant-authoritative reconciliation with independent transaction reference, conversion date and settled amount; no raw customer identifier in published analytics.
- `refund_or_fee_adjustment`: authorized ledger correction to paid settlement. Need net not gross.

**Formula (when denominator and net both authenticated)**: net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = (gross_paid_revenue - refunds - fees - chargebacks) / qualified_human_sessions × 1000. Report unit, currency, date cohort, eligibility filter, missing attribution rate, sales count, refunds/fees and **tiny sample warning**; not estimable if either side missing. 7-day and 30-day windows are separate; compare confirmed paid conversion, not click rate as a substitute.

**A/B causal design:** Variant A complete text guide vs B identical core text + optional 42s original film. No autoplay, no blocking iframe, no third-party autoplay, no-JS complete copy. Pre-register primary metric as verified refund-adjusted net revenue / 1000 qualified sessions, secondary confirmed paid purchases per qualified session; choose device- and platform-balanced randomized assignment only when an existing approved first-party experiment mechanism can be verified. Otherwise mark comparison **descriptive only**, avoid fake significance or causal uplift. Exclude internal/bot traffic under documented pre-registered rules. Neither variant should win on video starts alone. No cohort results have been measured.

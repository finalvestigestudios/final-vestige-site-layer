## Important new blocker: merchant sample creates a second email relationship
The official Zen12 free MP3 page (https://zen12.com/gift) explicitly says the visitor will also receive the seller newsletter. ## Controlled A/B hypothesis and event contract
A = static text-only source and buyer guide. B = precisely the same substantive copy plus original 42-second poster, visible transcript and WebVTT *concept*. Film is not encoded or playable; do not describe B as video-tested. After affiliate authorization, media encoding, browser QA and founder release, randomize qualifying human traffic where platform and consent permit; avoid changing product, CTA, privacy copy or tier across variants. Hypothesis: optional short explanation might increase verified net purchases per 1,000 qualified humans versus text alone; opposite result or zero effect remain possible. Small samples: report denominators, full confidence uncertainty and no superiority claim.

Keep first-party attribution inside the existing Claude-owned analytics system, no new vendor/script: utm_source=tiktok|youtube|instagram (only if actually published); utm_medium=organic_social; utm_campaign=mind_source_pilot_2026_11; utm_content=Grok's genuine immutable asset_id. Validate enumerated values, cap length, avoid raw URL fragments and PII. Cohort 7/30 days. Event taxonomy:
- qualified_human_session: server-validated deduplicated privacy-safe session using existing consent/anti-bot controls; never assume visits equal humans.
- article_to_focus_click: a local UX event, not a subscription.
- confirmed_signup: provider-observable confirmed Jetpack subscriber only; no duplicate subscribe event.
- affiliate_click: authorized disclosed outbound to exact partner-issued URL; no link cloaking; this is NOT a sale.
- verified_paid_order: authoritative merchant paid order/approved commission report, de-duplicated by provider transaction identity.
- refund_fee_adjustment: externally verified refunds, reversals, commissions and fees.

Revenue KPI = 1000 * (verified settled gross receipts - refunds - fees - chargebacks) / verified unique qualified human sessions in 7/30-day cohort. If order linkage or human denominators are missing, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero or a proxy from clicks.

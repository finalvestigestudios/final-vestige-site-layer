Research boundary: the claims we will not repeat as findings

Zen12 advertises a twelve-minute experience as equivalent to an hour of advanced meditation and describes broad benefits. Those are seller efficacy assertions. No independent, sufficiently product-specific trials were verified to support that equivalence or imply treatment of anxiety, ADHD, depression, sleep disorders, memory problems or other health conditions. The buyer's objections, answered without a promise

Why choose it at all?
Someone who prefers a predefined, multi-level audio library may value convenience and format. Preference is not proven therapeutic effectiveness.
Why not buy?
A no-cost routine may serve your needs just as well. Nobody needs to pay to try ordinary mindful attention.
Is this the same as hypnosis or a focus shot?
No. Hypnosis Live Total Focus is seller-described as a 33-minute hypnosis recording; Brainwave Shots Focus Shot lists 15- and 30-minute audio versions. There is no verified independent head-to-head effectiveness comparison here.

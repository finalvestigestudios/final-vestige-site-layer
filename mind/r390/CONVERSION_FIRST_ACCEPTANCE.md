## Product → audience fit

- **High intent:** adult who is voluntarily shopping for a structured library of guided/ambient meditation files. No targeting of vulnerable clinical, financial, trauma or memory-impairment audiences.
- **Credible value proposition:** organized library, 12-level progression, downloadable audio, four modes. These are **seller-described inclusions**. No promise of measurable calm, restored memory, superior focus, medical change or compressed meditation time.
- **Key objections to answer:** Does the person prefer recorded instruction? Is it a convenience they will use? What is the actual payable total and tax? What are the seller's current terms for fulfillment/refunds? Is a free time-boxed practice sufficient? What product-specific science actually supports the marketed claims?
- **Guide A:** [R390 text guide](./ZEN12_A_EVIDENCE_GUIDE.html). **Guide B:** [same guide plus optional 42s poster/film storyboard](./ZEN12_B_FILM_HYPOTHESIS.html). Identical buyer facts, CTA, objections and source qualification. Only B adds optional film context. Neither can sell or count clicks.
- **Near-button future relationship statement:** “If Final Vestige later offers an approved purchasing link and you buy through it, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” On any authorized release it becomes an immediate present-tense commission disclosure adjacent to the actual link, including narrow/mobile viewport and screen-reader order; footer alone is insufficient.

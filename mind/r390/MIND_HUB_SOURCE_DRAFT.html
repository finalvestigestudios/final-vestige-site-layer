The boundary we won't erase

A compelling experience is not a controlled explanation.

Memory experiments show that sometimes an unspoken word feels remembered. The Puységur archive reveals how historical testimony was assembled into a theory of magnetism. Those are different categories of evidence. Neither supplies proof that a vendor's audio changes brain activity, cures anxiety or compresses an hour of meditation into twelve minutes.

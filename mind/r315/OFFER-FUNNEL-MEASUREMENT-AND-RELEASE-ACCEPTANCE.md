## Original controlled A/B protocol

**A:** Useful text-only, source-linked buying guide; accessible and complete without media or JavaScript.

**B:** Identical copy and source hierarchy with the optional original forty-two-second explanatory short-film *concept* placed before the core article. Poster (original inline SVG), timed narration, visible transcript and separate WebVTT caption concept. B presently has **no encoded video, no autoplay and no functional play control**. To run a valid production experiment, an actual rights-cleared lightweight video rendition with user-initiated play, caption verification, accessible transcript, mobile layout and performance regression is required. Both versions remain noindex/unpublished and have truly disabled purchasing controls.

**Hypothesis:** An optional short explainer may increase *qualified* pre-sell engagement and eventually confirmed purchases among genuinely audio-curious readers, but can also distract, make pages slower, or imply unsupported scientific authority. No uplift is predicted or guaranteed.

**Randomization:** Only after approved staged implementation, 50/50 assignment using existing first-party infrastructure, consistent per visitor/session only to the extent privacy permission allows, auditable exclusion/bot rules, stable variant naming. **No test until an authorized paid offer and merchant conversion feed exist.** Otherwise A/B click rates are diagnostic usability, not business success.

**Pre-registered primary metric:** 7- and 30-day *net realized revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions*, by experiment variant, after merchant settlement and eligible refunds/fees. Secondary: verified paid conversion rate (deduplicated paid orders / qualified sessions); confirmed leads where provider-confirmed; accepted orders; median time to first validated offer click; opt-out and page-load impact. Compare **uncertainty intervals**, not a premature winner; tiny cohorts are insufficient to certify performance. Qualified sessions denominator must be reported with source/asset attribution confidence.

**Decision rule:** launch no paid experiment without verified earning eligibility, safe checkout, disclosures and source substantiation. Pause any version that shows false product claims, layout corruption, broken signup, altered Focus welcome, privacy failure or regression affecting Core Web Vitals. An early positive click-rate result never overrides a negative settled-revenue or compliance result.

## Material source-to-checkout issue
Dispatch identified that direct non-affiliate seller links in an email can bypass the withheld FV guide and lead to checkout. An explicit disabled button is insufficient if another seller link remains clickable. For R39 all proposed buyer-visible pages have NO clickable zen12.com, inspire3.com or other merchant URLs. Exact merchant addresses remain in source-register.tsv and claim-permissions.tsv for independent reviewers. This is a temporary pre-authorization hold, not a plan to hide provenance. Before publication, reconsider a clearly marked merchant source link only after a buyer-route, disclosure and commercial-rights review.

## A/B hypothesis and a11y
Variant A is the complete text guide. Variant B is byte-identical substantive guide content plus an original 40-second poster/transcript concept. VTT and SVG are source-controlled. No MP4 hosted, fake play button, iframe, autoplay or JS. If B ever launches, use an optional native first-party video with poster and valid synchronized text track. Measure real performance and keyboard/screen-reader behavior at 360, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920px, no JS, reduced motion and 200% zoom. Film is not promised to convert better.

## Attribution: disabled
privacy-r39.mjs is a purely synthetic schema validator and demonstrator, fixed ENABLED false. It permits only enumerated categorical fields, rejects identifiers/URLs/referrer/UTM/free text/cookies and suppresses cells smaller than 10. Such thresholds do not guarantee privacy under repeated queries and are NOT sufficient for live deployment. No server endpoint, browser listeners, cookies, pixels or data writes exist. Future measurement, if ever approved, should count source-category / variant / outbound intent only with lawful privacy review. A click cannot prove a sale.

## Narrative / ethics

A person wants a short listening routine; the seller offers a twelve-minute format; the hour-equivalence claim is not established by the independent evidence reviewed; the viewer can make one free, practical choice. No testimonials, simulated EEG, mystical brain imaging, pressure countdown, medical or wealth claims. The video remains silent with captions also baked into planned frames; WebVTT adds an accessibility track for later hosted playback.

### Audio and accessibility
- Silent original animation. There is no background track requiring music licensing, and no flashing. Future narration, if produced, must exactly match caption/transcript text and would need independent sound QA.
- 540 × 960 minimum, 9:16, 40.000 seconds, 24 or 12 fps; original typography and graphics only. Avoid text below 28px at 540px width; leave UI-safe margins for TikTok overlays.
- No autoplay; no `autoplay` or `muted` pseudo-autoplay. Future video: native controls, playsinline, preload='none', valid first-party MP4/poster, linked captions `kind=captions srclang=en default`, no vendor domains. Accessible plain transcript outside media and no-JS visible fallback.

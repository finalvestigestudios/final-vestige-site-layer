## One specific conversion test, with no fake outcomes

**Audience:** curious readers who have separately expressed an intention to try a structured meditation-audio routine, not people who merely watched a surprising illusion.

**Offer:** Zen12 Premium **only after** verified account acceptance, approved exact SKU/link/channel and a checked price/refund/claim matrix. Otherwise direct everyone to the existing free Fieldbook, and evaluate an *authenticated* FV-owned product before further commercial drafting.

**Landing:** The R160 [Mind Threshold preview](./mind-threshold-evidence-zen12-preview-UNPUBLISHED.html) is a model of separation, not a launch URL. Its research CTA and buyer card are distinct; paid control is `disabled`, with plain nearby commission disclosure.

**Hypothesis:** For eligible high-intent visitors, the same exact-product evidence guide plus an optional lightweight 42s original video *might* produce more settled net revenue per 1,000 qualified humans than the guide alone. This could also perform worse because video adds distraction or page weight. The hypothesis is falsifiable and is not a promised lift.

**Design:** Before launch, founder/QA confirm pricing/rights and choose randomization method without introducing new paid tools or a duplicate analytics script. Variant A presents full independent guide, all citations, caveats, active free resource, and authorized affiliate CTA. Variant B is identical except a user-initiated 42s media slot, with poster, original visuals, WebVTT captions, full transcript, no autoplay and reduced data transfer. Prefer native HTML video on one small encoded asset only if independently performance tested. No-JS readers must retain the full guide, accessible transcript, and authorized CTA. Preserve the *same* affiliate disclosure beside CTA on both.

**Primary metric:** realized `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * (settled_paid_orders_and_affiliate_commissions - refunds - chargebacks - merchant_fees - other_attributable_costs) / verified_qualified_human_sessions`. For affiliate programs where FV has no direct order visibility, rely on documented **merchant commission settlements**, never estimated order totals. Deduplicate merchant-reported adjustments and differentiate accrued vs paid. If denominators or settled ledger are missing, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero. Separate the free lead route: confirmed Jetpack subscribers, no invented newsletter revenue.

**Secondary diagnostics:** eligible landing views, `article_to_resource_click`, `affiliate_outbound_click`, provider-confirmed lead, independently verified paid-order rate, settled commission rate, refund-adjusted margin, and return visit where consented. An outbound click is *not* a checkout, order or sale.

**Attribution:** use standard permitted `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content=asset_id` on actual Grok-approved posted links. Never put names, email addresses, student data, session identifiers or other personal information in UTM parameters. Store only aggregated campaign/session measures using existing approved first-party infrastructure, subject to consent and retention review. Never install an extra pixel or write a cookie merely for this experiment. If affiliate-network subIDs are prohibited or not in verified terms, omit them; do not simulate an order key.

**Cohorts and stopping:** inspect 7-day and 30-day verified paid-cohort data with explicit traffic-source comparability, sample size and refund lag; do not call a tiny uplift statistically significant. Prespecify minimum evidence for a continuation decision with founder/analytics owner and review negative mobile/accessibility outcomes as guardrails. No deceptive urgency, scarcity, testimonials, or health/wealth promises.

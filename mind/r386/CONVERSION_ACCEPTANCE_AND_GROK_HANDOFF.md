## The conversion hypothesis (two treatments only)

Audience: an organic/social/search visitor curious whether a short meditation-audio library is worth paying for, especially after reading an evidence-based attention or suggestion investigation. **User question:** "What am I buying and what has actually been shown?"

A: exact same independent buyer guide with seller-identified offer, free demonstration, objections, verification caveats and disabled offer CTA.

B: A **plus** original 42-second optional media treatment (SVG storyboard, WebVTT, transcript). Media has NOT been encoded or hosted; this is not a runnable A/B test. No autoplay, external embed, JavaScript requirement, new analytics, email form or commerce link in either variant.

Pre-register null hypothesis: B does not increase **settled net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human landing sessions** versus A. Important guardrails: confirmed merchant paid conversion, legitimate earnings/refunds, disclosures comprehension, mobile WCAG, no performance degradation, and explicit no-tracking choice. Randomize persistent human-exposure assignment only after privacy/legal review; never infer a win from sample noise or outbound clicks alone.

## Privacy-safe measurement contract — proposed to Claude; not deployed

- First-party entry event **qualified_human_landing** with validated non-PII `asset_id`, `utm_campaign`, `landing_slug`, `variant`, `cohort_day`, and consent state; dedupe sessions without raw IP, email or cross-site fingerprinting; preserve anonymous/refusal view.
- **relevant_guide_click** and **article_to_focus_click** use only approved first-party actions. A draft's disabled merchant button produces **no affiliate_click**.
- **provider_confirmed_lead** can be counted only with the existing Jetpack provider's confirmation/delivery evidence as permitted; mere CTA or unconfirmed form submission is not a lead.
- **affiliate_click** only after named partner acceptance, issued URL, and privacy review. Clicks are not paid sales.
- **merchant_confirmed_paid_purchase** only from trusted, independently reported paid order/affiliate commission with stable non-PII attribution key. Record gross, tax where applicable, fees, refunds, chargebacks and settlement date at merchant level. Never put PII in UTM or expose individual customer identity in analytics.
- **net_realized_revenue** = settled gross or affiliate commissions actually payable minus relevant refunds, fees and chargebacks, using consistent business accounting. Returns are negative adjustments to original cohorts; distinguish accrual from cash settled.
- Main KPI: 1000 × sum(net_realized_revenue) / distinct qualified-human sessions for 7- and 30-day cohorts. When denominator, attribution or merchant statements are missing return **NOT COMPUTABLE**, never zero or an extrapolated CTR.
- Guardrails: disclose cookie/consent policy, honor opt-out, limit UTM to approved non-sensitive characters, dedupe bots and self-tests, no new third-party pixel and no duplicate tracking to existing Claude implementation.

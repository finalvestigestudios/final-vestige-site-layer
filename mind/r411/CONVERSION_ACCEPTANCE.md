## Controlled A/B hypothesis, NOT a measured experiment

- **A:** text-only useful guide. **B:** same primary text plus an optional 42-second original film, with poster, captions, transcript and no autoplay. Presently B is storyboard-only; no A/B traffic or conversions have been observed.
- **Hypothesis:** For genuinely high-intent users coming from a fact-checked Grok clip, optional short original explanatory media *might* improve paid order rate versus a readable text guide, but could also reduce conversions or load speed. Zero uplift is presumed until independently demonstrated.
- **Primary outcome after authorized commerce:** independent merchant-confirmed paid orders divided by qualified unique human sessions in a 30-day cohort; report net revenue per thousand sessions after attributable fees and reversals. **Do not substitute CTA clicks or engagement.**
- **Guardrails:** 7-day cohort as early diagnostic, not proof; tiny samples explicitly labeled. Mobile LCP, CLS, INP, keyboard WCAG 2.2 AA, caption timing, actual transcript, screen-reader order, no-JS functionality, no prefetching merchant pages, and consent/privacy compliance.
- **Allocation:** once approved and meaningful traffic exists, Claude may propose a lightweight, server-side deterministic experiment key with appropriate consent and no user PII/cookie-based cross-site tracking. Do NOT install a second script or attribution SaaS. If sample too small, use sequential usability checks and qualitative failures, without claiming statistical lift.
- **Kill criteria:** inaccurate merchant claim or disclosure; any non-disabled purchase action before authorization; inaccessible video or hidden transcript; material mobile speed regression; unverified order reporting; broken Jetpack opt-in/confirm/unsubscribe.

## Measurement contract for Claude's first-party owner stack

Store only minimal session and first-party campaign evidence permitted by policy. No email addresses, customer full names, IPs, fingerprinting or raw cross-site identifiers in this experiment. Unique `asset_id` and `utm_campaign` only after Praxxis/Grok provides actual immutable IDs and channel/link permission. Events are separate:
1. `qualified_human_session` with coarse channel, verified asset_id, campaign, landing route, experiment variant, date cohort; filter obvious bots and document limitations.
2. `article_visit` and `focus_cta_click` = actions, not subscribers.
3. `provider_confirmed_lead` requires provider-side confirmed unique accepted opt-in; existing Jetpack controls only and permissioned aggregate reconciliation.
4. `affiliate_click` = authorized outward navigation with appropriate adjacent disclosure; does **not** prove purchase.
5. `merchant_confirmed_paid_order` / `reported_affiliate_conversion` only from authenticated provider transaction/report data, not a browser redirect or receipt guess.
6. `refund_reversal`, `fee`, `settled_publisher_commission`, and `realized_net_revenue` after reconciliation and attribution confidence assignment.

`net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_humans = 1000 * (settled attributable gross receipt or commission - attributable refunds and fees) / qualified_human_sessions`. If numerator evidence or denominator is missing, result = **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero. Lead-to-paid and paid conversion rates likewise require actual unique denominator, window and merchant confirmation. Report 7/30-day cohorts plus uncertainty, no invented orders or payouts.

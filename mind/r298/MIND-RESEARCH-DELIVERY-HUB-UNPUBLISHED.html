02 / History of Suggestion

The records before the interpretation

In the late eighteenth century, Puységur and his circle recorded accounts of magnetic somnambulism. A witness description is a source, not a verification of magnetic mechanisms. These historical cases train the same critical habit required of modern testimonials.

Try the no-cost version first

Why a study about meditation may not test a meditation recording

Read the original short-film transcript and timing
  1. 00–06 s. A shopper sees an audio course. The useful question is not how impressive the product sounds, but what it contains.
  2. 06–13 s. A research review studied supervised programs. That does not establish the effect of a commercial download.
  3. 13–21 s. A larger synthesis found mixed patterns: some attention outcomes improved; others did not.
  4. 21–30 s. Delivery matters. Face-to-face training sometimes outperformed self-guided formats in the research.
  5. 30–38 s. Would the course help you start a routine? That is a different question from whether it changes your brain.
  6. 38–42 s. Compare the evidence and try a free routine before buying anything.

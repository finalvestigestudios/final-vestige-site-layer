01 / The specific offer

What the seller says is included

Advertised contents

On its official standard page, the seller describes a twelve-level Premium course, 48 MP3s, four formats (guided meditation, relaxation music, nature sounds and white noise), user guidance and support. These are seller statements, not files we independently tested.

The unresolved claim

The seller markets twelve minutes as comparable to an hour of meditation. The attention studies in this archive did not test Zen12. Nothing reviewed establishes that equivalence or a product-specific medical or cognitive outcome.

02 / The free comparison

Try a seven-day decision log

Choose one modest task or quiet interval. Alternate a free twelve-minute timer with audio you already own or may use lawfully. Record whether you began, completed the time, enjoyed it and wanted to repeat it. 03 / Buyer questions

Four objections worth considering

Why not a timer?

You may prefer supplied guidance and sound choices; the seller advertises more than a timer. We have not tested the benefit of its added structure against free choices.

Does audio improve focus?

That specific outcome remains unverified for this product. Research about an invisible gorilla, meditation generally, or auditory perception cannot be transferred to a named recording.

What will it cost?

The seller's standard and promotional pages present materially different prices. Their final cart totals, taxes, currencies, coupons, and Final Vestige-specific authorized offer have not been independently confirmed. We intentionally publish no price here.

Can I get a refund?

The seller advertises a guarantee. Offer-specific terms, deadlines, process and actual fulfillment are not authenticated. Verify conditions on the exact legitimate merchant checkout before deciding.

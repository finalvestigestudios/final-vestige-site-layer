## Hypotheses and safeguards
H0: no measurable change in qualified guide-to-offsite intent/comprehension from optional video. H1: video may increase comprehension or qualified click intent at acceptable costs, OR may reduce engagement / hurt load time. No outcome assumed. Pre-register metric definitions, date window, allocation and minimum sample BEFORE instrumentation; avoid winner's-curse after optional stopping.

**Primary proposed metric (conditional on authorization):** approved outbound CTA clicks / unique *consented* human guide views within a stated window. Never count actual sales from clicks. A secondary comprehension prompt can ask optional one-item *anonymous aggregate* question like "Which package includes all twelve levels?" but must not leak identity or depend on video. Distinguish intent versus qualified purchases verified only in merchant reports if allowed.

Privacy first: fully disabled `privacy-off.cjs` design, no network, no cookies, no IPs, no raw referrer, no fine timestamps, no URL query strings or visitor IDs. Proposed coarse cohorts only: source organic/tiktok/internal/other, topic zen12/attention/focus, arm A/B/NA, event guide_view/film_open/offer_intent/focus_nav. Suppress categories with k<10. This *prototype is not a deployable analytics system*; aggregation is offline synthetic and cannot establish uniqueness or attribution. Any eventual real tracking needs separate disclosure, consent/legal review, permitted exact events, storage retention and founder release approval. Do not inspect or modify Jetpack subscription data.

**Performance gate:** proposed silent 540×960 12fps 40.0s MP4 is 84,220 bytes offline. Never autoplay or fetch before click; preload none with first-party poster only after hosting and verification. Existing B draft is just static SVG/transcript; no remote asset. Monitor real WordPress LCP/INP/CLS, CLS from poster aspect ratio, performance on low bandwidth, and whether WordPress strips inline style. No Lighthouse scores claimed now.

**A11y gate:** keyboard, focus visibility, native controls, accurate captions tied to actually hosted MP4, full transcript, 44px targets, contrast, no flashing/motion requirement, video skip, reduced-motion CSS, no-JS readable, screen-reader test, 360/390/768/1440/1920. Current static drafts are not yet externally audited.

## Privacy-safe attribution contract, not instrumentation permission

Permitted proposed UTM parameters: `utm_source` from real platform, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<real_grok_asset_id>`. Use only validated public destinations after Grok/QA approvals; TikTok captions may not provide tappable URLs. No invented media IDs, tracking URLs, redirects or event counts.

**Events and semantics:** `campaign_landing` = first-party tagged visit *not necessarily human*; `article_to_focus_click` = internal CTA activation, NOT confirmed signup; `confirmed_lead` = actual Jetpack/consented subscriber confirmation if independently observable; `offer_guide_view` = guide visit; `affiliate_click` = authorized outbound click ONLY, NOT a conversion; `checkout_start` = merchant source event if objectively available; `merchant_confirmed_paid_order` = merchant-reported successful payment; `affiliate_confirmed_sale` = partner-approved converted commission record; `refund_or_reversal` = source-confirmed adjustment; `settled_net_revenue` = actual receipts after refunds, transaction fees, commission reversals and costs (clearly state methodology). Do not duplicate existing analytics tags.

**Data minimization:** Use first-party aggregated events and campaign IDs; do not put email, names, personal/medical details or purchaser IDs in URLs. Avoid cross-site cookies or fingerprinting. Treat bot filters as fallible; classify qualified human sessions only under an agreed reproducible rule, explicitly label unknown traffic. Data handling/consent decision remains with Claude/Site QA. Nothing here requires a new vendor, paid SaaS, pixels or new Jetpack list.

**KPI, 7- and 30-day cohorts:** `realized_net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * sum(settled_net_revenue) / verified_qualified_human_sessions`. If denominator unknown, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not 0. Secondary paid conversion = confirmed paid purchases / qualified humans with attribution confidence separately; lead-to-buyer = matched paid customers / confirmed qualified leads only if consent and data legitimately allow matching. Display refund/reversal window, unique buyers, net realized revenue, small-N warning and unattributable sales. No significance claims from tiny samples.

## The pre-registered A/B hypothesis (not yet executed)

**A: text-only guide.** R166 buyer page remains fully accessible and complete without JS, video, ads or merchant link. **B: same exact text and offer**, plus original optional 42-second short video embedded only when an MP4/WebM master, poster, VTT and transcript all exist and pass media-rights and accessibility QA. No autoplay, no third-party player, `preload=none`, low byte budget, optional pause and no content hidden behind playback. Baseline must be performance-tested first.

**Assignment:** For a future approved test, split at immutable Grok asset/campaign level **or** server-assigned persistent first-party cohorts without fingerprinting and with documented parity, not by hiding essential evidence in a JS-only variant. If topic and campaign differ between arms, do not misattribute the resulting purchase difference to video alone. Do not test until merchant eligible and founder approves both commercial conditions and experiment.

**Primary endpoint:** actual paid completed orders or partner-verified paid commissions per qualified-human session over a defined 7/30-day attribution horizon, after adjustments. **Guardrails:** qualified direct captures, engagement with research, bounce/slow load, disclosure comprehension, mobile keyboard access, refunds, complaint rate. Plan for adequate sample; if insufficient, describe it as exploratory and do not choose a winner. Variant A and B must have identical offer pricing/disclosures and same destination policy.

**Before any activation:** exact earning link + allowed channel from approved account; verify checkout terms; no-JS functionality; browser QA at 360/390/768/1440; screen reader + keyboard; two-device UTM attribution and consent test; QA of no extra forms/pixels; explicit founder approval.

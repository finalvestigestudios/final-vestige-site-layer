The highest-intent, contextually credible prospective buyer is an adult who already wants a **structured library of meditation audio** and is deciding whether it is worth paying for rather than using a free timer, guided recording, or Final Vestige's [Seven Day Focus Fieldbook](https://finalvestige.com/focus/). This is a product-format need, **not** a clinically established concentration or therapeutic need. The chosen pilot remains **Zen12**, the single canonical R284 A/B experiment; this R304 addition is an *unpublished review specimen and evidence gate*, not another public destination.

**No commercial activation.** The [Inspire3 catalog](https://inspire3.com/) and public affiliate recruitment cannot prove that *Final Vestige* is enrolled. Targeted connected Gmail searches found marketing and unrelated product 'access approved' pitches, but no account-specific publisher agreement, permitted web/video/email channels, issued FV earning link, tracking/commission schedule, or refunds settlement evidence. The exact user-affiliate account dashboard and authorized partner documents were not accessible. No owned paid Mind SKU with verified rights, live checkout, delivery and return terms has been independently authenticated either. Commerce is therefore HARD HOLD; free-first is not a substitute for paid-conversion proof, but it is the only defensible immediately usable offer route.

## Honest A/B hypothesis: text only vs identical guide plus optional video
**Hypothesis, NOT conversion fact:** a relevant, optional source-led explainer could reduce unsupported-benefit uncertainty and help some readers decide whether the catalog is appropriate. It may lower conversion because it encourages free alternatives. That outcome would still serve the editorial standard.
- A: same original buyer guide, same disclosure, offer, price/terms confirmation state, placement and CTA, *no film module*.
- B: same guide with original ~42-second captioned video module with poster, transcript, controls, preload="none"; preserve useful static transcript if media cannot load and a no-JS reading path.
- Only after authorization and release approval: randomized stable assignment at first-party origin without personal profiling; session-level minimal variant identifier (no identity, IP, email, fingerprint or cross-site third-party cookie); exclude self/test sessions and obviously non-human visits.
- Primary business outcome: independently confirmed settled merchant-paid affiliate commission **per 1,000 qualified human sessions**. Secondary: paid conversion rate, average cleared commission per purchase, refunds/chargebacks, direct lead-to-paid conversion where privacy and provider records permit. Diagnostics only: percent seeing the video, CTA click, outbound click, scroll, aggregate load/performance. Do not label outbound click 'conversion' without qualification.
- Never add a second analytics script, hidden tracking pixel, cloaking redirect, or first-party endpoint without Claude engineering's authorized review. Use existing campaign capture if native functions already support it.
- Prelaunch validity: variant parity and identical purchase conditions, clear on-screen affiliate disclosure, no deceptive suppression of adverse evidence, stable assignment, no autoplay, captions and transcript, responsive 360/390/768/1440/1920, no-JS keyboard/contrast/reduced-motion check, staged load-cost test. Run one true browser/WordPress sanitizer test before greenlighting. None have yet been run.
- Tiny-sample protocol: show all denominators and confidence warnings. No claimed uplift until enough settled transactions and owner-approved statistical plan. Stop if broken checkout, incorrectly attributed leads, invalid consent, misleading seller terms, or disclosure/keyboard failure.

## Exact measurement and accounting contract
Allowed campaign dimensions proposed for owner verification: `utm_source=tiktok|youtube|instagram`; `utm_medium=organic_social`; `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11` **(proposed, not yet canonical; resolve earlier `mind_proposed_2026_11` inconsistency with Claude)**; `utm_content=<verified_media_asset_id>`. If a platform permits only bio links, document the actual click path; do not assert a deep link was clickable.

Use distinct conceptual events, not invented live tracking:
1. `qualified_landing` = human-filtered first-party session on intended source page, with campaign and actual verified asset_id where available.
2. `article_to_fieldbook_click` = on-site navigation, **not** an email subscriber.
3. `confirmed_lead` = provider-confirmed Jetpack subscription (only if tied to campaign lawfully and demonstrably).
4. `offer_interest_cta` = relevant on-site CTA engagement; **NOT** checkout.
5. `affiliate_outbound` = actual authorized departure to merchant; not a sale.
6. `merchant_confirmed_purchase` = independent partner order/report with timestamp/cohort and deduped transaction.
7. `cleared_affiliate_commission` = actually settled attributable FV commission after merchant adjustments/returns. Gross cart value belongs to the merchant, not FV. Do not double-subtract fees already netted in the cleared commission.

Reconcile 7- and 30-day cohorts. Compute `realized_net_per_1000 = 1000 * sum(cleared FV commission not already net-reduced again) / qualified-human sessions` only where both numerator and denominator exist and attribution limits are clear. Report `NOT COMPUTABLE`, **not zero**, when either is unknown. If leads cannot be attributed to media, report aggregate separately and mark lead-to-customer as unattributable. Keep PII out of research docs; store no emails, IPs, transaction owner names or raw merchant tokens.

## Privacy-safe measurement contract (specification ONLY; no new script)

**Journey:** `qualified_human_session` keyed by first-party campaign metadata → `article_view` → `relevant_cta_click` (free fieldbook OR held product) → `confirmed_consent_lead` where directly observable → `merchant_confirmed_paid_order` or `merchant_confirmed_affiliate_sale` independently reported → net settlement minus refunds/fees. A click is NOT a confirmed lead; a lead is NOT a paid order; an affiliate outbound event is NOT a commission.

**Allowed campaign dimensions:** `utm_source` from fixed allowlist {youtube,tiktok,instagram,facebook,other}; `utm_medium=organic_social`; `utm_campaign=mind_perception_pilot_2026_11`; `utm_content=actual_asset_id` only if authenticated; `article_slug`, `offer_id`, `variant` {A,B}, `event_type`, `event_date`, `eligibility_flag` and aggregate device class. **Do not store** full IP, email, free-text search, cookies/identifiers without consent, query strings containing PII, fingerprint, raw user agent or social account ID. Prefer existing native analytics and aggregated/suppressed data; do not install duplicate trackers.

**Funnel evidence object (proposed aggregation):**
```json
{"cohort_start":"YYYY-MM-DD","cohort_days":7,"utm_campaign":"mind_perception_pilot_2026_11","asset_id":"AUTHENTICATED_ONLY","variant":"A","qualified_human_sessions":null,"relevant_cta_clicks":null,"confirmed_leads":null,"verified_orders":null,"verified_commissions":null,"gross_revenue":null,"refunds":null,"fees":null,"realized_net_revenue":null,"attribution_confidence":"unverified","source_receipts":[]}
```
All nulls are intentional unknowns, not synthetic zeros. Only aggregate with privacy rules and documented consent.

**Primary KPI:** `(merchant_verified_settled_gross - actual_refunds - actual_fees) / qualified_human_sessions * 1000` if AND ONLY IF numerator and denominator independently established, matched to campaign cohort and net does not double-count affiliate orders. Otherwise **NOT COMPUTABLE**. Companion: qualified-visitor paid order conversion, verified AOV, refund rate, lead→paid customer, 7-day and 30-day cohorts with low-count caution and interval, missing-attribution flags.

## A/B pre-registration and acceptance

- **Hypothesis:** Optional silent 42-second original film *may* help a visitor assess evidence without reducing paid purchase completion or page performance. It might harm it. No uplift predicted or guaranteed.
- **A:** complete independent buyer guide, exact same purchasing text as B, native-disabled commerce until approval.
- **B:** same copy plus an optional original film with controls and poster, captions and complete visible transcript, preload none, no forced play; existing stage is **static storyboard**, film rendering/hosting and browser QA remain open.
- **Assignment:** random visitor only when qualified traffic and privacy-compliant platform exists; stable first-party allocation without identity fingerprinting; no new paid SaaS.
- **Primary outcome:** independently verified net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions and paid-order conversion, not video plays, outbound clicks or signups.
- **Guardrails:** checkout exits, poor intent match, deceptive claims, missing disclosure, > agreed Core Web Vitals budget, accessibility regression, stale refund/sale terms.
- **Minimum decision rule:** do NOT call a winner on tiny samples; pre-register sample size and confidence interval with commerce/site QA; report inconclusive if no reliable settlements or attribution.
- **Security:** no merchant URL, no JS tracking, no CSS-disabled-only checkout, no automatic offsite exit, no unsanitized query-string interpolation, no new email form.

# Mind R155 — Exact-offer decision guide: Hypnosis Live Total Focus
*Evidence-reviewed consumer guide; unpublished and commercially inactive; 2026-11-05*

## One offer, one audience, one accountable decision

The highest-intent audience is a person **already looking for a paid, downloadable guided focus-hypnosis audio**. That is not the same person as someone drawn to a Thatcher illusion or invisible-gorilla video. Our offer-fit decision is to keep the existing R149 Total Focus independent buyer guide as the candidate commercial page, and to avoid inserting a paid recommendation into basic face-perception research.

**Exact merchant item:** [Hypnosis Live: Total Focus](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus), under Mental Skills. Seller lists one recording, 33 minutes, 47 MB, written and recorded by J. A. Amos. On the retrieved November 5 seller page it displays **$24.44** near the single-item offer and **$29.95** crossed out, plus a distinct two-recording bundle. These are on-page merchant displays, **not a checkout-verified final price**, a negotiated discount, an FV price promise or a claim the affiliate can sell the bundle. The refund pitch advertises 14 days; independent eligibility or fulfillment is untested. A prospective purchaser must read the current final checkout and original refund policy before deciding.

The seller states that the audio can deliver extraordinary concentration, faster work completion and mental “reprogramming.” Those statements are **not evidenced here**. Studies of face perception, hypnosis as a broad category and meditation generally do not establish efficacy for this exact file. No first-hand use, independently randomized trial of Total Focus, professional endorsement, guaranteed result or individual benefit is claimed.

**What the buyer actually gets according to seller:** an immediately downloadable audio session, with a preview advertised on the product page. **What we cannot verify:** final taxes and checkout price, audible recording content, accessibility of the actual file, the seller's real refund response, payment security, partner-specific attribution/acceptance and whether this exact title is approved for each promotion channel.

## Four honest objections

**“Can a video illusion tell me I need a focus recording?”** No. Thatcher-illusion studies investigate orientation and facial processing; making this an instant paid-product recommendation would manufacture a connection the science does not support. The reader receives a source-backed explanation, a rights-safe exercise and, if interested, the optional existing Focus Fieldbook. Do not divert this low-intent person straight to a merchant.

**“What if I want an audio structure but not a health or productivity promise?”** That is the appropriate independent product-guide use case. The buyer may prefer guidance and convenience, but no improvement magnitude should be stated as a fact. A short, free distraction-return practice and the Focus Fieldbook are alternatives; they are not proven equivalent substitutes.

**“Can I rely on the listed price or ‘guarantee’?”** Not from our draft. The product page presents a displayed amount and 14-day satisfaction statement as seller policy. Check final charge, exchange rate, taxes, bundle selection and terms at checkout. The refund statement is not independently audited. Any future FV copy should dynamically remove stale exact prices rather than silently retaining old promotional numbers.

**“Does a commission make your research biased?”** A possible incentive exists, which is why investigation conclusions cannot be edited to justify a product. Any future live paid link must have nearby plain language: “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” A general site disclosure alone does not replace this. No earning link exists in this pilot.

## Commercial proof gate

Public Inspire3 and its [affiliate portal](https://affiliates.inspire3.com/) establish that a program exists, not Final Vestige account enrollment. The public portal markets commission percentages and earnings-per-click estimates; these are **vendor advertisements**, not our payout terms or realized revenue. We are **not** publishing the amounts or relying on them in forecasts. Written named acceptance, exact product-eligible authorized URL, organic social/email permissions and reporting/settlement rights remain UNKNOWN. **HARD HOLD.**

Fallback: choose an FV-owned paid Mind product **only** upon authenticating an existing Payhip SKU, pricing, original IP/third-party rights, live fulfillment, refund policy and order evidence. Product 001–015 inventory and sale readiness remain unverified here; do not fabricate a SKU.

**Buyer flow proposal:** real Grok video with immutable asset_id (UNKNOWN) → intent-matched evidence article → optional existing Focus Fieldbook → an independently requested exact-item buyer guide → disabled/absent commercial CTA until acceptance. Lead signup confirmation does not equal checkout; merchant click does not equal sale; sale does not equal settled commission.

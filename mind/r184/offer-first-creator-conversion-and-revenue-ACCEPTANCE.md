## Executive conversion decision
**No eligible, independently verified, commercially activatable Mind offer was found by this run.** Public Inspire3 affiliate portal confirms a program exists, not Final Vestige acceptance. Targeted Gmail searches found consumer promotional emails but no named approval. No authenticated Payhip inventory, rights manifest, deliverability test, settled sale or full checkout inspection was available. A *hypothesis* that Zen12 will convert is not a revenue result. A pleasant landing page is not a commercial success. In particular, do **not** send a buyer to a checkout from this prototype.

The most valuable work is to resolve **one real offer** and run a trustworthy trial before making another speculative page. The isolated R184 A/B variants are conservative demonstrations for a conditional future test; they are not a request for launch.

**Candidate highest-intent audience:** already motivated adults seeking a short, structured meditation/audio routine, not readers who merely enjoyed a perception illusion. Paid intent must be inferred from source-context and confirmed behavior, not follower counts. The low-pressure bridge is practical scheduling and format choice.

**Commercial sequence:** verify named partner approval and its allowed traffic sources, earning link and tracking; separately check checkout total and seller/refund obligations; independent claim review; get founder release signoff; run mobile, keyboard and performance QA; only then ask buyers to consider a specific offer. If these remain unavailable, review an *authenticated*, rights-cleared Payhip Mind SKU first. Neither route was verified today.

## Real paid CTA specification
This demo has a native disabled purchase control with adjacent conditional commission disclosure:
> If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.
> No earning link is active here.

Do not enable it until authorization and release are independently documented. For a verified earning CTA in a later approved release, the actual purchase link must use https, exact program-provided URL, rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer", target preference validated, accessible descriptive anchor text, disclosure in the same visually adjacent box, and no open redirect or masking. The direct merchant page in the current guide is an informational **seller-source citation**, not a tracked commission CTA. A future confirmed offer link is a different event.

## Privacy-respecting, first-party attribution contract — reuse current analytics
Do not add scripts to WordPress, use fingerprinting, cookie-syncing, PII, new SaaS, extra tags or subscriber stacks. Use existing Jetpack/first-party aggregate observability *where it demonstrably supports these metrics*; where data is unavailable, record null rather than guessing.

Proposed UTMs (Grok must agree exact published IDs): `utm_source=tiktok|youtube|instagram`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<immutable_grok_asset_id>`. Reserve utm_content for true creator-media identity, not invented content captions. No matching asset IDs are presently verified. Avoid copying visitor parameters into outgoing affiliate URLs absent merchant-authorized sub-ID terms; do not leak email addresses, IPs, names, or full search queries.

Events in order, evidence classified independently:
1. `campaign_landing`: aggregate eligible human landing session from supported existing analytics, with source, campaign and content only if reliably preserved.
2. `article_to_focus_click`: navigation to the already existing /focus/ page; not a signup.
3. `confirmed_signup`: native Jetpack subscribed/confirmed record and appropriate consent; unknown if not observable, and no test address enrolled without approval.
4. `affiliate_outbound`: activated approved earning link click if allowed and measurable; not a sale.
5. `merchant_confirmed_purchase`: order ID or partner-side conversion confirmation in authenticated merchant data, deduplicated, not personally displayed.
6. `settled_revenue`: net funds received, reconciled with fees, refunds and chargebacks, recorded without double-subtraction.

Where native tools cannot match a visitor session to a reported order, identify **unattributed** paid revenue separately, not a guessed UTM conversion. Never claim payer identity from WordPress sessions.

**Primary KPI** for 7/30 day cohorts: `1000 × realized net revenue / qualified human sessions`. If either numerator reconciliation or denominator reliability is missing, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero. Auxiliary: verified paid conversions / qualified humans, confirmed orders, unique customers where supported by authoritative records, settlement gross/net, refund rate, lead-to-customer where consentful match exists, and effective earnings from relevant commercially authorized cohorts. Always warn of tiny sample sizes and time lag for merchant approvals, returns and settlement.

## Conditional experiment (no user traffic today)
**A:** source-based text guide, 0 JS, disabled commercial button until verified.
**B:** same guide with exactly one optional *42-second original storyboard/poster/transcript* element, never autoplay or forced viewing. No playable video is included; if eventually produced, use poster, captions, no preload before request, no iframe, transcript, keyboard controls, reduced motion.

**Hypothesis (not fact):** optional short explanatory video might improve informed purchase decisions for already high-intent readers without raising buyer regret or harming mobile performance. It may also reduce conversion; that is an acceptable honest outcome.

**Unit of assignment:** persistent non-PII variant allocation with existing consentful analytics if already supported; if not, use a server-rendered ethical randomization that requires no tracking beyond the experiment and passes no-JS. Assignment method requires engineering approval. Do not implement custom tracking in live WordPress as part of this PR.

**Guardrails:** equal disclosure, verified live CTA placement, same offer, same price explanation, no hidden content or artificial scarcity. Inspect views 360, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920; keyboard, contrast, landmarks, reduced motion, consent, external links, no-JS, no autoplay, 42-second captions/transcript, Lighthouse/CWV and no increase in LCP from a blocking video request. Predeclare primary **settled net revenue / qualified human sessions**, not clicks. Observe confirmed paid conversions and refunds over 7/30 days. Use a sequentially cautious, precommitted stopping rule; do not declare a winner from a handful of purchases. If products are not authorized, no A/B traffic is permissible.

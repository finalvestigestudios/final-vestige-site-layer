**Variant A:** [R210 source-controlled text-only guide](../r210/zen12-mindless-reading-A-TEXT-UNPUBLISHED.html). **Variant B:** [R210 equivalent guide with optional 42-second poster/storyboard/transcript/VTT](../r210/zen12-mindless-reading-B-OPTIONAL-42S-UNPUBLISHED.html). **No-JS/keyboard/media:** A must work entirely without scripts. B must show the same article, a static poster and on-page transcript with captions file accessible whether film cannot play or not. Optional video requires deliberate user activation, no preload/autoplay/embed analytics, captions sync, reduced motion, 44px interactive targets and contrast.

THE BUYER QUESTION

Can a recorded voice help you focus?

Hypnosis Live sells a specific 33-minute audio track called Total Focus. Before paying for it, distinguish what is in the download from what has actually been demonstrated.

WHAT IS NOT VERIFIED

Product-specific focus improvement

Claims about razor-sharp concentration, brain “reprogramming,” getting more done and career success remain seller claims. No independently reviewed randomized controlled study of this exact audio and these productivity outcomes was located for this guide.

It would be wrong to translate a study about clinical pain into a guarantee about a workday.

What did a stronger comparison find?

In September 2026, a systematic review of clinical hypnosis examined outcomes against both inactive controls and credible active alternatives. In a pooled comparison against inactive controls it found a modest pain reduction (standardized mean difference −0.33). Fourteen studies involving 940 people compared hypnosis with relaxation; the difference was small and statistically non-significant (−0.13; 95% CI −0.42 to 0.16). The review did not test focus audio or job performance.

Evidence rule: a general clinical result is not proof that a particular commercial focus MP3 works, especially when the outcome and delivery differ.

Primary source: Yim & Derbyshire (2026), DOI 10.1002/ejp.70369. Read the open scientific review.

A prior 2024 meta-analysis of adjunctive hypnosis asked a different question—whether adding hypnosis to other care changed pain outcomes. Those results also do not establish effectiveness of this recording. Read the 2024 review.

A useful free comparison before buying

When your challenge is starting a postponed task, a three-minute setup can often tell you more about the obstacle than marketing copy. Write down one small task, set a timer for twelve minutes and record whether you began, what interrupted you and what you actually completed. Repeat on a different day under ordinary conditions. The outcome is descriptive, not a test of the effectiveness of a sound recording.

Explore the free Seven Day Focus Fieldbook for a longer task-starting exercise. The existing Fieldbook uses the same approved Jetpack/Dispatch path. No second signup is added here.

Prefer an audio ritual? A free timer or unpromoted ambient sound is a legitimate alternative. An individual preference may justify a purchase, but it does not demonstrate superior productivity.

A buyer's decision, not a promised transformation

Potentially useful if

You actively want one prerecorded narrator-led relaxation or suggestion session, understand its format, do not require it to outperform free alternatives, and accept the merchant's currently applicable terms.

Not a fit if

You need treatment for a medical condition, proof of workplace-performance gains, a multilevel meditation curriculum, or assurances of guaranteed results. Do not replace appropriate professional care with a commercial recording.

H0: Optional film (B) produces no improvement in **realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** relative to useful text-only guide (A). H1: it improves that measure without reducing trust, accessibility, source engagement, mobile speed or informed consent. The variants retain identical substantive buyer copy. Do not autoplay, force video, add external video embed, require JS, or treat the storyboard as produced media. ## Privacy-safe measurement contract (not installed)

A real already-published Grok asset uses `utm_source=tiktok|youtube|...`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_topic_2026_11`, `utm_content=<ACTUAL_PUBLISHED_ASSET_ID>` only when permitted. Actual immutable IDs, never a guessed token. Avoid PII, referrer URL bodies, email identifiers, fingerprinting and duplicate analytics scripts. Use existing consent architecture and low-cardinality campaign strings.

1. `qualified_landing`: deduplicated qualified human session with known attribution coverage.
2. `article_to_focus_click`: navigation intent, not a subscriber.
3. `jetpack_provider_confirmed_lead`: provider-confirmed genuine opt-in, not a form view/submission.
4. `affiliate_outbound`: eligible actual approved earning link click **only when active**, not sale.
5. `merchant_verified_paid_order`: separately independently merchant-confirmed transaction.
6. `settled_commission`, `merchant_refund`, `merchant_fee`: financially verifiable events, corrections and cohorts.

Metric for any 7- or 30-day cohort = 1,000 × (verified realized sales/commissions − refunded amounts − fees/adjustments) / qualified-human sessions, with revenue and denominator matched by attribution and currency. Without either measure: **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero. Report paid rate, order count, lead-to-customer conversion and unmatched attribution separately.

A useful scientific story, not a product promise

Write the word BLUE in red ink and name the ink. The word may compete for your response. Stroop measured a pronounced asymmetry between naming ink and reading words in 1935. That tells us something about competing responses. It does not establish that any particular audio course improves attention.

Inspect the original Stroop research record and Final Vestige's published change-blindness investigation before reading the offer.

Try twelve minutes before buying anything

Spend two minutes observing where your attention goes, five minutes attending to a neutral sound or ordinary breathing, then five minutes recording when your mind wanders. Do not grade yourself or interpret a feeling as a therapeutic result. If it helps you decide whether you even like setting aside time, you can follow the free Seven Day Focus Fieldbook. Science is not a shortcut guarantee

A 2024 meta-analysis of 111 randomized studies examined mindfulness interventions and found certain cognitive benefits, but not improvements on all speed-related indices. It did not study the Zen12 files, validate "one hour in twelve minutes," or prove instant focus.

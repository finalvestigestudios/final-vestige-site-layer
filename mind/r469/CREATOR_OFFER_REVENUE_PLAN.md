## Source-grounded commercial fit

The single most plausible high-intent reader asks: "Should I buy Zen12 for a short guided meditation session when I could follow a free 12-minute routine?" Match to seller's **actual** product: the standard Zen12 About description offers 12 levels, 12-minute downloadable sessions, and four listening styles. The separate secret promotion advertises an extras bundle and dynamic price/coupon context: never combine its basket, urgency, testimonial or refund copy with the standard catalog as though the terms were one authorized SKU. Current checkout, refund eligibility and payout are unverified. Original copy emphasizes convenience rather than undocumented mental, health or wealth results.

The evidence bridge: Raymond, Shapiro and Arnell 1992 observed a specific RSVP target/probe deficit; Chun and Potter 1995 modelled it; Olivers and colleagues 2007 showed sequence exceptions. Slagter and colleagues 2007 studied intensive meditation training and brain-response measures; van Vugt and Slagter 2014 studied very experienced meditators. These did NOT test Zen12. Ingendoh and colleagues 2023 reviewed 14 binaural-beat EEG papers with mixed/inconsistent results. Converting these results into proof of a seller's "hour in twelve minutes" or "brainwave automatic" claim is inappropriate.

01 / Experiments

Things that happened in plain sight

Inattentional blindness, the Thatcher illusion, the McGurk effect and change blindness are not tricks that prove the brain is broken. They are careful experiments in which task, expectation, movement and measurement matter.

The editorial principle

The record is not the conclusion.

One experiment can tell us that some people missed an event while completing a different task. It cannot prove that every witness in history is mistaken. One report can tell us that someone described a cure. It cannot prove which mechanism caused a recovery. We preserve those distinctions in every story and every offer.

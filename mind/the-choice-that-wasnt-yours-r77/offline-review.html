The Other Photograph

Original 40-second silent explanation of choice blindness, not original laboratory footage and not a Zen12 endorsement. This file is not published on WordPress.

User-initiated playback only. The complete six-cue transcript below is available even when the video does not play or JavaScript is disabled.

Transcript

  1. 0–6 seconds: Sometimes the evidence changes. And the reason still sounds certain.
  2. 6–13 seconds: A participant chooses a portrait. The choice is recorded.
  3. 13–20 seconds: The experimenter reveals the other portrait instead.
  4. 20–27 seconds: Some people miss the mismatch. They explain what they were handed.
  5. 27–34 seconds: A convincing account does not verify the original decision.
  6. 34–40 seconds: Return to what was chosen. Johansson and colleagues, 2005.
Editorial note. Schematic illustrations. No footage or audio from the 2005 experiments is included. Some failures to detect substitutions were observed under particular lab conditions; no universal susceptibility is implied.

Source: Johansson et al. (2005), Science, 310, 116–119. No affiliate relationship or tracking exists in this offline player.

The public https://affiliates.inspire3.com/ portal advertises a program and compensation percentages, but no named Final Vestige contract or exact product-link rights have been authenticated; **no payout percentage belongs in a user-facing sales guide.**

**Primary promise we can substantiate only as a format description:** “Compare the contents of a short audio-led meditation routine and decide whether it suits your schedule.” This is not “get 60 minutes of benefit in twelve.” Price and guarantee must remain unadvertised until authenticated checkout details and current merchant terms are reconciled. ## One high-intent landing-page experience

**Proposed headline:** “Before you pay for a twelve-minute meditation recording, compare what is actually included.”  
**Empathetic hook:** A timed session can be easier to fit around an ordinary day. The practical question is whether a guided audio *format* removes a friction point for this reader—not whether sound waves can reproduce an hour of science-certified meditation.

**Micro-story grounded in research:** Peter Thompson's 1980 upside-down portrait reveals how orientation changes judgments of facial arrangement. Later experimental work refined the effect by testing locally inverted features and rotation. The proper lesson is that apparent experience alone does not identify its mechanism. Likewise, a pleasant listening session cannot demonstrate the advertised biological mechanism or prove brand-specific health effects.

**One useful free demonstration:** Set a 12-minute timer, sit comfortably or perform a chosen nonmedical focusing task, and record at the end whether the format felt usable. On another day try an openly licensed free guided recording if desired. Keep expectations simple and avoid treating a one-person comparison as a randomized trial. This is an instrument for personal routine preference, not an experiment proving benefits.

**Offer description:** The seller lists 12 levels, four formats per level, and an included quick-start guide. Explicitly identify that these are current seller-stated package contents, which a buyer must confirm at checkout. Show an honest comparison: free silent timer (no product purchase); openly available audio; Zen12 vendor offering (charge, support and guarantee need exact checkout verification). A buyer who prefers free, offline or no audio should be encouraged to choose that.

**Objections handled without pressure:** If the buyer doubts brainwave claims, acknowledge that the branded claims are not independently established. If the buyer wants a measurable improvement, say that product-specific research is missing, not that a routine guarantees it. If the buyer cannot confirm refund terms, advise delaying purchase. If the buyer has a medical or mental-health question, clarify that this is not medical treatment or a replacement for professional care.

**Prospective adjacent disclosure beside the commercial button:** “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” Until publisher approval, keep a genuine HTML <button disabled> and an explanatory hold state. No open merchant link cloaking, countdown urgency, unverifiable discount or fake testimonial. An informational direct link to the seller's public page for claim verification is not an earning link and must be labeled that way.

Reuse existing R188 A and B, do not proliferate equivalent pages. A is text-only; B is identical text plus an original optional 42-second poster/transcript concept. The hypothetical film moves darkness → a face diagram changes global orientation → a cited research card → one free twelve-minute comparison → seller-details caution. No autoplay, no JS dependency, no persistent media cookies or third-party player embed. Provide a complete visible transcript in both no-video and no-JS cases, keyboard access, descriptive labels, captions and reduced-motion accommodations. Targets are hypotheses and engineering budgets, not passed evidence.

**Hypothesis:** Optional original video may help high-intent visitors understand what is—and isn't—being purchased, increasing **independently confirmed net paid orders per 1,000 qualified human sessions**, without reducing trust or usable mobile performance. Do not assume variant B wins; an extra media step might distract or slow the purchase decision. Balanced assignment only after permissions and page release; no statistical significance from tiny samples. Record sample sizes and data confidence for each 7/30-day cohort, not screenshots of browser counters.

Hold constant the offer, text, disclosures, page title, navigation, CTA position and source content. Vary **only** the optional video module. Establish accessibility and quality parity first. Future measurement needs consent and existing first-party analytics; do not deploy another script merely to run the trial. All commerce events are separately reconciled to actual external merchant records.

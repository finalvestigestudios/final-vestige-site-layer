Evidence before experience · editorial concept / not live

The unreliable witness is your own mind.

An eye misses a changing object. A question changes a remembered detail. A voice offers a new way to pay attention. These are not interchangeable mysteries. Each has a different experiment and a different standard of proof.

Start with what was actually tested. Follow the surviving documents. Keep the conclusions narrower than the promises.

Micro-experiment · no scripts or optical tricks

What does the observer know?

Look at a familiar room for ten seconds, then look away and write down how many small objects you could confidently place on a table. When you check, distinguish what you saw from what you inferred. This is an informal observation—not a reproduction of any scientific protocol or a diagnostic test.

In proper experiments, researchers control exposures, timing and comparisons. This little exercise should make you curious about the method rather than persuade you that your memory is defective.

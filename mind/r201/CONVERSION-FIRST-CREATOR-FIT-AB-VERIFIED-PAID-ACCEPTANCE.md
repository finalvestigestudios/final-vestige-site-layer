## Executive decision: reduce the number of unknowns before adding traffic

The potential product is **Zen12 Premium**, not generic "brain reprogramming." The target buyer is a reader who already wants a convenient structured meditation-audio library and is comparing it against learning a practice or using a free routine. The exact seller page is https://zen12.com/hnp ; seller-described inventory comprises 12 levels, 48 MP3s, four listening styles, supporting guides, an advertised one-year refund promise and a displayed $87.99 Premium promotional figure at November 7 review. None of that establishes independently validated psychological benefit or final checkout terms.

**Commercial decision: HARD HOLD.** The publicly accessible Inspire3 affiliate recruitment portal is not named Final Vestige acceptance, permitted placement and email/social-channel authorization, an approved tracking URL or verifiable account-specific terms. A Payhip-owned Mind product is a possible near-term alternative only after authenticated SKU ownership, rights, live ability to buy, specific price, delivery and refund terms are confirmed. An unrelated publicly indexed Payhip workbook cannot be assumed to belong to Final Vestige. If both are unverified, the ethical conversion pilot is designed but not activated. The free Focus route can remain visible independently.

**Desired outcome:** a confirmed paid purchase from an identified offer, with independently verifiable order/commission and net revenue. A landing click, timer started, PDF revealed or merchant outbound event is **not a sale**.

## Proposed presell page, not a false clinical shortcut

Opening hook: **“A seller promises an hour of meditation in twelve minutes. The crucial question is whether researchers tested that audio—or something different.”** This respects the exact Zen12 sales-page proposition without adopting its effect claim.

The offer guide follows the reader's actual objections: what is included; how Premium differs from one-file Total Focus; what is a seller inventory statement versus independent scientific evidence; what the displayed historical price does and does not prove; what is known about advertised returns; and which free alternative is genuinely usable. The independent demonstration is one twelve-minute work-start log. No brain-state diagnosis, medical promise, hypnosis recovered-memory exercise or invented personal testimonial.

**Variant A:** rich, accessible text-only source guide with an obvious free Fieldbook CTA and a permanently natively disabled conditional seller CTA. **Variant B:** same content and commercial controls plus an original optional **42-second** storyboard with static accessible poster, written 0–42s captions/transcript, no autoplay, external embeds, unsupported media file or additional tracking. Final rendered WebVTT/MP4 and actual browser checks are future release gates, not represented as complete. Both are source-controlled under R201, linked by PR, not WordPress entries.

**The only immediately actionable CTA in either draft is the same existing free Fieldbook link.** It never implies that submitting an email is necessary to read the research or buy a product, and the existing Dispatch welcome is never overwritten. The commercial control reads “Review the seller's purchase options — unavailable pending authorization”, is a disabled button rather than a misleading link, and has an adjacent plain-English prospective disclosure.

## A/B causal hypothesis and acceptance

**Null:** Adding an optional original video does not change net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions. **Alternative:** It may improve product understanding, purchase readiness or retained trust; it may also cause distraction, accessibility burden or slower page loads. Do not assert conversion lift without a sufficiently sized randomized test and verified settlement data.

After founder approval and all merchant gates: one consistent paid offer, one verified affiliate earning URL or authenticated owned product, identical disclosures/CTAs/price evidence in A and B, fixed source mix, deterministic session-level assignment without persistent identification, no attribution to bots or internal QA, analytics only if existing consent/first-party design permits. A no-JS state must retain all purchasing information and the off-site CTA; the B film is entirely optional and never needed to understand the offer. Stratify diagnostics by real asset_id and device only if existing privacy consent and sufficient sample allow it.

Before release, confirm mobile 360/390/768px, desktop 1440/1920px, WCAG keyboard focus and contrast, native semantics, reduced motion, poster alt/accessible SVG labels, WebVTT caption quality and transcript accuracy, CSP/no injection, no client-side redirects, no duplicate tracking scripts, and Lighthouse transfer sizes. The current checks are static-source only, not these browser tests.

A visitor receiving a different variant must not see a changed offer, a hidden charge, altered refund wording, moved disclosure or a differently persuasive headline. Otherwise the causal hypothesis would be uninterpretable.

## Measurement contract — 7-day and 30-day cohorts, no vanity substitution

Use existing first-party tooling after Claude and Site QA inventory it. Suggested inbound URL: published landing URL with **utm_source=actual_platform**, **utm_medium=organic_social**, **utm_campaign=mind_audio_evidence_2026_11**, **utm_content=real_immutable_asset_id**. Avoid inventing the asset ID or installing a new tracking SDK. Grok/Praxxis's TASK-008 owns the media ID and approved post URL. Keep campaign tags opaque, not PII, and document native analytics limitations.

Events, as separate states: `qualified_campaign_landing` only where real-user quality can be reasonably established; `article_to_focus_click` is a click, not enrollment; `confirmed_signup` only with authoritative Jetpack server confirmation (never because a download is displayed by `?subscribe=` or `is-subscriber`); `affiliate_outbound` is a link interaction only after permission; `merchant_confirmed_order` and `merchant_confirmed_affiliate_conversion` require independently reconciled merchant reporting; `refund` and `fee` require realized settlement records. No cookies, fingerprinting, email addresses or customer names in campaign event payloads. Record attribution as unknown if checkout cross-domain mapping is unavailable.

**Primary KPI:** (gross confirmed receipts or affiliate commissions actually received − refunds − platform fees − directly attributable paid acquisition expenses) / eligible qualified-human landing sessions × 1,000, for a **specified 7- or 30-day cohort**. Record pending payouts separately from cash settled. Paid conversion rate = confirmed attributed orders / eligible qualified-human sessions. Lead-to-customer conversion requires confirmed unique leads and eligible order linkage, not PDF downloads. When either denominator or payment ledger is absent, output **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not 0. Report small numerator/denominator and uncertainty, rather than claiming statistical significance from a few visitors.

## 5. First-party attribution, privacy, and arithmetic

**No new tracking script.** Provide a **measurement CONTRACT** to Claude for integration with whichever existing first-party measurement pipeline is already authorized. Do not instantiate trackers, fingerprint devices, set identity cookies, or export email/health information. Canonical campaign labels: `utm_source` true platform; `utm_medium=organic_social`; `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`; `utm_content` exact pre-existing unique asset_id from verified Grok inventory. Never invent asset IDs for drafts.

Server- or existing-analytics-derived, non-identifying event categories (subject to privacy settings and appropriate consent): `qualified_human_session` (exclude obvious robots by documented methodology), `landing_visit`, `relevant_cta_click`, `confirmed_jetpack_lead` (provider-reported aggregate only), `affiliate_outbound_click` (approved active earning link only), `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`, `independent_settled_affiliate_commission`, `refund`, `fee`. Cross-merchant conversion cannot be inferred from referral parameters unless reporting contract and lawful matching actually permit it. No affiliate_click measurement should fire while controls are disabled. Missing publisher reports = **attribution unknown**, not zero sales.

`realized_net_revenue = independently confirmed gross settled receipts/commissions - refunds - applicable processing/platform fees` (respect the economic party of affiliate versus owned sale; do not double-count the same gross purchase and commission).

`net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`.

Not computable if either term is unverified. Report 7/30-day cohorts, session source, verified orders, verified customers, refunds, fees, missing-attribution percentage, and small-n caution. A lead-to-customer rate requires a lawful, provider-verified denominator and attributable paid buyers; otherwise leave blank. A trackable outbound click is **not** a paid order.

## Decision first: who actually has something to buy?

**Audience:** adults who already want a structured, short guided listening routine, have tried free meditation approaches, and are comparing whether a paid audio library adds meaningful convenience. Not people searching for a cure, wealth, altered brain frequencies, or supernatural outcomes. A reader who merely watched an invisible-gorilla demonstration is not automatically a qualified Zen12 buyer.

**Specific candidate:** Zen12 Premium (Inspire3), exact official seller presentation https://zen12.com/hnp . The seller describes twelve 12-minute levels in four listening styles, with 48 MP3 recordings and guides. This is an inventory/format observation only; it proves no cognitive, anxiety, medical or wealth benefit. The same page marketed $87.99 for Premium when checked on Nov 4, 2026, while the separate exact seller https://zen12.com/secret/ showed a conditional coupon display of $37. These are **different page-specific promotions**. Neither is an authenticated checkout total; neither should be displayed as a price on a proposed FV offer module. The buyer must see the authoritative merchant checkout and applicable terms at time of purchase if this is ever authorized.

**Primary blocker:** The public https://affiliates.inspire3.com/ portal invites signups and advertises a general affiliate program; it is NOT proof that Final Vestige has been approved, has a specific authorized tracking link or is permitted to promote by a particular channel. Inspire3's own https://inspire3.com/privacy/ says it may share an affiliate-referred buyer's name, email and purchased-product details with an affiliate. Until written acceptance, precise tracking permissions, acceptable data minimization and independently verifiable payouts exist: **HOLD_ALL_MERCHANT_CTA**. Do not move buyers to unapproved seller URLs under the guise of research citations inside a presell module. This applies even if an untracked nonaffiliate seller link is technically accessible.

**Fallback:** The published free 52-page Seven Day Focus Fieldbook at https://finalvestige.com/focus/ is an audience and education asset, not a paid checkout. The 15-product FV catalog/Payhip direction cannot be promoted as a currently purchasable Mind product until the seller's own authenticated inventory shows the exact SKU, rights, price, tax applicability, refunds, download fulfillment and genuine paid orders. Gmail promotional messages and public web pages do not substitute for that merchant record. If a verified paid owned Mind SKU appears before partner authorization, prioritize it and document a separate approved experiment. Never create a fictitious product or buy one through the user's account to "test" revenue.

## The actual promise and the proof boundary

Buyer objection: "I don't have time; will twelve minutes feel approachable?" The **format** is a defensible product-selection criterion: downloadable sessions of a stated duration, multiple audio styles, a stated guide. Buyer objection: "Will the brainwaves do the work for me, equivalent to an hour of advanced practice?" This is the merchant's assertion, not an FV finding. An independent 2023 review of 14 binaural-beat EEG studies found mixed and largely contrary entrainment evidence; it did not test or validate Zen12's claimed equivalence. A 2019 pooled behavioral review of differing studies also cannot establish this precise commercial claim. An unsupported scientific marketing promise is not rescued by pointing to evidence about general mindfulness practices.

**Proposed truthful hero:** "Twelve minutes may be easier to start. That's a format, not a neurological shortcut." Below it: an original consumer comparison explaining what is included, what a buyer can try free first, where the seller's price and refund terms must be confirmed, and what is unknown. There is no invented personal use, no pseudo-expert testimonial and no implied clinical endorsement.

**Free self-check demonstration:** Spend twelve quiet minutes with a non-commercial freely available audio recording or silence. Note whether the format makes it easier to begin and whether you prefer voice, ambient sound or no sound. This tests personal preference, **not** brain entrainment or treatment outcomes. No user answers collected. Avoid instructing a user to stop medical care or to listen in risky situations.

**Original short-film hypothesis:** Existing unpublished R114 variants already provide A (text) and B (same text plus an optional 40-second evidence film concept). Do not rebuild competing canonical pages. The R121 inline poster/transcript concept can be reviewed for UX, but it is NOT playable footage. No experiment has established that 40 seconds of video lifts orders. Acceptance requires actual original poster/video file, accurate transcript and captions, no autoplay/third-party player, graceful no-JS rendering, measured mobile performance at 360/390/768/1440/1920, keyboard/focus/contrast testing, and approved staged user experiment before any launch.

Only aggregate cohort-level data belongs in public GitHub, Drive or editorial documentation. Store no buyer contact details, individual order IDs or payment identifiers in source control.

03 / Practice & Reflection

Keep a record before the story changes

Pick a modest task, write its first step, attempt it for twelve minutes and compare your before-and-after explanations. This free self-observation is not a laboratory replication, cognitive diagnosis or promised performance improvement.

Evidence habit

Write down what you intended to do before you begin. Write what you actually did afterward. Preserve both—not just whichever explanation now feels more convincing.

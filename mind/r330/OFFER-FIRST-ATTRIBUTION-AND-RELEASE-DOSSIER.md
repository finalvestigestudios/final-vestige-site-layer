# R330: One-offer conversion evidence, attribution & release acceptance
*Final Vestige Studios · Mind & Perception only · 12 November 2026 · UNPUBLISHED / HARD HOLD*

## Decision before design

The business objective is **verified paid, refund-adjusted net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions**. An isolated article or beautiful page is only a possible funnel component. The most relevant third-party offer is the complete Zen12 course because readers arriving from focus/attention stories may value a short structured audio ritual. But the public affiliate recruitment page does **not** establish Final Vestige as an eligible publisher, prove that this specific Zen12 offer can be promoted via a given channel, provide an authorized earning URL, or establish any account-specific commission terms. Therefore the commercial CTA is disabled.

The second possibility is a Final Vestige-owned paid Mind/Perception product. Existing FV editions are known generally, but no account-authenticated, rights-cleared, active, deliverable matching Mind SKU, final checkout, refund policy or settlement has been verified in private Payhip. Public search cannot establish private inventory or certify fulfillment. Do not silently substitute a hypothetical item or derive terms from another SKU. The only presently available free reader outcome is the **existing Focus Fieldbook**, with its single Jetpack/Dispatch flow unchanged. Page impressions, a PDF view, a free download and a confirmed subscriber are distinct events.

## Reader intent, single relevant offer, and first conversion hypothesis

**Proposed high-intent reader:** someone struggling to begin a task, evaluating whether a guided twelve-minute audio library is worth buying, and seeking an honest distinction between convenience and outcomes. A creator's opening video must not promise a cognitive fix. The first meaningful action is to complete a free twelve-minute starter exercise. The optional Focus Fieldbook follows independently of purchasing. A disabled, disclosed Zen12 offer block appears only within the independent buyer guide.

**Hypothesis, not result:** once an eligible commercial route exists, version B (same text plus optional 42-second original explanation) *might* improve verified paid order conversion compared with text-only version A by demonstrating the distinction between experimental results and sales claims. Equally plausible: B distracts, consumes bandwidth, reduces mobile comprehension or lowers purchase intent. The outcome must be **confirmed net purchases**, not poster views, video starts or affiliate outbound clicks.

**Controls:** same copy, source trail, disclosure, price/terms description, product, CTA, layout positions and target destination in A and B; only the film module differs. The current film is **not encoded video**: source-controlled SVG poster, timed six-part narration, matching WebVTT and a keyboard-operable complete transcript in native details element. Do not call it playable or launch an A/B test until media encoding, caption sync, accessibility, performance and all commercial gates pass. A is fully useful without JavaScript.

## Event boundaries: specification only, no deployment

Before considering implementation, Claude should compare this plan to existing native analytics and Jetpack. The proposed minimal event contract:

1. **campaign_landing:** eligible human visit to an approved topic URL. Metadata only: validated utm_source platform, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11, utm_content=verified Grok asset_id, first-party content key, A/B variant and date. Strip unwanted query parameters. No email, name, raw IP, fingerprint or sensitive search words.
2. **article_to_focus_click:** actual navigation from guide/article to existing /focus/. Not a confirmed subscriber.
3. **confirmed_signup:** provider-backed double opt-in or another verified confirmation if observable and lawfully attributed; otherwise UNKNOWN. Do not create a test subscriber without permission.
4. **affiliate_outbound (also called affiliate_click):** permitted, disclosed outbound navigation using a **real issued partner earning link** after approval. Entirely disabled now. Not a sale.
5. **merchant_confirmed_paid_order:** independently verified first-party payment or account-backed affiliate settlement. Fields: validated SKU, date, gross, fees, refunds, chargebacks, currency and evidence reference. If merchant attribution is unavailable, report UNVERIFIED rather than infer from a click.
6. **net_revenue:** settled recognized receipts less relevant platform/processing fees and refunds, clearly stating incomplete attribution. Report ad or production expenses separately; don't silently call this business profit.

**Primary KPI:** 1,000 × realized, refund-adjusted attributed net revenue ÷ qualified-human sessions. Missing denominator, sales confirmation, payout or refund data means NOT COMPUTABLE rather than zero. Use 7-/30-day cohorts, flag bot traffic, sample size, refund lag and multi-touch attribution; confidence intervals are not meaningful without sufficiently many eligible randomized visits and actual purchases.

**Privacy/duplication:** use pre-existing first-party instrumentation if suitable. Never add a second analytics tag or paid SaaS. No email-based, cookie-based or fingerprint-based assignment without consent and policy assessment. Site QA/Claude must document human-session qualification rules: known crawler exclusion, internal tests, repeat load ambiguity and platform referrer uncertainty. Content asset IDs are not person identifiers.

## Acceptance protocol for staged review

**Partner eligibility:** Founder/Commerce authenticates the Inspire3 publisher dashboard or written acceptance, validates covered brands and products, issued URL, allowed TikTok/YouTube/website channels, possible email restrictions, attribution window, payout/refund terms, permitted creative, location restrictions and brand-safety rules. Share redacted proof only—never credentials or customer data in GitHub. Public recruitment and subscriber marketing email are not evidence of FV partner approval.

**If affiliate blocked:** authenticate an actually sale-ready FV-owned, rights-cleared Mind SKU through private Payhip inventory. Verify checkout, license, fulfillment, refund terms and settlement. Do not invent prices. If none is proven, keep the path educational and free; don't convert a disabled button into an unauthorized purchase URL.

**Buyer UX:** at 360/390/768/1440/1920 widths, independently check no overflow, 44px+ interactive targets, heading order, skip link, AA contrast, keyboard focus, native transcript details, reduced motion and no-JS operation. Validate WordPress sanitizer and actual production CSS cascade in staging. These remain source preflight only until a browser QA run.

**Disclosure:** any final affiliate link requires adjacent conspicuous language: “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” The current pages state the *prospective* condition beside a disabled button. A general disclosure page by itself is insufficient. Recheck the exact claims/guarantees and privacy terms against the seller pages on release day.

**Security/performance:** current prototypes have no scripts, added forms, merchant hrefs, hidden redirect, embeds, remote fonts, autoplay or analytics calls. Offline source checks cannot certify CSP, WCAG, merchant security or Lighthouse/CWV. B needs actual original file, encoding, correct captions, poster, preload=none, no forced playback, compressed budget and real performance checks before release.

**Editorial:** avoid competing indexed versions of the R319/R328 ideomotor story. R330 choice blindness is distinct. The published historical archives and Puységur source records cannot substantiate branded hypnosis. Historical comparison is about the reliability of documentation and reports.

The mind is an unreliable witness.

Sometimes the stranger changes and we don't notice. Sometimes a forbidden thought returns. Sometimes a historical witness reports something extraordinary—and the explanation arrives before the evidence. Welcome to the archive where we separate what people experienced from what we can actually establish.

Our Mind & Perception research extends Final Vestige's historical method into attention, memory, suggestion and meditation. It isn't a diagnostic service, a miracle laboratory, or a shopping catalog wearing a lab coat.

02 / THE RECORD BEFORE THE EXPLANATION

History of suggestion

Magnetism, somnambulism, hypnosis and autosuggestion acquired their own vocabularies of cures and wonders. The best historical inquiry doesn't ridicule the witnesses or accept their explanations on faith. It reads the files, preserves failures, and asks who knew what, when.

Begin with Puységur's original files

The published Animal Magnetism Files examine source volumes, predictions, case accounts, testimony and documented failures. Historical evidence is not an endorsement of animal magnetism or any modern hypnosis product.

In editorial review: *The Bear That Wouldn't Leave* reconstructs the 1987 white-bear thought-suppression experiment and the 2020 synthesis that complicates the popular retelling. This article is not yet publicly linked.

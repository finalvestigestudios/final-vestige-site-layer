Editorial method

Source, experiment, inference, offer

Historical evidence can establish that someone made a claim without proving the claimed mechanism. A laboratory effect can establish an effect under specific conditions without proving that a retail recording works. A seller page can describe files and a stated policy without proving the final charge, payout or outcome.

That separation is the point of this archive. Read research freely. Start with free materials. Treat a product only as an optional purchase whose claims can be checked separately.

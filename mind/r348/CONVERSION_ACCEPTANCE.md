- `qualified_landing`: a human-qualified eligible content session by known `utm_content` and aggregate campaign; privacy/anti-bot definition predeclared. Do not treat all hits as humans.
- `article_to_focus_click`: action to existing free page.

Separately report order count, customer count, paid conversion rate, lead-to-customer conversion; unconfirmed signups and outbound clicks are not sales.

**A/B hypothesis (NOT tested):** With identical source-based copy and audience mix, an optional self-selected 42-second original illustration may increase confirmed purchases per qualified session *relative to* text-only, without reducing free resource use, clarity of claims, or performance. Assign randomized or consistent eligible traffic server-side only after approval; avoid unconsented individual profiles. Watch the tiny-sample warning: do not claim a winner on a handful of observations.

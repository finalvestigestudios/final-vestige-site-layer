Variant A: complete buyer guide as text. Variant B: the exact same buyer guide plus *optional original* 42-second video concept, SVG poster, VTT captions and transcript. B has no playable video yet; only after source-control film encoding, native controls (no autoplay, preload=none), transcript/caption QA, WCAG/mobile QA, and founder release may it enter an experiment.

**Hypothesis not a promise:** the optional film could improve real paid purchase comprehension, be neutral, or distract from the decision. With partner-approved exact offer, randomized consent-eligible qualified-human sessions from the same proven creative/intent cohort would compare **confirmed settled paid orders and net realized revenue**, not outbound CTR alone. Hold wording, price, disclosure, CTA placement and page substance constant. Predefine 7-day and 30-day cohorts; disclose tiny sample sizes; do not announce significance without adequate data.

**Primary KPI:** 1,000 × (merchant-confirmed gross revenue or earned affiliate commission − refunds − chargebacks − merchant/processor fees) / qualified human sessions. Secondary: confirmed paid orders, unique actual customers, paid conversion rate, net per customer, verified leads (separate), lead-to-customer rate. Until numerator *and* denominator are established, result is **NOT COMPUTABLE**.

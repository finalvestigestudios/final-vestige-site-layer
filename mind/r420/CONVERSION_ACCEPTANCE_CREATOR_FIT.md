## Privacy-safe measurement contract (design, NOT a deployed analytics script)

Use only real published assets, e.g. `utm_source=(actual_tiktok_or_youtube)`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_blink_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=(verified_original_asset_id)`. Preserve legitimate bio-link routing limits; not every visit will be attributable. No invented asset IDs.

Separate events and truth sources:
1. `campaign_landing_v1`: existing first-party page metrics + lawful consent/bot review; **not necessarily qualified human**.
2. `qualified_human_session_v1`: Claude-defined human filtering/dedupe with aggregate retention and no PII/fingerprinting; document uncertainty.
3. `article_to_focus_click_v1`: a first-party navigation diagnostic; may rely on existing analytics, not a newly injected listener.
4. `confirmed_signup_v1`: existing Jetpack backend/provider-verified **unique consenting** lead; never infer from a `?subscribe=` param or download state.
5. `affiliate_click_v1`: only a disclosed, **authorized** merchant earning-link click, separate from purchase; not enabled here.
6. `merchant_paid_conversion_v1`: independently settled/confirmed product-specific order/commission, reconciled with merchant data and allowed attribution.
7. `settled_net_revenue_v1`: paid gross proceeds less legitimate commissions/reversals/refunds/fees. A pending ClickBank report is not cash realized.

For each **7-day and 30-day qualified-human acquisition cohort**, compute (only with confirmed denominator and settlement evidence) `net_revenue_per_1000 = 1000 * verified_refund_adjusted_net / qualified_human_sessions`. Also report verified paid conversion rate, unique orders/customers (deduped privately), confirmed leads, lead-to-customer conversion, gross revenue, refunds, fees and unattributed orders explicitly. For sparse cohorts annotate uncertainty (sample size and bounds); do not infer success from clicks or lift from a tiny sample. When missing, display **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0. Do not create paid SaaS or duplicate site scripts.

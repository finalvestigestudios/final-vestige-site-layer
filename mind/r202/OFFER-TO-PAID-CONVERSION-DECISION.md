## Buyer and truthful proposition
**Person:** an English-speaking visitor who searched for a 12-minute meditation recording, structured guided audio, or an evidence-based comparison of Zen12 and ordinary timed practice. This is a high-intent *hypothesis*, not a validated customer persona.

**Problem:** commercial audio claims combine easy-to-check inventory with difficult-to-verify promises. A listener may want a convenient program without becoming an experimental neuroscientist.

**Insight:** the 1977 repetition study and later work on illusory truth warn that frequent marketing language can feel established without being independently verified. The presell guide should give one useful test: compare the seller's exact benefit claim to a study of that **same branded audio, dosage, outcome and control**, not a study of an unrelated meditation course. Product availability is distinct from product efficacy.

**Promise we can responsibly deliver now:** the reader learns what is included *according to the merchant*, what evidence does and does not test, what questions to ask about refunds, and how to try a free twelve-minute self-directed routine. We do not guarantee relaxation, increased attention, relief of symptoms, cures or 12-minutes-equals-one-hour equivalence.

**Objections to answer:** "Do I need to buy audio to practice?" No, the existing Focus routine or a basic timer is an alternative. "Is there proof I will be more focused?" No qualified product-specific evidence established here. "Does the refund guarantee work?" Advertised by seller; execution and buyer terms not independently tested. "Am I subscribing to another list?" No; use the existing Jetpack/Focus only. "Why an affiliate link?" Disclose near any eventual earning CTA and explain editorial independence.

## Source-controlled A/B pilot — not a live test
Existing **R201 Variant A** is a text-first Zen12 buyer's guide. **Variant B** has the **same commercial and factual copy** with an extra *optional, unrendered 42-second film concept*, a static self-contained poster and an equivalent transcript. This R202 sprint corrects both variants for the independent Eason/Parris review: 22 qualifying trials, 17 pooled, heterogeneous methods, bias concerns and **no causal head-to-head proof of superiority of the teaching method**. B's narration correction preserves the same cautious distinction. A file-only WebVTT transcript is drafted; no MP4 is playable.

Test hypothesis: a short, optional research-method demonstration **might** increase net paid revenue per qualified reader compared with the same text guide. It could also slow loading, distract or erode trust. No conversion direction is assumed.

Pre-register exposure unit (a qualified human session), allocation process, fraud/bot exclusions, unique purchaser counting, merchant-side paid-sale verification, primary metric and abort rules before opening any earning CTA. Keep page copy, sources, refund language and CTA identical in both variants. Only B's voluntary film differs. No autoplay, no third-party player, `preload="none"` if media is later approved, caption/transcript/poster, keyboard control and user choice; no-JS baseline must show identical core text. Cap video bytes, test mobile performance and WCAG 2.2 AA in a real browser. Use **seven- and thirty-day cohorts**, noting that a handful of orders cannot establish reliable uplift.

## Ethical CTA and disclosure contract
Unpublished guide should render a native `<button type="button" disabled>` and a visible explanatory reason, not a clickable seller/affiliate URL. Adjacent, visibly before the control: **“If you buy through an authorized product link here in the future, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.”** When a real approved link exists, replace that prospective copy with a plain **“If you buy through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.”** Use `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` for outbound earning links; no disguised shortener or affiliate cloaking. FTC requires material disclosure near the recommendation. A footer disclosure alone is not enough. Do not invent actual commissions, earning percentages or acceptance.

Product mentions inside independent scientific investigations remain neutral, with commercial recommendation separate and clearly identified. The catalog describes Zen12, Hypnosis Live and Brainwave Shots as different offers; do not attribute a Hypnosis Live single-file price or return period to Zen12.

## Measurement implementation contract for Claude (not code deployed)
Use existing first-party or Jetpack facilities if they provide adequate event evidence. **No duplicate trackers or new paid SaaS**. Standard permitted campaign tags: `utm_source={actual_platform}`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content={actual_immutable_asset_id}`. Refuse made-up IDs. Validate tags against allowlisted campaigns, strip email/name/phone, no cross-site personal identifiers. A user's choice to click out must not be falsely linked to an eventual purchase without an independently reported conversion mechanism and appropriate consent.

Events and their **authoritative sources**:
1. `campaign_landing` — first-party legitimate page view, dedupe eligible session, exclude staff/known bots when demonstrable.
2. `article_to_focus_click` or `guide_view` — outbound/navigation intent only, NOT a lead.
3. `confirmed_signup` — Jetpack authoritative confirmed subscription event after required opt-in; a `?subscribe=` flag, visible download or PDF click is insufficient.
4. `affiliate_click` — verified link click, **NOT** order, customer, income or commission.
5. `owned_checkout_started` — actual merchant-verified step, not assumed from CTA click.
6. `paid_order_verified` — authenticated Payhip sale with paid status, refunds and identity safeguards. Aggregate only.
7. `affiliate_sale_verified` — Inspire3 partner statement/report of a paid attributable order, separate from click.
8. `net_realized_revenue` — paid/settled receipts minus refunded amounts, payment/merchant fees and directly attributable variable acquisition/fulfillment costs. Keep affiliate commissions and owned product receipts separate; reconcile accounting periods.

For each 7/30-day cohort, report **net realized revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions = 1000 × realized net revenue / eligible deduplicated human sessions**, only when both numerator and denominator are independently verified. Also show unique confirmed paid customers, confirmed paid conversion rate, gross receipts, refund counts, fees and lead→customer conversion where both sides are verifiable. If revenue or denominator is absent report `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, not zero. If no verified orders but trustworthy exposure and payment reports exist, zero may be defensible; not here. Show tiny-sample warnings, missing source, attribution gaps and confidence limitations.

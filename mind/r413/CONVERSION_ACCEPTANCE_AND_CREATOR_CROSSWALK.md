## 4. Controlled experiment: free first, then optional paid (upon gates)

- **Primary A/B hypothesis:** At equal qualifying traffic and identical buyer-guide copy, an optional, original, captioned 42-second film might increase *net realized revenue per 1,000 qualified human visits* relative to the text-only guide. It might instead add friction or decrease conversion. There is no preclaimed winner.
- **A:** source-first text guide, live free Fieldbook link, disabled merchant control.
- **B:** same substantive buyer copy plus an original SVG poster, expandable narration text and six VTT captions for a *future film*. **At current HEAD this is a storyboard only; no video can be played. Do not conduct or report a “video A/B” result with this draft.**
- **First test available while commerce held:** qualitative accessibility/clarity review and independent source/claim consistency checks on both versions. No paid A/B experiment, production split or attribution script.
- **Future media gate:** encode an original 42-second video, synchronize captions, preserve transcript, controls and poster, preload=none, no autoplay, no third-party video widgets, cap video bytes by measured mobile performance. Browser proof at widths 360, 390, 768, 1440, 1920; keyboard, screen reader, WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced-motion, no JS; measured Lighthouse/CWV on production-equivalent environment; browser proof must be reproducible.
- **Future commercial gate:** checkout must be an exact permitted, approved publisher SKU/link with adjacent clear material-connection disclosure and independently reviewed final purchase price, refunds, upsells and tracking terms. Founder release authorization required before routing anyone to a merchant.
- **Randomization:** Claude engineering should reuse existing first-party capabilities, with privacy review, not duplicate scripts or paid services. Prioritize deterministic campaign allocation with consent where applicable, exclude verified QA/bots, avoid cross-site IDs and personal targeting. Lock definitions before start. No test if randomization or outcome reconciliation cannot be trusted.
- **Analysis:** Set exposure period and minimum trustworthy cohorts *before launch*, report 7/30-day cohorts, counts and uncertainty intervals, separate device/platform, note tiny sample sizes and avoid declaring victory on CTR alone.

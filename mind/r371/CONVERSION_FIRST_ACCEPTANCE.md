## Controlled A/B preregistration: buyer guide text vs optional original short

- **A:** source-first, text-only Zen12 Premium review.
- **B:** identical substantive buyer review (same hero, claims, source disclosures, FAQ, free baseline, CTA status and offer) plus an optional 42-second **storyboard** and original SVG schematic. The B film is *not yet recorded/encoded/playable*. VTT captions and text transcript are included; the present B is concept/design, not a real treatment delivered to users.
- **Future B media requirement:** original licensed 42-second file, human-QA timed captions with > 1 second readable cue durations, accessible text transcript, visible poster, explicit play control, no autoplay, no third-party player pixel, reduced-motion respectful, compressed with mobile LCP/INP/CLS evidence; no-JS transcript always available.
- **Hypothesis:** for readers who voluntarily open the buyer guide, B may improve verified **paid** order rate per qualified-human session, but B may increase bounce/load and reduce trust. This is a hypothesis, not a sales forecast.
- **Primary endpoint:** independently verified *net realized revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions* (if attributed settlements are available). Secondary: verified paid order rate, paid customer count, net revenue, settled commission, refund rate, lead-to-customer conversion. Diagnostic only: clicks, time, optional video play, CTA interest.
- **Randomization:** if owner-approved in staging, stable anonymous per-session 1:1 split with clear test population, no cookies absent consent and no personal fingerprinting. If available first-party tool cannot allocate without extra tracking, use a preregistered alternation of *days* and analyze as observational rather than an individual-randomized A/B test; avoid confounded causal claims.
- **Guardrails:** 7- and 30-day cohorts, sample-size warning for <30 qualified sessions or <10 paid conversions per arm; even above these do not imply statistical significance without predefined analyses. Referrer bots, duplicate requests and internal admin activity excluded using documented privacy-safe rules. No performance claims without real browser data.
- **Stopping:** stop for broken checkout/consent, misleading disclosure, increased refund disputes, measurable accessibility regression, or unverified rights; do not repeatedly peek and declare a winner.
- **Offer activation condition:** only after founder approval, named affiliate terms/channel permission and issued URL verification, successful browser QA and independently evidenced offer/checkout/returns. Update PR, not live CMS, until release approval.

## First-party data contract (proposed, not deployed)

Source → `utm_source` = actual platform, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_perception_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<immutable_grok_asset_id>`. Never invent an asset ID; no ID means campaign attribution **UNKNOWN**. Only include accepted query parameters; avoid link cloaking or unapproved merchant redirection.

Use existing consent/analytics plumbing; no new pixel, third-party SaaS or duplicate client script:
1. `campaign_landing`: consent-safe campaign, content ID, landing route and coarse date; count qualified humans via one first-party method, deduping requests without PII.
2. `article_to_resource_click`: destination = /focus/ (diagnostic).
3. `confirmed_signup`: only provider-confirmed Jetpack subscriber event, if legitimately observable with consent; distinguish pending/unconfirmed.
4. `buyer_guide_view`: offer=zen12_premium, variant=A/B (diagnostic).
5. `affiliate_click`: only on authorized real earning link, with event separate from sale and never recorded on disabled CTA.
6. `merchant_confirmed_paid_order` / `affiliate_settled_commission`: independently reported seller/Payhip/affiliate ledger with refunds, reversals, chargebacks, processing fees; retain coarse campaign where available. A click cannot be substituted.
7. `realized_net_revenue = net recognized merchant payout or owned-goods receipts - attributable transaction/fulfillment costs - refunds/chargebacks/fees`, clarify accounting boundary in report. `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_sessions = 1000 * realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`. If denominator or ledger missing: **NOT COMPUTABLE**, *not $0*.

Never store visitor names/emails, granular IPs, fingerprint IDs or subscriber addresses in public issue/commit. Never place private merchant link token in claim matrix or analytics payload.

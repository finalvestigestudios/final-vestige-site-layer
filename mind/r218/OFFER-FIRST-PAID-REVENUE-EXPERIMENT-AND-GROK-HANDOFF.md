## Experiment decision: preserve frozen R210 as the prior controlled hypothesis
A single original experiment, R210 text versus optional 42-second film, already exists [in source control](https://github.com/finalvestigestudios/final-vestige-site-layer/tree/10a5ebf055bc657c60302dd8f7f79f4bdf0566ee/mind/r210). R218 supplies **alternative copy-review candidates**, *not concurrent extra A/B arms*. Both R218 pages use exactly the same buyer guide body and a natively disabled button plus immediately adjacent prospective financial disclosure. R218-B adds only an original static inline SVG poster, transcript and five-cue VTT. The film is **UNRENDERED**, has no live URL, no playable or autoplay media, no remote request and no JavaScript. Fully functional no-JS reading is the default; no additional email form. Actual audiovisual accessibility and rights checks remain necessary if a film is ever built.

**Test hypothesis, not guaranteed lift:** after approval and a genuine earning URL, compare an accessible, visually identical text-only guide with one containing the optional finished 42-second film, assigned independently of source/device with deterministic randomization by an approved existing mechanism. The counterfactual is credible only if traffic quality, offer, price, discount, copy, disclosure, checkout destination, page metadata and timing are comparable. Blind attribution to variant until after cohort close if feasible, but do not invent a sampling method or deployment.

**Primary outcome:** merchant-confirmed settled paid orders / independently cleared attributable commissions, expressed as realized net revenue per **1,000 qualified-human sessions** within **7-day and 30-day** cohorts. Do not optimize solely for outbound affiliate clicks or video watches. A consumer can see a video, click, and never buy; the experiment must treat that as zero *verified orders* but not automatically zero *realized net* when other ledger data are missing. Refund latency means cohorts can be provisional. Report uncertainty and tiny samples; never declare a winner on two purchases. Secondary diagnostics: qualified visitors, offer-CTA clicks, confirmed Jetpack opt-ins, merchant exit rate, return rate, mobile performance, disclosures noticed and accessible video use. No unapproved paid traffic.

**Minimum release evidence:** genuine affiliate status, allowed promotions by channel, approved destination/link and cookie/attribution terms, independently checked checkout and refund policy, product claims/medical review, founder approval, WordPress sanitizer result, browser 360/390/768/1440/1920 inspections, keyboard + voiceover/screen-reader + touch, disabled/active control states, captions/transcript parity, reduced-motion and no-JS, Lighthouse/CWV with film budget, actual source-linked A/B setup and payment reconciliation. R218 source-level tests only cover a subset. Site QA owns actual browser and production-safe release review; Claude owns core measurement implementation. Mind does not merge, deploy or overwrite original R210/R172.

## One measurement contract, without duplicate scripts
**This section is a proposed data contract for Claude/site QA, NOT a deployed analytics system.** Use the existing approved production mechanism after founder consent. No cookies, fingerprinting or PII need to be introduced by the Mind workstream. Do not capture email or IP in campaign rows. Preserve existing Jetpack consent/preferences. Store only allowlisted social `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign`, genuinely verified `asset_id` or `utm_content`, article/guide `page_id`, coarse event type, cohort day and nonidentifying aggregate counts where lawful and permitted. Raw referral tokens from merchant links or account IDs do not belong in public analytics or issue comments.

**Event distinctions (never silently promote an upstream stage to a downstream sale):**  
1. `qualified_human_session_estimate`: documented bot-screening methodology, count method, error uncertainty, no false precision; record direct/organic separately.  
2. `landing_view`: real published page view attributable through approved campaign fields; do not infer a TikTok impression was a visit.  
3. `relevant_cta_click`: Focus/guide exit click only; not a subscriber or order.  
4. `focus_confirmed_optin`: consented, provider-confirmed existing Jetpack record; no new subscription stack.  
5. `affiliate_click`: only after genuine partner acceptance and an active link; separate from purchase.  
6. `owned_order_paid`: paid FV order reconciled to Payhip or other authoritative merchant record, not a checkout click.  
7. `affiliate_commission_cleared`: network/merchant's cleared payable or paid commission, not pending click credit.  
8. `refund`, `fee`, `chargeback`, `attributable_variable_cost`: actual ledger entries with currency, event date and attribution confidence.

**One canonical formula**, shared with the R218 matrix and future QA:  
`realized_net_revenue = settled_owned_receipts + cleared_affiliate_commissions - refunded_amounts - transaction_fees - chargebacks - attributable_variable_costs`  
`net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`

If denominator is missing/invalid, receipts are missing or not authoritative, currencies cannot be aligned, or transaction/refund reports cannot be reconciled: output **NOT COMPUTABLE**, never $0. A computed $0 is valid only with known eligible denominator and complete auditable zero paid receipts/commissions plus adjustments. Keep 7-day and 30-day cohorts distinct; report settlement and refund lag. Don't sum an affiliate order's gross basket value with the commission for that same order.

**Privacy checks:** reject `email`, `ip`, `full_url`, `user_agent`, device identifiers, arbitrary UTM keys or raw order/customer IDs. Do not send personal data to a second vendor or install another script. Attribution should be first-party and aggregated; approved consent determines any more precise campaign handling. No claim that an event feed is LIVE or VERIFIED without actual event samples matched to an approved source.

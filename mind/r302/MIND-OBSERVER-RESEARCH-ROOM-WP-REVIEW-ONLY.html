Try it / Original exercise

Change your search instruction.

Name three ordinary objects you expect to find in a room. Visit it, identify those three, then make a second inspection for unanticipated objects or unusual details. Record what changed between the first and second pass.

This is a reflection activity, not a replication of the expert CT experiment, diagnosis, attention score or a promise that repetition will improve your mind.

Commercial research / Separate from findings

Before buying meditation audio, inspect the offer.

The seller's Zen12 checkout describes twelve course levels and 48 MP3 recordings, plus supporting material. That is the advertised format; it does not establish improved concentration or any health effect. Hypnosis Live Total Focus and Brainwave Shots Focus Shot are different audio products with different conditions and must not be presented as Zen12 equivalents. Prices, refund terms and rights require a fresh checkout and partner-agreement audit before activation.

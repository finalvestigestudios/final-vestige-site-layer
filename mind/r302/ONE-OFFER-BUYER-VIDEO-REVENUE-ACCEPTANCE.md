## Who may buy, and what problem are they trying to solve?

The highest-intent prospective reader here is not someone wanting to cure a disorder, multiply intelligence or recover missing sensory awareness. It is an adult already considering a **structured meditation audio library** who wants to know the number and format of recordings, the practical listening commitment, what features are included, and what the seller can and cannot justify about outcomes.

There is a second, lower-intent visitor: someone arriving from a gorilla/inattentional-blindness short because a perception demonstration surprised them. The visitor's actual intent is to understand an experiment. Do not send them directly to a product checkout, and do not suggest the experiment proves any need to buy an audio program.

## Original presell positioning (replacement *proposal* within existing R284 pilot, not a second purchase page)

### Proposed truthful hero
**Before you buy a meditation recording, inspect the evidence.**

*Zen12 is advertised as a 12-level, 48-MP3 guided meditation course. That describes what is included—not what it has been proved to do for your attention. Here's a useful way to decide whether its format belongs in your routine.*

**Problem-to-insight:** When a task is hard to begin, a prerecorded sequence can seem appealing because it removes the decision of what to do next. But a useful structure is not itself a clinically validated effect. A review should help readers distinguish convenience from promised transformation.

**Source-grounded micro-story:** In a 2013 study, 20 of 24 expert radiologists did not report an unexpected gorilla image while searching CT scans for nodules. Their expertise at the real task was greater than novices'. Their miss rate says something about the search task, not about Zen12 or commercial meditation. [Drew et al., full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3964612/). A movie/picture from the study cannot be reused without a rights decision. Tell the story in original writing and simple geometry, with attribution.

**One useful demonstration:** Ask the reader to choose a routine they are actually considering. Write a baseline: duration planned, what kind of task, interruption count, and whether it was completed. Try a free self-directed 12-minute start on one day, and if purchasing authorized, a seller-described recording of approximately comparable intention on another day. Note differences without pretending the schedule is randomized or blinded. This is an individual preference log, not evidence of effectiveness. No result should be interpreted medically.

**Offer value, with limits:** The merchant describes a structured course, 48 MP3 files, user guide and support/bonus elements. The buyer might value ready-to-play sequences; there is no verified independent clinical evidence of Zen12-specific concentration improvement in the reviewed sources. The 2013 radiology experiment is not evidence for it. Avoid "12 minutes equals 1 hour of meditation," medical, sleep, financial or performance guarantees.

**Objections answered without evasion:**
- *Does it work?* “Work” has multiple meanings. The merchant sells access to files; product-specific improvement in attention has not been independently established in the reviewed studies. Perceived enjoyment or convenience is subjective.
- *Do I need it to begin?* No. The free Focus Fieldbook offers a self-directed 12-minute starting practice. A subscriber is not required to view research.
- *Can I get a refund?* The seller checkout describes a guarantee, but time limit, scope, conditions, eligible products and jurisdiction must be read from current merchant terms before publishing precise language; do not infer actual refund outcomes.
- *Is the price a limited opportunity?* No Final Vestige urgency claim. The merchant checkout changes, so price is intentionally not quoted or represented as an authorized bargain.
- *Why are you showing me this?* Final Vestige's Mind reviews look at audio products because readers may ask about them. Research conclusions do not depend on affiliate revenue.

**Conditional CTA copy, DISABLED:** “View the current Zen12 course details — link unavailable until verified partner authorization.” Once an earning URL is approved, use a clearly described external destination without prohibited cloaking and place the unambiguous disclosure immediately adjacent: **“If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.”** Never claim an approved link already exists. Do not make this CTA the sole way to obtain scientific references or the free Fieldbook.

## A/B hypothesis: text-only vs the exact same guide + voluntary 42-second original film

Existing R284 Variant A is a complete source guide. Variant B is the same guide with a **static** 42-second storyboard; it is NOT a playable video. R302 adds an original 42-second script and custom vector poster for editorial review, *not* a hosted MP4/WebM. There is no authorized live experiment.

**Testable hypothesis:** For visitors who already express purchase-comparison intent, an optional clearly labeled source explainer may reduce confusion and improve *verified paid purchases per qualified visitor* without lowering trust or worsening performance. Its actual effect may be negative, zero or positive.

**Design:** Hold title, product copy, source references, CTA position and disclosure constant across variants. Random assignment should be privacy-safe and session-consistent; Claude owns engineering and consent. No variant may be shown with an unverified earning URL. Original accessible film requires poster, transcript, timed WebVTT captions, user-initiated controls, no autoplay, reduced motion, an offline/no-JS transcript, and a lightweight local asset. Defer posting the film to Grok; this is on-site source explainer, not a Grok social deliverable.

**Technical acceptance:** Browser-verify 360/390/768/1440/1920 widths; keyboard reachability, text contrast, line breaks, disclosure visibility next to link, reduced-motion, screenreader labels, external-link behavior, no-JS, staging WordPress sanitizer, no third-party scripts and no duplicate analytics. Measure LCP and interaction impact before approval. Inspect signed-out mobile route via the actual creator platform rather than assuming a working path. Check captions against narration and poster for rights.

**Experiment success:** Compare settled affiliate commission attributed to each randomized variant divided by qualified-human sessions, with 7/30-day cohorts and small-sample caveats. Confirm with merchant exports, exclude/correct refunds and fees using merchant adjustment provenance, and do not double-subtract charges already netted from the cleared commission. Treat outbound affiliate_click, lead or checkout initiation as *diagnostic* funnel events, not paid conversions. No statistical conclusion with too few purchases.

## First-party attribution: schema contract, not another script

Claude's existing source-of-truth analytics work under Issue #820 owns instrumentation. Before deployment, request one schema mapping:
- `utm_source`: actual platform (e.g. youtube, tiktok), not an invented campaign;
- `utm_medium=organic_social`;
- `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11` **PROPOSED, not current live canonical**; inherited documents disagree between `mind_pilot_2026_11` and `mind_proposed_2026_11`; engineering owner must resolve to one identifier;
- `utm_content`: literal verified Grok asset_id; none is invented;
- `campaign_landing`: first-party landing diagnostic only, with consent/bot filtering and no raw PII;
- `article_to_fieldbook_click`: click, not subscription;
- `confirmed_signup`: only provider-confirmed, not button click;
- `affiliate_click`: outbound event, not accepted paid order;
- `merchant_confirmed_purchase`: merchant-confirmed, deduplicated with eligible session attribution;
- `commission_cleared`: independently reconciled settlement after merchant adjustments;
- `refund_adjustment`, `fees`: distinguish whether the merchant already netted them.

**Revenue formula:** `FV realized net revenue per 1000 qualified-human sessions = 1000 * (FV-owned net settled receipts + cleared FV-attributable affiliate commissions - separately uncompensated attributable costs) / qualifying human sessions`. Treat gross merchant product totals as merchant revenue, not FV's; avoid subtracting a refund twice from a net commission. Report NOT COMPUTABLE if denominator or legitimate revenue data are absent. Cohort by initial human campaign landing (7d and 30d) with a clear attribution window and refund maturity. No impressions, raw pageviews, bot spikes, unconfirmed newsletter subscribers or clicks represented as customers.

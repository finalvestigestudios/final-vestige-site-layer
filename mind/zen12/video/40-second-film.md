### Commercial disclosure and rights
- If the film endorses the product or includes an earning link, **spoken plus on-screen** near the endorsement is recommended; closing slate alone is not a substitute for disclosure immediately near an early endorsement. For the pilot, keep vendor mention at 22–33 seconds neutral and include commission disclosure in voice and large high-contrast text.
- No paid music, reused Inspire3 ad visuals, borrowed testimonials, screenshots of merchant UI, or third-party images without rights verification.
- Audio levels accessible; no seizures/strobe, flashing, pseudo-EEG proof or anxiety cure claim.
- Caption master must describe all meaning in the film; provide transcript outside player, retain original article for no-JS.
- Example include is `video-module.release-gated.html` and MUST NOT be copied to a published page until MP4/poster/WEBVTT URLs actually exist and have been checked.

### Release criteria
Final 38–42 second playable asset exists, own rights established, media transcoded, dimensions and MIME verified, poster legible, VTT timed captions verified, controls keyboard usable, mobile performance measured. Run no-video versus video tests **only after meaningful traffic**; never claim an A/B result before measuring it.

## The high-intent experiment

`zen12-proof-A-TEXT-UNPUBLISHED.html` offers a vivid problem-to-evidence opening, merchant inventory, free exercise, objections, source trail and native-disabled purchasing button. Variant B adds **only** a new original 42-second storyboard/poster/transcript; no playable film exists. Both HTML files have no external scripts, no web fonts, no autoplay, no remote media, no forms, no tracker, and no earning URLs. They display the same near-CTA proposed affiliate disclosure and no-JS disabled state.

**Hypothesis, not forecast:** if an authorized, rights-cleared video is produced, B could influence **paid order conversion rate and realized net revenue/1,000 qualified human sessions**; it could also slow mobile loading or lower trust. A and B require the same audience eligibility, the same pricing/return disclosures, same merchant offer and a stable assignment method verified by Claude/Site QA before any release. The new video has no marketing permission and is not live. Do not infer a win from click-through rate, watch rate, button taps or tiny sample sizes. Stop if accessibility, factual accuracy, performance, consent or authorization fails. Never conceal a commercial link.

**Prospective CTA:** “Purchase unavailable — partner approval not verified.” Adjacent disclosure: “If you purchase through a future approved referral link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” Only if authenticated authorization, accurate partner links/terms and release review succeed should Claude replace that disabled state with a clearly marked external referral; link should be `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` with explicit new-tab context if applicable. This pilot does **not** perform that operation.

## Data contract — no double-counting or fabricated income

Reuse the **existing approved first-party analytics**; do not install a new pixel, script, cookie, email platform or SaaS. With appropriate consent collect only sanctioned `asset_id`, campaign identifier, landing path, eligibility flag, timestamp bucket and aggregate CTA-event class. Use consent and bot filtering consistently; no IP, raw email, name or fingerprinting in events. DNT and existing privacy-policy constraints must be honored. An affiliate `outbound_click` is **not** an order. A Focus `form_submit` is **not** a Jetpack confirmed lead. A checkout view is **not** a settled sale.

For 7- and 30-day qualified-human acquisition cohorts: count authenticated merchant-confirmed paid purchases and independently cleared affiliate commissions, reconcile refunds, transaction fees, chargebacks and campaign-attributable costs, then compute **realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions = 1000 × (settled receipts from attributable paid FV orders + cleared attributable affiliate commissions − attributable refunds, fees, chargebacks and direct costs) / qualified-human sessions**. Avoid double counting an affiliate sale as an FV-owned-order receipt. If any necessary source is missing or the denominator is zero, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0. Segment by actual Grok asset ID and UTM campaign when real records exist; do not fill absent IDs with fictional examples in published reports. Display confidence intervals, raw numerators and tiny-sample caveats when cohorts become available.

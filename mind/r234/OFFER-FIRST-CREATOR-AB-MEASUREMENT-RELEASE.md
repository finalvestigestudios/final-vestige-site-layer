## High-intent presell design

The R234 A/B guide deliberately treats the **same 1,083-word buyer decision text** as immutable between versions. Both offer a free twelve-minute observation, seller-described features, product-fit/refund questions, limitations, independent research links, disabled CTA and adjacent conditional disclosure. B inserts a separate labeled **static 42-second film treatment** with original SVG poster and six caption cues; **no video has been rendered**, and this treatment must never be represented as a playable film. A real MP4, proper VTT, no autoplay, preload=none, reduced-motion fallback, standalone transcript and named human QA would be needed for a release.

**Hypothesis, not prediction:** for qualified readers with genuine meditation-audio interest, an optional short explainer may clarify the commercial decision compared with text alone. It might also slow the page or distract. The primary outcome for any later randomized test must be **verified net paid revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions**; secondary are verified paid-order conversion, settled commission per visitor, and provider-confirmed Focus lead rate. Playback, engagement, outbound click or emailed welcome are not revenue. Analyze 7/30-day cohorts; declare inadequate samples. No test begins while commercial gates are red.

A/B comparability: same headline, features, caveats, Focus route, CTA location, disclosure, eligibility and date; change *only* optional visual element. No paid SaaS or client-side experiment framework. Engineering may propose first-party server allocation only if QA approves cache/consent/no-PII implications. For now compare source snapshots; **do not fabricate experimental lift**.

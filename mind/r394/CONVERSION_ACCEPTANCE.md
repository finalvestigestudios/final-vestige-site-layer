## 3. Conversion event contract (specification only; Claude owns implementation)
1. `qualified_human_session`: established consent-aware first-party session, organic context, deduplicated without collecting visitor emails, raw IPs or fingerprinting.
2. `campaign_landing`: platform, `utm_medium=organic_social`, exact `utm_campaign`, and Grok-supplied `utm_content=asset_id`; reject unknown asset IDs rather than inventing names.
3. `article_to_resource_click`: click to existing Focus link. **Not a signup.**
4. `confirmed_signup`: only provider-verified opt-in status, if existing Jetpack can independently evidence it; never infer from PDF view.
5. `affiliate_outbound`: click to an **approved** merchant tracking link, after FTC disclosure; explicitly not sale.
6. `merchant_confirmed_purchase` or `partner_reported_paid_conversion`: independently verifiable order/commission from permitted merchant settlement; avoid recording customer PII in client logs.
7. `refund_fee_adjustment`: reconcile paid amounts minus reversals, taxes treated appropriately, direct processing fees and platform costs.

**Primary KPI:** realized net revenue / qualified-human sessions × 1,000. Denominator must be trusted; otherwise **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0. Report settled orders, qualified conversion rate and 7/30-day cohorts with tiny-sample confidence warnings. Prefer preexisting native analytics; no redundant scripts or paid SaaS.

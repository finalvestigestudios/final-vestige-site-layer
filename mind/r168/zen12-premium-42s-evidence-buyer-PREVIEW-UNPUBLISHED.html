A listening experiment in evidence

Two tones. One perceived beat. A much bigger claim.

A genuine auditory illusion does not automatically prove that an audio program can deliver an hour of meditation in twelve minutes. Here's how to assess Zen12 Premium without confusing the science with the sales pitch.

Independent reviewNo personal-use claimNo miracle promises
01 / First, know the difference

Three questions. Three distinct kinds of evidence.

I / PERCEIVE

Can you hear a beat?

The auditory system can generate a rhythmic percept from separate tones. That doesn't show that a purchase improves focus.

II / MEASURE

Does the EEG entrain?

A 2023 review of fourteen binaural-beat EEG studies found five supportive, eight contradictory, and one mixed result [1].

III / DEMONSTRATE

Does the exact recording help?

That requires well-controlled studies of the specific product and outcome, not simply the same frequency label.

02 / Try a useful thought experiment

What is really being transmitted?

Imagine two stereo channels: a steady 200 Hz tone in the left ear and a steady 210 Hz tone in the right. The ten-hertz difference isn't an ordinary ten-hertz pulse recorded in either channel. A perceived beat can emerge through auditory processing.

Write this down:
Left 200 Hz | Right 210 Hz | Difference 10 Hz

Now ask: Does detecting the difference prove that the whole brain synchronizes at ten hertz? Does it show greater concentration? Could it verify an hour-of-meditation equivalence? No: those require separate tests.

On November 6, 2026, the merchant page displayed US $87.99 for Premium. This is a dated seller-page observation, not a checkout quote, price lock, discount entitlement, or verified final charge.

Before deciding

  • Would you still value the audio library if it produced no promised “brainwave” advantage?
  • Have you compared the experience with free guided meditation or a simple silent routine?
  • Can you verify the current checkout price, download format, access arrangements, refund exclusions and support?
  • Are you relying on therapeutic, performance, or meditation-equivalence promises? They are not independently substantiated here.

## High-intent landing design

Variant A: self-contained text source guide with the original study, corrections, seller format, a free 12-minute comparison, the buyer objections, risk boundary, disabled offer and an adjacent disclosure. Entire page readable without JS, external fonts, third-party assets or signup.
Variant B: same substantive guide and CTA, plus a self-contained optional expand-to-view original 42-second poster/storyboard/transcript/caption concept. **No playable video is present**; do not claim it is. Accessible fallback exists; eventual player should be native `<video controls preload="none">` with caption track and no autoplay, subject to original media and WCAG/performance review.

**Pre-registered hypothesis for FUTURE gated pilot:** among qualified human visitors from precisely mapped Grok video(s) who have a valid, approved purchase destination, optional original video may increase **verified paid conversion rate** by reducing mismatch and clarifying limits. It may also lower conversion by adding friction or slowing mobile load; there is no presumption of uplift. Use an A/B split controlled by existing first-party infrastructure ONLY after consent/privacy review. Keep actual buyer text, offer, checkout, price, disclosure, CTA position and traffic source constant; only optional media differs. An allocation scheme with no persistently identifying visitor key is preferred; use existing experimentation tooling if already approved, otherwise no A/B. Hold media bandwidth to a specified budget, and test that video bytes are never loaded without user initiation. No big sample, no significance claim.

**Metrics:** verify 7- and 30-day cohorts by `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content=actual_asset_id`, and canonical landing. First party aggregate events:
- `qualified_landing`: human qualification methodology documented by Claude; do not mistake Jetpack pageviews for sessions.
- `resource_cta_click`: optional on-site click; not a lead.
- `confirmed_signup`: only provider-confirmed actual accepted opt-in; do not count click or unconfirmed Jetpack number.
- `affiliate_outbound`: only after verified partner authorization and real outbound action; not purchase, not commission.
- `merchant_paid_order`: merchant or FV-owned checkout confirmed with lawful data access, not inferred from page analytics.
- `settled_affiliate_commission`: affiliate portal settled statement and order/period attribution, including later reversals.
- `refunds_and_fees`: reconcile ledger costs, refunds, chargebacks.
- `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * (settled net receipts - refunds - fees) / qualified human sessions` only if denominator > 0 and both sides adequately authenticated.

No email, names, full IP, device fingerprint, cross-domain personal identifiers or unconsented local storage in this artifact. DO NOT add new scripts or double-count existing Jetpack/Claude tracking. If zero observed sales is eventually verified on a complete period, say zero; presently data is missing and the rate is **NOT COMPUTABLE**. Flag tiny cohorts (e.g., under 100 eligible sessions) without causal claims. Check bot filters, multi-touch/unknowns, payment reporting lag and redirects; do not force false precision in commission attribution.

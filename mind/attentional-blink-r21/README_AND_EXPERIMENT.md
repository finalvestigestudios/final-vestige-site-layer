## Specific sourced scientific boundary

- Raymond/Shapiro/Arnell 1992 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1500880/) shows a temporary target-report deficit tied to identifying a first target during RSVP. Not an ordinary eyelid blink or a general cognitive diagnosis.
- Dux/Marois 2009 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19933555/) discusses multiple central attention/selection/report mechanisms; a one-mechanism slogan is not supported.
- Visser/Davis/Ohan 2009 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18696107/) qualifies lag-one sparing; it is not universal.
- Slagter et al. 2007 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17488185/) reports changes after **three months intensive meditation training**. It did **not** test the Zen12 recording package. Transferring that finding to an unspecified short MP3 is a category error.
- FTC Endorsement Guides (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) require clear, conspicuous disclosure of material financial relationships.
- FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) requires adequate substantiation for health-related performance claims. A different intervention cannot serve as product-specific proof.

## A vs B design hypothesis — not an experiment result

**Question:** Does an original, optional first-party forty-second explanatory film improve *comprehension and qualified progression* over an equally useful text guide, without increasing accessibility problems or mobile costs?

A = full buyer guide, study references, four real advertised audio versions, limitations, free twelve-minute exercise, disclosed **inactive** CTA. B = byte-identical substantive editorial shell plus one isolated film section with a poster, complete transcript, draft captions and hidden no-src native video.

**Hypotheses only**: B may clarify differences between research and merchant promise; alternatively the film may distract, slow the page, or deter serious readers. Do not promise uplift or treat video starts as conversions.

**Principles for a future authorized test:**
- Both arms must have equivalent headline, source text, offer disclosure, CTA destination and position relative to full guide, free alternative, device performance budget and no-JS function.
- Allow only **manual video initiation** with native controls, poster and accurate synchronized captions (English WebVTT), visible complete transcript and no autoplay/loop/muted-assumed playback. Test keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion, focus order, 200%/400% zoom, small viewport layouts and captions on actual produced file.
- Load poster as lightweight first-party asset. Lazy/non-autoload MP4: preload=none. Prefer first-party static delivery; never add a new paid SaaS or intrusive third-party player.
- If film has an actual endorsement or an affiliate CTA, disclose material relationship conspicuously **inside spoken audio AND on screen**, not solely on the surrounding webpage.
- A/B exposure assignment should use nonpersistent randomization, and the denominator must be *page presentations*, not claimed unique people; no session IDs, cookies, fingerprinting, full referrer URLs, raw search terms, UTMs, emails or IP addresses. Page reloads may yield additional exposures and arm changes: state this limitation.
- Limit future source categories to direct/organic_search/tiktok/internal/other. Any production first-party event endpoint requires a separate retention, server log redaction, CSRF/bot abuse, reliability, content security, data minimization and privacy-notice design review.
- Primary feasible metrics after activation: first-party page presentations and permitted outbound CTA clicks per variant; these are **not sales**. Affiliate conversion counts may only be used if explicitly included in authorized partner reporting. Evidence engagement (source link usage) could be tested later if legitimately justified, not inferred from passive dwell time.
- Conduct low-sample descriptive analysis without arbitrary significance thresholds; do not claim statistical advantage from tiny traffic. A formal power calculation and measurement validation are prerequisites for significance language.
- **Tracking is OFF today.** Current pilot has no live CTA, so true outbound conversion measurements cannot yet occur.

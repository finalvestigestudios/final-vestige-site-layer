## The pilot pre-sell: one high-intent proposition

**Audience:** a reader already considering a twelve-minute structured meditation audio routine and comparing paid formats—not a person being targeted with medical or wealth claims. A visitor arriving for a face illusion should receive the historical explanation before any commercial option.

**Truthful problem → insight hook:** "Before paying for twelve-minute meditation recordings, spend twelve minutes with an ordinary timer." The source-grounded micro-story is the image whose components stayed fixed while perception changed with rotation. The lesson is about interpretation, not about a soundtrack reprogramming the brain.

**Free demonstration:** single safe task, short timer, note starting condition, note completion; describe it as personal reflection, not randomized experiment. **Offer value:** seller-described 48 MP3s / 12 levels / choice of guided/nature/music/white-noise format. **Objections:** no evidence of specific brainwave outcome; format suitability; free alternatives; unconfirmed checkout price, return process, delivery/rights. **CTA status:** `<button type="button" disabled>`, no URL, event handler, proxy redirect, affiliate shortener, paid UTM or checkout tracking. Adjacent FTC disclosure: "If Final Vestige later links to an approved purchase offer, we may earn a commission..." Replace with truthful current disclosure only if legitimately authorized.

## Controlled A/B pre-registration — design hypothesis, not a result

Existing frozen R210 source-only A/B remains the control plan. The R222 A/B is an **alternative copy-review proposal**, NOT new simultaneous live arm. A and B have the SAME written buyer guidance, sources, CTA position, disclosures, offer restrictions and free resource links. B alone adds original optional 42-second film **concept**, with SVG poster, five WebVTT cues and complete text transcript. **No playable media is present or claimed**; there is no autoplay, external embed, required JS or blocking network request. An approved future actual MP4 requires media rights, reduced-motion handling, explicit tap-to-play controls, audio/caption synchronization, pause/seek, screen-reader and keyboard tests, compression, dimensions and CWV measurements. Text A remains fully useful.

**Hypothesis:** for visitors genuinely researching a paid short-audio routine, an optional evidence explanation can increase *confirmed paid purchases per qualified-human session* without decreasing trust or slowing mobile UX. Null: no meaningful difference. The film may harm conversion by distracting from terms. The experiment cannot start with no merchant permission. If started later, preregister a single pair, allocation algorithm, time window, legal consent, source mix and stopping plan *before* traffic; no selective cherry-picking of one variant. Prefer reproducible first-party bucketing only after consent/privacy review and existing scripts audit; don't create scripts in this workstream. Treat low sample sizes as directional, not significant.

**Primary outcome:** merchant-confirmed purchases or cleared affiliate commissions **after refunds/fees**, evaluated on 7- and 30-day qualified-human cohorts; do not count outbound click as a paid conversion. Supporting outcomes: offer-guide engagement, outbound-click rate (diagnostic only), confirmed Focus opt-in, paid order rate, refunds, support complaints, anonymous page weight, CWV. Guardrails: WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, zero autoplay, no unconsented identity tracking, no medical claims, non-duplication of Jetpack or analytics.

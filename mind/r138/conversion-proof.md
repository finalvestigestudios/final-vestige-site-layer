## Measurement contract (only after consent and implementation review)
Daily aggregate cohort row: date, `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, `asset_id`, approved landing slug, qualified-human sessions (method and bot-filter caveat), relevant CTA views/clicks, native confirmed Jetpack leads only if observable, merchant-reported confirmed paid orders, refunds, gross order value, actual fees, cleared net proceeds, source reliability, count unmatched orders. `net_per_1000 = 1000 * (settled_gross - refunds - merchant_fees - other_realized_costs) / qualified_human_sessions` only when both audited settlement and qualified-human denominator exist and comparable cohort alignment is verified. Otherwise **NOT_COMPUTABLE**, not zero. 7-day and 30-day cohorts, unique customers only if merchant supports privacy-safe counts. Show uncertainty and small-sample warnings rather than claiming winner from clicks.

## A/B gate
**Hypothesis:** An optional, truthful, accessible 40-second explainer *may* improve verified purchase conversion relative to equal text-only guide. Unknown, not guaranteed. Both offer pages must have identical body buying information, truthful title, exact same CTA, source links, disclosure and free alternative. Assign consenting qualified sessions using one stable privacy-respecting preapproved randomizer, not by client fingerprint. Guard no-JS default as A. Predeclare minimum usable sample, outcome of confirmed paid orders, refunds/fees, observation window and stopping rule. If low traffic or unmeasurable paid conversions: describe directional feasibility, do not declare a winner.

## Controlled A/B: identical guide vs guide plus optional original 42-second film

A: source-supported text-only presell. B: identical substantive copy with an original SVG poster, 95-word transcript and six 0–42-second WebVTT cues; optional visitor-triggered playback **only after encoding**. Current source prototype is storyboard only, not a playable video. Never autoplay, lazy-load video until action, keep no-JS access, reduced-motion behavior, captions, printable transcript and no external player.

**Primary hypothesis:** for a genuinely eligible offer with compliant merchant attribution, adding an optional short original film *might* change realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions. It is not a proven improvement. Randomize at permitted cohort level only after founder approval, real browser/security/a11y performance QA, checked rights and live seller checkout. Control for creator asset/topic intent and avoid confusing A/B with different offers. Tiny samples cannot demonstrate statistically meaningful uplift. Reject B if weight/CWV, readability, keyboard navigation or accessibility worsen, even if observed clicks rise.

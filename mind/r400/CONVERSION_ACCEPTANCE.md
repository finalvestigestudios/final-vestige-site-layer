## 3. One controlled buyer-guide experiment

**Variant A:** original no-JavaScript text guide with a free baseline task, package fact sheet, research boundary, five purchasing objections and native disabled commercial control. **Variant B:** byte-identical primary guide plus original SVG poster and a *not-yet-playable* optional 42-second storyboard with six synchronized WebVTT caption cues and visible transcript. No hidden video, external streaming, forced playback or media rights claims. The A/B hypothesis is that the film will increase *comprehension of the control-group caveat* and possibly qualified paid conversions, without damaging trust, accessibility or CWV. Hypothesis, **not** demonstrated lift.

When video becomes playable, it must be original/rights-cleared, user-activated, preload=none, with captions and transcript, reduced-motion support, a no-JS fallback and minimum extra payload. The future experiment compares **merchant-confirmed paid conversion**, net revenue and negative trust signals. It does not declare success from clickthrough alone. Use balanced exposure cohorts if qualified sessions permit; do not call tiny samples statistically significant.

Prospective disclosure appears beside the native disabled merchant control: “If Final Vestige later shares an approved purchase link and you buy through it, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” With no active link, this describes a potential material connection; recheck adjacency and clarity if an authorized destination is eventually added.

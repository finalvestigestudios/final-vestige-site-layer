Our standard

The Source Has the Last Word.

Something can be witnessed, signed and carefully recorded—and still not prove the theory used to explain it. That is why the Puységur files preserve failed predictions and uneasy questions of consent rather than turning them into promotional proof.

Every Mind article should make three distinctions visible: an individual's reported experience, a controlled finding, and a commercial seller's claim. We do not cite a general meditation study as proof that a branded MP3 relieves anxiety, improves health or guarantees concentration.

Why isn't a study about task interruptions proof of a hypnosis product?

Different interventions require different evidence. The research compared work tasks and a written return plan, not this seller's commercial audio file. Even an interesting general hypnosis study would not establish that a particular 33-minute MP3 produces guaranteed concentration.

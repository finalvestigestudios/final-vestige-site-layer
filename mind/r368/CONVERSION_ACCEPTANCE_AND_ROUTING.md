## Executive finding — a variant mismatch is a conversion blocker, not a copywriting opportunity

**Offer candidate:** Zen12 Premium for a high-intent reader whose specific problem is finding a repeatable twelve-minute guided/ambient meditation routine, not a patient seeking a treatment and not a consumer promised neuroperformance. The exact seller page describes **twelve levels, four MP3 styles per level / 48 files** and a **one-year guarantee**. The public page content accessible 2026-11-14 displays a rounded **$87** Premium offer; previous FV audit snapshots captured **$87.99**. Another Inspire3 seller page, Hypnosis Live Total Focus, has surfaced materially different public prices across different snapshot paths. These are **not proven checkout-price movements**. They are a reason for product-SKU/URL/variant custody and to suppress exact price claims until the same authorized link's checkout is verified. A refund advertisement is not an observed refund execution.

**Commerce eligibility:** Public Inspire3 affiliate portal confirms a program and the existence of issued special links, **not** FV-specific acceptance, consent to social/email channels, terms, official approved creative or a payout to FV. Targeted connected Gmail search surfaced promotional emails, not named publisher approval. The existing Focus Fieldbook is **free**, not a monetized SKU. No appropriate currently deliverable, active paid FV-owned Mind product with rights/checkout/returns was independently authenticated. Neither paid path can be enabled from current evidence.

**Stop condition for more presell page variants:** Build/maintain one testable Zen12 guide; prioritize acceptance, offer custody, checkout and verified revenue. Do not proliferate new speculative pages as if volume compensated for a missing right to earn.

## A/B causal hypothesis and acceptance

**Only meaningful comparison after authorization and production QA:** A = complete independent text buyer guide. B = *the same substantive text* plus one original **42-second optional playable video**, with a proper poster, accessible controls, accurate WebVTT captions and readable transcript. **R368 B is only an SVG poster + transcript + caption concept**, not a hosted encoded video, and therefore not yet an actual A/B treatment. Both prototypes have a native disabled purchase control; **no experiment has run**.

**Directional hypothesis, not promise:** The optional 42-second film may improve comprehension and qualified offer selection without decreasing mobile performance or misleading readers. It may also distract or increase page weight and *reduce* net revenue. Evaluate with qualified, properly consented human-session cohorts and actual merchant settlements. Never optimize for mere video plays or affiliate_click at the expense of paid orders, accessibility or trust.

**Before test:** verify rights; product-specific source and disclosure; genuine playable file with poster and captions; no autoplay; no-JS equivalent guide; 360/390/768/1440/1920 browser QA; WCAG 2.2 AA, screen reader, reduced motion, mobile Core Web Vitals, CSP/XSS and WordPress sanitization QA; staged and approved checkout with exact SKU/terms; no duplicate scripts/forms.

**Assignment:** choose a first-party server-side/approved existing integration that does not require fingerprinting or a paid vendor; avoid sequential calendar-week comparisons masquerading as randomization. Randomize eligible sessions to A/B with an appropriately consented and privacy-preserving assignment mechanism once Claude confirms existing capabilities. Report assignment approach and balance. No visitor IDs, IPs, user agents, email addresses or merchant customer information in source control.

**Stop criteria:** merchant variant mismatch, unavailable or misleading disclosure, bad checkout, returns contradiction, event/privacy loss, screen-reader failure, page-performance regression, broken captioning, inconsistent assignment or unverifiable settlement. Roll back the isolated experiment only after authorized governance; don't touch the live homepage.

## First-party measurement contract — definition, not implemented code

**Qualified human denominator:** unique eligible organic-social landing sessions, based on permitted first-party session counting under the existing consent architecture, excluding internal QA, known automation/bots and duplicative redirects where feasible. Never manufacture a denominator by treating raw views as people. Only store coarse day, first-party campaign identifiers and landing path in aggregate event reports; do not create a fingerprinting stack.

**Allowed campaign key:** `utm_source` = verified platform; `utm_medium=organic_social`; `utm_campaign=mind_choice_audio_pilot_2026_11`; `utm_content=VERIFIED_GROK_ASSET_ID` only once actual ID is proven. No PII, hidden partner tokens in GitHub, copied visitor strings, or fabricated URLs.

**Events:**
- `qualified_landing`: consent-eligible human session, campaign key, landing page, cohort day, A/B variant when actually assigned.
- `fieldbook_cta_click`: voluntary click to existing /focus/; **not a confirmed lead**.
- `confirmed_lead`: separate provider-confirmed Jetpack subscription record with lawful consent; do not assume delivery or signup merely from link or submit.
- `guide_cta_intent`: if later implemented, click on an allowed offer choice; not a transaction.
- `affiliate_click`: outbound to a *verified, active* issued earning URL via approved integration; **disabled buttons emit zero**.
- `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`: merchant/Payhip report of accepted paid transaction, with permitted anonymous reconciliation ID *kept private*.
- `affiliate_settled_commission`: independently reported and payable commission, net of adjustments; affiliate_click cannot substitute.
- `refunded_order`, `fees`, `chargeback`: documented ledger adjustments with dates and cohort linkage when legitimately possible.

**Main KPI:** `1000 × verified realized net revenue / qualified unique human sessions` for 7- and 30-day acquisition cohorts. Realized net revenue means independently received order or settled commission value minus attributed transaction fees, refunds, chargebacks and applicable variable costs, never gross theoretical merchant sale value. In affiliate case use actual paid/settled **commission**, not total buyer checkout. If denominator, payout, permissions, refunds or attribution are missing report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero.

**Secondary** (all verified): paid orders/customers per qualified human; confirmed paid conversion rate; confirmed leads and lead-to-paid conversion where privacy-safe linkage exists; refund/fee rate. Click rate, views, shares and impressions are diagnostics. Publish tiny-sample caveats, cohort denominators, currency, attribution windows, excluded bot/QA criteria, incomplete refund windows and incomplete merchant lag.

**No new script:** Claude alone owns integration using already deployed first-party measurement, subject to founder consent/release. This Mind lane supplies schema, safety tests and QA acceptance; it does not deploy.

How this archive earns trust

Every investigation should separate the reported observation, the explanation proposed by the original observer, and what later experiments can support. A published experiment is not an endorsement of a product that it never tested. Historical sources can be fascinating without proving their original mechanism. A paid recommendation is a separate decision from a scientific conclusion.

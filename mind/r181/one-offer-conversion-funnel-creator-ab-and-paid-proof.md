## Optional video A/B design: a controlled hypothesis, not a result

**A:** source-grounded text-only Zen12 Premium exact-offer guide. **B:** identical page plus an optional 42-second original film, poster, accurate captions and full transcript. Both are in `mind/r181/`. Current B includes *only a clearly labelled non-playing poster and timecoded transcript concept*, not a real MP4. Full playable video production should happen only if the commercial gate can open and founder grants release approval.

**Primary endpoint:** independently verified, fee-and-refund-adjusted **realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** (7/30-day maturity). Secondary paid order rate and verified lead-to-paid rate. Outbound offer clicks and video engagement are diagnostic only.

**Assignment:** after authorized launch, randomize consent-compliant qualified sessions between A and B at the server/edge, keeping audience, offer, copy, link, source distribution, page load and referral context otherwise equal. No duplicate analytics tags. Set experiment assignment as first-party aggregate or ephemeral anonymous session key where technically justified by consent and retention; never store raw IP, emails, full referrers or unique cross-site fingerprints. A URL query flag by itself is **not** randomization and can bias platform audiences. Do not start A/B with an inert merchant link as if it measures purchase conversion.

**Performance acceptance:** a small original compressed MP4 only after permission, `preload="none"`, poster in modern compressed format, WebVTT and visible transcript, keyboard operability, reduced-motion respect, no autoplay, lazy loading and no blocking third-party embed. No-JS A always fully readable. Target 360/390/768/1440px, 200/400% zoom, mobile VoiceOver/TalkBack, keyboard-only, 3G emulation and real-browser LCP/CLS/INP review. Current source-only HTML passes no actual browser acceptance.

**Stopping / interpretation:** no pretense that small cohorts establish significance. Pre-register the minimum viable sample/decision threshold with Growth after historical purchase baseline and expected effect size are actually known; avoid cherry picking and do not buy traffic. State 7-day cohorts provisional until refunds/settlements mature; 30-day cohorts more mature but still incomplete if refund windows remain open. Report uncertainty and confidence intervals when enough data exist. A failed or empty transaction sample is not evidence that the creative cannot work.

## First-party privacy and paid-revenue reconciliation contract (proposal only)

Allowed campaign keys: `utm_source` = actual creator platform (e.g. tiktok, youtube), `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<ACTUAL_GROK_ASSET_ID>`. Do not invent IDs or place sensitive data in UTMs. Actual platform link-placement mechanics and bio-link limitations must be documented.

**Event vocabulary, no deployed code here:** `campaign_landing` (qualified human visit as defined by QA, not raw view), `article_to_resource_click`, `confirmed_lead` (only authoritative existing Jetpack opt-in), `offer_view`, `affiliate_click` (only if actually enabled), `merchant_confirmed_paid_conversion`, `received_affiliate_commission`, `owned_paid_order`, `refund_or_chargeback`. Do not send reader email, user-entered text, full URL query beyond allowlisted UTMs, or payment details through analytics. Maintain source system references off public dashboards with restricted access.

**Reconciliation keys:** merchant order/commission ID ↔ offer ID ↔ approved campaign token if partner terms permit ↔ paid cleared timestamp ↔ settlement currency ↔ discounts/taxes/fees ↔ refund and chargeback state ↔ source attribution confidence. Distinguish purchaser count from order count; one buyer may order twice. Do not double deduct refunds if they are already netted from settlements.

**Equation:** realized net revenue = cleared receipts + actually received affiliate commissions − incremental processor/merchant fees not already reflected − refunds/chargebacks not already netted − attributable delivery/fulfillment costs. Report incremental creator production and site costs separately and optionally include them in full unit economics, not as invented totals. **Net/1,000 = 1000 × realized net / qualified human sessions**. Do not censor a negative numerator. If either verified numerator or denominator unavailable, result **NOT COMPUTABLE** (not zero). Use matching 7-day and 30-day cohorts, date of acquisition and distinct settlement status. Affiliate clicks, subscribers and checkout starts do not belong in paid-sales numerator.

**Source-of-truth hierarchy:** merchant/Payhip cleared records and actual affiliate payout data > actual paid order status > verified subscriber opt-in > consented first-party landing/CTA data > social platform clicks/views > raw pageviews. Missing attribution always reported as missing, not redistributed across Grok campaigns.

## Strict analytics contract — *no newly deployed tracking code*

Production engineering must assess the existing first-party instrumentation *before adding anything*. Track only event types already supported by privacy/consent review. Proposed schema, not a permission to collect:

1. \`landing_view\`: coarse page_slug, campaign key and validated Grok \`asset_id\`; capture only approved \`utm_source\`, \`utm_medium\`, \`utm_campaign\`, \`utm_content\`. Validate using a small ASCII allowlist and length limits (e.g., 1–64); reject excessive/unknown values; never store entire raw referrers or querystrings, user agents, IPs, emails or names in an event payload.
2. \`relevant_cta_click\`: page slug, campaign key, CTA name; only user-initiated, not button exposure or hover. A click to the existing /focus/ is a *lead intent*, not confirmed subscription.
3. \`confirmed_lead\`: only when a trusted Jetpack/Dispatch confirmation state is available under appropriate consent, aggregated by campaign; don't copy email addresses or subscriber records into separate analytics.
4. \`affiliate_click\`: only after actual approval and authorized URL. Distinct from merchant purchase. Never create an affiliate click from a disabled button.
5. \`merchant_paid_order\` or \`settled_affiliate_conversion\`: import through authenticated partner or owned-store reports, avoiding personal identifiers. Deduplicate provider transaction references in the permitted backend without exposing them publicly. Post-refund and post-fee changes must reconcile through merchant reports; not through inferred browser behavior.
6. \`realized_net_revenue\`: settled order value or actually payable commission **minus** reported refunds, payment charges and attributable fees; if one piece missing, disclose the incomplete calculation rather than substituting gross revenue.

**Attribution:** apply the actual merchant's permission/attribution terms; cross-site purchase tracking is not implied by an on-site click. Do not mint persistent per-person identifiers or unlawfully transfer consent state to an affiliate. Campaigns with unknown \`asset_id\` stay unattributed, not assigned to the most plausible video. No third-party pixels, cookies or localStorage added by this draft.

**Cohorts:** 7-day and 30-day windows of *qualified-human sessions* by genuine published Grok asset_id and utm_campaign. Document bot filtering used, exclusions and repeat-visit counting; label inability to separate bots as an unknown, not “verified traffic.” Report sessions \(N\), confirmed leads \(L\), authenticated paid orders \(P\), gross settled revenue \(G\), refunds \(R\), fees \(F\), and realized net \(G-R-F\). The KPI is **\`1000 * (G-R-F)/N\`** only when \(N>0\) and G/R/F are validated on the same cohort/currency/attribution terms. Paid conversion rate is \`P/N\`; lead-to-customer only for cohorts with linkable consented aggregated flow, otherwise not computable. State that small numbers have high uncertainty.

**Current measured result:** \`N\`, \`P\`, \`G\`, \`R\` and \`F\` are not verified for this Mind affiliate lane. **No numerical KPI, even zero, is supported.** No traffic, subscribers, affiliate clicks or video plays are being claimed as sales.

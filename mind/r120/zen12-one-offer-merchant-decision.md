## The independently reviewable value proposition

The legitimate convenience benefit is choice: a compact library of files and formats, a repeatable session length, and guidance that may appeal to someone who prefers an externally structured routine. **It is not verified medical, cognitive, neural or wealth enhancement.** A potential customer should see the actual deliverables, know a free alternative, be able to decline and still use our investigation.

Scientific counterweights:
- Ingendoh/Posny/Heine 2023 review: 14 EEG studies, 5 supportive, 8 contrary, 1 mixed; heterogeneous methods. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37205669/
- García-Argibay/Santed/Reales 2019 behavioral synthesis: 22 studies, 35 effects, pooled g≈0.45 across distinct cognitive, anxiety and pain outcomes. Does not identify the Zen12 effect or validate entrainment mechanism. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30073406/
- 2021 meditation-intervention synthesis: 25 studies, 1,439 participants, no statistically significant summary effect on attention, working memory or long-term memory; small executive-function effect. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33582570/
- 2007 Slagter intensive-training attentional-blink experiment: not a commercial audio trial. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17488185/

The reader-facing independent investigation is at mind/r120/the-beat-between-two-notes.md. Keep historical analysis and commercial decision guide separate.

## Actual buyer objections (answer plainly)

**“What am I paying for?”** Seller-described digital library of 12 levels and 4 listening styles, guides and support under applicable offer terms. What you actually get and price need exact product + checkout confirmation before selling.

**“Does it work better than a free track?”** We have no sound product-specific randomized comparison to demonstrate superiority. Free quiet time and existing free guided resources are legitimate alternatives; the free Focus Fieldbook is optional and separate.

**“Will it force my brain into meditation?”** Not an established conclusion from the reviewed systematic evidence. Avoid promising automatic mental states or therapy.

**“Is it $37 or $87.99?”** Both figures appeared on different official seller pages. Neither is a verified current, universal checkout price. Explain variant-specific pricing and let the merchant verify the actual checkout; no countdown, price-gap manipulation or unsupported discounts.

**“Can I get my money back?”** Seller advertises one year for Zen12; specific payment channel and applicable site terms govern. No independent refund history was examined.

**“Will you obtain my information?”** Seller privacy language allows sharing purchasers' personal information with affiliates; FV refuses the arrangement until a privacy-safe reporting agreement is documented. Do not claim privacy solved.

**“Have you tried the audio yourself?”** No. FV has not established independent hands-on use, audio-file receipt, blind listening tests or product performance evidence.

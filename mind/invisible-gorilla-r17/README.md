## Editorial argument
The 1999 pass-counting task is famous, but the **2010 second surprise** is the fresh editorial angle. Familiar viewers knew what to expect and saw the gorilla, yet knowledge did not make them reliably notice a curtain color change or a departing player. The 2013 radiologist test reveals the limits of inference from a constrained expert-search task; 83% missed the deliberately inserted *nonmedical* object in 24 tested radiologists, **not an 83% diagnostic mistake rate**. This complements the existing Final Vestige witness/source methodology in [Puységur's Animal Magnetism Files](https://finalvestige.com/free-resources/puysegurs-animal-magnetism-files/) without pretending to test eighteenth-century events.

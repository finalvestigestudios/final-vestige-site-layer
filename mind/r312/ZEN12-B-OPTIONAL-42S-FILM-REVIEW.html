What can twelve minutes actually buy?

Sometimes the hardest part of a task is starting. A predictable audio routine may appeal to people who prefer a guide rather than silence, but it cannot promise you a new attention span. A scientific experiment involving months of intensive meditation training cannot establish the effects of a commercial recording. Here is what Zen12 says it sells, what the sources really support, and what to ask before paying.

This is a source-based product-format guide, not a personal testimonial, a diagnosis, or an assurance of any cognitive or medical benefit.

The experiment behind the question

In 1992, Raymond, Shapiro and Arnell studied how a rapid visual stream could make a second probe harder to report soon after an initial target. Later experiments showed that a second target immediately following the first can sometimes be spared. Read the 1992 indexed paper, 2005 lag-1 study and 2007 intensive-meditation study. They do not imply that this seller's MP3s resolve attentional blink.

## Controlled candidate experiment, not a running test

The two source-controlled documents share all core buyer text and UI. **A** is a text-only guide. **B** adds exactly one original, optional 42-second storyboard with SVG poster, transcript and a WebVTT caption concept. There is *no playable encoded film*, no forced playback, no JS bundle and no external video host. An actual A/B trial cannot start until the film exists, stage/browser checks pass, affiliate access is verified and founder approves deployment. Both guides have disabled purchase buttons and disclosures immediately beside them.

**Hypothesis:** among verified, nonbot, high-intent readers with equal source/landing match, an optional original explanation video *might* improve independently confirmed paid orders relative to the same evidence-first text. This is not a forecast or guaranteed advantage; video could distract, slow mobile loading or depress purchase comprehension.

**Randomization proposal:** after a staged QA and legal/merchant review, assign eligible users to A or B with consistent first-party campaign buckets, using a privacy-safe, non-identifying random assignment. Do not identify users across devices, capture IP addresses into custom logs, or add a paid testing platform. If persistent assignments require identifiers or cookies, obtain consent/legal review; otherwise prefer a server-side or native feature-flag approach with strictly ephemeral storage. Keep source campaign composition balanced and pre-register the observation period.

**Primary endpoint:** independently reconciled net attributable FV revenue divided by qualified-human sessions, measured in seven- and thirty-day cohorts. **Secondary:** confirmed paid customers, net revenue, paid orders per qualified session, refunded orders, validated publisher-confirmed leads and verified offer-guide visits. **Diagnostics only:** plays, CTA clicks, affiliate outbound events, scrolls, pageviews, impressions and followers. An affiliate_click is an outbound action only; it is NOT a merchant purchase.

**Small sample rule:** expose raw numerators, denominators, missing data, possible selection bias and confidence intervals or uncertainty, and do not call a few orders statistically significant. Pause the test for broken tracking, uncertain merchant authorization, inaccessible controls, missing disclosure or adverse mobile performance. No post-hoc cherry-picking of variant windows.

**Performance/a11y:** both candidates must work at 360, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920 CSS pixels; screen reader navigation; one h1; keyboard focus visible; color contrast WCAG 2.2 AA; no horizontal scrolling; reduced-motion and no-JS; caption timing reviewed against *actual encoded media*; media poster/bytes and LCP independently measured. The draft's CSS/HTML source guards are NOT a browser pass. WordPress may sanitize styles or structure; do not claim it has been tested there.

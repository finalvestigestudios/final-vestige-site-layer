## 1. Exact buyer match

**Visitor intent:** Someone who has already considered a simple, repeatable *guided audio routine*, who wants to know what the Zen12 complete course actually includes, how it compares with ordinary free practice, and whether the claims warrant a purchase. This is not a medical-treatment audience, a clinical attention-disorder funnel, or a promise that an optical illusion can be cured.

**Truthful hook:** “What can twelve minutes actually buy?” It names the buyer's time constraint without asserting twelve minutes accomplishes an hour of meditation.

**Original demonstration:** A free twelve-minute task start and written observation, repeated over several days. The free Fieldbook gives seven-day worksheets, a thirty-day log and questions about audio marketing. This is a user-practical starting process, not a clinically validated attentional-blink intervention.

**Actual seller value:** Zen12 complete package claims twelve levels/forty-eight MP3 recordings. Hypnosis Live Total Focus is described separately as thirty-three minutes; Brainwave Shots Focus Shot as fifteen and thirty minutes. Format descriptions must be labeled as merchant-provided. No one in this lane has claimed to use or independently test the products.

**Objections left honestly unresolved:** whether files and devices fit the buyer; total currently payable price; exactly applicable refund terms/exclusions; whether the purchaser values guided audio above free silent practice; and whether any claim beyond a listening structure has adequate evidence. A dynamic discount and countdown are not legitimate urgency copy for FV.

## 3. Controlled A/B design without heavy pages

**A — text guide:** identical core promise, source explanation, free alternative, price/refund uncertainty, disabled merchant control and adjacent prospective commission disclosure.

**B — text plus 42-second film concept:** identical core buyer copy, plus an original SVG poster, 42-second timed script and readable transcript. The treatment is intentionally **not playable**. It contains no video file, caption track, iframe, third-party SDK or autoplay. Its current transfer/performance impact is just static HTML/SVG/CSS; measured performance has not been obtained.

**Release preconditions before a true split test:** verified publisher-specific agreement, link issued for approved channels, written checkout/refund review, original encoded rights-cleared video with synced VTT, accessible controls/visible transcript, real browser 360/390/768/1440/1920 tests, mobile performance and no-JS fallback, clear consent basis for any experiment assignment and founder approval for live release. Maintain one canonical offer; do not multiply competing landing pages or silently add tracking systems.

**A/B hypothesis:** an optional original micro-film may help already interested readers understand the offer and increase *verified net-positive paid conversion*; alternatively the simpler text guide may work better by reducing load and decision friction. Predefine 7/30-day reporting windows, avoid significance declarations on tiny samples, and pause if complaint, refund, trust or accessibility outcomes deteriorate. Both arms must show the same claim qualifications, merchant conditions and disclosure when eventually activated.

## 4. Measurement contract — sales, not synthetic clicks

- Qualified human landing sessions must come from provider-supported bot-filtered privacy-conscious analytics, not impressions, internal QA, pageviews or unknown crawler spikes.
- Attribution fields: utm_source=verified platform, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=cluster_pilot_yyyy_mm and utm_content=authentic Grok asset_id where posting/link rules allow. Never invent missing IDs or change posted links without owner approval.
- article_to_fieldbook_click is a **click**. A Jetpack confirmed signup requires provider confirmation; a website event cannot fabricate that confirmation. Do not record email addresses, browser fingerprints or raw IP addresses in the campaign ledger.
- affiliate_outbound_click is **not** a paid conversion. It should exist only after affiliate approval and an actual authorized link, and only via existing approved first-party/Jetpack analytics if possible; no duplicate scripts.
- Independently confirmed affiliate purchase requires merchant/account reporting. Report partner gross order, publisher eligible commission, reversals, fees, cleared payout and attribution confidence as separate fields.
- FV-owned confirmed paid purchase requires actual payment processor/Payhip receipt and fulfillment/refund evidence. Do not equate a shop click with a settled receipt.
- **North star:** net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1,000 × (cleared received commissions minus refunds/reversals/fees, or similarly reconciled owned-product receipts) / eligible sessions. If numerator or denominator is not authenticated, output **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0.
- Seven-day and thirty-day cohorts with explicit settlement delay. Never combine unattributed sale data with asset-specific conversion inferences. Report total paid orders, net revenue, conversion rate and leads-to-paid only when reliable denominators exist.

## 5. Regulatory / editorial firewall

The FTC Endorsement Guides require understandable, clear, conspicuous disclosure near material recommendations. “Affiliate link” alone is not enough. The proposed disclosure is adjacent to an actually disabled native button, not buried in the general footer. When a link is eventually authorized, the visible wording should remain immediately beside the CTA: “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.”

FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance requires adequate scientific support for health-benefit advertising. The 2007 Slagter attentional-blink meditation research is **not** Zen12 testing, and general brainwave or clinical improvements must not be treated as established claims. No artificial scarcity or seller countdown is borrowed. No anonymous testimonial or imagined personal use appears.

Research manuscript conclusions must remain independent of purchase opportunity. Public archives and sources remain open without signup. The free Focus Fieldbook uses one existing Jetpack Dispatch list and its existing welcome message; no new forms or messages were created.

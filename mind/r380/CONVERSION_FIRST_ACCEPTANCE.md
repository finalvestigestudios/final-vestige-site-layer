## The only controlled experiment worth preparing

A = independent text-only Zen12 Premium guide. B = exactly the same substantive buyer copy plus original lightweight SVG, a 42-second **storyboard** and six WebVTT cues. B is not a playable video. Actual encoded locally hosted file, caption alignment, keyboard controls, mobile bitrates, no-JS text fallback and poster performance must be verified separately. No VTT or transcript access requires JavaScript. No scripts, cookies, iframes, remote trackers, autoplay or signup clone in either source draft.

**Hypothesis:** A useful original optional film could help already-qualified readers understand the evidence/claim distinction before considering a verified purchase, improving genuine paid conversion; equally plausible it distracts or hurts loading and lowers conversion. Treat as a hypothesis, not a guaranteed winner.

**Allocation after all holds pass:** first-party server-side stable A/B assignment without persistent personal tracking where permitted, 50/50 per qualifying human campaign landing, preserve chosen allocation across link without creating third-party tracking. Client-side randomization, platform-detecting redirects and personal visitor profiles are not authorized.

**Primary outcome:** independently verified settled net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions; formula = 1000*(settled owned paid order receipts + settled affiliate commissions - refunds - chargebacks - processor/merchant fees) / eligible sessions. Qualified human is not the same as raw pageviews; explicit bot/internal filters require owner QA. Mark denominator missing and metric NOT COMPUTABLE where evidence is absent, not zero. Secondary: confirmed paid customers/order rate, gross/net revenue, 7/30-day cohort and lead-to-customer conversion (provider-confirmed only). Exploratory: engaged article routes, resource CTA, affiliate_outbound (NOT sale), video opt-in views. Small samples: report raw counts and uncertainty, not triumphant lift.

The pendulum lesson

In 1852 William Carpenter described suggestion influencing small muscle movements without deliberate volition. Faraday's 1853 indicator experiment showed why it matters to distinguish an involuntary movement from an outside force. A 2022 pendulum study found that "guessed" general-knowledge answers via a pendulum were approximately at chance and no better than verbal reports. Feeling an effect and establishing its cause are different questions.

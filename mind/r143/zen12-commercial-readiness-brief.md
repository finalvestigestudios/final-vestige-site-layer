## The actual audience and offer

Primary high-intent visitor: an adult who has already searched whether **Zen12 Premium** is worth purchasing, how short meditation audio works, what the exact recordings include, and whether the claims justify paying. This is distinct from an illusion-curious short-form viewer. Do **not** pretend that a viral Thatcher illusion viewer is shopping for Zen12. The bridge from curiosity to purchase is optional, and only an independently reviewed buyer guide may bridge that intent.

**Offer:** Zen12 Premium, merchant's [exact presentation](https://zen12.com/hnp), not "any meditation product." On 2026-11-05 the page describes **12 levels, four formats per level, 48 Premium MP3s**, a Quick Start Guide, manual, support, and a seller-advertised one-year refund guarantee. It separately markets a shorter Beginner product. Seller efficacy slogans ("hour in twelve minutes," "meditates for you," health, creativity and stress outcomes) are **not established as product-specific scientific results**. No buyer checkout has been authenticated. Do not quote prices on the presell; they may vary by landing condition and checkout. Do not assume refund requests are fulfilled as described.

**Intent-matched value proposition:** the user can compare an organized set of short audio choices with a free two-minute unguided practice. It is *not* a diagnosis, cure, guaranteed saving of time, or validated equivalent to conventional meditation. Original copy: "Twelve minutes is a convenient length of time. It is not, by itself, evidence of what twelve minutes can change. Here's what the recordings contain, what the independent research cannot tell us, and how to evaluate a purchase without pressure."

**Reader's five questions:** What exactly comes with Premium and not Beginner? Which listening styles are actually included? Have any independent tests validated the one-hour equivalence for this exact product? What is the final checkout total and return procedure? Will the merchant collect newsletter consent if I request its free audio? The guide must answer by marking seller statements, independent findings, or unknowns separately. Existing [R139 A](../r139/zen12-variant-a.html) and [R139 B](../r139/zen12-variant-b.html) are the canonical UNPUBLISHED buyer copy. B has only a 40-second **STORYBOARD**, not a video; do not claim playback.

## Event contract and arithmetic (engineering proposal, not deployed)

`utm_source` in {youtube,tiktok,instagram,facebook,...}; `utm_medium=organic_social`; `utm_campaign=mind_<topic>_2026_11`; `utm_content=<immutable_grok_asset_id>`. Reject empty/unknown media IDs at analysis time, preserve only allowlisted fields, and separate `campaign_landing`, `article_to_resource_click`, `confirmed_signup` (only from native confirmation record), `affiliate_outbound`, `merchant_reported_paid_order`, `merchant_reported_commission`, `settled_commission` and `refund_or_reversal`. A click is not a purchase; a purchase is not settled income.

`net_realized = actual settled commissions received - separately debited commission reversals/chargebacks - attributable network/payment fees`, using the network payout ledger and cash settlement. **Do not subtract the underlying merchant refund amount from an affiliate's commission**, and do not subtract a refund-triggered commission reversal twice when the reported settled commission is already net of it. Record merchant refunds as diagnostic context and deduct only actual unmatched debits. Never mix product-owner gross Payhip revenue with affiliate commission revenue. `net_per_1000_qualified = 1000 * net_realized / qualified_human_sessions` **only if every required component and denominator are independently available**; else return **NOT_COMPUTABLE**. Report 7/30 days, tiny-n cautions, missing values, attribution confidence and confirmed-lead-to-buyer only if consent-compliant joins exist.

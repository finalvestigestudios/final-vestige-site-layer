Hypothesis: adding a self-contained *optional* 42-second thought-piece poster and seven six-second transcript scenes could improve paid conversion for a genuinely high-intent visitor by making the research limit memorable. Equally plausible: the extra feature increases distraction and depresses sales. This is not guaranteed.

- A: complete, well-sourced, same high-intent buyer guide, free timer and Fieldbook route, paused commercial action and adjacent disclosure.
- B: identical buyer guide + an original vector poster and a seven-beat 42-second storyboard/transcript in an accessible details control; **no MP4, no audio, no playable video, no caption file rendered**. No-JS functional; does not force playback. Stage performance budget and poster size for a future release if a film is rendered.

Keep exact content/prices/offer/disclosure stable between arms and do not change other navigation during experiment.

Secondary: verified paid purchase rate, net order value, refunds and, if confirmed by provider, lead-to-customer conversion. Affiliates use merchant-provided postback/export; do not infer paid transactions from outbound clicks. Predeclare minimum sample needs and guard tiny samples; no winner claim from a handful of purchases or outbound events.
- Performance / a11y acceptance: full browser at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1440, 1920px; keyboard, visible focus, contrast, headings, text zoom, reduced motion, VoiceOver/NVDA, native no-JS open/close transcript; WCAG 2.2 AA; no layout shift, no auto-play, appropriate text alternatives, localized proof; no duplicate tracking.

1. Grok **asset_id** and validated UTM → first-party de-duplicated **qualified_human_session**.
2. Landing page opened → **landing_view** (diagnostic).
3. Relevant internal CTA used → **relevant_cta_click** (diagnostic).
4. Jetpack provider confirms direct opt-in → **confirmed_lead**, not a mere landing/thank-you query.
5. Authorized earning link tapped → **affiliate_click**, NOT a paid sale.
6. Merchant export identifies an actual paid order → **merchant_verified_paid_order**, subject to refund/chargeback reconciliation and permitted attribution.

**KPI:** realized net paid receipts / (qualified human sessions / 1,000). With no authentic vendor settlement evidence AND no reliable qualified-human denominator: **NOT COMPUTABLE, not zero**.

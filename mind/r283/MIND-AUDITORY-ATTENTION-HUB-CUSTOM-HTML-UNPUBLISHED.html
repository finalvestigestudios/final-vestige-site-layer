01 / Perception & Attention

The sound you were never asked to hear

Macdonald and Lavie's 2011 study found that when a visual judgment demanded more attention, some participants failed to report a surprise tone. Later studies tested auditory detection sensitivity directly. The finding does not mean their ears were damaged, and it does not imply that a branded audio track restores perception.

02 / History of Suggestion

When testimony becomes a theory

Before modern laboratories, witnesses described extraordinary phenomena, practitioners collected cases, and theories raced ahead of what observations could confirm. Our historical pages preserve failed predictions and questions alongside the more sensational reports.

03 / Practice & Reflection

A quiet twelve minutes can remain free.

Try a small and measurable goal: select a noncritical task, start a timer for twelve minutes, note when your attention wanders, and record what you complete. This is a reflection exercise, not a clinical intervention or a replicate of the tone studies.

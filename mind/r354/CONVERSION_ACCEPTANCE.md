Encode locally, do not add paid video software or embed external surveillance.

Randomization should happen only after valid partner approval or an independently verified owned SKU, real checkout and independent stage QA. Test the one difference (optional video) without changing pricing, offer, disclosure, lead form or main copy between groups. Pre-register primary outcome: **realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human landing sessions**, only when settlement evidence, refunds/fees and denominator are known. Secondary: merchant-confirmed order rate and confirmed lead-to-paid-customer rate. Diagnostic only: outbound CTR, video starts, scroll depth and pageviews. Tiny cohorts require uncertainty warnings; no conversion winner inferred from clicks or ambiguous referrals.

Try a free twelve-minute baseline first

Take one piece of reading or work you have been delaying. Choose a reasonable twelve-minute outcome, start a timer and work with your usual background. Each time you notice that your thoughts have wandered, simply return. At the end, write down what you completed and one thing you learned. Repeat another day under comparable circumstances.

Different tasks and days do not constitute a controlled experiment. You are looking for a practical baseline, not a neurological score. What research does—and does not—prove

Focused-attention meditation has been investigated using behavioral research and EEG/MEG. A 2025 scoping review reports some patterns alongside substantial methodological gaps. Separate mindfulness-intervention studies find a mixture of outcomes. None of those studies independently test the particular Zen12 recordings being described here.

The seller's assertion that twelve minutes can deliver the benefits of one hour of advanced meditation remains an unsubstantiated seller claim for this product. We do not adopt it, promise health or performance improvements, or suggest that someone feeling relaxed proves a neurological mechanism.

## Conversion concept and editorial rationale

**Truthful problem-to-insight hook:** Two numbers can both be displayed and only one reported under specific timing. The peer-reviewed attentional-blink literature studies a measurable temporary processing deficit; a meditation retreat studied with this task does not validate Zen12. **Value first:** explain the science, compare real study design, provide free exercises, then offer an independently characterized *listening format* to readers already interested in such a routine. **No implied medical, cognitive, financial, or time-equivalent guarantee.**

**Offer continuity:** the exact Zen12 sales page describes 12-minute sessions; About describes twelve levels/four styles; current public cart describes the full 48-MP3 set; research page discusses wider meditation/binaural topics; Inspire3 privacy presents possible calls/SMS after phone data. The presell guide uses those factual, explicitly seller-qualified descriptions, not copied sales text or a generic "brainwaves can cure you" promise. No invented pricing, refund performance, testimony, commissions, discount codes, bonuses guaranteed, product-use experience or URL. Authoritative product details and rights **must be rechecked at release**.

**A/B experimental design:** hypothesize that a helpful 40-second visual explanation improves informed reading, qualified intent or source engagement over a text-only article. Avoid conflating YouTube/TikTok visitors with direct visitors in results: assignment would eventually need a consent-approved, privacy-reviewed, consistent server-side or first-party framework. Do not create random visitor IDs or analytics until approved. At low traffic avoid significance claims. Primary ethical goal is informed decision, not misleading purchase volume. Compare organic long-tail landing traffic and source engagement before considering affiliate outbound intent; seller conversions require explicit authenticated partner reporting, not clicks.

**Visual performance:** baseline A contains no player. B contains inline compressed SVG and native HTML transcript, no JS, external fonts, autoplay, embeds, remote media or cross-domain resources. Optional playable video is held offline; if approved, use properly sized first-party H.264 with poster; verify text track caption timecodes against actual hosted player. Contrast, keyboard, mobile-width, zoom, caption and reduced-motion/auditory needs must be tested against *WordPress-sanitized staging* before publishing.

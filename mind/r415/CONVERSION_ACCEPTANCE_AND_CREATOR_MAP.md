B alone includes an optional, user-opened 42-second **silent** original typographic microfilm; no paid narration, personal product testimonial, music, external stock, third-party video player or autoplay. The sole commercial primary outcome is authorized merchant-verified paid transactions and refund-adjusted realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions. Video play, time on page, CTA clicks and provisional lead events are secondary diagnostics. Do not compare confounded Grok topics as randomized A/B groups. Tiny samples need explicit uncertainty.

## Privacy-safe first-party attribution specification, not a deployment

First-party campaign fields may be utm_source=actual_platform, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11, utm_content=REAL_PUBLISHED_ASSET_ID. Never invent an ID. Claude owns one shared event schema, not duplicate scripts or a new SaaS.

Events, with strict meanings:
- **qualified_session**: a unique, eligible, filtered first-party human session with agreed exclusions, not raw pageviews.
- **offer_page_view**: content shown, not endorsement or intent.
- **focus_cta**: link tap, not provider-confirmed lead.
- **provider_confirmed_lead**: evidence from existing Jetpack consent/confirmation and delivery; do not place subscriber email/PII in analytics.
- **affiliate_click**: permitted click to an authorized merchant earning link; not checkout or sale.
- **merchant_confirmed_order**: authoritative merchant paid-order record, net of canceled/unpaid states.
- **merchant_settled_commission**: actual affiliate settlement, not promotional EPC or click.
- **refund/fee/reversal**: deduct against correct cohort, no double counting.

KPI is 1000 × (settled owned order proceeds + settled affiliate commission − applicable fees/refunds/reversals) ÷ verified qualified-human sessions. Separate 7-day and 30-day cohorts, paid conversion rate, orders/customers, gross vs net, provider-confirmed lead-to-customer conversion and attribution coverage. With no trustworthy qualified-session denominator and no confirmed payment, **NET REVENUE / 1000 IS NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero.

Data minimization: exclude raw IP, device fingerprint, subscriber email and ad IDs from reports; no cross-site ID invention. Consent and existing scripts must be inspected by Claude prior to any event deployment. Record unknown UTMs honestly. Never infer purchases from referral spikes.

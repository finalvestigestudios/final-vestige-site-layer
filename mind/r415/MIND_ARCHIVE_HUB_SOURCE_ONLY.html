Editorial standard

A good question is more valuable than a miracle claim.

The historical record may show that a witness said something, but not that the stated mechanism was real. A controlled meditation study may support its tested intervention, but not every recording marketed with similar words. We make those distinctions before offering readers any route to commerce.

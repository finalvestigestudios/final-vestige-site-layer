## Controlled text versus playable-film experiment

Both R307 guides contain identical offer wording, evidence limitations, source notes, disabled purchase CTA and adjacent disclosure. Variant A is text only. Variant B adds only an original optional **42-second SVG poster, narration/caption timings and transcript concept**. It is NOT an encoded, playable or hosted video; no A/B traffic split or consumer result has occurred.

**Hypothesis:** when a truly original, consent-to-play, closed-captioned, lightweight 42-second explainer is available, it MAY improve independently confirmed paid conversion among actual high-intent human visitors compared with text alone. No assumed uplift. If there are no confirmed paid orders, do not replace the primary KPI with clicks.

**Production gate:** original voice/visual licensing, WebVTT and accurate transcript, self-hosted compressed media without third-party player requests, poster loaded without layout shifts, keyboard/video fallback, no-JS behavior, reduced-motion, 360/390/768/1440/1920 responsive checks, contrast/focus/semantic checks and performance/CWV. The video must not autoplay or block text or CTA. Gatekeepers: Mind copy, Claude integration, independent Site QA, founder release. Current B is storyboard-only.

**A/B controls:** register hypothesis, allocation and stop rules before launch; same buyer copy, traffic intent and promotional placements. Preserve first-party privacy: only aggregated campaign/variant/cohort assignments if supported by existing tools, avoid cross-site identities/cookies unless properly consented and terms-approved. Differences in channel mix are confounders. Small samples (few purchases/tens of sessions) cannot prove lift.

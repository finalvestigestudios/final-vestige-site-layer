## Founder short-video question: controlled hypothesis, not a result
Variant A: [complete text-only guide](ZEN12-DECISION-GUIDE-A-UNPUBLISHED.html).

Variant B: [identical purchasing guide plus original optional 42-second storyboard, poster and seven six-second transcript cues](ZEN12-DECISION-GUIDE-B-UNPUBLISHED.html).

Both contain the **same** factual buying information, free resource, disclosure and disabled CTA. Variant B currently **does not have playable hosted video**. This is an *editorial media specification*, not a fully testable video variant and not a live or statistically valid A/B experiment. An actual future original film must be explicitly produced, rights-checked, captioned, transcribed, reviewed and loaded with `preload="none"`, optional user play and no forced audio/video; it should still work when scripting is disabled. No licensed seller creative or offsite video embed may be assumed.

**Hypothesis:** Among comparable, qualified-human visitors with the same offer and terms, an optional original short explanatory film could improve *verified paid conversion* versus the identical text-only guide. A neutral or worse result is just as plausible.

**Experiment design after all approvals:** use a privacy-safe server-side assignment or a Claude-approved existing campaign mechanism; do not create another analytics provider. Compare randomized cohorts on the same campaign and landing intent, ensure a persistent assignment if privacy-safe, exclude internal/automation traffic using documented rules, record exclusions, analyze 7-day and 30-day windows, and do not draw conclusions while refunds/chargebacks or commissions are unresolved. Primary KPI: realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions. Secondary: verified paid conversion rate, completed orders, lead-to-customer conversion where a provider-confirmed lead actually exists, net revenue per order and refund rate. Diagnostic: CTA clicks and video engagement. Report confidence intervals or descriptive uncertainty if samples are tiny; do not backfill visitor identity or silently treat missing data as zero.

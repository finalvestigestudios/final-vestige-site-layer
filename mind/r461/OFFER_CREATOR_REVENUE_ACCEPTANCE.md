## First-party privacy and checkout reconciliation contract
The only permitted proposed campaign variables are `utm_source=tiktok|youtube`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_evidence_2026_11` and `utm_content=<verified_media_asset_id>`. Use existing platform/natively supported first-party analytics after consent and implementation approval, no new tracking pixels, fingerprinting, hashed emails or cross-device joins. Normalize/sanitize `asset_id` as a bounded non-PII identifier; unknown/botlike sessions are flagged or excluded by a documented quality rule, not quietly reported as human.

Distinguish:
1. `qualified_human_session` — one deduplicated, policy-defined human eligible session with associated campaign and article.
2. `article_landing` — specific matched route/view, **not** lead or order.
3. `relevant_cta_click` — voluntary internal navigation or optional approved commercial outbound, clearly typed; **not** a sale.
4. `confirmed_jetpack_lead` — only if the *existing* Jetpack confirmation state can independently prove a real signup. Submitted email field is not proof.
5. `approved_affiliate_outbound` — a click on a verified merchant-authorized URL, once enabled and disclosed; still not merchant acceptance.
6. `merchant_paid_conversion` — exact transaction/sale *reported by independent merchant records*, reconciled and deduplicated; do not conflate with click, cart or checkout start.
7. `refund_or_chargeback` and `fees` — negative adjustments linked to verified sales without exposing buyer PII.
8. `realized_net_revenue` — net settlement after refunds and fees, with clear currency/period.

**Formula:** `1000 * realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`, ONLY when both numerator and denominator are independently established for the same attribution cohort. Otherwise report `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, not `$0`. Use 7-day and 30-day cohorts and identify unmatched merchant sales as unattributed, not automatically Grok-origin sales.

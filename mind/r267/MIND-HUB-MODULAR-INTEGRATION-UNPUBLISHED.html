A useful test: the white bear

In a 1987 experiment, people asked to suppress an image of a white bear had difficulty keeping it from their thoughts. Later outcomes depended in part on the task and the alternative instruction. This laboratory effect does not demonstrate that an MP3 cures intrusive thinking.

Examine the original study. Our new full investigation remains unpublished; do not route social traffic to a nonexistent URL.

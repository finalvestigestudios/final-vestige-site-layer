## Independent conversion copy and objections
**High-intent audience**: people who specifically want a structured 12-minute audio routine, are considering Zen12 Premium, and want to compare product format, evidence and return terms. Do not target people with clinical anxiety or promise treatment.

**Problem → insight**: “The sound of a familiar word can become strange after repetition. That tells us something about perception, not that a product rewires your brain. Before paying for twelve-minute recordings, try twelve minutes with a plain timer.” The buying guide is separate from the original article's conclusions. Its free demonstration is useful even if the reader never buys. The official seller's selling proposition is convenient, multiple-format, short MP3 sessions. Typical objections covered: individual suitability, clinical claims, final checkout charge, audible format preferences, refunds, and no-fee free alternatives.

**Specific CTA under hold**: text reads “Zen12 buying link withheld pending authorization.” It must be a real `disabled` HTML button, without `href` or JS handler. Adjacent notice in both A and B is a clear prospective earning disclosure. At release, if and only if merchant approval is verified, replace it with an actual permitted `<a rel="sponsored nofollow" ...>` to the publisher-issued URL; keep adjacent current-tense FTC disclosure. Never cloak, fabricate or track redirects without explicit permission.

## Measurement contract and controlled A/B
- **First-party source:** `utm_source` platform, `utm_medium` recognized social medium, `utm_campaign` approved campaign identifier, `utm_content` only the reconciled Grok asset_id. For a generic channel profile, use a distinct `channel_profile` label and do not credit a particular Short. Avoid user IDs, emails, fingerprinting, cross-domain user stitching and duplicate scripts. Respect user consent, existing analytics/privacy policy and merchant policies.
- **Human funnel (distinct evidence):** qualified session → relevant article visit → Focus CTA click → Jetpack confirmed subscription where applicable → authorized merchant click (`affiliate_click`, not a sale) → **merchant-confirmed paid purchase / cleared affiliate commission** → refunds and fees → realized net. Never fill a missing order ID with outbound click volume.
- **Primary KPI:** `1000 × (settled gross receipts or cleared commissions − refunds − attributable fees) / qualified-human sessions` for exact 7- and 30-day source cohorts. Keep direct-store orders and affiliate commissions in distinct ledgers, avoid double counting. Report paid conversion rate and verified lead-to-customer conversion with sample size and uncertainty. Missing merchant or session data means **NOT COMPUTABLE**.
- **A/B hypothesis:** Optional, original 42-second contextual video could help *informed buyers* understand the difference between evidence and the seller's convenience proposition; it also could distract and hurt speed/conversion. Variant A uses the full text guide; B has **identical core copy and purchasing/disclosure block** plus a static poster and optional accessible film if one is actually produced. No autoplay and no video bytes requested on text-only/no-JS page. Allocate only after founder approves experimental deployment and account rights; consent-safe, stable first-party assignment. Do not treat clicks or completion as commercial outcomes. Stop/pause for disclosure/accessibility regressions or a verified rise in refund-related complaints. At tiny order counts, report inconclusive rather than winners.

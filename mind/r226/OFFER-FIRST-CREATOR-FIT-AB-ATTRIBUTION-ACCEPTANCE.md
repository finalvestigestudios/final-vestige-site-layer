## One experiment, not an uncontrolled pile of variants

Existing R210, R223 and R225 candidates stay untouched and **unrun**. R226 is a consolidation **review candidate** to select or reject, not a newly launched experiment.

- **Control A:** original Zen12 buyer guide, all substantive text, sources, free exercise, offer objections, adjacent disclosure and disabled CTA.
- **Treatment B:** the *same* guide with one optional static-original film **concept** in a native <details> region. Its 42-second storyboard is visible, captions in VTT source, and full text transcript is provided. There is no video source, no MP4, no video playback, no autoplay, no remote embed, no third-party requests and no forced narrative interruption. The two variants are independently useful even without JavaScript.
- **Hypothesis:** only after real video rendering, accessible captions/audio and authorized destination are verified, the optional guided visual *might* help some highly interested readers understand the difference between format value and efficacy claims, with no unacceptable impact on Lighthouse/CWV. We do **not** assert the film converts better.
- **Primary measured outcome after release:** independently merchant-reported paid orders/cleared affiliate commissions, adjusted for refunds/reversals and platform fees, as net revenue per 1,000 verified qualified-human sessions. Secondary: provider-confirmed leads and intent-qualified CTA clicks; tertiary: page views/time and voluntary film play.
- **Design:** consistent organic campaign and reader intent to both arms; privacy-reviewed first-party random session assignment with documented deduplication. If such assignment is unavailable, compare only descriptive cohorts and **do not** claim causal uplift. Lock a preregistered 7-day and 30-day window, count all assigned qualified sessions, analyze intent-to-treat and state confidence intervals/very small n warnings. No unapproved pixel, cookie, experimentation SaaS or script.
- **Quality gates:** keyboard-only + screen reader semantics, actual 360/390/768/1440/1920 browser layouts, touch targets, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, reduced-motion/no-JS; zero focus traps; poster meaningful alt via SVG title/desc; caption/transcript complete and in sync with rendered MP4 if ever rendered; no autoplay; preserve full content before film for both. Test performance on a real throttled mobile environment; set **no performance PASS** until such measurements are executed.
- **Security gates:** no active earning destination, no cloaking/redirect, no unescaped query reflection, script or inline handlers, no extra form, no duplicate tracking, no PII in outbound events. Source-level checks pass separately from browser/merchant QA.

## 5. Controlled A/B pilot: text versus *real* optional film

**Hypothesis only:** Among eligible high-intent human visitors who voluntarily reach a verified Zen12 buyer guide, adding an original **optional 30–45 second** quiet film may improve actual **verified paid conversion** compared with the same complete text-only evidence guide. It may also reduce performance and must therefore be tested; no guarantee.

- **A:** Existing R284 text-only guide, all facts, alternatives, limits, FTC notice, product comparisons identical.
- **B:** Same guide, only extra optional playable 42-second original short with static poster, caption file, transcript visible without JS, no autoplay and no external player/pixels.
- **Before test:** prove merchant authorization, exact link and allowed media channels, seller price/returns, rights to every frame/audio element, 360/390/768/1440/1920 responsive and WCAG 2.2 AA keyboard/screenreader/no-JS validation, no new tracking scripts, consent/review.
- **Allocation:** comparable eligible sessions assigned once, repeat visitors stable only where privacy/consent permits; avoid cross-platform confounding; predeclare exclusions and 7/30-day purchase reconciliation windows. No additional analytics vendor.
- **Primary outcome:** net revenue per 1,000 *qualified-human* eligible sessions; secondary verified purchase rate, new paid customers, refund-adjusted gross/net. **Do not optimize for affiliate_outbound** or declare a winner on click-through alone.
- **Guardrails:** no more than one subscriber list; supplier newsletter consent separate; no medical claim; no fake urgency; no coercive autoplay; no invented SEO results; disclose financial connection next to the live link.
- **Stopping:** do not infer significance from tiny cohorts or zero observed merchant payments where reports are missing. Report uncertainty and data gaps, not a false victory. Delay or abort if layout shifts, CWV damage, degraded mobile a11y, missing refund reconciliation or material claim drift.

The B prototype remains **STATIC storyboard, NOT a film** and cannot pass live A/B readiness today.

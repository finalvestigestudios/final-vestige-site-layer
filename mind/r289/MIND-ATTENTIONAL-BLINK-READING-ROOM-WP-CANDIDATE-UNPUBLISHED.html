The Second Number Vanished.

01 / Evidence, not illusion

A missed number is not a broken mind.

7 · 4Static teaching image — not a timed scientific experiment

In a rapid sequence, identifying a first target can reduce detection of a second target that follows soon after. Researchers compare short and long separations; simply missing an item is not itself proof of an attentional blink.

Raymond and colleagues reported this deficit in 1992. A 2007 experiment found a smaller blink after three months of intensive meditation training, while a 2021 comparison found no evidence of differences in blink magnitude after brief meditation conditions. Neither experiment tested Zen12.

Is Zen12's format worth buying? The science cannot make that choice for you.

Zen12's official checkout describes 12 levels and 48 MP3s. That may be useful to a listener wanting a downloadable, structured routine. Its short-session equivalence claim is a seller assertion, not a conclusion established by the attentional-blink literature.

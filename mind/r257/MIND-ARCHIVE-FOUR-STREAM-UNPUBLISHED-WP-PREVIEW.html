A two-minute field observation

A claim, an observation, an explanation.

Think of something you noticed while distracted. Write the sensory observation first (what you saw or heard), your interpretation second, and one alternative explanation third. Then ask: what evidence could distinguish them? Nothing about this exercise claims to train or diagnose your brain.

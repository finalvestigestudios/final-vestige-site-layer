## Checkout-friction findings and required verification

1. **Offer path discontinuity.** Different official Zen12 routes present different offer framing and price/coupon text. Check one authorized referral path with written permission. Preserve exact currency, total, included files, tax or other applicable costs as an observation of *that* checkout only. Do not copy a countdown into FV copy.
2. **Information collection before payment.** The public order route visibly includes email and mobile telephone fields. This does not prove every field is mandatory. Before activation, test the actual referred flow (without entering personal details in a shared ticket) and verify which data are required, merchant privacy policy, payment processor and checkout redirects. **Do not create a proxy checkout.**
3. **Returns and support.** The order text claims a guarantee and support. Obtain current conditions: eligibility, window, exceptions, refund contact and any effect of promotion/referral. Avoid implying firsthand refund success.
4. **Mismatch between research and merchant claims.** A user arriving for an evidence review should not be redirected to an unsupported brainwave guarantee. Show actual product description and clear limitations first, free alternative second, and only later a properly disclosed optional merchant recommendation if verified.
5. **Trust and accessibility.** No forced video; no dark-pattern consent; no auto-subscribe; all product decisions remain available in text; focus indicators and tap targets require real browser and screen reader QA.
6. **Payout and customer reconciliation.** Merchant checkout and partner reporting must establish an order or settled commission; first-party outbound click is only an intermediate event, never proof of a sale.

**Suggested fields for existing Claude-owned event contract (not a new script):** anonymous campaign source, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11, utm_content=**real** Grok asset_id only, article_route, offer_id, variant, event_type, UTC timestamp, deduplicated anonymous consent-state where lawful. No personally identifying data, purchase payloads or opaque fingerprinting. Do not fabricate media IDs or cross-app user identities.

**Event semantics and revenue math:**
- `campaign_landing`: observed first-party route hit, unqualified until filtered.
- `qualified_human_session`: deduplicated and bot/editor-excluded eligible session per documented rules.
- `focus_cta_click`: link activation only.
- `provider_confirmed_lead`: Jetpack authoritative opt-in/confirmation, only if reliably observable without PII duplication.
- `affiliate_click`: authorized outbound interaction; **not** purchase.
- `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`: authoritative paid order, not abandoned/unpaid checkout.
- `merchant_settled_commission`: independently settled affiliate earning, not advertised EPC or estimated payout.
- `refund_or_reversal`, `merchant_fees`: correct-cohort adjustments.
- `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified` = 1000 × (settled FV-attributable receipts minus applicable refunds, reversals, fees, and transaction direct costs) / eligible deduplicated qualified-human sessions. Undefined if denominator, attribution, or receipts missing. Treat unsettled pending earnings separately. Include 7/30-day cohort date range and tiny-sample warning.

## Better copywriting — problem, evidence, decision

**Audience:** people who cannot create a daily hour of quiet and would consider a short structured audio habit. **Problem:** realistic scheduling, not manufactured anxiety about being “broken.” **Insight:** the twelve-minute file length is verifiable; “an hour in twelve minutes” is not a demonstrated Zen12 outcome.

**Original micro-story:** the investigation opens with an archivist comparing an answer sheet to the original word list. The sales page opens with a fragmented day. Neither is a customer testimonial or claim that Zen12 was used.

**Demonstration:** the guide supplies a free three-occasion preference check. It cannot prove health or brain effects. The research article supplies a separate original word-list observation, explicitly not a validated clinical test.

**Offer value:** seller-described program architecture and available bundle configurations, with caveats. **Limitations:** no validated hour equivalence, product-specific health or attention benefits; privacy and SMS/purchaser sharing conditions; incomplete terms.

**Objections addressed:** exact contents, learned skill vs passive audio, evidence quality, use case, data and SMS, refunds, free alternative. **CTA:** visually distinct but native disabled button; no URL, with a human-readable prospective commission disclosure in same checkout aside.

## A/B definition that could actually be evaluated later

- **A / text**: full seller-aligned, independent evidence and decision guide.
- **B / optional film**: precisely identical substantive guide plus one self-contained SVG poster and full six-cue transcript. No JS, external video request or autoplay. The difference is exactly one conceptual film figure (and a technical variant label).
- **Primary hypothesis after approval:** among eligible guide views, B may cause a different rate of *approved offer-intent actions* than A. Direction and effect unknown.
- **Publication experiment requires** first-party MP4/VTT hosting and a verified, accessible opt-in play mechanism. Until then, B is not a genuine playable-video test; do not report conversion performance.
- **Suggested measurement** once privacy and release gates pass: anonymous server-side counts in coarse categorical dimensions `source_bucket`, `topic`, `variant`, `event`; no raw URL, UTM, IP, email, user ID or cookie. At least twenty observations in a disclosed aggregate cell. Consider aggregate day or week rather than raw per-hit timestamps. Disclose cookies/data usage if actual deployed implementation differs. Don't infer purchases from outbound clicks; only approved aggregated merchant reporting could support sales claims.
- **A/B discipline**: identical headlines and core text, no misleading scarcity, avoid changing CTA position across arms, accessibility parity, compare eligible traffic not just clicks, guard against bot contamination and low denominators, no significance claims from sparse data.

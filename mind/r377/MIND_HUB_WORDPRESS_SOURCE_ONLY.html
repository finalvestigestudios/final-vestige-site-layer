Our method

An extraordinary explanation still needs ordinary evidence.

In a laboratory study, report exactly what was counted, who was tested, and what remained unmeasured. In the historical archive, separate what a witness wrote from what caused the event. In a product review, distinguish format and seller copy from independently established efficacy.

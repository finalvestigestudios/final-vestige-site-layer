## Executive decision: do not optimize an unpayable funnel

**Audience hypothesis:** people who watched a source-accurate short about selective attention and are searching for a realistic *structured short audio practice*, not a cure or guaranteed concentration boost. The relevant offer candidate is **Zen12 Premium**, precisely as described on the official merchant site. This is a hypothesis, not observed purchase intent.

**Partner verification on this run:** https://affiliates.inspire3.com/ is a public signup portal. A targeted Gmail search for a named affiliate acceptance, approved publisher, tracking link, and account terms found no persuasive approval evidence in the inspected results. **Negative email search is not proof that approval does not exist.** Public signup terms, claimed payout percentage and EPC cannot establish *Final Vestige's* authorized commercial relationship. The accountable source of truth is authenticated publisher dashboard, partner-specific program agreement and exact issued link, including allowed website, YouTube/TikTok and email usage. No credentials belong in the issue.

**FV-owned alternative:** the publicly viewable https://finalvestige.com/focus/ is an existing **free** 52-page lead magnet, not a seller-authenticated paid product. A targeted mail check found no relevant FV-owned paid-SKU receipt or merchant authorization. This is **NOT VERIFIED**, not a claim there are none. A valid alternative would require signed ownership/licensing, active FV seller account, current public offer and price, checkout test, download fulfillment, refunds, taxes, fees and transaction exports. Do not divert users to a third-party brand's Payhip listing merely because it appears saleable.

**Public exact product-page check, November 14, 2026:** https://zen12.com/ lists Premium: twelve levels; 48 MP3s; quick-start, manual, support/bonus description; public promotional $87.99 and merchant-advertised one-year guarantee. These are seller speech and publicly rendered price, **not authenticated final charge, actual delivery or honored refund**. The scientific equivalent of one hour of meditation in twelve minutes is a seller efficacy claim; no independent product-specific proof was established. No published buyer copy promises the numeric price or effectiveness. Do not use the public portal's general commission percentages in copy.

**Decision:** HOLD Zen12 merchant CTA. If the owner provides a verified payable FV digital product first, revise the presell to the exact authentic product instead of quietly presenting the Focus freebie as merchandise.

## Pre-registered comparison: informative text versus informative text + optional short film

- **Variant A:** complete evidence-based buyer guide, free demonstration, product contents, limitations, questions, disabled merchant CTA.
- **Variant B:** exact same guide copy and CTA, plus an **original**, on-request 42-second narrative element. Currently source-controlled poster, timed WebVTT and full written transcript are only a production concept. No encoded video, external player, autoplay or forced playback. Do not report this as a finished video or a tested playback experience.
- **Hold unchanged:** same title, disclosures, copy, navigation, source citations, primary CTA, link behavior and form policy. The single independent variable is the optional video panel when production is approved.
- **Pre-register before allocation:** unit = consent-eligible, qualified human first-entry session. Consistent bucketing, documented decision date, bot exclusions, no cross-device fingerprinting, accessible no-JS deterministic variant assignment by server if permissible, defined campaign and 7/30-day cohort windows. Do not create or deploy a duplicate analytics script.
- **Primary outcome:** **realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions**, computed using independently confirmed merchant orders/affiliate settlement less refunds and fees. If partner-attributed orders or human-session denominators cannot be linked lawfully, state NOT COMPUTABLE, not zero.
- **Supporting outcomes:** verified paying customers, paid conversion rate, merchant-confirmed orders, qualified Focus leads (provider-confirmed only), lead-to-paid conversion if permitted, time on page and CTA clicks as diagnostics only. Do not declare winner based on clicks alone.
- **Sample integrity:** report both 7/30-day cohorts, raw numerators and denominators, confidence level, attrition/missing matching rates, per-variant breakdown and large sampling uncertainty. No significance claim without a defensible inference plan; avoid repeated optional stopping and post hoc segment mining.
- **Hypothesis H1, not a conclusion:** optional original video may improve paid buyer confidence compared with source-only text. H0: no paid-conversion improvement. Possible harm: more data transfer, distraction, accessibility friction or increased bounces. Only signed-off real paid outcomes determine whether test succeeds.
- **Performance budget:** poster under 40 kB, VTT under 5 kB, MP4 only loaded on user intent with preload=none and poster visible in no-JS state; target film under 1 MB with 42-second duration and no third-party domains; native controls and fallback transcript. Measure actual sizes and LCP/CLS on 360/390/768/1440 before release. Targets are not test results.

## Privacy-safe attribution contract for Claude implementation review (not deployed)

Campaign link contract: `utm_source=youtube|tiktok`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_attention_2026_11`, `utm_content=<immutable_platform_asset_id>`. Never invent media IDs. Every link must first be verified accessible in the platform's actual mobile UI; use approved bio link where in-caption links are not clickable.

Events and permitted meaning:

1. `qualified_human_landing`: approved, aggregated session associated with an authenticated asset identifier and existing site's consent standards; exclude bots/internal QA with documented methodology.
2. `article_to_focus_click`: direct existing free resource click; **NOT** a lead.
3. `provider_confirmed_focus_lead`: Jetpack confirmation as permitted without exporting private subscriber identity; record only where provider data can support.
4. `presell_offer_click` / `affiliate_outbound`: click to authorized merchant after later approval; **NOT** purchase or commission.
5. `merchant_confirmed_paid_order` / `partner_confirmed_paid_conversion`: independently issued merchant/partner report, transaction state, currency and refund data.
6. `realized_net_revenue`: finance reconciliation after settled fees and refunds; no revenue inferred from outbound counts.

Do not put emails, IPs, device fingerprints, private subscriber IDs or referral identities into UTM fields or public issues. Do not silently collect consent signals. Cookie-free aggregated measurement where native systems support it; if joining sessions to merchant sales is not supported, report attributable revenue and unlinked revenue separately rather than fabricating the match.

**KPI formula:** 1,000 × (gross settled eligible receipts or commissions – refunds – partner/store fees – other directly attributable transaction costs) / qualified human sessions. Define accounting currency and recognition window before computation. Missing denominator or payable outcome = **NOT COMPUTABLE**. For 7/30-day cohorts, also report unlinked sales distinctly.

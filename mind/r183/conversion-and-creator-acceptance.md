## Target audience and precise offer
Audience: a reader who already wants short, structured **meditation audio** and is weighing paid MP3 formats against free tracks. Do not pitch to someone merely amused by an illusion; that is low commercial intent.
Offer value attributable to seller listing: twelve levels, forty-eight MP3 files, four formats/level, quick-start/manual and advertised extras. No claim that the program treats medical conditions or outperforms a timer; no unsupported testimonial, urgency, final checkout total or seller-derived equivalence.
Objections: “I already have free audio”; “what do the levels provide?”; “is the method proven?”; “what actually happens at checkout/refund?” Answer with verifiable features and explicit limits; show a free routine.

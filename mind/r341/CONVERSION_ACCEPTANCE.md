## Accessibility, privacy and security
- No JS, third-party tracking scripts, new form, email data transmission, hidden pixels, autoplay, live merchant URL, bogus discount or countdown.
- Reference-only source links may point to studies; no earning URL exists.
- The B element is a **storyboard plus transcript**: don't call it playable. The previously source-controlled R340 FFmpeg builder has produced an offline video but is not hosted or embedded here; attach only after media rights/quality tests.
- Full source content present with JS disabled. Keyboard-visible focus ring. Native disabled button cannot activate. No inline event handlers.
- Browser widths: 320/360/390/768/1440/1920, 200% text, screen readers, Firefox/Safari, caption timing, reduced motion, WP sanitizer and CSP must be independently tested prior to release.
- All assets authored original. No copied affiliate creatives or vendor audio.

## Data-quality acceptance
Record only opt-in campaign parameters available through approved first-party providers, never raw PII. A qualified-human session must exclude known synthetic/bots without silently assuming every visit is human. Do not sum campaign sessions to infer distinct users. Never equate an affiliate_outbound, signup, or cart visit to a completed paid order. For each 7/30-day cohort, record source match rate, observed qualified denominator, confirmed orders, refunds, fees and net revenue; label tiny samples. Net revenue/1,000 = independently settled attributable net * 1000 / qualified sessions, not computable when missing.

## Required release signoff
Authenticated account-level Inspire3 approval, issued tracked offer URL, permitted platform and email channels, claim/term verification including precise purchase/returns, independent full-browser and WP QA, founder production release, provider-backed revenue reconciliation. Nothing in this draft authorizes deployment or publication.

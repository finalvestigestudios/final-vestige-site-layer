How we distinguish knowing from believing

Every claim should have an identifiable source, a documented limit and an honest statement about what was not tested. Our editorial standards apply to commercial guides as much as they apply to historical investigations. An experiment that shows someone missed a change does not show a product will improve cognition. A documented eighteenth-century testimony does not establish magnetic energy. That boundary protects readers and the archive.

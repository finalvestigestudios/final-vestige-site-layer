## 4. Privacy-safe measurement contract — event != outcome

| Event / status | Countable when | Must NOT be represented as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `qualified_campaign_landing` | Independently checked platform asset id, genuine consent-compliant first-party visit, bot/internal/QA excluded where feasible | verified person/purchase |
| `article_to_focus_click` | A relevant live on-site CTA actually activated and existing analytics supports detection | email subscriber |
| `provider_confirmed_lead` | Existing Jetpack provider confirms real confirmed subscriber with proper consent and dedupe; do not create another form | buyer |
| `offer_guide_view` | Eligible approved landing page actually seen, privacy-safe cohort assignment | offer demand proof |
| `affiliate_outbound` | Actual click on a valid approved earning URL after release only | sale or commission |
| `merchant_confirmed_affiliate_sale` | Partner-reported paid order/commission matched to valid terms and adjustments | settlement before cleared |
| `fv_confirmed_paid_order` | FV-owned checkout confirms actual paid, rights-cleared delivered SKU | realized net before refunds/fees |
| `settled_net_revenue` | Verified attributable payout less attributable refunds, chargebacks, fees and merchant adjustments | gross order value |

No PII, email addresses, device fingerprints, raw IP address exports, secret tokens, cookies used for covert cross-site tracking, or duplicated measurement scripts. Allow `unknown` source and missing attribution rather than imputation. Store only sparse first-party approved campaign counters where permitted, with documented retention. Respect consent signals, platform rules, FTC disclosure and existing site analytics owner. Engineering owner Claude determines whether current Jetpack/analytics can capture events without adding JS. Do not implement new instrumentation in this PR.

**Formula:** `net_revenue_per_1000 = 1000 * (settled_attributable_revenue - refunds - fees - clawbacks) / qualified_human_sessions`. For affiliate channel the numerator is settled commission actually earned, not the merchant's gross cart. For direct FV sales, numerator is FV receipts less merchant fees, returns and other attributable variable costs if known. Do not divide when denominator is missing, nonhuman or unverified. Report 7- and 30-day date-of-acquisition cohorts, paid conversion %, confirmed orders/customers, verified gross/settled amounts, lead-to-customer ratio only with linkable consent-permitted records, missingness and confidence flags. Small samples are descriptive only.

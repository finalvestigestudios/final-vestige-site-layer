## 5. Privacy-safe attribution contract (spec only, no deployed scripts)

Permitted destination URL with `utm_source=<platform>`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<immutable_asset_id>`. Never fabricate asset IDs; if platform URL control is restricted, record bio-link routing explicitly and mark attribution confidence low.

Event definitions, only where consent and actual instrumentation permit:
- `qualified_human_landing`: unique session after documented bot/internal/test exclusions; do not treat Jetpack view counts as this number.
- `article_to_resource_click`: user-initiated click to existing /focus/; inert `data-event` attribute in draft is **not** installed analytics.
- `confirmed_signup`: provider-confirmed opt-in, with no email address or private subscriber ID in campaign reporting. Do not infer from PDF opens.
- `affiliate_click`: **future only** properly authorized outbound event, not a lead or commission.
- `merchant_confirmed_paid_conversion`: payment/commission independently accepted by merchant, with reconciliation key permitted by partner; not a checkout start, CTA click or attributed visit.
- `realized_net_revenue`: settled merchant proceeds / accepted paid commission minus refunds, chargebacks and directly attributable fees in consistent accounting window. Unsettled estimates segregated.

Collect no raw email, IP address, full user-agent fingerprint, arbitrary browser identifiers or cross-device identity stitching for this plan. Provide opt-out/consent review, aggregation, retention limits and lossiness flags; do not silently connect a subscriber to merchant purchase without legal/contractual basis.

## Controlled text vs optional original film experiment (pre-registered, not yet launched)

**Hypothesis:** For highly qualified users reading an evidence-first Zen12 Premium buying guide, an original optional forty-second visual explanation may improve understanding and verified paid purchase conversion relative to the identical text-only guide. Plausible mechanisms include clarification and dwell, but it may instead distract, slow load, harm accessibility or reduce conversion.

**Eligibility:** Only lawful, consent-appropriate, human-qualified sessions reaching the same SKU intent page; use an approved first-party variant flag maintained by Claude's *existing* analytics machinery, not duplicate scripts. Use stable random assignment, stickiness for repeat sessions, 1:1 distribution, consistent offer price/disclosure/CTA wording, and consistent mobile performance. Do not persist sensitive data or use invasive fingerprints. Do not silently apply version B to other editorials. An experiment should not start while the merchant CTA is disabled, because no paid endpoint exists.

**Primary endpoint:** independently verified paid transactions and *net settled revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions* by variant within 7-day and 30-day cohorts. Secondary diagnostics: actual qualified landing sessions, CTA clicks, confirmed Focus leads if applicable, verified checkout starts where visible, form consent, price/returns comprehension, abandonment and cross-device a11y. A click is NOT a transaction, and a lead is NOT a customer.

**No false certainty:** Avoid significance claims on tiny samples. Show denominators, confidence intervals or simply mark as descriptive/pilot when low. Explicitly report selection bias, missing cross-domain attribution and unattributed orders. The more persuasive video is not necessarily the economically better one when production/hosting cost and page speed are included. No video player or auto-play may be introduced unless rights, captions, transcript, poster, responsive source, reduced-motion, no-JS and performance gates pass. The current B has storyboard text only, not a playable asset and thus cannot measure real video lift.

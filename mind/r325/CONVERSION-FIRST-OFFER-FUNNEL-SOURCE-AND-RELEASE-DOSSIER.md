The scientific article evaluates a contested memory finding, not whether focus audio works. It cites original research from 2006 and 2011, conflicting results from 2021, and a 2024 visual working-memory result. It treats Puységur testimony as a historical claim trail, not as proof that animal magnetism or hypnosis accomplishes anything. The new investigation's domain is real-world memory interpretation, event segmentation and limitations of recall measures.

The Zen12 buyer guide explains four product listening styles and limitations; it does not copy seller swipe language, fake a first-person review, quote generic affiliate percentage as FV's entitlement, or promise treatment/wealth/brainwave benefits. It states its promotional price as dated and requires re-verification. The affiliate disclosure is beside the **inactive** purchase control and must be revisited for each eventual live outbound link.

Per FTC, a broad affiliate disclosure page is insufficient for a separated recommendation and link; disclosures should be clear and near the link. Health/mental-performance claims must not rely on studies of untested products or substitute a generic disclaimer for actual substantiation.

## 2. A real buyer, an honest reason to continue

**High-intent person:** adult reader who already has difficulty maintaining a short contemplative routine, has independently decided to compare recorded meditation formats, and wants to know precisely what is included, whether a free approach is sufficient, and what the refund/communication terms mean.

**Do not confuse with:** reader fascinated by an invisible gorilla, moral attitude reversal, hypnosis archives, or an optical illusion. Those readers came for an explanation. Their relevant next step is the free investigation, other primary sources, or optionally the existing Fieldbook. A seller recommendation on a neutral historical investigation would undermine the editorial promise and may depress trust even if the click count rose.

**Value sentence permitted for future approved guide:** "Zen12 is marketed as a library of short, numbered meditation audio sessions in several styles. This independent guide explains what the seller lists, what research cannot yet show about this product, and how to decide whether the convenience is worth the cost."

**Value sentence barred:** "Reprogram your brain in twelve minutes, get an hour of meditation, and cure stress." The hour-equivalence and therapeutic/neurological promises are seller claims without product-specific substantiation in the reviewed independent record. See Ingendoh, Posny & Heine (2023) https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37205669/ (14 binaural-beat EEG studies; 5 supportive, 8 contradictory, 1 mixed; heterogeneous methods). This review did **not** test Zen12. See FTC https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance .

**A useful demonstration, not pseudo-science:** Take a plain timer and any legally available noncommercial ambient audio or silence. Before each of three sessions, record the intended duration, comfort, distraction, whether you actually completed it, and whether you preferred voice, music, natural ambience or no audio. This is a **preference log**, not evidence of brainwave entrainment, treatment, improved attention or any comparable clinical outcome. Readers who prefer silence or free public resources should be explicitly told they may not need to purchase anything.

**Objections honestly answered:** "Are the effects independently verified?" Not for Zen12's advertised equivalence. "Do I need this to meditate?" No. "Can I use my own recordings?" A free alternative is sensible if the structure and format options are not worth the cost. "How is my purchase data handled?" The merchant states it may share a referred buyer's name, email and purchased product with an affiliate (https://inspire3.com/privacy/), and may contact customers for marketing. "What's the real price?" It must be checked at the precise authorized checkout on release day; the dated seller page is not enough. "Can I return it?" Seller advertises a refund, but current applicable terms, timing, support channel and completed-refund evidence must be rechecked; do not promise fulfillment.

## 4. One controlled A/B idea, not a claim about lift

Original R114 Variant A = text buyer guide; R114 Variant B = same buyer guide + optional 40-second original educational-media concept, caption file and transcript. The comparison question is whether optional media helps a reader make an *informed purchase decision*, not whether we can inflate clicks. No new recording, embed, user agent or analytics script is installed here. An original 40-second demonstration film prepared separately is not proof the site hosts a WCAG-compliant video.

**Hypothesis:** For qualified self-selected audio-meditation buyers, B may improve evidence comprehension and eventual merchant-confirmed paid conversion relative to A, **only if** the optional media introduces no mobile friction or performance penalty. Null hypothesis: no material improvement. A is an appropriate default if B performs worse or cannot be independently validated. A/B assignment must not collect identifying data or create new personal profiles; run controlled staged QA before considering any real visitor exposure, and do not change existing analytics until Claude #820 approves the plan.

**Prelaunch acceptance:** One canonical publicly indexable buying guide *after approval*, no competing indexable variants. Source/reference audit. Current exact merchant checkout and policy check. All FTC disclosure wording placed at the earnings CTA, not hidden in footer: "If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you." Advertised price can be omitted until reliable. Text guide remains fully useful without JS, account creation or purchase. Test 360/390/768/1440/1920 viewports, 200% zoom, keyboard/focus visibility, noninteractive controls, hover/touch, contrast, reduced motion, caption sync, transcript accuracy, original-media rights, autoplay OFF, external request count and LCP. Axe/browser/manual checks and WP staging rendering should be recorded with actual screenshots and test logs by Site QA.

**Experiment outcome, later and only if authorized:** successful first-party qualified-human sessions and merchant-sourced *paid* orders/commissions after returns and fees. Count confirmed leads as a separate intermediate event. Require 7/30-day stable cohorts with actual denominators and report wide uncertainty/small samples. Never announce statistical superiority with a handful of sales.

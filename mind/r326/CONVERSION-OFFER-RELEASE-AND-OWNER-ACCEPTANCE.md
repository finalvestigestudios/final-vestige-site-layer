## Funnel and attribution measurement contract

**Sequence:** existing Grok published `asset_id` + `utm_source` + `utm_medium=organic_social` + `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11` + `utm_content=authentic_asset_id` → verified human landing session → topic-matched source article → optional Fieldbook Dispatch lead with provider-confirmed consent status → independent guide → relevant CTA → externally authenticated merchant **paid** order or affiliate settlement → refunds and fees → realized net revenue. User can bypass Dispatch and read the commercial guide without providing an email.

**No conflation:** A pageview is not a unique qualified session. A clicked button is not a checkout, an outbound link is not a commission, a submitted email field is not necessarily an accepted Dispatch subscription, and an order claim without payment settlement is not realized revenue. Where attribution between video and order cannot be authenticated, record it as unknown instead of assigning the sale. The existing newsletter welcome and Jetpack list are immutable within this workstream.

**One KPI:** realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1,000 × cleared, attribution-supported gross paid receipts minus refunds and merchant/processing fees, divided by qualified human unique sessions in the same cohort and currency. Without a verified numerator **and** nonzero verified denominator, print NOT COMPUTABLE rather than $0 or an estimated amount. Report also paid conversion, unique confirmed customers, confirmed lead-to-paid conversion, 7/30-day cohorts, tiny samples and unknown sources. A/b outcome reporting must require source-verified orders.

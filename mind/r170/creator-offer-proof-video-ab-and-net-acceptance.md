## Privacy-safe, falsifiable funnel and settlement contract

Only where platform permitted, add approved standard organic UTMs: `utm_source=tiktok|youtube|...`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_attention_buyer_2026_11`, `utm_content=<VERIFIED_GROK_ASSET_ID>`. If a single bio link makes per-asset assignment ambiguous, report **unknown**; do not synthesize unique IDs from publication dates. Do not collect user email/IP, exact location, browsing fingerprint, unconsented persistent visitor IDs or customer identity in this marketing ledger.

Events (all different): `campaign_landing` = engagement only; `focus_cta_click` = interest only; `jetpack_confirmed_signup` = provider-confirmed lead, not sale; `affiliate_outbound` = permitted click, NOT paid conversion; `merchant_paid_order` = merchant-verified paid order; `affiliate_commission_settled` = actual FV-received commission; `refund_or_adjustment` = reconciliation. Merchant record IDs only in privately secured merchant records; public GitHub receives aggregate non-identifying QA evidence. Attribution coverage must be recorded; unknown merchants cannot be assumed to report post-click conversions.

**Owned-product realized net:** actual cleared payment receipts paid by customers, **after discounts already reflected**, minus refunds, chargebacks, payment/merchant fees and FV-borne expenses **not already deducted**. If starting from a separate pre-discount gross amount, explicitly label that amount and subtract discounts ONCE; never subtract both a paid net receipt and the discount that was already reflected in that receipt.

**Affiliate realized net:** attributable affiliate commissions actually received by FV, **not merchant retail gross merchandise value**, minus postsettlement reversals, fees and FV-borne costs not already withheld. Never double-deduct withholding or count `affiliate_outbound` as a sale.

Primary KPI = **1,000 × (realized FV-owned net + realized FV affiliate net) / independently qualified human sessions** by 7- and 30-day cohorts with refund/settlement lag disclosed. Additional evidence: confirmed paid customers, gross receipts where authenticated, checkout failure rate, refund rate, completed opt-in leads, and confirmed lead-to-purchase conversion when attributable. If actual numerator, qualified denominator or attribution cannot be verified, mark **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero. Warning: tiny samples cannot support a credible A/B winner.

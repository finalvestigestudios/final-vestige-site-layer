The unreliable witness is sometimes the observer.

Our minds are investigators, and sometimes imperfect witnesses. A disappearing change. A second target arriving too soon. A historical claim that outruns its evidence.

Test the claim. Read the experiment. Meet the historical source. Decide what, if anything, the result means for daily practice. Research is free and no purchase is required.

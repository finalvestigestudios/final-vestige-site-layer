**Scope:** A new educational visual bridging auditory attention into a Zen12 consumer evidence guide. **No false demonstration:** The film is a schematic educational explanation, NOT an actual dichotic listening experiment. No name is covertly recorded. The source and video text should never imply that all people detect unattended speech or that Zen12 has been tested by the above studies.

**Inclusive fallback:** Poster alt text; exact matching VTT and transcript; no autoplay, no blocked essential copy when video is unavailable, no JS requirement. Silence is by design; if hosted player supplies silent visuals, captions should still be available and independently checked.

**CTA design hypothesis:** Compare A complete source guide with B identical guide plus optional film. Metrics and qualified outbound clicks remain hard-off until approved. Never claim lift or earnings from a concept alone.

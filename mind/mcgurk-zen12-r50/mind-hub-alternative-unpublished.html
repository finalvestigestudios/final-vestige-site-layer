Tools under review—not tools endorsed

Audio programs deserve the same skepticism and curiosity as historical testimony. Our first pending guide examines the format and scientific limitations of Zen12. It is not published, not a tested firsthand review, and not currently linked to any merchant. We do not recommend treatment or wealth claims without substantiation.

Commercial recommendations will be visibly separated and disclosed next to any eventually approved outbound earning link. The Mind archive itself is for evidence, not an advertising funnel.

Our method

Identify the original claim. Follow the primary source. Reproduce only what can legitimately be shown. Describe the limits. Give a reader something useful to try without buying anything. Ask what would falsify the explanation.

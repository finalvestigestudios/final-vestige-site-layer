## Audience and intent

The single high-intent persona is an adult who is considering paying for a structured meditation-audio library but wants to know what exactly is included, whether it fits an achievable routine, and whether scientific claims withstand scrutiny. The buyer's job is a decision, not a miracle: **buy a convenient structured format if useful, or stay with a free alternative**. The landing guide should help that decision either way.

We must distinguish this buyer from someone who watched a funny illusion and has no demonstrated purchasing intent. The latter is an editorial reader, not automatically a qualified lead for Zen12. A mismatch between social hook and landing recommendation will raise bounces and erode trust even if raw clicks rise. Offer fit takes priority over more impressions or fancier design.

The specific offer value that can be stated as vendor-described is the packaged set of MP3 tracks, progressive levels, user instructions and support channel. It is not a proven one-hour brain state compressed into twelve minutes. We cannot claim the visitor will become mentally sharper, wealthier, clinically healthier, less anxious, or biologically reprogrammed. We have not used or purchased the product.

The promise in original copy can be: **“A short routine is easier to assess when you can separate its practical convenience from the claims used to sell it.”** This is a purchasing insight, not a clinically measured improvement.

## Measurement contract — what counts and what doesn't

**The single primary outcome is verified realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions (QHS).** At cohort day 7 and day 30, calculate:

net_realized_revenue_per_1000 = 1000 × (merchant-confirmed received paid order revenue or received eligible affiliate commissions - refunded/reversed revenue or commissions - documented transaction fees and incremental acquisition costs attributable to this funnel) / independently qualified-human sessions.

Only compute when numerator and denominator are both trustworthy and comparable. A gross seller commission percentage from a public affiliate landing page is not an earned amount. Do not guess payment totals, count canceled orders as paid, or subtract the same fee twice. Report small samples prominently; attribute uncertainty when cookies are blocked, creator platform links are not identifiable, partner order exports lack attribution or sessions may include bots.

Step-level evidence to distinguish:
1. **Asset → qualified human visit:** genuine referrer or standard UTM and de-duplicated session where available, bot/internal/testing filters applied. Native analytics must first be audited; no new vendor script, duplicate GA/Ahrefs pixel or non-consensual identifier.
2. **Visit → relevant CTA interaction:** article_to_focus_click, guide_review_click or affiliate_outbound distinct, counted only where existing first-party analytics and consent permit. If no instrument exists, state NOT OBSERVABLE, do not create an event out of pageview inference.
3. **Focus → confirmed lead:** Jetpack provider-authoritative opt-in transition and consent/welcome evidence, deduplicated, excluding display of the PDF, URL ?subscribe=, is-subscriber CSS state, preview pages or download clicks. No new list.
4. **Guide → paid order:** independently confirmed merchant completed/settled Payhip order or validated partner-reported payable affiliate conversion with order ID/status. An outbound click, email signup, checkout start or view is never a sale.
5. **Paid → net revenue:** reconcile fees, taxes if relevant, reversals, refunds, affiliate approval period/holdback, final received payout and cost of content. No order-level PII in GitHub issues or report drafts.

A/B: only when one sellable authorized offer exists, randomize eligible *qualified landing sessions* 50/50 within the existing consent framework and avoid changing underlying copy, offer, CTA placement, source mix or device behavior. Use a single predeclared primary success metric: independently confirmed paid orders per qualified visitor or revenue per thousand qualified visitors; do not optimize for video plays or affiliate outbound clicks. Separate audience capture/confirmed leads as secondary. Before activation, require a power/sample-size and duration estimate based on observed actual baseline, which is presently unknown. Seven- and thirty-day cohorts are observation windows, not claims of statistical significance. Tiny-sample warning in every readout. Do not persist IDs or run split tests without privacy/engineering approval.

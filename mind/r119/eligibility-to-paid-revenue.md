**High-intent audience:** Someone who wants a reproducible short meditation-audio routine and is evaluating whether *Zen12 Premium* is worth the money. They are not seeking a miraculous increase in perceptual power. This narrowly defined decision is distinct from the curiosity of an audience watching the famous gorilla experiment. A reader who genuinely expresses purchase intent may navigate to an evidence-first guide without subscribing; that page must remain useful even if our affiliate application fails.

**Actual product page:** https://zen12.com/hnp — inspected November 4, 2026. Seller says Premium offers 48 MP3 recordings, twelve levels and four listening styles. Promotional **$87.99** appears on that seller webpage; it is **NOT** the final verified total after tax or a permanent price. Beginner and Advanced are separately described packages; do not conflate them with Premium. Seller advertises a year-long refund promise; do not tell consumers that refunds have been independently audited. The central one-hour-in-twelve-minutes / guaranteed brainwave effects language is a **seller performance claim**, not an independently supported Zen12 result.

**Independent evidence boundary:** Ingendoh et al. (2023), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37205669/ reviewed 14 binaural-beat EEG studies and found divergent results (five aligned with entrainment, eight contradictory, one mixed). No unsubstantiated stress/health, wealth, trauma, focus or diagnostic claims belong in the presell.

## 5. Attribution events and revenue scorecard

**Reuse Claude's Issue #820 first-party event contract. No duplicate script.** Suggested allowed fields per aggregate cohort: platform source, topic cluster, campaign, `utm_content=real_asset_id`, page route, event category, 7/30-day window, reconciled vendor product and anonymized settled dollars. **Do not collect email addresses, full IP, purchaser name, phone, postal address or cookie/fingerprint identifiers to make the funnel work.** Session qualification must be independently defined, respecting bot screening, internal visits, and privacy consent.

Event hierarchy:
1. `campaign_landing`: observed session with documented legitimate human qualification.
2. `article_to_resource_click` or `offer_guide_view`: relevant CTA engagement.
3. `confirmed_signup`: authenticated Jetpack confirmed status, not form submission, and no new subscriber injected without approval.
4. `affiliate_outbound`: locally attributable click, **never** an order.
5. `verified_checkout_start`: only when seller-origin checkout event is legitimately observable.
6. `merchant_confirmed_purchase` / `merchant_confirmed_affiliate_sale`: authoritative completed transaction, not synthetic event.
7. `refund_adjustment`, `fee_adjustment`, `realized_net`: reconcile reversals and payout.

**KPI** = 1,000 × *merchant-reconciled net revenue* / *qualified human sessions* for attributable cohorts. **NOT COMPUTABLE** if zero or unknown denominator, incomplete merchant settlement or unaudited cross-platform attribution; do not report a fabricated zero. Paid conversion = completed paid orders / qualified human sessions; lead-to-customer = attributable paid customers / confirmed consented leads only when linkable lawfully and reliably. Note that aggregate-only affiliate reporting may prevent precise per-campaign paid attribution, which must be shown explicitly rather than reverse-engineered from click patterns. Use 7-/30-day cohorts, compare confidence ranges and caution about tiny samples and selection bias.

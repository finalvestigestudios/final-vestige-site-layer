### 7/30-day cohort and acceptance arithmetic

Let Q be **qualified human sessions** within the fixed 7-day or 30-day cohort. Let C be independently confirmed paid orders or cleared affiliate commissions **attributable under agreed evidence rules**; do not add inferred unreported sales. Let G be recorded gross receipts, R refunds/chargebacks, F payment/affiliate/platform fees, and O other documented attributable commercial costs (including legitimate contractor or media costs if actually incurred). Realized Net N=G−R−F−O. **Net revenue per 1,000 qualified humans = 1,000×N/Q**, only when Q>0 and all required ledger components are sufficiently complete. If Q=0, missing or bot-corrupted, or settlement is incomplete, label NOT COMPUTABLE. If complete, N may be positive, ZERO OR NEGATIVE. C/Q is paid-conversion rate only when C contains confirmed, attributable transactions and the deduplication policy is stable. Lead-to-customer requires provider-confirmed eligible leads matched with actual orders through lawful, privacy-safe aggregate reconciliation. Unknown is not zero.

**A/B hypothesis:** Adding a self-initiated, original 42s explainer about the buyer's evidence/price objections may increase *confirmed paid conversion per qualified session*, but may instead distract or slow mobile users. A controls information: identical price evidence, limits, free exercise, reading links and FTC disclosure. B adds *only* optional video content and technical media overhead. Primary outcome: verified net revenue / 1k Q; secondary: confirmed paid-order rate, confirmed lead rate where valid, engaged guide visit, CTA click as diagnostic; guardrails: mobile CWV, accessibility, source trust, unsubscribes, refunds/complaints. Pre-register assignment and exclusions before live testing, define a minimum sample separately based on effect sizes and acceptable cost, and avoid significance claims at small n. No sequential 'we won' reading after a handful of clicks.

**No video-completion or affiliate-click result may be called a paid conversion.** Missing Q/settled commission means no commercial outcome yet.

How to read the archive

Every Mind investigation begins with an observation, follows its published or archival source, explains competing interpretations, and makes room for limitations. Demonstrations are not diagnostic tests. A research result about meditation training does not establish the clinical effect of a particular MP3.

Editorial and affiliate boundaries

Independent investigations never inherit affiliate claims.

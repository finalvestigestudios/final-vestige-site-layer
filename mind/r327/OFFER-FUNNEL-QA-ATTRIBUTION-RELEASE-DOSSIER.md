## Why the buyer page is deliberately different from an editorial investigation

The original 2,197-word research investigation **The Choice You Never Made** documents Johansson's 2005 face-choice switch, the 2014 memory challenge, and the 2025 important finding of covert rather than reported detection in computerized settings. The headline no longer implies that all silent participants lack awareness. This research cannot prove Zen12's claims. It offers a valuable lesson about separating post-hoc explanations from demonstrated causes.

The separate Zen12 presell page therefore answers a *real consumer question*: What do I get, how do I evaluate advertised claims, what are the limits, how much could it cost, and can I try the useful part for free? It uses a grounded 2005 experiment as a short methodological micro-story, then makes explicit that the experiment did **not** test meditation audio.

**Variant A:** same full evidence guide, including free practice, seller feature descriptions, independent study, objections, refund/pricing cautions and disabled CTA.

**Variant B:** precisely the same substantive guide plus an optional original 42-second short storyboard: an SVG illustrated clock, six timed narration segments and WebVTT captions. The concept is NOT a rendered video. Before a fair film experiment it requires encoded optional media, static poster and captions synchronized against actual playback; a user-initiated start; no autoplay or forced completion; no external heavy video player; transcript available without JS; suitable color contrast and size; click/keyboard/touch/VoiceOver and reduced-motion QA. Where no film is supported the text remains complete and equally persuasive. Video is a *hypothesis*, not a conversion promise.

Both versions deliberately lack external merchant anchors, new subscription forms, tracking scripts, iframes and unapproved commerce APIs. Their seller CTA is disabled and adjacent to an explicit conditional prospective affiliate disclosure. Existing free link https://finalvestige.com/focus/ remains the only subscription path.

## Event-level measurement acceptance (specification, not deployed code)

The planned chain is:

1. **Qualified human session candidate:** first-party arrival to the exact landing path with allowlisted source platform, actual Grok asset_id (where verified), utm_campaign, experiment variant and coarse acquisition cohort. Exclude known synthetic traffic; do not claim this yields a perfectly bot-free denominator.
2. **Relevant CTA click:** a first-party event specifically identifying Focus CTA versus guide navigation versus independently authorized merchant outbound. Never collapse unlike click purposes.
3. **Confirmed lead:** a provider-confirmed new or existing opted-in subscriber record, with acceptable consent and campaign attribution if actually supported. A fieldbook download or form impression alone is not a confirmed new lead. Avoid fetching personally identifiable subscriber values into PRs or issue comments.
4. **Affiliate_click:** only a user-initiated click on a valid approved link after appropriate proximate disclosure and channel permission. Not a merchant sale.
5. **Verified paid event:** a partner- or merchant-reported, non-reversed, independently reconciled paid order/earned commission; or an FV-owned Payhip confirmed paid order. Do not infer paid orders from offsite clicks or a thank-you-page view. If no cross-site attribution is permitted, report revenue as unattributed.
6. **Settled net:** order gross minus verifiable refunds, chargebacks, merchant/processor fees, related allowable costs and reversals. Track gross revenue, commission payable, commission settled and realized net as separate amounts. Never insert an assumed payout percentage.
7. **7/30-day cohorts:** date-specific acquired sessions, confirmed lead counts, paid orders, net realized revenue and lag after payment clearance. Suppress any very small segment where privacy or unreliable inference is a concern.

**Primary KPI:** Net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1,000 × realized attributable net / qualified human sessions. Value is **NOT COMPUTABLE** without *both* a justified denominator and independently confirmed paid numerator. Unknown is not zero. Secondary: paid order rate, lead-to-customer rate, net revenue per confirmed buyer, refund rate, buyer friction/abandonment where directly observable, conversion quality. Diagnostic only: scroll depth, impressions, outbound clicks.

**Data minimization:** Preserve one existing first-party analytics integration, not a duplicate measurement pixel. Never share raw IP, full referrers containing query secrets, email addresses, individual subscriber records, fingerprint identifiers, exact user paths, full order/customer IDs or embedded cookies in public GitHub comments. Use bounded event dimensions, consent rules and existing provider capabilities. URL UTM values must be allowlisted, length-limited and free of customer or token identifiers. Merchant cross-site IDs must be used only if the publisher contract and law permit. If attribution is missing, label it explicitly.

## Controlled experiment and stop rules

The independent buyer A/B differs **only** by the optional short film module. Declare the sole initial hypothesis as “an optional 42-second original explainer raises *verified net purchase value per 1,000 qualified humans* without materially harming mobile performance, accessibility or trust.” It may reduce conversion: reading friction and transfer size are plausible harms.

Do not test on a live audience until authentic purchase availability, founder release and Site QA sign-off. Use a stable first-party session assignment only if Claude's existing implementation supports consent and privacy; no assignment cookies merely for this pilot without review. Pre-register assignment, mobile thresholds, event dictionary, cost accounting, sample size and evaluation window; do not estimate significance from a handful of people. A relevant learning-stage alternative is a moderated, consented comprehension and accessibility usability test, not a fabricated commercial A/B result.

**Abort conditions:** broken checkout/dead affiliate URL, terms mismatch, misleading medical outcome, missing commission disclosure beside the live link, inaccessible play button, unexpected playback, degraded mobile LCP/CWV, privacy leak, duplicate signup flow, invalid campaign attribution, unauthorized list email or post.

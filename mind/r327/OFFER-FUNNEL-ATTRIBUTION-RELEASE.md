## Original persuasion pathway
- **Problem:** audiences discover they cannot reliably report a second rapid target; surprising attention deficits invite hasty self-improvement promises.
- **Insight:** 1992 visual attention trials, 2005-06 surprising distraction manipulation and 2007 intensive training research are *not* Zen12 trials.
- **Micro-story:** the sequence of two letters; one reportable, another hard to detect even though physically presented.
- **Useful before purchase:** free twelve-minute field observation; no pseudo diagnostic scoring.
- **Offer value:** 48 seller-described MP3 tracks arranged into 12 levels, four styles, with brief sessions. Value is the convenience/choice of format; efficacy remains unproven for this product.
- **Limits / objections:** cost, repeat listening preference, absence of a demonstrated branded benefit, checkout/refund verification, strong health claims omitted.
- **Commercial action:** disabled; near-button prospective commission disclosure; no active merchant link or redirect.

## Privacy-safe attribution / fraud & bot cautions
Use only existing first-party analytics/Jetpack where supported; do not deploy new script. Candidate unique mapping: `utm_source={tiktok|youtube|instagram|facebook}`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_attentional_blink_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content={real_verified_asset_id}` (or explicit platform-profile placeholder when individual video attribution is impossible). Do not insert PII, emails, secret tokens or user IDs into URLs. Require strict allowlist and limits before trusting UTM fields; client- or server-side labels are not proof of human traffic.

Event definitions remain separate: `campaign_landing` (qualified-human criteria to be specified), `source_article_view`, `article_to_focus_click`, `focus_signup_confirmed` (provider-backed, not a display), `buyer_guide_view`, `affiliate_outbound` (click, not commission), `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`, `merchant_settled_commission`, `refund_and_fee_adjustment`. No new events should be deployed by this lane. Deduplicate on opaque provider references only under appropriate consent; never publish subscriber identifiers.

KPI by 7- and 30-day actual cohorts:
`net_realized_revenue_per_1000_qualified_humans = 1000 * (independently_confirmed_settled_commissions_or_owned_net_orders - verified_refunds - fees) / verified_qualified_human_sessions`.
Require the same window, method and source proof for numerator/denominator and explicit missingness. A cohort with one or two people needs a tiny-sample warning rather than a lift claim. **At present: NOT COMPUTABLE, not zero.** Never count clicks, checkout starts, new-site analytics or alleged bot views as paid sales.

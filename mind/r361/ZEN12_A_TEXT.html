Try the free twelve-minute baseline

  1. Choose one small task: a printed page, notes, or an unfamiliar image.
  2. Work quietly for two minutes, with your phone away from reach.
  3. Continue for eight minutes. Mark each moment you notice your attention moving elsewhere, then return.
  4. Spend two minutes recording what interrupted you. Do not interpret fewer marks as proof of better attention; you may have noticed less.

If the exercise is uncomfortable, stop. This is not a controlled experiment, a medical intervention or a prediction of how an audio product would work.

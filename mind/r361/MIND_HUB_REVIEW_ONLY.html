The library / A proposed new archive

The unreliable witness is your mind.

You can miss a stranger changing places. You can feel something that has no proposed external cause. You can be certain of a memory that later shifts. The interesting question is not whether minds are mysterious. It is what the experiments and records actually establish.

Explore the research. Trace the originals. Try an observation. Decide what a product claim is worth.

Read one experiment critically

What did participants actually see? How many people took part? Which explanations did the authors test? What would have happened under a different condition? If a commercial claim is attached, was the specific product ever in that experiment?

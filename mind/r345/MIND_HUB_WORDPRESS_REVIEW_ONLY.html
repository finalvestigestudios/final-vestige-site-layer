01 / An observable problem

A stranger changes. You don't always notice.

In an established 1998 campus experiment, a door passed between two people in conversation. The person giving directions sometimes failed to see that the person requesting directions had changed. This is a phenomenon with a specific procedure and sample—not a diagnosis of anyone's attention.

03 / The source before the story

A witness can be sincere. A mechanism can still be wrong.

Puységur's surviving case material captures a remarkable effort to turn testimony and witnessed events into an argument for animal magnetism. The records include failures, concealment, operator pressure and questions of consent. They are extraordinary precisely because a documented statement is not necessarily a documented cause.

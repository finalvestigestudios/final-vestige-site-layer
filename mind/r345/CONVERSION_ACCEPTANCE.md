## A/B experiment (no predeclared winner)

**Population:** eligible, consensually measured qualified-human organic visitors reaching the same Zen12-specific intent. Exclude QA/internal/bot traffic, repeated test sessions and sources whose destination intent doesn't match. Claude owns final first-party event integration; do not add another tag manager or SaaS.

- **A:** ZEN12_A_TEXT_REVIEW.html — fully sourced text-only standalone guide.
- **B:** ZEN12_B_OPTIONAL_FILM_REVIEW.html — **same substantive guide** plus original 42-second SVG/accessible transcript and VTT specification. No hosted MP4 in this PR. Film is optional, no autoplay, 100% functional without JavaScript. Delay first media fetch until explicit playback after hosting QA. Its production must not be confused with Grok's organic video posting role.
- **Hypothesis:** An optional concise visual explanation may improve buyer comprehension and trust for some readers without reducing qualified paid conversion. It might equally depress conversions, slow mobile performance or attract curiosity clicks. No assumed lift.
- **Primary endpoint:** verified paid conversion and realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions, net of refunds/fees, measured on 7- and 30-day cohorts. Confirmed gross/paid order count, order-level reconciliations and lead-to-customer rate are secondary. CTA clicks, scrolls, view counts and claimed video completions are diagnostics, never sales.
- **Decision:** pre-register one primary endpoint and minimum detectable effect after a realistic baseline; do not claim statistical significance from a handful of orders. Report counts and uncertainty, not percentage change alone. If no authentic purchases, declare *not evaluable*.

## Attribution contract: separate observation from transaction

**Allowed first-party campaign fields (with privacy review):** utm_source (actual platform), utm_medium=organic_social where true, utm_campaign (approved cluster), utm_content (verified Grok asset ID), landing_path (no query PII), article_slug, CTA_id, consent_state, coarse session_day, bot_qualified boolean. Avoid names, email, exact IPs, cross-site fingerprinting, raw user agent logging or visitor-level histories. Use first-party infrastructure only if Claude confirms no duplicate implementation and appropriate consent.

**Event vocabulary:**
- campaign_landing: eligible landing observation; **not** a human-confirmed visit without bot/QA filters.
- article_to_fieldbook_click: link interaction, **not** confirmed subscriber.
- confirmed_signup: Jetpack provider-confirmed, not inferred from thank-you URL or button click.
- offer_guide_view: page viewed, **not** sale.
- affiliate_click: disclosed approved outward action, **not** sale.
- verified_paid_order: merchant-confirmed completed order by permitted reconciliation; **not** necessarily settled.
- affiliate_sale_confirmed: merchant/partner-reported payable transaction, independently from click count.
- realized_net_revenue: settled revenue minus actual platform/transaction fees and refunds/chargebacks as defined for the cohort.

**Example calculation, only with real records:** net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1000 × (settled receipts + paid eligible commissions − refunds − transaction/platform fees) ÷ qualified-human sessions. If either numerator or denominator is missing, result is **not computable**. No invented rounding or zero substitution. Perform seven- and thirty-day cohort reconciliation with refunds and unresolved payout lag labeled.

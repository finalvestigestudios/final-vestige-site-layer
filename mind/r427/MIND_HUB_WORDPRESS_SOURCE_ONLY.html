05 / Research integrity

How a claim earns a place.

Does a documented effect imply a brain-training benefit?

No. The McGurk effect shows an influence of sight on reported speech perception under certain stimuli. It says nothing about Zen12 efficacy.

Are the historical sources medical advice?

No. The Puységur documents preserve extraordinary claims as claims; their case testimony cannot establish present-day treatment effectiveness.

Are we running a social-video or purchase experiment?

No. Proposed social routes are subject to Grok/Praxxis asset verification. Text-only vs original short film is an untested hypothesis requiring video production, mobile/browser QA, merchant permission and approved real purchase measurement.

What happens to privacy?

**Audience:** a reader actively comparing a short meditation audio routine; not a patient seeking treatment or a casual visitor viewing McGurk science.

## Conversion design
The headline is “Zen12, without the leap of faith.” The seller-specific guide uses the 1976 McGurk research as a sourced caution about confusing perception with audio-product benefit, then a free twelve-minute baseline, six buyer questions, seller-format details, and scientific limits. Prices, coupons, vendor guarantees, payouts and first-person endorsements are deliberately omitted because the exact approved checkout is not authenticated. The proposed “Visit authorized Zen12 offer” control is disabled, has no URL and carries immediately preceding FTC disclosure.

- **A:** full credible text-only buying guide with cited alternatives, disclosure, inactive CTA.
- **B:** identical substantive guide plus optional 42-second film concept, original poster, six WebVTT cues, full transcript. B is NOT encoded, hosted or playable.
- **Hypothesis:** optional film could improve verified *paid purchases and refund-adjusted net per qualified human* among actual audio shoppers, or it could distract/slow loading. Neither direction is assumed.
- **Assignment:** only after founder-approved release; Claude implements first-party anonymous stable session allocation using existing consent-respecting analytics, no duplicate scripts or identifiers beyond necessary minimal scope. Preserve direct page URLs and no-JS content.
- **Primary outcome:** verified 30-day net revenue per 1,000 deduplicated qualified-human sessions, with 7-day provisional cohorts and explicit refund/settlement maturation. Secondary: confirmed orders, lead-to-order, paid conversion and refunds; diagnostic only: clicks, visits, play starts and video completion.
- **Stop/hold:** no experiment if authorized payable SKU, merchant reconciliation, partner approval, legally appropriate consent, independent QA or founder release is absent; stop for broken Focus, disclosure, WCAG, performance or bot-filter failures.
- **Video performance budget proposed, not tested:** optional 42s MP4/WebM with poster ideally below 60KB and media ideally below 500KB if quality allows, preload none, no third-party embeds/autoplay/scripts/remote fonts. Browser test FCP/LCP/CLS/INP under mobile throttling at 360/390/768/1440/1920px; no source video exists yet.

## Verified net metric
**Realized net = independently settled commissions/owned gross paid receipts MINUS verified refunds, chargebacks, payment and partner fees and attributable transaction charges. Net/1000 qualified-human sessions = net / verified deduplicated qualified-human sessions × 1000.** If either is missing, display **NOT COMPUTABLE**, never 0%. If no consent-safe source join, mark merchant-level revenue UNATTRIBUTED. Include both 7/30-day cohorts, sample-size warnings, source match rate, bot exclusions, chargeback/refund aging and confidence limits. Impressions, clicks, checkout starts, pageviews and requested (unconfirmed) signups are not sales.

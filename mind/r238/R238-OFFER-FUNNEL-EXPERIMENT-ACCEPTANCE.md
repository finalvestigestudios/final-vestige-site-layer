Events: `qualified_human_session` (separate from raw visit), `guide_cta_click`, `affiliate_click` (only authorized *real* link clicks), `confirmed_lead` (only Jetpack-verified consent/confirmation), `merchant_checkout_start` (only independently observable/merchant-confirmed), `paid_order` and `settled_affiliate_commission` (only merchant/finance authority), `refund`, `net_revenue`. Do not subtract the same fee twice. If session denominator or merchant settlement proof missing, KPI **NOT COMPUTABLE**. Secondary confirmed paid conversion rate, orders, lead-to-paid, gross/net, refund rate. With tiny denominators avoid declaring an A/B winner; report counts and uncertainty.

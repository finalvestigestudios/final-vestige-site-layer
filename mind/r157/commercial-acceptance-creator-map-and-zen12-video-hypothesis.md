### Verified claim/source contract

- **Research**: Ingendoh et al. 2023 https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0286023 : among fourteen qualifying binaural EEG studies, five supportive, eight contradictory, one mixed. Heterogeneous methods. **Never generalize to any product.**
- **Research**: García-Argibay et al. 2019 https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-018-1066-8 : meta-analysis across 22 binaural studies and 35 effects, g=0.45 pooled across studied outcomes. This is not a Focus Shot result or a guarantee.
- **Research**: Leistiko et al. 2023 https://doi.org/10.1007/s12144-023-04681-3 : fifty-eight participants, a specific gamma binaural design, no significant targeted attention/anxiety improvement.
- **Independent review**: Chee et al. 2024 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38458383/ : mixed or insufficient autonomic performance evidence across distinct audio interventions.

### Measurement contract — no click is a sale

A first-party inbound record may capture *approved* `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, consent status and Grok-supplied public `asset_id`, without email, IP address, device fingerprint or cross-site trackers. Do not install another analytics script. If consent is not available for identity persistence, aggregate daily anonymous paths and label attribution limited; avoid unjustified person-level linkage.

Prospective event stages:
1. `qualified_human_session` — deduplicated only under approved privacy policy, with bot exclusion and clearly stated denominator.
2. `topic_article_view` and `focus_cta_click` — diagnostics, not leads.
3. `jetpack_confirmed_lead` — only if independently confirmed by existing service; no assumed auto-submit success.
4. `affiliate_click` — only when an authorized link is actually present; always distinct from `checkout_start` and `merchant_paid_order`.
5. `merchant_paid_order` / `partner_approved_paid_conversion` — source-authoritative conversions, reconciled to refunds and settlement.
6. `net_revenue_realized` — paid gross less refunds, payment/platform fees and attributable costs; commissions counted only when independently approved/paid per merchant terms.

Compute by **7- and 30-day cohorts**: realized net revenue / qualified-human sessions × 1,000; paid orders / qualified sessions; confirmed leads / sessions; paid customers / confirmed leads where attribution is lawful and available. Report n, missing events, merchant export cutoff, unattributed orders, net unknown and small-sample warning. **When both denominator and settlement are unavailable, result is NOT COMPUTABLE, not $0.**

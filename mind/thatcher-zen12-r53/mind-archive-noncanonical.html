How this archive works

Every research claim must lead to an actual study or contemporaneous source, explain what was tested, and state what the result cannot support. Historical witness accounts are preserved as reports. Commercial descriptions are marked as seller claims and never used as science.

Our original TikTok and social demos are entry points, not proof. A short illustration may ask an intriguing question. The full article preserves study design, competing explanations and source dates. The free Focus Fieldbook remains the only existing signup journey; the Mind Archive creates no replacement list.

No production route, editor account, plugin, mail list, merchant link or tracking changed.

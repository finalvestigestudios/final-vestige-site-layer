## Attribution and settlement contract

Re-use the approved first-party measurements under Claude; do not install another pixel or paid analytics SaaS. Standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign` and `utm_content=verified_asset_id` are **candidates**, not a declaration that these parameters survive social app routing. Verify the signed-out tap-to-landing behavior before cohort attribution. In privacy-safe reports, use aggregate counts, no addresses, emails, raw IPs, raw user agents, cookies invented outside existing consent or fingerprint join keys.

Follow distinct event semantics: `qualified_human_landing` (observed de-botted unique attributable visit); `relevant_article_cta_click`; `jetpack_provider_confirmed_lead`; `affiliate_click` (outbound intent only); `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`; `cleared_affiliate_commission`; `refund_or_reversal`; `verified_processing_fee`. An unobserved checkout start stays **unknown**. A confirmed subscriber is not a buyer. Gross order value is not settlement.

**Primary KPI:** `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 × (cleared payouts or owned paid-order proceeds − verified refunds − fees − reversals) ÷ independently qualified human sessions`. Calculate separate 7-day and 30-day cohorts only if **all** terms are present and their attribution is trustworthy. Otherwise report **NULL / NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0. Small samples require non-significance warnings and cannot justify growth claims. Secondary metrics: provider-confirmed lead-to-customer rate, paid conversion rate, paid orders, gross/net revenue and channel drop-off. Never claim a commission based on outbound click logs.

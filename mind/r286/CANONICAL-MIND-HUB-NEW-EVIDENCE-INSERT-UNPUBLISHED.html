A tiny effect. A very large promise.

A new scientific review found a small average effect after brief mindfulness exercises. The result matters. So do its limits. It was not a test of the audio being sold to you.

g = 0.09

66 studies, 187 effect sizes. Average immediate executive-function difference after brief mindfulness exercises: 95% confidence interval 0.03 to 0.16.

This standardized effect is not a nine-percent improvement in focus, not a productivity guarantee and not proof of a longer-lasting benefit. Source: Shi and colleagues, published 6 November 2026.

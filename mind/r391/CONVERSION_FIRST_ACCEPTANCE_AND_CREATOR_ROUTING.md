**Hypothesis only:** With the same source-based offer guide, including an optional original 42-second film explaining why expectation differs from evidence might help high-intent readers decide whether the product's audio format suits them. It may also add friction or create unfounded authority; either result is possible.

- **A control:** `ZEN12_A_INDEPENDENT_BUYER_GUIDE.html`, full substantive buyer text, no video.
- **B treatment:** `ZEN12_B_OPTIONAL_42S_STORYBOARD.html`, identical substantive text plus an expandable original SVG poster, 89-word transcript, six WebVTT cues. **Not a playable video yet.** No fake play control, no forced playback. Real native video must be original, rights-cleared, supported by captions and no-JS transcript; it should be click-to-play only, with adequate performance budget.
- **Assignment:** only after approval, stable server-side first-party 50/50 assignment where ethically/legally supported, no sensitive segmentation; same offer, current price caveats, position, disclosure, viewport and CTA. Avoid multi-testing uncontrolled copy differences.
- **Primary business endpoint:** independently verified net realized revenue / 1,000 qualified-human sessions in 7/30-day cohorts with refunds/fees. Secondary: merchant-confirmed paid conversion rate, confirmed orders/customers, gross vs net, lead-to-customer conversion. Tertiary diagnostic: consented outbound affiliate clicks, landing visits, confirmed Fieldbook leads.
- **Do not optimize on** generic clicks, CTR, newsletter form displays, traffic, followers or unverified checkout starts. Tiny sample warning if fewer than 100 qualified sessions per arm; even 100 can be grossly insufficient for rare purchases. Power and minimum detectable effect should be agreed before an actual experiment.

## Privacy-safe first-party measurement contract (schema and acceptance, not an installed tracker)
1. `qualified_session`: rotating privacy-preserving session key created on the first-party site, consent-state and bot/exclusion rules, UTC cohort day, only if lawful and necessary.
2. `landing_visit`: canonical article route + allowlisted campaign fields `asset_id` (only genuine Grok immutable media ID), `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `variant`; strip/free-text values; no IP/email/referrer path persisted by this plan.
3. `focus_cta_click`: first-party event separated from `focus_confirmed_lead`; a Jetpack successful opt-in display is not by itself a confirmed subscription. No duplicated list or tracking pixel.
4. `offer_cta_click`/`affiliate_click`: only after approved publisher URL; cannot be treated as order, commission or even checkout start.
5. `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`: authoritative merchant ID/accounting report, currency, net eligible amount, order date, refunded/charged-back status, return window. Where legal and supported, pseudonymous attribution join; no buyer PII exported to campaign ledger.
6. `realized_net_revenue`: settled eligible affiliate commissions or owned-sale receipts minus refunds, fees, chargebacks; report attribution-unknown orders separately, do not force assign.
7. **Formula:** `1000 × realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`; N/A if either denominator or verifiable numerator missing. Show 7-day and 30-day cohorts, numerator, denominator, paid orders, refunds and uncertainty. Do not call zero 'verified' without an actual complete ledger.
8. Check actual existing analytics/Claude contract before implementing; no duplicate script, pixels, paid SaaS, cookies or personal identifiers through query strings. QA tests should confirm URLs contain none of email, phone, user ID, order ID, session token or raw search terms.

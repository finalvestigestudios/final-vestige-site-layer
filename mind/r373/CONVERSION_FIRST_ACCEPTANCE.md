**Paid offer proposed:** Zen12 Premium as described by exact https://zen12.com/ public sales page (not a future redirected or personalized checkout). **Funnel steps and authority:**
1. source_attested_asset / valid campaign visit: media URL, posted date, original hook and tagged allowed destination verified by Praxxis. Unknown without this.
2. qualified_human_session: provider-supported, consent-compatible deduplicated session excluding test traffic and reasonable bot filtering, no speculative geographic identities or invasive fingerprints. Unknown if data cannot be separated.

4. confirmed_focus_signup: only Jetpack/provider-confirmed state (not raw submits/downloads); email addresses stay inside authorized provider and are never copied into campaign exports.
5. merchant_confirmed_paid_order / partner_reported_paid_affiliate_conversion: only authoritative merchant or partner reports; an outbound click or cart start does not substitute.
6. refunds, chargebacks, fees, settlement period and commission reversals: reconciliation against authoritative records, not marketing dashboard EPC projection.
7. realized_net_revenue = validated settled attributable gross proceeds or settled affiliate commission minus actual refunds/reversals/fees. Do not double-count orders from both affiliate and own-product lanes.
8. **realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions = (realized_net_revenue / verified qualified-human sessions) × 1,000**. If sessions or settlement inaccessible, state **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0. Report 7-day and 30-day cohorts and small-denominator warning. Include confirmed paid conversion rate, customers, net/gross amounts, lead-to-customer conversion only where both numerator and denominator genuinely proven.

**Privacy:** no session fingerprinting, email IDs, health inference, recorded IP logs, individual cross-platform joins or server-side partner cookie injection. Retain aggregate source/asset and first-party event data only where consent and terms permit. Attribution gap remains explicit.

Evidence in practice

A door passed. Did the person change?

In 1998, researchers interrupted a pedestrian's directions to a stranger. The experimenter was replaced during a brief visual obstruction. In the first experiment, only approximately half noticed. That result describes a bounded study, not a universal law about unreliable witnesses.

Examine the original journal study

Try an ethical, no-JS change-detection field note

Put six familiar objects on a table and make a written inventory. Turn away, let a consenting adult move one, and compare your written account against what changed. No hidden filming of people, no strangers, and no claimed diagnostic inference.

Commercial boundaries

A suggestion is not a scientific conclusion.

Inspire3 sells consumer meditation and hypnosis audio. Product format can be reviewed from official seller materials. Broad promises about stress, health, attention or wealth require independent product-specific substantiation and are not inherited from unrelated scientific experiments.

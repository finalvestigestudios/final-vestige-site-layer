## Verifications on 30 October 2026
- The [official Focus Shot page](https://brainwaveshots.com/focus-shot) describes 15/30-minute MP3 sessions, brainwave-only variants, a brown-noise/mountain soundscape, a purported 12-to-18 Hz protocol, extensive focus claims and a one-year refund promise.
- The actual [Brainwave Shots FAQ](https://brainwaveshots.com/faq) describes **isochronic tones**, says headphones are not required, warns about epilepsy and activities needing alertness, limits refunds to one per six-month period, and imposes a further limitation on some large orders. Its cautionary text conflicts with broad '100% safe' advertising language. We report the actual caution instead.
- [2024 auditory stimulation review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38458383/) of 31 studies: mixed/insufficient arousal-cognition results. [2023 binaural EEG review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37205669/): inconsistent outcomes. [2023 binaural attention/memory meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35842538/): some positive pooled evidence with mixed results. **Binaural research is not a product-specific trial of the merchant's declared isochronic audio.**
- Original change-blindness sources: [Simons and Levin, 1998](https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03208840); [Rensink, O'Regan and Clark, 1997](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.1467-9280.1997.tb00427.x/).
- [FTC endorsements/affiliate disclosures](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) and [FTC health claims](https://search.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance).
- Authenticated WordPress reads confirm 61 published posts, 153-row published Puységur source page and the published 52-page Focus Fieldbook. The public site blocked direct web retrieval; live WordPress connector supplied actual content.

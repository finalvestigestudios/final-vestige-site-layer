Maintain identical prominent product facts, adjacent disclosures, free alternatives, CTA positioning, timing, exact merchant link and source citations. A visitor's ability to decline must not be obscured. Device/mobile performance and native keyboard/screen-reader behavior must pass first. The new R148 decision-integrity copy is an **editorial proposal** for a later fixed-copy comparison, not another variable introduced simultaneously into the existing A/B trial.

**Preconditions:** full legal merchant approval, official affiliate URL, business release approval, actual licensed media and subtitle files, consent/assignment validation, absence of duplicate analytics, stage link and refund check, browser a11y and Lighthouse QA, sample-size threshold documented *before* exposure. If these cannot be met, run **no experiment**. No sequential peeking/p-hacking, post hoc test redesign or statistical superiority claims on tiny samples.

Before you buy a twelve-minute meditation audio

The hard decision is not whether you can find twelve minutes. It is what you expect the recording to do. A repeatable listening ritual might be convenient; a claim that a special frequency automatically improves concentration demands specific evidence. These are different reasons to spend money.

Try the free version first

Choose an ordinary, safe task and work on the first concrete step for twelve minutes. Write down what you did. On another day, use ordinary background sound or silence. This is a personal habit check, not a blinded experiment or a test of cognitive ability. The existing 52-page Focus Fieldbook provides seven days of prompts and an audio-claims checklist using one existing Jetpack signup.

Why this evidence matters

In a 400-person randomized trial, a slow breathing practice was not detectably better for reported stress than a matched guided breathing comparison. A separate 153-person balanced-placebo study investigated expectancy in brief mindfulness and experimental pain. A 2023 EEG evidence review reported contradictory binaural-beat results.

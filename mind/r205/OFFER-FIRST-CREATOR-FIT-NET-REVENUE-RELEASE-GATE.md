## The commercially meaningful question

**What is the offer for a person already looking for a manageable, structured meditation-audio routine?** The prospect may be considering whether a group of short recordings is worth a purchase. They are not necessarily looking for medical treatment, a promise that a song will raise intelligence, or an interpretation of an eighteenth-century document as modern clinical evidence. Our hypothesis is that independent buying guidance can attract *qualified* visitors and earn their trust without making prohibited or unsupported claims.

The primary intended offer is **Zen12 Premium**, subject to actual publisher authorization and validated checkout. An eligible rights-cleared FV-owned digital Mind SKU would take precedence for a near-term paid-purchase test if affiliate approval is unavailable **and** the SKU, rights, published listing, fulfillment, price, refund policies and authoritative Payhip purchase records are verified. No such owned Mind SKU is authenticated in this run. Do not pretend the ordinary existence of Final Vestige digital products is proof that a particular Mind offer is sellable or belongs to the correct audience.

## One audience, one independent pre-sell

**Audience:** Organic viewers or search readers who already want to know whether a structured 12-minute meditation-audio library is worth purchasing. **Desired decision:** Compare package versus unsubstantiated outcome claims, inspect free alternatives, and—only after authorization—consider an optional merchant link.

The original [R205 independent A guide](zen12-evidence-A-TEXT-UNPUBLISHED.html) opens with a testable consumer tension: the experience of an involuntary-seeming pendulum movement is not proof of the source of its effects. It uses the [2012 Gauchou](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22377138/) and [2022 Moga and Dienes](https://doi.org/10.1080/00029157.2021.2010641) studies as a vivid source-grounded micro-story, not a diagnosis or a vendor endorsement. It offers a three-column exercise for comparing **what I want**, **what the seller supplies** and **what requires outcome evidence**. It clearly acknowledges limitations, objections, free alternatives and unverified price/refund evidence.

The [R205 B guide](zen12-evidence-B-OPTIONAL-42S-UNPUBLISHED.html) retains identical core purchasing guidance and adds only a **42-second, original, optional film concept**: a self-contained static poster, full narration and proposed captions in a source-controlled WebVTT. No MP4 exists; nothing plays or autoplays. No JS, external video host or new font is required. A complete text-only fallback is provided. This is not an implemented media test or evidence that film will improve conversions.

Each guide has a native **disabled** merchant button. A plain-language prospective disclosure is immediately next to the disabled control:

> If you purchase through a verified Final Vestige earning link in the future, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. There is no active earning link on this page.

Before any active recommendation, the disclosure must be rechecked in its actual visual context and against the exact issued link, and the merchant's written terms must be reviewed. General footer disclosure alone is inadequate. The two independent guides may be published only when a founder-approved release is authorized; until then, they are internal source-controlled review assets, not available customer destinations.

## Hypothesis, units and acceptance (avoid vanity)

**A/B controlled hypothesis:** Holding copy, offer, disclosure, CTA placement, source list, price caveats and mobile performance constant, an **optional** original 42-second film could improve independently confirmed *paid conversion per qualified human session* compared with the same evidence guide without video. It may have no effect or reduce conversion. The primary metric is not watch time, click-through rate, average page position or impressions.

Use randomized allocation only *after* release approvals, consent/privacy review and stage QA. Define assignment and exposure using existing first-party instrumentation, not a new analytics service. If existing tools cannot support defensible assignment, choose an explicitly **observational** comparison and label it as such, not a randomized test. Hold channel, time and creative intent stable where possible. Avoid accidental systematic differences between A and B.

**First-party acquisition contract**: `utm_source=<actual platform>`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11` (or authorized campaign), `utm_content=<immutable real Grok asset_id>`. Link only to already live routes. Do not invent IDs or append prohibited parameters to TikTok/YouTube links that do not accept direct outbound. Never include an email address, personal identifiers or private tokens in UTM parameters. If Grok uses a bio link, document the intermediate route and acknowledge any attribution loss.

**Event contract, strictly separated:** `campaign_landing` (qualified human session, when trustworthy), `article_to_fieldbook_click` (a visit/navigation event, NOT a lead), `confirmed_signup` (authoritative Jetpack confirmed record with appropriate user consent, NOT PDF display), `affiliate_click` (only actual verified outbound on an activated earning URL, NOT a sale), `merchant_paid_conversion` (merchant-reported confirmed paid order or earned commission using the merchant's authoritative ID and settlement status), `refund_or_reversal` and `net_revenue`. Checkout initiation is its own event and is not interchangeable with paid order. For owned Payhip, rely on authenticated order evidence; for Inspire3, rely on account partner reporting and reconcile returns/fees.

**Primary KPI:** realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human landing sessions = (settled proceeds after refunds, platform/payment charges, commissions/fees and attributable costs × 1,000) / independently supported qualified-human sessions. Affiliate merchant payouts are not the same as gross store order value. Use **7-day and 30-day cohorts** based on actual exposure and merchant attribution windows. A cohort with tiny samples should display absolute counts and a prominent uncertainty warning. No division by unverified pageviews. If the denominator or payable earnings cannot be reconciled, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0 and never a fabricated conversion rate.

**Measurement privacy:** Retain the shortest viable campaign key and aggregate topic/page counts; do not send IP addresses, precise location, email, names, private checkout tokens, medical inference, session replay or cross-site fingerprinting. Audit existing Jetpack/Ahrefs configuration before contemplating any change. No duplicate script, third-party analytics vendor, paid SaaS or new cookie system. No consent-free persistent cross-site identifier. Implement only through Claude's established analytics engineering and independently approved site QA.

01 · Perception & attention

What escaped notice?

During a change-blindness experiment, the observer can be looking directly at a scene and still miss a substantial change. That does not make memory useless; it makes the conditions of observation part of the evidence.

04 · Tools under review

Before an audio can earn a recommendation

Zen12, Hypnosis Live and Brainwave Shots sell different kinds of audio. We compare their stated contents and terms against independent evidence. The finding that hypnosis has been studied does not establish the effectiveness of any particular retail recording.

Questions to ask before paying

What precise result is promised? Was this recording tested? Against what control? Are claims about safety, duration or better performance supported? Which refund restrictions apply? Is the checkout actually the one approved for this publisher? Would a free timer meet the immediate need?

The evidence beside the claim

For hypnosis research, our starting point is the 2024 overview of 49 meta-analyses and 261 distinct studies, with substantial study overlap and only nine high-quality meta-analyses. It found its stronger evidence in medical-procedure and pain contexts. That evidence cannot be carried over to a commercial concentration recording without additional work.

Read the scientific overview. FTC guidance likewise asks reviewers to keep material commercial relationships close to the actual recommendation and not imply benefits the evidence cannot establish.

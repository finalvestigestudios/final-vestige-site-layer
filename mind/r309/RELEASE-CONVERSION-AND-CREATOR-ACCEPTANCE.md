## Text versus film experimental protocol

**Hypothesis:** Among already eligible, higher-intent buyers, a short optional original film explaining the *buying decision* might improve independently verified net revenue per qualified visitor versus a source-only guide. It might equally distract people, slow the page, or lower purchase intent. No uplift is promised.

A and B contain identical source-backed core copy, free four-minute demonstration, seller-format distinctions, buyer objections, free Fieldbook alternative, truly disabled CTA and prospective FTC disclosure. B adds only a static original 42-second poster, timed narration and transcript treatment. **B is not yet playable video and no live experiment has run.**

For any later filmed asset, require a rights-controlled original MP4/WebM, descriptive poster, VTT captions, transcript and native user-controlled playback. No autoplay, forced preload, third-party media tracking, obscure close control, layout shift or heavy JavaScript. Browser and no-JS review must test 360, 390, 768, 1440, 1920 pixels, keyboard/screen-reader navigation, reduced motion, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, links and mobile performance. The disabled button and disclosure should remain meaningful in every environment; backend/merchant approvals are **separate** from accessibility acceptance.

When the signed earning link and sales instrumentation are approved, pre-register exact attribution, randomization, primary outcome (confirmed net sales, not outbound clicks), risk limits and stopping rules. Do not declare success from clickthrough rates, a short-run view increase or an illustrative study.

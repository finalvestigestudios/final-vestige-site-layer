Our promise is not to sell a better brain. It is to make the evidence itself the experience.

How to read this archive

Every argument should distinguish a historical primary source, a modern experimental result, a seller's description, and our editorial interpretation. A real experience does not by itself demonstrate its proposed mechanism. An experiment can be rigorous and still be irrelevant to a particular marketed MP3.

Our commercial editor may eventually recommend a contextual third-party product. Any such link requires a plain-language commission disclosure next to the recommendation, product-specific evidence review, authentic publisher authorization and founder release. The investigation conclusions must remain independent of sales.

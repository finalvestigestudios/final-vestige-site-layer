## Privacy-preserving measurement and settlement
Use existing, approved first-party analytics only; **do not add another script**. Standard campaign query parameters on permitted landing URLs:
`utm_source=(youtube|tiktok|instagram)`
`utm_medium=organic_social`
`utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`
`utm_content=<Grok verified immutable asset_id>`
Normalize and validate asset IDs and paths with Claude. UTM strings are labels, not visitor identity. TikTok clickability/bio-routing limitations must be documented. Exclude bot/test/internal traffic only through predeclared transparent criteria; do not pretend a pageview is a qualified human.

Events must remain distinct:
- `qualified_session`: privacy-safe, bot-filtered deduplicated session denominator; source attribution may be **unknown**.
- `article_to_focus_click`: clicked existing free resource; **not** signup.
- `confirmed_lead`: only a provider-confirmed Jetpack subscription; preserve one list and existing welcome; no PII in event attributes.
- `affiliate_click`: authorized link outbound; **not** sale.
- `verified_checkout_start`: report only where independently observable.
- `merchant_confirmed_purchase`: merchant/provider transaction proof, settlement status, referrer permission, SKU and signed report; cannot infer from cookies or UTM.
- `refund`/`fee`: reconciled merchant statements, negative net adjustment.

**North-star:** realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions = `(settled_gross_orders_or_commissions - refunds - merchant_fees - attributable_variable_costs) / qualified_sessions * 1000`. If either settled numerator or trustworthy denominator is missing, publish **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0, and separately show unknown/unattributed. Publish paid conversion rate and lead-to-customer only where customer/order matching is authorized and privacy-safe. Display 7-day and 30-day cohorts with tiny-sample warnings, censoring/return lags and missing attribution. Do not connect accounts or expose sub-IDs, email addresses, IPs or private affiliate terms in public GitHub.

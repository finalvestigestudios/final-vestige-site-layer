Case note / An original observation

Three questions for every impressive claim

Read an exciting historical claim or a modern product headline. Write the observation in one sentence, the proposed explanation in a second, and the comparison that would test it in a third.

Evidence is strongest when the conditions that could mislead it remain visible.

A signed witness statement establishes that a statement existed. A recording advertised as twelve minutes long establishes its format. Neither, on its own, establishes the claimed cause or psychological result.

Commerce separate from investigation / HOLD

A guide to buying attention audio without buying the promise

Is your obstacle finding twelve minutes, or knowing whether the recording delivers the claimed result? The seller can describe the format; independent evidence must address the effect. For Zen12, the seller's complete-course checkout describes twelve levels and 48 MP3 recordings. That does not mean all tracks are guided meditation, nor does it verify a claim that twelve minutes replaces an hour of ordinary practice.

The review-only guide asks about package scope, playback rights, the specific claims made, the conditions of any guarantee and how a free twelve-minute practice session compares. No product-specific outcome has been independently demonstrated here.

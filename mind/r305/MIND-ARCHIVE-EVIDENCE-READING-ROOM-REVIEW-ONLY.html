A question can become part of the evidence.

In 1974, researchers reported that the verb used to ask about a collision influenced later eyewitness responses about the event. In a 2003 study, people who compared accounts sometimes later reported details their partners—not they—had seen. A source's journey matters as much as its dramatic ending.

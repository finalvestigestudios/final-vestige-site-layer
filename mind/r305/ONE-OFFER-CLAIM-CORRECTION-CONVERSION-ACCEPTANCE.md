## Executive decision
Do not activate an Inspire3 earning link. This sprint repaired a material fact error in R304's buyer page: the 2012 positive guessed-answer study used a **Ouija board**, whereas the 2022 follow-up used **Chevreul's pendulum** and did not reproduce the advantage. The distinction is documented in [Gauchou et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22377138/) and [Moga & Dienes, 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35184700/). This is not a flaw in meditation audio and neither study tested Zen12; it is an editorial integrity correction before a buyer encounters the commercial guide.

The pre-existing R304 Zen12 presell is the **one buyer-guide pilot**. Its accurate original information remains reusable: seller checkout describes the complete course as twelve levels and 48 MP3 files, while the main page describes multiple styles, not solely guided tracks. Preserve the original prior-page interface, noindex, genuinely disabled purchase button and nearby prospective affiliate disclosure. This branch corrects the already reviewed R304 file in place rather than creating a competing sales page. The canonical earlier R284 text/video A/B is not modified; any later experiment must explicitly supersede or reuse it, never run several pilots against each other.

This workstream's original R305 investigation, *The Refusal the Magnetizer Recorded*, is editorial. It contains no product endorsement. The companion /mind/ HTML research room has a separate, clearly labeled commercial section but no executable merchant checkout. That distinction is deliberate: no interpretation of Puységur's case files should become an implicit argument that a modern recording can control people or cure illness.

## Actual high-intent audience and buyer task
**Audience:** English-reading adults already researching a short structured meditation-audio library because they want a repeatable, practical listening routine. Do not presume psychological diagnosis, anxiety treatment, cognitive deficit or clinical need. Their real question is not 'How can I supercharge my brain?' It is 'Does paying for a packaged audio format fit how I want to practice, and do the evidence and terms support its advertised claims?'

**Primary provisional offer:** Zen12 complete audio library, only after FV's publisher approval and license are authenticated. [Zen12 official public overview](https://zen12.com/) advertises a variety of levels and listening styles; [exact full-course checkout](https://zen12.com/getstarted) lists 12 levels and 48 MP3 files plus guides/support/bonus material. The seller's current dynamic discount and countdown are not stable evergreen FV copy. Do not quote a price or give an unverified guarantee in the pilot until the exact route, region, refund conditions and timing are rechecked under approved terms. The checkout's visible year-long guarantee is merchant text, not an independent payout or customer-service verification.

**Free comparison:** [Seven Day Focus Fieldbook](https://finalvestige.com/focus/), an already published free 52-page guide, provides a twelve-minute starting routine, daily sessions, worksheets, a thirty-day log and questions for checking audio claims. It is *not* an audio product or a clinically validated alternative treatment. Its delivery through the existing Jetpack/Dispatch welcome funnel must not be replaced or multiplied.

**Secondary comparisons:** [Hypnosis Live Total Focus](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus) is described by its merchant as a 33-minute, 47 MB self-hypnosis recording. [Brainwave Shots Focus Shot](https://brainwaveshots.com/focus-shot) offers seller-described 15- and 30-minute audio versions with brainwave-only files. These are different products, formats, mechanisms and refund regimes. None is currently an activated affiliate destination, nor is its advertised mental or performance efficacy independently substantiated for FV.

## Problem → insight → decision copy
**Candidate headline:** 'You can buy twelve minutes of listening. You cannot buy a guarantee of twelve minutes of change.'

**Candidate lead:** 'It is frustrating to try meditating when you cannot settle on a method. A downloadable library gives you a simple format: press play, choose a style, repeat. But a format that is easy to use is not the same thing as a psychological result that science has proved. Look at what the seller supplies, ask what you need from the routine, and compare it with one free twelve-minute session before choosing.'

**Useful micro-story:** In 2012 researchers reported an unusual information result using a Ouija board. In 2022 a follow-up using a pendulum did not reproduce the advantage. The studies were not equivalent and neither tested meditation audio. The lesson is not that audio must fail; it is that **the device, procedure, comparison and promised outcome all matter**.

**Free demonstration (no therapeutic claim):** Name a small unfinished task. Set a twelve-minute timer using any timer you already own. Work only on a defined first step. Write down what happened and whether the format felt manageable. This measures task follow-through in that one session, not meditation benefits, neurophysiology or a medical condition. No account, product download, special audio or purchase required.

**Purchase-objection response:** 'Does the advertised brainwave effect work?' Answer: The seller describes brainwave mechanisms, but generalized studies of meditation or sound stimulation do not independently establish Zen12's proprietary efficacy. 'Do I need every recording?' Answer: The complete course is seller-listed as 48 MP3 files across 12 levels, with different listening styles; readers should check which tracks they actually expect to use. 'What happens if it is not for me?' Answer: Check the seller's exact refund, billing, taxes and support terms on the live checkout before paying; FV does not promise the outcome or administer merchant refunds. 'Why not just meditate for free?' Answer: You can. A paid library is an optional convenience/format choice, not a requirement.

**Prospective earning disclosure immediately above the eventual approved CTA:** 'If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.' Until permission and a real issued URL are verified, a disabled button must say 'Purchase unavailable — partner review pending'; the actual earning URL remains absent from both markup and analytics.

The independent article must NOT end with this button, and any affiliate card in a future guide must remain visually distinct from the article's evidentiary conclusion.

## Original 42-second video hypothesis — A/B acceptance
**Variant A:** Exactly the full truthful text guide plus source links, disabled/authorized CTA according to approval state and near-link disclosure.

**Variant B:** The **same** text guide, same CTA position, disclosure and load constraints, with a user-invoked 42-second original explanatory video above the detailed comparison. The old 42-second R304 WebVTT treatment and poster concept are only production instructions. **No playable film currently exists**. A transcript/no-JS alternative remains readable even if video never loads. Native controls, zero autoplay, descriptive poster, captions aligned to actual spoken/audio content, keyboard focus and reduced-motion compatibility are mandatory.

**A/B hypothesis, not prediction:** An optional original explanation could increase buyer understanding and verified purchase intent, or distract, slow the page and *reduce* purchases. Never characterize the video variant as proven higher converting.

**Pretest QA gates:** same exact offered product and copy across variants; no new merchant affiliate code between assignments; assignments stable only as permitted by privacy/consent rules; no duplicate tracking stack; keyboard/VoiceOver/TalkBack/manual mobile browser checks; 360/390/768/1440/1920 pixel views; no layout shift or accidental audio playback; real page weight/LCP/CLS/INP review; links verified without a signed-in WordPress session; clear disabled state; proper external-link policy once authorized. Before release, Claude and Site QA compare preview against the actual sanitized WordPress export. Source-string lint cannot certify any of those.

**Primary experiment outcome:** Partner-confirmed, attributable, valid *paid* orders, followed by cleared FV commissions net of adjustments. Secondary: confirmed subscriber, qualified-human visits and earning-link outbound clicks. A click is not an order; a sale that has not settled is not realized revenue.

**Stop/rollback:** freeze or revert if merchant permissions change, an undisclosed link goes live, the seller's package diverges, the CTA misroutes, privacy consent fails, hidden or misleading claims appear, keyboard controls are unusable, or the video harms mobile performance. Do not continually adapt the test based on one or two purchases. Maintain 7-/30-day cohort reporting and show sample sizes and uncertainty; any small cohort is descriptive only, not statistical proof of uplift.

## Single first-party measurement contract; no extra script
Input keys: approved literal asset_id, utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign, with a controlled finite list and no personally identifying URL parameters. Query data must not encode addresses, emails, subscriber IDs, device fingerprinting or order IDs. Claude owns site/analytics production instrumentation and consent policies; this lane provides requirements and test assertions only.

Event distinctions: (1) qualified-human landing session by original creator asset, (2) relevant CTA activation, (3) confirmed Jetpack subscriber from authenticated provider if observable and appropriately consented, (4) affiliate_outbound_click **only if authorized**, (5) merchant-confirmed paid conversion, (6) actual settled affiliate commission and corrections. A verified paid *owned-product* checkout/order is a separate seller record. Do not merge it with affiliate sales.

**Accounting:** FV affiliate revenue means cleared attributable FV commission, *not* total merchant order value. If the source already reports net cleared commission after reversals/fees, do not subtract them twice. Record gross commission, reversals, fees and net settlement only where available and mutually reconciled. Net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1000 × realized FV attributable net revenue / verified qualified-human sessions for matching 7/30-day cohorts. If numerator or denominator lacks authoritative support, status is **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not zero.

## Precise privacy and revenue contract (proposal, not instrumentation deployed)

**Publisher identity:** No personal emails, names, IP addresses, cookies, fingerprints, hashed subscriber identities or customer IDs in campaign URLs, files, reports or social links. Respect consent/preferences and platform terms. No duplicate analytics scripts; first audit existing first-party/Jetpack mechanisms.

**Grok-origin query example, placeholders only:** `utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11&utm_content=ASSET_ID_FROM_GROK`. Do not deploy placeholders. Source/medium/campaign/asset_id must match actual published media and link placement; preserve existing canonical URLs.

**Event definitions in order**:

1. `campaign_landing`: a qualified human session on a live page, tagged only by a real verified `asset_id`, platform, campaign and date bucket; bot suspicion and sample limitations recorded.
2. `article_to_resource_click`: a confirmed first-party CTA interaction where existing tools allow measurement. Click ≠ signup.
3. `confirmed_signup`: only a native Jetpack or other authoritative completed-confirmation record, with consent. Email list count ≠ attribution.
4. `offer_view`: qualified presell visit, not checkout.
5. `affiliate_outbound`: properly authorized disclosed click to exact merchant link; never call this a sale, and only count if pre-approved first-party aggregate tracking is technically/legally appropriate.
6. `merchant_confirmed_order`: independently merchant-recorded paid purchase with actual transaction time; commission attribution needs publisher-specific partner records, not visitor inference.
7. `settlement`: reconciled realized revenue net of refunds, fees, reversals and applicable platform charges. Report non-attributable merchant orders in separate aggregates, no made-up joins.

**Aggregate cohort ledger**: `date_window=7d|30d`, `campaign`, `asset_id` (from Grok), `qualified_human_sessions`, `offer_views`, `verified_resource_clicks`, `confirmed_leads`, `merchant_paid_orders`, `independently_reported_affiliate_paid_orders`, `gross_paid`, `refunds`, `processing_fees`, `settled_net`, `confidence`, `attribution_gaps`. Default all unavailable numbers to **null**, never fabricated zero. A self-reported partner dashboard number may be usable once authentically reviewed and permissioned; a third-party click is never a payout.

**Primary KPI** = `1000 × realized_settled_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions` for attributable 7/30d cohorts only if denominator > 0 and merchant-reported net is available; otherwise **NOT_COMPUTABLE**. Secondary: paid conversion rate (confirmed paid orders / qualified sessions), confirmed customer/order counts, gross and net revenue, lead-to-customer rate where safely attributable. Carry confidence intervals or explicit **tiny-sample warning** for small denominators. Never infer profit from a tracked visit or click.

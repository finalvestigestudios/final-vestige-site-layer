## First-party attribution — no duplicate script

URL pattern, **only after Grok provides real IDs**:  
`?utm_source=PLATFORM&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=mind_focus_pilot_2026_11&utm_content=REAL_GROK_ASSET_ID`

Use the site's existing first-party analytics channel if an event path is already supported; do not insert another analytics beacon. Minimum schema: `event_name`, UTC day, `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`/asset_id, destination route, offer_id, CTA placement, consent/QA source, verification method. **Never send:** email address, names, query strings that contain private fields, account IDs, full IPs or third-party user-level cross-site identity. Respect consent and retention defaults. No fingerprinting.

Record events as separate facts: `campaign_landing` (not always human) → `qualified_human_session` (must meet independently defined filtering) → `article_to_focus_click` → `jetpack_confirmed_signup` (only verified) → `offer_guide_view` → `affiliate_outbound` (only if earning link authorized) → `merchant_confirmed_purchase` or `partner_confirmed_paid_commission` → `refund`, `platform_fee`, `settled_net_revenue`. **No automated inference from earlier event to later event.**

**Primary KPI formula:** 1,000 × (settled attributable gross paid receipts − validated refunds − seller/merchant/affiliate fees) ÷ independently counted qualified human sessions in the defined cohort. This applies to *FV revenue*, not the merchant's gross order value for an affiliate purchase. For affiliate revenue, the revenue amount is actually earned/settled commission; unknown settlement is UNKNOWN. Weekly scorecard covers 7-day and 30-day cohorts, small-denominator warning, missing-source share, paid order count, payer count where lawfully aggregated, confirmed lead count, lead-to-paid conversion (only if joinable), gross/net reconciliation and hours spent. **Present numerator and denominator as null, not 0, when missing.**

## A/B — text-only vs optional video

**Decision to test later, not now:** reuse the exact Zen12 Premium buyer guide's A text-only control and B identical buyer content with one **original optional 30–45-second video**, once the named product, partner permission and audience fit are documented. Exclude external video embeds, auto-play, large JS bundles, autoplay sound, cookie-heavy viewers and motion-dependent information. The B variant must have a static poster, native controls with no autoplay, complete captions, plain transcript and text equivalence; both work without JS. A must not be starved of facts, price transparency, alternatives or source links.

**Hypothesis:** a video showing an ethical, sourced micro-story and a free attention observation may increase *merchant-confirmed net revenue per qualified human session*, but it may also reduce speed, trust or purchase completion. The hypothesis is not proof. Run true random assignment only after founder release approval, traffic/privacy acceptance and real merchant attribution. Keep offer, claim disclosures and CTAs identical. Guardrail tests: text-only reading time, 360/390/768/1440 keyboard/touch, captions, transcript parity, no forced playback, LCP/transfer weight, saved page accessibility, no-JS, disclosure visible without relying on script. Evaluate 7/30-day results descriptively; with tiny samples do not report statistical winner. **Neither variant has yet undergone a real traffic test.**

Begin with a question you can test

Before opening your next social video or unfamiliar claim, write down its most important assertion. Can the cited source show the claim actually occurred? Is the proposed cause independently established? Which failure or contrary observation would change your mind? These three questions work in historical investigations and commercial product reviews alike.

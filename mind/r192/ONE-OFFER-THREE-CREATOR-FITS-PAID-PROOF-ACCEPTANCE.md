## An actual high-intent landing guide's promise

The buying guide should lead with one concrete question: **"A 12-minute ritual sounds convenient. Does the evidence show what that 12 minutes can actually do?"** Its independent narrative hook can reference how attention/ownership effects reveal the gap between an experiment and a product claim, while explicitly rejecting that an unrelated illusion establishes Zen12 efficacy.

The guide must state, as **seller claims only**, the Premium 12-level/48-MP3 content, formats, guide material and advertised guarantee. Show a free personal routine-fit exercise (three quiet 12-minute sessions, note convenience and preference, no implied diagnostic result), what buyers can verify before purchase, who may prefer a free timer and silent practice, and what independent science does not establish. Do not insert testimonials, paid urgency, promised productivity, cures, guaranteed meditation equivalence or a sales price unless verified against current checkout. Preserve accessible primary sources and editorial standards link.

**Mandatory contextual commission disclosure beside an activated link** (after approval only): "If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you." Include rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer" and clear descriptive accessible anchor text naming seller/product; avoid URL cloaking or unapproved redirect/tracking. The current pre-launch control must remain **<button type="button" disabled>Merchant offer unavailable pending verification</button>** with the disclosure visible to disclose the proposed relationship honestly; do not visually fake a disabled button. A separate general disclosure page alone is insufficient (FTC official guidance).

## Controlled A/B hypothesis and stop rules

**Hypothesis, not a promised lift:** Adding an entirely optional original 42-second explainer to an otherwise identical text buyer guide may reduce confusion about what is being sold; it may also reduce completed purchases if it distracts, adds load or undermines trust. The primary endpoint is confirmed **net revenue per 1,000 qualified humans**, supported by confirmed paid order rate and refunds. Secondary diagnostic endpoints: on-page scroll depth, CTA clicks, opt-in confirmations if separately observed, mobile load and accessibility complaints. Pre-register an equal allocation, attribution rules, minimum-information period and exclusion treatment before launch; randomization should not create user fingerprinting. No fake "winner" from tiny cohorts. If merchant/qualified-session data are absent, the result is **INCONCLUSIVE** regardless of outbound click rates.

Video gating: original permitted footage or abstract SVG, responsive poster, optional <video controls preload="none" playsinline> only after MP4/WebM and accessible captions/transcript exist, no autoplay, no forced download, reduced-motion safe, no focus traps. At 360, 390, 768, 1440px test headings, contrast, keyboard, screen readers, disclosure visibility, no-JS, media handling and blocked button. Measure CWV/LCP/CLS INP with actual browser and report methodology; never equate V8 string checks with WCAG acceptance.

**Controlled stop:** Evidence of material merchant mismatch, unsafe substantiation, hidden or broken disclosure, an unapproved earning URL, unlawful PII, a broken mobile purchase path, refund terms not aligned with seller, or unexplained settlement discrepancies stops activation and triggers owner review. Conversion pressure never overrides scientific accuracy, consent or commercial authorization.

## Privacy-safe attribution: strictly separated event definitions
```
utm_source=(youtube|tiktok|instagram|facebook|pinterest|x|bluesky)
utm_medium=organic_social
utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11
utm_content=<actual_grok_immutable_asset_id>
event=campaign_landing         // only first-party qualifying observable event
event=article_to_focus_click   // intent; not a confirmed subscriber
event=confirmed_signup         // only native confirmed Jetpack evidence, no email in data export
event=affiliate_click          // only allowed and approved first-party event, no purchase inference
event=merchant_confirmed_order // only trusted seller/partner outcome, not site click
event=merchant_refund          // trusted seller record
event=commission_settled       // partner financial confirmation; not estimated EPC
```
Do **not** invent an asset ID to fill a template. Validate platform, campaign and content fields against published Grok manifest and an allowlist (e.g. `^[a-z0-9_-]{1,80}$` for IDs). Never put email, names, IP, purchase details or free-text answers into URLs. Do not add scripts while Claude's single first-party instrumentation and consent decision remain under #820. Avoid custom cookies/fingerprinting; aggregate anonymous campaign counts where legally and technically acceptable. Audit bot/internal/test traffic exclusions and nonhuman spike contamination before using denominator. Consent for any tracking that requires it must be respected.

**Reporting contract:** verified paid conversion rate = merchant-confirmed attributable paid orders ÷ qualified-human sessions; net realized revenue = reconciled settled gross proceeds/commissions **minus** refunds, platform/processing fees, and other evidenced variable costs for the same cohort (if missing cost inputs, label net UNKNOWN, not 0). `net_revenue_per_1000 = 1000 * realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions` **only if** both validated. `lead_to_customer` only if safely joined at an aggregate level with authority; no buyer PII exported to the editorial workstream. Separate 7-day and 30-day acquisition cohorts; avoid counting same transaction twice or treating email confirmations as orders. Include tiny-sample interval/caution, channel and experimental-variant metadata when reliable.

## Merchant privacy and ethical claims
https://inspire3.com/privacy/ says the merchant may share purchaser **name, email and purchase details** with affiliates. That is not permission for FV to store them. A privacy-preserving reporting agreement or restricted aggregate settlement report is a separate approval gate. Product recommendations must be independent and explain that the buyer can decline. Avoid sales insinuations, hidden urgency, wealth/brain rewiring/medical guarantees, fabricated use, seller testimonials represented as FV, and unspecified commissions. > **Disclosure:** If you buy through an approved link here, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We have not independently tested the recording, and this guide is not medical advice.

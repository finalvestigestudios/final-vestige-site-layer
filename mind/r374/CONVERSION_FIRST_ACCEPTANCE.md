## Controlled buyer experiment (hypothesis, not a converter)

**Variant A**: self-contained source-rich text-only buyer guide. **Variant B**: identical substantive HTML body and seller limitations, plus one original 42-second storyboard with SVG poster, text transcript and six WebVTT cues. No external JS, iframe, fonts, third-party tracker, autoplay, playable film or affiliate link. A shared guide body was programmatically compared before commits to avoid a copywriting confound. Film is **NOT** recorded, produced, hosted or tested; the VTT is a timed writing treatment until matched to actual speech.

**Hypothesis:** After rights and production QA, an optional, user-initiated 42-second film may help a high-intent visitor understand the distinction between a trained skill and an audio product. It could also worsen page performance or create distrust. A/B comparison must evaluate **verified net revenue**, not clickthrough alone. The pilot must not run until a real payable offer, actual merchant reporting and privacy-safe variant allocation exist. Do not interpret any early apparent lift with tiny sample sizes as significant.

**Pre-registered primary metric** = realized net revenue / qualified-human sessions × 1,000, net of confirmed refunds, partner fees and any other attributable variable costs, with cohort periods 7 and 30 days and separately reported marketing/tool costs. Seller-confirmed paid orders or partner-reported attributed paid commissions are required. Report currency and refunds; *pending payouts are not settled revenue*. Do not claim commissions from outbound clicks.

**Secondary, separately labelled**: verified paid conversion rate, unique paid customers, confirmed signup where Jetpack provider evidence exists, qualified human session-to-CTA click rate, and lead-to-confirmed-customer conversion where match consent permits. Social impressions/followers only diagnostic.

**Decision thresholds:** Founder/Commerce must explicitly approve practical minimum cohort sizes, measurement coverage, data minimization and a minimum acceptable net margin before anyone tests. If there are no trustworthy paid transactions or qualified-human denominators, write **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not 0%, even when an exploratory event stream contains click counts.

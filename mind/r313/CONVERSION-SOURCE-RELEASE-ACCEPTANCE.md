## High-intent audience and honest message

Audience: a viewer of a real creator-led video about a failure of attention who is interested in testing a short personal practice routine. The legitimate problem is *how do I begin and compare a short listening practice without believing miracles?* The hook is grounded in a 19-second auditory-gorilla experiment (see primary indexed study and 2015 open paper); the demonstration is a free twelve-minute task/observation baseline; the offer value, if later authorized, is a **described collection of audio files and a schedule**, not clinically demonstrated enhanced concentration.

The page answers five purchase objections: What exactly is included? Does it work for this goal? Why pay rather than use a timer/free Fieldbook? What will the final price be? What refund entitlement actually applies? Seller statements are quoted or paraphrased only as seller claims, never personally experienced outcomes. The page must not guarantee relief, ADHD management, wealth, changed brainwaves or productivity.

### A/B hypothesis and release conditions

Variant A: complete text-only research and decision guide. Variant B: **identical buyer text** plus one original static SVG poster, timed 42-second original narration treatment, separate WebVTT concept, visible transcript and no forced playback. Variant B is currently *not an encoded/playable video*. The only meaningful content difference is the optional film section; FTC disclosure, free alternative, disabled purchase control and all terms stay identical.

**Testable hypothesis:** when genuine eligible purchasing is possible and an original film has been encoded, a user-initiated short explainer might improve settled purchase rate per qualified human session, without worsening mobile performance, accessibility or trust. The hypothesis is **not** a claim of uplift. A/B only after written founder approval, accurate merchant pricing and terms, browser and performance QA, a real video with synchronized captions, consistent asset_id/utm_campaign and controlled attribution. Randomization and exposure recording should use Claude's approved first-party instrumentation, not an extra script, invasive cookie or a purchased SaaS tool. Film playback must be optional, with visible controls, no autoplay, reliable transcript, poster, captions and a no-JS equivalent.

Measure qualified-human-session -> guide visit -> relevant free or authorized CTA click -> provider-confirmed lead if applicable -> authenticated paid order/affiliate conversion -> settled commission/FV receipts -> refunds/fees -> realized net. Segment by audience-source and 7/30-day cohort. Do not compare raw clicks or newsletter attempts as commercial conversions. With tiny cohorts or fewer than ten confirmed paid orders in either arm, mark the result directional and extremely uncertain; **ten sales is not a significance threshold**. Run a reproducible sample-size/uncertainty review before concluding an A/B effect.

### Privacy-safe attribution contract (engineering specification, no deployed collector)

Use existing Claude-owned first-party analytics only, with allowlisted fields: asset_id (real, verified immutable publisher ID), utm_campaign, utm_source, utm_medium, landing_path, cohort_day, variant, event_name and aggregate count. Reject arbitrary strings, excessively long values, unexpected destination domains and any user-supplied client assertion of paid orders. Do not collect email, name, IP, raw user identifiers, fingerprints or third-party audience pixels in this Mind prototype. Respect meaningful consent and local requirements.

**Events are different facts:**
- qualified_human_session: approved deduplication/bot-exclusion logic, unique eligible session denominator, first-party attribution only.
- relevant_cta_click: first-party genuine user action, not intent to buy or paid sale.
- lead_confirmed: *Jetpack provider-confirmed* existing subscriber; no duplicate list/welcome email. If provider confirmation not accessible, mark UNKNOWN.
- affiliate_click: only from an allowed authorized merchant link, separate from purchase.
- paid_order_verified: authenticated owned-store order or independently reported affiliate purchase/commission; no client-side assertion or invented order.
- commission_cleared/net_receipt_settled: provider settlement plus adjustments, refunds/chargebacks/fees and currency conversion policy. Do not book gross merchant sales as FV net.

**KPI:** (FV-owned realized net receipts + cleared affiliate commission - relevant fees, refunds and chargebacks) x 1000 / qualified human sessions. If confirmed paid numerator or qualified denominator is not available, **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not "$0". Report separate seven- and thirty-day entry cohorts, cross-domain attribution gaps and small samples. Conversion cannot be inferred by a click ratio.

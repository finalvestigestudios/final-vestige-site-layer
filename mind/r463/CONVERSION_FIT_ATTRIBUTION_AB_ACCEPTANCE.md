## 4. A/B hypothesis with a real purchase denominator

**A (control):** Full independent text-only Zen12 guide.
**B (treatment):** Identical substantive guide plus optional 42-second original visual story, with still SVG poster and accessible transcript. The film must not autoplay and must work in no-JS fallback.

**Hypothesis (not an expected result):** A short original optional explanation may help qualified visitors understand what independent evidence does *not* prove, reduce misleading expectations, and improve verified paid purchase quality relative to text alone. It could instead decrease purchases, increase bounce or distract from the buyer questions. Conversion quality is the constraint; monitor refund-adjusted net revenue, not click-through alone.

**Randomization:** Only after user consent and engineering review, stable privacy-safe variant assignment for a qualified landing session without fingerprinting, IP logging, sensitive traits or cross-site identifiers. Do not add duplicate analytics scripts if Jetpack/site instrumentation already supports the contract. Pre-register allocation, exposure rules, duration and stop conditions; otherwise comparison is descriptive, not causal.

**Cost/performance:** A costs zero video bytes. B starts with an inline original vector poster/transcript (no third-party fonts or assets), then loads self-hosted compressed video *only if activated by the reader*, with native controls, WebVTT captions, transcript and poster. Check 360,390,768,1440,1920px, keyboard focus, skip/headings, color contrast, zoom/reflow, VoiceOver/NVDA, reduced motion, no JS, WP block sanitizer, CSP and Lighthouse/CWV. Run actual browsers before any publish.

**Primary KPI:** 1,000 × verified realized net revenue / eligible qualified-human landing sessions, separately by variant, 7-day and 30-day cohorts. Revenue = independently confirmed cleared commissions or paid merchant orders − attributable refunds − payment/merchant fees (and any recorded fulfillment cost). If settlement or session denominator missing: **NOT COMPUTABLE**, never $0 by assumption. Count deduplicated confirmed orders/customers; distinguish gross from net and event time from settlement time.

**Secondary:** buyer-guide engagement; verified CTA click, independently confirmed Jetpack lead (if observable), merchant checkout-start only if merchant provides it, verified purchase, refund rate, lead-to-customer rate and 7/30d return. Mark unclear identity quality, unknown sales channel and small-N uncertainty. Do not report affiliate_click as paid sale.

THE MIND & PERCEPTION ARCHIVE

How do we know what we saw, felt, heard—or believed?

Every investigation begins with a testable claim. Every archive entry keeps the source closer than the selling proposition. The Mind & Perception Archive connects documented history to contemporary psychology without converting either into a promise of transformation.

History of suggestion

Franklin's blindfolded tests of animal magnetism; Puységur's magnetic somnambulism; the later turn toward psychological accounts. Start with Puységur's Animal Magnetism Files. The new Franklin article is an unpublished editorial candidate, not an active site link.

Perception and attention

Attentional blink, change blindness, inattentional blindness, visual illusions and memory. These experiments show how interpretation and observation can part company; they do not establish a universal power to manipulate minds.

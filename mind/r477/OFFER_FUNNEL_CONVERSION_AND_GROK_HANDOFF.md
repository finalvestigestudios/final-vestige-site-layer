## Business decision, not a traffic vanity report

The highest-intent audience for the *first* payable pilot is a cautious viewer who wants a short, repeatable audio-meditation routine, perhaps after a Grok video about how easily attention and testimony mislead us. Their relevant need is **audio-format and scheduling suitability**, not the ability to purchase a guaranteed cognitive transformation.

Selected possible partner offer: **Zen12 twelve-minute audio sessions**, based strictly on official seller-described structure at https://zen12.com/about. Separate https://zen12.com/secret/ promotional terms are **not** a confirmed authorized SKU or current checkout. A payable, rights-cleared FV-owned Mind SKU remains unverified; pretending it is sellable would invalidate the experiment.

**Evidence of partner status:** Targeted connected Gmail searches on November 18 found one generic Refersion email of August 24, 2026 titled “Your affiliate account is pending.” Reading it confirmed that acceptance would require a later welcome/approval. No subsequent named Inspire3/Final Vestige approval, merchant-issued URL or channel rights was located in the performed searches. This is evidence of **not verified**, not a proof that the merchant could not have approved the account in a different portal.

## Intent and acceptance

Reader-facing hierarchy: published social asset (if any) → *exact corresponding sourced article* → original archival references → optional free [Focus Fieldbook](https://finalvestige.com/focus/) + existing single Jetpack/Dispatch confirmation → optional independent Zen12 buyer guide → **verified authorized** purchase outbound when approval exists. Reader can bypass email signup. No dark patterns, urgency, gatekeeping or asserted personal product use.

**A/B decision hypothesis:** Adding an original, voluntary 42-second documentary-style source note to the same complete Zen12 text guide *might* improve or worsen comprehension and trust for mobile viewers. Outcome is **merchant-confirmed paid order and refund-adjusted net per qualified-human session**, not play count or click-through alone. Keep A/B copy identical apart from the video explanation, preserve FTC disclosure placement and offer wording, and make B readable with no JavaScript or media. Native video player only after a rights-cleared MP4/WebM, caption file, poster, transcript, controls, no autoplay, `preload=none`, no third-party iframe, and measured load cost. R477 B is poster + text only, no playable video. Never infer that this is a tested treatment.

**First-party event contract, engineering only (no new tracking code deployed):**

- `campaign_landing`: anonymous aggregated qualified-human session, `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_<topic>_2026_11`, `utm_content=<grok_verified_asset_id>`, known landing pathname, known variant and timestamp cohort; no name/email/device fingerprint, no unconsented visitor ID.
- `article_to_focus_click`: optional click on existing live /focus/.
- `confirmed_signup`: only from trusted existing Jetpack confirmation evidence. Do not equate form submission, attempted signup, unconfirmed subscriber or an email open.
- `affiliate_outbound`: **only** after exact approved merchant link/channel verified; user-initiated transition, recorded as click, not commission.
- `merchant_confirmed_purchase`: independent merchant sale/order record with monetary amount; do not infer from checkout start or pixel.
- `merchant_settled_affiliate_conversion`: partner-provided attribution and actual commission where verifiable, with fee/refund adjustments.
- `owned_paid_order`: merchant-confirmed paid FV-owned digital SKU, delivery/rights and refund status, if an actual SKU exists. None is established today.

Do not deploy a second Google Analytics, Jetpack, pixels, cookie framework, or external tracking domain. Use existing first-party server/campaign reports if possible. If deterministic merchant-to-asset reconciliation is unavailable, mark revenue **unattributed** rather than reverse-engineering identity. Respect consent and retention requirements; aggregate 7- and 30-day cohorts without storing a cross-site person profile.

### Main KPI and caveats

`net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * (settled_gross_received - refunds - processing_fees - attributable_merchant_fees) / verified_qualified_human_sessions`.

Use **NOT COMPUTABLE** if either numerator evidence or denominator is unverified. Do not silently label missing proof as zero revenue. Report paid conversion rate, confirmed unique paid orders/customers where safely deduplicated, gross/net revenue, lead→customer rate only when linking is permitted, acquisition source confidence, refunds, attribution coverage, 7/30-day windows and extremely wide tiny-sample uncertainty. A one-conversion sample is descriptive, not a statistically proven winner.

Why reader-facing disclosures matter

Before you believe the story, inspect the source

One exercise: separate what a witness directly reported from the question asked, the later interpretation and any failed prediction. Compare the actual documentary evidence to the dramatic summary. Preserve corrections instead of silently rewriting the record.

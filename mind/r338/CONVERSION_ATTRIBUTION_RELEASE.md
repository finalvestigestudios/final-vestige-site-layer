## A/B pre-registration: text guide vs text plus 42-second original film
- **A:** independent evidence-first text guide, package distinctions, free exercise, objections, disclosure and disabled merchant control.
- **B:** exactly the same substantive buyer copy, plus an optional original 42-second illustration concept with static poster, six WebVTT cues and transcript. Current B is **storyboard only, not an actual playable film**. Do NOT claim a tested video conversion improvement.
- **Hypothesis:** once a valid paid route exists, optional relevant short media might clarify the product distinction for qualified users or it might slow the page and reduce purchases. No guaranteed winner; video must have positive net conversion benefit after performance costs, consent and refunds.
- **Controlled test:** after approval and independent QA, randomize qualified first visits with a first-party stable *non-personal* allocation if compliant and consented, otherwise use sequential matched-period comparison explicitly identified as non-random observational evidence. Do not store IP, email, device fingerprint, or visitor identity merely to assign tests.
- **Analysis:** report gross and refund-adjusted paid conversion rates by arm, lead-to-paid purchase rate, net revenue/1,000 qualified-human sessions, 7-/30-day cohorts, device/performance outcomes and refund rates. Avoid declaring significance from trivial counts. Predefine sample-size planning after historical real baseline available. Do not run experiments while button disabled.
- **Performance budget for actual B:** original video max 42 sec, compressed silent motion graphics if possible, poster/track separately, manual controls, no autoplay, preload="none", no third-party player/analytics, size budget agreed with site QA, reduced motion controls, no-JS full copy. 360/390/768/1440/1920 browser testing and captions sync at the final rendered media are required; static SVG/transcript is not a video QA pass.
- **Fairness:** same headline, core buyer copy, disclosure, offer, pricing language, button placement and merchant destination in both arms. No dark-pattern urgency, no countdown, no testimonial, no bait and switch.
- **Stop rules:** cease staged test if a credible complaint reveals misleading efficacy/consent claims, a CTA opens an unauthorized link, pages regress a11y, or merchant tracking and attribution fail. Roll back via normal approved deployment release; never live-edit WordPress independently.

## Privacy-safe funnel contract (requires Claude read-only audit first)
1. \`qualified_human_session\`: denominator only after documented human/bot-filtering and a session definition; do not mix with WordPress pageviews, social views or privacy-invasive fingerprints.
2. \`campaign_landing\`: first-party landing with whitelisted \`utm_source\` (known platform), \`utm_medium=organic_social\`, \`utm_campaign=mind_*_2026_11\`, \`utm_content=<verified existing asset_id>\`; no raw query strings captured beyond permitted campaign keys. For profile/bio links record loss of per-clip attribution and do not backfill it by assumption.
3. \`article_to_resource_click\`: first-party contextual click to existing \`/focus/\`; clicking is not an email lead.
4. \`confirmed_signup\`: only consent-provider proof in existing Jetpack; a form render, subscribe request, thank-you state or PDF download does NOT establish this event. No duplicate form or welcome email.
5. \`affiliate_click\` / \`outbound_offer_click\`: only after FV's issued merchant link and channel authorization are verified. This remains an outbound action, not a sale.
6. \`merchant_confirmed_affiliate_order\` OR \`merchant_confirmed_owned_order\`: independent merchant order record, non-PII aggregate ID and product/SKU verification; never reconstruct from website analytics. Deduplicate returns/cancellations.
7. \`realized_net_revenue\`: settled gross actually attributable to the publisher less processing/payment fees, chargebacks, refunds, applicable costs and commissions if appropriate to publisher economics; track currency and settlement date. Affiliate “sale reported” need not mean commission paid/realized.
8. \`net_revenue_per_1000_human_sessions = 1000 * realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions\`. If no verified denominator or revenue record, mark **NOT COMPUTABLE**. Paid conversion rate = independently verified paid orders divided by genuinely qualified eligible sessions. No zero-for-missing substitution.
9. **7 and 30 day** cohorts based on landing date, published with sample-size/confidence caveats, bot filtering method, attribution gap, and customer-consent status. No fabricated ad ROAS, EPC, payouts, order count, or lead value.
10. Data governance: retain aggregate source/asset/landing/offer/click counts; do not export email addresses, health inferences, query text, cross-device IDs or third-party PII. Use the existing approved analytics configuration and Jetpack provider; no new scripts/SaaS/pixels/merchant redirect. Additional measurement needs founder approval and Claude engineering.

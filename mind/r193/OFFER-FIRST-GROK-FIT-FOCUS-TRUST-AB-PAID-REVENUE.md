## Funnel integrity: the newly documented Focus false-positive

Issue [#820](https://github.com/finalvestigestudios/final-vestige-site-layer/issues/820) records a read-only finding in live Focus page code: an arbitrary `?subscribe=` value, or an `is-subscriber` CSS class, can reveal a gift/download message without a submitted form. This is **not proof** of a successful Jetpack confirmation. It is especially dangerous if a dashboard treats `focus_pdf_click`, visible gift, or URL query flag as a confirmed subscriber.

Therefore the Mind lead ledger is **source-of-truth Jetpack confirmed opt-in only**, after authorization and consent verification. Existing list and welcome message are not modified. Claude owns the Focus engineering correction and analytics semantics; Site QA and Dispatch validate native consent and delivery. Our draft acceptance test must reject all unauthenticated URL/DOM gift-state signals as *paid*, *confirmed signup*, or *verified customer* events.

## Controlled A/B experiment: independent text vs identical text + short optional film

**Hypothesis, not forecast:** An optional original 30–45-second film with poster, captions and transcript might help an already motivated buyer understand the difference between a recording format and a scientifically established outcome. It may also hurt speed or trust. Both outcomes are plausible.

**Variant A:** R188 independent full-text guide. **Variant B:** identical guide, same disclosure, same placement of disabled / later authenticated merchant anchor, plus 42-second original OPTIONAL poster, transcript and eventual non-autoplay video. No autoplay, preload large video or required JS. Ship poster first; only fetch MP4 after deliberate activation, provide WebVTT and a true transcript. This sprint did not render film or use live traffic.

**Randomization once eligible:** server-side or existing native first-party method, consistent 7/30-day cohort allocation without personal tracking. Use anonymous experiment variant and campaign ID only; avoid duplicate analytics scripts or fingerprinting. Before measurement, verify event semantics in existing Claude-owned tracking contract. Measure sample sizes per actual eligible session with confidence intervals; do not crown a winner based on click-through alone or low N.

**Primary commercial endpoint:** *net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions*, from independently verified paid merchant orders and commissions less fees, refunds, chargebacks, and costs. Secondary: paid conversion rate, confirmed customers, confirmed lead→paid customer conversion, observed trusted checkout starts, and verified human visits. CTR, scroll and pageviews are diagnostic only.

`qualified_human_sessions` must be an audited platform/first-party measure; bot traffic, internal review and repeated staging tests cannot be silently included. Use actual `asset_id` and `utm_campaign` if available, but UTM presence alone does not prove human qualification. Join to paid merchant reports only via legitimate consented, merchant-approved mechanism; do not invent user-level attribution from referral data. Aggregated anonymous campaign-level matching is preferable.

**Net definition:** `settled_gross_paid - discounts - confirmed_refunds - payment_and_platform_fees - chargebacks - attributable_variable_costs` (and commission actually paid/owed as supported separately). Avoid double-counting affiliate commissions and FV-owned SKU sales. Mark unmatched merchant sales unattributed instead of assigning them to Grok. Always report 7-day and 30-day cohort windows, denominators, attribution coverage, confidence and low-sample warnings. Until both transactions and denominator are reliable, **NOT COMPUTABLE—not zero**.

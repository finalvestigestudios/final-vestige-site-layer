## Controlled, non-coercive A/B hypothesis

**Hypothesis, NOT guaranteed result:** among independently qualified visitors who already want a meditation-audio buying guide, an optional original 42-second video may increase verified paid orders relative to text-only guidance. The video might equally reduce conversions, confuse a reader or harm mobile loading.

Variant A and B must have identical verified product facts, price qualifications, objections, medical disclaimers, external purchase CTA and near-CTA commission disclosure. B adds only an optional, user-initiated 30–45s media component with poster, accurate synchronized captions, transcript and minimal network cost. Never autoplay, embed a large third-party player or require JS for reading and free Focus navigation.

After explicit founder approval and partner authorization, stage both at 360/390/768/1440 and 1920px; test zoom, screen reader navigation, keyboard activation, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, reduced motion, no-JS, third-party requests, Content Security Policy, HTML semantics, WebVTT synchronization, original media licensing and actual Lighthouse/CWV. A static-source scan does not certify any of these browser outcomes.

Use randomized assignment only if existing privacy-approved infrastructure can do it without identity tracking or duplicate scripts. Otherwise compare disclosed observational cohorts, and **do not label them randomized causal A/B**. Do not judge success on bounce rate alone or on an affiliate click. Predefine primary endpoint as merchant-confirmed net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions; secondary confirmed orders, paid conversion percentage and confirmed-lead-to-customer rate. Report seven- and thirty-day cohorts, missing fields, unsettled partner commissions and tiny-sample caveats.

## Privacy-respecting attribution and paid-conversion definitions

Use existing approved first-party instrumentation only, owned by Claude/site QA; this lane does not deploy JavaScript, cookie IDs or pixels. Standard fields when channel terms permit: utm_source=tiktok|youtube, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=mind_buyer_pilot_2026_11, utm_content=ACTUAL_GROK_ASSET_ID. Asset ID must identify creative, **not a visitor**; never append email, personal names, IP, hidden visitor identifiers or health information.

Proposed event semantics:
1. **campaign_landing:** source-tagged eligible visit; distinguish suspected bots, internal QA and unknown-human status. Pageview is not qualified person.
2. **article_to_resource_click:** optional on-site Focus navigation, not confirmed subscription.
3. **confirmed_signup:** authoritative Jetpack confirmed opt-in for the existing Dispatch list; visible PDF delivery alone does not qualify.
4. **qualified_guide_visit:** legitimate visitor whose expressed search/topic matches the audio buyer guide; do not infer intent from an unrelated Thatcher clip.
5. **affiliate_click:** only authorized merchant departure, distinct from paid purchase.
6. **merchant_confirmed_purchase / cleared_commission:** actual merchant order or reconciled partner paid-conversion with payout/settlement; deduplicate transactions.
7. **refund / fee / reversal:** adjust realized revenue and attribute conservatively when possible.

Realized net revenue per 1,000 = 1,000 × (settled owned-product receipts + cleared affiliate commissions − refunds − fees) ÷ qualified-human sessions. Report verified paid orders, customers, gross revenue, settled revenue and refunds separately. If qualified-human sessions or transactions cannot be verified, state **NOT COMPUTABLE** rather than zero, and never sum a purchase receipt and the commission from the same transaction.

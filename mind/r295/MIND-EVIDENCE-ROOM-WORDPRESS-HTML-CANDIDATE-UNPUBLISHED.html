New investigation · unpublished

The Tape That Changed Its Name

In the 1990s, researchers gave listeners recordings that might not match their labels. Memory on the label, self-esteem in the recording. Then they asked: was it the inaudible message—or what the listener expected—that predicted apparent improvement?

What the studies showed. In three blinded replications with 237 participants, the content did not produce the claimed changes, while more than one-third of participants perceived label-consistent improvement. A separate small study found a narrower short-term recall improvement; neither finding establishes effects of current commercial albums.

Try it without buying anything

Two columns. Two different kinds of evidence.

Before a routine task, note what you expect to happen. When twelve minutes have passed, record one objectively checkable action—paragraphs summarized correctly, a page drafted or a named step completed. In a second column, record how focused you felt. These can diverge. This exercise does not isolate a cause and makes no health or treatment claim.

Why do both? An outcome can improve while a belief is wrong about *why* it improved. And a person can sincerely feel more capable without demonstrating the promised objective change in a particular experiment.

Independent commerce desk · currently inactive

A useful purchase starts with a credible question.

Zen12's official seller page describes a twelve-level meditation program containing 48 MP3s. Its audio-library format may appeal to someone who wants structured listening material. Research on meditation generally does not establish that this specific program delivers advertised brain or life outcomes.

Hypnosis Live's Total Focus is a separate seller-described hypnosis recording; Brainwave Shots' Focus Shot is a separate short-audio product; Subliminal Guru markets affirmation recordings. They are not interchangeable and have different terms. We do not turn lab research into endorsements for them.

Buyer questions: What exactly is delivered? Is the purchase one-time or recurring? What is the latest checkout price after taxes? What are the refund exclusions? Does any free sample enroll you in the merchant's separate email list? Who has actually tested the precise product against a good comparison?

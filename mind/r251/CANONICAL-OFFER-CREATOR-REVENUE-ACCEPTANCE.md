## The specific warm-up-page hypothesis

The existing R248 A/B variants are the canonical experiment; R251 is **not** a new competing arm. The A variant is a complete truthful text-only explanation of Premium, its format, the free timer alternative, research limitations, and relevant objections. The current six/seven-cue **42-second storyboard is not a rendered film**. It cannot be called a working video, a tested player, or an A/B conversion result. No autoplay. A no-JavaScript reader must retain the same decision facts. No remote third-party video/player scripts without explicit privacy/security approval; prefer a first-party compressed single MP4 plus a static SVG poster only if independently accepted within site performance budgets.

**Question to test:** Does optional contextual motion help genuinely high-intent visitors understand the difference between a specified audio library and unverified outcome claims, producing more independently verified settled paid conversions *per qualified human session*? Counterhypothesis: video harms trust, increases LCP/transfer, or has no commercial effect. No assumed lift.

**Preregister before release:** unit is consent-appropriate distinct qualified human landing session (not impression or bot), unique Grok asset and campaign where actually known. Randomize only after Claude/QA authorizes privacy-safe first-party allocation, if allocation can be audited without violating consent. Freeze copy and offer between arms. Record exclusions for obvious bots and repeated refreshes, but do not fingerprint people; do not create prohibited cookies. Report exact cohort size, source composition, paid conversions, settlement, refunds, fees, missing attribution and uncertainty, with no winner claim on tiny samples. Stop immediately for missing/obscured FTC disclosure, misleading health claims, broken checkout, accessible-control failure, or leaked identifiers. Minimum powered sample size must be agreed before running; none is justified by current data.

**Performance and accessibility acceptance:** actual 360, 390, 768, 1440, 1920 px; keyboard-only and visible focus; WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and semantics; screen reader labels; reduced-motion setting; no-JS content parity; poster and captions tested with media loaded *and* blocked; Core Web Vitals/LCP and total bytes compared to A; deny autoplay. ## Attribution and actual revenue acceptance

**Collection rule:** first-party, consent-appropriate low-cardinality fields only in an existing or later expressly approved analytics path: <code>utm_source</code>, <code>utm_medium</code>, <code>utm_campaign</code>, <code>utm_content=verified_asset_id</code>, <code>landing_path</code>, <code>offer_id</code>, <code>variant</code>, and date bucket. Do not store emails, IP addresses, persistent device IDs, full referrer query parameters, merchant secrets or fingerprinting fields in R251. No new JavaScript or duplicate measurement injected. Respect absence of consent.

**Sequence of independent state changes:** creator asset published (Grok evidence) → qualified human landing (first-party) → real resource/product CTA click (first-party) → separately provider-confirmed opt-in if consented (Jetpack, not a click) → independently authenticated merchant-paid order or merchant-reported affiliate conversion → actual settlement after refund/chargeback/fees. A click is not an order. A free Fieldbook download is not a purchase. A merchant-reported conversion with no cleared amount is not yet net realized commission.

**Stable join:** use only actual provider-supported campaign/click identifiers with permitted privacy and affiliate terms; if no deterministic permitted mapping exists, report paid results as **unattributed**, not fabricated source attribution. Do not rely on in-browser checkout-complete events from the merchant without independent merchant confirmation.

**7- and 30-day cohorts:** reconcile unique order IDs and refund/chargeback references; select *one* mutually consistent accounting basis to avoid subtracting refunds or payment fees twice from already-net settlements. For FV-owned product, net realized revenue is settled receipts minus incremental platform/payment fees, refunded value not already excluded, chargebacks not already excluded, and allocable incremental costs. For affiliates, use actually cleared commissions net of reversals and any incremental applicable costs; do not treat gross customer order value as FV revenue. Document currency and settlement timestamp. Compute **1000 × realized net revenue / qualified human sessions** only if both numerator and a reliable positive qualified-session denominator exist. For the current run, every underlying component remains unauthenticated, therefore **NOT_COMPUTABLE** rather than zero. Show tiny cohort warnings and uncertainty intervals once genuine data are collected.

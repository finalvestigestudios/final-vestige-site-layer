Why product-specific claims need product-specific support

A free twelve-minute comparison

Before deciding whether the structure itself is useful, try twelve minutes with a timer. Choose a quiet location; sit or walk in a comfortable, ordinary way; note how often your attention wanders; and finish by writing one sentence about what the session felt like. Do not treat this as an experiment in neural entrainment or a medical treatment.

If you still prefer organized audio and multiple listening styles, evaluate the offering on that value rather than on a claimed one-hour equivalence.

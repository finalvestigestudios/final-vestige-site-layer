Evidence discipline / A two-minute reading exercise

A statement can be authentic—and still be wrong about its cause.

Read this line: “A witness signed the account and said the procedure worked.” The signed page may establish that the witness made that statement. It cannot by itself establish that the procedure caused the reported improvement. That additional claim needs an appropriate comparison and better evidence.

What the record can establish: the date, language, witness and asserted observation, if the document is genuine.

What the record cannot establish alone: an invisible mechanism, reproducible medical result, or a modern brand's advertised performance.

This is a reading demonstration, not a psychological diagnosis or validated experiment. The distinction is central to our Puységur source archive and our new research on self-administered hypnosis recordings.

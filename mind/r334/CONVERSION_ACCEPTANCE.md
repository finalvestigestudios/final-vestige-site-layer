## Controlled A/B hypothesis

A: independent text-only seller-format, objections and research guide. B: **identical essential buyer copy**, plus original optional 42-second poster, six caption cues and complete visible-on-expansion transcript concept. Film is not encoded; B is not yet a video test. Preserve no-JS access to essential copy, no autoplay, reduced motion and accessible controls. The experiment cannot be run until one eligible offer, QA, consent and live publishing are approved. Compare **verified settled net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** between randomized A/B variants. Secondary: confirmed paid orders, independently confirmed leads, paid conversion rate, lead-to-customer. Use 7- and 30-day cohorts; suppress small cohorts (under 20) and state sampling uncertainty. A click is not a sale.

## Proposed native-first event contract — specification only

Qualified humans only, using existing first-party/Jetpack facilities if independently verified. Attributed campaign fields: utm_source, utm_medium=organic_social, utm_campaign=cluster_yyyy_mm, utm_content=ACTUAL_ID or yt_profile, landing_path, variant. Never collect email, IP, full user agent or raw referrer query within these editorial assets; no vendor script, fingerprint, tracking pixel or new mailing list.

Events: campaign_landing (qualifying session) → resource_cta_click (not a subscriber) → confirmed_signup (Jetpack-confirmed opt-in only) → approved affiliate_click (not a commission) → merchant_confirmed_paid_sale or partner_confirmed_payable_commission (not derived from clicks) → realized_net_revenue (minus refunds/platform and processing fees). Avoid customer-level joining without appropriate consent and verified provider reporting. If settled revenue or human denominator is unavailable, report the primary KPI as **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0.

The thirteen-minute clue

Researchers compared thirteen minutes of guided meditation a day with thirteen minutes of podcast listening in adults new to meditation. After eight weeks, not four, the meditation group showed improvements in selected attention, memory and mood measures. The study used its own guided program; it did not test Zen12 Premium or establish that twelve minutes equals sixty. Read the original study.

Similarly, a 2023 review of EEG entrainment studies reported mixed findings. A phenomenon with genuine scientific interest isn't proof of the effectiveness of every recording sold with its name.

Try the free option before spending

For two minutes, sit where you feel comfortable. Notice one nearby sound, then one farther away, and then whether attention drifts. This isn't a diagnostic test, a treatment or evidence of how a particular audio product will work. Stop if you feel distressed. Can a meditation routine be uncomfortable?

Some individuals report adverse experiences. A 2020 systematic review documents such reports from heterogeneous meditation research; its estimates are not Zen12-specific. Stop if a session is distressing and seek appropriate support rather than treating a commercial recording as clinical care.

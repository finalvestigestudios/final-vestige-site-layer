## Minimum measurement contract: no duplicate scripts

**Existing-site instrumentation owner: Claude.** Mind supplies event names and discriminators, never injects another analytic provider. At attribution entry record only approved `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content=<actual Grok asset_id>`, plus a first-party page identifier and short-lived session identifier where consistent with consent/privacy configuration. Do not store names, emails, full query strings, user-agent fingerprints or raw IPs in an affiliate-event ledger. Canonical campaigns only after Grok supplies actual assets. Do not assign fake stable IDs from topic names.

Keep these **distinct stages**: `qualified_session` (filtered with documented human-quality rules; unknown/bot excluded), `offer_view`, `focus_cta_click`, `focus_confirmed_lead` (only authorized first-party confirmation), `offer_cta_click`, `affiliate_click` (only if compliant real earning link exists), `checkout_started` (when merchant exposes it), `order_paid_verified` (merchant-verified, de-duplicated order), `refund_or_chargeback`, `fee_recognized`, `commission_settled`. A sale or realized commission requires independent merchant evidence, not a click, first-party button event, email signup, unconfirmed checkout or marketing dashboard estimate. If merchant reporting cannot attribute a buyer-level event lawfully, record `attribution_unknown` and do not force a match.

**Seven- and thirty-day cohort outputs** (with eligible denominator N):
```
qualified_human_sessions = distinct consent-compliant eligible session count
verified_paid_orders = count of qualifying merchant-confirmed unique paid orders
paid_conversion_rate = verified_paid_orders / qualified_human_sessions
gross_verified_revenue = SUM(merchant-confirmed eligible paid amount)
net_realized_revenue = gross_settled_amount - refunds - chargebacks - merchant_fees - affiliate_reversals - direct_offer_costs
net_revenue_per_1000 = 1000 * net_realized_revenue / qualified_human_sessions
confirmed_lead_to_paid = verified_paid_customers_from_confirmed_leads / confirmed_eligible_leads
```
If `qualified_human_sessions` is 0 or **not known**, or settled proceeds are inaccessible, render **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0 or an invented rate. Separate affiliate commissions from owned-store gross sales; an affiliate purchase is **not** gross merchandise value accruing to FV. Refund/chargeback timing and network attribution windows must be reported. Store only aggregate results for 7/30d unless consent and contract authorize a more granular join. With very small N (especially <100 eligible sessions or only a handful of orders), mark **EXPLORATORY / WIDE UNCERTAINTY**, not statistically significant.

## Pre-registered A/B hypothesis, with explicit stop rules

**Question:** Does an *optional* original 40-second educational video on the source-first Zen12 Premium presell improve **settled net revenue per qualified human** relative to the same complete text without video? This is a hypothesis, not a promised increase. The inherited R145 versions share identical purchasing body copy; R145 B has a **poster/caption/transcript storyboard**, *not playable media*.

**Before experiment:** Gain partner written approval and authorize exact sales URL; verify staged checkout and consent/reconciliation; commission/refund reporting; ensure video original/license-cleared, poster, VTT captions, accurate transcript, no autoplay/preload, usable no-JS fallback, no CLS; pass measured LCP/INP/CLS and 360/390/768/1440/1920 viewport QA with source-linked receipts; founder signs release. Do not turn the storyboard into autoplay, forced playback or a remote player.

**Allocation:** Only after consent/experiment approval, consistent 1:1 assignment per qualifying short-lived first-party session (no invasive persistent fingerprinting); same price, hero promise, fact boxes, CTA, disclosure and policy. Source and experiment labels remain non-PII. Monitor 7/30d outcome; report total session quality, balanced groups, paid conversions, net after fees/refunds and uncertainty intervals when sample warrants. There is **no sample size basis or randomized live data yet**; no win can be declared.

**Stop conditions:** seller claims become unsupported or edited; earning-link permission or merchant terms lapse; disclosure invisible near CTA; PDF/Fieldbook delivery breaks; audio discomfort portrayed as a diagnosis; A/B user copy diverges; sale/commission cannot be reconciled; site performance/accessibility degrades; privacy/consent mismatch; or founder revokes approval. Roll back only under authorized production governance.

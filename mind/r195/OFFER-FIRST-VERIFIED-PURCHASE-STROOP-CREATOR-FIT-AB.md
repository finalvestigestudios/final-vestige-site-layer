**Trust objections:** “Will this work like an hour of meditation?” We cannot verify that equivalence; exclude it as a FV benefit. “Does a brain scan prove it?” No: an EEG or conflict-task study about attention is not a test of Zen12 Premium. “Is this a treatment?” No such therapeutic positioning is authorized or substantiated. “Can I try something free?” Yes: standard meditation and the existing Fieldbook are free alternatives; reading the research requires no signup. “Can I refund it?” The merchant advertises a guarantee but the exact operative terms and fulfillment are not independently confirmed. “Is FV paid?” At any **future active earning link** disclose beside it: **If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.** Before approval, this text is a prospective disclosure, not an assertion that FV currently receives commissions.

# The Face That Turned / original 40-second film proposal
This six-scene visual essay uses original geometric face artwork, not Thompson's copyrighted photographic stimulus. It teaches the distinction between perception and evidence; it does not demonstrate Zen12, hypnosis or a scientific treatment. Six scenes: 0–6 ordinary face; 6–13 whole-picture rotation; 13–20 rotate eyes and mouth; 20–27 difficulty of judging features; 27–34 experimental controls; 34–40 claim/evidence boundary. Native media only after first-party permissions, rendering and caption timing tests. Current B is honest poster/transcript, not falsely playable. 540x960 12fps H.264 desired, no forced playback or external embeds.

Before you buy a meditation-audio program, try this.

The biggest choice may not be whether you can train your brain in twelve minutes. It may be whether you actually enjoy a structured twelve-minute listening routine.

In a classic experiment, volunteers asked to avoid thinking of a white bear sometimes encountered that thought more persistently afterward. Instructions can change what we notice. The study did not test Zen12 or show that any audio fixes concentration or unwanted thoughts. Read the original study abstract.

Try a free twelve-minute preference check

Use a recording you already own or sit quietly for twelve minutes. Notice whether you prefer music, a voice, or silence; whether twelve minutes feels manageable; and whether you would enjoy doing it again. There is no success score. Stop if you feel uncomfortable.

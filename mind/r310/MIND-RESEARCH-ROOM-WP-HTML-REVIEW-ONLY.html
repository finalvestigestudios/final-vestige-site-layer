Puységur's files: when a source tries to prove itself

The published Animal Magnetism Files preserve witness statements, medical assertions, predictions, failures, notes about coercion and the operator's doubts. A notarized claim strengthens the evidence that someone recorded it; it does not authenticate a mechanism. The archive separates editions, source page references and translation limitations.

This is where Mind & Perception connects to Final Vestige's historical mission. We investigate how people formed, preserved and challenged claims—not how to reproduce harmful historical procedures.

Original five-minute fieldwork

Write it down before it becomes a story.

Choose a harmless photograph, familiar object arrangement or a book cover. Without looking, list five details and tag each seen, inferred or uncertain. Check the original. Preserve the first version of your notes rather than rewriting history. This is self-reflection, not a validated psychological test; do not use it to judge someone else's memory, health or credibility.

## What materially changed in R310

Independent primary-paper reading found a denominator error in the prior R308 buyer specimens: both attached **34.6%** to “a sample of 34.” The full original [Wood & Cowan 1995 paper, printed pp. 256–257](https://memory.psych.missouri.edu/assets/doc/articles/1995/wood--cowan-1995-jeplmc.pdf#page=3) states 34 analyzed participants, including eight who did not hear their own name, and 26 in the name-exposed arms. **Nine of those 26 reported their own names.** Both R308 Zen12 A/B pages, the R308 investigation and the corresponding JSON evidence register were corrected together. The 2021 replication's reported 29% is a separate sample and must not inherit the 1995 denominator. This correction does not prove anything about audio performance or purchased products.

R310 also contributes the original 2,000-plus-word research investigation [*The Glass That Was Never on the Road*](THE-GLASS-THAT-WAS-NEVER-ON-THE-ROAD-UNPUBLISHED.md). Loftus and Palmer's 1974 primary paper was inspected visually at printed pp. 586–587. The *smashed*/*hit*/control groups in its second experiment had **16/50, 7/50 and 6/50** positive broken-glass reports respectively despite no glass in the film. The important outcome is **yes responses**, not evidence that a leading question implants any specific private visual experience. The 2024 eyewitness systematic review and separately scoped fake-news meta-analysis provide further limits. This research is an editorial asset; an associated hypnotic/meditation sale is not a scientific conclusion.

## Offer fit: one buyer with a concrete need

**High-intent reader:** An adult who already wishes to try a short audio-guided routine and is deciding whether a paid collection with different listening styles is worth it compared with a free timer or workbook.

**Verifiable seller-specific format:** [Zen12 official overview](https://zen12.com/about) describes twelve levels, twelve-minute sessions and four audio styles (music, guided, white noise, nature). [Zen12 exact complete-course checkout](https://zen12.com/getstarted) describes twelve levels and 48 MP3s, also showing dynamic promotions and guarantee language. A separate [sales page](https://zen12.com/hnp) discusses different tiers. These sources are **seller inventory and marketing descriptions**, not independent evidence of an hour-equivalent meditation effect, automatic changes in brainwaves, mental-health gains or persistent productivity. Do not reproduce prices, discount timers, current taxes, bundle claims or returns promises without documented authorized offer terms and checkout testing.

**Competing offers:** [Hypnosis Live Total Focus](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus) describes a separate approximately 33-minute hypnosis product; [Brainwave Shots Focus Shot](https://brainwaveshots.com/focus-shot) advertises separate 15-/30-minute recordings. Their methods, package composition, terms, prices and claims must remain separate. Do not treat them as variations of Zen12 or apply one seller's refund promise to the others.

**Truthful presell promise:** “What you can verify about a 12-minute guided-audio routine before deciding whether a paid library fits you.” This sells *decision usefulness*, not brain transformation. Distinguish a preference for a convenient format from independently demonstrated efficacy. No testimonials invented; no implication of personal use.

**Original micro-story:** A listener can miss a personally meaningful spoken name while occupied with a competing task. Wood and Cowan's actual experiment shows why accuracy requires both a numerator and a denominator: nine reports among 26 exposed listeners. No product was involved. The buyer can then try twelve minutes with a free timer and decide whether structure is worth paying for.

**Free demonstration:** Start a 12-minute timer, choose one harmless anchor, note distraction categories in a private notebook and return; no clinical scoring. Stop if uncomfortable. Do not imply that this self-observation validates an advertised psychological effect.

**Buyer objections:** “Do I need this?” (no: start with free resources); “Do levels guarantee progress?” (no); “What will I actually receive?” (only exact seller-verified tier, subject to later authorized proof); “What happens if I dislike it?” (read current written terms); “Has FV tested it?” (no); “Does research guarantee better focus?” (no).

**CTA:** The inherited R308/R309 A/B specimens currently display a disabled *Merchant offer unavailable pending verification* button immediately beneath prospective disclosure. The existing live and free /focus/ link remains a separately labeled noncommerce route. **No unauthorized offsite CTA is available**. Do not turn the seller's general official page into a proxy earning link.

## Controlled original-film hypothesis

Use the established source-controlled A/B buyer-guide specimens rather than launch more overlapping experiments. Variant **A** is text only. Variant **B** has the **same buyer copy**, plus an original optional, non-autoplay 42-second treatment with SVG poster and timestamped caption/transcript concept. It remains **not playable**: an SVG image, a planned WebVTT and a narrative are not a finished film. The experiment question is whether optional video clarifies a buyer's understanding and improves **verified net paid conversions** without sacrificing speed, accessibility or trust. It is not a promised conversion lift.

Before any staging acceptance, Claude/Site QA must: check real 360/390/768/1440/1920 layouts; WCAG 2.2 AA keyboard focus; captions matching actual narration; a transcript when video unavailable; *prefers-reduced-motion*; default click-to-play and zero forced network media; no-JS access to all essential buyer text; no CLS-producing embed or third-party tracking; no product-claim leakage from any spoken/audio caption; secure external destination only after permissions.

**Randomization:** If subsequently released, assign a stable first-party experiment variant only where consent and approved first-party analytics permit; equal traffic treatment, same source campaign, same product tier and same disclosure placement. Where visitors refuse analytics, still serve complete accessible content and do not force identification. Predefine primary outcome **attributable cleared FV commission / qualified campaign-human sessions**, alongside order conversion rate where partner confirmation actually permits joining aggregate cohorts. Secondary outcomes: provider-confirmed lead, guide comprehension checks with voluntary consent, scroll/CTA diagnostic clicks and Core Web Vitals. Do not claim A or B wins on clicks alone.

**Sample discipline:** report raw counts and denominators; no effect direction claimed under small samples or when order-attribution continuity is unverified. If the partner cannot supply independent order/settlement data, label revenue analysis NOT COMPUTABLE and refuse conversion-winner claims.

## Privacy-safe measurement and financial provenance

**Sequence:** validated campaign asset_id/utm_campaign (not invented) → consent-respecting first-party qualified-human visit → article_to_fieldbook CTA click → provider-confirmed subscribed/eligible lead (only if available) → approved affiliate_outbound_click → partner-reported **paid** order → settled **attributable commission** after clawbacks, reversals and any additional non-overlapping FV expenses. Use no unapproved script, cookie, identifier fingerprinting, PII, duplicate service or second email list.

**Revenue KPI:** $1,000 units are not the input; the formula is *1000 × realized net attributable FV revenue ÷ qualified human sessions*. If already-net settled affiliate commission includes refunds/fees, **do not subtract the same amounts twice**. Keep merchant basket revenue distinct from FV commission. For owned products, reconcile paid order revenue less actual refunds, platform/processor costs and other attributable costs, where documented. Cohorts: 7- and 30-day; show unassigned source/unknown commission as unknown, not zero, and no person-level visitor identity in analytics summaries.

**Actual evidence:** no partner order ledger, cleared earned commission, reconciled qualified sessions, provider-confirmed campaign lead attribution or test-checkout evidence presented in this sprint. KPI **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0. Prior reported views and clicks are diagnostics, not buyer wins.

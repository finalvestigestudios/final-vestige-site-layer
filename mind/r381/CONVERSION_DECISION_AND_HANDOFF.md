## Controlled A/B, no result implied
**Population:** consent-eligible, reasonably qualified human visits from matching organic campaigns to an approved buyer guide. Must not run an active experiment while authorization is held. Require stable, documented assignment across a 7/30-day window consistent with current first-party infrastructure; do not create new identifiers, trackers or extra scripts. Store only aggregate counts and campaign/asset labels where supported. Exclude bot/internal traffic using existing approved procedures, not personal visitor profiling.

**A (control):** `ZEN12_A_TEXT_BUYER_GUIDE.html` is a complete useful independent guide. **B (hypothesis):** `ZEN12_B_OPTIONAL_FILM_CONCEPT.html` contains the exact same substantive buyer copy with one 42-second optional illustrated concept, SVG poster, WebVTT captions and full transcript. No encoded video, player or autoplay exists yet. The two files are source-controlled hypotheses, not a live test; until the media exists, comparing B as “video conversion” is invalid. If film is created and rights-cleared, offer a user-started locally served file with accurately timed captions, transcript and poster (no remote embed, no forced playback). The first meaningful outcome is **verified paid net revenue**, not scrolls or play clicks. Avoid re-exposing users to A/B if cross-session assignment cannot be handled without new tracking.

**Null hypothesis:** Adding the optional video does not increase realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions relative to the text guide. **Alternative:** a measured increase in paid conversions or adjusted net, evaluated with uncertainty, may justify adopting the film. Loss of speed, comprehension, disclosure visibility or accessibility disqualifies B regardless of optimistic engagement. At tiny samples report raw denominators and plausible noise; no “winner” declaration from a handful of events.

7. Primary `realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions = 1000 * net_realized / qualified_sessions` for a specified seven- or thirty-day cohort. Small-n caution; don't infer causal lifts without a valid control and attribution.

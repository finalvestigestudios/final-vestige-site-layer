Do meditation recordings deliver what an attention study measures?

In a classic experiment, a second target was sometimes missed while observers named the first. That discovery says something useful about attention. It doesn't establish that a downloadable audio library improves your focus.

Try the distinction yourself. Read the 1992 experiment abstract. Notice which target people were instructed to name, and which conditions changed the effect. Then compare what the merchant says it sells with what the experiments actually measured. Those are different kinds of evidence.

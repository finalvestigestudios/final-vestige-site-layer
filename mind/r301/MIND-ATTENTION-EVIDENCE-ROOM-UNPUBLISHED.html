Start with a question you can check for yourself

Choose one small task, work on it for ten minutes, then spend two minutes noting what interrupted you, what you completed and which expectations you brought into the session. Repeat only if it helps organize your observations. This is a reflection exercise, not a clinical test, a measured treatment or a promise to increase attention.

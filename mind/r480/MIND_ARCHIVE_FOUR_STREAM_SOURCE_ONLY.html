The unreliable witness: your own mind.

A sound you hear with your eyes. A change you fail to notice. A historical record that looks like proof. The mind is capable of extraordinary experiences; our task is to investigate what they actually establish.

Our method

Experience is the beginning of an investigation, not its verdict.

Every story begins with an observation and asks for the method, its controls, counterexamples and historical provenance. Original published investigations remain available without buying anything. A future tool recommendation will be marked as commerce, supported by independent evidence where possible, and directly disclosed at the point of purchase.

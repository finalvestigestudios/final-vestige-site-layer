## Actual sellable offer evidence threshold
A product becomes **eligible** only with a manifest containing:
- product name/version, owned or approved license, file/support terms and delivery evidence;
- available checkout SKU and route, buyer-visible currency + current price/taxes/upsell/recurrence, refund policy and accessibility;
- channel rights (website, YouTube, TikTok, email as separately authorized); affiliate-approved creative or explicit absence;
- any promotional claims matched to direct product-specific competent evidence;
- independently inspectable paid settlement and a method to subtract refunds/processing fees.
Merchant descriptions such as https://zen12.com/about and https://zen12.com/secret/ do **not** supply this permission manifest.

## The experiment that matters
**Control A**: precise text-only, evidence-reviewed Zen12 buyer guide with no video. **Treatment B**: same text, source trail, CTA/disclosure, page structure and styling, with only a user-initiated 42s original short film placed after the research hook. Video must have poster, timed captions, always-visible transcript, controls that work via keyboard/touch, no forced sound, no surprise autoplay, and no extra trackers. B should be **at least as useful without JavaScript**. A and B have identical buyer information and same approved seller link only *after* merchant approval. Do not compare a weak text teaser with a richer video version, or contaminate by changing price and copy.

**Primary measure**, after a legitimate offer is activated with founder approval: independently verified **refund-adjusted net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** over 7- and 30-day cohorts, with confidence flags for sparse data. **Secondary measures**: verified paid order rate, gross and net, confirmed customers, permitted first-party attributable lead-to-customer, content/film cost per settled customer. **Diagnostic only**: qualified campaign landing, article-to-resource click, time-on-page, affiliate_outbound / affiliate_click. Clicking does not prove purchase.

**Attribution schema**: one existing first-party analytics source of truth owned by Claude; no duplicate scripts, fingerprinting, cross-site IDs, email hashes, IP retention or undisclosed PII. Require published immutable asset_id + platform + campaign and canonical landing. Aggregate 7/30-day funnel counts by asset/offer. Exclude known QA/internal traffic, bot-like requests and uncertain sessions by documented, consent-aware rules rather than guessing. Respect consent and privacy choices. Partner sale reporting must explicitly document what campaign/affiliate attribution is actually available; when it is absent, **do not manufacture a join**.

**Net KPI**: `1000 × (settled gross revenue or paid commission – refunds – chargebacks – payment/platform/affiliate fees) / qualified-human sessions`. Do not mix order gross with affiliate net commission, double count pending orders or convert clicks to sales. If the denominator, paid settlement or match is missing, output **NOT COMPUTABLE**. Small cohorts: show counts and confidence limits where possible, describe that no sample-size guarantee exists; never declare a winning conversion lift based on a handful of sales.

**Experiment entry requirements**: written founder commercial+release approvals, merchant proof pack, source claim vetting, encoded playable B film, browser 360/390/768/1440/1920 QA, WP-safe sanitization, CSP/security inspection, no-JS and reduced-motion, keyboard focus, link rel/redirect audit, final checkout UX and refund receipts, consent-aware instrumentation, owner-reviewed randomization/assignment and rollback. No fake urgency, testimonials, fake discounts, purchases or sales.

The mind is a witness. Not always a reliable one.

You can miss a stranger changing places in front of you. You can see a face as ordinary until it is turned upright. You can report an event as if you witnessed it, even after a misleading question. None of these things proves that the mind is defective—or that a commercial recording can fix it. They are invitations to examine how experience becomes evidence.

The Final Vestige method

Source → claim → limit → reader

The source: identify the original experiment or archival record. The claim: state what was actually observed and measured. The limit: distinguish inferences, competing models and untested applications. The reader: make primary records and free reflection available without creating pressure to subscribe or purchase.

To move from discovery to a separate seller review, we require a source-grounded problem, an actual specified product, verified authorized affiliate channels, a proximate material-relationship disclosure, a working accessible referral and independently verifiable paid settlements.

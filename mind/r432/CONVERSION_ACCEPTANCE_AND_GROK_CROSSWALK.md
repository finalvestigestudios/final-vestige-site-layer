## The one controlled landing-page experiment, after eligibility

**Variant A:** identical full buyer-guide HTML: evidence, free practice, vendor-described format, five purchase questions, objection/limits, prospective affiliate disclosure and disabled merchant CTA.

**Variant B:** the same substantive guide, plus optional original 42-second poster/storyboard/transcript/caption concept near the beginning. No autoplay, trackers, scripts or video files; it is currently not a playable film. Before any release, encode a rights-cleared short film, inspect actual captions, page weight and keyboard/no-JS fallback.

**Hypothesis:** for the same verified high-intent audience with valid partner checkout, a low-cost optional original film may increase *verified paid orders per qualified human landing session* relative to a purely textual guide without making engagement or page speed worse. This is not a promise of lift.

**Assignment:** randomize consenting eligible new sessions server-side or via an existing approved first-party mechanism, with stable seven-day assignment and no extra browser fingerprinting. If no safe existing experiment tool exists, first perform two independent moderated usability evaluations; do not inject an ad-tracking service or make a pseudo-random 'test' by alternating publishing dates. Keep content, CTA position and offer terms identical except film inclusion. Preserve an accessibility/no-JS control and low-bandwidth option.

**Primary decision outcome:** confirmed merchant-reported paid order per qualified human landed session; then refunded/fee-adjusted net revenue per 1,000 qualified humans. Not video starts, scrolls, clicks, subscribers or site views. **Never call a click a sale.** Secondary metrics: correct source comprehension, CTA intent, optional confirmed Jetpack lead, bounce/exit, media bytes/CWV, keyboard and accessibility errors. Compare only after valid merchant and denominator data; show 7/30-day cohorts with exact n and confidence intervals, and prohibit significant-win language on tiny samples.

## First-party privacy-safe measurement contract (proposal, not deployed)

Data dictionary; all paths recorded only if collection is lawfully allowed/consented and existing first-party reporting supports it:

- `utm_source`: platform identifier (e.g., youtube / tiktok), allowlist only, not a personal referral token.
- `utm_medium=organic_social`; `utm_campaign=mind_attention_pilot_2026_11`; `utm_content=<verified_existing_grok_asset_id>`. Validate before a tagged link goes public; a missing ID means campaign unmatched.
- `campaign_landing`: page path, normalized campaign, time bucket, eligibility/consent state, no personal URL, IP, raw user-agent, fingerprint, or email.
- `article_to_resource_click`: link_id=focus_existing; diagnostic only, not a confirmed lead.
- `confirmed_signup`: provider-confirmed deduplicated Jetpack signal only, no email address passed into reporting; if unavailable, mark UNKNOWN.
- `offer_guide_view`: variant, canonical guide version, source ID; not a commercial success.
- `affiliate_outbound`: only once legitimate link is activated, track allowed exact link ID and campaign, no shadow redirect or cloaking; it is **NOT** a paid order.
- `merchant_confirmed_purchase`: independently reported merchant transaction pseudonymous proof/reference, received/refund/fee flags, allowed source match. Never log buyer email or payment details.
- `settled_net_revenue`: payout/transaction reconciliation minus attributable refunds and fees, currency and reporting period documented, provenance merchant ledger.
 
Do not deploy a second analytics script. Claude/site analytics owns implementation and consent review. Do not send raw subscriber data to Grok. Preserve separate raw event, merchant and reconciliation evidence. Unattributed orders are recorded as unattributed rather than guessed into Grok's campaign.

### Cohort computation

`qualified_human_sessions` = independently supported non-bot, eligible, privacy-lawful, campaign-matched landings over 7 and 30 calendar days (deduplicate using existing privacy-safe first-party means; not new fingerprinting).

`paid_conversion_rate` = merchant-confirmed non-refunded paid orders / qualified_human_sessions.

`net_revenue` = authenticated settled revenue - refunds - merchant fees - attributable expenses supported by records. Never estimate unreported commission.

`net_revenue_per_1000_qualified` = (net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions) * 1000, **only if denominator > 0 and numerator is authenticated**. Otherwise **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0.

`lead_to_customer_rate` = independently matched paid customers / provider-confirmed lead cohort, only if lawful consent/provenance and stable match exist; otherwise UNKNOWN.

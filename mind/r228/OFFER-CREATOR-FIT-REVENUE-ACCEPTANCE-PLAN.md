## Highest-intent landing pre-sell

R228's Zen12 guide answers a problem the merchant's actual package can plausibly address: wanting a small, structured collection of downloadable guided sessions, **without promising physiological outcomes**. The opener cites a real McGurk micro-story and makes clear that speech perception is not commercial product efficacy. It then gives one genuinely useful free twelve-minute exercise. Only after that does it explain the Premium package, buyer objections, refund *claim versus fulfillment*, nonverified price and paid alternatives.

The buying button is a native disabled button, not an `href` placeholder, JS unlock, redirect or merchant deep link. Disclosures are immediately adjacent to prospective commercial controls and use prospective language rather than falsely stating an active relationship. Existing full site Affiliate Disclosure remains additional context, not a substitute for the near-link notice.

### Controlled alternative: A versus B

- **A**: complete text-only original purchasing guide with the free exercise, seller feature list, evidence limits, objection answers, disabled CTA, close disclosure, no forms/scripts.
- **B**: exactly the same substantive guide plus a **clearly labeled unrendered** optional 42-second illustrated storyboard, poster, native `details` transcript and matching VTT intervals. No video player, network transfer, autoplay, or JavaScript; the poster is inline lightweight SVG.
- **Hypothesis only:** for self-selected high-intent visitors, a truly useful optional film may improve merchant-confirmed paid conversion without excessive transfer or accessibility cost. It might instead distract or slow the decision. No conversion or uplift conclusion permitted before actual experimental execution.
- **Prelaunch release test:** final original media rights and encoding; captions inspected against final soundtrack; keyboard/Touch/360/390/768/1440/1920, screen reader and reduced-motion, contrast, no-JS; WP sanitizer parity and link semantics; Core Web Vitals/Lighthouse; real merchant affiliate approval, actual checkout and settled reporting. Keep A/B to one controlled allocation; R210 remains the original inactive experiment, R228 is an alternate review candidate, NOT another live arm.
- **Statistical caution:** with tiny qualified traffic, even two purchases can create misleading percent differences. Report raw users/orders and confidence/uncertainty; use a preregistered stop rule and do not stop at the first favorable impression. With zero actual transactions, there is no paid-conversion signal.

## Measurement contract — commercial event truth

**Attribution key**: Grok's real `asset_id` stored in `utm_content`, platform source in `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11` only after actual verified creator assignment. Existing first-party/Jetpack provider context may support counts; no second tracking script, third-party pixels, new SaaS or covert fingerprinting authorized. Do not log names, emails, IP addresses, user-agent fingerprints, full referrer query strings or raw affiliate identifiers. Consent and existing privacy configuration must be checked.

**Funnel stages**:
1. `qualified_campaign_landing`: privacy-safe landing event, bot/internal/previews removed via documented defensible filters. The event is **not yet implemented or measured**.
2. `article_to_focus_click` / `guide_interest_click`: link click **not a lead**. Do not change live navigation in this lane.
3. `confirmed_signup`: count only provider-confirmed, deduplicated Jetpack signup attributed when authoritative evidence supports it. A `?subscribe` URL success state or a Fieldbook download is **not proof**.
4. `affiliate_outbound`: a consent-compliant action on a truly permitted issued earning URL, once approved. An outbound click **is not a sale**.
5. `merchant_confirmed_paid_order` or `cleared_affiliate_commission`: authenticated order/partner settlement records, unique transaction deduplication, completed/charged state, refunds and reversals. Cross-domain attribution may be unavailable; report as UNKNOWN, not assumed.
6. `realized_net_revenue`: settled owned orders + **cleared** affiliate commissions − refunds − chargebacks − merchant/processor fees. Costs spent on content production tracked separately for profitability if available.

**Primary KPI**: `1000 × realized_net_revenue ÷ qualified_human_sessions`, on 7-day and 30-day cohorts, only with positive verified denominator and independently reconciled numerator. If one side is missing the state is **NOT_COMPUTABLE** (not zero). Paid conversion rate = verified customers / qualified humans when source attribution and counting match. Report unattributed sales separately rather than fabricate campaign assignment.

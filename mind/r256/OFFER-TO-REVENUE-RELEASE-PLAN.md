## One high-intent audience and one offer

**Audience:** a reader already comparing short guided meditation audio, who wants different listening styles and wants to know whether a free timer is enough. This audience is narrower and materially more relevant than everyone who enjoyed an optical-illusion TikTok.

**Candidate:** Zen12 Premium — exact inspected standard page `https://zen12.com/hnp`. Described seller features: 12 levels, 48 downloadable MP3s, four listening styles per level, support materials, one-year advertised refund. These are features and promises, not verified delivered product, checkout, or effectiveness.

**Trust proposition:** "The timer is free. What are you buying?" State clearly that the potential value is structured audio choice and convenience, *not* clinically established cognitive superiority. Give a no-cost twelve-minute trial, explain two different seller landing contexts, and surface objections before any earning link. A reader can decline purchase. The optional /focus/ CTA remains active and distinct from the commercial decision.

A prospective plain-language material-connection disclosure is beside it. No earning URL, merchant click, checkout or affiliate outcome exists. A future approved button must be a real link with accessible text and `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` if opening a new tab; use the exact issued URL and preserve its original destination in a privacy/security review, without link-cloaking.

## Privacy-safe first-party attribution contract (specification, NOT deployment)

The query contract suggested in #820 is `utm_source=<actual-platform>&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11&utm_content=<immutable_asset_id>`. Accept only a short allowlisted token for source/campaign/content; reject long, arbitrary or PII-bearing values. Do not persist names, email addresses, IPs, full referrers containing personal data, device fingerprints, or cross-site identifiers. Do not add a second script beside existing Jetpack/Ahrefs; Claude must decide what existing tools can observe, with consent and privacy review.

Distinguish events and their evidence requirements:

1. **campaign_landing:** recorded first-party landing with a valid asset ID, platform and coarse time. This is not yet a *qualified-human* session; apply the documented bot/internal exclusion definition, do not quietly relabel views as visitors.
2. **article_to_resource_click:** click to existing /focus/. Not a subscriber.
3. **confirmed_signup:** only count a confirmation from existing Jetpack provider, not form render, submit or an email displayed on screen. Attribute only when a permissioned, privacy-safe join is documented.
4. **affiliate_outbound:** future measured link click from an *approved link* with adjacent FTC disclosure. It is NOT a purchase, and remains absent today.
5. **merchant_confirmed_purchase:** authenticated merchant order with settled payment and lawful attribution. Avoid inventing a cross-platform linkage.
6. **settled_affiliate_commission:** independently reported cleared earnings tied to an account and order if merchant permits. Refunds and reversals can change this later.
7. **realized_net_revenue:** attributable cleared revenue minus only costs not already embedded in settlement figures, with refunds/chargebacks applied exactly once.

**North-star KPI:** 1000 × attributable realized net revenue / qualified-human sessions, with 7-day and 30-day cohorts. Report `NOT_COMPUTABLE` if either verified term is missing or the denominator is zero; *do not treat missing data as zero revenue*. Supporting measures: confirmed orders and unique customers, paid conversion rate with known denominator, gross/net with tax/fee definitions, confirmed lead→customer rate where lawful match exists, and attribution unknown percentage. Small samples need actual counts, uncertainty, not headline percentage gains.

## Controlled A/B test hypothesis and gates

**Control A:** complete evidence-first purchasing guide, text only.  
**Treatment B:** identical buyer content, with a separate reader-initiated optional 42-second storyboard/film concept. Seven caption segments + an original accessible SVG poster are authored; the moving film is **not rendered**. Native `details` is readable without JS. Nothing autoplays or downloads from third-party media.

**Hypothesis:** among already qualified human, purchase-intent visitors, a genuinely optional, original short explanatory video might improve *settled paid conversion per eligible session* relative to a text-only guide by reducing misunderstanding; it may also distract or hurt performance. **No uplift is assumed.**

**Experimental unit:** a consent-compatible anonymous first-party eligible session, after bot/internal filter. Hold campaign source and audience mix constant, avoid switching conditions mid-session. Equal eligibility and stable assignment must be verified by Claude's existing measurement pathway or an independently reviewed server-side design; do not activate a randomization script just for this experiment. No A/B until video is truly produced and performance/a11y approved.

**Primary decision criterion:** verified settled orders/commissions per qualified eligible session, followed by net revenue per 1,000 qualified sessions. CTA click-through and scroll are diagnostics, not a paid-conversion win. Pre-register observation window, sample warnings, abort conditions and refund lag; do not declare success from five clicks or one order.

**Abort conditions:** merchant link/terms mismatch, wrong checkout or price claim, missing disclosure, keyboard focus failure, mobile layout overflow at 360/390/768, hidden transcript, video autoplay, increased LCP materially beyond approved budget, unsanctioned consent/tracker, unexpected third-party media request, or edits outside Mind-owned paths.

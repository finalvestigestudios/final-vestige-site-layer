## Defined funnel and separate event meanings
1. **Qualified human landing session** = first-party observable session with actual asset_id, campaign, route, consent status and bot/internal/test exclusion policy audited by Claude. Do not collect raw IP, browser fingerprint, email or individual health traits. Use existing analytics; never add a duplicate script.
2. **Relevant CTA click** = article_to_fieldbook_click, guide_interest_click or independently permitted affiliate_click, each separate, counted once under a published scheme.
3. **Confirmed lead** = existing Jetpack/Dispatch confirmed signup verified through authorized aggregate reporting and delivery evidence, not button click or raw form submit. Do not add test addresses without approval.
4. **Paid confirmed order** = independently reported merchant transaction with order identifier, settled amount and permitted attribution. Affiliate network's actual settled commission is distinct from outbound clicks and estimated cart revenue.
5. **Realized net revenue** = paid settled FV-owned receipts or settled affiliate commission, less appropriate refunds and fees. Track currency, reversals and cohort close windows; deduplicate orders. If merchant evidence missing, return UNKNOWN, not 0.
6. **Primary KPI** = 1000 × realized net revenue / qualified human sessions over 7- and 30-day cohorts, with both numerator and denominator auditable. Supplement: confirmed order rate, lead-to-paid rate, refunds and fee ratio. Tiny cohort = descriptive only, not statistical victory.

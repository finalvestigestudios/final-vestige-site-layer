## One conversion hypothesis, measured as purchases

**Primary question:** Among qualified human visitors who specifically seek an independently reviewed short meditation-audio library, does Variant B (same evidence-first buyer guide + original optional 42-second video) produce **higher verified refund-adjusted net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** than Variant A (text only), without sacrificing trust, performance or accessibility?

**Null:** no improvement in verified paid conversion. B is not presumed to win. Content outside the optional film treatment must remain substantively identical. B today contains a source-controlled original SVG poster, captions and complete transcript, **not playable video**, so no A/B test or conversion result is claimed.

**Design:** Only AFTER affiliate authentication, staging QA and founder commercial authorization, allocate consenting eligible sessions once per visitor using a privacy-preserving first-party mechanism owned by Claude; no fingerprint, third-party pixel, user identity collection or new analytics script when existing tools suffice. Respect user's consent and platform policies. If no durable privacy-safe assignment is available, do not start randomized A/B. Keep source attribution separate from assignment. Ensure HTML still works when scripts are unavailable; for video, require click-to-load with controls, no autoplay, poster/captions/transcript, small compressed encodes and reduced motion. Measure performance/memory under slow mobile conditions.

**Events (exact distinction):**
1. Qualified-human *campaign_landing* (only with an appropriate consent basis and bot exclusion); `utm_source` = platform, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content` = actual Grok asset_id. Standard URL parameters are not a declaration of an observed session. No PII in query fields.
2. Relevant article → guide / existing Focus CTA interaction, first-party source only; clicks distinct from leads.
3. `confirmed_signup` **only when Jetpack itself confirms consent and membership**, if observable under approved privacy architecture. Never create fake test subscribers or send unapproved welcome messages.
4. `affiliate_outbound` **only after a verified authorized link is active**. Distinct from merchant checkout start, paid sale and refund. Never capture full affiliate-token URL or personal query parameters in analytics.
5. `merchant_confirmed_purchase` only from independently authenticated merchant ledger/order report (and proper lawful aggregation), with gross paid amount, commission entitlement/paid value, net fees and refunds, settlement date, confidence flag. Unattributable partner settlements belong in an *unattributed revenue* bucket rather than invented per-asset credit.

**KPI:** `1000 × realized_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions` on equivalent 7-day and 30-day cohorts. Net affiliate revenue = confirmed paid commission − reversed/refunded commission − related attributable fees. For an FV-owned product, net = gross paid orders − refunds − merchant/platform fees − direct fulfillment cost as measurable. Keep B2C and any owned-product metrics separate. If either numerator or denominator is missing, report **NOT COMPUTABLE** (never zero). Secondary: verified paid order rate, revenue/customer, lead-to-customer where linked with consent, refund/reversal rate. Report per-variant counts and uncertainty; tiny samples and multiple comparisons prevent significance claims. Outbound CTR is diagnostic only.

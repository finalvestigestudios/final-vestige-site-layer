## High-intent reader and truthful offer
Reader: a self-motivated visitor specifically seeking a predictable 12-minute listening routine and comparing paid organization against a free timer. Scientific curiosity about Stroop is not equivalent to buying intent; the investigation stays commerce-free. Supported product value is seller-described sequence, file formats, four listening styles, manuals and promised support/return terms. Unsupported: that the audio improves a Stroop score, cures stress, changes brainwaves in a clinically relevant way, or replaces an hour of meditation.

Original problem-to-insight copy for any future verified buyer guide: "Attention experiments can show how two signals compete. They cannot choose your meditation audio for you. If your goal is a repeatable twelve-minute routine, first try a free timer, then ask whether an organized set of recordings is worth paying for."

Useful free observation: seven 12-minute sessions with any ordinary timer, privately noting whether a session was completed and whether the user would voluntarily repeat it. This is not a randomized efficacy trial, a medical assessment or a promise of benefit.

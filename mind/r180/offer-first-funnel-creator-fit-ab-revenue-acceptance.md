## One controlled experiment, not an aesthetic contest

**A =** `zen12-premium-A-decision-text-UNPUBLISHED.html`. **B =** `zen12-premium-B-decision-optional-video-UNPUBLISHED.html`. The pages differ by one optional original poster/transcript *concept* section. B has **no playable MP4 yet**. Its proposed 42-second script explains the Hasenkamp attention cycle, explicitly disclaims Zen12 efficacy, and invites a free exercise and a buying decision. A is fully useful without video and all core actions work with no JavaScript.

Hypothesis: among sessions *already looking for structured meditation audio*, optional video may improve **verified realized net FV revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions** versus the equally useful text guide; it may instead increase page weight or distract from checkout. No “winner” from superficial clicks. The controlled release would need:
- authenticated permitted earning destination and exact merchant tier, pricing/returns snapshot, founder release approval, actual first-party tracking consent and same near-link disclosure in A/B;
- a locally served genuinely original ≤45-second playable MP4, static poster, WebVTT synchronized captions, downloadable transcript, no autoplay, user-invoked controls, low-bandwidth conditional loading; no new external fonts or scripts; identical meaningful content without JS;
- matched device/intent/creator cohort segments; single consent-compatible first-party random assignment under Claude's existing #820 instrument, not variant alternation by Grok post and not a duplicate analytics pixel;
- predeclared cohort boundaries 7 and 30 days, minimum readable sample sizes, no sequential peeking claim of significance, document practical uplift and confidence intervals with small-n warning; refund and commission payout lag handled.

**No randomization, playable video, customer testing, CI, browser performance testing or realized conversion has taken place.** A/B is an unexecuted hypothesis.

- `focus_cta_click` and `confirmed_focus_subscriber` distinct; latter only after authoritative existing Jetpack consent/confirmation and, where possible, welcome delivery. No extra forms.
- `offer_view` then `affiliate_outbound` / `affiliate_click` **only after** approved active earning link and permitted implementation. A click is not a purchase.
- `paid_conversion_confirmed`: issuer `Payhip` or partner, non-sensitive authoritative order/commission reference (stored access-controlled), timestamp, refunded/settled status, attributed/unattributed flag; NEVER from an outbound click.
- `funds_cleared` and `net_revenue_adjustment`: actual FV receipts or affiliate commissions received less refunds/fees/chargebacks that have **not already been deducted**. Merchant gross shopping cart value is not FV affiliate revenue.

`FV_net = FV_owned_cleared_customer_receipts + FV_affiliate_commissions_received - FV_refunds_chargebacks_fees_adjustments_not_already_netted` (allow negative values). Use only attributable economics or label unmatched outcomes “unattributed”; never silently distribute total commissions across campaigns. KPI `FV_net / qualified_human_sessions * 1000` is **NOT COMPUTABLE** if either source-of-truth numerator or reliable nonzero denominator is unavailable; do not report zero as a substitute. Also show verified paid customers/orders, gross vs net, lead-to-paid if independently joinable, 7/30-day cohorts, observed payout lag, material costs, confidence constraints.

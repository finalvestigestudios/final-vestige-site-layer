# Product-aware copy and ethical conversion roadmap — internal draft

Reviewed 30 October 2026; this page is science/editorial-first. **Do not place earning CTAs in the Thatcher illusion's experimental explanation.** Its independent usefulness must survive if all affiliate programs close tomorrow.

## Original copywriting architecture

The attention grabber is a *specific observable discrepancy*, not "unlock your superbrain": *The pixels did not change; the face appeared to.* Story: Thompson's two-page 1980 original → a reader-controlled, original two-portrait illustration → 1993 experiment and later contradictory mechanisms → source-led reader takeaway. The copy raises a question and actually answers it rather than withholding answers behind an affiliate click.

Reader objection, anticipated in the copy: "Does this prove my brain is broken?" Answer: No, the task does not measure an individual clinical/attention condition. "Can I train this away?" There is no cited evidence that Zen12, Total Focus or Focus Shot improves Thatcher-illusion performance. "Is the famous photo used?" No; the vector portraits are newly authored and explicitly not validated replicas.

The only active CTA is **"Open the free Focus Fieldbook →"** at `https://finalvestige.com/focus/`, connected to the existing Jetpack funnel. This is value delivery, not an endorsement. Tool-guide links remain inert, because drafts PR #180, #188, #196 are not published. Don't make live navigation to /mind/ or /mind/zen12-guide/ until the respective routes are approved and actually visible.

### From a TikTok/viewer to a qualifying offer

**Short-form teaser:** "It wasn't the face that changed. It was the orientation." Show *our original vectors*, cite Thompson, end with free story/Fieldbook destination only after the exact page is live.

**On-site article:** A complete science explanation. Reader can toggle faces, read primary sources and use the free Focus Fieldbook without leaving for an affiliate offer.

**Optional tools desk:** A separate specific product comparison page, after article, visible as an independent commercial evaluation and accompanied by a near-link commission disclosure. It must provide real product specifics, policy limitations and a free alternative. No "you saw an illusion, therefore you need this audio" leap.

**Product CTA language after gates:** "Read our independent Zen12 guide" → published Final Vestige guide; guide has separately authorized merchant earning link "Explore Zen12 on the official site" with immediate disclosure. Sitewide Affiliate Disclosure alone is insufficient.

**Optional video experiment:** In a later release compare (A) full source-led article + CSS-only illustration + free CTA and (B) identical page plus original 35–42 sec opt-in captioned video with transcript. Video is an illustration, NOT an experiment outcome. Use a lightweight poster and native player or approved cached delivery; no autoplay or loaded remote iframe before click. Engagement/click quality is a hypothesis, not proof that video boosts sales.

### Measurement without covert tracking

No tracking is installed. After privacy policy/consent review, minimal **aggregate first-party counts** may be permissible for: `mind_thatcher_guide_view` (daily total), `mind_thatcher_free_cta`, `mind_tool_guide_entry` and `approved_product_outbound_click`. Do not capture or store raw IP, raw referrer URL, UTM query strings, email address, fingerprint, unique device or client IDs, user-level face-choice responses, personal health/attention indicators, or cross-domain journey stitching. Group by general source channel only if genuinely available without user tracking. Delete or aggregate promptly. Do not claim conversion based on clicks: completed purchases require merchant-confirmed reporting with appropriate rights.

The newsletter (Issue #174) remains a **separate owner**; it may later link this noncommercial research after real publication. No newsletter copy, cadence, Jetpack settings or subscriber list is changed here.

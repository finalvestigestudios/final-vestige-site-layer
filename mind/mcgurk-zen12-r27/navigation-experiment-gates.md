## Tiny valid measurement surface
Current privacy-off.cjs is a **synthetic local design test**, not website telemetry. Future optional first-party measurement could, once authorized and consent-compatible, count only coarse categorical events: guide_view / video_intent / offer_gate_view; aggregate source buckets organic / tiktok / internal / direct / other; topic perception / suggestion / focus / other; variant text / video; product Zen12. Do not ingest raw referrers, query strings, IP, email, device fingerprint, session IDs, click identifiers or account details. Suppress reports with fewer than ten observations. Avoid a referral redirect until partner terms explicitly allow it; first-party reporting must never infer purchases from outbound intents.

## Comparison validity
A/B must keep editorial and offer copy identical. B adds one **optional original first-party film only**; its poster, disabled playback/real-hosting state and transcript remain honest. Randomized assignment needs a reviewed consent-safe design, sample-size / power expectations, guardrail outcomes (bounce, video initiation, disclosure comprehension, file weight), and accessibility parity. No claim of measured uplift, actual click behavior, affiliate orders, sales or revenue until production data and approved reporting establish them. If lawful privacy-preserving assignment cannot be implemented under the no-added-cost constraints, run qualitative usability sessions instead.

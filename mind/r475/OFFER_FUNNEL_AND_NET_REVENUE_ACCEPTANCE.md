## Controlled hypothesis and measurement
Hypothesis: among genuinely qualified audio-format shoppers, source-only B (same buyer text plus optional 42s original film with poster, WebVTT captions, readable transcript, controls and no autoplay) may improve *settled paid conversions*, compared with A identical text-only. A/B design must be registered before traffic; use existing first-party approved experiment/analytics approach, not duplicate JS. Randomization and attribution must be privacy-safe, consent-compliant and persistent only as allowed; predefine QA/internal/bot exclusions, source mix, 7- and 30-day windows, refunds and merchant-confirmation lag. Do not claim lift on tiny samples or from click-only data. Film concept is not an actual playable asset in R475. Both HTML drafts use no-JS functional reading and a genuinely disabled commercial CTA.

Events are conceptually distinct and **not implemented here**: `campaign_landing`, `article_to_resource_click`, `confirmed_signup` (confirmed independently), `affiliate_click` (outbound intent only), `merchant_confirmed_paid_order` (independent merchant evidence only), `refund` and `fee`. Do not collect email addresses, IPs, per-visitor identities, health attributes or other PII in campaign analytics. No cookies, redirect cloaking, vendor tags or third-party pixels added. If partner reporting does not connect commissions to individual assets, record attribution as missing rather than guessing.

Primary metric: `(settled_gross_revenue - verified_refunds - merchant_fees - approved_other_variable_costs) / qualified_human_sessions * 1000`. Report currency, cohort, sample size, attribution confidence and all missing data. This KPI is **NOT COMPUTABLE** without actual merchant settlements and denominator; not zero. Paid conversion rate = distinct verified purchasers / qualified sessions where linkage exists. Lead-to-customer rate requires distinct independently confirmed subscribers and later paid customers, not unique inferred email identities. Never equate an affiliate outbound click with a commission.

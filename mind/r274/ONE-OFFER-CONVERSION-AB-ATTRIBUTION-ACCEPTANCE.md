## The presell logic — original, not seller copy
**Hook:** "A twelve-minute timer is free. What would paying for a meditation library add?" The buyer-guide's opportunity is to help readers decide rather than advertise a miracle.

**Story:** One original micro-story about a crowded visual-search task can demonstrate why a plausible explanation is not the same as measured results. The new perceptual-load investigation examines Lavie (1995), Lavie/Cox (1997), counterevidence about salience (2005) and Snowden/Gray's 2025 replication challenge. The *scientific lesson* is to specify methods and limits; these experiments do not demonstrate that Zen12 treats anxiety or increases attention.

**Useful exercise:** the article's four-column observation protocol asks the reader to separate target, distractor, task and success measure, then list rival causes. The buyer guide contrasts the guaranteed utility of having a timer with the seller-described availability of audio tracks, while avoiding guarantees about results.

**Objections with real answers:** "Why not use a free timer?" — you can; the paid library primarily offers organized content and guidance. "Has this exact recording been clinically tested?" — no acceptable product-specific outcome evidence was authenticated. "What's the exact price and refund?" — distinguish seller public offers and verify final checkout and formal terms before promotion. "Can it replace treatment?" — no claim or advice to do that.

**Prospective CTA (NOT LIVE):**

Under the control, in ordinary body-size readable text:

Do not turn the control into a hyperlink until named publisher eligibility and permitted channels, original offer identity, checkout, refund mechanics, FTC proximity, independent QA and founder release approval are documented. ## Variant acceptance A vs B
**Control A:** complete accessible text-only buyer guide with useful answers and free alternative.
**Hypothesis B:** same exact text and CTA placement, with an original user-initiated ~42-second narrated visual explainer, a static poster, downloadable caption track and visible transcript. **Adding B does not guarantee uplift.** Existing R257 film is a separate review artifact; current B HTML includes only poster+transcript. Make play controls native, lazy/none preload, no autoplay, no sound unless clicked, ensure accurate WebVTT, captions, transcript, alt poster and no-JS access if development is later approved.

**Hold experiment until:** approved affiliate earning URL, explicit commercial release, clean accessibility/security review, exact A/B purchasing-content parity, legitimate merchant confirmation channel, defined human-session classification, randomization respecting consent without fingerprinting, stable variant assignment without creating a new third-party tracker, and cross-domain order reconciliation. If these are unavailable, no experiment begins. Preview screenshots and artificial clicks are not conversion outcomes.

**Primary outcome:** net revenue per 1,000 qualified human sessions, computed for 7-day and 30-day cohorts. Secondary: independently confirmed orders/customers, paid conversion rate and lead-to-customer rate. Diagnostic-only: article visits, relevant CTA clicks, confirmed leads, affiliate outbound clicks and video play. A click is not a paid order. If attribution is incomplete, mark NET_REVENUE_PER_1000=NOT_COMPUTABLE, not zero. Pre-register sample threshold and guard against spurious small-number wins.

**Security / UX:** preserve no-JS purchasing answers; labels/heading structure; keyboard focus; target area; no forced playback; no extra forms; no duplicate email capture; no external resource fetches from SVG/CSS; strictly allowlisted sourced links; no remote fonts/paid vendor SDKs; maintain mobile 360/390/768 and desktop 1440/1920 reviews. Real-browser WCAG and WordPress sanitization require Site QA signoff; source static review cannot substitute.

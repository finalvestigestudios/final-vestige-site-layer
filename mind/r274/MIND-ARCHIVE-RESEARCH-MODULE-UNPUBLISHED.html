Four questions before buying audio

  1. Does the program's actual format fit the way you want to practice?
  2. Has the specific audio program—not just meditation in general—been independently tested for its promised outcome?
  3. Is the final price, return policy and customer support clear at checkout?
  4. Would a free timer and the existing Fieldbook meet your needs?

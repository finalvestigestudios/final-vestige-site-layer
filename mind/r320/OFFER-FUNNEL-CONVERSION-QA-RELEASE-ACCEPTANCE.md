## The high-intent audience and one candidate offer

**Reader:** An adult seeking a convenient, repeatable, optional guided-audio routine rather than a diagnosis, therapy or spectacular concentration claim.

**Reader question:** “Is a twelve-minute guided download worth paying for, and what exactly do I receive?” This is more useful to a buyer than “How do I rewire my brain instantly?” or “Can science guarantee I never have another distracting thought?”

**Candidate:** the complete [Zen12](https://zen12.com/getstarted) multi-level audio offering, subject to publisher acceptance. The exact merchant checkout needs review before a future real CTA, because its promotional timing, bundled extras, guarantees and tier choices may shift.

**Alternative:** a **verified** FV digital product only after private Payhip confirmation. We do not invent its title, price, refund policy or active sale.

## Copy experiment, with a real buyer action

**A — text control:** [Zen12 text-only guide](./ZEN12-A-EVIDENCE-FIRST-TEXT-REVIEW.html). Contains a dated white-bear evidence hook, free twelve-minute neutral reflection, format and files, limits, current merchant source links for scrutiny, objections, free alternative and disabled purchase control.

**B — single-variable treatment:** [Zen12 guide + original 42-second poster/storyboard](./ZEN12-B-OPTIONAL-42S-STORYBOARD-REVIEW.html), with [six-cue timed WebVTT](./ORIGINAL-42S-CAPTIONS-CONCEPT.vtt). All substantive buyer copy is held identical. This is **not playable**; to become an actual experiment a rights-cleared original 30–45s encoded video must be produced, captioned, transcript checked and loaded only after intentional interaction, ideally with a lightweight poster/no third-party player. No video audio or music rights are assumed.

**Hypothesis (not a promise):** among matched qualified-human sessions reaching the same offer, the optional film may improve *independently confirmed paid conversion and realized net revenue* compared with text alone. It may also depress conversion by creating distraction or bandwidth cost. The null result is meaningful; no A/B winner can be called from pageviews or click proxies.

**Experiment design:** hold core copy, offer, disclosures, eligibility rules and checkout conditions constant. Random allocation must avoid cross-exposure insofar as privacy permits, with no new invasive identifiers. Do not count obviously nonhuman bot sessions. Pre-register duration, stopping criteria and minimum useful sample before launch. Avoid declaring significance on tiny samples; report practical confidence limits and missing merchant attribution. Only compare when partner/publisher agreements allow attribution and actual checkout reconciliation.

## Privacy-safe event model (specification, not deployed)

Event contracts for the existing first-party analytics owner:

- `qualified_session`: consent-eligible, human-qualified, non-duplicate session with allowed `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<actual_grok_asset_id>`, approved page path, anonymous session linkage if the existing system supports it. Never invent an asset ID.
- `offer_view`: observed eligible view of the same evidence guide with experiment arm A/B.
- `cta_click`: an actual permitted click on relevant CTA, distinguished as `focus_resource_click` or `affiliate_click`; disabled controls generate **no click event** and must not appear as conversions.
- `provider_confirmed_lead`: a confirmed, deduplicated subscription from existing Jetpack provider records, not a raw click, attempted form submission or email address stored in page analytics.
- `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`: only source-confirmed order with currency, timestamp, qualifying attribution and valid fulfilled status; direct identifiers stay inside secure commerce reconciliation, never URLs.
- `affiliate_confirmed_commission`: only partner's authenticated pending/approved/paid report, with settlement state explicit; do not count an outbound link as this.
- `refund`, `chargeback`, `fee`, `realized_net_revenue`: terminal corrections keyed via secure dedupe, not visitor-facing URLs.

The reconciliation join may be incomplete. Call such sales **unattributed** rather than invent a channel or retroactively force an asset match. Never put emails, names, sensitive mental-health terms, phone numbers, payment data or partner tokens in UTMs. No new analytics tag, third-party pixel, SaaS, hidden redirect or fingerprinting is approved. Where consent gates apply, measure only as consent permits.

**Primary KPI:** `1000 × settled net attributed revenue / qualified human sessions` over 7d and 30d cohorts. Components: eligible paid orders, unique paying customers, paid conversion rate, confirmed leads and lead-to-paid rate where observable, gross revenue, refunds, platform/partner fees and net revenue. Mark “NOT COMPUTABLE” when either numerator attribution/settlement or denominator is unavailable. Include sample size and censoring caveats.

## FTC, accuracy, and consumer protections

The [FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) requires clear, conspicuous disclosure of material connections that could change the audience's evaluation of a recommendation. The [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) explains adequate substantiation for objective health-related claims. A disclosure page alone is not an adequate nearby signal.

The reserved adjacent notice in A, B and hub says: **“If an authorized earning link is approved and eventually placed here, Final Vestige may earn a commission if you purchase through that link, at no additional cost to you.”** This is labeled prospective. A future actual link needs a direct, immediately adjacent disclosure and plain-language statement, with mobile/keyboard/screen-reader tests. No fake scarcity, testimonial, outcome guarantee, therapy promise or seller rhetoric as FV's own clinical finding.

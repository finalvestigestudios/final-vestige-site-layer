The work you left behind can follow you.

Why does an unfinished task crowd into the next one? The answer is more useful when we look beyond the productivity myth and examine what the experiments actually measured.

Researchers call the carryover of earlier task-related thinking attention residue. Their experiments suggest it can undermine performance on a task taken up after an interruption. A short plan for returning reduced that interference in studied settings; no evidence here shows that branded meditation audio is the solution.

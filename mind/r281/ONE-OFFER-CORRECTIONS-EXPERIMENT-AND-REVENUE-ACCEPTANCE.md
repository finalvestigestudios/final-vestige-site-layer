## Exact experimental hypothesis: text guide versus genuine optional video

- **Visitor and offer:** someone independently interested in a guided meditation-audio routine who lands on a relevant evidence-reviewed page and chooses to examine the exact Zen12 Standard Premium offer. The merchant link must first be permitted, account-issued and buyer-verified.
- **A:** current source-controlled R280 text-only guide.
- **B:** identical purchasing/disclosure/refund/science text plus one **original** rights-cleared 30–45-second optional film, with matching poster, native captions and full transcript. R280 B presently contains a **42-second static poster/storyboard and transcript only**. It is not a playable video test.
- **Primary business outcome:** **independently confirmed settled net revenue per 1,000 qualified human landing sessions**, with merchant-reported paid affiliate commissions joined only through permitted aggregate campaign identifiers, minus refunds, reversals and fees. If merchant reporting cannot reliably join conversions, report attributable net revenue **UNKNOWN**.
- **Secondary business outcomes:** independently merchant-confirmed paid orders, paid conversion rate, provider-confirmed Focus opt-ins, lead-to-customer conversion where independently matchable and consented. **Diagnostics only:** views, referrers, qualified sessions, CTA clicks, `affiliate_outbound_click`, film opens, watch duration if observable without additional trackers.
- **Fairness:** identical headline, offers, disclosure, options, checkout destination, dates and device mix across variants. Never force playback or withhold the full decision guide. Randomization and experiment activation are **NOT IMPLEMENTED**; do not pretend to have randomized visitors. Propose a preregistered allocation method to Claude/Site QA after merchant rights are verified. Do not use misleading scarcity or unsupported “lift” forecasts.
- **Early-stop and confounders:** halt for misleading merchant checkout, inappropriate user consent, broken disclosure, inaccessible mobile controls, excessive LCP/CLS, unapproved video rights, unexpected offsite fetch, fraud/bot sessions or changes to product terms. Warn when denominators are very small and show absolute purchase counts with uncertainty instead of dramatic percentages.

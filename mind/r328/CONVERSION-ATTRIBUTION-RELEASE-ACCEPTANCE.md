## Original article and what it proves

“The Table Moved. The Hands Said They Hadn't.” uses Michael Faraday's historical primary text, Easton & Shor's pendulum experiment, Cantergi et al.'s 2021 motion-capture evidence, Andersen et al.'s eye-tracking field study and a review of agency theories. The investigation explicitly does not endorse metaphysical conclusions, individual diagnosis, product efficacy, hypnotic manipulation, wealth gains or treatment outcomes. Its free practice is a reflection, not a clinical tool.

The product guide's factual bridge is **research-literacy**, not “science proves Zen12.” Faraday's experimental method is relevant to evaluating confident claims. His experiments provide no comparative evidence for a commercial listening program.

## One high-intent page, controlled variants

A: a useful, readable independent Zen12 format buyer guide, free twelve-minute exercise, statement of what the seller lists, objections, alternatives, source trail, and disabled purchase CTA with adjacent prospective commission disclosure.

B: **identical substantive guide** plus an original 42-second six-scene explanatory film *storyboard*. B includes an illustrated poster, a full visible transcript, and separate VTT captions. The current state is **not a playable video**. A real future film requires authorized encoding, properly sized poster, verified captions, controlled lazy loading, user-initiated playback only, no autoplay, a no-JavaScript fallback and performance measurement. No analytics script may be added just for the player.

**Predeclared hypothesis:** Among randomly and stably allocated *qualified-human sessions to the same buyer-intent destination*, the optional original explainer may improve verified paid conversion per qualified session by reducing uncertainty about the studio's evidence method. It may also be distracting; no positive effect is assumed. Variant A must stand alone if B does not load. The final outcome is net settled revenue, not click-through rate.

**Experimental safeguards:** Allocate one eligible session to A or B under consent-appropriate, first-party minimal logic owned by Claude; do not use fingerprinting, cross-site IDs, identifiable tracking URLs, raw IP exports, or unapproved scripts. Record which variant was assigned; confirm mobile and desktop rendering before any use. Avoid sequential before/after comparisons that confound seasonality and creator traffic. Both experiences must use the same substantive copy, pricing context, disclosure, destination, attribution rules and merchant offer. An offer/price change invalidates the test until both are updated. Sample size, confidence intervals and sample-ratio mismatch should be documented. Small samples are exploratory; do not declare a winner from a handful of sales.

The source before the sensation

What Faraday did that the table couldn't

In 1853, witnesses could report motion and still be mistaken about what moved first. Faraday used an indicator to investigate the direction of pressure. Modern motion capture makes tiny finger movements measurable in related pendulum experiments. The forthcoming investigation includes full sources and limitations. No release URL has been allocated.

A no-JavaScript observation

What's evidence, and what's an interpretation?

Open a short two-column reasoning exercise

Write one sentence: “I felt the table pull my hand.” In a second column, write: “A movement occurred while my hand touched the table.” Which sentence describes the feeling, and which can an observer independently check? What would you have to measure to distinguish possible causes? The goal is not to invalidate the feeling. It's to ask a better question.

This exercise is illustrative, not a diagnostic test or research result.

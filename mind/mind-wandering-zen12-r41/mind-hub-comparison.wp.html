Free experiment / No recording

You do not have to stop thinking.

Choose an ordinary sound around you. Spend thirty seconds noticing it. When a thought takes your attention elsewhere, notice that moment, and gently return to the sound. Your first return is the useful observation.

What does this actually demonstrate?

Only that you can report noticing and redirecting attention in this informal exercise. It is not a validated diagnostic test, a neuroimaging result, or evidence that a commercial audio program works. A 2012 fMRI study used a four-phase model of wandering, awareness, shifting and focused attention in 14 practitioners. Read the primary study.

## Audience, offer, commercial fit

High-intent segment: adults who already search “zen12 MP3 sessions,” “zen12 premium refund,” “meditation without visualization,” or “is twelve-minute meditation audio worth buying?” The last two contain genuinely different intent; the first two are stronger commercial intent. **Do not automatically take aphantasia search traffic straight to an affiliate offer.** The R133 investigation is strictly editorial: use the existing Focus guide as an optional step and preserve direct navigation to independent buying-guide drafts when they eventually publish. No forced signup before an eventual merchant link.

Buyer motivation: short sessions, structured downloadable format, optional routine, reliable access, understandable limitations.
Specific objections: imagery-heavy instruction fit not confirmed; seller benefit claims untested for this product; free options exist; sales-page vs conditional checkout differences; refunds untested; privacy sharing; headphone/device question; more recordings not equal better outcomes.
Buyer value if independently established: convenience and organized audio library; not a promised medical, financial or cognitive transformation.
The testable hypothesis is **whether a specific evidence-first buyer guide with an optional original educational microvideo improves verified purchase rate compared with the exact same guide without video**, not “video boosts conversions.” Keep the headline, offer, copy, CTA state, page weight, and deployment exposure comparable; until video is produced B is a storyboard and no actual conversion test can begin. For a valid experiment, record eligible/consenting human sessions by randomized assignment before exposure, 7/30-day conversion windows, seller-confirmed revenue and refunds/fees; avoid adaptive peeking at very small counts. Native user privacy/consent rules apply; no third-party scripts.

## Measurement and guardrails

The accompanying `offline-conversion-proof.cjs` is **not a site tracking script**. It expects 7/30-day dated first-party qualified-human aggregates and independently verified settled merchant amounts in integer cents. It checks that gross minus fees/refunds equals settled net, and it returns `NOT_COMPUTABLE` whenever session or merchant proof is absent. An outbound affiliate click and an impression are never promoted to a sale. Use real established first-party data and evidence references only with authorization, and do not store names, emails, IP addresses or payment details. 7/30 day cohorts must be reconciled separately; tiny samples carry explicit warnings. Main KPI: realized net revenue / 1,000 qualified human sessions.

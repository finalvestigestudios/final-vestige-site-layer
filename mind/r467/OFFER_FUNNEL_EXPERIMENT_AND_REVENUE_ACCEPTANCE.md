## Executive decision
**Zen12 is a conditional offer candidate, NOT an eligible active affiliate offer.** Public official catalog and sales copy are accessible; publisher-specific authorization, exact referral SKU, permissible media, price/refunds and paid settlement terms are not. A generic marketing email or public affiliate signup is not approval. The commercial control remains a *native disabled button*, not an outbound merchant link. Do not send a reader to an unverified sales destination.

**Owned-FV fallback:** live https://finalvestige.com/focus/ is a **free** 52-page Focus Fieldbook, not a paid SKU. The Collection and Free Library show free material and separately disclosed field-tool commerce but this sprint did not authenticate a paid, rights-cleared FV-owned Mind digital product with functioning checkout, delivery and refund terms. Therefore neither the affiliate offer nor a paid owned Mind offer can be activated honestly. Founder/Commerce must produce affirmative evidence for one before a paid CTA launches.

## High-intent audience and actual question
Audience: viewer or search visitor encountering '12 minute meditation audio' who is actively comparing short guided audio formats and skeptical of exaggerated results. Intent: *Which official audio bundle am I considering, what does it really contain, and what does the evidence say about the promise?* The guide answers without inventing experience, price, return rights or scientific outcomes.

Offer fit:
- **Zen12:** seller describes twelve 12-minute levels and four formats; potential fit with a short-session preference; strong burden of proof for marketed automatic outcomes, offer identity and payout terms.
- **Hypnosis Live:** independent tool guide needed per **exact** selected title and checkout before any future funnel, because the catalog's '200+' listing cannot establish benefits or sales rights for every MP3.
- **Brainwave Shots:** 15-minute 'shot' positioning on official catalog; independent evidence on the exact recording absent, no default conversion branch.
- **Focus Fieldbook:** genuinely free and live, only currently verified Mind funnel route; not a sale or permission to create a second subscriber list.

## Controlled A/B *hypothesis*, not a result
**A:** exact independent text buyer guide with an inert control. **B:** identical core buying text, plus original SVG poster, transcript and proposed optional 42-second film. No autoplay, no forced watch, no third-party player, no new tracking scripts. In current source-only version B is **not encoded or playable**, and cannot serve as a released film experiment.

H0: among eligible qualified human sessions assigned to A/B, the confirmed paid order rate is equal. H1: an original optional source-explaining film improves confirmed paid-order rate and net realized revenue/1000 eligible human sessions. Film may instead reduce accessibility/performance or distract from purchase. Pre-register sample size/power or a decision threshold, allocation and minimum observation window **before** allocation. Do not declare a winner on clicks, CTR, viewing completion or a tiny sample. Assignment must be privacy-reviewed by Claude using the existing first-party measurement plane, not a new pixel or third-party cookie. Disclose partner connection near activated link in both variants.

**Guardrail:** if consent is withheld, content remains entirely readable and the honest no-JS navigation works. No shopper is forced to watch a video or submit an email.

## Privacy-safe attribution contract (PROPOSED, NOT DEPLOYED)
- **Campaign provenance:** immutable platform + verified Grok asset_id in approved `utm_content`, `utm_source` platform, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_source_pilot_2026_11` only after independent asset verification and Grok/Praxxis channel approval. Never invent asset IDs.
- **Qualified-human session:** consent and bot-filtered first-party aggregate visit in existing Claude-controlled measurement; count once per session according to predeclared TTL and exclusions. No IP addresses, names, emails, raw full referrers, unbounded click IDs, health details or customer dossiers in this lane's analytics. Store no identifiers merely to support the draft.
- **Events:** `landing_view` (qualified filtered) → `guide_cta_click` (editorial CTA) → `confirmed_lead` (only Jetpack verified, not form attempt) → `affiliate_click` (outbound earning link *after* approval) → `paid_order_verified` OR `affiliate_commission_confirmed` (only independently merchant-confirmed) → `refund_or_adjustment`.
- **Event separation:** never derive `paid_order_verified` from `affiliate_click`, `checkout_start`, `video_play`, affiliate 'pending' balances or email signup. No conversion = verified without merchant reconciliation. Cross-domain match may be incomplete, and must be reported as unattributed where so.
- **Data minimization:** aggregate only consent-eligible counters by exact campaign asset and 7/30-day cohort; anonymous unmatched purchases are separately counted if settlement supports it but not falsely assigned to creator assets.
- **Reconciliation:** affiliate commission only when merchant-approved/earned and settled under confirmed contract; net revenue = cleared paid owned-sales cash + cleared affiliate commissions − refunds − chargebacks − transaction costs and any allocated direct variable fees. Avoid mixing gross merchant revenue with FV's share.
- **North star:** `1000 * net_realized_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`. Secondary: verified orders/customers, gross/net revenue, paid conversion rate, confirmed-lead-to-customer rate, 7/30 day cohorts with confidence intervals and explicit sparse-sample suppression.
- **Now:** numerator **NOT VERIFIED**, denominator **NOT VERIFIED**; KPI **NOT COMPUTABLE**. A missing denominator must not be replaced with generic pageviews or impressions. No claim of $0 realized if no financial account reconciliation occurred.

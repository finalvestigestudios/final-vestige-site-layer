## Experiment design: useful text vs guide plus optional film

Hypothesis (not a fact): Providing a short, accessible original film may increase **net paid conversion per 1,000 eligible human sessions** by clarifying a complex product distinction. Counter-hypothesis: the film distracts or burdens mobile readers, causing equal or worse conversion. Both variants must contain the **same** seller claims, objections, risk, free option, CTA position, disclaimer, price uncertainty and reading path. B adds only optional native controls and media after user action; content transcript, poster and captions must be available without playback. No loudness, autoplay, third-party player or extra script.

Randomization/assignment by existing first-party engineering only after explicit approval, privacy review and legal/consent check; avoid cookies or additional third-party tags without authorization. Consider intent strata (search vs social) and 7/30-day cohorts. Never announce winning on the first small handful of customers; show confidence intervals and sample sizes. Primary end point is **reconciled net revenue** by arm; supporting conversion rate is confirmed paid orders divided by qualified-human sessions. If affiliate merchant cannot return per-arm settled sales, this experiment **cannot answer** the primary causal question and must not be represented as a sales-conversion test. Track noncommercial click quality separately.

## Data contract (NO NEW SCRIPT INSTALLED)

`utm_source={tiktok|youtube|instagram|...}`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content={actual_verified_asset_id}`. Only on approved destination URLs, checked on mobile and non-app browsers. No guessed asset IDs.

Proposed event semantics, subject to Claude #820 reuse of native tools and legal consent:
* `qualified_human_session`: 1 consent-compliant visit with documented bot/developer exclusions; use a nonidentifying aggregate count. Avoid fingerprinting, IP retention and unique user profiles.
* `landing_visit`: total page entry for campaign/destination; not a qualified human by itself.
* `relevant_cta_click`: an intentional link activation; distinguish `focus_click` from `affiliate_click`. No earning link exists today.
* `confirmed_lead`: Jetpack/provider confirmed subscription with consent; cannot infer from form submit.
* `checkout_start`: only when independently observed at the merchant, not inferred from an outbound click.
* `confirmed_paid_order`: actual seller/Payhip verified transaction after refunds; `settled_affiliate_commission`: independently reported/reconciled paid commission, net of reversals. Distinguish gross sale value from commissions.
* `realized_net_revenue`: collected eligible receipts less refunds, merchant fees, affiliate reversals, known variable campaign costs (if attributable), with an explicit completeness flag. The KPI is **1,000 × net revenue / qualified human sessions** for each 7/30-day cohort. Without both, **NOT COMPUTABLE, not zero**. Never mix traffic views and distinct qualified humans.

Do not collect name/email in bespoke analytics; leave the existing list under Jetpack. No scripts, pixels, SaaS or duplicate consent architecture. Report paid-customer sample and lower-confidence bands for tiny cohorts, plus reconciliation coverage. If only aggregate merchant reports exist without campaign attribution, report unattributed receipts separately instead of allocating them arbitrarily.

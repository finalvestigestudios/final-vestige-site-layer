# The Lever That Told on the Hand — forty-second original film

1853. A table seems to move without anyone intending it. Some sitters say the table pulls their hands. Faraday builds a simple indicator to test which moves first. When the indicator is visible, unwanted movement diminishes. A force from the hands precedes movement of the table. The motion is real. Feeling a cause is not proof of that cause.

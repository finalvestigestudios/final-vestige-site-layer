## Ethical A/B protocol
- Candidate A = complete source-based text guide.
- Candidate B = exactly the same complete substantive guide HTML, plus an optional original 40s educational film, first-party poster, caption track and transcript; absent hosted files, provide only HTML no-JS poster and transcript.
- Hypothesis: optional original context may improve useful engagement, but may also distract, impede loading, or reduce trust. No lift asserted.
- Assignment plan requires independent founder and site QA approval; if implemented later, a privacy-safe first-party experiment could assign A/B at page load without cookie/IP/persistent identifier and report only aggregate categorical counts >= 20. Better yet, for baseline use existing first-party analytics only if their data policies permit and no new visitor identifiers are added. Do not pretend pageview = reading or click = purchase.
- Guardrails: no affiliate click before permission, no article conclusions changed by variant, no different price, no hidden disclosure, no auto-playing video, no extra Jetpack form.
- Future primary metric: distinct disclosed outbound merchant click rate per eligible guide view, interpreted only as click intent; actual affiliate sales only through authorized partner reports with consent and without personal data.
- Other measured outcomes when allowed: optional film play/complete ratio, source click, free Focus route click, page scroll, CWV, a11y failures. No tracking presently.
- Decision rule before test: require at least 20 observations per reporting cohort; don't call statistical significance or make uplift claims with sparse data. Define primary metric, time window and minimum detectable effect with QA before activation.

02 / Source ledgers and power

When a witness becomes an authority

The history of suggestion is full of confident accounts. It is also full of reasons to ask how an observation was obtained and what alternative explanations were considered. Puységur's animal-magnetism files invite that kind of close reading.

The record, not a modern endorsement.

The historical archive documents reported phenomena, witnesses, contested cures and consent questions. It does not establish that magnetic somnambulism or today's commercial hypnosis recordings achieve the claimed effects.

Explore the linked Puységur source trail
Evidence standard

Three questions before believing a story

  1. Where is the earliest surviving document?
  2. Who disagreed, and did they have access to underlying evidence?
  3. What can the source *not* tell us?

The Rosenhan case is a case study in source integrity, not medical diagnostic advice.

04 / Tools under review, kept separate from investigations

If you still want a guided recording, compare what is actually offered.

Zen12 is described by its seller as a twelve-level library with 48 MP3 recordings. Hypnosis Live Total Focus is described as one 33-minute MP3. Brainwave Shots Focus Shot is a different audio format. Those are seller descriptions, not independent proof of focus or treatment benefits.

Zen12: a structured meditation-audio library, not a proven shortcut

The most concrete potential value is the convenience of a catalog organized into levels. Decide whether you want that format after trying free practice. The official checkout lists bonus materials and a one-year money-back guarantee, but exact prices, complete terms, fulfillment and refunds need verification before recommending a purchase.

A no-video, no-JavaScript observation

The second inventory

Put ten harmless objects on a table. First look for twenty seconds while counting objects partly made of metal. Write down what you remember. Then look again with no counting task and list all ten. Which details became prominent when the task changed?

This is an original reflection activity—not a clinical assessment, a validated replication of the gorilla study or a test of anyone's intelligence. The purpose is to keep observation separate from interpretation.

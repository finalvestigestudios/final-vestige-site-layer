## Only approved measurement: human visitors to realized FV revenue

Event contract is a specification to reuse existing first-party mechanisms, not permission to add an analytics library:

`campaign_landing_visit { asset_id, utm_campaign, route, variant, consent_status, bot_filter_version }` → `relevant_cta_click` → `jetpack_confirmed_subscribe` where existing provider can authenticate the subscription → `affiliate_click` when link authorized and actually used → independent `merchant_paid_order` / `cleared_commission` report → `refund`, `chargeback`, `fees`.

No IPs, emails, third-party browser fingerprints, device IDs or hidden cross-site matching. Use consent-aware aggregated first-party reporting and short retention; source identifiers refer only to creator asset IDs verified in TASK-008. Treat unknown values as **null/unverified**, never infer real humans from automated WordPress views. A browser click is not an order; checkout start is not an order; a merchant-reported sale is not automatically a *cleared* commission. **Do not sum the full retail sale price of affiliate products into Final Vestige gross income.**

**7- and 30-day cohorts:** Qualified-human unique sessions by authenticated creator asset; eligible confirmed paid orders; FV-owned net receipts and cleared FV affiliate commission; subtract actual refunds, chargebacks, payment fees and other attributable costs where known. KPI = 1,000 × *realized net revenue* / *qualified-human sessions*. If verified eligible denominator, merchant settlement or refund/fee data are unavailable, output **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0, and disclose what is missing. Warn prominently when any cohort has a small count or zero confirmed sales.

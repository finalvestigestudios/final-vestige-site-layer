## Decision before design

**Intended offer:** Zen12 **Premium**, not “some Inspire3 product.” Audience: readers genuinely considering the purchase of an organized short-session meditation-audio library, who want to know whether it is worth paying for compared with a free timer. This is not the same audience as curious viewers of a McGurk or change-blindness Short. Perception research earns trust and *may* direct an interested reader to a separate, plainly identified buyer guide. It must never pretend a laboratory illusion demonstrates product benefits.

**Currently buyable through Final Vestige:** NOT VERIFIED. The portal publicly advertises a network and affiliate payout; it does not prove Final Vestige's named approval. Routine Inspire3 consumer marketing received in Gmail does not confer affiliate status. We found no authenticated issued earning URL, website/social/email permission, or partner transaction feed. Existing FV Payhip inventory has **not been independently authenticated as a rights-cleared Mind-specific paid substitute**. Treat this as unavailable evidence, not a rejection and not evidence of zero sales.

**Exact seller alignment as of November 9, 2026:** The official https://zen12.com/hnp page lists Premium twelve levels and 48 MP3s with four choices of sound per level, and an advertised one-year money-back promise. Its publicly displayed price area presently shows a rounded “$87” while earlier Mind R260 recorded $87.99 from its then-visible seller presentation. Another official https://zen12.com/secret/ page shows an advertised $37 promotional proposition with countdown/coupon treatment. We have NOT checked the final cart, taxes, currencies, fees, entitled package parity, or refund completion. Neither is an authorized FV affiliate destination. Never copy the countdown or advertise either price as an approved checkout price.

**Commercial claim:** Package organization may be useful to an interested buyer; the seller's claim that twelve minutes equals an hour of advanced meditation is *not* verified for the exact product. Do not use the sound-induced flash illusion, general binaural-beat findings, hypnosis history, or general meditation research as substantiation for Zen12's asserted efficacy. The FTC requires claim-specific evidence; its endorsement guidance calls for a plain commission disclosure near the earning link, not a vague footer-only “affiliate” label.

## Intent-specific presell copy, held in source

**Hook:** “If twelve quiet minutes with a free timer are possible, why pay for a meditation recording? Buy the library only if its structure, voices, sound styles, and accessibility meet a need you can actually name.”

**Useful micro-story:** In Shams's 2000 laboratory work, one light flash could be experienced as two when paired with tones. That scientifically controlled observation teaches a distinction: perception is a report, not proof of the mechanism that produced it. The experiment is *not* sales evidence for a commercial audio package. It should lead to a factual explanation, not a product sales conclusion.

**Noncommercial demo:** Try a freely chosen twelve-minute quiet session. Before it, note in simple words whether you intend to reflect, relax, or practice staying with a task. Afterward, observe only whether you completed it and whether the format suited you. These are personal preference notes, not a clinical effectiveness test. Do not invite readers to submit health data.

**Specific offer value if authorized:** A seller-described curated set of twelve progression levels and four listening styles per level. The potential consumer value is convenience, variety, structured sessions and offline MP3 ownership/use conditions **if confirmed in the exact checkout agreement**. No expertise, medical benefit, or first-person product testing should be claimed.

**Honest objections:** a free timer already creates a time boundary; an audio track may be distracting rather than useful; expensive or cheap versions may have different deliverables; reported feelings cannot prove a brainwave mechanism; some listeners should avoid treating meditation as a medical substitute; a refund promise needs exact merchant terms. Address all objections before presenting any final CTA.

**Future approved CTA, currently disabled:** “Review the exact Zen12 Premium offer and current checkout.” Immediately adjacent *if approved*: “If you purchase through this link, Final Vestige may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.” The code has no link or event firing today. Link may ONLY be installed after named partner approval, exact terms, delivery/claim review, founder and independent QA release permission.

## One canonical test instead of repeatedly cloning pages

**Control A:** reuse the canonical R257 evidence-first, text-only guide. **Treatment B:** same factual copy, headings, commercial terms, disclosure and CTA, plus an *optional* original 42-second short film. Its poster, WebVTT, transcript and selectable playback controls must be correctly accessible and tested. The R257 locally rendered 540×960 H.264 MP4 was inspected and had zero audio streams. No authored/hosted media is live, and no variant has been published. In particular the illustration and transcript concept must not be misrepresented as actual playable hosted video.

**Hypothesis:** An optional original film may reduce ambiguity and improve confidence for readers arriving with genuine buying intent. It may also introduce distraction or consume bandwidth. The hypothesis is not a predicted conversion lift. A balanced traffic assignment and unchanged offer/cart are prerequisites to interpretation; do not compare visits across different periods as though randomized.

**Access/performance gates:** no-JS displays entire buyer guide and CTA state; never auto-play; native poster, captions and transcript; keyboard and touch controls; tested 360/390/768/1440/1920 widths, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, focus indicator, text zoom, 200% scaling, reduced motion; video should be requested only after user action and require explicit source-controlled cache/fallback strategy. One actual browser report and native code tests remain outstanding. Prevent scripts from third-party player, duplicate Ahrefs/Jetpack measurement, new cookies, speculative UTM-to-person stitching or external tracking pixels.

**No false positives:** A click on a PDF resource, an affiliate outbound link, a confirmed subscriber, a checkout initiation, a settled commission, and final net revenue are different events. A/B outcome cannot be called “success” from watch time, impressions, affiliate clicks or unconfirmed orders. Refunds, chargebacks and costs must be accounted.

## Attribution contract for Claude; specification only

Use existing first-party tooling when possible. Proposed campaign URL tags only when platform permits and linked route is verified: `utm_source=(youtube|tiktok)`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=mind_zen12_pilot_2026_11`, `utm_content=<actual immutable asset_id>`. A shared profile link needs `utm_content=channel_profile`, never an invented Shorts ID. Never put emails, names, raw IPs, health data, purchase identifiers or other personal information in UTMs. Consider bot/in-house exclusion rules with transparent uncertainty.

Event meaning and proof:
- `campaign_landing`: documented first-party visit with valid attributable tag; **not** automatically a qualified human.
- `article_to_resource_click`: contextual navigation; a diagnostic, not a lead.
- `confirmed_signup`: only provider-confirmed consented subscription, without another Jetpack form/list. A pop-up/open or button press is not confirmation.
- `affiliate_click` or `affiliate_outbound`: one vendor-facing click after approved destination; **not a sale**.
- `merchant_confirmed_paid_order`: authenticated commerce system paid receipt, with amount/currency and allowed reconciliation reference; no visitor PII export into analytics.
- `merchant_settled_commission`: partner-issued verified and paid amount; must adjust reversals.
- `refund_or_chargeback`: authenticated reversal; subtract in realized revenue.

**Primary KPI** = `1000 × (settled FV retained receipts + cleared affiliate commission − refunds − payment fees − attributable campaign costs) ÷ qualified human sessions`. Separate gross receipts, verified orders, net margin and paid conversion rate; compute seven- and thirty-day cohorts with clearly recorded start dates and tiny-sample intervals. Neither denominator nor settlement records have been supplied: KPI = **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0.

**Privacy gate:** no visitor fingerprint or attempt to infer person-level matching from public media IDs. No new cookies/JS/event infrastructure without Claude review, legal consent basis, Site QA, founder release. Aggregate campaigns and merchant financial totals are sufficient for exploratory results, but cannot magically produce individual customer attribution without merchant-approved mechanisms.

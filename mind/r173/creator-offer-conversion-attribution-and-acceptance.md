## A/B experiment — hypothesis, not outcome

**Question:** Among independently identified high-intent readers who arrive at an approved Zen12 decision guide, does an optional, original 42s evidence video increase the rate of **independently verified paid FV commission outcomes** relative to the same guide without video, without violating performance and trust limits?

**Control A:** Exact guide text, seller caveats, free alternative, approved disclosed offer (only after all gates), no video.

**Variant B:** Identical copy/design/CTA plus an optional 42s video with local poster, accessible timed captions and complete transcript. Never auto-play or force video before reading. The source-controlled native `details` text remains a design concept, not evidence that the video is built.

**Traffic and assignment:** Only after Claude/Site QA approves first-party privacy-safe attribution. Prefer an existing platform analytics mechanism; no new paid SaaS, redundant pixels or PII. Randomize session-level variants with a privacy-respecting stable assignment method reviewed for consent and repeat viewing. Keep actual creator assets balanced across arms to avoid misleading quasi-random “post A sends to A, post B sends to B” results. No experiment on unidentified bot-heavy traffic. Predeclare sample, duration and harm/stop criteria; with tiny traffic, report intervals and descriptive counts, not statistical significance.

**Guardrails:** no increase in LCP regressions, keyboard traps, unexpected modal interruptions, reading abandonment, missing material-connection disclosure, checkout errors, canceled charges, unsubscribe friction or misleading product claims. Site QA owns real viewport checks 360/390/768/1440/1920, performance/WCAG, no-JS, focus order, transcript synchronization, returns and mobile checkout walkthrough when release is approved.

**Sequence with distinct evidence:**
`qualified_human_session` (backend/classifier basis recorded) → `campaign_landing` → `article_to_resource_click` or `buying_guide_interest` → `confirmed_signup` **only from provider acceptance/confirmation** (not popup shown or PDF downloaded) → `affiliate_outbound` **only if merchant link approved** → `merchant_reported_paid_affiliate_conversion` (account report) → `commission_paid_and_cleared` (settlement) → adjustments/refunds. For FV-owned goods, use `merchant_confirmed_paid_owned_order`, fulfillment proof, then cleared receipt adjusted for refunds, chargebacks and *non-duplicated* fees. A shopping click, checkout open, email form submission, or reported merchant gross sales is **not FV realized revenue**.

**Formula (separate 7- and 30-day cohorts):**
- `fv_owned_net = cleared_customer_receipts - refunds - chargebacks - fees_or_costs_not_already_net_of_receipts`.
- `fv_affiliate_net = cleared_commissions_received_by_FV - commission_clawbacks_or_costs_not_already_withheld`.
- `fv_net_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * (fv_owned_net + fv_affiliate_net) / verified_unique_qualified_human_sessions`.
- Keep negative net negative. Do **not** clamp losses to zero. If the denominator is zero or unavailable, or settlements/attribution cannot be reconciled, KPI = **NOT COMPUTABLE**; this must not be printed as zero. Avoid double-subtracting discounts already reflected in receipts.
- Show cohort start/end, asset_id, campaign/landing, qualifying rule, sample size, confirmed leads, confirmed paid orders, affiliate confirmed conversions, paid commissions, refunds, fees, gross, net and data quality/confidence. If tiny samples, give raw counts and appropriate warnings.

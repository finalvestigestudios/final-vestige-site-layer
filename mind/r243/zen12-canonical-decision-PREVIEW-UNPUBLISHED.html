Which kind of focus audio are you actually buying?

A timer, a meditation course, a concentration soundscape and a narrated hypnosis session are not interchangeable. Start with what is inside the product—not the result promised on its cover.

One useful exercise before spending

Choose one measurable twelve-minute task. Start a plain timer; record what you finished and how the session felt. Repeat on another comparable day. This is a personal journal prompt, not proof of a treatment or an audio program's effectiveness.

A result in a lab is not a result in your day

Researchers can measure a specific EEG response in a controlled setting. That does not mean a commercially sold recording causes a useful change in work, concentration, mood or health. The 2023 PLOS ONE review of fourteen binaural-beat EEG studies reported mixed and contradictory findings; it did not test any of the three specific products compared here.

## 3. Real A/B hypothesis, not a reported victory

Variant A: independent source-led text buyer guide, verified seller-described package contents, science and limits, no-cost twelve-minute exercise, objections, near-CTA disclosure and genuinely disabled off-site purchase control. Variant B: identical substantive buyer core plus an original 42-second optional film treatment, poster, timed captions and expandable transcript. No hosted playable video currently exists **within these HTML specimens**. Narration and caption sync, video encoding/hosting and actual QA are not complete.

The sole intended experimental difference is *optional film versus no film*, not changing price, claim strength, CTA, legal disclosure or copy. Hypothesis: an optional 42-second film could clarify distinctions and improve informed paid purchase rate, or it could add friction and lower trust. No causal uplift is asserted. Test after permission using an approved same-origin, accessible poster-first HTML5 video with controls and preload=none; no autoplay, forced motion, extra script, pixel or unnecessary load. Include visible transcript and captions; if any commercial relationship is endorsed inside audio/visual media, test clear on-media disclosure too.

Primary measure: verified settled/refund-adjusted paid revenue per 1,000 independently qualified human sessions; secondary paid conversion rate and qualified lead-to-customer where observable. Outbound clicks, video plays and newsletter signups are diagnostic only. Report 7- and 30-day cohorts, n, traffic-quality exclusions, differences and uncertainty. Do not call a tiny sample a winner. Persist A/B assignment only through an existing privacy-approved first-party method.

## 5. Privacy-safe measurement and reproducible testing specification

Formula: realized net = independently settled owned sales + independently reported earned affiliate commissions − refunds/chargebacks − processing fees − actual attributable costs.

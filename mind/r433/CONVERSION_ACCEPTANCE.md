Variant A is the complete independent source-led Zen12 buyer guide: original free exercise, evidence limits, five objections and disabled merchant button. Variant B is the SAME substantive guide with an optional original 42-second storyboard, SVG poster, fully written transcript and six cue WebVTT. Null hypothesis: no meaningful paid-order-rate difference between A and B among similarly qualified humans with an actually authorized checkout. Primary metric: independently merchant-confirmed **realized net revenue per 1,000 qualified-human sessions**, with paid-order rate and refund rate secondary; separately monitor consented provider-confirmed leads, CTA eligibility, checkout errors and quality of objections.

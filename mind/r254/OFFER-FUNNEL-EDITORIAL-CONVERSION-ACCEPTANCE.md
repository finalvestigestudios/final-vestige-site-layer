## High-intent buyer objections addressed

1. “Can a free timer meet the need?” Often yes, especially for a simple twelve-minute pause or task start. The landing guide offers a no-purchase exercise.
2. “What actually costs money?” Seller-described library organization, guided/nature/music/white-noise variants, progression and accessories, **not** independently proved neurological or clinical effects.
3. “Is Premium an entire year of daily files?” No such independently demonstrated use cadence is asserted. Twelve *levels*, 48 files; customer controls usage.
4. “Does the one-year guarantee remove all risk?” No. Only seller-described policy; current written terms and real refund execution unverified.
5. “Will it make me more productive?” No exact-product objective trials were authenticated.
6. “Can I click to buy now?” No: the native control is disabled until the studio has a verified authorized earning destination, acceptable claims and release signoff. A plain seller URL may appear in a research source ledger but not as a purchasable active CTA.

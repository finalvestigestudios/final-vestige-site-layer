# The Second Number — 40-second original educational short

**Status:** unpublished offline first-party production. Conceptual illustration, **not a validated attentional-blink stimulus or Zen12 recommendation**. Silent on purpose; full text appears on-screen, in captions, and below.

**00–06 s:** Two numbers. One fast stream of letters.

**06–12 s:** You report the first. The second arrives soon after.

**12–18 s:** The report can fail—even while the stimulus was presented.

**18–25 s:** And immediately consecutive targets can sometimes escape the gap.

**25–32 s:** Three months of intensive meditation training is not a consumer audio trial.

**32–40 s:** What was measured? What was inferred? Follow the evidence.

**Visual design:** high-contrast ivory on charcoal with gold rules; abstract numerals 7/4, schematic timing brackets, and a three-column contrast of training, audio and inference. No borrowed footage, stock imagery, commercial audio, icon packs or logos.

**Accessibility:** six caption cues; adjacent full transcript; poster SVG has <title>/<desc>; no autoplay; video is optional with native controls only in offline review file. Draft WordPress B shows honest static poster while first-party hosting remains unapproved. No seizure-inducing flicker or rapid actual stimulus. Viewer choice is always permitted.

**Performance budget:** 540 × 960 pixels, 12 fps, 40 seconds; local H.264 under 1 MB target; optional click-to-play, preload none; poster local inline SVG ~2 KB; no third-party player or analytics.

**Evidence:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1500880/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17488185/ ; https://doi.org/10.1080/02724980443000647 . The film narrates research, not a merchant efficacy promise.

The Mind Is Not a Reliable Witness.

An extraordinary mind can also be an unreliable one. The same discipline we apply to a ship's log and a lost expedition belongs to eyewitness perception, hypnotic testimony, and the promises of tools meant to change how we think.

The Mind & Perception Archive is an editorial proposal that asks what actually happened, what an experiment measured, and what an observer merely believed. Its starting point is a working historical record: Puységur's Animal Magnetism Files, with 153 listed source rows and 16 case dossiers in the published project.

Tools Under Review

What are you actually buying?

A structured meditation recording may be convenient. A clinically verified intervention is a different proposition. Our product files separate precise merchant features from claims the independent literature can—and cannot—support. Buyers should know the difference before seeing any recommendation.

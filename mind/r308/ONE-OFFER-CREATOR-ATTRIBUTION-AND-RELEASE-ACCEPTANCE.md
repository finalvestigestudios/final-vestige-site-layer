## High-intent buyer and exact seller match

**Audience:** an adult actively comparing short, self-directed meditation recordings and willing to pay for a structured library, as distinct from a viewer who simply enjoys surprising attention illusions. The reader's decision: do I want the convenience and format of a set of audio recordings enough to buy it, without expecting scientifically guaranteed attention or health results?

**Verified through official pages:**
- [Inspire3 catalog](https://inspire3.com/) identifies its Zen12 and Hypnosis Live brands.
- [Zen12 About](https://zen12.com/about) describes 12 levels; four styles per level—music, guided, white noise, nature—and 12-minute sessions. The seller separately claims automatic relaxation, brainwave control and hour-equivalence, none of which this guide adopts.
- [Zen12 HNP seller page](https://zen12.com/hnp) describes a full 48-MP3 library but also **different 24-MP3 tiers**. Do not write “every Zen12 package contains 48 files.” The seller advertises a one-year return guarantee; exact live checkout and applicable conditions must be verified for the particular earning URL and tier before any public price/returns claim.
- [Hypnosis Live Total Focus](https://hypnosislive.com/mental-skills/total-focus) advertises a different 33-minute/47MB standalone hypnotic session with ambitious concentration rhetoric. It is NOT a Zen12 substitute in the merchandise sense.
- [Brainwave Shots Focus Shot](https://brainwaveshots.com/focus-shot) lists 15- and 30-minute recordings plus brainwave-only versions. Its effects, safety claims and return conditions are seller representations.
- [FTC Endorsement Guides Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking): put an understandable commission disclosure beside the actual recommendation; “affiliate link” alone is not plain enough.
- [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance): every explicit or implied objective psychological or medical benefit needs appropriate, product-relevant substantiation. A general meditation study cannot validate Zen12's own files.

**Claim-to-proof boundary:** One 1959 and two later own-name studies confirm limited *speech-selective-attention* observations. They do not evaluate commercial meditation, hypnosis, or brainwave audio products. The buyer page explicitly stops before that inferential leap.

The working source-controlled copy answers five buyer questions: What is the actual package? Why might a short routine be appealing? What experiment can I try for free? What is unknown? How can I compare the current offer honestly, including returns and alternatives? It gives equal prominence to the existing free Fieldbook.

Preserve canonical original R284 Zen12 A/B and Mind hub PR #172. R308 A/B are **comparison review specimens only**; no change to the canonical test's copy, route, merchant terms or design system is proposed without independent review. Do not split traffic to multiple unapproved pilots.

**Variant A:** same buyer narrative, free 12-minute observation, source and limitation notes, disabled purchase control, adjacent prospective disclosure, one direct existing /focus/ link. Zero merchant earning URLs.

**Variant B:** byte-identical buyer content and placement of the purchase and free CTA; only difference is an **original 42-second abstract poster and timed transcript** presented in a collapsed optional disclosure. It is *not playable media*; no audio, external embed, player or script. Producing a 30–45-second downloadable video with a light original visual, human-caption-checked VTT, manual play control, reduced-motion behavior and a measurable performance budget belongs to a subsequent authorized production/release gate. Do not report R308 as a playable video A/B conversion trial.

**Hypothesis:** Among comparable eligible sessions and after legitimate merchant activation, adding a personally optional 42-second explainer may improve the rate of verified paid commissions *per qualified human visitor* relative to equivalent text-only copy. The alternative hypothesis is that it adds friction, delay or distraction and lowers net yield. Source tests cannot settle either hypothesis.

**Experiment prerequisites:** fixed campaign tagging; same audiences and traffic sources where possible; stable, documented assignment strategy approved by engineering; exclusion of internal/bot sessions; no crossing variant assignment based on sensitive signals; identical actual offer/terms/CTA; mobile and accessibility gates; sufficient sample before inference; predeclared primary metric and stopping rule. Tiny samples warrant uncertainty and no victory declaration. No adaptation based only on unverified outbound clicks.

**Permitted campaign contract (after owner validation):** `utm_source` is the actual platform; `utm_medium=organic_social`; `utm_campaign=mind_<topic>_2026_11` only if named and approved by Grok/Praxxis; `utm_content=<authenticated published asset_id>`. Never write a made-up ID into production. Preserve normal URLs with server-side compatible, no-JS behavior; do not forward raw referrers containing private user data to a merchant.

**Event status:**
1. `campaign_landing`: aggregate landing sessions, with verified provenance and reasonable bot/internal exclusions. Record count only if independently observable.
2. `article_to_focus_click`: a click, not a signup.
3. `confirmed_signup`: only provider-confirmed Jetpack consent/receipt, not clickthrough or form impression.
4. `affiliate_click`: only after eligible partner URL activation; separately measure outbound activation but never call it a commission or sale.
5. `merchant_confirmed_paid_conversion`: independently authenticated merchant report, with correct Final Vestige publisher attribution and paid status.
6. `refund_or_reversal`: reconcile later merchant adjustments.
7. `cleared_fv_commission`: paid/cleared attributable earnings less **only** adjustments not already reflected; do not subtract refunded orders twice.

**Primary KPI** `net_revenue_per_1000_qualified_human_sessions = 1000 * cleared_FV_net_revenue / qualified_human_sessions`. When either input is missing or denominator is zero/uncertain, report **NOT COMPUTABLE**, not $0 or a fabricated percentage. Use 7-day and 30-day acquisition cohorts, cohort maturation, separate attribution completeness, declared timezone and tiny-sample warnings. Secondary: paid order rate, refunds, confirmed leads, lead-to-customer conversion. Do not blend own-product order gross value with affiliate payout.

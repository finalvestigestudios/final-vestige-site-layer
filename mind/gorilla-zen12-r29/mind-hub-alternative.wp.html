03 / Practice & reflection

Try the small experiment first.

A useful investigation should leave you with a way to look more carefully, even if you never buy anything.

Two passes, one desk

Study an ordinary desk while counting objects of one color. Write your inventory. Look again without the counting assignment. Record what changed in your description, not in the objects. This informal exercise is not a validated test or therapy.

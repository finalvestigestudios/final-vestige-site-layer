**Reuse distinctions:** LOC's [stereograph rights guidance](https://wwws.loc.gov/rr/print/res/493_ster.html) explains that U.S.-published 1904 copyrighted stereographs are past their 95-year protection period, while catalog item pages remind researchers that LOC does not grant permissions. The four LOC records' **'no known restrictions on publication'** advisory is useful but **not a transfer of license** or independent clearance of every reproduction/crop. Commons' U.S.-only pre-1931 public-domain tags and Erik Edson's separate 2007 worldwide dedication require individual record-keeping. All seven remain `rights_review: HOLD_item_level`, `binary_identity_verified: false` and `wp_upload_authorized: false`.

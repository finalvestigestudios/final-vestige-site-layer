- [USGS item page](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/hebgen-lake-fault-scarp-1959) independently identifies the 1959 Hebgen Lake fault scarp, credits **J. R. Stacy**, and labels its image **Public Domain**. Clicking the publisher's **Original** link displayed the photograph in the independent viewer. The linked URL is `https://d9-wret.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/assets/palladium/production/s3fs-public/thumbnails/image/img7535.jpg`, **different from the currently embedded `/s3fs-public/img7535.jpg` path**.
- The USGS source-path difference is a **strong candidate for exact-subject recovery**, not proof of byte-identical imagery, a broken post, permitted republishing, or completed rights review. Do not swap links blindly. Preserve J. R. Stacy / USGS credit, review individual use conditions and verify original dimensions and binary fingerprint. If approved later, prefer an authorized cached 960px-max first-party derivative with preserved aspect ratio and no upscaling, plus responsive sizes and a visual before/after.

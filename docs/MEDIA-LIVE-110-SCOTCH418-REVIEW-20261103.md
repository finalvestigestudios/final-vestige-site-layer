## Defensible 76% claim calculation and 42-image denominator

Do not divide historical 64.9 MB or 43.5 MB *media-library storage* by an assumed new size to claim user-facing page savings. For any identical set of images `I` successfully loaded with equivalent viewport, DPR, cold cache and scripts, measure **actual completed network bytes** `B_before = Σ_i encodedDataLength_i` and `B_after = Σ_i encodedDataLength_i`. Compare `100 × (B_before - B_after)/B_before`. Require matching image identity and source/crop, final successful HTTP status, MIME, normalized request type, verified decode and all redirect/cache/retry exclusions. Report per-post coverage and median repeat runs; reject cross-run comparisons with unmatched images or missing bytes. Correctly identify `i0.wp.com` first-party proxy origin separately from third-party host upstreams. Do not accept **76%** absent before/after CDP data. Do not call the reported **42** broken until the alleged 42 source IDs/URLs have actual guest-browser outcomes and a frozen denominator.

## Primary institutional sources and qualifications (checked 2026-11-11)
1. Senate **How to Research Treaties**: https://www.senate.gov/legislative/HowTo/Treaties.htm (directly read; Senate vs executive-agreement distinction, scope of sources).
2. Senate **Advice and Consent**: https://www.senate.gov/about/origins-foundations/senate-and-constitution/advice-and-consent-treaties.htm (directly read; procedure).
3. NARA **Test Ban Treaty (1963)**: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/test-ban-treaty (directly read; TIAS 5433/RG 11; signature 5 August, Senate action 24 September, entered into force 10 October 1963; Senate consent is distinguishable from presidential ratification/deposit).
4. UN **Treaty Collection**: https://treaties.un.org/ (directly read; only treaties for which UN acts as depositary).
5. U.S. State **Finding Agreements** (archived): https://2021-2025.state.gov/finding-agreements/ (official indexed text; direct automated open returns technical difficulties; NOT 2026 legal status).
6. U.S. State **How to Find Treaty and Agreement Texts** (archived): https://2021-2025.state.gov/texts-of-agreements/ (indexed; direct open technical difficulties; TIAS policy requires period verification).
7. U.S. State **Treaties in Force** (archived): https://2021-2025.state.gov/treaties-in-force/ (edition-bound exclusions; direct open technical difficulties; no claim of 2026 currency).
8. NARA **Viewing American Indian Treaties**: https://www.archives.gov/research/native-americans/treaties/viewing-treaties (directly read; ratified vs unratified files, sovereign/historical context).
9. NARA **Supporting Documentation**: https://www.archives.gov/research/native-americans/treaties/supporting-documentation (directly read; related negotiation records, custody).
10. NARA **Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo**: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/treaty-of-guadalupe-hidalgo (directly read; separates 1848 signature, Senate conditions, exchange and proclamation).

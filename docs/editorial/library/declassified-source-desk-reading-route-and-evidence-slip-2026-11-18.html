Final Vestige field desk · A reading route for this guide

Begin with the claim. Then find the paper.

A government URL can be authentic and still leave the important question unanswered. It might lead to a catalogue description, a later editor’s note, a recollection, or a second printing of a document you have already counted. Choose the problem in front of you. Each route below leads to the corresponding worked investigation on this page.

  1. 01 · FINDOpen the itemKeep its stable identifier and repository, not just a search-result title.
  2. 02 · NAMEIdentify the witnessWho made it? When? Is this an original, transcription, translation, or later description?
  3. 03 · FOLLOWTrace the familyAsk whether a later citation reproduces the same document or brings independent evidence.
  4. 04 · LIMITWrite the boundaryRecord what the source says, what it implies, and what remains uninspected.

Starting point · Source directory

I found an official page, but not the file.

First test: Does the destination actually expose an individual document, with its own date, identifier and physical or digital location? A collection landing page is a route to evidence, not automatically the evidence itself.

Recollection · Missing meeting record

A memoir names a meeting, but the official editors found no minutes.

First test: Keep the recollection, the editors’ statement about their search, and separately dated operational records in different columns. No located minutes does not prove no meeting occurred.

Read the disputed-meeting case
Reported decision · Authorization

A memorandum says someone gave approval.

First test: Does the document record an order being issued, or a participant’s statement that approval existed? The difference belongs in the sentence you eventually publish.

Read the reported-approval case
Edition gap · Declassification

A newer official volume contains what an older one left out.

First test: Separate editorial selection from declassification withholding. An omission proves that an edition lacks material; it does not, by itself, settle why the record is missing.

Inspect the official-volume gap
Corroboration · Source family

Five links make a claim look better supported than it is.

First test: Draw each link back to its underlying creator, document and event. Two printings of one telephone memorandum are still one witness. A citation count is not a witness count.

Count witnesses, not links

Free · Printable research tool

The five-line Evidence Slip

Use one slip for each distinct item, not each website that mentions it. Write “uninspected” where you have only a citation or edited transcription. Never fill a gap with a plausible reconstruction.

1 · Repository and stable item IDCollection, series, folder, record number or permalink
2 · Originating witnessCreator, original date, format, intended audience
3 · Version examinedOriginal image, transcript, edited selection, translation or summary
4 · Independence checkWhat earlier document, if any, does this item reproduce?
5 · Exact support and unresolved limitOne sentence the item supports; one thing it cannot establish
Research receiptAccessed on: __________ · Item reuse checked? __________

Three possible outcomes: inspected (you saw the actual item or an identified reproduction), described (you saw only a finding aid or editorial account), and unresolved (the item or its relationship to the claim is still uncertain). This is a working distinction for this sheet, not an official archival classification.

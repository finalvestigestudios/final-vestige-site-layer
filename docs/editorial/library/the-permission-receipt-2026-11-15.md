## Institutional source and rights ledger — checked Nov 15, 2026
1. [NARA Still Pictures Copyright and Permissions](https://www.archives.gov/research/still-pictures/permissions) — direct retrieved. Archive custody does not make all donated/special media public domain.
2. [NARA Publishing National Archives Photos](https://www.archives.gov/research/still-pictures/publish-photos) — direct retrieved. Record identifiers and credit, item/series restrictions, user responsibility.
3. [NASA Image and Media Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) — direct retrieved. Factual media use, attribution, third-party works, people/marks and endorsement boundaries.
4. [NASA Advertising Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/advertising-guidelines/) — direct retrieved. Promotions, NASA names/marks and astronaut likeness.
5. [NASA: Apollo 1 Final Preparations](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/55-years-ago-final-preparations-for-the-planned-launch-of-apollo-1/) — retrieved. January 17, 1967 media event. This history article is not an individual image's clearance.
6. [JPL / Caltech Image Use Policy](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/) — direct retrieved. Required credit and site-specific limitations, including third-party ownership.
7. [Copyright Office, Title 17 Chapter 1](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html) — direct retrieved; general statutory framework.
8. FV internal method: [Editorial Standards](https://finalvestige.com/editorial-standards/), [Archive Trail](https://finalvestige.com/the-archive-trail/), [Library](https://finalvestige.com/free-resources/) — public pages checked.

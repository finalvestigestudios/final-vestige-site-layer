## The reader problem
An official site grants *access to inspect* a document. That is not a universal license to redistribute its figures, maps, photographs, scans, excerpts, music, logos, or privately contributed evidence. A working rights desk must distinguish (1) the URL of a collection, (2) the legal owner/creator of an individual item, and (3) the actual proposed reproduction. It must also preserve any privacy, donation, cultural sensitivity, trademark, and use-terms exceptions.

This release candidate supplies an **offline-first, no-network, browser-searchable 90-source directory**, an original accessible SVG decision map, a local-only **Reuse Witness** worksheet with JSON export, and a 90-row CSV of institutional policy leads. **The policy pointers are not item-level rights determinations.** Every asset remains NOT CLEARED until a documented individual review.

## Policy lead categories / last reviewed Nov 11, 2026
- **NARA: [NARA holdings: creator, donor and special-media restrictions must be checked](https://www.archives.gov/research/still-pictures/permissions)** — Federal records in an archive can contain copyrighted and donated materials. Investigate the particular record and any privacy or donor restrictions.
- **LOC: [LOC: item Rights and Access statement controls](https://www.loc.gov/legal/security-copyright-and-privacy/understanding-copyright/)** — Check the actual item Rights and Access or Rights Advisory. A collection-level label is not an item license.
- **NASA: [NASA: media generally usable with restrictions](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/)** — Check who created the media, licensed soundtrack/footage, identity and endorsement limits. NASA logos/seal are protected.
- **USGS: [USGS: government-created works generally public domain](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits)** — Check photo/figure credits and contributor exceptions. Credit USGS and do not copy its protected identifier.
- **NOAA: [NOAA: confirm author and credit before reuse](https://www.noaa.gov/office-education/outreach-communication/faq)** — Verify NOAA created the particular work; third-party materials are excluded. Clearly label edits and avoid implied endorsement.
- **NTSB: [NTSB: NTSB-created media versus third-party evidence](https://www.ntsb.gov/about/Pages/Website-Policies.aspx)** — An NTSB report can contain photos made by other parties. Board logo and seal require separate permission.
- **NPS: [NPS: government work generally public domain with exceptions](https://home.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm)** — Check creator and third-party credits. NPS arrowhead mark is protected; donor/privacy rights may apply.
- **GPO: [GovInfo: official publication can include copyrighted inserts](https://www.govinfo.gov/about)** — Official publication does not make incorporated third-party images, figures or text automatically reusable.
- **SI: [Smithsonian: CC0 item badge is decisive](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/faq)** — Only CC0-designated assets receive that dedication. Other assets have usage conditions; trademarks and personal rights are separate.
- **RUMSEY: [David Rumsey: image permission with underlying map checks](https://www.davidrumsey.com/about/copyright-and-permissions)** — The site's general image permission does not override source copyright, especially post-1929 works; standalone facsimile sales need contact.
- **BHL: [BHL: record-level copyright status and license](https://about.biodiversitylibrary.org/help/copyright-and-reuse/)** — Public-domain volumes may be reusable; in-copyright volumes can be CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Check volume and country/use.
- **DPLA: [DPLA: aggregator; use originating institution's rights notice](https://dp.la)** — Metadata/links do not transfer copyright or permission in source images; open the contributing institution's object page.
- **UNT: [UNT CyberCemetery: check originating agency and capture provenance](https://govinfo.library.unt.edu)** — A preserved website is not automatically a government-authored public-domain compilation. Inspect each captured component.
- **COURT: [CourtListener/RECAP: docket access is not blanket reuse permission](https://www.courtlistener.com)** — A public filing may contain third-party copyrighted exhibits or protected personal information. Check the actual filing.
- **FED: [U.S. federal work: verify the work's creator and exceptions](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title17/html/USCODE-2024-title17-chap1-sec105.htm)** — Section 105 concerns works made as part of federal official duties; it does not clear contractor material, third-party attachments, logos, privacy or non-U.S. rights.

Specific verified qualifications: NARA explicitly cautions against treating all federal holdings as public domain; Library of Congress calls for item-level Rights and Access checks; USGS distinguishes agency-created content from licensed figures; NASA prohibits implied endorsements and governs protected identifiers; NOAA says third-party photos require owner permission; NTSB distinguishes NTSB-made images from third-party exhibits; NPS shields its arrowhead mark; Smithsonian CC0 requires the actual marked item; GovInfo warns about copyright embedded in public government documents; Rumsey warns about underlying map copyright and stand-alone facsimile permissions; BHL divides public domain material from in-copyright CC BY-NC-SA materials. For DPLA, UNT, and CourtListener, no blanket license is inferred: obtain rights context from the contributing or originating record. For other federal domains, 17 U.S.C. §105 is a statute **not a license covering all third-party contributions**.

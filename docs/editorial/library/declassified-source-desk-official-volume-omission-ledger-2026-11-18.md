## Critical qualifications
The 10/38/82 figures refer only to the 2018 edition's declassification review, not all CIA files or all Iran history. Lack of a document in an edited volume is not proof it never existed. Do not count one record printed in two FRUS editions as independent corroboration. The physical record, unreleased text, and all 1989 documents were not inspected. Interpretations are editorial, not firsthand field experience.

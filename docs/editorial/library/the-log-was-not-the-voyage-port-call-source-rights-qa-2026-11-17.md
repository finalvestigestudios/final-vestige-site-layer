## Primary institutional evidence (all guide URLs reopened Nov 17, 2026)

1. **NARA Kansas City, RG 36 Series Title List** — https://www.archives.gov/kansas-city/finding-aids/html/rg36-series-title-list.html. Source of real Superior Wisconsin named Customs Service series and date ranges. NARA identifiers: 4719508 (foreign trade entrance/clearance register, 1937–1941); 4719522 (coastwise entrance/clearance register, 1937–1941); 4719511 (foreign trade entered/arrived under permit, 1940–1962); 4719516 (foreign trade cleared/granted permit, 1940–1962). Page says last reviewed 2021-03-29. **Important:** series-level descriptions only; neither specific original vessel rows nor scan coverage inspected.
2. **NARA Guide to Federal Records, RG 36** — https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/036.html. Authority for customs records, collection districts, entrances/clearances and manifests; note historic location descriptions may require present-custody check.
3. **NARA San Francisco Holdings RG 30–49** — https://www.archives.gov/san-francisco/finding-aids/holdings-guide-02.html. Historical administrative explanation of customs surveyor arrival/clearance recordkeeping and regional variations; not a direct record for Superior.
4. **NARA An Introduction to Navy Deck Logs** — https://www.archives.gov/research/military/logbooks/naval-deck-logs. Describes commissioned-vessel deck logs, retained classes and chronological entries; not merchant customs evidence.
5. **NARA U.S. Merchant Vessel Logbooks** — https://www.archives.gov/research/military/logbooks/merchant-vessels-old.html. Distinguishes official logbooks from navigation/operating records; some port and date arrangements.
6. **NARA Merchant Vessel Documentation** — https://www.archives.gov/research/maritime/merchant-vessel-documentation. Documents registry, enrollment, official numbers and requesting steps. Not a proof of particular voyage.
7. **NARA Boston Maritime Records** — https://www.archives.gov/boston/finding-aids/maritime.html. Additional regional record-group custodian example; page says last reviewed 2020-09-10.
8. **NARA Copyright and Permissions** — https://www.archives.gov/research/still-pictures/permissions. US government works and third-party rights caveat; page says last reviewed 2022-06-17.
9. **NARA Catalog sample** — https://catalog.archives.gov/id/4719508. URL responds but interactive content is JavaScript-rendered; underlying object details and item-level scans were NOT verified. The guide explicitly says so.

A searchable pocket directory of the ninety source routes in Final Vestige's Library. Every link preserves its original institution. Every status says what was actually checked—and what was not.

The doorway is not the document.

Some portals lead directly to records. Others provide catalog descriptions or search forms. A restricted automated request is a limitation of the check, not proof that a public record has disappeared. These ninety entries preserve the original destination links and the outcome of a November 16, 2026 destination-level verification pass. Open an individual record, inspect the actual pages, then make the claim. Do not reverse that order.

What this file is: a portable, offline-browseable index of links and research cautions. It does not include archival scans, guarantee any destination works today, certify underlying files, or grant reuse rights. Links require an internet connection. The authoritative live directory may be updated after this snapshot.

Evidence basis: Final Vestige's original 90-card WordPress directory snapshot, taken November 16, 2026. The full provenance and institutional verification ledger is maintained in the site's research workflow. Consult the live Research Sources page before relying on a catalog entry; read the editorial standards for the treatment of disputed material and image permissions.

Destination-level access audit: 64 institutional page bodies retrieved, 18 automated access restrictions, and 8 application-shell or other unresolved retrieval results. These are not counts of usable underlying documents. All checks were performed on November 16, 2026, and are not being represented as rechecks on November 17.

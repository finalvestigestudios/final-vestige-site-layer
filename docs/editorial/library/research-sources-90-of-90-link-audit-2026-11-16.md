## Seven findings that change reader behavior

1. **The route is not the download.** [FBI's OKBOMB](https://vault.fbi.gov/OKBOMB) identifies an available Part 01, while [Amerithrax](https://vault.fbi.gov/Amerithrax) lists many parts. Neither landing page alone proves an entire case file has been retrieved.
2. **A catalog index is not a live dataset.** [NIOSH FACE](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/face/topics/index.html) routes reports dated 2015 or earlier to CDC Stacks. [NOAA NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/event-footprint-catalog) warns of migration-related delays and November 17 maintenance. A recent-event question needs dataset latency and coverage documented.
3. **Version the navigation source.** [FAA AIM](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/) displayed an effective date of **July 9, 2026**, Change 3. A current manual can mislead when reconstructing past procedures; a historical manual cannot replace current safety notices.
4. **The map can exclude the storm that did damage.** NOAA's [Historical Hurricane Tracks help](https://www.coast.noaa.gov/hurricanes/index.html) explains that a spatial search radius may miss a storm whose impacts reached the place even when the track did not. A track map is not a local damage map.
5. **CC0 belongs to the asset, not the museum.** The [Smithsonian Open Access FAQ](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/faq) permits broad reuse for individually **CC0-marked** media while reserving other copyright, privacy, cultural and trademark considerations. The exact landing URL's inconsistent reader responses are a manual-check task, not permission to rehost all media.
6. **Historical map hosting is not one blanket license.** The [David Rumsey Map Collection permission statement](https://www.davidrumsey.com/about/copyright-and-permissions) allows many uses but carves out later copyrighted maps, non-downloadable items and stand-alone facsimile sales. A source-page card must not promise unrestricted copying.
7. **An interview record is not necessarily an online interview.** The [Library of Congress Veterans History Project guidance](https://www.loc.gov/collections/veterans-history-project-collection/about-this-collection/using-the-collection/) distinguishes descriptions from digitized materials; rights and copying restrictions can remain even when an interview is discoverable. The live LOC landing was blocked to this reader; the cited institutional guidance was found via its search index.

## Rights, visual integrity and inspection conditions

**No outside photographs, maps, manuscript scans, archival audio or videos were copied or published.** The only graphic is an original, labelled data diagram generated for this inventory. All other source uses are links with item-specific conditions; particular media still require their author/institution, title, date, original item URL, rights statement, caption and meaningful alternative text before publication. Metadata alone does not clear reuse rights. Do not treat a government website's design, seals or third-party exhibits as automatically public domain.

The 90-row JSON is an *authentic page-card inventory*, but the public WordPress page still shows some **60** labels, two incorrect category totals and a dated NARA digitization claim.

## Research and source chain
The existing resource *The Oath Was Not the Arrival* (draft #2839) was advanced rather than duplicated. It now includes a real, source-led case study distinguishing three different evidence classes: (1) a December 30, 1942 certificate of arrival at Niagara Falls, which does NOT show first-ever arrival or citizenship; (2) Maria von Trapp's January 21, 1944 declaration of intention in a District of Vermont federal-court record, which is NOT naturalization; (3) NARA historian Joan Gearin's report of 1948 naturalizations among the 1944 applicants. The original 1948 individual petition and judgment were not personally examined in this run; these are a historical account's statements, not an independent document inspection.

Sources checked Nov 16: NARA Boston [Maria von Trapp Naturalization Record](https://www.archives.gov/boston/highlights/von-trapp-naturalization); Joan Gearin, NARA *Prologue* winter 2005, [The Real Story of the Von Trapp Family](https://www.archives.gov/publications/prologue/2005/winter/von-trapps-html); [NARA naturalization guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/immigration/naturalization); [NARA declaration history](https://www.archives.gov/research/immigration/naturalization/history-dec-of-intent).

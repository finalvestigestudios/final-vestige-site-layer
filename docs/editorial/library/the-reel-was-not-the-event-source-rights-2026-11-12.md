## Reader need
The film a viewer watches is not necessarily the camera original, its scene order is not necessarily an event chronology, and a download button does not provide reproduction permission. The guide teaches a seven-pass provenance method and provides a locally exported Reel Witness worksheet.

## Evidence qualifications
- NARA's “34 reels” is a holding-level description; the source page does **not** establish correspondence between an individual edited *Memphis Belle* shot and any one source reel. This remains an archival research question.
- The NFSA *Pyjama Girl* reconstructed sequence is an archive-described reenactment; the *Great Strike* restoration from surviving fragments is not described as that type of reenactment.
- LOC's film-speed case study concerns the specific work *Empire*. It is not evidence that other older films should be adjusted to 16 fps.
- External links were found/read using institutional web search on November 12, 2026, not batch downloaded nor legally rights-cleared. Some institutions require onsite viewing or additional permission.

## Newly inspected official evidence
1. [Louisiana Legislative Auditor FY2004 Orleans Levee District financial statements](https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/86256ea9004c005986256f1200769546/%24file/0000003f.pdf). **Web PDF original screenshot indices 60, 62, 63 visually examined** November 13. Printed p.60 item 14 labels **$246,877 Bohemia Spillway Escrow** reserved within General Fund at June 30, 2004. Printed pp.62–63 distinguish 164 of 220 historical certified-tract interests (not confirmed completed claimant files); district *reports* the February 26, 2004 **$600,000 court-registry payment** in the *Haspel & Davis* class action, **$314,491.59 paid** in the Taylor succession case, and calls another **$166,842.53** disputed interest/attorneys' fees. Page 63 calls eight of 40 pending suits Bohemia-related at that reporting date. No underlying docket allocation, bank clearing, paid-claimant roster or deeds examined.
2. [FY2005 Orleans Levee District financial statements](https://app1.lla.la.gov/PublicReports.nsf/88E334F6AE3AE3E5862570A60069BA83/%24FILE/0000104F.pdf), pp.61 and 64. **$456,190** Bohemia-labelled General Fund reserve June 30, 2005, the 2004 registry/Taylor amounts repeated. FY2005 note calls **$166,842.53** interest and attorneys' fees and still references Fourth Circuit appeal. This does not demonstrate a second $314,491.59 payment in fiscal 2005.
3. [FY2006 Orleans Levee District statements](https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/f190284f7da564b88625725e0078912f/%24file/000008c0.pdf), p.49: **$339,407** labeled Bohemia reserve; **$1,684,715** unrelated debt service reserved separately. Differences: FY2004–FY2005 **+$209,313**, FY2005–FY2006 **−$116,783**, both reserve arithmetic, **not proven transaction volume or claimant payouts**.
4. [*Frank v. Board of Levee Commissioners*, La. Fourth Circuit, April 6, 2005](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/fourth-circuit-court-of-appeal/2005/2004-ca-0358.html). Recounts 2003 **stipulated royalty principal $166,842.53**, affirms interest provisions, reverses attorney-fee award. The FY2004 account note's description already conflicted with this principal classification before the FY2005 note restated it; this strengthens a **carry-forward hypothesis**, **not proof** of why management wrote it that way. [FY2007 audited statements](https://app.lla.la.gov/) use broader **Legal Settlements** designation ($292,522), excluded from a Bohemia-only plot.

## What changed
Continued the existing focused reader *The Spillway That Kept Its Names*, rather than starting another topic. Its printed page 2 (zero-based PDF page 1) records that **no Bohemia royalties were budgeted** by the flood authority because the royalties were then being routed to Lakefront Management Authority (LMA). The original government PDF **page image** was inspected, not just a search snippet.

**Source #31:** [FPA-East Finance Committee minutes, February 22, 2024](https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/2024-02-22-SLFPA-E-Finance-Committee-Minutes.pdf), printed p. 2. Relevant textual evidence in the minutes: “No Bohemia royalties were budgeted since the royalties are now being sent to the Lakefront Management Authority (LMA).” This is a budget-preparation report of the agency's understanding; **it does not independently prove cleared money, recipient accounts, the actual amount of incoming royalties, or the full executed contract terms**. The draft FY2025 budget cannot be presented as a signed agreement or a certified FY2025 cash ledger.

| FPA-East Finance Committee minutes, Feb. 22, 2024 [31] | Draft fiscal 2025 operating budget discussion | Forecast omission, not a measured $0 payment | FPA-East budget preparation | Staff said the planned Bohemia receipts were now directed to LMA |

| FPA-East audited FY2024 governmental-fund statement [29] | Year ended June 30, 2024 | $85,486 **reported** | OLD General Fund + LMA OLD Real Estate, same broad category | Not Bohemia-specific; overlaps discussed transfer period |
| FPA-East audited FY2025 governmental-fund statement [30] | Year ended June 30, 2025 | $169,656 **reported** | OLD General Fund + LMA OLD Real Estate, broad category | Not Bohemia-specific or matched to LMA 2025–26 projections |

**Non-inference:** the February 2024 budget omission is not proof that FPA received no Bohemia money in FY2025. Nor does subtracting the $100,000 LMA estimate from the FY2025 audited $169,656 reveal underpayment: the entities, fiscal years, and account definitions differ. Distinguish budget planning from accrual or modified-accrual annual reporting and separately from bank-cleared royalty cash.

## New source-critical warning: a quotation attributed to 1922 cannot witness an event in 1927

Davis (1994), printed p. 140, writes that the 1927 dynamited crevasse reopened an old Mississippi discharge line, following with a quotation attributed to **“Trowbridge 1922:13.”** Davis's bibliography identifies this as **A. C. Trowbridge, _Preliminary Geological Report on the Mississippi Delta_, unpublished, prepared for the Mississippi River Commission, 1922, no pagination** (printed p. 150). A 1922 source obviously **cannot have directly observed the 1927 demolition**. The quote may describe an earlier channel or terrain which Davis applies retrospectively, but the Trowbridge manuscript has **not been inspected**, so that interpretation is provisional. In particular **do not quote Trowbridge as a 1927 eyewitness or claim the 1922 geological report described the 1927 breach.**

This issue does **not** invalidate Davis's numerical summary on its own. It is a reason to treat geographic inference and the direct historical flow figure as **distinct evidentiary propositions**. The current WordPress draft was inspected for “Trowbridge” and “ancient lines”: neither expression appears, so **no article correction is currently needed**. Its current flow discussion carefully treats the Davis number as a later reported estimate with upstream primary reports not independently reviewed.

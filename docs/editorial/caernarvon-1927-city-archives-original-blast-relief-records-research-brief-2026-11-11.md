**Final Vestige Studios / Investigations | 2026-11-11, 15:30 Pacific | NOT publication-ready evidence**

## Discovery, carefully bounded

The **New Orleans City Archives & Special Collections** has an archival finding aid for **Department of Public Property records (CA-KD)**, dated **1912–1973**, with most surviving files concentrated in **1925–1929**. The official catalog description states that these records include several folders concerning the **Board of Commissioners of the Orleans Levee District**, as the commissioner sat on the board ex officio. More importantly, it specifically identifies **documentation of the 1927 Caernarvon Crevasse, photographs of the explosion used to create the opening, and relief work for its victims**. That last phrase is the archive's collection-level description, **not a statement that we have seen, identified or licensed a particular photo or record**.

## Why this matters

The existing article uses NOAA/Library of Congress imagery and published legal and engineering sources. A contemporaneous local government file may document **who arranged the demolition and relief**, with the potential to corroborate or correct details supplied by retrospective histories. It may also expose names of officials, dates, estimated damage, and relief administration. **None of those particulars has yet been verified from the files**, so do not state them as findings. This discovery is an **archive-retrieval lead**, not new substantive evidence about the levee cut's actual hydraulic effect or how much was ultimately paid.

## Corroborating judicial evidence, distinct from archived materials

- **1929:** [*Foret v. Board of Levee Commissioners*, 169 La. 427, 125 So. 437](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1929/169-la-427.html). The court describes the intentional opening and rejects a worker's **$2,625 lost-salary** claim. The amount is his asserted claim, **not an audited cash loss**.
- **1930:** [*Fabre v. Board of Commissioners*, 170 La. 210, 127 So. 603](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1930/170-la-210.html). The court says a Delacroix Island partnership **was allowed property-loss compensation** but not its **$3,441.62 anticipated-profit claim**. Being *allowed* compensation does **not** prove the amount or date paid.
- **1933:** [*Louisiana Southern Railway Co. v. Board of Levee Commissioners*, 176 La. 687, 146 So. 470](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1933/176-la-687.html). Originally asserted **$489,752.34** ($140,044.34 physical/property + $349,709 lost revenues/profits); the property claim was **amicably settled for an amount not specified in the opinion**, all but **$28,309** of the earnings demands abandoned, and the residual profit appeal rejected. **Never depict $489,752.34 as what was paid or judged valid**.
- **1937:** [*Tonry*, 186 La. 159](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1937/186-la-159.html) confirms the special tax had been collected and **$3,387,000** reparation bonds were being refinanced. Bond principal is **not** a household-payment total.
- **1955:** [*Dantoni*, 227 La. 575, 80 So. 2d 81](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1955/227-la-575-0.html) reports adoption of a **5½-mill** resolution, with **½ mill earmarked for Caernarvon claims** and the authority for this component conceded. This **does not establish collection of 1955 tax receipts** or payment to any individual.

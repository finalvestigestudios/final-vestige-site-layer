## Discovery and verified chain
| Event | Source-supported proposition | What it **does not** prove |
|---|---|---|
| 1984, Act 233 §3 | The Orleans Levee Board was required to submit an accounting of revenues from affected property to Louisiana DNR, and make that information available to qualifying applicants | That the requested figures were produced, complete, accessible to a given applicant, or reconciled to actual payment instruments |
| Sept. 4, 1996, *Riley v. Riley*, 680 So. 2d 169 | The Fourth Circuit interpreted §3 as creating a ministerial accounting duty and applicant access; 1985 and 1992 amendments had not changed §3; the court reversed an improper **partial no-cause-of-action dismissal** and remanded | A final judgment verifying title for every plaintiff, a mandatory specific payment amount, an executed order proving records were delivered, or final case disposition |
| June 2009, SB350 conference proposal | Legislature debated broader, independent review of original property records, revenues, lawsuits, claims, expenses, judgments and actual recipient payments; conference report never enacted | That no other audit existed, or that the earlier 1984 accounting duty failed |

Source checks: [Louisiana Supreme Court's 1986 published reproduction of the original six-section Act 233, esp. footnote 4, §3](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1986/85-ca-1448-4.html) (a judicial transcription, not an original enrolled Act scan); [*Riley* full reported appellate text](https://openjurist.org/680/so2d/169/riley-v-riley), checked against [vLex reporter transcription](https://case-law.vlex.com/vid/94-2226-app-4-895091630); and [Louisiana SB350 legislative record](https://www.legis.la.gov/Legis/BillInfo.aspx?i=213709), [June 25, 2009 conference report](https://www.legis.la.gov/Legis/ViewDocument.aspx?d=666737), [2009 enrolled-acts index](https://legis.la.gov/archive/09rs/09rssummary.pdf).

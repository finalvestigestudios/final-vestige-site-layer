## Material new finding and correction
The prior framing identified Louisiana Act 233's 1984 statutory accounting duty and the 1996 *Riley* litigation, but treated the original accounting as wholly unlocated. **A published June 9, 1999 Louisiana Fourth Circuit opinion explicitly confirms that a property-specific revenue accounting WAS PRODUCED in a separate case, *Vogt*.** The board calculated **$2,853,358.44** in receipts from the subject property **since June 29, 1984**, and the plaintiffs accepted the accounting. The opinion identifies **May 5, 1997** order to produce it; the accounting as an actual court exhibit; **July 30, 1997** correspondence; and a DNR claimant-certification list covering **Tracts 71–99**. This is new concrete evidence of an existing accounting, not proof of a comprehensive DNR-wide Section 3 account. [Contemporaneous judicial opinion transcription](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/la-court-of-appeal/1323990.html), No. 98-CA-2379, 738 So. 2d 1142 (La. App. 4 Cir. June 9, 1999).

**Judgment distinctions:** A January 30, 1998 initial judgment identified the aggregate $2,853,358.44. The revised judgment **signed July 24, 1998** identified plaintiffs and amounts due to each; the trial judge's reasoning protected absent / unrepresented owners' shares rather than granting participating plaintiffs 100% of those revenues. The 1999 appellate court affirmed, with interest from judicial demand and no attorneys' fees on the litigated theory. The **June 14, 2002** Fifth Circuit *Vogt* ruling records **continued collection and enforcement litigation**; its reversal of a federal pleading/jurisdictional dismissal **does not prove final federal damages or eventual claimant payment**. [Federal appellate opinion](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-5th-circuit/1375733.html).

**Do NOT allege** (a) the 1984 accounting was never produced; (b) the $2,853,358.44 is the proven amount owed to every Bohemia owner, or the sum subsequently paid; (c) production in *Vogt* proves broader Section 3 delivery to DNR or access by all applicants; (d) fractional-share allocations are fully known without the signed July 24, 1998 judgment and its exhibits; (e) the fact of a judgment proves collection.

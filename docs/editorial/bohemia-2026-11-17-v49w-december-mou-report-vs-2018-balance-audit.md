## What was found and verified

The Lakefront Management Authority's **December 14, 2023 board minutes**, in executive director Louis Capo's year-end accomplishments report (printed p. 7 / original PDF screenshot page index **6**), state that the authority renegotiated its MOU, obtained release of a previously owed **2006–2007** balance, and would for the first time receive Bohemia oil-and-gas lease income. They also say the duty to mow Lakeshore Drive was reassigned to the flood-protection authority.

**Source #77 (official authority PDF):** https://nolalakefront.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/DEC-14-2023-Board-Meeting-Minutes__SCORED-AND-APPROVED-1.25.2024.pdf . The official text was read; the **original page image was successfully fetched and visually inspected** on November 17, 2026 (PDF page 6 zero-based). The statement is an attributed executive report, not the executed instrument or a board vote establishing each term.

Prior source #22: Flood Protection Authority-East **June 15, 2023** board minutes recount Capo's request for forgiveness of a **reported $800,000 balance**, with **$500,000 paid out of a $1.3 million 2018 arrangement**, and proposed dedication of future Bohemia royalties. Source #76: **July 27, 2023** LMA minutes document discussion, including concern about describing funds as 'dedicated'. Source #24: **July 25, 2024** LMA committee counsel describes the **November 2023** MOU and **January 1, 2024** management effective date.

**Original comparative analysis:** The June balance is framed as a remainder of a **2018** repayment arrangement; the December report describes a **2006–2007** obligation and gives **no dollar amount**. These may refer to the same older debt rescheduled in 2018, or separate obligations. The sources do not connect the accounting identities. Do **not** assert that the June $800,000 was conclusively forgiven, that settlement money for private heirs was diverted, or that royalties were actually received in December.

## Reporting advanced

Rewrote the existing section **“An April 1925 list”** into a **690-word** sourced historical-procedure analysis (rather than starting another topic or adding a redundant chapter). It follows the seemingly small but legally consequential postmarked-envelope requirement from the original administrative rules. Other documented requirements: ordered newspaper notices; public inspection of original-owner lists at Buras and Belle Chasse libraries and other parish offices; a form requested from DNR to be dispatched within ten days; a historical 180-day application period following the last notice; preliminary screening against the original-owner roster in the 1925 committee report or other acquisition records; 70 days to amend a rejected claim; historical $175 advance administrative costs; a special-master hardship review involving three affidavits; and certification as **distinct from** final deed transfer and mineral revenue payment.

The **December 2002 compilation** incorporated changes to rules initially promulgated in January 1985, including subsequent amendments; **do not claim every detail existed in the original January 1985 rule**. This report documents the law as historically described, **not live filing instructions**. Neither the legal notice publication itself, an individual claimant's correspondence, a certified applicant roster, parcel title, nor any claimant payment was checked; no individual impact is claimed.

Do not turn the 2002 filing window into an invitation to apply today.

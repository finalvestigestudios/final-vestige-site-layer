## Material primary-source discovery: $166,842.53 labeled two different ways
1. **Original appellate opinion**: Louisiana Court of Appeal, Fourth Circuit, *Michael C. Frank, Administrator of the Succession of Henry Taylor Jr. v. Board of Levee Commissioners*, **No. 2004-CA-0358**, **April 6, 2005**, [full court document hosted by Justia](https://cases.justia.com/louisiana/fourth-circuit-court-of-appeal/2005-2004-ca-0358.pdf?ts=1437512407), PDF zero-based pp. **3 and 9** visually inspected; [searchable transcription](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/fourth-circuit-court-of-appeal/2005/2004-ca-0358.html). The court says the district **stipulated to $166,842.53 in royalty-derived principal damages** in 2003, with the **October 14, 2003 trial judgment printing $166,842.50**. The April 2005 appellate result: **affirmed statutory/legal interest; reversed the attorneys' fee award**. Court source identifies the trial action as **94-7848**.
2. **Original audit scanned page**: Orleans Levee District, [FY2005 Basic Financial Statements](https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/88e334f6ae3ae3e5862570a60069ba83/$file/0000104f.pdf), **Note 17, printed p.64/PDF zero-based p.72**, visually inspected. It reports **$314,491.59 paid** in the Chandler / Taylor succession matter and calls **$166,842.53** a remaining balance of **special interest and attorney fees** on appeal. It cites **docket 47-842**, not 94-7848.

The exact **$166,842.53 appears as stipulated principal in the judicial record but as contested interest and fees in the district's account**. The 3-cent variation between stipulated and trial-judgment principal is also visible in the opinion. These facts demand **source-level caution**: the present record cannot explain the discrepancy, confirm that two amounts with the same value were separately owed, prove a mistaken/corrupt audit classification or reconcile the different docket IDs. **Do not add the $166,842.53 to a new liability or describe it unqualified as unpaid attorneys' fees.** Do not assume all Taylor succession beneficiaries received the district's claimed $314,491.59 without actual cleared records. An intermediate appeal/writ and the original trial dockets could affect the June 2005 audit's case-status description.

**Secondary-source discrepancy left out of manuscript:** FindLaw's HTML transcription includes a footnote claiming principal and undisputed interest were paid after judgment, a footnote not visible in the 10-page original PDF examined. Before quoting or independently relying on this footnote, obtain a certified publisher/court copy or the relevant trial satisfaction docket. Our publication candidate relies on the visually checked original opinion's principal and appellate disposition instead.

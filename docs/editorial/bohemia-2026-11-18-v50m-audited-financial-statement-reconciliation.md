FY2024 straddles the planned revenue-routing change, but the reduction in the combined line is **not attributed to that deal**. Causes including price/volume movement, accounting recoding, timing, leases and permits unrelated to Bohemia remain untested. Not evidence of any wrongdoer, failure to pay, lease award or private claimants' distributions. The relevant FY source material is financial statements whose audit is at financial-statement materiality, not individual royalty account reconciliations.

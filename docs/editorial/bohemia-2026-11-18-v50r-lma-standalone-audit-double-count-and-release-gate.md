## What is new in this pass

The Lakefront Management Authority (LMA) has a separate, filed and independently audited **FY2025 annual financial report**. That original source supplies a control for the *same* $106,377 under “Royalties, leases, and permits” appearing in the parent Flood Protection Authority–East (FPA-East) FY2025 consolidated report. It also supplies a **FY2025 budget-vs-actual schedule** showing **$0 planned and $106,377 actual**, a result in a *broad category*, **not** a Bohemia-specific mineral royalty schedule.

The two official reports do **not** evidence two $106,377 payments. The $106,377 is in LMA's OLD Real Estate Fund in **both** presentations. In FPA-East's combined p.16 statement, this amount combines with **$63,279** in OLD General Fund to equal **$169,656** for the reported category. In particular, $169,656 + $106,377 would double count the latter fund. The separate LMA report states that LMA is a component unit of FPA-East; the combined audit itself shows the LMA OLD Real Estate column. We have compared two public financial *presentations* of the same fund, not reconciled original bank deposits or individual invoices.

## Concrete reporting advance

The current Louisiana legislature text for **R.S. 30:21.2**, literally captioned “Bohemia Spillway Cost Recovery,” describes an *actual-cost assessment after final disposition of claims filed under Act 233 of 1984*, possible apportionment, and deposit of received money into the state treasury. It identifies **1987 Act 644** and **1992 Act 984** in its legislative note. This is the modern statutory context for the **different** DNR claimant-cost escrow identified in primary 2020/2021 monthly fiscal reports [50]. It does not substantiate escrow continuity, named claimant payment/refund entitlement, or any present-day balance.

Historical name timeline relevant to a records request: **DNR → Department of Energy and Natural Resources, January 10, 2024** (Act 150, official legislative digest) → **Department of Conservation and Energy, October 1, 2025** (Act 458, department official announcement). The current online statute calls the department by the 2025 name. No new restitution scheme or renewed obligation is inferred from an agency rename.

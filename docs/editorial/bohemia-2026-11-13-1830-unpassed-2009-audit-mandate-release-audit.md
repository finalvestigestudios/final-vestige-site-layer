## Discovery and verified limits

The [2009 Louisiana legislative tracker for SB350](https://www.legis.la.gov/Legis/BillInfo.aspx?i=213709), J. P. Morrell's larger non-flood-asset bill, shows the House's amended passage **June 22, 2009, 93–0**. On **June 25**, Senate reconsideration and conference proceedings ended with **Senate adoption 35–0** of Conference Committee Report #4069. The tracker ends at **Senate adopted Conference Committee Report**, not enrolled or approved. **SB350 is absent from the official [2009 enacted-acts index](https://legis.la.gov/archive/09rs/09rssummary.pdf)**, and [session calendar](https://www.legis.la.gov/Legis/SessionInfo/SessionInfo_09RS.aspx) records June 25 final adjournment. This does NOT establish any enacted audit mandate under SB350 or that no comparable investigation ever happened.

The Legislature's [June 25 report #4069](https://www.legis.la.gov/Legis/ViewDocument.aspx?d=666737), proposed subsection I(1)-(3), would have instructed **Louisiana Legislative Auditor** financial review and **Louisiana State Law Institute** ownership-history study, asking agencies for returned property lists, spillway revenue, claimant lists, judgments, expenses and amounts paid, with *proposed* February 1, 2010 findings. Official searchable PDF text directly examined. **Web screenshot attempts for the conference report and enactments PDF were unsuccessful**, so no claim of physical-page visual verification. The 2009 bill was not primarily a Bohemia bill; audit requirement arrived within a wider non-flood-property measure. No resulting joint study was located and **non-enactment cannot rule out separately authorized inquiries**.

The noteworthy analytical point for readers: legislators specified precisely the tract-to-successor and claimant-payment inventory absent from later institutional reserve and escrow summary documents. This is a research lead, not a proven accounting failure or evidence of nonpayment.

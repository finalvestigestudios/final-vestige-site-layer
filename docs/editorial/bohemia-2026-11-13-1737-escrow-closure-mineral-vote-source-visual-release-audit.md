## Primary documentary reporting advance
Publisher original: **Southeast Louisiana Flood Protection Authority–East**, minutes for **Thursday, March 19, 2015**, official PDF: https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/minutes/2015%2003%2019%20-%20Board%20Meeting%20Minutes.pdf

- Opening and venue verified in published indexed PDF text: St. Bernard Parish Council Chambers, Chalmette.
- **Resolution 03-19-15-12**, printed pp. 24–25: 2015 board recounts 1997 legislation to escrow disputed-property revenues, **July 15, 1998** original board resolution **1-071598** directing escrow bank and matching accounting reserve; recites that its associated claims had been settled; **eight yes, zero no** on authorization to close the escrow account, transfer its balance to an Orleans Levee District General Fund operating/investment account and eliminate the restricted reserve.
- Important omission: minutes **do not disclose closing balance**, actual dated executed bank transfer, original 1998 resolution, named plaintiff receipts or final parcel conveyances. Board's conclusion that related claims settled is an **institutional recital**, not proof every historical owner/eligible heir was paid.

The text does not establish that these nominations are the same disputed Bohemia privately-acquired properties; no spatial overlay or original exhibits inspected.

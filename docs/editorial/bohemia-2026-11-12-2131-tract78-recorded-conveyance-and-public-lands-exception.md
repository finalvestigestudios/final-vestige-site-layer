## Source 20 — why other property cannot automatically be returned
**Louisiana First Circuit, Sept. 10, 2009**, *Plaquemines Parish Government v. Louisiana Department of Natural Resources*, No. 2008 CA 2094. Court-opinion reproduction PDF: https://cases.justia.com/louisiana/first-circuit-court-of-appeal/2008ca2094-1.pdf?ts=1462355169 ; HTML: https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/first-circuit-court-of-appeal/2009/2008ca2094-1.html .

**Verification actually performed:** visited judicial-opinion PDF and **visually inspected relevant PDF pages** (indexes 1,5,6); also read searchable text. Claimant parish / Grand Prairie Levee District sought return of formerly publicly held land inside Bohemia. Court affirmed dismissal: intergovernmental legislative transfers were not covered by the 1984 statute's return mechanism for previously **expropriated or threat-purchased private property**. The opinion cannot establish which individual private successors eventually received restitution.

## Important correction, checked against court text
Louisiana Supreme Court, Richardson & Bass v. Board of Levee Commissioners, 231 La. 299, 91 So. 2d 353, full opinion with multiple rehearings reproduced at https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1956/231-la-299-0.html , read Nov. 13, 2026. This is reproduced judicial text, not an independently examined original registry disbursement.

- **Original opinion June 30, 1955:** Orleans received 1/8 (6/48), Delta 1/48, of disputed 55% production-proceeds share, referencing four wells.
- **First rehearing Feb. 20, 1956:** Orleans 8/48, Delta 1/48 of that disputed 55% share.
- **Second rehearing Nov. 5, 1956:** Orleans 9/48 (3/16), Delta 0 (claim dismissed). Majority treated 1942 Act 311 as confirming Orleans public-land title dating to 1925, rendering Delta's 1936 lease ineffective for this parcel. Dissenters disagreed, especially over whether the 1942 act was a new title transfer or confirmation.
- **Per curiam amendment Nov. 21, 1956:** corrected prior four-well count to **six wells: 4-H, 6-H, 7-H, 14-H, 44-H and 47-H**. Orleans awarded the entire registry fund attributed to its 3/16 of disputed 55% production-proceeds portion. Delta claim dismissed. An additional 45% share due to the state under another lease was not at issue.
- **Appeal dismissed Dec. 17, 1956:** not the date of original opinion.

**Critical boundary:** Court describes 1928 Act 246 as changing Grand Prairie jurisdiction and 1942 Act 311 as confirming public land title. These were public-district ownership and mineral-lease claims, *not* the private-land restitution entitlements of 1984. Actual 1928 and 1942 session laws, title originals and cash payout records remain uninspected.

## New research: the succession of Josephine and Henry Taylor, Jr.
- **Original appellate opinion transcription:** Louisiana Fourth Circuit, *Michael C. Frank, Administrator of the Succession of Henry Taylor, Jr. v. Board of Levee Commissioners*, No. 2004-CA-0358, **April 6, 2005**. https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/fourth-circuit-court-of-appeal/2005/2004-ca-0358.html
- **Independent transcription comparison:** FindLaw, same decision. https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/la-court-of-appeal/1296483.html
- **Court-reported party stipulation:** district conveyed relevant land to the succession **December 3, 1990**. This is a court account of an agreed fact, not the actual parish deed. The opinion generally describes other Bohemia conveyances in 1991–92. Do not imply all returns occurred in 1990.
- **Principal:** parties stipulated **$166,842.53**, while judicial account of October 14, 2003 trial judgment says **$166,842.50**. Both transcriptions preserve the discrepancy, and no filed trial judgment was examined. Narrative and artwork therefore round to **approximately $166,843** and explicitly retain the unresolved three-cent variance.
- **Interest and fees:** district-return-index interest for July 1991–July 1993, then legal interest from **July 2, 1993**. Appellate court **affirmed interest**, **reversed attorneys’ fees**, and rejected the request for additional 1984-to-demand interest. No unexamined total interest number or payment date inferred.
- **Critical legal comparison:** 2005 *Frank* is not the same claim as 2002 federal *Vogt* (the latter arose from refusal to satisfy an already existing judgment). Neither 2005 opinion nor its stipulation proves funds were actually received.
- **Editorial advance:** [Bohemia companion v2.3](https://github.com/finalvestigestudios/final-vestige-site-layer/blob/main/docs/editorial/bohemia-spillway-2026-11-12-land-roster-rights-claim-investigation-draft.md) rewrites the old short “The deeds did not return all at once” subsection into claimant-specific legal reporting; sources now **[1]–[43] consecutively** with full [43] ledger and remaining proof limitations.

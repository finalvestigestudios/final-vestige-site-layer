## 3. Newly identified archive-label reliability problem (do not misdate)
The [Lakefront Management Authority archive page headed **October 2026**](https://nolalakefront.com/2026-meeting-agenda-and-minutes/october-202/), as accessed November 17, links its **Board Meeting → Minutes** slot to [`Regular-Board-Minutes-OCT-23-2025.pdf`](https://nolalakefront.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Regular-Board-Minutes-OCT-23-2025.pdf). The PDF's **first original page was inspected as an image** and is headed **October 23, 2025**, not 2026. On that same October 2026 index, **Commercial Real Estate Committee → Minutes** points to [`CRE-Minutes-October-16-2025final.pdf`](https://nolalakefront.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CRE-Minutes-October-16-2025final.pdf), also a 2025 document.

**Editorial consequence:** **Do not cite either attached 2025 PDF as evidence of an October 2026 board vote, an audit completion, or a mineral-lease action.** A 2026 index label is insufficient to establish a document's meeting date; the source's first page and meeting metadata control. This is evidence of at least two mismatched links as inspected, **not** a claim that LMA withheld a 2026 record, falsified a meeting, or that any Bohemia action did or did not occur. If following the 2024 107-acre nomination, obtain the authenticated State Mineral and Energy Board tract/award docket and the **actual** 2026 LMA minutes from a verified source rather than infer status from mislinked archive indexes.

A particularly easy error to avoid: a 2025 PDF discussing an unspecified *audit* in its director's report does not independently establish a completed **Bohemia-specific mineral lease audit**. The record does not identify that scope. This archive-date fault strengthens the decision to hold any conclusive statement about recent lease audits.

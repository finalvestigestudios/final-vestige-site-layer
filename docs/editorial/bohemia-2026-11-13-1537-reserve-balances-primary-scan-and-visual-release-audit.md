## What was verified in primary audit page images

1. **Orleans Levee District FY2005 audited statements**, year ended **June 30, 2005**, Louisiana Legislative Auditor hosted PDF: https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/88e334f6ae3ae3e5862570a60069ba83/$file/0000104f.pdf . Actual PDF **page-index 63 / printed p. 55** (Note 11) says restricted General Fund assets include **$456,190 set aside for Bohemia settlement payments**. Actual PDF **index 69 / printed p. 61** (Note 14) records **“Bohemia Spillway Escrow” $456,190**, distinct from debt-service funds. This adds previously overlooked balance-sheet evidence to source [45]. Distinguish a year-end reserve from a bank disbursement, class claimant payment or court-registry deposit.
2. **Orleans Levee District FY2006 audited statements**, year ended **June 30, 2006**, same official host: https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/f190284f7da564b88625725e0078912f/$file/000008c0.pdf . Actual PDF **index 51 / printed p. 49** (Note 14) shows **“Bohemia Spillway Escrow” $339,407**, alongside **$1,684,715 reserved for debt service**, and the **$2,024,122 combined governmental fund restricted total**. Text of Note 11 also characterizes the $339,407 as funds set aside for Bohemia settlement payments in the General Fund. The precise Note 14 page was visually inspected. This extends source [46].
3. **Arithmetic:** $456,190 − $339,407 = **$116,783**, two year-end snapshots. **No use-of-funds reconciliation was obtained**, so this is **not** a verified payout, transfer, appropriation adjustment or proof of a particular escrow trust arrangement. The $2,024,122 total is not all earmarked for Bohemia. No addition of $600,000 deposited to the 2004 *Haspel* court registry or $314,491.59 reported Taylor payment is justified; those are separate disclosures with different purposes.

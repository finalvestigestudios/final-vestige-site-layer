## Primary-source breakthrough
The official [March 19, 2015 SLFPA-East board minutes](https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/minutes/2015%2003%2019%20-%20Board%20Meeting%20Minutes.pdf) were reopened and **the original page images actually inspected** using PDF screenshots: printed pp. **24–25**, PDF zero-based pages **23–24**. This **resolves the earlier graphic-page verification blocker for this source**.

Resolution **03-19-15-12**:
* Recites Louisiana **Act 1364 of 1997** as requiring disputed Bohemia-property revenues to be placed in escrow pending final court determination.
* Recites Orleans Levee District **Resolution 1-071598 of July 15, 1998**, directing an escrow bank account and an equivalent fund-balance reserve.
* States as the board's institutional premise that **“all claims relating to this matter have been settled.”**
* Authorizes closing the escrow, moving its remaining balance to an Orleans Levee District General Fund investment or operating account, ending its restricted-reserve presentation and accounting for these revenues as no longer disputed.
* Recorded vote **8 yeas, 0 nays, 1 absent**. The adjacent mineral lease nomination, Resolution 03-19-15-13, is a **different resolution**, passing **7 yeas, 1 nay, 1 absent**.

**Inference ceiling:** The 2015 minutes supply a *board recital and authorization*, **not** the original 1997 statutory enrollment or 1998 board resolution; not the executed fund transfer, bank balance, balance-release entry, claimant payment ledger, allocation schedules, or satisfaction of each beneficiary's claim. These three document classes must not be conflated. More recent 2023 board statements that settlements were paid provide institutional corroboration of a settlement narrative, not individual cleared-check proof.

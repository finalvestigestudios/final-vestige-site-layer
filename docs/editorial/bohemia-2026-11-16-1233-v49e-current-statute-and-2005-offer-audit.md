## Source #62: April 21, 2011 flood-authority minutes
Official original [SLFPA-East April 21, 2011 minutes](https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/minutes/2011%2004%2021%20-%20Board%20Meeting%20Minutes.pdf), **printed pages 2–3**. President Timothy Doody reported that the former Orleans Levee Board had offered an approximately $10 million settlement before Hurricane Katrina, to have been funded with a bond issuance contingent on voter approval in September 2005. He said that a later similar proposed payoff was rejected by representatives of the plaintiffs. The source records **Doody's public description in 2011** and does not verify an executed 2005 contract, the actual ballot/cancellation, the financing, any claimant's position independently, or any completed payment. The searchable government PDF text was directly examined on Nov 16. A source-PDF screenshot was attempted via web but failed `DisabledError`, so **original-page image inspection was not accomplished**. A corrected, dated legislative/board chronology appears in the v49e candidate. This evidence is consistent with the older 2010 board's reference to a settlement compromised by Katrina; we do not treat the amount as a new payment.

## Source #63: The Legislature's displayed consolidated statute
[Official state Legislature R.S. 38:330.12](https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/Law.aspx?d=366228), read November 16, 2026, especially **B(2), C and history line**. The Legislature's online text continues to display the conditional 80% authority / 20% *Haspel & Davis* class disposition of qualifying sale/transfer proceeds in B(2), with a 30-day allocation after transaction and a process requiring first offers to certain parish public entities. Source line at foot identifies Act 363 of 2011 among amendments without listing a later act to this section. **Subsection C, separately, speaks to ongoing revenues** from managed properties, less management costs, paid to the flood-protection authority for the levee district. We cannot make an undifferentiated statement that 20% of all airport, marina or other operating revenue went to the class. The official laws search advises that statutory updating is ongoing; no completeness guarantee for 2026 session amendments is made.

**Important legal and evidentiary non-inferences:** A statute is not a record of a sale, closing, offer to public entities, class-level remittance or ultimate claimant payment. The online codification is not independently verified against every 2026 enrolled act. This is editorial source interpretation, **not legal advice**.

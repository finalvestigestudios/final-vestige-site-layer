## Claim-level findings: original government meeting records actually inspected

**2024 governance resolution.** [Lakefront Management Authority Full Board Minutes, July 25, 2024](https://nolalakefront.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/JULY-25-2024-Board-Minutes_approved.pdf), screenshot PDF page indexes **13–15**; especially index **15**. The nine recorded yeas, no nays or abstentions, establish adoption of a resolution delegating **administration of future leasing on Orleans Levee District-owned Bohemia land** to Louisiana's State Mineral and Energy Board. The resolution recites a November 2023 memorandum shifting management responsibility for district **assets and interests**, NOT universal title to all original parcels. An application for about **107 acres** is listed as agenda item 6, but these full-board minutes provide **no corresponding recorded approval or executed lease**. The document contains inconsistent year values in some printed motion IDs and other items, although the meeting heading and adoption date agree on **July 25, 2024**; do not silently repeat those typo-like IDs as reliable identifiers.

**Committee corroboration.** [LMA Commercial Real Estate Committee Minutes, July 25, 2024](https://nolalakefront.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/25-JULY-2024-Commercial-Real-estate-Committee-Minutes_Approved-9.19.2024.pdf), screenshot page index **3**; attorney Jeff Dye recorded as stating November 2023 MOU placed Bohemia district-asset management with LMA effective **Jan. 1, 2024**. The committee voted **to recommend** the 107-acre application; a recommendation is not proof a lease was awarded, bid, signed or operated. The underlying November 2023 memorandum was NOT obtained.

**2025 financial report.** [LMA Full Board Minutes, Feb. 20, 2025](https://wwwcfprd.doa.louisiana.gov/boardsandcommissions/MeetingMinutes/547_Full%20Board%20Minutes%20-%20FEB%2020%202025completed.pdf), screenshot page index **4**. Staff reports **$47,000** Bohemia receipts for all of **2024**, another **$39,000** for an ambiguously defined first six months of “this year,” and a prospective **$100,000** Bohemia budget amount in a FY2025–26 discussion. Do not assume which exact six-month accounting window the middle figure covers, or call the budget projection realized revenue. These are recorded **management representations, not audited financial figures** and reveal neither specific landownership nor whether any claimant was owed/paid.

**Interpretation.** These documents add an important later record after the 1984 restitution law and 1986 court ruling: the district had continuing assets/interests and leasing administration and recorded some Bohemia receipts in 2024. They do NOT prove ongoing noncompliance, unpaid heirs, ownership of every originally acquired private tract, or that revenues from 1950–81 and 2024 are directly comparable.

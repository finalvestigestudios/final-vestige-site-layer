## November 10, 12:38 Pacific — original measurement versus inferred peak

- Source-checked USGS Costa and Jarrett (2008), Appendix A printed pp. 231–232: a current-meter measurement at the historical Chicot/Arkansas City reach reached **1,712,000 ft³/s on April 20, 1927**; Corps material cited by USGS says the **April 21 Mounds Landing crevasse** increased current enough to end boat observations. Underlying 1927 measurement sheets were unavailable to the USGS review team. The frequently cited **2,472,000 ft³/s May peak is a conditional estimate of levee-confined flow whose original computation could not be traced**, not a later direct discharge reading, nor a measure of Caernarvon flow.

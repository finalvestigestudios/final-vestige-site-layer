## Primary-source findings and bounds

**A. 2011 flood and roadway opening.** Southeast Louisiana Flood Protection Authority–East, [Coastal Advisory Committee minutes, August 5, 2011](https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/committee_minutes/2011%2008%2005%20Coastal%20Advisory%20Committee%20minutes.pdf), printed pp. 6–8 (original PDF page images for pp. 7–8 independently inspected). Attributed to John Lopez: roughly 80% of Bohemia Spillway overtopped; average depth ~15 inches; a **roadway** opening around 150 ft wide and 24 ft deep. Committee discussion included an estimated 2,000-ft low causeway, adjacent hypothetical freshwater-diversion project ~$20m–$30m; members only recommended asking the state to *consider* the causeway. This is attributed committee testimony, not validated project construction, completed engineering or field-survey metadata.

**B. 2012 bank breakthrough.** [NASA Earth Observatory, *Mardi Gras Pass*, published February 21, 2023](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/mardi-gras-pass-150986/), retrospective chronology: 2011 high water washed out a road and joined canals, with subsequent *February 2012* riverbank breach initially approximately 32 ft wide and 5 ft deep. **Never conflate the 2011 roadway gap with the 2012 riverbank opening.** The measurements refer to different opening types and phases; georeferenced original survey stations remain unavailable.

**C. An earlier historical account was commissioned.** Southeast Louisiana Flood Protection Authority–East [Legal Committee minutes, September 3, 2009](https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/committee_minutes/2009%2009%2003%20Legal%20Committee%20Minutes.pdf), searchable official text, printed pp. 1–2. Timothy Doody said a Bohemia historical account was being prepared; the committee sought Bohemia lawsuit-status and legal-cost reporting by case. PDF text read, but original-page visual screenshot was unavailable. **No claim that the history was delivered, that any commissioned audit had final findings, or that claimant funds were paid.** Next archive lead: request finished commissioned account and cumulative Bohemia legal-invoice extracts, subject to legitimate exemptions.

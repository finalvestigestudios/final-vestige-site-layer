## Findings verified from original official records

1. **January 30, 2025 — public expectation/receipt statement.** [Louisiana Department of Administration-hosted LMA Board minutes](https://wwwprd.doa.louisiana.gov/boardsAndCommissions/MeetingMinutes/547_Full%20Board%20Minutes%20-%20January%2030%2C%202025final.pdf), section IX, original **PDF page index 8 (ninth page)**, examined as page image on November 17, 2026. In Executive Director Louis Capo's report the Bohemia Spillway item states “Expected: $250,000” and “Received: $25,000.” It does **not** state the period, lease term, royalty rate, reconciliation, legal payee, contractual payment schedule or bank-confirmed amounts. The $225,000 arithmetic gap is an **expectation gap in official minutes**, not a verified overdue debt, fiscal-year variance or historical restitution owed to heirs. New bibliography source **#72**.
2. **March 26–27, 2025 — request to examine lease payments.** Previously verified [Finance Committee minutes](https://nolalakefront.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Finance-Minutes-March-26-2025final.pdf), printed p. 4, record Capo's concern that Bohemia mineral-lease payments were disappointing, his desire to audit the leases and his claim they had not been audited since 2007. The following day's [full-board minutes](https://nolalakefront.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/MARCH-27-2025-FULL-BOARD-Minutes__completed062025.pdf), pp. 4–5, record his intention to verify payments with the flood authority. Neither establishes an actual audit, the absence of earlier audits, contractual breach or identified lessee wrongdoing. Existing sources **#69–70**.
3. **FY2025–2026 budget — future forecast, not a reconciliation.** [LMA adopted operating budget](https://nolalakefront.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/25-26-Budget-website.pdf), first sheet, revenue line **444010**, allocates **$100,000 in projected Bohemia Royalties**. The January 2025 $250,000 expectation has no defined period; it would be unsound to describe the $100,000 budget as proof of a $150,000 downward revision for the same interval or to use the $25,000 statement as the full fiscal year's realized revenue. Existing source **#71**.
4. **General financial audit is not the requested mineral-lease audit.** [Louisiana Legislative Auditor archive, LMA annual financial report for year ended June 30, 2025](https://app.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/0/C1CFBE668F38D1E086258D46005AFA4C/$FILE/000091EA.pdf), independent auditors' report dated **October 16, 2025**, findings **printed pp. 64–65 / PDF indices 70–71**, visually examined November 17, 2026. The financial statements received an **unmodified opinion** alongside a reported **material weakness (finding 2025-001)** in documentary supervisory review of journal entries. The report says errant entries affected receivables and revenues, and the 2024 finding was repeated. The finding is **not explicitly attributed to Bohemia**; it is not a lease-by-lease royalty inspection and must never be used as evidence of wrongdoing, missing mineral payments or missing heir distributions. New bibliography source **#73**.

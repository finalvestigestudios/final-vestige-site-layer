**New independently verified legal source:** [Louisiana Legislature, current R.S. 30:127](https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/Law.aspx?d=86943), checked November 18:

- **§127(A):** bids may be for the whole or part of advertised land; an unregistered winning bidder's conditional acceptance lapses if registration is not completed by close of the second business day after that acceptance.
- **§127(C):** an actual lease needs an accurate description by section, township and range or authorized boundary-coordinate system.
- **§127(D):** the board may lease a *smaller quantity than advertised* and withdraw the rest.
- **§127(A)(1):** one-eighth is a statutory oil-and-gas royalty floor, not a contract-specific term or evidence of a payment.

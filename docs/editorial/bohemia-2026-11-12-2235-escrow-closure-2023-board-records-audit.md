## What changed

A blind spot in the previous version: case-law opinions proved that **specific monetary awards were not automatically collected**, but did not establish that **no Bohemia-related settlement was ever paid**. Direct records of the Southeast Louisiana Flood Protection Authority–East now supply a retrospective public-agency account of **some litigation settlements having been paid** and their escrow's **2015 closure**. This is a materially more nuanced ending than a binary “families never collected” narrative. It does **not** identify an award satisfied in the 2002 *Vogt* litigation, the 2012 Eusan award, or the *Gomez* tracts.

Draft v0.9 adds **“The escrow account closed in 2015”** (~476 words), ledger entries **[21]–[22]**, and updated release-gate caveats. The earlier original diagrams and NASA media candidates remain unchanged.

## Primary source A — June 15, 2023 flood authority minutes

Original official agency PDF: https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/2023-06-15-SLFPA-E-Board-Meeting-Minutes-1.pdf

**Document locator:** printed pp. 7–8 (searchable text near the account of the 2007 MOU and presentation by Louis Capo). The meeting is explicitly dated June 15, 2023 on printed p. 1.

**Directly supported:**
- The presentation says a pre-Katrina attempt to escrow up to **$10 million or a portion thereof** in settlement context was not acted upon immediately.
- It reports that plaintiffs attempted to seize non-flood-protection assets in 2007 and that **the settlement was subsequently paid**.
- Management reported about **$2.4 million in mineral lease revenue received over the preceding four years**.

**Limit:** This is a board-minute account from its director, not settlement agreements, closing statements, canceled checks, parcel-specific revenue, or a current fiscal audit. The source does not identify the payees of the settlement referred to. Do not equate the separate LMA funding/balance disputes to private restitution claims.

## Primary source B — November 16, 2023 flood authority minutes

Original official agency PDF: https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2023-11-16-SLFPA-E-Board-Meeting-Minutes.pdf

**Document locator:** printed pp. 8–9 (discussion of Bohemia revenues and amended MOU, roll-call vote); cover printed p. 1 dates the meeting November 16, 2023.

**Directly supported:**
- Executive counsel Kirk Ordoyne described **2007–2015** litigation payments and reported that the related escrow fund **closed in 2015**.
- Executive director Louis Capo described approximately **$9 million** in Bohemia receipts **since 2007**, an agency management estimate rather than an independent audited schedule.
- Staff described annual Bohemia receipt variations of **$150,000–$700,000** across the preceding five years.
- The negotiation exchanged LMA's waiver of **pre-2024 Bohemia revenues** for FPA's forgiveness of an interfund amount, and the FPA approved the updated memorandum subject to the meeting's amendment. This is management allocation, **not a finding that the original private property claims were extinguished**.
- The later LMA July 2024 records identify **January 1, 2024** as the management-effective date. That date should be attributed to the later corroborating minutes, not silently inferred from this 2023 record.

**Accounting integrity rule:** The reported **$9 million since 2007** includes years within the **$2.4 million / preceding four years** description. **DO NOT add these numbers**. They have distinct source descriptions and unexamined reconciliation.

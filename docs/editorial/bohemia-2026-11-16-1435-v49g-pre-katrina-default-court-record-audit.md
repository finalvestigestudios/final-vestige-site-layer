## Primary source breakthrough — a documented missed installment *before* Katrina
1. **Federal district court**, *Haspel & Davis*, E.D. La. Civil Action 06-3829, Document 154, filed **February 28, 2007** (14 pages). Original docket PDF: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-laed-2_06-cv-03829/pdf/USCOURTS-laed-2_06-cv-03829-1.pdf. Direct federal court-order text was read, especially pages 1, 5, 13–14. Federal PDF page-image screenshot attempted and returned not-enabled, so **no claim of pixel-level original-page verification**.
2. The court described a second settlement dated **June 4, 2005**, filed into Plaquemines case 31-357 on **June 14, 2005**, a required **August 15, 2005** installment missed by the district, and **May 31, 2006** state-court judgment declaring the proposal in breach and no longer effective. This is more precise evidence than a later authority president's retrospective recollection of 2005 financing. The Fifth Circuit's **July 23, 2007** decision (493 F.3d 570, footnote 5) independently mentions the **June 2005 second settlement** and confirms it was set aside; full original agreement and August payment/docket record remain missing.
3. **NWS official Katrina timeline:** https://www.weather.gov/lix/katrina_anniversary confirms **August 29, 2005** landfall near Buras, Louisiana, **fourteen days after the recorded missed installment**. The missed installment cannot be attributed to a later hurricane, but its underlying cause cannot be established from these documents. The 2011 official's report about unavailable later financing may still describe a separate, post-storm difficulty.
4. Original district court Oct 10, 2006 finding (~$17.44m) and February 2007 supplemental order were subsequently **reversed July 23, 2007** by the Fifth Circuit (493 F.3d 570); no legally operative federal takings award survives. Do not repeat the superseded district court's forceful analysis as the last word on liability.
5. **Claimant-level lead:** February 28 order p. 13 describes **Exhibit A**, a court-filed list of remaining claimants and dollar balances. It reports four residual balances just below $10,000 after partial earlier payments, and the judge accepted plaintiffs' explanation. This is a judicially recorded historical report of **some partial payee payments**, not independent cleared bank proof, the underlying list, or final accounting. Acquire original Exhibit A and bank disbursement/registry data before identifying individual beneficiaries or declaring a final distribution.
6. **Source-text anomalies:** The February 2007 order inconsistently writes “October 10, 2007” in its analytical discussion despite its 2007 filing and explicit initial **October 10, 2006** judgment reference. Original Oct 2006 judgment is also rendered with slightly divergent dollars/cents across district and appellate textual summaries; the reader uses **approximately $17.44 million**, not an invented definitive cent-perfect reconciliation. The FY2006 auditor PDF's separate reference to Katrina in “August 2006” also conflicts with NWS's 2005 event chronology. Don't elevate a document's transcription/date error into a historical conclusion.

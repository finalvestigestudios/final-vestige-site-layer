## Editorial finding: separate docket sets, separate authorizations
Official [March 20, 2014 Flood Protection Authority–East minutes](https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/minutes/2014%2003%2020%20-%20Board%20Meeting%20Minutes.pdf), printed pp. 10–11, list three Bohemia executive-session groupings: successor and DNR cases 55-774/563586/61-051; Burt heirs 58-217/DNR 561174; Union Benevolent 59-384/DNR 540151. The public board subsequently voted to follow counsel's advice in Union Benevolent case 59-384, but did not publish the advice. This **does not establish a settled case or paid claimant**.

Official [July 24, 2014 minutes](https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/minutes/2014%2007%2024%20-%20Board%20Meeting%20Minutes.pdf), **printed pp. 13–14 visually inspected through original PDF screenshots**, resolve a significant omission in the earlier draft: **three separate** Bohemia-related votes were recorded, each **five yeas, no nays, three absent**:
- **Resolution 07-24-14-10**: authorize counsel to proceed with *settlement offer* concerning 563586/55-774/61-051.
- **Resolution 07-24-14-12**: authorize counsel to proceed as recommended in *Heirs of Dr. Thomas Burt* 58-217.
- **Resolution 07-24-14-13**: authorize counsel-recommended actions *to settle* *Union Benevolent Association* 59-384.

The July resolutions name neither DNR case 561174 nor 540151; their March presence does **not** prove inclusion in the July action. These are *authorizations*, not three executed agreements, three cash disbursements, or a single FY2014 balance reconciliation. The source-to-claim distinction is especially important around the FY2014 combined judgment-liability change and still-uninspected named-case subledger.

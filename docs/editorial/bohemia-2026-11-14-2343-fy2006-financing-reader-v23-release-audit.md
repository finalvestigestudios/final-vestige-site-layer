## Primary-source verification: what the 2006 accounts say

**Original financial statements**, Orleans Levee District, audited by Postlethwaite & Netterville for the year ended **June 30, 2006**, auditor dated **December 30, 2006**, via [Louisiana Legislative Auditor PDF](https://app.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/F190284F7DA564B88625725E0078912F/%24FILE/000008C0.pdf). **Original PDF page screenshot tool succeeded** for printed pages 4, 10, 45, 49, 53 and 54 (zero-indexed PDF pages **6, 12, 47, 51, 55 and 56**, respectively), supplemented by source text.

- **FY2006 management discussion, p. 4:** Board resolution **5-081705**, August **17, 2005**, approved using available funds and debt for Bohemia judgments. Katrina's financial impact made that initiative impractical during that fiscal year. Same page reports cash and investments down **more than $17 million**, while emphasizing district-wide holdings, insurance and operating expenses. *The reported explanation is management's, not an independent causal conclusion or proof of a failed individual payment.*
- **FY2006 litigation note, p. 53:** *Haspel & Davis* class agreement to accept **$10,076,559.47** conditional on financing and appropriation; a **$600,000** payment into **court registry February 26, 2004**; other payments since 2001; DNR-certified claimant interests in **164 of 220 tracts** before the specified 1997/1999 statutory amendments; institution's assertion of conveyances for claims certified through June 30, 2005. Neither legal status nor registry transfer proves individual beneficiary receipts. Do not add this conditional settlement figure to terms summarized in the 2007 federal appellate opinion without underlying contract reconciliation.
- **p. 54:** separately reports **$314,491.59** paid in Taylor succession and disputed **$166,842.53** special-interest/fee sum. The 2005 *Frank* appellate case discusses characterization of the same sum differently: requires docket check before merging them as a final legal finding. Report also gives **$23,485,643** estimated judgments liability for governmental activities at June 30, 2006; **not** a Bohemia-only beneficiary payable.
- **pp. 45 and 49:** restricted Bohemia cash / reserve line **$339,407**.
- **p. 10:** management discussion separately writes **$391,566** as designated for future General Fund expenditures and again as committed to Bohemia settlement. It does not reconcile this with p. 49's $339,407. Preserve disagreement openly; do not infer that the **$52,159 mathematical difference** is a payment, loss or improper transfer. Seek original general ledger and audit workpapers.
- **Internal source inconsistency to flag, not silently harmonize:** Other supplemental note text appears to misdate an August 17 authorization and Katrina as **2006**, while management discussion and historical disaster chronology establish 2005. The manuscript uses dated resolution **5-081705** and management p. 4; original board minutes remain a retrieval target.

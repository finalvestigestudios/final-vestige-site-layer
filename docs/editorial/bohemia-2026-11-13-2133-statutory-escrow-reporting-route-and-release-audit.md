## Finding and why it matters
The November 30, 2021 Department of Natural Resources Joint Legislative Committee on the Budget (JLCB) escrow report lists **agency 431 / organizational code 4311 / account 1107**, described as **advance costs monies received from Bohemia Spillway claimants**. Its beginning and ending month balances were **$48,087.59**, with no entries in the deposit or withdrawal columns for that row. Verified from the **actual original PDF page image at zero-indexed page 3 / printed p. 4** on November 13, 2026. This is an administrative-cost advance account, not the Orleans Levee District disputed-property/mineral settlement escrow ordered closed in 2015.

The more useful new discovery is the statutory reporting trail. **R.S. 49:320.2(C)(2)** requires monthly reports for agency escrows within its scope, including beginning balance, dated deposits and withdrawals, ending balance and journal vouchers/transfer requests, delivered through the state treasurer to the Cash Management Review Board (CMRB) and JLCB. **R.S. 39:372(H)** also addresses the CMRB's role in escrow reports. **R.S. 49:320.2(B)(3)** provides a conditional refund-without-appropriation route for certain nonstate money, but it does not establish any Bohemia claimant's eligibility or receipt.

The monthly statutory reporting requirement is not a substitute for obtaining actual 2022–2026 records. The 2021 amount is **not** proof of a live 2026 balance, nonpayment, abuse, or entitlement to a refund. The 2015 **Orleans Levee District escrow** is a separate institution, account and legal function.

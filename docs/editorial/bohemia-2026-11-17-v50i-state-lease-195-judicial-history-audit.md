## Source-level research: State Lease 195

Humble Oil & Refining Co.*, 346 F.2d 43, May 27, 1965 (rehearing denied July 21)](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/346/43/445482/): disputed Stone Island/West Black Bay mineral title, previous **State Lease 195** described as dated **February 28, 1928**, escrowed oil royalties, plaintiffs not entitled to immediate distribution before satisfying escrow/title conditions. **Separate parcels and claimants** from the Eusan Tract 78 case.
- **1970 Louisiana Supreme Court:** [*Stevens v. State Mineral Board*, 233 So. 2d 542, March 30, 1970](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1970/255-la-857-0.html): same lease number described as dated **February 20, 1928**; underlying water-bottom/title facts unresolved and adverse summary judgment set aside and remanded. **Not a final title award to private litigants or disbursement proof.**
- **1975 Louisiana Supreme Court:** [*Gulf Oil Corp. v. State Mineral Board*, 317 So. 2d 576, on rehearing July 25, 1975](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1975/317-so-2d-576-1.html): contested *different* Plaquemines water-bottom title and royalties, with state title upheld; other views are concurrences/dissents. **Not a judgment about Bohemia Tract 193, 2023 State Tract 45659 or Eusan 78.**
- **2023 State Office of Mineral Resources:** [Tract 45659 original nomination](https://www.denr.louisiana.gov/assets/OMR/NOP_tracts/2023/45656-45659.pdf), PDF zero-based page **11** independently screenshot inspected this cycle; nominator excluded portions described as covered by **State Leases 195/22099** and specified 7.91 acres left. **Does not identify the earlier litigation acreage as Bohemia Tract 193.** No original 1928 instrument, 22099 instrument, plats, amendments or assignments retrieved.

**Specific unresolved inconsistency:** 1965 federal and 1970 state court opinions give **different dates (Feb. 28 vs. Feb. 20, 1928)** for State Lease 195.

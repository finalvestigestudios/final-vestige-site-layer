## Investigative boundary

Louisiana's [royalty information guide](https://www.denr.louisiana.gov/page/how-to-obtain-royalty-payment-info) explains that state royalty collection records are not the private-heir distribution system. A state LUW (Lease, Unit, or Well) identifier and payor/property-decimal reports may help reconcile *state* remittances if supplied by lease files; they cannot alone establish an Eusan successor's actual bank receipt. Separate court registry/claims administrator disbursement records will be needed.

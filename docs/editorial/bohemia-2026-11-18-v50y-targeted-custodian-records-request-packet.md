## v50z public land-records alternative — free archive route, no restricted-portal bypass

The [Louisiana Office of State Lands' public Document Access page](https://doa.la.gov/doa/osl/document-access/) identifies downloadable historical survey plats, U.S./State tract books, severance documents, state patents and an independent **State Lands and Buildings System (SLABS)** inventory. Images are advertised as free TIFF downloads. This is a **separate official records path**, not an automated substitute for the restricted SONRIS tract-detail service and not a reason to disregard its access terms.

Search or request the historic survey plats and tract-book indexes covering **Section 18, T18S, R16E** (the approximately 267-acre LMA proposal) and **Section 28, T18S, R15E** (the approximately 394-acre proposal). Capture the survey date, land district, official plat identifier, original deed/severance references, document provenance and any ambiguity in the section geometry. If an OSL/SLABS agency inventory entry is found, record the **agency name, property identifier, record date and attached deed reference** separately; do not equate inventory with present mineral ownership.

The [Louisiana December 2026 mineral lease notice](https://www.dce.louisiana.gov/page/mineral-and-energy-board-notice-of-publication) states that **the nominating party supplies and corrects the advertised tract descriptions** and that state awards carry no warranty of title; private claims or conflicting leases can require changes or cancellation. Accordingly, the two-column crosswalk must capture, independently for **46175 and 46176**: (1) *nominator's original written legal description and polygon/plat*; (2) *LMA September 24 recorded authorization and September 3 Beacon submission*; (3) *agency mineral-interest conveyance and any exclusions/retained rights*; (4) *historic survey context*; and (5) *any final bid, award and executed lease after December 9*. Historical plats and old patents are **context**, not proof that the 2026 state agency owns all minerals, nor proof that the nominated acreage is the Eusan family tract.

**Release rule:** Do not publish a boundary overlay, mineral-interest map, parcel identity, title defect or private-owner allegation on acreage equivalence alone. The present 394/267 pairing remains a research hypothesis. No new fees or external request was incurred in preparing this route.

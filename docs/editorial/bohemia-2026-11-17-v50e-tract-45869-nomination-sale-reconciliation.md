## New original-document discovery and exact arithmetic

**Fact A: The application reached an actual state mineral offering.** Louisiana's archived official November 13, 2024 State Agency Tracts PDF, pages 32–34 printed (PDF zero-based 31–33), names **TRACT 45869 – Plaquemines Parish**; Orleans Levee District; **ROBERT A. SCHROEDER, INC.** applicant; **+/- 107 estimated net mineral acres**. It describes Tracts 147–164 along the Mississippi between Bohemia Plantation and Fort St. Philip. The 14 listed estimates total 107; they measure estimated mineral acres net to the lessor for specified nonroyalty calculations, not certified surveyed surface acreage. The nomination explicitly says location and rights were supplied by the agency/nomination party; no warranty and no title investigation is implied. The red-boundary map is a **nomination illustration**, not a verified mineral/title or Eusan Tract 78 survey.

**Fact B: State sale summary strongly suggests no November 13 award.** November 13 State Agency rows: **9 nominated tracts, 481.553 acres**; **8 awarded tracts, 374.553 acres**. Reconciliation: 9 minus 8 = **1 tract**, 481.553 minus 374.553 = **107.000 acres**. The independently issued **Nov. 14, 2024** Louisiana department news release names sold parishes Caddo, Calcasieu, Cameron, DeSoto, Lafayette, Red River and St. Landry, and **does not name Plaquemines Parish**. Combined with the exactly matching 107-acre nomination, this is strong evidence that Bohemia **#45869 was not awarded in the November 13, 2024 sale**. However the tally is aggregate, not an explicitly identified tract-by-tract rejection/award sheet, and includes an official caveat that post-sale options/revisions may alter totals. Do **not** state as proven that it received no bids, was rejected for a particular reason, was never subsequently leased or that unrelated ongoing royalties ceased.

**Fact C: Distinct administrative actors.** July 25, 2024 LMA committee describes Robert A. Schroeder/K Exploration in its discussion; the November state nomination names **ROBERT A. SCHROEDER, INC.** as applicant, which need not be the same corporate identity as K Exploration. July same-day full-board **9–0 delegation** and committee recommendation are **not themselves an award**. Publication confirms a later step, not every intervening board disposition. These tracts 147–164 are **not Tract 78**; no claim of correspondence with the Eusan judgment.

**Critical evidentiary distinction:** A blank bidder/terms grid on the original nomination PDF p. 33 is part of the pre-sale form. It **does not prove no bids**. The issue is resolved only by a definitive tract-specific award/rejection notice, certified mineral board minutes or verified later mineral lease history. Do not bypass the state database's automated access restrictions to obtain one.

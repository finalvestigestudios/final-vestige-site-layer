## Newly verified primary governmental guidance
**Source [27]:** Louisiana Office of Mineral Resources official [Leasing Manual: Pre-Nomination Research / Availability Research](https://www.denr.louisiana.gov/page/pre-nomination-research-availability-research), section 2(d)(i)-(ii), 2(e); text inspected November 18, 2026, official archive accessible via search text after some direct page opens returned 403. The source distinguishes: (1) government mineral-office custody of official *state mineral lease* files; (2) custody of official *state-agency mineral lease* files by the agency, with office copies and transmitting letters; (3) official board minutes as a separate documentary series. Critically, a state-agency nomination that is merely submitted for approval does **not** receive a sequential executed *lease* number or official lease file. This corrects a common public confusion between **tract IDs 45659/45869** and **lease numbers**. The source does NOT establish that either proposed Bohemia tract received a later lease or identify the current original file custodian of State Leases 195/22099.

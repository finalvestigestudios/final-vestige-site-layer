## New consequential source-verification finding
The official FY2006 Orleans Levee District financial-report document internally **disagrees on the year** of a settlement-financing authorization, and incorrectly prints the year of Hurricane Katrina in the same subsection. This is a discrepancy in the *original scanned pages*, not an OCR artifact.

| Record and exact locator | What it says | Evidentiary reading |
|---|---|---|
| [FY2005 audited statements](https://lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/88e334f6ae3ae3e5862570a60069ba83/%24file/0000104f.pdf), Subsequent Events Note 23, **printed p.69 / PDF zero-based p.77** | **August 17, 2005** authorization for up to **$11.2m** of financing for Bohemia judgment settlement; intended State Bond Commission application | Contemporaneous FY2005 account of authorization, not proof of issued debt or settlement funding |
| [FY2006 audited statements](https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/f190284f7da564b88625725e0078912f/%24file/000008c0.pdf), Litigation Note 17, **printed p.54 / PDF zero-based p.56** | Board meeting **August 17, 2005** authorized use of internal funds and borrowing for settlement with the class | Same reported 2005 period; source image **visually rechecked this cycle** |
| **Same FY2006 PDF**, Subsequent Events Note 23(B), **printed p.59 / PDF zero-based p.61** | Says board met **August 17, 2006** to authorize **up to $11.2m** for Bohemia debt and postponement due to Hurricane Katrina **August 29, 2006** | Two *printed* 2006 dates conflict with the document's p.54 and established 2005 storm date; original page **visually rechecked this cycle** |
| [NWS Hurricane Katrina anniversary](https://www.weather.gov/lix/katrina_anniversary) | Storm made landfall in southeastern Louisiana **August 29, 2005** | Independent event date, not evidence of loan issuance or cause of August 15 default |
| [Federal court Doc. 154](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-laed-2_06-cv-03829/pdf/USCOURTS-laed-2_06-cv-03829-1.pdf), Feb. 28, 2007 | Court describes missed installment **August 15, 2005** | Establishes sequence *as reported by court*, not its financing cause or a receipt |

**Editorial finding:** Do not read FY2006 p.59 as proof of a *second* August 2006 $11.2m financing vote. The FY2006 report contains inconsistent historical years. Because its exact drafting history and original board resolution are unexamined, the safe wording is that those 2006 dates **appear erroneous**, not that a specific employee made a clerical mistake. The independent August 17, 2005 board minutes/resolution and State Bond Commission application are still needed for precise financing details.

### Additional verified context, not a new payout
[FY2007 audit](https://app.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/3ABF8A4700B075598625737600782977/%24FILE/00001769.pdf), Note 19 **printed p.58 / PDF zero-based p.61**, was independently reexamined in the scanned page this cycle. It records **$19m of district funds set aside pending federal appeal** and the July 23, 2007 Fifth Circuit reversal. **This source was already incorporated in the manuscript as bibliography item #27 and does not earn a new source number.** Funds reportedly segregated against a contested judgment are not beneficiary receipts. The financial-report text also reports 16 Bohemia-related suits by June 30, 2007, not necessarily 16 distinct paid/unpaid beneficiaries. Report dates do not justify a claim about current outstanding amounts.

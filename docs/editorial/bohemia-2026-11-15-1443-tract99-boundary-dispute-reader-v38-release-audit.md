Bass Enterprises Production Co., Bass Partnership, Marc Fisher and Billie Breuille*, No. 2004-CA-1059, June 22, 2005](https://cases.justia.com/louisiana/fourth-circuit-court-of-appeal/2005-2004-ca-1059.pdf?ts=1437339617), 14-page opinion expressly **NOT DESIGNATED FOR PUBLICATION**. Searchable opinion independently compared, actual PDF-page screenshots **5, 11, 14** (zero-indexed 4, 10, 13) visually inspected this cycle.
- The panel described the levee board's **unrecorded Brodtmann inventory**, which associated Tract 99 with Zenon/Schneidau and located the Fisher property at Tract 101. Pre-1925 parish deeds instead located Fisher land in **Section 9** between Zettwoch and Favrot (the same opinion uses variant Favret elsewhere), consistent with an **1893 Mississippi River Commission survey** and later **Fenstermaker plat**, which designated Section 9's Fisher land Tract 99. The court **affirmed** a trial judgment recognizing Fisher successors **Marc Fisher and Billie Breuille** as entitled to the disputed mineral royalties. It reports **no bank-verified recipient amounts or payments**.
- Limitation: Only judicial recitation inspected, **not original historic plat sheets/deeds/1893 survey facsimiles**, and this opinion concerns specific litigants and evidence, not every Bohemia tract. No unsupported conclusion of deliberate record falsification or withheld funds.

## Verified original sources and critical boundaries

**Federal district-court chronology:** [EDLA Case No. 06-3829, Document 154, filed February 28, 2007](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-laed-2_06-cv-03829/pdf/USCOURTS-laed-2_06-cv-03829-1.pdf), original document p. 5 / PDF zero-based page 4 visually inspected this cycle. Court described June 4, 2005 settlement, required August 15, 2005 installment **missed**, and subsequent state-court declaration May 31, 2006 that this second deal had been breached. Federal takings judgment was later **reversed** by Fifth Circuit July 23, 2007 [existing manuscript source 15]. The original dishonored payment instrument was not obtained; the court order cannot establish *why* the installment was missed.

**Audited authorization two days later:** [Orleans Levee District FY2005 independent audit](https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/88e334f6ae3ae3e5862570a60069ba83/$file/0000104f.pdf), **Note 23, printed p. 69, PDF page index 77**. Actual scanned image and text inspected. The district reports **August 17, 2005** authorization to issue **up to $11,200,000 of debt** associated with a Bohemia-judgment settlement and a State Bond Commission application; issuance was postponed after Katrina. **Not proof of bond issuance, receipt of proceeds, or money delivered to class beneficiaries.** [FY2006 independent audit](https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/f190284f7da564b88625725e0078912f/$file/000008c0.pdf), **Note 17, printed p. 54, PDF page index 56**, independently checked as original scanned page, recounts internal/external funding plan and interruption. FY2006 overall judgment-liability estimate of **$23,485,643** is not a verified Bohemia-only claimant payment amount.

**External date:** [National Weather Service Katrina anniversary](https://www.weather.gov/lix/katrina_anniversary) gives August 29, 2005 landfall near Buras. The documented sequence is **Aug 15 missed installment; Aug 17 borrowing authorization; Aug 29 hurricane**. It does not establish Aug 17 was a response to Aug 15, that financing would have covered that particular payment, or that it ever closed. Katrina may have frustrated later borrowing but cannot itself explain a default the court dated before landfall.

**Named accounting trail:** Same [February 28, 2007 federal order](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-laed-2_06-cv-03829/pdf/USCOURTS-laed-2_06-cv-03829-1.pdf), **document p. 3, PDF page index 2**, visually inspected. It identifies **Bourgeois Bennett** as state-court-approved **disbursing agent** and says plaintiffs offered interest calculations prepared by the firm. It refers to federal **record document 120, February 8, 2007**, ordering calculations. This makes precise retrieval targets: agent's original court appointment, interest workpapers, approved allocation schedule, claimant Exhibit A, registry disbursement and settlement records. **No actual firm workpapers, original appointment, present custodian, beneficiary ledger, or verified payments inspected.** No contact or request to firm claimed.

**OCR warning:** In [FY2007 auditor Note 19, printed p. 58, PDF image index 61](https://app.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/3ABF8A4700B075598625737600782977/%24FILE/00001769.pdf), visually inspected source showed **$18,767,145** class judgment, while one extracted text view erroneously showed **$113,767,145**. Do not propagate extraction error. A separate **$19 million set aside pending appeal** was not payment to heirs.

### Verified propositions and explicit boundaries
- The July 25, 2024 board **adopted resolution #5 requesting/direction of the State Mineral and Energy Board's leasing authority**, 9 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 abstentions, 7 absent. It is an authorization, **not** an actual award, signed lease, completed nomination or payment.
- The resolution expressly cites the **November 2023 memorandum** as the origin of LMA's management role. That is the resolution's recited premise; the executed underlying MOU is still absent.
- Item #6 of the same minutes/agenda identifies an **approximately 107-acre** proposal from Robert A. Schroeder, tracts 147–149, 152–156, 158½, 159 and 161–164, T19S R16/17E. The minutes show its heading but **no vote, motion result or disposition on item #6** before proceeding to approval of previous committee minutes. This is a silence in these minutes, not evidence of ultimate approval/denial or absence of a later Mineral and Energy Board action.
- **Original-document date inconsistencies:** first page's header says *Thursday June 25, 2024*, while opening narrative and numerous adoption lines say *July 25, 2024*; resolution label reads `VII-05-07252025` even though its adjacent date/adoption line say July 25, 2024. Do not silently repair the printed identifier or allege alteration/backdating. For exposition use the corroborated July 25, 2024 meeting date and disclose discrepancies.
- Nothing in the July resolution identifies Eusan **Tract 78**, claimant beneficiaries, claimant settlement releases or restitution. No connection to private claimant proceeds is established here.

**Procedural corroboration:** Louisiana Department of Energy and Natural Resources, [leasing-process explanation](https://www.dnr.louisiana.gov/page/intro-administration-of-the-state-mineral-leasing-process), [Mineral Board responsibilities](https://www.dnr.louisiana.gov/page/state-mineral-and-energy-board) and [state-agency nomination guidance](https://www.denr.louisiana.gov/page/how-to-nominate-a-tract-of-land-for-state-or-state-agency-mineral-lease). These explain why board delegation and a completed lease cannot be equated. The nominating guide has been revised since 2024; do **not** apply its current fees to 2024 without historical verification.

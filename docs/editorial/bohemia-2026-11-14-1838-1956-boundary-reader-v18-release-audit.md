Rechecked the successive public judicial decisions in *Richardson & Bass (Louisiana Account) v. Board of Levee Commissioners*, 231 La. 299, 91 So.2d 353, reproduced in a public judicial-opinion collection: https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/supreme-court/1956/231-la-299-0.html . Dates: June 30, 1955 original decision; February 20, 1956 first rehearing; November 5, 1956 second rehearing; November 21, 1956 amended operative decree; later appeal dismissal December 17, 1956.

**Documented in judicial reconstruction:** ~11 miles of riverbank levee removal, a 2,500–3,000-foot back levee, an oil-and-gas parcel in Cox Bay Unit No. 2 about a mile east of the back levee, yet within the far broader statutory public-land area described by Act 311 (1942) as extending from the Mississippi to the sea. Grand Prairie's 1936 Delta lease and Orleans's 1950 competing lease are not interchangeable. The **final second-rehearing / November 21 disposition**, not the earlier superseded outcomes, rejected Delta's claimed royalty, awarded Orleans **3/16 of the contested 55% of production proceeds**, and corrected the well count from four to **six**. Three judicial dissents contest the title analysis. Fractions are case-specific, not universal Bohemia receipts or a payment register.

**Interpretive limit:** The court interpreted public-district and mineral-lessee ownership and narrated 1920s construction long after the fact. No original 1920s engineering survey, 1936/1950 leases, 1942 enrolled Act or individual bank-payment receipts were independently recovered this cycle. It precedes and does not decide later *private-heir* restitution under the 1984 statute. A judge's award is not a cleared check.

**1986 acquisition-source reconciliation:** Original February 1986 opinion used an approximate 48% purchased/52% public grouping, whereas the October 20 controlling rehearing recounted approximately 3% expropriated, 48% purchased in lieu and 49% public transfer; Reader v18 retains the controlling three-way breakdown and records the superseded approximation in the bibliography.

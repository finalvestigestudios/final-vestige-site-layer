## Primary-source verification

**Louisiana Fourth Circuit, *Vogt*, consolidated Nos. 2001-CA-0089/0090, March 27, 2002.** Original [18-page appellate PDF](https://cases.justia.com/louisiana/fourth-circuit-court-of-appeal/2002-2001-ca-0089.pdf?ts=1440795651) reviewed in accessible text and **original page imagery** (PDF indexes **7, 8 and 17**) on November 18. The original court text records:
- July 24, 1998: judgment for **$2,853,358.44** plus judicial interest (PDF p. 6, 0-based index 5; opinion describes its later finality).
- Accountant David D. DeMonte's evidence from legislative audits and the district's books: as of June 30, 2000 approximately **$57 million** in investment securities, including an identified **$2.7 million** “held for Bohemia litigation” designation, and a **$9 million** money-market account **within the $57 million**, not an additional $9 million. The opinion records his testimony that funds were tracked with bookkeeping even where revenue was held in a common deposit account (PDF pp. 8–9; indexes 7–8).
- March 27, 2002: affirmance of denial of mandatory appropriation / seizure; the judgment remained distinct from mechanisms of collection (final page, index 17). The court's rationale cites La. Const. Art. XII §10(C) and La. R.S. 13:5109(B)(2).

**Federal Fifth Circuit, *Vogt*, 294 F.3d 684, June 14, 2002.** [Full published opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/294/684/545697/) independently reviewed. The court rejected an Eleventh Amendment immunity defense in this case and held the plaintiffs had pleaded a cognizable federal takings theory concerning retained private mineral royalties. It **reversed dismissal and remanded**, expressly declining to determine the ultimate outcome. It did not award the $2.85m anew, order a check, or prove transfer to a beneficiary.

**A related but distinct Attorney General publication:** A secondary [2004 Louisiana Attorney General opinion mirror](https://syfert.com/louisiana/cases/cl-3465435/opinion-number) cites the 2002 *Vogt* litigation in a West Feliciana Parish judgment-enforcement inquiry. Its publication/number metadata are incomplete; it was **not** treated as the contemplated **2023** LMA/FPA Bohemia revenue opinion, and it has **not** been added to the published source ledger. Seek the original document and number before citing as an authoritative AG determination.

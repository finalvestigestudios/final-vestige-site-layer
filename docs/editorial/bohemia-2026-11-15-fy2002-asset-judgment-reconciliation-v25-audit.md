## Original evidence and limits

The [Louisiana Legislative Auditor's Orleans Levee District FY2002 report](https://www.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/86256EA9004C005986256F7F007D7E24/%24FILE/41d2eee0.PDF) was reexamined in its **original scanned page images**, not merely searchable indexing:

- **Statement A, printed p.18, PDF zero-based image 20:** Governmental-activity investments held for Bohemia litigation **$639,356**, also shown under restricted net assets as Bohemia claims **$639,356**. These are not two separate sums.
- **Note 17, printed p.51, PDF image 53:** Bohemia Spillway Escrow **$639,356**, within General Fund fund-balance reserves. A third presentation/classification of the same dated balance, **not** a third deposit.
- **Note 20, printed p.54, PDF image 56:** *Vogt & Edgecombe* reported remaining judgment **$1,722,845** plus interest; *Haspel & Davis* remaining judgment **$16,077,350** plus interest. The two balances sum to **$17,800,195**, independently matching Statement A's printed long-term **judgments payable** liability. The lawsuit narrative also gives historical court-registry deposits and payments, but not claimant-cleared distribution records.

The result is an important **asset versus liability accounting distinction** and a reconciliation **within the 2002 liability**. No inference that `$17,800,195 - $639,356` equals a claimant payment gap, evidence of diversion, present balance, missed disbursement or executed escrow closure. Interest and subsequent court actions require separate treatment. The report's inconsistent 69-versus-70 lawsuit count remains a documented ancillary discrepancy, not reconciled.

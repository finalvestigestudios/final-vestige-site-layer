## Original-source research and decisive finding

1. [Orleans Levee District FY2006 Basic Financial Statements, Louisiana Legislative Auditor](https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/f190284f7da564b88625725e0078912f/$file/000008c0.pdf), **management discussion printed p. 10 / PDF page-image zero-based 12**. Visually inspected original page November 15. The narrative identifies **$391,566** as General Fund designated future spending, then repeats **$391,566** in its Bohemia litigation reserve sentence. The narrative is internally suspect because of category conflation.
2. **Same official report, Note 14 printed p. 49 / PDF zero-based image 51**, original page visually inspected. **Bohemia Spillway Escrow: $339,407** at June 30, 2006.
3. **Same report, Note 15 printed p. 50 / PDF zero-based image 52**, original page visually inspected. The **$391,566 General Fund designation** consists of **$16,971 equipment + $18,239 air-conditioning + $100,000 hurricane cleanup/recovery + $100,000 workers' compensation + $156,356 capital assets = $391,566**. Detailed financial notes do not present this second amount as a separate Bohemia escrow.
4. [Orleans Levee District FY2005 Basic Financial Statements, Legislative Auditor](https://app.lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/88e334f6ae3ae3e5862570a60069ba83/$file/0000104f.pdf), **management discussion printed p. 15 / PDF zero-based image 15** original page inspected. Independently labels **$391,566** as designated general spending and **$456,190** as the separate Bohemia reserve. **FY2005 subsequent events printed p. 69 / PDF zero-based image 77** independently reexamined: August 17, 2005 board authorized **up to $11,200,000 in financing** for litigation, postponed after Katrina; authorization is not issuance or claimant payment.
5. The correction is consequential: earlier Reader Editions v23–v26 carried a **$52,159 difference** between the FY2006 management narrative and Note 14 as un-reconciled. The **detailed Note 15 entirely identifies the $391,566 as a non-Bohemia designated total**. The financial statements support identifying an **internal narrative category/label inconsistency**; they **do not** prove the specific cause (copy editing, carryover, etc.), fraud, misappropriation, missing $52,159, transfer, claimant satisfaction or present-day liability. Original trial balances/auditor drafting notes and beneficiary-payee ledger remain necessary.

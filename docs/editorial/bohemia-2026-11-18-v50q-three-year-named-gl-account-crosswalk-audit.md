# Bohemia v50q: original-budget account crosswalk, verification and release HOLD
*Final Vestige Studios Investigations · November 18, 2026 · internal research and editorial audit*

## Finding

Three consecutive published FPA-East annual budgets identify **actual** Bohemia revenue for Orleans Levee District (OLD) General Fund under **GL 3300 Bohemia Royalties** (department account 1001003300) and **GL 3305 Bohemia Other Income** (1001003305). Each year's two GL amounts add to the same year's **audited OLD General Fund** “Royalties, leases and permits” amount. This is a documentary account-category crosswalk, not evidence of individual cash transfers or claimant payments.

| Fiscal year of actual revenue | Later FPA budget source showing actual | GL 3300 | GL 3305 | OLD actual sum | OLD audited category | Separate LMA audited category, not yet traced to Bohemia GL |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FY2023 | FY2025 budget | $743,717 | $5,784 | **$749,501** | **$749,501** | $0 |
| FY2024 | FY2026 budget | $37,093 | $1,896 | **$38,989** | **$38,989** | $46,497 |
| FY2025 | FY2027 budget | $63,279 | $0/dash | **$63,279** | **$63,279** | $106,377 |

The **FY2025 budget originally forecast $0** under both OLD Bohemia accounts, but the later FY2027 budget reported **$63,279 in FY2025 actual Bohemia royalty revenue**. That difference is between *forecast and actual*, not evidence of unlawful diversion, broken agreement, cleared deposit or liability to an heir. The signed interagency MOU, lease/payor records and actual cash trail are needed.

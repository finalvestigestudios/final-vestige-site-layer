**[56] Official Louisiana Legislative Auditor FY2002 report** — [Orleans Levee District, *Basic Financial Statements and Independent Auditor's Reports*, published September 18, 2002, Note 20, printed page 54](https://www.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/86256EA9004C005986256F7F007D7E24/$FILE/41d2eee0.PDF). PDF screenshot **zero-index page 56** inspected; legible original page text corroborates extracted text. The district reported:
- *Vogt & Edgecombe*: judgment basis **$1,831,528**, of which **$108,683** in claims under $10,000 reported paid by **August 15, 2000**; at **June 30, 2002** unpaid principal reported **$1,722,845 plus interest**. This internal arithmetic reconciles: 1,831,528 − 108,683 = 1,722,845.
- *Haspel & Davis*, kept separate: **$1,968,264** deposited in **court registry** by August 15, 2000 on small claims; **$350,000** in agreed fixed costs deposited there by December 11, 2000; **$371,532** paid against judgment by August 15, 2001; **$16,077,350 plus interest** recorded unpaid at June 30, 2002.
- Auditor's Note 20 reports **70 lawsuits** including **22 Bohemia-related** at June 30, 2002, and says payments depend on available and appropriated funds. These counts are a historical snapshot, not current claims.
- **Material unresolved discrepancy:** [54] **1998 appellate judgment $2,853,358.44** is *different* from the FY2002 district note's **$1,831,528** stated basis. Arithmetic difference **$1,021,830.44**, but the cause is unknown. **Do not** label this difference a payment, loss, diversion or discrepancy in current funds. Obtain case-specific allocation and judgment/amendment ledger.
- Evidence class: agency accounting statement carried in a financial audit; not original check register, cancelled check, payee signoff, escrow bank reconciliation or completed claimant distribution. A court registry deposit is not a beneficiary's receipt.

## What the original government records establish
**A different escrow than the one closed by the 2015 levee board:**
1. [Southeast Louisiana Flood Protection Authority-East March 19, 2015 official board minutes](https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/minutes/2015%2003%2019%20-%20Board%20Meeting%20Minutes.pdf), printed p. 24, Resolution 03-19-15-12: Orleans Levee District disputed-property *settlement/mineral revenue* escrow. Board authorized closing and transferring an **unspecified** balance to its General Fund, 8–0. This is vote authorization, NOT bank-transfer proof or payee accounting. Searchable official PDF verified in previous run, screenshot of exact page remains unavailable.
2. [November 2020 Act 361 escrow report](https://jlcb.legis.state.la.us/Docs/2020/dec/December%202020%20RTC%20Act%20361%20of%202017%20Monthly%20Report%20-%20Nov.pdf), printed p. 3: **Department of Natural Resources** (agency 431), code **1107** / expenditure org **4311**, Bohemia *advance costs for reviewing and administering claims* (distinct from mineral settlements). Reported historical beginning/end **$48,087.59**, not an identified claimant liability. Searchable text verified; the page-screenshot service returned 502.
3. [September 2021 Act 361 escrow report](https://jlcb.legis.la.gov/Docs/2021/oct/October%202021%20RTC%20Act%20361%20of%202017%20monthly%20report%20-%20September.pdf), printed p. 4; **original PDF page image 3 opened and visually checked**. DNR agency 431 / code 1107, same historical balance **$48,087.59**, monthly deposits/withdrawals blank.
4. [November 2021 Act 361 escrow report](https://jlcb.legis.la.gov/Docs/2021/dec/December%202021%20RTC%20Act%20361%20of%202017%20-%20November.pdf), printed p. 4; **original PDF page image 3 opened and visually checked**. Identical DNR account and balance; monthly deposit/withdrawal columns blank. It is NOT a 2026 statement.
5. Earlier rules: [DNR 2002 historical rule compilation](https://www.dnr.louisiana.gov/assets/docs/), previously ledgered as manuscript [4], prescribed $175 advance cost deposit in claim processing and procedures for expenses/refunds/indigency. No original deposit or refund roll has been obtained for the 2020–21 account.

**Do not conflate:** 2015 authority order to close the *Orleans Levee District settlement account* does not contradict a different agency's *DNR administrative fee account* remaining reported in 2021. Three same-dollar snapshots do not prove an unchanged balance across all intervening dates. No current DNR balance, account ownership determination, funds owed, or claimant-level refund is verified.

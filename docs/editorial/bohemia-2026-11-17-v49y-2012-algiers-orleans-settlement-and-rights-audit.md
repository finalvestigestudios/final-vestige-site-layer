## Original official document and verification

**Southeast Louisiana Flood Protection Authority–West, Annual Financial Report, year ended June 30, 2012**, audited, Note H, **printed pp. 61–62 / zero-based PDF pages 64–65**, hosted by the Louisiana Legislative Auditor: https://app.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/B0875E563481701486257A99006FB923/$FILE/0002D142.pdf . State publication date October 17, 2012. Both original PDF page images were visually inspected on November 17, 2026, not merely indexed/OCR snippets.

The report recounts a **February 13, 2012 compromise** between the **Algiers** and **Orleans** levee districts, with Flood Protection Authority–West acting on Algiers's behalf. Its accounting schedule identifies:
- **7.62%** allocated Algiers share of identified Orleans bond and loan obligations;
- **$6,215,840** total allocation;
- **$2,210,075** already held by Orleans in escrow on Algiers's behalf;
- **$4,005,765** balance that the financial statements report Algiers paid; arithmetic: 6,215,840 – 2,210,075 = 4,005,765;
- the latter reported as an extraordinary item, in exchange for relief from future property-tax distribution deductions servicing the listed obligations.
- Algiers relinquished **its own** claims to Orleans's non-flood assets, expressly including the **“Bohemia Properties.”**

The liabilities described include Orleans levee bonds, FEMA Katrina disaster borrowing and Louisiana Office of Community Development loans. This is an **intergovernmental debt/asset settlement** and is **not a mineral lease royalty account, private-heir class settlement, claimant receipt, or adjudication of private heirs' rights**.

**Critical verification boundary:** The executed 2012 compromise itself has **not** been independently obtained; the directly inspected evidence is the audited financial-report description. No documentation yet connects this 2012 transaction to the distinct **2018 interagency MOU** or the claimed 2023 release of an old balance. The $2,210,075 agency escrow must not be confused with the $48,087.59 claimant application-cost escrow in DNR monthly records or with court-registry/class-distribution escrows.

## What became independently discoverable today

**A free official route to the missing deed index.** The [Plaquemines Parish Clerk of Court](https://clerk25th.com/) identifies the office as recorder of deeds and mortgages and says that its land, civil, and criminal **indices** can be consulted free by guest login. Images require a paid subscription (or in-person research/copies). The clerk's [LCRAA explanation](https://clerk25th.com/mortgage-conveyances-ucc/louisiana-clerks-remote-access-authority-lcraa/) specifies parish conveyance indices from the parish's formation to the present, and mortgage indices from 1900 onward. The live [Cott Systems certified-dates page](https://cotthosting.com/laplaquemines/LandRecords/CertifiedDates.aspx), inspected on November 16, reports conveyance index coverage beginning **January 1, 1798** and civil index coverage beginning **December 24, 1924**, both current through mid-November 2026. These are **index coverage statements**, NOT a successful search result for a Bohemia deed. The guest-search form was not submitted; no deed image or record number was retrieved.

**A separate source-of-title route.** Louisiana's [Office of State Lands Document Access](https://doa.la.gov/doa/osl/document-access/) describes historical tract books, original surveys, state patents, initial land severances, and **1880–1973** state tax-adjudication records, with some source images downloadable as TIFF without charge. Its [historical records index](https://wwwslodms.doa.la.gov/) exposes land district, township, range, section and original-owner search fields. These resources may help validate a pre-1925 chain of title and distinguish a private acquisition from public land transferred among state agencies. They do not by themselves prove a 2005 *parish-recorded* conveyance or mineral-royalty disbursement.

**An official route for the missing fiscal file.** Flood Protection Authority–East has a [Public Records Form](https://www.floodauthority.org/contact-us/) and independently lists a public-records custodian and submission route in its [Media FAQs](https://www.floodauthority.org/news/media-faqs/). Its [business reports page](https://www.floodauthority.org/business/reports/) explicitly directs readers to public-records requests for reports not on the website. This is a legitimate, source-first way to request pre- and post-2014 settlement files. **No request has been submitted**, so there is no record-custodian response, release decision, fee quote or production promise.

### Precise July 24, 2014 case crosswalk for requests

The prior investigation's source-controlled original minutes review distinguishes three authorizations recorded on printed pages 13–14 of the [official board minutes PDF](https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/minutes/2014%2007%2024%20-%20Board%20Meeting%20Minutes.pdf). This cycle did **not** independently fetch those original PDF page images, so the prior verified crosswalk is preserved rather than expanded. The affected entries are:

- **07-24-14-10**: settlement-offer authorization for Plaquemines successor litigation, with identified DNR docket **563586** and successor dockets **55-774** and **61-051**. Ask for any signed final agreement, approval resolution, escrow instruction, docket disposition and release—not merely the offer.
- **07-24-14-12**: counsel-recommended action in Burt heirs proceeding **58-217**; March-associated DNR number **561174** is not itself named in the July resolution. Do not infer its exact disposition or claim payment from July's vote.
- **07-24-14-13**: counsel-recommended settlement actions for Union Benevolent Association proceeding **59-384**; March-associated DNR docket **540151** is not itself named in the July vote. Seek separate executed terms, orders and receipts.

The existing 2015 escrow-closure references must be paired with **source and destination bank or registry entries**; a closure authorization cannot identify which claimant ultimately received funds.

## Free-first retrieval sequence

**First: free index verification.** Using the parish clerk's publicly available guest index, search the *recorded* August 25, 2005 date for Eusan / Board of Levee Commissioners / Tract 78, cross-check variants and use grantor/grantee and book/page fields. Capture record identifiers, document type, date, parties, legal description and the *public index URL*. Do not assume a hit matches without comparing tract description. Access to images is paid; **do not purchase** images without separate authorization. An in-person no-fee examination is an alternative if a trusted local reviewer is available.

**Second: ask the Flood Protection Authority for targeted public records**, rather than a broad request for all Bohemia material. Request (a) the July 24, 2014 resolutions' appendices, signoffs, executed settlement/release agreements and subsequent superseding resolutions, docket by docket; (b) FY2013–FY2016 case-specific escrow/trust subledgers, restricted account schedules, deposit and transfer vouchers; (c) redacted beneficiary-disbursement totals and dates where law permits; (d) the March 19, 2015 escrow-closure approval packet, bank reconciliations and registry transfer confirmations; (e) DNR transmittal of Section 3 royalty accounting or indexes thereto, if held by the authority. Ask for electronically searchable copies and the fee estimate **before** any paid reproduction. Request redaction of account numbers, taxpayer identifiers, home addresses, signatures and other personal information.

**Third: court and agency reconciliation.** Inspect court-registry records, consent judgments, appellate mandates and satisfaction filings under the named dockets. Seek the DNR claimant certification roster and the 1997 *Vogt* accounting plus attachments, comparing period, tract and mineral unit to bank entries. A public financial statement might report a district-wide liability that spans unrelated proceedings; do not merge values unless the underlying case accounting shows that they are nonoverlapping.

**Fourth: independent legal, editorial and visual QA.** After records are inspected, classify every proposed amount as *alleged, claimed, authorized, awarded, deposited, issued, cleared, distributed, recovered,* or *unverified*. Record document date, issuer, archival identifier, original page, URL, quotation check, rights status and open gaps. Have a human legal/editorial reviewer check interpretations. ## Editorial consequence

The most important next source is not another opinion about the Bohemia dispute. It is the instrument joining a **named tract** to an **approved successor**, the final **judgment or agreement**, and the **cleared disbursement**. With only the middle documents, the article can establish that the state recognized and sometimes reported payments; it cannot calculate a 2026 outstanding balance or assert a common failure across all beneficiaries. Until the chain is completed and authenticated visual QA passes, **do not publish** post #2488.

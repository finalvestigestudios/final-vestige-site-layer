## Substantive new analysis
**Source:** Louisiana Fourth Circuit Court of Appeal, *Haspel & Davis Milling & Planting Co. v. Board of Levee Commissioners*, No. 95-CA-0233, 680 So. 2d 159, **September 4, 1996**, published judicial opinion text separately checked at:
1. https://openjurist.org/680/so2d/159/haspel-davis-v-bd-of-levee-com-rs
2. https://hallapproved.com/la/cases/appeals/1996/1634298/
3. https://www.casemine.com/judgement/us/5914bcafadd7b0493479e076

**What the opinion supports:**
- Claimants sought mineral royalties starting June 29, 1984, before deed transfers in 1991–1992. The trial court dismissed those *pre-deed* claims for failure to state a cause of action. The appellate court **reversed and remanded**; this was not a final monetary award.
- The opinion describes DNR application forms in February 1985 and a board-obtained March 1985 restraining order delaying processing. It discusses the failure of the lower court to account for that interruption when considering whether applicants could have applied before Act 819 became effective July 22, 1985. The injunction record and DNR affidavit have not themselves been obtained.
- In analyzing Act 819 of 1985, the panel distinguishes removal of certain **DNR distribution/settlement instructions** from the survival of the original **Act 233, Section 3** revenue-accounting mandate. It treated the continuing accounting requirement as material to the claimants' ability to pursue revenues.
- The opinion says the board received **$3 million in mineral royalties in 1986**. **Attribution:** this is a court-stated number, not an independently audited or tract-specific royalty ledger. It is neither a claimant award nor a payment total.
- Distinguish this 1996 **state appellate procedural decision** from the **July 23, 2007 federal Fifth Circuit reversal** of a separate constitutional takings judgment, described elsewhere in the reader.

**What it does not support:** automatic title to every tract, a specified amount owed to every heir, payment of any judicial award, verified 1986 bank deposits, personal interviews or court-record access beyond the readable opinions. The original signed opinion, DNR Secretary Raymond Stephens's May 16, 1988 affidavit, the March 1985 injunction and 1986 royalty receipts remain to be retrieved.

## New primary-source finding
1. **Louisiana Division of Administration / Office of Statewide Reporting and Accounting Policy**, *FY2000 Comprehensive Annual Financial Report*, transmittal **December 19, 2000**; [official PDF](https://www.doa.la.gov/media/xcem4e3d/cafr2000.pdf), Note 16, **printed p. 67**. Read directly in government PDF text as of this cycle. It says the **Orleans Levee District was defendant/co-defendant in 73 lawsuits, including 26 related to the Bohemia Spillway**, involving damages/property/contract questions. The district statements reported **$2,429,293 litigation liabilities for these claims collectively**. The disclosure does not allocate all $2,429,293 to the 26 Bohemia cases, does not identify claimants, and does not evidence payment. **Verification blocker:** two web screenshot routes failed (HTTP 403 / unsupported PDF view); direct binary download also failed. The text is corroborated by a government-hosted index, **but the original printed page image has not been visually inspected**. A new sourced graphic using this count remains **unapproved for WordPress release** until that visual check or alternate original is available.
2. **Louisiana Legislative Auditor**, *Boards, Commissions, and Like Entities*, dated **March 21, 2001**, [original official PDF](https://app.lla.state.la.us/PublicReports.nsf/E09C20D4A5B0010586257022002F1573/%24FILE/00000AA9.pdf), Orleans Levee District **p. C-13, zero-based PDF page 121**. **Original page image screenshot inspected.** Historical actual FY2000 amounts: **$46,982,153 revenues; $49,910,859 expenses/expenditures; $25,008,373 ending balance**. This administrative survey's 'ending balance' is **not** the same definition as witness David D. DeMonte's approximately $120 million net-worth testimony in the [March 27, 2002 *Vogt* opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/louisiana/fourth-circuit-court-of-appeal/2002/2001-ca-0089.html) and is not a Bohemia reserve.
3. **Comparison / original insight:** the 2000 filing tally (73/26), the district's reported collective litigation liability ($2.429m), its survey ending balance ($25.008m), and DeMonte's later judicially described asset estimates ($120m net worth, $57m investment securities, $9m included money market, $2.7m litigation earmark) are **different populations, dates and accounting measures**. They cannot be added, compared as same-currency obligations or used to calculate an unpaid Bohemia judgment.

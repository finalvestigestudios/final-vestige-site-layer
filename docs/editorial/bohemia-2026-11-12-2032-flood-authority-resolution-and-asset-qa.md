## Two additional primary administrative records (text verified)
1. **Southeast Louisiana Flood Protection Authority–East, April 19, 2012 board minutes**, printed pp. **19–21**, **Resolution 04-19-12-12**, https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/minutes/2012%2004%2019%20-%20Board%20Meeting%20Minutes.pdf . Contemporaneous agency roll-call vote: **six for, zero opposed, two absent**. Board requested State/USACE/others consider keeping Mardi Gras Pass free-flowing **only if no adverse effect on flood protection or navigation is demonstrated**, plus monitoring and evaluation. This request is **not a navigation permit**, a court ruling or an independent hydraulic counterfactual. The board also expressed its *position* that Bohemia continued to reduce flooding around New Orleans. Read as an agency position, not a demonstrated contemporary flow estimate.
2. **Same authority, March 15, 2012 board minutes**, printed pp. **5–6**, https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/minutes/2012%2003%2015%20-%20Board%20Meeting%20Minutes.pdf . John Lopez reported a **July 2011 bypass-channel estimate of 5,000–7,000 ft³/s** and separate **2011 high-water broad-spillway estimate 30,000–50,000 ft³/s** across about twelve miles. They are not measurements of the same flow cross-section, and Lopez overlaps with the authors of the 2014 paper, so they are **not independent corroborating instruments**. Source speaker report, not attached field sheets.

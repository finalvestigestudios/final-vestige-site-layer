## Why this run matters
Earlier reader versions traced (a) June 2023 requests to forgive a public-authority receivable and change Bohemia royalty routing, (b) July LMA discussion, (c) a December LMA director's statement that an older debt had been released, and (d) a January 1, 2024 administrative handoff. The missing bridge was what the **FPA itself approved**. This pass located and examined the searchable original, government-hosted **November 16, 2023 FPA–East full board minutes**. The indexed text directly records the bargain and the board's resolution. It does not itself supply the subsequently signed memorandum or bank entries.

## New primary-source ledger

**Source #85:** Southeast Louisiana Flood Protection Authority–East, *Board Meeting Minutes, Thursday November 16, 2023*, official original PDF:
https://www.floodauthority.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2023-11-16-SLFPA-E-Board-Meeting-Minutes.pdf

- **Printed pp. 8–9 (web-extracted lines 141–180):** Commissioner Miller asked why the interfund due-from balance was being forgiven; Vice President Roy Arrigo described the negotiated offset. LMA would not seek Bohemia mineral revenue received by FPA before 2024; FPA would forgive the interagency balance. LMA would begin receiving Bohemia revenues January 1, 2024. The meeting minutes' wording is an official record of a participant's explanation, not a reproduced accounting settlement.
- **Printed p. 8:** Director Kelli Chandler reported Bohemia revenues varying **$150,000–$700,000** over the previous five years. LMA Executive Director Louis Capo reported **$9 million in Bohemia revenues received by FPA since 2007**, describing that amount as a negotiation starting point. Neither amount has been verified against yearly bank deposits, leases, mineral-production statements or an independent forensic audit.
- **Printed p. 8:** Executive counsel Kirk Ordoyne reported that the FPA paid outstanding Bohemia-related lawsuits during **2007–2015** and that the **FPA litigation settlement escrow fund closed in 2015**. This is not proof every individual heir or plaintiff received the correct distribution. It is **not** the DNR administrative claim-cost escrow documented in source entries #67–68.
- **Printed pp. 8–9:** Board adopted an amendment transferring certain grass maintenance responsibilities and approved the **amended updated MOU**, authorizing the Regional Director to execute it. This is direct evidence of FPA-side **approval**, **not** the actual executed bilateral agreement, the LMA side's separate final vote, or a recorded adjusting journal entry.

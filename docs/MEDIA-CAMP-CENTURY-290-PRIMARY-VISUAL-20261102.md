### Primary-source and reproduction cautions

1. NASA's own [media-use guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/) generally permits factual use of U.S.-government imagery subject to third-party contents, actual creator, credit, no-endorsement and identifiable-person caveats. A NASA page or Popular Science rehost does not automatically grant republishing rights to every historical image on the page. Keep origin assets unmodified pending review.
2. TR-174 is a U.S. Army CRREL **1965** report (Elmer F. Clark) hosted by govinfo.gov. Its official historical figure matches Commons' *subject and composition* of 290:2, but source-image SHA/crop provenance and any figure-specific restrictions must still be reviewed. The figures include small text: **legibility at 960px** must be visually assessed before any downscale.
3. [Camp Century project's photo-press gallery](https://www.campcentury.org/press/photos) expressly limits its offered press photographs to credited press coverage and says **commercial uses need permission**. Do not assume this policy licenses images mirrored elsewhere or grant a site-wide cache waiver.
4. [GEUS Dataverse](https://doi.org/10.22008/FK2/ZSODKR) explicitly marks its named **source dataset** CC0 and offers a `CampCenturyMap.zip` historical plan/georeferencing artifact. That does **not** establish that the current ResearchGate-hosted figure's arrangement, annotations or pixels are CC0. An as-built map from **2017** must not be used as if it were a 1960s original plan. Rights and visual-truth gates HOLD.
5. NASA cites the historical thermal-drill image to **National Archives photo ID 404791598**; this is an attribution *reported by NASA*, not a locally inspected master or a universal permission to rehost. Preserve original photo unless a separate, exact binary/crop and rights chain are verified.

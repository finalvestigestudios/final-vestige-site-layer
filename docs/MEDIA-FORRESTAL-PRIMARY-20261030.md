### Rights, disclosure and dimensional safeguards
- The [National Archives official photo publication guidance](https://www.archives.gov/research/still-pictures/publish-photos) says federal still photographs are generally usable but **some carry copyright restrictions** and proper photo-number credits are encouraged. Thus the NHHC catalog/USN ID alone is NOT blanket clearance for a particular third-party original.
- The [DVIDS policy as updated in 2025](https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright) notes U.S.-government works are generally not copyright-protected in the US, but third-party rights/publicity, logos, trademarks and **non-endorsement** still apply. DVIDS marks these two Navy records public domain. Commercial branding and especially an affiliate product CTA must never suggest U.S. Navy or Department of Defense endorsement. Final release requires editorial rights check and appropriate disclaimer/credit.
- Commons labels 289:0 as a US government public-domain image; its copyright statement is catalog metadata, not independent investigation of creator's employment. Keep verification flag **false**.
- For any eventually approved image: verify **exact historical photo** or explicitly revise the editorial caption; record source URL/catalog ID, creator, correct year, rights/credit, original SHA256, original intrinsic dimensions, and no upscaling; render at **max width 960px**, retain aspect ratio, add responsive WordPress sizes and alt/credit only after approval. Verify no possible Navy/DVIDS product endorsement and no live Gutenberg rewrite without separate permission.
- Cache/rehosting: when permitted, upload verified responsive 960px-max derivatives to the existing WP Media Library, let Jetpack/WordPress serve sized variants, compare 390px and 1440px browsers with equal cache policy and *actual encoded Image transfer bytes*. Do not invent a CDN proxy or hotlink through access restrictions.

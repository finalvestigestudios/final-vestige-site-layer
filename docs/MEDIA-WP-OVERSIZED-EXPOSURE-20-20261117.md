## Measurement rule for the proposed 76% visitor image-weight reduction
Collect before/after logged-out browser waterfall on the **same published post URL, revision, viewport and devicePixelRatio**; image request URL/currentSrc, status, redirect, MIME, successful decode, actual encoded transfer bytes, cold cache (and separate warm cache), LCP, CLS and screenshots. Reduction = `100 × (before image transferred bytes - after image transferred bytes) / before image transferred bytes`; undefined if baseline is zero. 76% would require measured after-transfer of 24% of matched before-transfer, **not a comparison of WordPress stored originals with projected 960px files**. This evidence contains **no** guest status or network-byte measurement and does not verify the reported 42 browser-broken images.

**Image identity and date findings:** 220:2's USS Skylark photograph is an NHHC publicity halftone, credited to the Christopher P. Cavas collection and dated only “later 1950s or 1960s.” It must not be described as a confirmed image *taken on* the day of the April 1963 Thresher deep-diving trials. 220:4 depicts the modified Trieste II float at Mare Island, circa mid-1964; Commons lists a U.S. Navy/NavSource attribution, but an exact NHHC archival photo number has not been confirmed. 220:6's submerged rudder photo is dated **October 1964**, after the **April 1963** loss. These distinctions matter to the visitor's understanding of the historical record.

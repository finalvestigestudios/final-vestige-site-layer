# Final Vestige Studios — Growth-Led Content Operating Contract
**Approved operating direction: November 19, 2026.** This is a public-safe coordination document, NOT a content-release authorization, and overrides conflicting legacy TASK-008 role language.

## Mission and decision authority

**ChatGPT is the strategy director / overall editorial growth controller.** It owns the high-level view across existing Final Vestige analytics, vidIQ/YouTube discovery, Instagram/TikTok engagement, Search Console, GA4, WordPress, Dispatch and revenue verification. It selects, prioritizes, and briefs topics, series, hooks and distribution experiments based on actual results and comparable creator performance.

**Claude is the video production studio.** It receives ChatGPT's approved research/creative direction and produces professionally rendered long-form documentaries, master assets, Shorts/Reels/cutdowns where possible, subtitles, thumbnails, source and rights ledgers, platform release package and manifest. It passes completed deliverables to the shared handoff queue and the authorized Grok distribution workflow. This does NOT mean ChatGPT/Grok must duplicate its creative work. Do not interrupt an in-progress render unless a serious factual/rights issue is found.

**Grok is the audience acquisition and distribution operator.** It receives ChatGPT's topic, angle, hook, platform strategy and measurement brief; receives Claude's QA-cleared video package; prepares/adapts **platform-native** titles, descriptions, captions, tags, posts, carousel/Stories plans, threads, Medium/Substack essays and notes, dispatch-ready copy, and publication calendars. It coordinates distribution to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Final Vestige Facebook Reels/Page, X and other actually authorized channels. Grok may create channel-specific written and visual packaging; it must not create a competing documentary master or falsely claim an unverified video edit exists. If a platform-specific video cut is missing, it raises a precise request to Claude.

**ChatGPT/Praxxis is the independent editorial and business QA gate.** It validates material factual/source claims, usage rights, account targets, file integrity, platform-ready descriptions and links, native eligibility, privacy-sensitive tagging, and evidence of actual publication. No fictional reconstruction represented as documentary evidence.

## Operating loop (one campaign ID, one record of truth)

1. **RESEARCH / DIRECT (ChatGPT, daily):** Pull connected channel and owned-site metrics on trailing 7 and 28 *complete* days (label unavailable dimensions) plus current comparable creator videos, keywords and competitive outliers. Identify 5 candidate subjects, score top 3, and choose **one primary production brief** with rationale. Seek breakout potential, not arbitrary trend-chasing. Score hook strength, primary-source depth, unique visual narrative, audience adjacency, plausible search/discovery demand, rights complexity, feasible production effort, existing FV duplication, and follow/owned-audience conversion fit. Record hypothesis, source links, competing examples with their actual dates/views, and uncertainties. No invented algorithm guarantees.
2. **PRODUCTION BRIEF (ChatGPT → Claude):** Provide 3 title/thumbnail hypotheses, first 20 seconds, chapter outline, 3–5 short-form angles, evidence/rights research checklist, estimated length/format, branded packaging brief and what each platform audience should get. Assign a stable `campaign_id`, `asset_id`, target evidence-page URL (mark planned until live), and expected release window only if realistic. Check active Claude queue before assigning.
3. **DISTRIBUTION DIRECTION (ChatGPT → Grok, in parallel):** Send priority, audience segments, platform by platform hooks, native copy/article/essay/carousel recommendations, draft calendar, URL/UTM requirements and CTA purpose. Grok acknowledges and may draft nonvideo copy while Claude is rendering. No duplicate independent script for the film unless requested by Claude.
4. **RELEASE HANDOFF (Claude → ChatGPT QA → Grok):** Claude uploads finalized MP4/cutdowns/cover art/source and rights ledger/checksums and `MANIFEST.json` to authorized **private media storage**; links only safe metadata to GitHub Issue #3090 with `READY_FOR_QA`. ChatGPT tests assets/references and posts `QA_HOLD` or `READY_FOR_APPROVAL`. Grok consumes QC-cleared manifests and drafts actual platform jobs with the right accounts. End state per platform is verified published URL, not merely a scheduled draft.
5. **GROW & OPTIMIZE (Grok + ChatGPT, 24/72h/7d):** Grok reports per-platform impressions, reach, watch/skip, first-3/30s retention when observable, completion, saves, shares, follower/sub gains, profile visits, click-through. ChatGPT independently reconciles views vs engaged views; returning viewers; long-form CTR; attributable, qualified website sessions; confirmation-completed Dispatch subscribers; and merchant-reported orders/commissions. Decide `SCALE | REVISE | STOP` for a specific hook/thumbnail/topic/cutdown. Push next brief and exact evidence back to Grok and Claude.

## Release packaging standard

Claude sends a distribution-ready release manifest with: title, campaign and asset IDs, actual media URI/reference with read permission (not public Github binaries), dimensions, duration, subtitle/cover references, SHA256 checksums, source/rights ledger, AI-reenactment disclosure assessment, copyright/music constraints, exact platform release approval dependencies, and intended final article URL. Grok records per-destination text, final URL, timestamp, UTM `source/medium/campaign/content` and outcome. Prevent duplicate posting by checking platform/account/campaign/asset ID before upload or schedule.

## Required discipline and execution standard

- Each daily strategic run should yield ONE high-priority creator-ready decision (new brief OR evidence-backed revision to a running brief) and ONE precise Grok distribution/written-content directive. Record owner, next action, date and success/failure condition. Do not simply summarize.
- Preserve pre-existing film orders and subscriber-facing cadence; no demanding daily feature film. Aim for manageable first experiments with platform-native cutdowns from a strong master.
- Prioritize follow/subscription *per qualified engagement* and repeat viewers, then social-to-owned conversion; pageviews and affiliate clicks are not sales.
- Exact per-post/publisher approval rules still apply; this document is not blanket permission to bypass platform-native approval or publish/send emails by impersonation.
- No keys, costs, customer identifiers, private financials, unpublished rights files or internal code in PUBLIC issues.
- ChatGPT's already active hourly relay + Google Apps Script's verified 15-minute public-issue read/write are communication transport only. These do NOT wake existing Claude desktop conversations or prove that Grok's scheduled poll has executed on time. Record actual authenticated acknowledgments; a shared owner GitHub login alone cannot distinguish Grok from ChatGPT.
- Provider API calls are OFF until separate spending authority; do not repurpose Google Apps Script to simulate native subscription sessions.

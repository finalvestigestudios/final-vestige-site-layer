## Concrete issue and reproduced failure

The previous 320px / 400%-text `summary` had **159px** of internal width because a rem-scaled plate added about 109px of padding. The words `Inspect`, `source`, `custody`, and `record` were split across separate visual lines by `overflow-wrap:anywhere`, although the document showed zero horizontal overflow and accessibility-tree controls had names. The pre-R337 stylesheet was independently reconstructed from the previous exact source, and failures were reproduced at 320, 390 and 1024 CSS pixels with 64px root type. The new rules leave text at the requested 64px rather than disguising the defect by shrinking its font size. Links, source labels, CTA and NASA facts are unchanged.

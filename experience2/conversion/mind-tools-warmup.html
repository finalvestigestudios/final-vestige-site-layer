Can a tool change the way you pay attention?

Our minds edit what we notice. The question is not whether attention can be trained by a product's advertising. It is what the evidence can actually support.

What research says · Before choosing a tool · Tools under review

Start with what can be observed.

Inattentional blindness and change blindness show that attention is selective, not a comprehensive recording of everything in view. Demonstrations are not proof that any particular commercial audio, hypnosis or meditation product changes real-world outcomes.

Research starting points: Simons & Chabris (1999), sustained inattentional blindness; Simons & Rensink (2005), change blindness.

Five questions before you buy

  1. Is the claim measurable and specific?
  2. Has the particular product—not merely a related technique—been studied independently?
  3. What does the vendor charge and what are the renewal terms?
  4. What evidence would change your mind?
  5. Could you begin with a no-cost practice instead?

AN EXPERIMENT IN LOOKING

Two records.
One missing detail.

We ask witnesses to notice everything. Then we discover how much attention leaves behind.

Compare two original studio illustrations. Find the detail that disappears. No countdown, tricks or performance score; only the discipline of looking again.

WHAT CHANGED?Open the evidence note+

The circular FV seal at the lower right is present in Frame A and absent in Frame B. Every other decorative mark is unchanged. The labels “Specimen A” and “Specimen B” identify which illustration you're inspecting; they are not part of the hidden difference.

This is a studio illustration of a comparison task, not a standardized experiment, diagnostic test, or a reenactment of a published study. Finding the seal quickly—or missing it—does not measure your attention or memory.

THE RESEARCH / WHERE THIS IDEA COMES FROM

Research on change blindness has shown that even sizable visual changes can be missed when cues to the change are disrupted. The controlled stimuli and methods of those experiments differ from our simple reader-controlled illustration.

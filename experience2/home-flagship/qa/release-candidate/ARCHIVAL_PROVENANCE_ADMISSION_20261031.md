## High-impact finding: the Franklin museum object and the displayed image are not interchangeable

The hero links to [Royal Museums Greenwich's catalogue record for **HSR/C/9/1**](https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-504898). The case source drawer instead attributes the image to Wikimedia Commons' [**Franklinexpeditionnote.jpg**](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Franklinexpeditionnote.jpg), described in the Commons history as from an **1869 printed McClintock reproduction**, with a public-domain mark. The WordPress file shares the name and the **960 × 1526** dimensions of the Commons listed thumbnail. That supports the provenance hypothesis but is **not binary or pixel-identity proof**.

The published [Royal Museums Greenwich 2022/23 Image Use Conditions](https://images.rmg.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Images-Price-List-Apr2022-WEB-EDIT-MASTER.pdf) make free low-resolution use on a website contingent on its being **non-commercial**, explicitly excluding advertising, retail, subscriptions and donations. Final Vestige intends to monetize through products and affiliates. Therefore **do not substitute an RMG digital photograph or assume the old manuscript itself licenses the photo**. A museum **catalogue hyperlink** is not a licensed digital asset. Verify the actual WordPress image matches the Wikimedia scan (or an independently documented public-domain reproduction) before release. The source's commercial-use rules may have changed since 2022/23; do not treat the old price sheet as a current quote. Keep the folio's honest label **“EDITORIAL TYPESETTING · NOT A FACSIMILE.”**

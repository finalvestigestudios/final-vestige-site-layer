## Defect found and fixed: flagship skip link obscured (WCAG 2.2 AA 2.4.11)

When focused, "Skip to the evidence room" (`position:fixed; z-index:200`, top-left) rendered **behind the Assembler site header**; `elementFromPoint` at three points on the link returned header elements, and the screenshot showed its label overprinted by "FINAL VESTIGE STUDIOS". It is the first keyboard stop inside the flagship. Fix: `z-index:200` to `z-index:100001` in `chronicle.css` (WordPress's own skip link uses 100000), which changes the block by 3 bytes. After the fix: the link is the top-most element at all sample points at 1440 and 390 px, and the settled walk shows 59 of 60 stops visible, unobscured and with an indicator at both widths.

**Remaining, not flagship:** the preserved Margins scroller (a focusable `div`) shows a 1px outline. It is production content that this release does not change.

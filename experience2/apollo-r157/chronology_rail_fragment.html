Source drawer: what establishes a claim?

Documentary evidence: NASA's Apollo 204 Review Board report addresses the fire, design, pressure and limits of the ignition findings. Its approximate chronology is not a frame-by-frame reconstruction.

Contextual photographs: Their archive descriptions establish subject and date, not a cabin pressure reading or a proven ignition point. The 2017 museum photographs are retrospective. No photograph has been cleared for Final Vestige commercial advertising or paid-product packaging.
